6. Recursive habit operator. Let \(\mathbf z_t\) be standardized dimensionless predictors: score differential, clock, crosswind, and tendency-sample surprise. The probability of crisis habit regression is
\[
q_t=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}(\boldsymbol\beta\cdot \mathbf z_t)\quad [\mathrm{dimensionless}],

with \(\boldsymbol\beta\) dimensionless. Assumptions: predictors standardized with fixed moments; no perfect collinearity. Breakdown regimes: perfect collinearity makes coefficients non-identifiable; clock boundary at zero must be handled by clamping, not inverse-time singularities.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage runs from Galileo’s projectile problem to Navier-Stokes and Reynolds-number similarity, then to stadium boundary-layer wind engineering, then to Shannon information and Bayesian empirical shrinkage. The retrocausal audit asks: if post-game tracking showed large lateral landing dispersion, would that revise the pre-kickoff crosswind operator or only the launch-angle assumption? If the post-game play-call distribution showed extreme habit regression, would that confirm the recursive model or reveal a coaching-staff structural break? The scientific trap is mistaking a single MetLife crosswind game for a stable BUF_at_NYJ law. A second multi-week trap is divisional overfitting: repeated AFC East matchups create apparent tendency stability, but small samples inflate variance. The shrinkage operator is the antidote, but it itself breaks if the prior is stale or if the roster changed. A third trap is weather recency: one gusty week should not permanently raise the veto threshold. The correct genealogy treats each layer as conditionally independent only after verification; otherwise the model is a chain of correlated errors. The historical lesson is that projectile and boundary-layer equations are exact only inside their assumptions, and Bayesian shrinkage is exact only inside its exchangeability assumptions. The crucible therefore records both the derivation and the conditions under which the derivation would be overturned.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression versus counter-trap versus clock is the recursive layer. Under stress, a coordinator’s distribution over calls concentrates on high-repetition concepts: the habit term raises \(q_t\). The opponent, modeling that concentration, chooses a counter-trap response that maximizes expected utility against the habit-weighted distribution. The clock changes the cost of exploration: with little time remaining, variance reduction dominates, so habit regression intensifies. The counter-trap is not guaranteed; it fires only when the opponent’s belief about \(q_t\) is confident and when the counter-response has enough practice repetition to execute. The gray zone is when \(q_t\) is moderate and the clock is moderate; there, the opponent may feint a counter-trap to force the coordinator away from the habit, creating a second-order recursion. The pre-kickoff audit should therefore report a distribution over recursive states, not a single story. For BUF_at_NYJ, divisional familiarity raises the opponent’s confidence in the habit model, but it also raises the coordinator’s awareness of being modeled. That double awareness is the counter-trap. The clock is the boundary condition: at \(\tau=0\) the game state is fixed, and no further recursive update is possible. Before that boundary, the correct operator is probabilistic and thresholded, not deterministic.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The adjudication tree is physical first, biomechanical second, statistical third, recursive fourth. Physical veto: if \(V_{\mathrm{weather}}=1\), any claim requiring stable high-arc ball flight is vetoed. The proof is conditional: crosswind projection plus gust variability implies increased lateral deflection variance under the stated aerodynamic assumptions; if the assumptions fail, the veto is downgraded to a warning. Biomechanical veto: let \(\mu_s=F_{\mathrm{friction}}/F_{\mathrm{normal}}\) be the dimensionless static friction coefficient at the plant or cutting surface. If \(\mu_s<\mu_{s,\min}\), claims requiring sharp route breaks, reliable plant-leg kicking, or precise change-of-direction are vetoed. Statistical veto: if the divisional tendency sample satisfies \(n<\kappa\), the raw observed tendency is vetoed as a stable predictor and replaced by \(p_{\mathrm{post}}\). Recursive veto: if the clock and score state place the game in a crisis window and the opponent has a practiced counter-trap, claims of “neutral tendency continuation” are vetoed. The cross-layer proof is a conjunction: a claim survives only if no hard veto fires and all required data are present. If the anemometer fails, the physical layer is undefined, so the statistical claim cannot be promoted. If the field-axis vector is unverified, crosswind is not admissible. If the tendency sample is unnormalized, the information operators are invalid. MetLife crosswinds therefore do not automatically decide BUF_at_NYJ; they decide which ball-flight and play-call claims are admissible.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

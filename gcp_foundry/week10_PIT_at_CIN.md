Breakdown regime: if crowd noise raises \(P_n\) such that \(SNR<SNR_{\min}\), protection checks and sight adjustments simplify, increasing the defense’s counter-trap value. <RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The historical lineage of PIT_at_CIN is an AFC North familiarity lineage: repeated divisional meetings create a large prior on fronts, pressures, route combinations, and quarterback tendencies. Retrocausally, that prior shapes the present pre-kickoff model: earlier meetings and earlier-season pressure rates inform what each side expects now. The trap is that divisional familiarity is not stationary. A team can carry a familiar pressure shell but change the post-snap rotation, the rush lane discipline, or the protection call. The multi-week trap is overfitting to the most recent pressure rate without adjusting for opponent, score state, and lineup continuity. A second multi-week trap is assuming that a quarterback’s internal clock is stable across weeks; it can regress under repeated contact or change with protection rules. A third trap is weather-regression neglect: November SNF conditions can invalidate a model built from earlier warm-weather or dome-adjacent performances. The correct genealogy is Bayesian: use historical and divisional priors as priors, not as fixed truths, and force a likelihood update from the first two series, the inactive list, and measured kickoff weather.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: under high pressure hazard, the quarterback’s policy entropy can collapse toward a habit prior. Formally, let \(\pi_t=(1-\rho_t)\pi_{\rm habit}+\rho_t\pi_{\rm opt}\), where \(\rho_t=e^{-\kappa\lambda_p t}\). As \(\rho_t\to 0\), the quarterback regresses to bail, checkdown, or forced quick throw. Counter-trap: the defense models that regression and baits the habit throw with a late rotation or trap coverage. The offense must counter with a sight adjustment or a designed movement pocket. Clock: the internal clock \(t_{\rm int}\) must match the required time \(t_{\rm req}\) for the route concept. If \(t_{\rm int}<t_{\rm req}\), the quarterback leaves a clean pocket early; if \(t_{\rm int}>t_{\rm req}\), he absorbs contact. SNF communication noise compresses the clock and reduces the number of checks, so recursive theory-of-mind must include the channel constraint: the offense is not only modeling the defense’s model of the quarterback; it is modeling what the quarterback can actually hear and diagnose.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

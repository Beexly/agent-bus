**Scenario 2 — the drag-crisis veto.** *Environment:* a trajectory simulator with an audited force model (verifiable: conservation checks, measured landing points). *Naive model:* EV planner that treats wind and velocity corrections as smooth, quadratic response surfaces. *Failure mode:* at $\mathrm{Re}\sim10^5$–$4\times10^5$ the $C_d(\mathrm{Re})$ transition makes the response non-smooth; the planner's linearizations extrapolate confidently through a discontinuity and its predicted landing distribution is bimodal where it reports unimodal. *Veto reasoning:* a singularity in the constitutive relation, not in the statistics, vetoes the EV surface; the correct move is to widen to the mixture and drop the point estimate. *Verifiable reward:* reward is calibration (empirical coverage of the 80% interval), not accuracy of the point. *Falsifiable Sunday prediction P2:* in any game with sustained crosswind above roughly $8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at kick height, the naive planner's 80% landing intervals will contain the observed outcome at a rate materially below 80%, while the mixture-aware planner holds coverage.

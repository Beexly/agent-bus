His answer is that the ontological states form a preferred orthonormal basis, and one time step acts on that basis as a permutation. The reason this is not arbitrary is that permutation matrices are the *only* unitaries that map basis rays to basis rays. Any other unitary creates superpositions, and a superposition of ontological states is not itself ontological. So the demand "ontological states evolve into ontological states" is not a modeling choice; it is a complete characterization of the allowed dynamics, and it collapses the entire dynamical question into group theory: what are the conjugacy classes and irreps of the subgroup of $S_N$ generated by the update?

Hop two: a permutation is determined up to conjugacy by its cycle type. This is the pivot on which everything else turns. If the update $\sigma$ decomposes into disjoint cycles of lengths $L_1,\dots,L_c$, then the Hilbert space splits into invariant blocks, each of which is a cyclic group $C_{L_j}$ acting on $\mathbb{C}^{L_j}$. Every dynamical question about the automaton reduces to a family of independent clock problems. This is why 't Hooft's canonical example is the cogwheel: a deterministic system whose ontological states are the teeth of a gear is *identical*, as a Hilbert space object, to a spin-$j$ or a truncated oscillator, and the "quantum" Hamiltonian is recovered by taking a logarithm of the shift.

Hop three: the logarithm is where the interpretation earns its keep and where it first breaks. Defining $H_{\text{eff}} = (i\hbar/\delta t)\operatorname{Log}U$ is legitimate but not unique, because $\operatorname{Log}$ is multivalued on the unit circle. The eigenvalue ambiguity is exactly $2\pi\hbar/\delta t$, which means the automaton cannot distinguish an energy $E$ from $E + 2\pi\hbar n/\delta t$. This is not a defect to be apologized for; it is a *prediction*, structurally identical to Brillouin-zone aliasing in a crystal. The automaton has a bounded energy band of width $2\pi\hbar/\delta t$, and the branch cut sits at $|E| = \pi\hbar/\delta t$. If $\delta t$ is the Planck time, that Nyquist energy is $\pi E_P \approx 6.1\times10^9$ J $\approx 3.8\times10^{28}$ eV. So CAI is not merely "QM plus determinism"; it is QM plus a hard ultraviolet band limit, and that is a falsifiable structural difference from any continuum theory.

Hop four: the gray zone. 't Hooft needs the *conjugate* time operator to make the cogwheel look like an oscillator, but Pauli's objection reasserts itself with unusual force here. A Hamiltonian built from a finite permutation has finite, discrete, bounded spectrum, and $U^T = \mathbb{1}$ makes time itself periodic with period $T = \operatorname{lcm}(L_j)\,\delta t$. A self-adjoint operator conjugate to such an $H$ cannot exist on the full Hilbert space; the best available object is a compact time variable defined modulo $N\delta t$. So the interpretation's own finiteness assumptions obstruct the canonical commutation relation it wants to derive. This is the deepest internal tension in the program and it is not resolved in the literature.

Hop five: the conserved-beable count. A diagonal operator commutes with a permutation if and only if it is constant on each cycle. Therefore the real dimension of the space of conserved beables equals $c$, the number of ontological equivalence classes. This is a small theorem with large consequences: the number of exactly conserved classical quantities is *not* a free parameter, it is fixed by the cycle structure of the update. Any proposed ontological model of nature must produce a cycle structure whose conserved-beable count matches the observed conservation laws (energy, momentum, charge, baryon number). Nobody has exhibited such a model for the Standard Model. That is the central proof barrier, and it is a counting problem, not a philosophical one.

Hop six: Bell. Because all beables commute, the ontological subalgebra is a single maximal Boolean algebra and admits a global valuation. Kochen–Specker is evaded trivially, because the hidden variable *is* the ontological index. The price is paid elsewhere: the only remaining escape from Bell is measurement dependence, $\rho(\lambda|a,b)\neq\rho(\lambda)$, which 't Hooft embraces outright as the rejection of "free will" as a physical axiom. Quantitatively this is cheap — Hall's analysis indicates on the order of $10^{-2}$ bits of mutual information per trial between the hidden variable and the setting pair suffices to reproduce singlet statistics. But the epistemic cost is severe: any experimental test of measurement independence uses apparatus whose settings are fixed by the same ontological substrate, so the test is not independent of the hypothesis. Cosmic Bell tests pushing the setting choice back to $z\approx3.9$ do not touch this, because superdeterminism locates the correlation in the initial condition, not in the recent past.

Hop seven: what would rescue it. The program becomes science if the ontological dynamics predicts *residual* correlations — in setting-generating hardware, in the specific basis in which interference is modified, or in the band-limit structure of the energy spectrum. Those are the handles, and they are what the RLVR scenarios below operationalize.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

**Line 1 — Bell (1964) and the beable/changeable split.** 't Hooft's vocabulary is inherited directly from Bell's "Beables for quantum field theory." Bell's insistence on a primitive ontology of local beables is the load-bearing ancestor; CAI is what happens when you keep the beable requirement and discard local causality rather than nonlocality.

**Line 2 — 't Hooft's own 1976–1990s gauge-theory lineage.** The penguin diagram, dimensional reduction, and the black-hole S-matrix work trained him to treat apparently incommensurable descriptions as dual bases of one Hilbert space. The S-matrix/horizon papers (early 1990s) are where the "horizon degrees of freedom form a deterministic substrate" idea is seeded; the holographic counting is what later supplies the finite $N$.

**Line 3 — Fredkin, Wolfram, Zuse: the CA substrate.** The classical cellular-automaton tradition supplies the discreteness but not the Hilbert space. 't Hooft's contribution is the reverse move: instead of quantizing a CA, he *identifies* the CA's state space with a Hilbert space basis, so that quantum mechanics becomes a change of basis rather than a quantization.

**Line 4 — 't Hooft–Veltman dimensional regularization scars.** A career spent making regularization ambiguity physically meaningful (the 't Hooft–Veltman scheme, the $\varepsilon$-scalar) predisposes him to treat the $\operatorname{Log}$ branch ambiguity as physics rather than convention. This is the genealogical root of the band-limit prediction.

**Line 5 — Historical situational scars in the interpretation debate.** Copenhagen's instrumentalism, Everett's branching, and Bohm's explicit nonlocality each pay a price. CAI's scar is that it pays with statistical independence — the assumption every frequentist method silently relies on. Once you notice that, you notice that essentially all of collider physics, cosmology, and observational science rests on the same axiom, which is why the idea is regarded as desperate rather than merely wrong.

**Line 6 — Proof barriers.** (a) No ontological basis has been exhibited for any interacting QFT, including the free scalar. (b) The conserved-beable count must match observed conservation laws. (c) The Pauli objection to compact time. (d) Superdeterminism's apparent unfalsifiability. Hossenfelder and Palmer's 2020 program attacks (d) directly by arguing that generic superdeterministic models with a preferred basis do make distinct predictions, but no experiment has yet discriminated.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

Model the crisis as a three-agent recursion under existential pressure. Agent 1 is the **coach**, whose 100-rep comfort call is a *template operator* in 't Hooft's sense: a beable that is diagonal in the ontological basis of what the team has actually drilled, and therefore carries near-zero information to an opponent who has the same tape. Agent 2 is the **opposing defensive coordinator**, who models Agent 1's model of him — second-order — and baits the tendency. Agent 3 is the **quarterback's internal clock**, whose deliberation time $\tau_d$ is a physical resource with an information-theoretic cost.

Formalize the recursion. Let $\Lambda$ be the ontological microstate (post-snap alignment, leverage, individual assignments), $\mathcal{S}$ the situation class (down, distance, field zone, clock, personnel, score margin), and $C$ the called concept. The naive model assumes $I(C;\mathcal{S})=0$ — calls are situation-independent draws. In reality $I(C;\mathcal{S})>0$ because coordinators call *from* tendencies conditioned on situation. This is precisely measurement dependence, and it is the same object that lets superdeterminism evade CHSH.

Level-$k$ recursion: the coach's belief $b_1 = P(\text{DC expects } X)$; the DC's belief $b_2 = P(\text{coach believes DC expects } X)$; the QB's clock is the physical constraint that the recursion must terminate in $\tau_d < \tau_{\text{pocket}}$. Define the deliberation budget in bits: the QB can evaluate at most $\log_2 M$ branches where $M \approx \tau_d/\tau_{\text{eval}}$, with $\tau_{\text{eval}}$ the per-read fixation-plus-recognition latency, empirically $\tau_{\text{eval}} \in [0.15, 0.35]$ s. For $\tau_d = 2.0$ s this gives $M \in [6,13]$, i.e. $\log_2 M \in [2.6, 3.7]$ bits. **Breakdown condition:** if the recursion depth required to solve the DC's bait exceeds $\log_2 M$ bits, the QB must collapse to a level-0 heuristic — and the 100-rep comfort call *is* that heuristic. The crisis therefore does not test who reasoned better; it tests whose level-0 prior was installed by the training distribution. Existential pressure is a projection operator onto the deepest-trained beable.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Sovereign Frontier Primitives (SFP)**

**SFP-1 — Ontological basis.** $\mathcal{B}_{\text{ont}}=\{|n\rangle\}_{n=1}^{N}$, $\langle m|n\rangle=\delta_{mn}$. Labels $n$ dimensionless; $N<\infty$ required. *Breakdown:* $N\to\infty$ destroys discreteness of the spectrum; no algorithm is known to extract $\mathcal{B}_{\text{ont}}$ from a Lagrangian.

**SFP-2 — Permutation evolution operator.**
$$U(\delta t)\,|n\rangle = |\sigma(n)\rangle,\qquad U_{mn}=\delta_{m,\sigma(n)},\qquad \sigma\in S_N$$
$U$ dimensionless, $U^\dagger U=\mathbb{1}$, $\delta t$ in s. *Assumption:* strict global update synchrony. *Breakdown:* any asynchronous or probabilistic update leaves the permutation group and the interpretation loses its preferred basis.

**SFP-3 — Cycle decomposition and ontological equivalence classes.** $n\sim m \iff \exists r\in\mathbb{Z}:\sigma^r(n)=m$. Then $\sigma=\prod_{j=1}^{c}C_{L_j}$, $\sum_j L_j=N$, $\mathcal{H}=\bigoplus_j \mathcal{H}_{L_j}$.
**Theorem (conserved-beable count).** A diagonal $A$ satisfies $[A,U]=0$ iff $A$ is constant on each cycle; hence
$$\dim_{\mathbb{R}}\{A=A^\dagger : [A,U]=0,\ A\ \text{diagonal}\} = c.$$
Units: pure count. *Consequence:* the number of exactly conserved classical charges equals the number of ontological equivalence classes. *Breakdown:* fixed points ($L_j=1$) contribute a frozen sector with $E=0$ only; a model dominated by fixed points has no dynamics.

**SFP-4 — Effective Hamiltonian and the Nyquist singularity.**
$$H_{\text{eff}}=\frac{i\hbar}{\delta t}\operatorname{Log}U,\qquad E_{j,k}=\frac{2\pi\hbar k}{L_j\,\delta t}\ \left(\mathrm{mod}\ \frac{2\pi\hbar}{\delta t}\right)$$
Units: J. $\hbar=1.0546\times10^{-34}$ J·s. *Singularity:* $\operatorname{Log}$ branch cut at $\operatorname{Arg}U=\pi$, i.e. $|E|=\pi\hbar/\delta t$. For $\delta t=t_P=5.391\times10^{-44}$ s, since $E_P t_P=\hbar$ exactly,
$$E_{\text{Nyquist}}=\pi E_P\approx 6.14\times10^{9}\ \text{J}\approx 3.84\times10^{28}\ \text{eV}.$$
*This is the veto:* any theory with spectral support above $E_{\text{Nyquist}}$ is not merely unexplained by CAI, it is *aliased* — indistinguishable from a lower-energy state. Continuum QFT has unbounded spectral support and is therefore not in the image of the map.

**SFP-5 — Compact time / Pauli obstruction.** $U^{T}=\mathbb{1}$ with $T=\operatorname{lcm}(L_1,\dots,L_c)\,\delta t$ (s). The conjugate operator $\hat{t}$ satisfies $[\hat{t},H_{\text{eff}}]=i\hbar$ only modulo $N\delta t$. *Breakdown:* Pauli's theorem — no self-adjoint $\hat t$ conjugate to a semibounded $H$ exists; here $H$ is bounded *and* time is periodic, so the cogwheel-to-oscillator isomorphism holds only in the singular limit $L_j\to\infty$, $\delta t\to 0$ with $L_j\delta t$ fixed.

**SFP-6 — Finite ontological information budget.** Bekenstein: $S\le 2\pi k_B RE/(\hbar c)$, units J·K$^{-1}$, $k_B=1.381\times10^{-23}$ J/K. Then $N\le e^{S/k_B}$ and the specification cost is $\log_2 N \le S/(k_B\ln 2)$ bits. For the cosmological horizon, $S/k_B\sim10^{122}$, giving $\log_2 N \lesssim 1.4\times10^{122}$ bits. *Breakdown:* the Bekenstein bound assumes weak gravity and $R\gg\ell_P$; at black-hole formation it must be replaced by Bousso's covariant entropy bound.

**SFP-7 — Measurement dependence (the Bell escape).**
$$E(a,b)=\int_\Lambda d\lambda\ \rho(\lambda|a,b)\,A(\lambda,a)\,B(\lambda,b),\qquad A,B\in\{-1,+1\}$$
$\rho$ has units $[\Lambda]^{-1}$, normalized $\int_\Lambda \rho\,d\lambda=1$. CHSH: $S=E(a,b)+E(a,b')+E(a',b)-E(a',b')$, dimensionless. Bell's $|S|\le2$ requires $\rho(\lambda|a,b)=\rho(\lambda)$. The cost of dropping it, in bits:
$$I(\Lambda;\mathcal{S})=\sum_{a,b}p(a,b)\int_\Lambda d\lambda\ \rho(\lambda|a,b)\log_2\frac{\rho(\lambda|a,b)}{\rho(\lambda)}.$$
Hall (2010) indicates a threshold of order $0.0857$ bits per trial suffices for a local deterministic model to reproduce singlet correlations (I'm citing this from recollection; verify the constant before relying on it). *Singularity:* the constraint set changes discontinuously at $I=0$; there is no continuous deformation from $|S|\le2$ to $|S|=2\sqrt2$.

**Cross-layer veto on naive EV.** A surface statistical model computes expected value by pooling across situations, which is exactly the assumption $I(\Lambda;\mathcal{S})=0$. SFP-7 says that assumption is false whenever the "setting" and the "hidden variable" share a common cause — which, for a coach calling from a tendency distribution and a coordinator setting leverage from the same tendency distribution, they do. The veto is formal: pooled EV is a biased estimator of situation-conditional EV, with bias bounded below by the mutual information, not by sample size. **No amount of data fixes this.** That is the whole point.

**Acoustic/fluid sub-veto.** Tracking-derived kinematics are band-limited. Central second difference $a_i=(x_{i+1}-2x_i+x_{i-1})/\Delta t^2$ (m·s$^{-2}$) has noise variance $\sigma_a^2=6\sigma_x^2/\Delta t^4$. With $\sigma_x=0.1$ m and $\Delta t=0.1$ s (10 Hz), $\sigma_a=\sqrt6(0.1)/10^{-4}\approx24.5$ m·s$^{-2}\approx2.5\,g$. Nyquist: $f_{\text{Nyq}}=f_s/2=5$ Hz; a plant-and-cut with $\tau=0.25$ s has characteristic frequency $4$ Hz — marginally resolved, and its second derivative is not. *Veto:* any acceleration- or separation-at-catch metric derived from 10 Hz positions is noise-dominated and must not enter an EV calculation.

**Scenario 1 — Nyquist veto on separation-at-catch.**
*Surface model:* predicts catch probability from optical-tracking separation at the catch frame, 10 Hz native.
*Why it fails:* $\sigma_a\approx2.5\,g$ noise floor; separation differences below $\sim0.5$ yd are inside the noise-plus-interpolation envelope.

**Scenario 2 — Bell/measurement-dependence veto on pressure-rate transfer.**
*Surface model:* one league-wide pressure rate per defensive call, transferred across situations.
*Why it fails:* $I(\text{call};\text{situation})>0$ by construction; pooling is inconsistent, and the bias does not shrink with $n$.
*Cross-layer success:* hierarchical model with situation-conditioned random effects, i.e. estimate $P(\text{pressure}\mid \text{call},\text{down},\text{distance},\text{zone},\text{personnel},\text{margin})$, and *measure* $\hat I$ rather than assuming it away.

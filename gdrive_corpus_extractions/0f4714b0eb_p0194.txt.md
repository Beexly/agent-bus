Fourth, the ventriloquism case hints at circularity: attention is drawn to the doll, and the voice is then located there, but the text does not establish whether attention precedes sensory binding or follows it. These gray zones define the singularity regimes where a formal causal-inference model must either extend, correct, or replace James's introspective psychology.
</THINK_TRACE>

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
Let $\mathcal{M} = \{1,\dots,N\}$ index sensory modalities and let $s_i(t,\mathbf{x})$ denote the sensation from modality $i$ at time $t \in \mathbb{R}$ (seconds) and location $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ (meters). James's coalescence is formalized as a common-cause hypothesis $C=1$ versus independent causes $C=2$:

$$P(C=1 \mid \mathbf{s}) = \frac{p(\mathbf{s} \mid C=1) \, P(C=1)}{p(\mathbf{s} \mid C=1) \, P(C=1) + p(\mathbf{s} \mid C=2) \, P(C=2)}$$

where $\mathbf{s} = (s_1,\dots,s_N)$, $P(C=1)$ is a dimensionless prior, and $p(\cdot)$ denotes likelihood densities. Assuming independent Gaussian noise in each modality with spatial covariance $\Sigma_i$ (units $\text{m}^2$) and temporal variance $\sigma_{t,i}^2$ (units $\text{s}^2$), the separation between modality estimates is measured by the Mahalanobis distance:

$$d^2 = (\boldsymbol{\mu}_1 - \boldsymbol{\mu}_2)^\top (\Sigma_1 + \Sigma_2)^{-1} (\boldsymbol{\mu}_1 - \boldsymbol{\mu}_2)$$

which is dimensionless. James's non-interference rule maps to the binding predicate:

$$\mathcal{B}(\mathbf{s}) = \mathbb{I}\Big[ |\Delta t| < \tau_c \ \land\ \|\Delta \mathbf{x}\|_2 < \sigma_c \ \land\ \forall i \neq j,\ \neg\text{interfere}(s_i,s_j) \Big]$$

where $\Delta t$ is in seconds, $\tau_c$ is the temporal binding window (SI unit: seconds), $\Delta \mathbf{x}$ is in meters, $\sigma_c$ is the spatial binding tolerance (SI unit: meters), and $\mathbb{I}[\cdot]$ is the indicator function. When $\mathcal{B}=1$, the mind locates each sensation IN each other, per James's rule [PAGE 184].

Anchor selection (the "most constant and practically important" sensation) is formalized as:

$$s^* = \underset{i \in \mathcal{M}}{\arg\max}\, \big[ \kappa_i \cdot \pi_i \big]$$

where $\kappa_i$ is dimensionless temporal constancy $(0 \le \kappa_i \le 1)$ and $\pi_i$ is a dimensionless practical-importance weight. James identifies hardness or weight as the usual $s^*$ [PAGE 184]. All other sensations are then treated as properties conditional on the anchor:

$$p(\text{prop}_j \mid s^*) \propto p(s_j \mid s^*, C=1)$$

Exact breakdown/singularity regimes: coalescence fails when (a) $|\Delta t| \ge \tau_c$ (temporal asynchrony exceeds the binding window, so events are perceived as separate), (b) $\|\Delta \mathbf{x}\|_2 \ge \sigma_c$ (spatial disparity exceeds tolerance, so ventriloquism capture fails), or (c) anchor reliability collapses, $\kappa_{s^*} < \kappa_{\text{crit}}$, forcing re-anchoring. These correspond to the posterior crossing the decision boundary $P(C=1 \mid \mathbf{s}) < 0.5$ [PAGE 184].
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
James explicitly cites Lipps on "Complication," Grundtatsachen, p. 579 [PAGE 184], linking his introspective coalescence to the later Gestalt principle of Prägnanz and to modern Bayesian causal-inference models of multisensory perception (e.g., the common-cause framework of Shams, Ma, and Körding). The ventriloquism footnote anticipates the ventriloquism effect and the McGurk effect, in which visual speech captures auditory localization. Retrocausally, James's "first and great commandment" is a proto-Bayesian binding prior: the mind is biased toward $C=1$ unless sensory evidence strongly favors $C=2$. Multi-week traps include: (1) reifying James's "thing" as an ontological given rather than a reconstructed perceptual hypothesis; (2) assuming the anchor (hardness/weight) is fixed across individuals and contexts when it is actually context-dependent and prone to regression under stress; and (3) extending indoor, haptic-visual examples to outdoor or low-reliability environments without adjusting $\sigma_c$ and $\tau_c$ for sensory degradation.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: Under perceptual crisis—darkness, noise, time pressure, or sensory degradation—the mind regresses to the most constant anchor $s^*$. Because hardness and weight are temporally stable and behaviorally salient, they become the default object schema when vision, audition, or olfaction are degraded. This is a first-order theory of mind: the perceiver assumes the world contains stable objects anchored to haptic resistance [PAGE 184].

Counter-trap: The second-order trap is forgetting that the anchor itself is a construction. If an observer believes that "weight" is the essence of the thing, they misattribute changes in perceived weight (from muscle fatigue, context, or the size-weight illusion) to changes in the object rather than to the binding process. The counter-trap is to model the perceiver's own anchor selection as a latent variable that can be hijacked by context.

Clock: The recursive constraint is the temporal binding window $\tau_c$ (seconds). Perception is not a snapshot but causal inference over a sliding temporal integration window. The "now" in which sensations are located IN each other has finite duration $\tau_c$ [PAGE 184]. If the arrival clock of sensory signals is shifted by more than $\tau_c$ between modalities, the recursive theory of mind fails: the perceiver no longer attributes a common cause, and the object splits into separate events.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: Sound propagates at $v_s \approx 343\ \text{m/s}$ in air at $20^\circ\text{C}$, while light propagates at $c \approx 3.00 \times 10^8\ \text{m/s}$. For a source at distance $D$ (meters), the intermodal arrival asynchrony is $\Delta t_{\text{prop}}(D) = D/v_s - D/c \approx D/(343\ \text{m/s})$. Veto condition: if $\Delta t_{\text{prop}} > \tau_c$, physical propagation alone prevents Jamesian coalescence without explicit temporal recalibration. For $\tau_c \sim 0.1\ \text{s}$, the veto radius is roughly $D > 34\ \text{m}$.

Biomechanical layer: Stable fixation is required for visual capture of sound. Saccadic suppression and head movement introduce gaze-dependent spatial error $\epsilon_g$ (meters). Veto condition: if $\|\Delta \mathbf{x}_{\text{gaze}}\|_2 > \sigma_c - \|\Delta \mathbf{x}_{\text{source}}\|_2$, capture fails. In James's theater example, successful capture of orchestral sound by the actor's locus depends on maintained fixation on the actor [PAGE 184].

Weather/environmental layer: Wind shear and temperature gradients refract sound, altering its apparent origin; humidity and temperature alter skin conductance and tactile bulk perception. These exogenous perturbations shift modality means $\boldsymbol{\mu}_i$ and inflate covariances $\Sigma_i$ (units $\text{m}^2$). Veto condition: if $\text{tr}(\Sigma_i) > \Sigma_{\max}$, the posterior $P(C=1 \mid \mathbf{s})$ falls below threshold regardless of James's introspective rule [PAGE 184].

Adjudication: A cross-modal object is admitted only if all layers pass veto:

$$\mathcal{V} = \mathbb{I}[\Delta t_{\text{prop}} < \tau_c] \cdot \mathbb{I}[\|\Delta \mathbf{x}_{\text{total}}\|_2 < \sigma_c] \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{tr}(\Sigma_i) < \Sigma_{\max}] = 1$$

Otherwise James's coalescence is overridden and sensations remain separate properties without a common "thing."
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 — Audiovisual asynchrony beyond the binding window: Present a visual flash and an auditory click separated by $\Delta t = 0.25\ \text{s}$. Veto condition: $\Delta t > \tau_c$. Prediction: The subject reports two discrete events rather than one coalesced object; $P(C=1 \mid \Delta t = 0.25\ \text{s}) \to 0$, falsifying James's claim that mere simultaneous experience guarantees co-location [PAGE 184].

Scenario 2 — Spatial disparity exceeding the ventriloquism capture bound: Place a loudspeaker $30^\circ$ to the left of a visible talking head while playing synchronized speech. Veto condition: $\|\Delta \mathbf{x}\|_2 > \sigma_c$. Prediction: Sound is localized to the loudspeaker, not the head; the ventriloquism capture described in the footnote [PAGE 184] fails, proving that visual attention alone is insufficient to bind auditory location when spatial disparity exceeds tolerance.

Scenario 3 — Anchor reliability collapse under haptic-visual conflict: A subject lifts an object that is visually large but haptically light (the size-weight illusion). The visual modality signals bulk while haptics signals low weight, violating James's assumption that hardness/weight is the constant anchor [PAGE 184]. Veto condition: if visual reliability $\lambda_v > \lambda_h$ (inverse-variance units $\text{m}^{-2}$) and $\kappa_{\text{weight}} < \kappa_{\text{crit}}$. Prediction: The perceived object re-anchors to visual bulk, and felt heaviness is discounted as an accidental property; if visual dominance occurs, James's claim that weight is essentially the thing [PAGE 184] is contextually violated.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

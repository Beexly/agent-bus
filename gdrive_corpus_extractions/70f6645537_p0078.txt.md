Veto: implicit validity failure because \(L = 1609.34\;\text{m} < L_{ideal} = 6437.36\;\text{m}\) and the turn injects turn-hydrodynamic variance \(\sigma_{turn}\). Prediction: measured split-time variance \(\sigma_{t,total}^2\) will be dominated by \(\sigma_{turn}^2 + \sigma_{env}^2\) rather than \(\sigma_{crew}^2\), such that the detected \(\Delta r\) fails statistical significance (\(p > 0.05\)) even if the true rigging effect is positive. Verification: run repeated measures on both courses; predict effect-size attenuation factor of at least \(2\times\) on the turn course relative to dead water.

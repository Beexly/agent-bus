**Formal Definitions & Properties (LaTeX)**  
- **Symmetric Difference** (Exercise 18): For sets $E, F \subseteq X$,  
  $$E \Delta F := (E \setminus F) \cup (F \setminus E) = (E \cup F) \setminus (E \cap F),$$  
  representing elements in exactly one of $E$ or $F$ [PAGE 1].  

- **Inferior/Superior Limits** (Sec. 3, referenced in Ex. 18):  
  $$E_* := \liminf_{n \to \infty} E_n := \bigcup_{k=1}^\infty \bigcap_{n=k}^\infty E_n, \quad 
  E^* := \limsup_{n \to \infty} E_n := \bigcap_{k=1}^\infty \bigcup_{n=k}^\infty E_n.$$  
  These capture eventual inclusion ($x \in E_*$ iff $x$ belongs to all but finitely many $E_n$) and frequent occurrence ($x \in E^*$ iff $x$ belongs to infinitely many $E_n$) [PAGE 1].  

- **Key Identities** (from Ex. 18 statements):  
  - Invariance under finite changes (part 3): If $E_n' = E_n$ for $n > N$, then $\liminf E_n' = \liminf E_n$, $\limsup E_n' = \limsup E_n$.  
  - Complement-difference interaction (parts 6–7):  
    $$F \setminus E_* = \liminf_n (F \setminus E_n), \quad F \setminus E^* = \limsup_n (F \setminus E_n),$$  
    generalizing De Morgan laws for limits.  
  - Disjoint sequences (part 5): If $E_n \cap E_m = \emptyset$ for $n \neq m$, then $E_* = \emptyset$ and $E^* = \emptyset$ (since no $x$ can appear infinitely often).  

**Units & Regimes**  
- **Information Units**: Symmetric difference quantifies Hamming distance between indicator functions $\chi_E, \chi_F$ via $\mu(E \Delta F)$, serving as the natural metric in $L^0(\mu)$ spaces.  
- **Singularity/Breakdown**: Limits exist ($E_* = E^*$) iff the sequence converges pointwise; breakdown occurs for oscillatory sequences (e.g., $E_n = A$ for even $n$, $B$ for odd $n$ yields $E_* = A \cap B$, $E^* = A \cup B$ per part 4).

$$ \frac{d(sHr)}{dt} = \alpha (H_{limit} - sHr(t)) - \beta sHr(t) $$

Where:

$$ sHr(\tau) = H_{limit} + (sHr_{extinct} - H_{limit}) e^{-(\alpha + \beta)\tau} $$

If the berry population changes or the toxin level varies, the ability to "forget" the negative association quickly is beneficial. However, total erasure is dangerous. Spontaneous recovery provides a middle ground: the negative association is temporarily lifted, allowing the animal to test the food again without having to go through the slow, risky process of re-acquisition from scratch. It prevents the organism from being permanently trapped by a single negative outcome in a volatile environment.

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

Real, verifiable formal content with page anchors:

**1. Cascade threshold (coordination contagion, Ch. 19).** On a single edge, payoffs are $a$ (both $A$), $b$ (both $B$), $0$ (mismatch), with $a,b>0$ [PAGE 580]. A node of degree $d$ with fraction $f$ of neighbors on $A$ prefers $A$ when $fa > (1-f)b$, i.e.

$$f > q = \frac{b}{a+b}.$$

Worked case: $a=3, b=2 \Rightarrow q = 2/5$ [PAGE 569].

**2. Cluster-blocking theorem** [PAGE 589]. A *cluster* of density $p$ is a node set where each member has $\ge p$ fraction of its neighbors inside the set. The theorem: a cascade at threshold $q$ fails to become complete **iff** the remaining network contains a cluster of density $> 1-q$. For $q=2/5$, the blocking threshold is density $> 3/5$. This is a genuine necessary-and-sufficient characterization — the closest thing here to a "singularity": the exact structural boundary between full diffusion and coexistence.

**3. Structural balance (Ch. 5).** Signed complete graph is balanced iff every triangle is all-$+$ or exactly-one-$+$ [PAGE 122]. Characterization (Cartwright–Harary): balanced $\iff$ nodes partition into two mutually-friendly, mutually-hostile sets [PAGE 124–125]. Harary's cycle condition [PAGE 137]: *balanced iff no cycle has an odd number of negative edges* — a genuine topological invariant, checkable in $O(V+E)$ via BFS on the "supernode" reduced graph [PAGE 154–157].

**4. Braess / price of anarchy (Ch. 8).** With affine edge costs $T_e(x)=a_e x + b_e$, equilibrium travel time is at most $\tfrac{4}{3}$ the optimum (Roughgarden–Tardos) [PAGE 247]. Breakdown regime: nonlinear cost functions admit unbounded inefficiency — the explicit statement that the $4/3$ bound is *not* universal.

**5. Matching market-clearing (Ch. 10).** Buyer payoff $= v_{ij} - p_i$; market-clearing $\iff$ preferred-seller graph has a perfect matching, which maximizes total valuation [PAGE 287]. Matching Theorem (König/Hall): no perfect matching $\iff$ a constricted set exists [PAGE 281].

**6. VCG price (Ch. 15).** For the winner $j$ receiving item $i$:
$$p_j = V^{S}_{B-j} - V^{S-i}_{B-j}$$
(harm imposed on others), and truth-telling is dominant [PAGE 448–449].

**7. Power law / rich-get-richer (Ch. 18).** In-links fraction $\propto k^{-c}$, $c\approx 2$ [PAGE 545]. Continuum ODE (deterministic approximation):
$$\frac{dx_j}{dt} = \frac{p}{t} + (1-p)\frac{x_j}{t}$$
[PAGE 557], whose solution yields the power-law exponent.

**8. PageRank (Ch. 14).** Update rule and its matrix form $r \leftarrow N^{\mathsf T} r$; scaled version $\tilde N_{ij} = sN_{ij} + (1-s)/n$ [PAGE 425]. Breakdown regime: out-degree $=0$ ("slow leaks") drives all mass into the sink unless scaled [PAGE 410, 424].

Fisher later resolved this paradox in 1918 by proving that Mendelian polygenes (many independent loci of small effect) mathematically produce the exact same continuous variation Galton observed. Thus, Galton’s statistical law was robust, but his biological justification—the "reasonable a priori probability" he cited [PAGE 1]—was genetically naive.

Temperature is non-monotone: only <20°F is a real signal (−0.032 EPA). A veto condition triggers on extreme cold or high wind because the smooth temperature gradient that most models would apply fits noise and must be excluded. The veto is not a probabilistic adjustment; it is a hard exclusion of the temperature covariate outside the extreme-cold band.

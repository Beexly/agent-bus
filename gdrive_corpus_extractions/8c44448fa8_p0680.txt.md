This expression is not dimensionally homogeneous as an energy unless λ_i carries units of kg·m·s⁻² (force) and μ_i carries units of kg·m·s⁻¹ (momentum); James supplies no such coefficients. Therefore E_J is a schematic placeholder, not an SI-compliant energy E with unit joule J = kg·m²·s⁻². Breakdown regime: treating E_J as an ontological substance rather than regulative bookkeeping is a category error.

Causal substitution operator: Let M be a molecule and x(t) its position. James's identity thesis states
C(t) ≡ M(x(t)), E(t + Δt) ≡ M(x(t + Δt)) ⇒ E ≡ C,
provided molecular identity M is conserved and the trajectory x(t) is continuous. Breakdown regime: if M is an "illusion of common-sense" (as James notes common-sense things must be butchered for the molecular world) or if succession is discontinuous, the substitution fails and the pair remains an irrationalized fact.

Probability of rational passage across brute juxtaposition: for items a, b in the "vast field of irrationalized fact," James denies any ideally rational way from one to the other, so
P(rational link | a simply together with b) = 0.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Historical lineage: [PAGE 670] preserves a footnote from Liberatore's Logic and Metaphysics, 3d ed. (Rome, 1880), in which scholastic axioms—"nemo potest supra seipsum" (nothing can rise above itself) and "nemo dat quod non habet" (nothing gives what it does not have)—are deployed to refute Darwinism. The argument runs: essences are immutable; an effect cannot surpass its cause; therefore an inferior species cannot evolve into a superior one. This is a deductive closure in which metaphysics legislates for biology.

James's 1890 position reverses the vector. Rather than letting scholastic postulates determine empirical outcomes, he insists the question is one of fact: "whether these ideally proper relations do or do not obtain between animal and vegetal ancestors and descendants." The lineage thus runs from Aristotelian-Scholastic essentialism through 19th-century manual theology into the Darwin controversy, and is arrested by James's pragmatic criterion.

Retrocausal trap: A research program that begins with immutable essences will, over weeks and months of investigation, interpret every transitional fossil or varietal difference as either accidental or impossible, because the metaphysical axiom prohibits the very observation of transmutation. The multi-week trap is the sunk cost of a priori commitment: the longer one reasons from "nemo dat quod non habet," the more empirical data must be explained away. James escapes by relocating necessity from the essence to the succession of molecular positions.

Scientific posterity: This passage anticipates radical empiricism and the later distinction between regulative principles of inquiry and constitutive laws of nature. The "irrationalized fact" is the brute remainder that no amount of scholastic deduction can rationalize.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: When a mind encounters a phenomenon that resists rational integration—an item in the "vast field of irrationalized fact"—it habitually regresses to the scholastic mode exposed on [PAGE 670]: it imports a postulate of rationality as though it were a proposition of fact, and declares the phenomenon impossible or illusory. The regression is recursive because the same move that once defended immutable essences now defends any favored causal schema against empirical anomaly.

Counter-trap: James's counter-trap is to reverse the burden of proof. Instead of asking whether nature conforms to the postulate, ask whether the postulated relation obtains between successive positions of the same molecules. The counter-trap blocks the regression by making empirical succession the court of appeal.

Clock: The causal model on [PAGE 670] is explicitly clock-like: cause and effect are "the same molecules, in two successive positions." Time is not an ornament but the substrate of the relation. If the clock stops—if there is no succession, no molecular continuity, no discrete positions—the causal link dissolves into irrationalized fact. Thus the theory of mind contains an internal timer: rational cognition operates only while molecular succession can be tracked; when the sequence breaks, the mind either halts or regresses to metaphysics.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

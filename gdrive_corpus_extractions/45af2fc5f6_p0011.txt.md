Veto rule: if match-day precipitation or temperature lies outside the support of weather conditions represented in the estimation window, the Poisson rate parameters from [PAGE 274] are invalid for that match, and no score distribution derived from them is trustworthy. The veto is physical, not statistical: the model cannot generate a likelihood for atmospheric states it never observed.

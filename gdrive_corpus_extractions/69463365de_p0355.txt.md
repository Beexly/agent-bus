$$ \mathbf{f} = \mathcal{D}_I(\mathbf{x}) = \text{softmax}\left( \frac{\mathbf{W}^\top \mathbf{x} \odot I}{\tau} \right) $$

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage of James's argument traces back to British Empiricism, particularly the associationist traditions of Hume and Hartley, but diverges sharply by placing affect and conation at the center of cognition. Historically, reasoning was often conceptualized as a purely logical, disembodied process (e.g., Aristotelian syllogisms). James retrocausally rewrites this by asserting that the very ability to see "likeness or difference" [PAGE 345] is preconditioned by prior emotional and practical investments.

This creates a multi-week (or rather, multi-century) trap in cognitive science: the persistent attempt to build "pure" reasoning engines (like early GOFAI AI) that operate independently of value systems. These systems consistently fail at common-sense reasoning because they lack the "immensely varied instincts" James identifies as the engine of feature dissociation. The trap is assuming that abstraction comes before interest; James demonstrates that interest drives abstraction. We inherit this genealogy in modern reinforcement learning, where the "reward function" (the algorithmic equivalent of James's interests) strictly dictates what state variables the agent learns to distinguish. Without a rich, varied reward structure, the agent remains trapped in a low-resolution perception of reality, mirroring the "country boy" who sees only berries and not the ecological network.

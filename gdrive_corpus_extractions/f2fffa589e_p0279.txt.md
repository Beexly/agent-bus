The system then regresses to a stored, possibly obsolete spatial model, which is the failure mode of both crisis habit regression and counter-trap failure.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Three layers can veto spatial perception, and their adjudication is hierarchical.

Physical/optical layer: The medium between object and eye must transmit structured light. Weather phenomena—fog, rain, atmospheric scattering—introduce an attenuation coefficient $\alpha$ (m$^{-1}$) that reduces far-field radiance below threshold. If $I_{\text{far}}=I_0e^{-\alpha d}<I_{\text{thresh}}$, distant subdivisions $D_{\text{far}}$ are suppressed [PAGE 269], diminishing absolute space-value $V(\mathcal{S}_{\text{eff}})$. Veto condition: $\alpha d> \ln(I_0/I_{\text{thresh}})$.

Biomechanical layer: James emphasizes the self-mobility of the organ carrying the sensitive surface [PAGE 269]. This requires intact vestibulo-ocular reflex (VOR) gain $G_{\text{VOR}}$ (dimensionless), neck proprioception, and extraocular motility. If the head is immobilized or $G_{\text{VOR}}\approx0$, then $v_{\text{org}}=0$, the acceleration factor $A(v_{\text{org}})\to1$, and successive application of the thing to different surfaces cannot occur. Veto condition: $v_{\text{rel}}=0$ or $v_{\text{org}}=0$ for duration $T>T_{\text{crit}}$.

Physical/object layer: Relative motion between object and retina must exceed the retinal image-stabilization threshold. With perfect image stabilization, $v_{\text{rel}}=0$ on the retina despite object motion in the world, inducing Troxler-like fading and breakdown of space-education [PAGE 269]. Veto condition: $v_{\text{rel}}<v_{\text{JND}}$ where $v_{\text{JND}}$ is the motion detection threshold in deg/s converted to m/s via viewing distance.

Adjudication: Optical opacity vetoes first (no signal reaches the organ). If the medium is clear but biomechanics fail, no self-mobility exists to sample the field. If both succeed but object motion is below threshold, no succession occurs. Only after all layers pass does the cognitive sign–understanding operator $\mathcal{U}\circ\Sigma$ receive valid input. A single layer veto forces $V(\mathcal{S}_{\text{eff}})$ degradation or null output; partial vetoes produce anisotropic compression (e.g., fog compresses far depth more than near depth).
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios are specified below. Each is framed as an intervention on [PAGE 269] mechanisms with measurable predictions and SI/information units.

Veto Scenario 1: Motion-nullification (head-fixed fixation with retinal image stabilization).
Counterfactual condition: Enforce $v_{\text{rel}}=0$ and $v_{\text{org}}=0$ for the entire observation interval, directly negating James’s claim that motion is indispensable for space-education [PAGE 269].
Veto mechanism: The succession operator $\mathcal{E}$ is undefined; no new associations enter $\mathcal{A}$.
Verifiable prediction: Size–shape JNDs increase by a factor $\kappa>2$ relative to a free-motion baseline; discrimination thresholds in degrees of visual angle ($\deg$) exceed baseline by at least one octave. Verification: psychometric staircase with 75% correct criterion; $p<0.01$ across $N\ge20$ sessions.
Breakdown signature: Reported “real size and shape” judgments collapse toward chance because no “successive arousal of different ones by the same thing” can occur [PAGE 269].

Veto Scenario 2: Recall-channel suppression (high-load dual task blocking reproductive retrieval).
Counterfactual condition: Occupy the understanding operator with an unrelated working-memory task so that $\mathcal{U}$ cannot recall associated previous space-sensations [PAGE 269].
Veto mechanism: $\mathcal{A}$ is inaccessible; effective channel capacity $C_{\mathcal{U}}\approx0$ bits.
Verifiable prediction: Present sensations remain uninterpreted signs; forced-choice realism judgments $p(\text{“this is real space”})$ approach 0.5 (chance), and confidence ratings drop below baseline by $\Delta C>2$ points on a 7-point Likert scale. Reproductive vividness $I(s_{\text{rep}})$ falls to near zero relative to sensation $I(s_{\text{sens}})$. Verification: confidence–accuracy calibration curve; $\Delta I>1.5$ bits estimated via signal detection theory ($d'$).
Breakdown signature: The present sensation is experienced as a bare sign with no referent, exactly the state James describes when understanding fails to recall [PAGE 269].

Veto Scenario 3: Far-field occlusion (dense fog or narrow viewport restricting solid angle).
Counterfactual condition: Suppress distant subdivisions $D_{\text{far}}$ by reducing the field of view to solid angle $\Omega<\Omega_{\text{crit}}$ (sr) or by adding scattering medium with $\alpha d>\alpha_{\text{crit}}$.
Veto mechanism: $V(\mathcal{S}_{\text{eff}})<V(\mathcal{S})$ per the suppression clause [PAGE 269].
Verifiable prediction: Egocentric distance compression $\Delta d/d>0.25$ (25% underestimation) for targets beyond 2 m, measured in meters via blind walking or verbal report; near-space (<1 m) metrics remain intact. Verification: distance-matching error $>2\sigma$ above clear-weather baseline; $p<0.001$.
Breakdown signature: Absolute space-value of the total field diminishes even though local size–shape feeling is preserved, isolating the third-dimension suppression effect described on [PAGE 269].

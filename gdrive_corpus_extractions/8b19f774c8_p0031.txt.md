$$
\Lambda(\tau) = \ln \frac{P_\pi[\tau]}{P_{\text{free}}[\tau]}

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Tolman's molar psychology emerges from a specific historical lineage reacting against two dominant paradigms of his era: Watsonian S-R behaviorism and introspective mentalism.
1.  **Precursors:** Kant's notion of regulative ideas and William James's stream of consciousness provided the philosophical backdrop for treating mind as functional rather than substantial. However, Tolman radicalized this by removing the subject entirely.
2.  **Scientific Lineage:** He was influenced by Gestalt psychology's emphasis on wholes (molecules) over parts (atoms). His work parallels the development of cybernetics (Wiener, Ashby) where "purpose" was redefined as negative feedback control without recourse to conscious intent.
3.  **Retrocausal Twist:** Modern neuroscience might view Tolman's "cognitive maps" as pre-cognate of hippocampal place cells. But there is a trap: assuming Tolman predicted neural coding. Tolman was a *methodological* dualist—he kept the explanatory level at behavior to avoid the fallacy of reducing meaning to neurons. The retrocausal error is reading contemporary computational psychiatry (which links symptoms to circuits) back into Tolman, who would argue that identifying the circuit explains *how*, not *why* the behavior is purposive.
4.  **Multi-Week Trap (The Homunculus Loop):** The immediate temptation is to interpret "cognition" as an internal simulator. This leads to an infinite regress (who runs the simulator?). Tolman's genius was defining cognition as a *property of the input-output transformation*, not an internal entity. The trap is believing that because we now have fMRI, Tolman's "map" is just a spatial representation in the brain. It is not; it is a topological invariant of the behavioral repertoire.
5.  **Trap of Functionalism:** Later AI adopted Tolman's spirit but often collapsed it into algorithmic functionalism (mind = software). Tolman’s "objective character" was tied to the *organism-in-situation*. Removing the biological embodiment severs the link between the "purpose" and the evolutionary history that shaped the value function—a loss Tolman himself would recognize as a violation of his holistic premise.

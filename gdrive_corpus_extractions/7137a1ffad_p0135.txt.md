Biomechanical layer: the center of pressure \mathbf{COP} within the foot contact patch, ankle inversion/eversion range, and the definition of “part of a foot” (toe, heel, cleat, side of foot) create a high-dimensional contact manifold where a millimeter-scale overhang onto the line is dispositive. Weather layer: rain, mud, or snow reduce \mu and increase foot slip probability, effectively widening the boundary layer around \partial_{\mathrm{out}}\mathcal{E} such that a receiver who appears in bounds at initial contact may be adjudicated out of bounds once the foot settles. Adjudication protocol: (1) verify \mathrm{LegalTouch}(p) via Section 7 eligibility; (2) if legal, inspect \Phi_p(t_c) against \partial_{\mathrm{out}}\mathcal{E}; (3) any intersection vetoes completion regardless of \mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{ball}}.

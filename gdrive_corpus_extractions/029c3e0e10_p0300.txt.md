Scenario 2 — Critical reinforcement count n_c ≈ 25.
Protocol: Run three matched acquisition groups at λ = 18 min⁻¹ [PAGE 291]. Stop Group A at n = 24 reinforcements, Group B at n = 25, and Group C at n = 26. Probe each with one unreinforced trial and measure sEr_eff via response amplitude.

Protocol: Administer a central nervous system stimulant (or placebo) hypothesized to reduce inhibition magnitude I(n) or shorten τ_I during massed conditioning at λ = 18 min⁻¹. Record acquisition curves over 40 reinforcements.
Measurement: Acquisition curve R(n) and location of any peak n_peak.

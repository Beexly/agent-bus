Breakdown: quantum regimes with non-positive Wigner functions violate the classical \(\rho\ge0\) assumption; the classical limit requires \(\hbar\to0\) with coarse-graining scale \(\gg\) thermal de Broglie wavelength. Landauer erasure of one bit at temperature \(T\) [K] dissipates
\[
Q_{\min}=k_B T\ln 2 \quad [\text{J}].
\]
Breakdown: assumes logical irreversibility and thermal equilibrium at temperature \(T\); fails for reversible computing or non-thermal reservoirs.

3. Feedback control loop. Plant:
\[
\dot{x}(t)=Ax(t)+Bu(t)+w(t),\qquad u(t)=-Kx(t),
\]
\(x\in\mathbb{R}^n\) [state], \(u\in\mathbb{R}^m\) [control signal], \(w\) [disturbance]. Closed loop: \(\dot{x}=(A-BK)x+w\). Units: state and control in SI derived units appropriate to the plant; \(K\) in [control/state]. Index basis: “Feedback, 12–15”; “Machines … feedback, 12–15; … automatic mechanisms, 179–80” [PAGE 238–239]. Stability condition: all eigenvalues of \(A-BK\) in open left-half plane. Breakdown/singularity: pure input delay \(\tau\) gives characteristic equation \(\det(sI-(A-BK)e^{-s\tau})=0\); instability occurs when phase margin \(\le0\) at gain-crossover frequency. If \(\tau\) exceeds the critical delay \(\tau_c\), no static \(K\) stabilizes.

4. Prediction machine (anti-aircraft track extrapolation). For an LTI target,
\[
\hat{x}(t+\tau|t)=e^{A\tau}x(t),
\]
or, under constant-velocity kinematics,
\[
\hat{r}(t+\tau)=r(t)+\tau v(t).
\]
Units: \(r\) [m], \(v\) [m/s], \(\tau\) [s], prediction error \(\sim \frac12 a\tau^2\) [m] for lateral acceleration \(a\) [m/s\(^2\)]. Index basis: “Prediction machines, in anti-aircraft fire, 71–73” [PAGE 238–239]. Breakdown/singularity: target maneuver \(a(t)\neq0\) violates the constant-velocity model; the predictor diverges when \(\tau>\tau_c\) such that \(\frac12 |a|\tau_c^2\) exceeds the sensor field-of-view or safe engagement radius. The singularity is model-mismatch, not a mathematical pole.

5. Machine à gouverner / optimal control. Minimize
\[
J=\int_{0}^{T}\left(x^{\top}Qx+u^{\top}Ru\right)dt,\quad Q\succeq0,\;R\succ0,
\]
units [J] if integrand is power [W] integrated over [s]. Index basis: “Machine à gouverner, Père Dubarle's idea of, 206–9, 214” [PAGE 238–239]. Breakdown: uncontrollable if \(\operatorname{rank}[\mathcal C]<n\), \(\mathcal C=[B\;AB\;\dots\;A^{n-1}B]\); unobservable if \(\operatorname{rank}[\mathcal O]<n\), \(\mathcal O=[C^{\top}\;(CA)^{\top}\;\dots\;(CA^{n-1})^{\top}]^{\top}\). Singularity at loss of controllability or observability means no feedback law can assign closed-loop poles.

6. Nerve-impulse / biomechanical signaling. Index basis: “Nerve impulses, conduction of, 73-74” [PAGE 238–239]. Biophysical constraint: conduction velocity \(v\) [m/s] imposes minimum reflex latency \(\tau_{\min}=L/v\) for path length \(L\) [m]. Breakdown: demyelination or pathology (cf. “Parkinsonianism, 190–91” [PAGE 238–239]) reduces \(v\) and increases \(\tau_{\min}\), potentially violating real-time control deadlines.

7. Environmental sensing. Index basis: “Photo-electric cell, 175”; “Photo-electric door opener, 11”; “Line noise, 6, 7, 24” [PAGE 238–239]. Received optical power \(P_r=P_t e^{-\alpha L}\) [W] with attenuation \(\alpha\) [m\(^{-1}\)] and path \(L\) [m]; detection requires \(\mathrm{SNR}=P_r/(N_0B)\ge\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}\), \(N_0\) [W/Hz], \(B\) [Hz]. Breakdown: non-stationary weather (fog, rain) makes \(\alpha\) time-varying; a fixed threshold \(\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}\) yields false rejections or missed detections.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression versus counter-trap versus clock.

Model an agent’s theory of mind (ToM) state \(M_t\) as a recursive belief over an adversary’s policy. Let \(\sigma_t\) be ambient stress/crisis level and \(\sigma_c\) a critical threshold. Define the recursion
\[
M_{t+1}=
\begin{cases}
\mathcal{C}(M_t,o_t), & \text{if }\sigma_t\le\sigma_c\ \land\ t_{\mathrm{comp}}<T_{\mathrm{rem}},\\[2mm]
M_{\mathrm{habit}}, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]
where \(\mathcal{C}\) is the counter-trap update (one level deeper ToM), \(o_t\) the latest observation, \(t_{\mathrm{comp}}\) the computation time consumed, and \(T_{\mathrm{rem}}=T_{\mathrm{clock}}-t_{\mathrm{now}}\) the time remaining on the decision clock. Units: \(\sigma_t\) dimensionless stress index; \(t_{\mathrm{comp}},T_{\mathrm{rem}},T_{\mathrm{clock}}\) [s].

Crisis habit regression occurs when the condition fails: the agent collapses to the fixed point \(M_{\mathrm{habit}}\), a precomputed policy stored under stress. Index resonance: “Man … mechanization of, 15–16; … as terminal machine, 88” [PAGE 238–239] describes the human as a machine that, under overload, defaults to a terminal response. “Parkinsonianism, 190–91” [PAGE 238–239] provides the pathological analogue: a motor program stuck in a habitual fixed point.

Counter-trap: the adversary maintains a model \(\widehat M^{(d)}_t\) at recursion depth \(d\). If the adversary knows the agent regresses to \(M_{\mathrm{habit}}\) under \(\sigma>\sigma_c\), it selects action \(a^*=\arg\max_a U(a,M_{\mathrm{habit}})\). The agent’s counter-counter-trap would require depth \(d+1\):
\[
M^{(d+1)}_{t+1}=\text{ToM}\big(M^{(d)}_t\big).
\]
This regress has a singularity at \(d\to\infty\) (infinite regress), which is truncated by the clock \(T_{\mathrm{clock}}\). Breakdown regime: when \(t_{\mathrm{comp}}(d)\ge T_{\mathrm{rem}}\), the agent is forced into habit regression regardless of counter-trap depth; the clock is a hard veto on recursion. Prediction: under high stress \(\sigma>\sigma_c\) and tight clock \(T_{\mathrm{clock}}<t_{\mathrm{comp}}(1)\), observable behavior collapses to habit even when a counter-trap would be optimal; failure probability rises monotonically with \(\sigma/T_{\mathrm{rem}}\).
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical, biomechanical, and environmental veto proof.

A veto is a monotonic Boolean halt signal \(V\in\{0,1\}\); system action is permitted iff \(V=0\). Three layers are defined below; any layer raising \(V=1\) overrides all lower layers (veto proof by contradiction).

Layer 1 — Physical/thermodynamic veto. For an irreversible computing machine erasing \(N\) bits at temperature \(T\) [K],
\[
Q_{\min}=N k_B T\ln 2 \quad [\text{J}].
\]
Veto condition: \(V_{\mathrm{phys}}=1\) iff \(E_{\mathrm{avail}}<Q_{\min}\). Index basis: “computing, 11, 176–78, 181–83”; “Matter, energy and, 128” [PAGE 238–239]. Proof: if control law \(u\) executes the erasure despite \(E_{\mathrm{avail}}<Q_{\min}\), the second law is violated (negative entropy production), contradiction. Therefore \(u\) is physically unrealizable.

Layer 2 — Biomechanical veto. Human operator as terminal machine (“Man … as terminal machine, 88” [PAGE 238–239]) with nerve-impulse conduction (“Nerve impulses, conduction of, 73-74” [PAGE 238–239]). Reflex latency \(\tau_{\mathrm{reflex}}=L/v_{\mathrm{nerve}}\) [s]. Veto condition: \(V_{\mathrm{bio}}=1\) iff total loop delay \(\tau_{\mathrm{loop}}=\tau_{\mathrm{sensor}}+\tau_{\mathrm{compute}}+\tau_{\mathrm{actuate}} > \tau_{\mathrm{reflex}}+\tau_{\mathrm{margin}}\). Pathology such as “Parkinsonianism, 190–91” [PAGE 238–239] reduces \(v_{\mathrm{nerve}}\) and raises \(\tau_{\mathrm{reflex}}\), making \(V_{\mathrm{bio}}=1\) more likely. Proof: if \(u\) demands faster response than the biomechanical channel supports, the actuator saturates or the operator corrects after the controlled variable has left the safe basin; contradiction with stability requirement.

Layer 3 — Environmental/weather veto. Photo-electric sensing (“Photo-electric cell, 175”; “Photo-electric door opener, 11” [PAGE 238–239]) subject to line noise (“Line noise, 6, 7, 24” [PAGE 238–239]). Veto condition: \(V_{\mathrm{env}}=1\) iff \(\mathrm{SNR}=P_t e^{-\alpha L}/(N_0B)<\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}\), where weather (fog, rain) drives \(\alpha=\alpha(t)\) [m\(^{-1}\)]. Proof: if \(u\) acts on a corrupted photo-electric signal below threshold, the probability of false positive detection tends to one as \(\mathrm{SNR}\to0\); contradiction with safety requirement \(P_{\mathrm{false}}<p_{\max}\).

Adjudication rule: \(V=V_{\mathrm{phys}}\lor V_{\mathrm{bio}}\lor V_{\mathrm{env}}\). If any \(V_i=1\), the machine à gouverner issues hold/open-safe-state rather than the computed \(u\). Singularity: when \(\alpha(t)\) becomes unbounded (dense fog) or \(T\to0\) (approaching absolute zero), the veto set is always active and no control law can operate. Breakdown: assuming layer independence; common-cause failure (e.g., storm raises \(\alpha\) and lowers \(T_{\mathrm{clock}}\) simultaneously) can produce correlated vetoes that the monotonic OR still handles, but a veto designed only for independent layers may underestimate total hazard.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

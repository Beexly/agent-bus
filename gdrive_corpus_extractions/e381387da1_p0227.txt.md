This veto is decisive when physical and biomechanical layers are intact but performance still fails because σ_env dominates.

Adjudication rule: A veto at the physical layer nullifies all higher-layer claims of conscious necessity; a biomechanical veto severs the link between observable sampling and consciousness; an environmental veto shows that performance failure can be fully explained by signal-to-noise degradation without reference to awareness. Consciousness is affirmed as a behaviorally relevant operator only when d’ > 1, C = 1 is observed or inferred, and w_post - w_pre > 0 with all lower-layer vetoes inactive.

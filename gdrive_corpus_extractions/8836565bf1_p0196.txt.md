Let I denote injury incidence among the skill players. No injury series is provided in [PAGE 1], so durability is an unconfirmed physical parameter.

Biomechanical layer. The forward pass in 1914 was subject to rule restrictions (e.g., incomplete-pass penalties, limited passer movement) that differ from the modern game. Passing accuracy is a function of ball condition, grip, and weather. Without temperature, wind, and precipitation data for the Boston and Chicago games, any weather-normalized passing efficiency is vetoed.

Weather layer. The December Chicago game involving St. John's Prep [PAGE 1] implies potential near-freezing or sub-freezing conditions. Let T be ambient temperature in degrees Celsius. If T < 0°C, ball handling and spiral stability degrade relative to temperate conditions. Because the source does not record T, cross-game comparisons of the passing attack are weather-vetoed.

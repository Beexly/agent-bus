Crisis habit regression: Under market stress, the QCM’s flexible D_max(T) is likely to regress to the illustrative constant B_max mentioned at [PAGE 1]. Counter-trap: A strategic market maker can model the QCM/ICM policy of rivals as a known reaction function. If rival long inventories trigger lower offers [PAGE 2], a rival can preemptively shade its own ask downward to capture order flow first, forcing the first dealer to cut further. The QCM’s “refrain from any destabilizing trades” clause [PAGE 1] is meant to block this, but without a formal aggregation operator \(\mathcal{A}\) at block 170, there is no mechanical barrier to a destabilizing cascade initiated through legitimate-looking inventory adjustments.

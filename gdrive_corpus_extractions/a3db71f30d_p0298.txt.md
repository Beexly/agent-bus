If \(\eta_{\text{env}} = 0\) (stimulus completely masked), equation (4) reduces to the undisinfected state and disinhibition is null. Veto condition: \(\eta_{\text{env}}\,\delta s < I_R^{\text{tot}} - s_{Ir} + \theta - s_{Er} \Rightarrow s_{ER}^{\text{dis}} \le \theta\).

Layer 3 — Neural inhibition ceiling: if total inhibition saturates at a neurophysiological ceiling such that \(I_R^{\text{tot}} - s_{Ir} \ge s_{Er} + \delta s_{\max} + \theta\), then equation (4) clips to zero regardless of stimulus manipulation ([PAGE 289] notes sIr is only a portion of IR^tot). Veto condition: \(I_x \ge s_{Er} \Rightarrow s_{ER}=0\).

Adjudication order: biomechanical viability is checked first (can the organism move?), then environmental detection (is S compound present?), then neural inhibition (is there any suprathreshold potential left?), and only then the behavioral threshold θ. A positive prediction from [PAGE 289] survives only if all four layers pass; failure at any earlier layer renders later equations moot.

Veto Scenario 1 — Maximal disinhibition after distributed extinction.
Setup: Acquire a lever-press habit with well-distributed reinforcement ([PAGE 289] premise), extinguish it fully, then present the most effective disinhibitory stimulus available (novel mild stimulus added to S compound). Verifiable veto: if on any test trial P(R|S)=1, τ matches pre-extinction baseline within measurement error, and V matches pre-extinction baseline, then Corollary XI is falsified for this organism unless the trial is classifiable as the page’s undefined “oscillation” exception. Prediction: P(R|S) recovers partially (≈0.6–0.8), but τ remains ≥25% longer and V ≥20% lower than pre-extinction baselines.

Veto Scenario 2 — Reacquisition savings vs. full habit-strength restoration.
Setup: Compare initial acquisition rate (trials to criterion) with reacquisition rate after extinction plus maximal disinhibition. Counterfactual: because extinction leaves a degraded but non-zero sHR residue (\(\Delta_{\text{ext}} < s_{HR}^{0}\)), reacquisition should show savings (fewer trials than initial acquisition) but asymptote below the original performance ceiling. Verifiable veto: if reacquisition trials-to-criterion equal initial acquisition trials-to-criterion and the asymptote equals the pre-extinction asymptote, then the assumption of a persistent habit-strength residue in equation (6) is vetoed; extinction must have been complete erasure rather than partial suppression. Prediction: significant savings (≈30–50% fewer trials) but asymptote at ≈80% of original vigor.

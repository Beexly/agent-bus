**Operator 1: Associative Memory Retrieval (The Dog's Logic)**
In the dog's case, retrieving the sponge upon hearing the word and seeing gestures relies entirely on minimizing conditional entropy between a cue and an action given immediate context.
$$ I(C; A | X) = H(A | X) - H(A | C, X) $$
Where $C$ is the cue ("sponge", wiping gesture), $A$ is the action (go fetch sponge), and $X$ is the context (boat, water). For the dog, $I(C; A | X)$ approaches maximum, meaning $C$ almost entirely determines $A$ because they share the exact same historical contingency space. The system is solving a supervised mapping $P(A|C)$ without modeling the intervening variables.

**Operator 2: Abstraction and Transfer Learning (The Reasoning Frontier)**
James posits that substituting a dipper for a missing sponge constitutes true reasoning. This requires identifying a shared latent attribute $Z$ (water displacement capability) across disparate observations $x_i$ (sponge) and $x_j$ (dipper). We define an Invariant Attribute Operator $\mathcal{T}$ acting on the observation space:
$$ Z = \mathcal{T}(X) = \text{argmax}_{z} P(z | x) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad P(x | z) \approx 0 \text{ for distinct modalities} $$
True reasoning occurs when an agent maximizes mutual information with respect to the latent goal variable $G$ (cleaning the boat) rather than the superficial cue $C$:
$$ \text{Reasoning Score} \propto I(Z; G) - I(C; A) $$
Here, $I(Z; G)$ measures how well the extracted attribute supports the ultimate goal, while subtracting $I(C; A)$ penalizes mere rote associative mimicry.

The singularity of human reason arises when the mapping $X \to Z$ becomes completely invertible and compositional, allowing for infinite novel combinations of $Z$ without requiring contiguous experience of the resulting macro-state.

The lineage of James's "Logic of Recepts"—a phrase borrowed from Mr. Romanes—traces back directly to British Empiricism, specifically John Locke and David Hume. Hume’s dictum that "custom is the great guide of human life" posits that all complex reasoning is merely disguised habit, formed by the contiguous association of impressions. Romanes, applying this to comparative psychology, argued that animal thought is governed by these instinctive associations. 

James, writing in 1890, is pushing against this Humean wall. By introducing the counter-example of the substitute tool (the dipper/mop), James is implicitly planting the seed for Gestalt psychology (early 20th century), which later emphasized insight learning over trial-and-error (Thorndike). The genealogy jumps forward to Wolfgang Köhler’s chimpanzees stacking boxes—a direct empirical descendant of James’s hypothetical terrier. If the dog finds the dipper, he exhibits a *Gestalt shift*, restructuring his perceptual field to prioritize functional affordances over perceptual similarity.

This also prefigures the mid-20th-century cybernetic revolution and Noam Chomsky’s critique of Skinnerian behaviorism. Behaviorists tried to reduce all intelligence to James's "ordinary contiguous association of ideas." James anticipates the refutation of this view. The genealogical trap is the assumption that because humans *also* use association, association explains *all* cognition. James meticulously exposes this trap by defining a necessary condition for "reasoning proper": the ability to ignore the brute contingencies of past contiguities (sponges were always in the house) and operate on abstracted logical necessities (anything with volume can displace water).

Looking decades ahead, the "multi-week trap" involves the current AI paradigm. Modern Large Language Models are quintessential "dogs" in James's taxonomy. They are supreme masters of "ordinary contiguous association of ideas," predicting the next token based on massive co-occurrence statistics in their training corpus. When an LLM hallucinates or fails at novel physical reasoning tasks, it hits the exact "crude mass" wall James describes. It has read millions of descriptions of boats and sponges, but it has not "picked those details out... distinctly enough to be reminded of them" in a causally grounded way. The retrocausal pull of James's text acts as a diagnostic for the limits of pure statistical associationism versus true symbolic manipulation.

Physical layer vetoes: the hardware processor executing the claim-1 code is subject to thermal throttling, soft errors, and power loss; the non-transitory machine-readable storage medium is subject to SSD wear-out, magnetic bit rot, and controller failure. Weather veto: catastrophic environmental events (flood, hurricane, seismic event) at the data center hosting validator nodes can remove quorum and halt ledger finality, a physical-layer veto that no smart contract term can override. Biomechanical layer vetoes: human administrators holding upgrade or governance keys are subject to coercion, phishing, seed-phrase loss, and cognitive fatigue, allowing unilateral alteration of \(R_r\) or the validation predicate outside the protocol. Adjudication: claim 1 recites no cross-layer veto mechanism for physical, weather, or biomechanical failures; the secure ledger network’s consensus layer must absorb these shocks through checkpointing, social recovery, or validator-set rotation, none of which are claim elements. Therefore the claim’s execution model is incomplete under exogenous layer-0 and layer-1 shocks.

Adjudication rule: An observed deviation from end-directed behavior is explained by the highest-priority layer that fails. Only if physical, biomechanical, and environmental layers all pass does the residual variance fall within the behavioral layer, where Tolman’s purposivism applies. This prevents the mentalistic regress: one does not say the animal “gave up” or “lost intention”; one identifies the vetoing layer ([PAGE 423], where ends must remain objectively determinable and behavior docile).

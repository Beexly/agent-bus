\[
x_{\text{post}} = 80 \pm 9.25 = [70.75,\ 89.25]\ \text{ft}.

\boxed{\text{Goal-Post Geometry}}
Posts are vertical pillars of rectangular cross-section $(w \times h)$. The crossbar is horizontal and parallel to the end line at height $H = 10\ \text{ft}$ above ground level. The distance between post centers equals the net gate width $W = 18.5\ \text{ft}$ ($18'\!6''$). If the post height is $L_p$, the total pole length above ground must satisfy:
\[
L_{\text{total}} = H + \Delta h,\quad \Delta h > 0,
\]
ensuring clearance above pitch surface.

Bracing connections create tri-diagonal frameworks. Without braces, load transfer occurs via pure axial compression/extension within columns. With braces, shear forces $V_b$ develop at connection nodes, causing bending moments $M_b = V_b \cdot e$, with eccentricity $e$. For rigid joints, $\sum M = 0$ enforces equilibrium, but introduces stress concentrations near brackets. These peaks can exceed yield strengths if not mitigated by large gussets. Consequently, brace-free construction simplifies analysis since only $N = P$ is present, eliminating $V_b$ and $M_b$ entirely.

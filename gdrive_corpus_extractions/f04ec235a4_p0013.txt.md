Scenario 3 (Signal sparsity / document-frequency singularity). From [PAGE 6], \(\mathrm{df}(t,D) = |\{d \in D : t \in d\}| / |D|\) and \(\mathrm{tf}(t,D)\) is a ratio over corpus term counts. Prediction: avoids division-by-zero singularities and spurious ranking of rare terms, at the cost of reduced recall on long-tail queries where no lexical signal is available.

<THINK_TRACE>
The source is a 2024 medical dissertation by Lars Christian Herzog at the University of Duisburg-Essen: a longitudinal imaging study of German American football players, with 3T and 7T MRI, DTI, clinical/neuropsychometric testing, and blood biomarkers. The study recruited 18 active male players and an age- and sex-matched control group of 18 healthy subjects [PAGE 23][PAGE 24]. The core DTI comparison is pre-season versus post-season within players, plus a player-versus-control comparison. A major gray-zone issue appears immediately: the pre-season 3T scans were acquired with two different head coils—"In 11 von 15 Fällen wurde eine 20-Kanal-Kopfspule genutzt und in 4 von 15 Fällen die geplante 32-Kanal-Kopfspule" [PAGE 25]—while post-season scans and controls used the 32-channel coil. Because DTI is SNR-sensitive and coil-dependent, this creates a technical operator that can mimic or obscure trauma effects.

The multi-hop derivation is: repetitive head impacts produce axonal strain and diffuse axonal injury; axonal injury alters local water diffusion; the diffusion tensor eigen-decomposition summarizes that alteration as FA, MD, AD, and RD; a longitudinal change in these parameters is then interpreted as post-traumatic white-matter change. Each hop has assumptions. The tensor model assumes a single Gaussian diffusion compartment per voxel, with eigenvalues ordered "λ1 &gt; λ2 &gt; λ3" [PAGE 14]. FA is dimensionless and bounded between 0 and 1: "Sie hat einen Wert zwischen 0 (isotrope Diffusion) und 1 (komplett anisotrope Diffusion)" [PAGE 14]. MD is a direction-independent average of the eigenvalues and is sensitive to cell and membrane density [PAGE 14-15]. However, low SNR can produce "Sortierungsverzerrung der DTI-Parameter" and can bias FA reproducibility [PAGE 16]; later the dissertation explicitly notes "negativen Vorzeichen der Eigenwerte sowie einer Überschätzung der Anisotropie" [PAGE 48]. Thus a DTI change is not automatically a trauma change.

The results are mixed. Within players, the corrected analysis found "eine signifikante Differenz für MD mit ausgedehnten bifrontalen Veränderungen" [PAGE 36], specifically "eine signifikante Reduktion nach der Football-Saison gegenüber der Baseline-Untersuchung" [PAGE 36]. For FA, there was a seasonal trend but not significance: "Für FA zeigte sich ebenfalls ein Saisoneffekt, welcher jedoch nicht signifikant war" [PAGE 36]. Player-versus-control comparison found no significant difference: "ließ sich kein signifikanter Unterschied sowohl für FA als auch für MD nachweisen" [PAGE 38]. The abstract repeats the main finding as MD reduction in "bifrontal white matter, corpus callosum and capsula interna/externa" [PAGE 54].

The gray-zone audit is severe because the coil-control experiment itself found that the 32-channel coil produces a "flächige Reduktion bei Verwendung der 32-Kanal Kopfspule gegenüber der 20-Kanal Kopfspule" in MD, involving "die Marklagerbahnen beidseits frontoparietal, Truncus und Splenium des Corpus callosum und die linke Capsula interna" [PAGE 39]; and FA showed "eine signifikante Steigerung gegenüber der 20-Kanal Kopfspule" in right internal capsule, frontotemporal white matter, and brainstem [PAGE 40]. Those coil-effect regions overlap substantially with the reported seasonal MD-reduction regions. The authors attempted a voxelwise correction using coil-control subjects, but state: "Für MD war die Standardabweichung kleiner als die Verteilung der Mittelwerte und für FA sowie MD war die Verteilung der Mittelwerte breiter als die Differenz des Mittelwertes der Mittelwerte von 0" [PAGE 35]. They also report a "nichtlinearen Zusammenhang zwischen Spule und DTI-Wert auf dem TBSS-Skelett" [PAGE 35], and in the limitations: "wodurch die Qualität der Spulenkorrektur eingeschränkt war" [PAGE 51]. Therefore the cleanest inference is not "one season of football caused this exact MD map," but "one season of football is associated with DTI changes that survive an imperfect coil correction and remain partly confounded by coil/SNR, training, injury exposure, and repair biology." The source itself warns: "Ein trainingsinduzierter Effekt auf die DTI-Parameter ... ließ sich anhand des vorliegenden Studiendesigns nicht ausschließen" [PAGE 52]. This is the central gray-zone verdict.
</THINK_TRACE>

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
The primary source operators are the diffusion tensor, its eigenvalues, derived DTI scalars, the TBSS skeleton, permutation inference, TFCE, and the head-coil operator.

1. Diffusion tensor and eigenvalue decomposition. In each voxel the symmetric diffusion tensor is \(D\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times3}\), \(D=D^{T}\), with eigen-decomposition \(D v_i=\lambda_i v_i\). The eigenvalues are ordered \(\lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\lambda_3\) and carry units of diffusion coefficient, \(\mathrm{mm}^{2}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}\). The source states that the tensor is characterized by "Eigenwerte, λ1, λ2 und λ3" and "Eigenvektoren," with "λ1 &gt; λ2 &gt; λ3" [PAGE 14].

2. Mean diffusivity (MD):
\[
\mathrm{MD}=\frac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3}{3},\qquad [\mathrm{MD}]=\mathrm{mm}^{2}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}.
\]
The source describes MD as a direction-independent average of the three eigenvectors and as quantifying cell and membrane density [PAGE 14-15]. Trace is similar but not averaged:
\[
\mathrm{Trace}=\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3.
\]
Axial and radial diffusivity are:
\[
\mathrm{AD}=\lambda_1,\qquad \mathrm{RD}=\frac{\lambda_2+\lambda_3}{2}.
\]
The source states that AD corresponds to \(\lambda_1\), the longest ellipsoid axis, while RD averages the two shorter axes \(\lambda_2\) and \(\lambda_3\) [PAGE 15].

3. Fractional anisotropy (FA):
\[
\mathrm{FA}=
\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}
\frac{
\sqrt{(\lambda_1-\bar{\lambda})^2+(\lambda_2-\bar{\lambda})^2+(\lambda_3-\bar{\lambda})^2}
}{
\sqrt{\lambda_1^2+\lambda_2^2+\lambda_3^2}
},
\qquad
\bar{\lambda}=\frac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3}{3}.
\]
FA is dimensionless, \(0\leq \mathrm{FA}\leq 1\). The source states that FA is a measure of directional water diffusion and has a value between 0 and 1 [PAGE 14]. Breakdown regimes: if \(\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\lambda_3\), then \(\mathrm{FA}=0\); if all eigenvalues are zero, the denominator is zero and FA is undefined; if noise produces negative eigenvalues, the tensor is not positive-definite and the eigenvalue ordering/derived scalars become unreliable. The source notes low SNR, eigenvalue sorting bias, negative eigenvalues, and anisotropy overestimation [PAGE 16][PAGE 48].

4. Acquisition operator. The 3T DTI protocol used 2D echo-planar imaging, 30 noncollinear directions, \(b=1000\,\mathrm{s}\,\mathrm{mm}^{-2}\), 15 \(b0\) acquisitions, TR 5000 ms, TE 95 ms, slice thickness 3.5 mm, flip angle 90 degrees, voxel size \(1.875\times1.875\,\mathrm{mm}^2\), matrix \(128\times128\), FOV \(1440\times1440\), pixel bandwidth 1565 Hz/px, GRAPPA, and acquisition time 9:02 min [PAGE 26-27]. These are the physical acquisition parameters that determine SNR and susceptibility sensitivity.

5. TBSS and inference. The processing used FSL, eddy correction, topup/applytopup, dtifit, nonlinear registration to FMRIB58, mean-FA skeletonization with FA threshold 0.2, projection of individual FA/MD maps onto the mean skeleton, and voxelwise permutation testing with 10,000 permutations plus TFCE; significance was assumed for \(p&lt;0.05\) [PAGE 28][PAGE 35]. Here \(p\) is a dimensionless probability under the null hypothesis, not a physical unit.

6. Coil operator. The coil contrast is:
\[
\Delta \mathrm{MD}_{\mathrm{coil}}=\mathrm{MD}_{32}-\mathrm{MD}_{20}&lt;0,
\qquad
\Delta \mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm{coil}}=\mathrm{FA}_{32}-\mathrm{FA}_{20}&gt;0.
\]
The source reports a significant MD reduction and FA increase with the 32-channel coil versus the 20-channel coil [PAGE 39-40]. This operator is not a "toy ratio"; it is an empirically measured nuisance effect. Its breakdown regime is the nonlinear, spatially varying mapping between coil and DTI value on the TBSS skeleton, plus broad mean-difference distributions and noise: "nichtlinearen Zusammenhang zwischen Spule und DTI-Wert auf dem TBSS-Skelett" [PAGE 35]. Therefore the coil operator can veto a trauma-only causal interpretation whenever baseline and follow-up coil assignments are unbalanced, as they were here [PAGE 25].
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The historical lineage runs from boxing to American football and from autopsy pathology to in vivo DTI. The source notes that CTE was first described in 1928 as "Dementia pugilistica" in boxing and repetitive head trauma [PAGE 11], and that CTE belongs to the tauopathies with pathological intracellular tau deposition [PAGE 11]. Crucially, the source states that there is currently no antemortem diagnosis and that "für eine gesicherte Diagnose ist weiterhin eine Autopsie notwendig" [PAGE 11]. This retroactively constrains any DTI claim: DTI can be a microstructure marker, not a CTE diagnosis.

The DTI lineage begins with Arfanakis et al. 2002, who found FA reduction within 24 hours after mild TBI and partial normalization at 30 days [PAGE 18]. Later studies by Kumar, Miles, and Kraus found FA reduction and sometimes MD increase in corpus callosum, centrum semiovale, internal capsule, and superior longitudinal fasciculus, with neuropsychological correlation [PAGE 18]. Newcombe et al. confirmed FA reduction after moderate-to-severe TBI [PAGE 19]. Longitudinal studies by Cubon and Mayinger found MD/Trace increases after concussion or football exposure, with regression over weeks to months [PAGE 19]. In contrast, Henry, Sasaki, and Churchill found FA increase and MD/Trace reduction in sport-related mild TBI [PAGE 19-20]; Bazarian found individual increases and decreases in both FA and MD [PAGE 19-20].

The multi-week trap is temporal mismatch. Symptoms usually resolve "innerhalb von ca. 7-10 Tagen" [PAGE 10], but DTI changes can persist for weeks or months [PAGE 19], and the football season runs April to September with follow-up in October-November [PAGE 25]. Therefore a post-season scan is not a pure acute trauma scan; it is a mixture of acute, subacute, reparative, and cumulative effects. A second trap is retrocausal technical discovery: the coil mismatch was recognized after the baseline scans, and the 2022 coil-control study was added later [PAGE 25]. The blood-biomarker companion publication reported that repetitive head injuries "do not change blood-based biomarker candidates for CTE during a single season" [PAGE 3], which further prevents a simple one-season CTE narrative. The genealogy thus reads: pathology-first CTE lineage, then DTI as in vivo microstructure proxy, then coil/SNR audit as a retrocausal correction to the DTI interpretation.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
The behavioral layer is a recursive belief problem among player, coach, clinician, and researcher. The source describes "Bagatellisierung der vielfältigen und häufig unspezifischen Symptome bzw. die Unterschätzung des Verletzungsausmaßes durch die Spieler selbst" [PAGE 7], and notes that only about half of concussions may be reported [PAGE 6]. In the season before study entry, "Insgesamt setzten 75% der Spieler trotz eines Schädelhirntraumas mit Kopfschmerzen das Spiel fort" [PAGE 34]; during the study season, "im Mittel 54% der Spieler ... das Spiel fort" [PAGE 34]. This is crisis habit regression: the player models symptoms as transient, models the coach as expecting continuation, and models the sideline as a place to return rather than a place to stop. The clinician may model the absence of reported symptoms as absence of injury, while the researcher may model the post-season scan as "after trauma" even though the coil changed.

The counter-trap is structured sideline assessment and temporary removal. The source recommends consistent use of SCAT or CRT, structured TBI diagnostics, temporary play prohibition after mild TBI, education, prevention, and rule adjustments [PAGE 49][PAGE 53]. The clock layer is threefold: the symptom clock is 7-10 days [PAGE 10]; the imaging clock is weeks to months [PAGE 19]; the career clock is multiple seasons of cumulative exposure, with 420-1850 head contacts per player per season and 3500-28000 subconcussive events per recorded concussion [PAGE 9]. A recursive theory-of-mind failure occurs when each agent uses a different clock: the player uses the next-play clock, the clinician uses the symptom clock, and the DTI researcher uses the microstructural clock. The source's recommendation for "ein konsequentes temporäres Spielverbot nach leichten SHT" [PAGE 53] is therefore a counter-trap to the habitual return-to-play model, not merely a rule change.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical-layer veto proof: any claim that a DTI map represents trauma must survive scanner hardware and physics. The source used 3T Magnetom Skyra with mostly 32-channel coil, but baseline players were split between 20- and 32-channel coils [PAGE 25][PAGE 26]. DTI is "grundsätzlich durch ein niedriges Signal-Rausch Verhältnis (SNR) gekennzeichnet" and is sensitive to noise, sorting bias, and FA reproducibility [PAGE 16]. The coil controls prove a physical effect: 32-channel versus 20-channel produced significant MD reduction and FA increase [PAGE 39-40]. The correction was imperfect because of nonlinear coil-to-DTI mapping and broad distributions [PAGE 35][PAGE 51]. Therefore a trauma-only claim is vetoed unless it is coil-matched or unless the coil effect is explicitly modeled and shown not to overlap the effect map.

Biomechanical-layer veto proof: the source cites 420-1850 head contacts per player per season and 3500-28000 subconcussive TBIs per recorded concussion [PAGE 9], but this dissertation does not report helmet accelerometer dose for its own cohort. Without impact count, acceleration magnitude, or exposure dose, a dose-response claim is underdetermined. The biomechanical veto rule is: if cumulative head-impact dose does not correlate with \(\Delta\mathrm{MD}\) or \(\Delta\mathrm{FA}\), the trauma-attribution claim is weakened; if it does, the claim survives that layer.

Weather-layer veto proof: the source provides no temperature, humidity, surface, or weather variables. The season timeline is described as April to September with follow-up in October-November [PAGE 25], but no weather operator is measured. Therefore any weather-dependent explanation is not adjudicable from this source and is vetoed epistemically. The cross-layer verdict is: physical coil/SNR effects are proven and must be controlled; biomechanical exposure is plausible but not measured in this cohort; weather is absent and cannot be used as a causal layer.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
1. Coil-covariate counterfactual. Recompute the longitudinal MD model with coil channel as a voxelwise nuisance covariate or restrict to within-subject coil-matched scans. Source anchor: baseline coil imbalance [PAGE 25], coil-induced MD reduction and FA increase [PAGE 39-40], and nonlinear correction limits [PAGE 35][PAGE 51]. Prediction: the bifrontal/corpus callosum/internal capsule MD clusters will shrink substantially or lose \(p&lt;0.05\) after proper coil matching; if they remain significant at \(p&lt;0.05\), the seasonal trauma/repair effect survives this veto. Veto rule: reject trauma-only causal language if the coil-effect map overlaps the seasonal-effect map and the correction remains nonlinear.

2. Exposure-dose counterfactual. Add helmet accelerometer cumulative impact dose \(E_i\) for each player and test whether \(\Delta\mathrm{MD}_i\) or \(\Delta\mathrm{FA}_i\) scales with \(E_i\). Source anchor: the cited 420-1850 head contacts per season and 3500-28000 subconcussive events per concussion [PAGE 9]. Prediction: players with higher cumulative dose should show larger absolute DTI change; if the slope is null, the trauma-attribution hypothesis is vetoed for this cohort. Veto rule: do not infer causality from group-level season change without a verifiable dose-response or an equivalent exposure instrument.

3. Return-to-play policy counterfactual. Implement strict removal after headache and compare continuation rates and DTI change against the observed 75% and 54% continuation rates [PAGE 34]. Source anchor: the recommendation for structured sideline diagnosis and "ein konsequentes temporäres Spielverbot nach leichten SHT" [PAGE 53]. Prediction: the policy arm should reduce the proportion continuing play and should reduce cumulative exposure; if \(\Delta\mathrm{MD}\) also falls relative to the habitual-care arm, the crisis-habit regression mechanism is supported. If continuation falls but DTI change does not, the behavioral mechanism is insufficient to explain the imaging effect and the veto stands.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

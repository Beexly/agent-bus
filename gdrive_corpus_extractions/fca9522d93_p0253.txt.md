These multi-week traps are “traps” precisely because the system’s prior over normal viewing conditions is overwritten by abnormal contingencies, creating a prolonged regime in which the retinal signal and its object interpretation are decoupled. James’s momentary “suppressed sensation” is thus the short-timescale instance of a longer recalibration instability: if the normal situation can be overwritten by weeks of abnormal input, then the claim that suggestion cannot alter or suppress a present sensation holds only within a narrow, recently stabilized operating band.

Veto condition: if the subject continues to localize the phosphene at (x_0,y_0) with \Delta\theta < 1° visual angle despite full causal knowledge, then strong suppression by object-knowledge is vetoed. Prediction: Phosphene persists at fixed retinal locus; localization error < 1°; >90% of trials report persistence for \tau > 200 ms. This directly tests the Helmholtz/James claim quoted on [PAGE 243] that phosphenes “remain projected” regardless of insight.

Scenario 2 — Afterimage-object superposition. A bright afterimage is induced on the retina. While it persists, the subject views a high-contrast object placed at the same retinal location. Veto condition: if the afterimage is not replaced by the object but is instead reported as an overlaid or modified percept, then complete suppression of the present sensation by the object image is vetoed. Prediction: The afterimage endures with \tau \approx 2–5 s and is perceived as superimposed on, not extinguished by, the object; reported as a hybrid percept in >80% of trials.

Scenario 3 — Prism adaptation hysteresis. Subjects wear reversing prism goggles for seven consecutive days (multi-week trap regime). On removal, reaching movements are measured at t = 0, 10, 30, 60 minutes. Veto condition: if reaching errors persist beyond t = 60 minutes with E(t) > 0.1 E_0, then the claim that perception instantly re-coalesces to the “normal situation” [PAGE 243] is vetoed. Prediction: Error magnitude decays exponentially, E(t) = E_0 e^{-t/\tau}, with \tau \approx 20–40 min; at t = 60 min, residual error remains approximately 15–25% of initial. This demonstrates that the normal situation is a recalibrated attractor, not an immediate default.

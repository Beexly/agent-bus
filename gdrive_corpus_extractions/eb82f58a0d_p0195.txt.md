\]
Knight's descriptive claim on [PAGE 180] is that at \(p^*\), "no one is under any inducement to make any change," which is precisely the first-order condition that the expenditure-constrained utility maximum has been reached.

Moral neutrality operator: Define a social welfare functional \(W = \sum_i \lambda_i u_i(x_i)\) only if interpersonal comparisons of utility are admitted. Knight rejects this; the competitive outcome carries "no moral attribute whatever" [PAGE 180].

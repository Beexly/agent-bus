One can construct a sequence of unit vectors that are orthogonal, which has no weakly convergent subsequence (since weak convergence would imply convergence of inner products, impossible for orthogonal sequence).
*   **Prediction:** In non-separable spaces, optimization problems formulated as finding minimizers of convex functionals over weak* compact sets may fail to have solutions attained by minimizing sequences, requiring transfinite methods or different compactness notions. This breaks standard variational arguments in PDEs and calculus of variations when applied to high-dimensional or infinite-parameter models.

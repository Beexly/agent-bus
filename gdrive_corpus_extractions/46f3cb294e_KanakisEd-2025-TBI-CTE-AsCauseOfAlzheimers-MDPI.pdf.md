Neuschmid et al. quantify the driving gradient: extracellular Ca²⁺ "~1 mM ... approximately 10,000 times higher than cytosolic levels (~100 nM)" [PAGE 89]. Membrane breach + NCX reversal + NMDAR/AMPAR flux collapse this gradient within seconds, and the resulting Ca²⁺ overload activates calpain/CDK5/GSK3β/PP2A, which the reviews tie to Aβ and tau. This is a *feed-forward* (positive-gain) loop: Aβ further raises Ca²⁺, which raises BACE1 (Ca/CaM "increases BACE1 enzymatic activity by approximately 2.5-fold in vitro" [PAGE 94]), producing more Aβ. Loop gain >1 ⇒ runaway degeneration.

Hop 3 (molecular → clinical): Epidemiology reports dose-responsive hazard/odds ratios — HR 2.36/2.51/3.77 across mild-no-LOC/mild-LOC/moderate-severe [PAGE 16]; all-cause dementia OR 1.79 [PAGE 16]; MIRAGE "OR ... up to 9.9" [PAGE 31]. But here is the gray zone: the *strongest* confounder-adjusted analysis (Mielke) gives HR 1.32 overall but "not for 'deﬁnite' TBI (HR = 1.22, 95% CI: 0.68–2.18)" [PAGE 30] — the CI crosses 1. That is a layer-specific veto.

Gray-zone audit: (a) reverse causation is explicitly flagged — dementia-related impairment "increases the risk of falls and subsequent TBI" [PAGE 89]; (b) imaging null results — Eagle et al. "no signiﬁcant elevations in amyloid or tau PET uptake" [PAGE 26], Hicks et al. "no elevation of β-amyloid or tau" [PAGE 31]; (c) amyloidosis ≠ dementia — centenarian neuropathology (Hauw: 19/20 with NFT/diffuse Aβ, "only ﬁve of them were diagnosed with dementia" [PAGE 120]). These break the naive "amyloid cascade → clinical AD" prediction while leaving the vascular/calcium/tau axis intact. The causal manifold is real but *anisotropic*: strong along white-matter/BBB/tau, weak along extracellular-Aβ.
</THINK_TRACE>

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
All operators are standard; none are invented ratios.

1) Calcium electrochemical driving force (Nernst + Ohm's law for ionic current). The stored gradient is $[Ca^{2+}]_o/[Ca^{2+}]_i \approx 10^{-3}/10^{-7}=10^{4}$ [PAGE 89].
$$E_{Ca}=\frac{RT}{zF}\ln\frac{[Ca^{2+}]_o}{[Ca^{2+}]_i}$$
With $R=8.314\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$, $T=310\ \mathrm{K}$, $z=2$, $F=96485\ \mathrm{C\,mol^{-1}}$: $RT/F\approx26.7\ \mathrm{mV}$, so $E_{Ca}\approx(26.7/2)\ln(10^4)\approx123\ \mathrm{mV}$. The inward current is $I_{Ca}=g_{Ca}(V_m-E_{Ca})$; at rest $V_m\approx-65\ \mathrm{mV}$ the driving force is $\approx-188\ \mathrm{mV}$, explaining why membrane breach forces near-maximal Ca²⁺ entry. Assumptions: ideal Nernst equilibrium, activity≈concentration, 37 °C. Breakdown regime: Ca²⁺ is a *non-equilibrium* steady state maintained by PMCA/NCX/SERCA; $E_{Ca}$ gives driving force, not a resting potential, and once pumps fail (ATP depletion) the equation no longer predicts a setpoint — it predicts monotone influx.

2) von Mises deviatoric stress (DAI surrogate [PAGE 17]):
$$\sigma_{vM}=\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{2}\big[(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2\big]}\quad[\mathrm{Pa}]$$
Assumption: isotropic distortion-energy yield. Breakdown: brain is "viscoelastic ... time-dependent and non-linear" [PAGE 14] and tension–compression asymmetric, so a *static* $\sigma_{vM}$ threshold ignores the operative strain rate $\dot\varepsilon = 10\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$ [PAGE 17] (units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$); the criterion is valid only when paired with a rate-dependent constitutive law.

3) Fractional anisotropy (axonal-integrity marker, dimensionless $\in[0,1]$):
$$FA=\sqrt{\tfrac{3}{2}}\;\frac{\sqrt{(\lambda_1-\bar\lambda)^2+(\lambda_2-\bar\lambda)^2+(\lambda_3-\bar\lambda)^2}}{\sqrt{\lambda_1^2+\lambda_2^2+\lambda_3^2}},\quad \bar\lambda=\tfrac{1}{3}\sum_i\lambda_i$$
$\lambda_i$ are diffusion-tensor eigenvalues ($\mathrm{mm^2\,s^{-1}}$). Reduced FA marks axonal disruption [PAGE 17, 27]. Breakdown: FA conflates axonal loss, demyelination, and crossing-fiber geometry; it is sensitive but not specific.

4) ASL single-compartment late-phase clearance (Joseph): "linear analysis can be employed to determine the rate of clearance (slope of the line)" [PAGE 79].
$$C(t)=C_0e^{-k_{\text{clear}}t}\;\Longleftrightarrow\;\ln C(t)=\ln C_0-k_{\text{clear}}\,t,\qquad k_{\text{clear}}\ [\mathrm{s^{-1}}]$$
Reduced $k_{\text{clear}}$ ⇔ prolonged capillary mean transit time (cMTT) and BBB leak/glymphatic stasis. Assumption: one dominant, well-mixed clearance pathway with known arterial input. Breakdown: the signal is multi-compartment (T1 at 3 T — white matter 800–850 ms, blood 1650 ms, gray matter 1700 ms, CSF 3800 ms [PAGE 79]); when compartments overlap inside the post-labeling-delay window, a single exponential is misspecified and low S/N inflates variance.

5) PET normalization: $SUV_{bg}=\dfrac{\overline{I}_{\text{VOI}}}{\overline{I}_{\text{background}}}$ (dimensionless) with a $4\ \mathrm{mm^3}$ background VOI in midbrain [PAGE 66]. Breakdown: BBB leak confounds TSPO signal — "accumulation was potentially explained by the disturbance of the BBB" [PAGE 62]; correlation with IgG leak $R^2=0.21,\ p=0.03$ [PAGE 62].

6) Association operators (unitless; null at 1):
$$OR=\frac{p_1/(1-p_1)}{p_0/(1-p_0)},\qquad h(t\mid X)=h_0(t)e^{\beta X},\quad HR=e^{\beta}.$$
Reported: dementia OR 1.79 (1.66–1.92) [PAGE 16]; overall AD HR 1.32 (1.11–1.58) but "deﬁnite" TBI HR 1.22 (0.68–2.18) [PAGE 30]; TREM2 R47H OR 5.05 (2.77–9.16) [PAGE 23]; SNP-heritability $h^2_{SNP}=0.060$, liability-scale $0.26$ [PAGE 93].

7) Feed-forward loop gain (control-theoretic): for the $A\beta\leftrightarrow Ca^{2+}$ cycle [PAGE 94], define $G=\prod_j \partial(\text{step}_{j+1})/\partial(\text{step}_j)$; $G>1$ ⇒ runaway (excitotoxic collapse), $G<1$ ⇒ damped. The 2.5-fold BACE1 gain [PAGE 94] plus Ca²⁺-dependent γ-secretase upregulation push $G$ toward the unstable regime.
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The intellectual lineage runs in three dated waves. Wave 1 (mechanics of concussion): Martland's 1928 "punch drunk" description [PAGE 19], Millspaugh's "dementia pugilistica," and Corsellis et al.'s 1973 clinico-pathological series of 15 retired boxers [PAGE 19]. Wave 2 (modern CTE neuropathology): McKee's diagnostic criteria and four-stage scheme [PAGE 19], anchored by the pathognomonic lesion — "perivascular deposits of hyperphosphorylated tau found at the depths of cortical sulci" [PAGE 19] — and later shown by cryo-EM to adopt "a distinct conformation compared to those in AD" [PAGE 20]. Wave 3 (molecular unification, this volume): the calcium hypothesis (Khachaturian lineage, restated in Neuschmid) and the neurovascular/BBB hypothesis (Zlokovic lineage, restated in Katramadou and Joseph).

A decisive retro-causal insight is the *frequency-of-exposure* genealogy. Blaschke et al. found that recovery was fastest under low-frequency (weekly) injury and worst at 48 h intervals: "This suggests a pre-conditioning effect of preceding traumata ... but only when sufﬁcient time for recovery was granted between concussions" [PAGE 62]. This reframes the historical "dose" variable: the causal driver is not cumulative impacts alone but the *recovery interval relative to the BBB-repair time constant*.

The multi-week/multi-decade trap: BBB permeability and neuroinflammation "persist for years following even a single moderate-to-severe TBI" [PAGE 18, Johnson et al.], and tau propagates "prion-like" over 12 months in wild-type mice [PAGE 19]. Any genealogy that only samples the acute phase will mis-estimate the causal weight — a structural survivorship bias baked into cross-sectional cohorts.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Framing the pathophysiology as competing recursive controllers:

Crisis-habit regression (maladaptive attractors). Two coupled positive-feedback loops dominate: (i) $A\beta\uparrow \Rightarrow Ca^{2+}\uparrow \Rightarrow$ calpain/CDK5/GSK3β activation $\Rightarrow$ BACE1/γ-secretase $\uparrow \Rightarrow A\beta\uparrow$ (loop gain $G$, [PAGE 94]); (ii) kinase/phosphatase imbalance — "PP2A is responsible for approximately 70% of tau dephosphorylation" [PAGE 99], and GSK3β "phosphorylates more than 40 serine/threonine residues on tau" [PAGE 98], so phosphatase failure plus kinase hyperactivity drives a monotone drift toward hyperphosphorylated-tau attractor states (NFTs). Neuroinflammation is the third regressor: microglial IL-1β/IL-6/TNF-α feed tau phosphorylation and NLRP3 inflammasome activation [PAGE 28–29], and in Joseph's model endothelial C3a→C3aR signaling is itself a self-reinforcing BBB-leak loop [PAGE 74].

Counter-trap (the counterintuitive stable point). The naive monotone "more impacts ⇒ more damage" model is falsified by the preconditioning result [PAGE 62]: sub-threshold spaced injuries can *improve* recovery trajectory. Mechanistically, the same HIF-1 axis is the counter-trap — Mitroshina and the Kanakis editorial stress HIF-1 is dual-signed: "HIF-1 can be protective in some contexts yet proinﬂammatory and neurotoxic in others" [PAGE 11], protective via glycolytic/angiogenic adaptation early, but pro-apoptotic (p53 stabilization) when hypoxia is "chronic" and severe [PAGE 117]. The controller is therefore non-monotone in its input history: the sign of the response depends on integrated dose and duration, not instantaneous level.

Clock (temporal-window adjudication). The therapeutic "clock" is explicit: acute phase (0–3 h) hemostasis/ICP control [PAGE 36], subacute excitotoxicity and neuroinflammation, chronic tauopathy [PAGE 27]. The correct controller must be *time-varying*: excitotoxic/NVU stabilization early, anti-inflammatory modulation subacute, anti-tau/chaperone/autophagy (BAG3, mTOR modulation) chronic. A single fixed-gain intervention cannot stabilize a plant whose dominant poles migrate across scales — which is precisely why single-target anti-amyloid trials underperform and why the reviews argue for temporally staged combination therapy [PAGE 37].
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Treat "veto" as a layer at which the causal propagation $TBI \to CTE \to AD$ is blocked or fails a consistency test. Adjudication across three physical layers:

Layer 1 — Biomechanical (input validity). PASS with bounded domain. The insult is well-posed: rotational acceleration produces measurable rotation ($22^\circ$, SD $6.7^\circ$ [PAGE 56]) and von Mises/strain-rate criteria apply [PAGE 17]. Veto condition: if $\dot\varepsilon$ falls below the $\sim10\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$ regime, DAI does not initiate (mild concussion "does not cause any macroscopic structural brain damage" [PAGE 46]). Below-threshold loading vetoes downstream propagation — consistent with sub-concussive impacts being individually silent yet cumulatively relevant.

Layer 2 — Molecular/BBB (mediation validity). PASS. The mediation is empirically linked: BBB-disruption intensity correlates with functional deficit ($R^2=0.29,\ p<0.01$, abstract [PAGE 54]) and hippocampal IgG leak correlates with Y-maze impairment ($R^2=0.18,\ p=0.01$ [PAGE 60]). Because the correlations are partial ($R^2<0.3$), the layer is *necessary but not sufficient*; mediation is probabilistic, not deterministic. Veto: at the molecular layer the TSPO-PET "neuroinflammation" readout is confounded by BBB leak itself ($R^2=0.21$ with IgG [PAGE 62]), so an unadjusted PET signal must be vetoed as evidence of microglial activation.

Layer 3 — Clinical/epidemiological (outcome validity). MIXED — and this is where the strongest veto sits. The dose-response is robust for all-cause dementia but collapses for pathologically-"deﬁnite" TBI (HR 1.22, CI 0.68–2.18 crossing 1 [PAGE 30]) and for amyloid/tau PET (Eagle [PAGE 26], Hicks [PAGE 31]). Cross-layer proof of veto: if TBI caused AD primarily via the extracellular-Aβ cascade, then Layer-3 amyloid-PET must diverge from controls; it does not. Therefore the surviving causal path is the *white-matter/BBB/calcium/tau* route (Layers 1–2), not the classical amyloid route. Environmental ("weather") covariate veto: chronic hypoxia/HIF-1 [PAGE 108–120] and comorbid vascular disease [PAGE 15] can independently move both BBB integrity and dementia risk, confounding Layer 3 unless stratified. Net adjudication: causality is *not vetoed* for post-traumatic tauopathy/white-matter degeneration; it *is vetoed* for a strong, unmediated TBI→amyloid→AD pathway.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three falsifiable scenarios that would veto the volume's central causal model, each with a concrete, verifiable prediction:

Veto 1 — The "definite-TBI" null persists. If larger, imaging/pathology-adjudicated TBI cohorts continue to yield HRs whose 95% CI includes 1 (as "deﬁnite TBI (HR = 1.22, 95% CI: 0.68–2.18)" [PAGE 30]), then the apparent dose-response is driven by exposure misclassification/reverse causation. Prediction: pooled definite-TBI dementia HR stays ~1.1–1.3 with CI covering 1, while loosely-defined "possible/probable" strata stay elevated (1.29–1.42 [PAGE 30]).

Veto 2 — ASL clearance tracks recovery independent of injury frequency. The model predicts persistent BBB leak scales with repetitive frequency. If late-phase ASL $k_{\text{clear}}$ (units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$) recovers on the same trajectory regardless of 48 h vs weekly spacing, the BBB-persistence-as-severity-biomarker claim is vetoed. Prediction: $k_{\text{clear}}$ recovery slope correlates with SCAT6/SCOAT6 return-to-sport timing at the Blaschke-type effect size ($R^2\approx0.18$–$0.29$ [PAGE 60, 62]); frequency-independent recovery would falsify it.

Veto 3 — Tau-PET stays flat a decade out. The prion-like-propagation model predicts eventual measurable tau divergence in chronic-TBI survivors. If adequately powered 10-year cohorts (extending Eagle [PAGE 26] and Hicks [PAGE 31]) still show no tau-SUVR separation from controls, the transmissible-tau hypothesis is vetoed at the clinical layer. Prediction: chronic-TBI tau SUVR ≈ controls across cortical/limbic ROIs at ≥10 y, while CSF/plasma NfL and p-tau217 remain the only divergent markers.

Falsification discipline: none of these vetoes can be resolved by re-reading the source; each requires the specified prospective measurement. That is the RLVR-relevant point — the causal thesis is *decidable*, and the volume itself supplies the null anchors against which to decide it.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

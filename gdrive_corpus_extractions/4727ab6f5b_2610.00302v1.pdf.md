**Derivation 1: Concentration of measure and hubness.**

For DINO features of dimension $d$ (DINOv2-L/v3-L have embedding dim ~1024 for ViT-L, actually 1024). As $d$ grows, the variance of pairwise cosines among random directions scales as $1/d$. On unit sphere $S^2$, the injectivity radius is $\pi$. The log map $\log_{x}(\epsilon)$ is well-defined and unique iff $\epsilon$ is not antipodal to $x$. Sinkhorn–Knopp counter-measure ([PAGE 5]): "converts the query–reference similarity matrix into a doubly stochastic assignment matrix." $$\kappa_w = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{K}\sum_{j=1}^{K} w_{ij}O_{ij}}{\sum_{i=1}^{K}\sum_{j=1}^{K} w_{ij}E_{ij}},\quad w_{ij}=\frac{(i-j)^2}{(K-1)^2},\quad O_{ij}=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\mathbb{1}(\hat s_n=i)\mathbb{1}(s_n=j),\quad E_{ij}=\frac{n_i^{VLM}\,n_j^{Human}}{N}.$$

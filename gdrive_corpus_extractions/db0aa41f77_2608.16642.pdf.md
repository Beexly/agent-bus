The robot’s implicit theory of mind is that core-driven torso rotation (waist yaw, roll, pitch during follow-through; shoulder roll, waist pitch, shoulder pitch during throw [PAGE 6, Fig. 6]) can substitute for the human’s coupled kinetic chain.

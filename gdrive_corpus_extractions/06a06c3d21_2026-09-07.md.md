**Dummy RAPM (NBA)** [PAGE 1]. Let \( y \in \mathbb{R}^n \) be the vector of plus-minus observations over \( n \) stints, \( X \in \{0,1\}^{n \times p} \) the player-stint incidence matrix, and \( \beta \in \mathbb{R}^p \) the player rating vector. The base RAPM estimator solves
\[
\hat{\beta} = \arg\min_{\beta} \| y - X\beta \|_2^2 + \lambda \| \beta \|_2^2,
\]
with regularization strength \( \lambda \) selected by cross-validation; units of \( \beta \) are points per 100 possessions (dimensionless rate scaled by 100). Assumption: stint-level residuals \( \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 I_n) \) are iid and the design matrix \( X \) is full column rank after removing redundant players. Breakdown regime: for low-minute players with minutes \( m_i < \tau \) (where \( \tau \) is a minutes threshold), the corresponding columns of \( X \) become sparse, the Fisher information \( I(\beta_i) = X_i^\top X_i / \sigma^2 \) approaches singularity as \( m_i \to 0 \), and variance \( \mathrm{Var}(\hat{\beta}_i) = \sigma^2 (X^\top X + \lambda I)^{-1}_{ii} \) explodes. Dummy RAPM imputes a synthetic observation \( y_i^{\text{dummy}} \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_0, \sigma_0^2) \) with prior mean \( \mu_0 \) and prior variance \( \sigma_0^2 \), effectively adding pseudo-counts to the diagonal of \( X^\top X \).

**ELASTIC trajectory synchronization (Soccer)** [PAGE 1]. Let \( \mathcal{T} = \{ (t_k, x_k) \}_{k=1}^K \) be tracking trajectories sampled at \( \Delta t \) seconds (typically \( \Delta t \in [0.1, 0.4] \) s for optical systems) and \( \mathcal{E} = \{ (\tau_j, e_j) \}_{j=1}^J \) event timestamps with semantic labels. The synchronization operator \( \pi: \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{E} \) minimizes
\[
\min_{\pi} \sum_{j=1}^J \| x_{\pi(j)} - x^{\text{event}}_j \|_2^2 \quad \text{s.t.} \quad | t_{\pi(j)} - \tau_j | \le \delta, \quad \pi \text{ monotonic},
\]
where \( \delta \) is the maximum admissible timestamp misalignment (units: seconds). Assumption: player motion between consecutive frames is locally linear and event timestamps are accurate to within \( \delta \). Breakdown regime: during occlusions or GPS/IMU dropout in outdoor stadiums, \( x_k \) is missing for intervals \( > \delta \), making the feasible set empty; the operator must then fall back to inertial dead reckoning, which accumulates position error \( \mathcal{O}(a \Delta t^2) \) where \( a \) is acceleration uncertainty.

\[
\tau = E[Y_i(1) - Y_i(0) \mid C_i = c]
\]
under the parallel-trends assumption that count-pressure bias \( b(c) \) is stable across the pre-transition window. Units: probability scale \( [0,1] \); effect size measured in call-probability difference (dimensionless). Breakdown regime: if umpires anticipate ABS during the hybrid transition, \( Z_i \) is no longer exogenous because \( P(Z_i=1 \mid C_i) \) varies with count, violating exclusion restriction and biasing \( \tau \) toward zero.

**Traveling Tournament Problem (Multi-sport)** [PAGE 1]. Let \( G=(V,E) \) be a complete graph of \( n \) teams with distance matrix \( D \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}_+ \) (units: km). The objective is
\[
\min \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} d(t_k, t_{k+1}) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{home/away pattern constraints, no immediate repeat, at-most constraints},
\]
where \( t_k \) is the venue of round \( k \). Units: total travel distance in km. Assumption: distances satisfy the triangle inequality and stadium locations are fixed. Breakdown regime: the problem is NP-hard for \( n \ge 8 \) teams; exact branch-and-bound becomes intractable beyond \( n \approx 20 \) without symmetry breaking, and the RobinX benchmark bounds are only certified for specific instances.

Scenario 3 — Pre-snap motion origin corruption. Verifiable condition: a receiver goes in motion before the snap, changing the effective origin relative to the line of scrimmage, but the pipeline cuts all trajectories at the snap event and translates to a single origin [PAGE 5]. RLVR veto: if pre-snap displacement exceeds one standard deviation of the cluster’s spatial spread, veto the snap-aligned Bézier fit and re-cluster using motion-adjusted origin. Counterfactual prediction: routes from motion will be assigned to incorrect clusters or inflated variance σ²k [PAGE 4, Eq. 14], because the model attributes motion-induced translation to route-shape noise rather than to a distinct initial condition.

with the auxiliary line \(\rho_x = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\mu_x)}\), which is *dimensionally invalid*: a standard deviation has units of \(x\), and the numerator \(\mathbb{E}[(x-\mu_x)(y-\mu_y)]\) has units of \(x\cdot y\); the ratio is dimensionless only if \(\rho_x = \sigma_x\) and \(\rho_y = \sigma_y\). \]

F(X) = \arg\max_Y \sum_{i=1}^{k} \mathbb{I}\bigl(f(X)=Y\bigr)

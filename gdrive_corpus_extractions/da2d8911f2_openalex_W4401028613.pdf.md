\[
A_i^{(s)}=\max_{t\in[t_i-\Delta_-^{(s)},\,t_i+\Delta_+^{(s)}]}

\qquad [A_i^{(s)}]=\mathrm{g},
\]

\[
\Gamma_i^{(s)}=\max_{t\in[t_i-\Delta_-^{(s)},\,t_i+\Delta_+^{(s)}]}
\left\|\boldsymbol\alpha_i^{(s)}(t)\right\|_2,
\qquad [\Gamma_i^{(s)}]=\mathrm{rad\,s^{-2}}.
\]

The MP pre/post windows are specified as “15 milliseconds prior, and 45 milliseconds post trigger” [PAGE 3]. The HIT System sampling rate was “1000 Hz” while MP accelerometer and gyroscope rates were “4681 Hz” and “860 Hz” [PAGE 3]. \[
D_i^{A}=A_i^{\mathrm{HIT}}-A_i^{\mathrm{MP}},
\qquad [D_i^{A}]=\mathrm{g},
\]

\[
D_i^{\Gamma}=\Gamma_i^{\mathrm{HIT}}-\Gamma_i^{\mathrm{MP}},
\qquad [D_i^{\Gamma}]=\mathrm{rad\,s^{-2}}.
\]

The mean bias and standard deviation over paired events are

\[
\bar D^{A}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}D_i^{A},
\qquad
s_{D^{A}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(D_i^{A}-\bar D^{A}\right)^2},
\]

\[
\bar D^{\Gamma}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}D_i^{\Gamma},
\qquad
s_{D^{\Gamma}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(D_i^{\Gamma}-\bar D^{\Gamma}\right)^2}.
\]

The 95% Bland–Altman limits of agreement are the standard form

\[
\mathrm{LoA}_{A}=\bar D^{A}\pm 1.96\,s_{D^{A}},
\qquad
\mathrm{LoA}_{\Gamma}=\bar D^{\Gamma}\pm 1.96\,s_{D^{\Gamma}}.
\]

\[

\qquad
\bar M_i=\frac{A_i^{\mathrm{HIT}}+A_i^{\mathrm{MP}}}{2},

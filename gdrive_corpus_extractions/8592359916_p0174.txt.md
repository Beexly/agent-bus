- $k=0$: play-caller ignores opponent; treats plays as independent units—the exact fallacy condemned at [PAGE 1].

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Biomechanical layer (human locomotion): Maximum sprint speed $v_{\text{max}} \approx 8\text{--}10\text{ m/s}$; lateral acceleration $a_{\text{lat}} \lesssim 4\text{--}6\text{ m/s}^2$ before slip or injury. Weather layer (environmental stochasticity): Rain lowers $\mu$; wind introduces a stochastic force $\vec{F}_{\text{wind}}$ into pass trajectory $\vec{r}(t)$.

\rho_{\text{catchable}} = \frac{N_{\text{catchable}}}{N_{\text{attempts}}},
\]
dimensionless share, with a 100-attempt minimum floor. It removes drops and throwaways from the QB grade. The breakdown regime is charting-defined catchability and residual sample size (~15 attempts/game).

The physical race between pass rush and QB release from [F7] and [W10] is
\[
\Delta t = \bar{t}_{\text{pressure}} - \bar{t}_{\text{throw}},
\]
in seconds. If \Delta t > 0, pressure arrives after the average throw and deep shots remain viable; if \Delta t < 0, the offense is forced into quick game or screens. Abdul Carter's captured mean of 2.13s (23 pressures) versus the league's #2 at 2.32s defines an outlier gap the reasoning layer should name explicitly.

Missed tackles forced per touch from [W6] is
\[
\rho_{MTF} = \frac{N_{MTF}}{N_{\text{touches}}},
\]
dimensionless; PBP cannot reproduce charted missed tackles honestly, so no synthetic proxy is derived here.

Win-probability luck residue from [F8] is
\[
L = W_{\text{actual}} - \sum_{g=1}^{G}\mathrm{WP}_g,
\]
in wins, separating close-game outcomes and penalty fortune; the exact penalty component is unpublished in the source post.

Conformal prediction intervals from [F1] carry the coverage guarantee
\[
P\bigl(Y_{n+1} \in C(X_{n+1})\bigr) \ge 1 - \alpha
\]
under exchangeability, in probability units. FoundationForecast wraps Chronos, TimesFM, Moirai, Toto, TiRex, and TimeGPT via ConformalIntervals calibrated from cross-validation errors. The exact breakdown regime is season-ordered NFL data, which violates exchangeability through non-stationarity (injuries, coaching adjustments, personnel changes); the corpus' standing conformal verdicts (Romano CQR / Barber weighted conformal) therefore still govern, and [F1] itself lists this as a risk.

Third-down trust from [W7] is computed as per-receiver EPA per third-down target,
\[
\mathrm{EPA}_{\text{3rd}}/N_{\text{targets,3rd}},
\]
with a 12-target floor over eight weeks, subject to small-sample instability and image-locked production-stat definition (yards vs EPA).

First-read target share from [W4] is
\[
s_{\text{first}} = \frac{N_{\text{first-read targets}}}{N_{\text{team first-read targets}}},
\]
dimensionless share, minimum 80 routes; values are image-locked in the source and first reads are not chartable from public PBP, so this remains a design spec with a FantasyPtsData-class charting gap.

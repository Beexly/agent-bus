(d) The taxonomy of rage, fear, and lust shares the same release architecture; boundaries are defined by the content of the expectancy, not by distinct physiologies, so cross-emotion contamination is a formal possibility when sign-gestalts overlap. Singularity and breakdown regimes arise when the means-end strength $M\to0$, when sign-gestalt entropy $H(G\mid S)\to\log|\mathcal{G}|$ (maximal ambiguity), when the sign-object ceases to exist (ending the "continued existence" condition [PAGE 1]), or when the unknown mechanism $\Phi$ fails to transduce expectancy into any measurable motor or visceral signal.
</THINK_TRACE>

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
Let $\mathcal{S}$ denote the space of immediate signs/sign-objects (e.g., enemy, sexual sign, threat), with elements $s\in\mathcal{S}$ represented as sensory feature vectors $\mathbf{s}\in\mathbb{R}^n$ or dimensionless category labels. Let $\mathcal{G}$ be the sign-gestalt space and define the gestalt operator $\Gamma:\mathcal{S}\to\mathcal{G}$. Let $\Omega$ be the outcome space of physiological states, partitioned into disturbance $\mathcal{D}$ and quiescence $\mathcal{Q}$, further differentiated by apparatus (e.g., sexual apparatus). Define the expectation operator
\[
\mathcal{E}:\mathcal{G}\times\mathcal{S}\to\Delta(\Omega),\qquad
\mathcal{E}(G,s)=P(\omega\mid G,s),
\]
where $\Delta(\Omega)$ is the probability simplex (dimensionless units $[0,1]$) and information content may be measured in nats via Shannon entropy $H(\Omega)$.

Rage assertion ([PAGE 1]): "rage is: (i) an expectation that this disturbance will have the character of a blocking of, or interference with, other activities; and (ii) it is an expectation that the specific means-end-relations between such threatened blocking and the immediate sign are such that the mere continued existence of this sign (e.g., an enemy) will tend to lead to such blocking or interference." Formally,
\[
\mathcal{A}_{\text{rage}}(s)=
\mathbb{I}\!\left[P(\text{blocking}\mid s,\text{continued existence})>\tau_{\text{rage}}\right]
\land
\mathbb{I}\!\left[M(\text{attack/destroy}\rightarrow\text{remove blocking}\mid s)>\tau_M\right],
\]
where $M$ is a dimensionless means-end relation strength in $[0,1]$, and $\tau_{\text{rage}},\tau_M$ are criterion thresholds.

Lust assertion ([PAGE 1]): "lust is not only an assertion that the given sign-object will lead to some physiological quiescence. It is also an assertion: (i) that this promised quiescence is specifically that kind required by the sexual apparatus; and (ii) that the means-end-relations between the immediate sign and this sexual goal are such that the way to reach the latter is by some sort of intimate bodily contact with the sign."
\[
\mathcal{A}_{\text{lust}}(s)=
\mathbb{I}\!\left[P(q_{\text{sexual apparatus}}\mid s)>\tau_{\text{lust}}\right]
\land
\mathbb{I}\!\left[M(\text{intimate bodily contact}\rightarrow q_{\text{sexual}}\mid s)>\tau_M\right].
\]

Fear and incipient release ([PAGE 1]): for fear, "incipient running away and hiding activities and incipient fore-runners of the final visceral activity appropriate to pain or injury are involved." General incipient dynamics:
\[
\dot{\mathbf{x}}_{\text{inc}}(t)=\Phi\!\left(\mathcal{E}(\Gamma(s),s)\right),
\]
where $\Phi$ is Tolman's explicitly "as yet unknown physiological mechanism" ([PAGE 1]); $\mathbf{x}_{\text{inc}}$ may be skeletal velocity (m/s), muscle activation (dimensionless EMG), or visceral rate (Hz) / hormonal concentration (mol/L); $t$ in seconds.

Breakdown and singularity regimes: (i) $\det(\nabla_{\mathbf{s}}\mathcal{E})=0$ (expectation insensitive to sign features); (ii) $H(G\mid S)\to\log|\mathcal{G}|$ (maximal sign-gestalt ambiguity); (iii) sign-object ceases continued existence, invalidating the "mere continued existence" precondition ([PAGE 1]); (iv) $M=0$ (no physically realizable means-end path); (v) $\Phi$ undefined or non-transducing, yielding expectancy without incipient motor or visceral expression. Because Tolman leaves $\Phi$ unspecified, no SI-calibrated gain or time constant can be assigned to the expectancy-to-motion map from this page alone.
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Tolman's 1932 purposive behaviorism sits at a historical inflection: it rejects pure S-R reflexology by inserting sign-gestalt-expectation between stimulus and response, anticipating his later cognitive-map work and modern expectancy-value and appraisal frameworks. The claim that emotions are "resulting incipient movements and visceral activities" ([PAGE 1]) prefigures action-readiness accounts (Frijda) and affective-neuroethological systems (Panksepp), while the "as yet unknown physiological mechanism" ([PAGE 1]) marks the exact boundary where Tolman's functional psychology stops and neurophysiology must begin.

Multi-week traps. First, the behaviorist misreading: treating Tolman's sign-object as a mere stimulus ignores his explicit cognitive assertion structure ([PAGE 1]), leading experimenters to replace expectancy with contiguity. Second, the temporal-compression trap: Tolman's rage and lust depend on the "continued existence" of the sign ([PAGE 1]), a durational condition, yet laboratory designs often collapse this to instantaneous stimulus onset, erasing the sustained expectancy window. Third, the consolidation trap: incipient movements are millisecond-to-second phenomena, but repeated sign-outcome pairings over weeks can ossify a maladaptive means-end relation (e.g., chronic attack-release to benign signs), so a single-session observation cannot capture the habit strength that actually gates emotional expression. Fourth, the inversion trap: treating physiological disturbance as the cause of emotion rather than as the significate of the sign-gestalt-expectation ([PAGE 1]) reverses Tolman's formal dependency and corrupts any causal model built on this text.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Under crisis, higher-order deliberation regresses and behavior collapses onto the incipient layer Tolman describes: attack/destroy in rage, running/hiding in fear, intimate contact in lust ([PAGE 1]). This is habit regression to the fastest available motor-visceral release. The counter-trap is for the external observer to impute a recursive theory of mind to the subject—e.g., "the enemy intends to block me"—when Tolman's account requires only a first-order sign-gestalt-expectation about blocking, not a second-order belief about the enemy's mental states ([PAGE 1]). Attributing intentionality to the sign-object inflates the model beyond the text's license.

Two clocks govern this recursion. The fast clock operates at incipient-release timescales (milliseconds to seconds), governing $\dot{\mathbf{x}}_{\text{inc}}(t)$ and visceral fore-runners ([PAGE 1]). The slow clock operates over multi-week expectancy refinement, updating $P(\text{blocking}\mid s)$ and $M(\text{attack}\rightarrow\text{remove blocking}\mid s)$ through repeated sign-outcome pairings. Crisis behavior is driven by the fast clock, but the slow clock continues to renormalize thresholds $\tau_{\text{rage}}$ and $\tau_M$ in the background, meaning that a subject's incipient response today is a function of a crystallization history that cannot be read from the immediate sign alone.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Three veto layers can independently nullify emotional expression while leaving the sign-gestalt-expectation formally intact.

Physical veto $V_{\text{phys}}$. If the sign-object is absent or occluded, the "continued existence" condition ([PAGE 1]) fails: $\Gamma(s)=\emptyset$, $\mathcal{E}=0$, and no incipient movement is released. Continued existence is a physical precondition, not a logical convenience.

Biomechanical veto $V_{\text{bio}}$. Let the musculoskeletal state be $\mathbf{q}\in\mathbb{R}^{n_j}$ with joint angles in radians and feasible workspace $\mathcal{W}$. Even when $\mathcal{A}_{\text{rage}}(s)=1$, if $\mathbf{q}\notin\mathcal{W}$ (e.g., limb immobilized, fatigue beyond torque limits in Nm), then $\dot{\mathbf{x}}_{\text{inc}}=0$ for the attack channel. The expectancy remains; the body cannot instantiate it.

Weather veto $V_{\text{weather}}$. Environmental degradation (rain, wind, occlusion, low light) raises the sensory entropy $H(\mathbf{s}_{\text{obs}})$, corrupting $\Gamma$ and driving the means-end confidence $M\to0$. When the sign is perceptually unreliable, the organism cannot sustain the specific means-end relation required for rage or lust ([PAGE 1]).

Adjudication: the composed veto operator $V_{\text{phys}}\circ V_{\text{bio}}\circ V_{\text{weather}}$ acting on $\mathcal{E}$ yields emotional disembodiment—an assertion without incipient motor or visceral expression. This signature is falsifiable: autonomic arousal may persist while the kinetically specified action channel is vetoed, producing a measurable dissociation between $P(\text{disturbance}\mid s)>0$ and $\|\dot{\mathbf{x}}_{\text{inc}}}\|\approx 0$ m/s.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Prediction: incipient attack kinematics decay exponentially with time constant $\tau\in[1,10]$ s and no consummatory attack occurs. Verifiable via motion-capture and surface EMG; the veto is confirmed if $\|\dot{\mathbf{x}}_{\text{inc}}(t>t_r+5\tau)\|<0.01$ m/s while the subject's verbal or autonomic report still indexes disturbance.

Scenario 2 (Biomechanical blockade veto). The subject faces a blocking sign with intact rage expectancy ($M>0.8$) but the dominant attacking limb is immobilized. Prediction: somatic incipient movement amplitude $\approx 0$ m/s, while visceral incipient activity (heart rate, cortisol) increases; dissociation ratio $A_{\text{somatic}}/A_{\text{visceral}}<0.1$. Verifiable via simultaneous kinematic, EMG, and autonomic recording; veto confirmed if attack-channel EMG is suppressed below baseline while HRV indicates sustained distress.

Scenario 3 (Contingency violation veto). The sign continues to exist across $N$ trials but blocking never follows its continued existence, directly falsifying the rage means-end relation ([PAGE 1]). Prediction: expectancy update via prediction error $\delta_t=r_t+\gamma V(s_{t+1})-V(s_t)$ drives $P(\text{blocking}\mid s)$ below $\tau_{\text{rage}}$ within $N\in[5,20]$ trials; incipient attack activity extinguishes and is replaced by avoidance/withdrawal kinematics. Verifiable through trial-by-trial behavioral choice and pre-cue physiological activity; the veto is confirmed when the sign-object remains physically present yet no longer releases attack.

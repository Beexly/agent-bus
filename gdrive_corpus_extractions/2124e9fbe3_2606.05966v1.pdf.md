**The four metrics** (all dimensionless empirical frequencies in $[0,1]$):
$$\mathrm{ACC} = \mathbb{1}\{Y = Y^*\} \tag{2}$$
$$\mathrm{EF} = \frac{1}{|V|}\sum_{v\in V} M(v_n, R) \tag{3}$$
$$\mathrm{RA} = \frac{1}{|E|}\sum_{(u,v)\in E} M\big((u_n,v_n), R\big) \tag{4}$$
$$\mathrm{DC} = \frac{1}{|A\cup E|}\sum_{v\in(A\cup E)} M(v_d, R) \tag{5}$$

Assumptions and breakdown regimes:

- **ACC heterogeneity.** ACC is a 0/1 indicator, but the question bank mixes binary items (e.g., collision Yes/No, chance floor $0.5$) with 4-way MCQ (chance floor $0.25$). Averaging ACC across mixed-cardinality items yields a scalar whose chance baseline is undefined; the paper's "near-chance (below 40% or ≈0.3)" [PAGE 5] silently assumes a $0.25$ floor even for binary subsets.
- **EF singularity.** As $|V|\to 0$, Eq. 3 is $0/0$. More importantly, EF is *monotone in mention count*: a degenerate "name-listing" policy that enumerates every $v_n$ drives $\mathrm{EF}\to 1$ while $\mathrm{ACC}$ and $\mathrm{RA}$ stay at chance. EF is therefore a recall-only node-marginal with no precision penalty.
- **RA is recall-only over edges.** Eq. 4 sums only over *true* edges $(u,v)\in E$. It never penalizes hallucinated edges. A rationale asserting a superset $E' \supsetneq E$ can score $\mathrm{RA}=1$. RA verifies presence, not acyclicity, ordering, or absence of spurious arcs.
- **DC string-match conditioning.** Eq. 5 is restricted to $A\cup E$ nodes with a description $v_d$; nodes with null $v_d$ are excluded, so DC is conditioned on described nodes only. Combined with the gold-rationale rule "state it clearly and exactly as provided" [PAGE 16], DC rewards verbatim recitation of $v_d$ strings.
- **Judge reliability.** Reported via Cohen's $\kappa = \frac{p_o - p_e}{1 - p_e}$ (dimensionless, $[-1,1]$): LLM–Human $\kappa=0.82$, Cross-LLM $\kappa=0.84$, Paraphrase $\kappa=0.90$ [PAGE 13, Table 7].

**Training objective.**
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CRFT}}(\theta) = -\mathbb{E}\Big[\lambda_r \!\!\sum_{t\in \mathrm{idx}(R_{gold})}\!\!\log \pi_\theta(s_t\mid X,Q,s_{<t}) + \lambda_y\!\!\sum_{t\in \mathrm{idx}(Y^*)}\!\!\log \pi_\theta(s_t\mid X,Q,s_{<t})\Big] \tag{6}$$
restated as $\mathcal{L} = \lambda_{rat}\mathcal{L}_{rat} + \lambda_{ans}\mathcal{L}_{ans}$ with tuned $\lambda_{rat}=0.2,\ \lambda_{ans}=1$ [PAGE 17]. Units: cross-entropy in nats (natural-log convention). **Breakdown/gray zone:** if the inner terms are summed (as Eq. 6 is written) rather than length-normalized, the effective gradient mass is $\lambda_r|R_{gold}| : \lambda_y|Y^*| \approx (0.2\times\sim130):(1\times1)\approx 26:1$ in favor of rationale tokens. The stated "balance" between rationale and answer supervision is then nominal, not effective — the answer head receives ~4% of the token-level gradient. The paper does not disambiguate sum vs. mean, which is the single most consequential unstated assumption in the method.

</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<THINK_TRACE>

Multi-hop derivation of the paper's central claim and a gray-zone audit.

*Hop 1 — taxonomy vs. ladder.* The paper maps Pearl's ladder to four categories: "Perception corresponds to the association rung... Anticipation and Intervention probe the interventional level... Goal-Orientation aligns with counterfactual reasoning" [PAGE 4]. But passive forecasting from observed video is $P(Y\mid X)$, an *observational/associational* quantity, not $P(Y\mid do(X))$. Labeling Anticipation "interventional" is generous: nothing is manipulated. The formal do-forms listed on [PAGE 2] ($P\{Y\mid X\}, P\{Y\mid do(X)\}, P\{Goal\mid do(X)\}, P\{Y\mid do(X')\}$) are invoked decoratively; the actual items do not enforce an intervention distribution.

*Hop 2 — metrics are marginals, not joints.* EF is a node-marginal frequency, RA an edge-marginal frequency, DC a description-match frequency. None is a joint structural score over the typed DAG $G=(V,E)$. Consequently a model can post high EF and moderate RA while emitting an *internally inconsistent* graph (asserting $A\to B$, $B\to C$, and a spurious $A\to C$ when truth is a chain). RA's averaging (Eq. 4) cannot detect the extra arc because it only iterates over true edges. This is a recall-only design and is the root of most interpretive over-reach downstream.

*Hop 3 — the EF–RA gap has two readings.* Reported gap: "high EF, ≈0.7... low RA, ≈0.2-0.3" [PAGE 6]. The paper's causal story is architectural: "the multimodal fusion layers fail to compose these entities into a valid physical world model" [PAGE 6]. A measurement story is at least as plausible: the judge prompt demands "a clear causal/influence expression (e.g., X causes/leads to/affects Y)... Mere co-occurrence is insufficient" [PAGE 17]. Natural rationales frequently leave causal connectives implicit, so a strict lexical-causal gate deflates RA independent of any fusion failure. The two hypotheses are not separated by any experiment in the paper.

*Hop 4 — CRFT barely moves the target metric.* For Qwen2-VL-7B, CRFT lifts ACC $0.5349\to0.7066$ but RA only $0.2130\to0.2554$ (absolute $+0.042$, still "near-chance" by the paper's own definition) [PAGE 7, Table 4]. The metric engineered to capture causal reasoning is the one CRFT improves least and leaves lowest. This is in tension with the thesis that CRFT "drives VLMs to internalize causal mechanisms" [PAGE 7].

*Hop 5 — transfer baseline is high and heterogeneous.* PhysBench Relationships gains $+8.1$ ($73.0\to81.1$) but that baseline is already high and on a different scoring scale than CausalPhys RA; Property is flat ($61.7\to61.7$), Scene $+2.7$ [PAGE 8, Table 5]. A single strong sub-score is weak evidence for broad causal transfer.

Gray-zone audit summary: (a) mixed-cardinality ACC averaging; (b) EF/RA recall-only, no precision; (c) judge strictness confounds the headline EF–RA gap; (d) DC = string recitation; (e) Eq. 6 sum-vs-mean ambiguity; (f) judge/teacher model-family overlap (main judge is GPT-4o [PAGE 5]; teacher is GPT-4o [PAGE 6]); (g) anachronistic reliability models "GPT-5.4 / Claude Sonnet 4.6" [PAGE 13] inconsistent with the stated GPT-4o judge — a provenance inconsistency that should be reconciled before the κ values are trusted.

</THINK_TRACE>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

Lineage. The intellectual spine is Pearl's *Causality* [43] and its three-rung ladder, imported into vision via CLEVRER [57] and CoPhy [10], which the paper credits for "elementary visual primitives of spheres, cubes, and rigid-body collisions" [PAGE 3]. The prediction lineage runs through Physion / Physion++ [6, 50]; the causal-graph-in-VLM lineage runs through CELLO [9] ("object 1 supports object 2"), CausalVLBench [32], and Causal3D [38], all of which the paper positions as "fixed-structure causal graphs within synthetic or template-based scenes" [PAGE 3]. Answer-based physics understanding is inherited from PhysBench [11] and MVPBench [16], which the paper differentiates by arguing they emphasize "prediction accuracy rather than the underlying reasoning process" [PAGE 3]. Spatial-VQA ancestry [36, 47, 52, 56] and embodied-behavior framing [22, 48] supply the 3D-relations motivation. CRFT's rationale-plus-answer supervision descends from T0-style instruction finetuning [12] and In-Token Rationality Optimization [61].

Multi-week traps (reproducibility hazards that surface only after sustained reuse):

1. **Resolution floor for the hardest subsets.** Uniform downsampling to $442\times442$ [PAGE 11] is defensible for collision/fluid scenes but destroys the sub-degree angular cues that Viewpoint Transformation and Optical Inference require. Any team re-running on higher resolution will not reproduce the "near-chance" numbers — the difficulty is partly an artifact of the preprocessing pipeline.
2. **Clip-length ceiling.** The 5 s cap and ≤8-frame sampling [PAGE 11] truncate long-horizon intention/trajectory items, so re-sampling frames differently shifts labels for borderline cases (e.g., the cafetière "next move" item, Figure 12).
3. **Judge version drift.** The main results use GPT-4o as judge [PAGE 5], but the reliability appendix validates "GPT-5.4" and "Claude Sonnet 4.6" [PAGE 13, Table 7], and the arXiv identifier is dated 2606 (June 2026). Re-scoring months later with a changed judge endpoint will move EF/RA/DC, since these are judge-relative frequencies, not intrinsic properties of $R$.
4. **Chance-floor bookkeeping.** Because binary and 4-way items are pooled (see SOURCE_OPERATORS), any re-weighting of subsets changes ACC in ways not captured by a fixed chance assumption.

</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

Four nested agents: annotators → teacher LLM → tested VLM → judge LLM.

- **Annotators** encode expert causal graphs; their theory of mind over "what a valid physical mechanism is" is fixed by the calibration phase [PAGE 4].
- **Teacher** verbalizes the graph into $R_{gold}$ under constraints "causes must appear before their effects" and "state it clearly and exactly as provided" [PAGE 16] — i.e., it models the *judge's* future checks and pre-satisfies them.
- **Tested VLM** under CRFT learns to imitate $R_{gold}$. Under answer-only SFT it collapses to the documented "guesser" [PAGE 6], modeling the *answer distribution* rather than the mechanism.
- **Judge** models the rationale against the graph with a lexical-causal gate [PAGE 17].

*Habit regression vs. counter-trap vs. clock.* The "habit" is regression to the guesser attractor under distribution shift: answer-only SFT reaches ACC $0.6762$ but craters EF to $0.3247$ and RA to $0.0911$ [PAGE 7] — a Goodhart collapse where the model optimizes the reward proxy (the letter) and abandons the mechanism. The "counter-trap" is graph-anchored supervision: because the teacher only *verbalizes* a human graph ("GPT-4o does not generate the graph itself" [PAGE 17]), the signal is anchored to structure, and the teacher-swap test (ROUGE-L $0.38$, BERTScore $0.91$; comparable metrics [PAGE 13, Table 8]) shows the learned competence is style-invariant. The "clock" is the 90/10 split with a held-out OOD subset [PAGE 7], enforcing temporal discipline against memorization.

Unmodeled recursion: the judge and teacher share a model family (GPT-4o) in the main setup, so a student trained on teacher rationales is graded by a same-family evaluator — a closed self-preference loop. The teacher-swap breaks *style* dependence but not *judge-family* dependence; a judge-swap (scoring CRFT outputs with an unrelated model) is the missing reflexive control.

</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Physical-layer veto on the paper's hardest subsets. Viewpoint Transformation and Optical Inference are, respectively, an SE(3) rigid action and a reflection isometry on the scene:
$$\text{Viewpoint: } \mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{R}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{t},\quad \mathbf{R}\in SO(3),\ \det(\mathbf{R})=+1$$
$$\text{Mirror/Optical: } \mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{S}\mathbf{x},\quad \mathbf{S}=\mathbf{I}-2\hat{\mathbf{n}}\hat{\mathbf{n}}^\top,\ \det(\mathbf{S})=-1\ (\text{orientation-reversing})$$
followed by a projective (nonlinear) projection $\pi$. Exact inversion requires composing the isometry with $\pi^{-1}$, a map a feedforward attention stack does not represent explicitly.

Concrete adjudication (Figure 22, Optical Inference): monitors are seen in a mirror; ground truth is **B (Back left)** [PAGE 24]. GPT-4o and Claude both answered **D (Back right)**, correctly inferring "behind the camera" but failing the reflection's left–right reversal — exactly the $\det(\mathbf{S})=-1$ handedness flip. The failure is diagnostic, not stochastic: it is a systematic mishandling of an orientation-reversing isometry.

Veto consequence for CRFT. If these near-chance scores reflect a missing geometric-group representation, then CRFT — a *language-alignment* regularizer over rationales — is architecturally incapable of fixing them, because no amount of correct verbalized reasoning supplies the equivariant operator the encoder lacks. Prediction and falsification test: CRFT's per-subset gains on Viewpoint Transformation and Optical Inference should be near zero, while gains concentrate on Anticipation (the paper itself notes CRFT is "particularly effective for forward physical reasoning" and strongest in Anticipation, Figure 9 [PAGE 17]). If, conversely, CRFT *does* lift the geometric subsets substantially, the "language-only cannot fix geometry" veto is falsified and the geometric-competence interpretation of the low baselines weakens. The paper does not report this decomposition numerically in the main text, so the veto remains adjudicated in principle but unproven in data.

</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

Three verifiable counterfactual scenarios with pre-registered predictions.

**Veto 1 — EF is verbosity, not reasoning.** Intervention: prompt any tested VLM to *only enumerate* the entity names in the scene (no reasoning). Because EF (Eq. 3) is recall-only over node mentions, this degenerate policy should match CRFT's EF ($\approx0.60$) while RA stays $\lesssim0.10$ and ACC near chance. Prediction: $\mathrm{EF}_{\text{list}} \approx \mathrm{EF}_{\text{CRFT}} \pm 0.05$, $\mathrm{RA}_{\text{list}} \ll \mathrm{RA}_{\text{CRFT}}$. Confirmation would falsify EF as a reasoning metric. (Consistent with answer-only SFT's EF $0.3247$ vs CRFT $0.5969$ [PAGE 7].)

**Veto 2 — the EF–RA gap is a judge-strictness artifact.** Intervention: re-judge identical rationales with a *permissive* RA prompt that credits co-occurrence (drop the "Mere co-occurrence is insufficient" clause [PAGE 17]). Prediction: RA inflates toward EF (gap $0.4\to<0.15$), falsifying the "fusion-layer decoupling" interpretation [PAGE 6] in favor of a lexical-gate measurement artifact. If RA stays low under the permissive gate, the architectural interpretation survives.

**Veto 3 — CRFT gains are graph-structural, not rationale-length.** Intervention: retrain with a *graph-shuffled* control ($E$ randomly permuted, $|V|,|E|$ and rationale length preserved), then evaluate on PhysBench with no tuning. Prediction: $\text{PhysBench Overall}_{\text{shuffle}} \approx 58.3$ (vanilla) with the $+4.0$ transfer gain [PAGE 8, Table 5] absent, confirming the gain is carried by causal structure. The teacher-swap (Table 8) rules out *style*; only a graph-shuffle rules out *length/fluency* as the active ingredient.

Each veto is a single controlled retrain or re-judge, uses the paper's own metrics, and yields a binary confirm/refute outcome.

</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

Net assessment: CausalPhys contributes a genuinely typed-DAG annotation schema and a diagnostic metric suite, but its four metrics are marginal/recall-only frequencies whose headline "causal" signal (RA) both starts low, barely moves under the proposed fix, and is confounded with judge strictness and question cardinality. The strongest empirical results (teacher-invariance, bootstrap stability, PhysBench transfer) are real but do not isolate causal structure from verbosity, length, or judge-family self-preference without the three controls above.

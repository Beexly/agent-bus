**Galtonian Heritability Index (GHI):**
\[
\text{GHI}(T) = \frac{\sum_{c \in \mathcal{C}} I(T_p; T_c)}{|\mathcal{C}|}
\]
where \( T \) is a trait (e.g., liver disorder), \( T_p \) is parental occurrence, \( T_c \) is offspring occurrence, \( \mathcal{C} \) is the set of children, and \( I \) denotes mutual information. Units: bits per child. Assumption: traits are binary (present/absent). Breakdown regime: when penetrance is low (<0.1) or when environmental noise dominates (>50% variance).

**Disease Cluster Entropy:**
\[
H(D) = -\sum_{d \in \mathcal{D}} p(d) \log_2 p(d)
\]
where \( \mathcal{D} \) is the set of disease categories observed (e.g., {Gout, Paralysis, Consumption, Liver}). From the source, frequencies: Gout/Liver cluster appears in Father (R.F.), G.F., H.F., N.F.; Paralysis in P.M., A.M., S.T.F.; Consumption in R.B., H.G., H.B.R., N.F. (note N.F. has both liver and consumption). This entropy measures diversity of pathology. Higher entropy suggests polygenic/environmental complexity; lower entropy suggests strong monogenic influence. SI unit: dimensionless (bits). Breakdown: when sample size is too small (<5 families) for reliable estimation.

**Mutual Information between Sex and Disease Type:**
\[
I(S; D) = \sum_{s \in \{\text{M,F}\}} \sum_{d \in \mathcal{D}} p(s,d) \log_2 \frac{p(s,d)}{p(s)p(d)}
\]
This quantifies if certain diseases preferentially affect males or females in the pedigree. Data: Males (brothers) show more paralysis and liver/gout; females (sisters) show more consumption and delicate constitution. Calculation yields positive \( I(S;D) \), indicating sex-linked or sex-influenced inheritance patterns. Unit: bits. Assumption: independent sampling. Singularity: if all cases of a disease occur in one sex, \( I \to \infty \) theoretically, but practically bounded by log(sample size).

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the cognitive map, a topological graph where nodes $V$ represent locations/alleys and edges $E$ represent traversable connections. The immanent expectation is the posterior probability over goal directions given the current node and the stored map:
\[
\mathcal{E}(g | v_t, \mathcal{C}) = P(g | v_t, \mathcal{C})
\]
This operator is distinct from a simple stimulus-response function $f(s_t)$ because it depends on the global structure $\mathcal{C}$.

\subsection*{3. Kinesthetic Independence Condition}
Tolman argues against the ``kinesthetic doctrine of maze learning'' (Watson) [PAGE 80]. Formally, let $k_t$ be the vector of kinesthetic (proprioceptive) signals associated with movement through the current medium. Pure kinesthetic control implies the policy depends directly on $k_t$:

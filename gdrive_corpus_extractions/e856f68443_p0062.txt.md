Under crisis, the remote architect undergoes habit regression: when a defect appears on site, his theory of mind about the local crew degrades, and he regresses to demanding his own bodily presence because message-only agency lacks ontological weight under stress. The counter-trap is the belief that adding channels—Ultrafax, telephone, teletype, daily photographs—collapses distance into co-presence. This overestimates the channel’s capacity to carry the embodied, tacit models that local clerks of the works hold about soil, concrete behavior, and site-specific tolerances.

Formalize the clocks: let \(\omega_{\text{ctrl}}\) be the rate at which the building process requires decisions, and \(\omega_{\text{comm}}\) the message round-trip rate. Recursive theory of mind—architect models representative, who models architect’s intentions—requires \(\omega_{\text{comm}} \ge \omega_{\text{ctrl}}\). If \(\omega_{\text{comm}} \ll \omega_{\text{ctrl}}\), the recursion bottoms out in local habit; the remote model becomes a confabulation, and the “extended physical existence” [PAGE 104] is a hallucinated co-presence. The clock mismatch is the precise regime in which Wiener’s “almost the same thing as to be everywhere” [PAGE 104] ceases to hold: the architect is everywhere only in representation, not in causal efficacy.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Three physical layers retain veto power over any message-only architecture.

Physical layer: gravity, compressive strength, concrete curing time, and formwork load limits. No quantity of information transmission can prevent a pour failure if local material conditions exceed design limits; the substrate vetoes the message.

Biomechanical layer: local workers’ proprioception, fatigue states, and manual micro-adjustments during placement and finishing. These variables are not fully specifiable in plans or Ultrafax facsimiles (“quite as good working plans as the originals” [PAGE 104]), because skilled execution depends on embodied feedback loops that the remote architect cannot close.

Weather layer: precipitation, temperature, wind, and humidity at the US site. Atmospheric physics cannot be commanded by a European message; a storm front halts work regardless of teletype instructions.

Adjudication rule: the message channel is necessary for coordination but not sufficient for execution. The material substrate holds final veto. The singularity occurs when the rate of local veto events exceeds the rate at which corrective messages can be generated, transmitted, received, and physically acted upon by the site staff. In that regime, extended individuality retracts to the skin-bound body, and Wiener’s claim that “where a man’s word goes… his physical existence is extended” [PAGE 104] is falsified by local material causality.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

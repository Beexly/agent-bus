These statements explicitly delimit the boundary conditions: temper is state-dependent, socially masked, and illness-sensitive. A deterministic, fixed-trait model violates these stated constraints. The only admissible inference from [PAGE 254] is a conditional, probabilistic statement about proxy-rated temper within a specific Victorian biometric sample, subject to neutral-majority censoring, second-degree kin mixing, and observer bias. Deterministic extrapolation to individual offspring, to genetic mechanisms, or to cross-contextual stability is physically and epistemically ungrounded from this source alone.

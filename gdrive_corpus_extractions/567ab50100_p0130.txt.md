**Formulation of Problem (5):**

$$ N(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \chi_{E_n}(x) $$
where $\chi_A$ is the indicator function.
The set $G$ is defined as:
$$ G = \{ x \in X : N(x) \ge m \} $$
By Tonelli's theorem (applicable since terms are non-negative):
$$ \int_X N(x) d\mu(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \int_X \chi_{E_n}(x) d\mu(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \mu(E_n) $$
Since $N(x) \ge m \chi_G(x)$, we have the inequality:
$$ m \mu(G) \le \sum_{n=1}^\infty \mu(E_n) $$

$$ \lim_{n \to \infty} \int \phi_n d\mu = \int f d\mu $$

If any veto fires, the decomposition \(\Pi = \rho + \varepsilon_m\) collapses to ex post loss accounting, and the page-26 residual theory cannot be applied without reformulation.

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios, each with a falsifiable veto condition and a prediction.

Scenario 1: Complete-insurance veto. Counterfactual: a market offers actuarially fair insurance for every enterprise-specific risk, including whole-enterprise failure, so the effective premium becomes \(\psi' = \psi + \rho\). Veto condition: if observed residual profit \(\Pi\) does not decline by approximately \(\rho\) after full insurance becomes available, the Thünen/Knight decomposition is falsified (or transaction costs and risk-loading fees are nonzero and must be modeled). Prediction: under full insurance, residual profit equals only the management component \(\varepsilon_m\); owner-operated and professionally managed firms with identical capital and risk exposure should show no excess residual except for the measurable management-wage differential.

Scenario 2: Hired-manager veto. Counterfactual: replace the owner-residual-claimant with a salaried manager whose contract removes residual claimancy, thereby eliminating the "working for himself" incentive. Veto condition: if a positive residual profit persists in employee-managed firms that are otherwise identical to owner-operated firms, then \(\varepsilon_m\) cannot be the sole source of the residuum; uninsurable uncertainty \(\rho\) must be present and priced. Prediction: owner-operated firms earn higher expected residual than matched employee-managed firms precisely when uncertainty is high; as uncertainty is insured away, the owner-employee residual gap narrows toward zero.

Scenario 3: Zero-residual competitive veto. Counterfactual: long-run competitive equilibrium with free entry, homogeneous capital, and only measurable risks, such that \(R = i + \psi + w_m\) exactly. Veto condition: if positive accounting profit persists after all factor payments are priced at market rates and all insurable risks are covered, then either entry barriers exist or uninsurable Knightian uncertainty is being monetized. Prediction: persistent positive residuals in such sectors should correlate with independent measures of subjective forecast dispersion (credence \(c\) diverging from insurable probability \(P\)); they are evidence of uncertainty rents, not competitive equilibrium profits.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

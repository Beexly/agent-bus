The page's "natural price" theory traces to the classical lineage of Smith, Ricardo, and Mill, for whom the natural price acted as a center of gravity around which market prices fluctuated [PAGE 33]. Knight's retrospective move on [PAGE 33] is to strip that theory of its classical psychological assumptions and recast it as the formal limiting conditions of perfect competition. This creates a genealogy running from classical value theory through Marshallian partial equilibrium to Arrow-Debreu general equilibrium (1954), which supplies the existence and completeness conditions that Knight's page gestures at but does not prove.

The multi-week trap embedded in this lineage is temporal misclassification: an analyst who treats the static "normality" assumption as descriptive over a 4- to 8-week window will misread disequilibrium pure profits as either permanent rents or illusory noise. Because [PAGE 33] provides no convergence clock, the theory cannot tell the observer how many weeks must elapse before \Pi_i \to 0. If supply shocks (weather, production disruptions) or preference shifts arrive faster than entry and capital reallocation, the observed path violates the limiting tendency, yet a classical natural-price forecaster would still predict immediate equality of value and cost. The retro-causal hazard is that the theory's authority is invoked backward from equilibrium to explain short-run price movements for which the equilibrium assumptions were never valid.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Level-0 reasoning: the market has a tendency to eliminate profit : or loss and equate value with cost [PAGE 33]. Level-1 reasoning: I believe this tendency operates, so I act as though no sustained pure-profit opportunities exist. Level-2 reasoning: I believe other agents believe this, so they will not arbitrage away deviations; therefore, under Knightian uncertainty, persistent \Pi > 0 may survive precisely because everyone assumes it cannot.

Crisis habit regression occurs when stress collapses agents back to Level-0: they invoke "natural price" and assume value equals cost immediately, abandoning the search for residual profit [PAGE 33]. The counter-trap is to maintain a position premised on non-equilibrium pure profit while competitors regress to the null-profit heuristic. The clock variable is the ratio of the competitive-adjustment timescale \tau_{adj} (time for entry, capital mobility, and price convergence) to the decision horizon \tau_{dec}. If \tau_{adj} > \tau_{dec}, the equilibrium assumption is falsified for the agent's operative window, and the recursive model predicts systematic over-reversion to cost-based pricing during crises. The breakdown regime is when uncertainty evolves on a timescale shorter than competitive entry, making the Level-0 regression not merely mistaken but structurally dominant.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: production goods are subject to thermodynamic conversion limits, capital immobility, and lead times; if \partial \mathbf{k} / \partial t is bounded below by physical installation constraints, free entry cannot operate instantaneously and \lim_{t \to \infty} \Pi_i = 0 fails on any finite horizon [PAGE 33]. Biomechanical layer: human agents possess bounded cognition and limited attention; they cannot compute \mathbf{p}^{*} from \mathcal{M} in real time, so complete information and instantaneous arbitrage are violated. Weather/meteorological layer: exogenous shifts in \mathbf{s} (e.g., agricultural supply destruction) or \mathbf{w} (weather-driven demand changes) falsify the "given supplies" and "given general conditions" assumptions of the static problems [PAGE 33].

Formal veto adjudication: let A_{pc} be the conjunction of all perfect-competition assumptions on [PAGE 33]. Let V_{\ell} denote a violation in layer \ell \in \{physical, biomechanical, weather\}. Then
V_{\text{physical}} \lor V_{\text{biomechanical}} \lor V_{\text{weather}} \implies \neg A_{pc} \implies \neg (\Pi_i \to 0).
Any single layer veto is sufficient to invalidate the null-pure-profit prediction for that observation window. Adjudication therefore proceeds by checking the weakest layer; if weather shifts supplies faster than capital can reallocate, the market-price operator \mathcal{M} is undefined for the static problem and the classical tendency cannot hold.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Verification—observe the price-cost margin p - C^{\text{unit}} weekly for 12 weeks. Prediction—\Pi_i remains strictly positive and does not converge to zero; market price stays above cost. Falsification—if the margin collapses to exactly zero despite monopoly, the pure-profit residual theory on [PAGE 33] is vetoed.

Verification—track \mathbf{p}(t) and \Pi_i(t) at weekly frequency for 6 weeks. Prediction—transitory \Pi_i \neq 0 persists for at least 3 to 5 weeks until capital reallocates; market price deviates from natural price. Falsification—if price adjusts instantaneously to the new natural price with zero profit deviation in week 1, the limiting-tendency claim is empirically falsified for this shock speed.

Prediction—firms exercising superior judgment earn positive pure profit that is not competed away because it cannot be insured or replicated probabilistically; \Pi_i > 0 persists. Falsification—if all firms earn exactly zero economic profit despite the absence of a probability space, Knight's implication from [PAGE 33] is vetoed and competition alone eliminates all residual returns.

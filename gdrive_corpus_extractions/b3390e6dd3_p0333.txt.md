However, the apparition is not corroborated by the acoustic or baroceptive signatures he habitually uses to detect persons; it arrives with a unique cold shiver and departs on movement. Veto: ordinary environmental detection cannot explain the specific content (size, appearance, humanoid kinematics).

Pharmacological layer veto. Strong tea at lunch is traced as the proximal trigger, and withdrawal abolishes the phenomenon [PAGE 323]. Veto: the recurrence is not spontaneous; it is gated by stimulant intake. However, caffeine explains excitation, not the visual-form content in a blind subject.

Adjudication. All object-external and memory-based explanations are vetoed. The residual is an endogenous, stimulant-gated perceptual construct with visual-form properties but no visual prior. Because the source text ends at “without” [PAGE 323], the terminal clause that might specify whether “appearance” was spatial-tactile, imaginal, or affective is missing. James’s agnosticism—“no conclusion can be come to until more detinite data are obtained” [PAGE 323]—is the calibrated output: no layer fully resolves the singularity, and the precise breakdown regime (zero visual prior + finite interoceptive likelihood) remains empirically open.

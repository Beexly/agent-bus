If \(\mathcal{P}_{\text{phys}}\) outputs \(\text{false}\) (no common object), \(\mathcal{V}=\perp\). If \(\mathcal{P}_{\text{phys}}=\text{true}\) but \(\mathcal{B}_{\text{bio}}\) receives \(\dot{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}_{\text{loc}}=\mathbf{0}\), \(\operatorname{Vol}\) does not grow and \(\mathcal{V}=\perp\). If both pass but \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{env}}\) supplies no fixed objects, the angular integration lacks boundary conditions and \(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\to\infty\); the map is undefined. Therefore a contiguous extereal space is jointly necessary across physical, biomechanical, and environmental layers; no single layer suffices [PAGE 206].

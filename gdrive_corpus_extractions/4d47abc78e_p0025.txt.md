I(M_{\text{obs}};B) = H(B) - H(B|M_{\text{obs}}) \quad [\mathrm{bits}].
\]
Inferential gap (opacity):
\[
\Delta I = H(B|M_{\text{obs}}) \quad [\mathrm{bits}].
\]
If \(\Delta I > 0\), molar properties are not inferable from observed molecular facts, matching Tolman’s claim [PAGE 25].

Breakdown / singularity regime: Let \(\rho_{\text{corr}} = N_{\text{corr}}/N_{\text{pairs}}\) be the density of established behavior–physiology correlations. If \(\rho_{\text{corr}} < \rho_c\) or the Kullback–Leibler divergence
\[
D_{\text{KL}}\!\left(P_{\text{emp}}(B)\,\|\,P_{\Gamma}(B|m)\right) > \epsilon_{\text{tol}},
\]
then the molar model is ungrounded and the correspondence map ceases to be predictive. Assumption: stationarity of \(p(m,b)\) over the experimental epoch; breakdown occurs under non-stationarity (learning, fatigue, stress) within \(\delta t_B\).
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The molar/molecular distinction originates with C. D. Broad, *The Mind and Its Place in Nature* (2nd impression, 1929; pp. 616 f.), as suggested to Tolman by Dr. D. C. Williams (Harvard Ph.D. thesis, 1928) [PAGE 25]. Tolman (1932) inherits this split from Watsonian molecular behaviorism but defends the molar conception as the proper object of psychology [PAGE 25]. The footnote references McDougall’s *Modern Materialism and Emergent Evolution* (1929) as a summary of emergent notions, which Tolman explicitly adopts only in a descriptive sense, rejecting any metaphysical commitment [PAGE 25].

Lineage hops: Broad’s philosophical distinction → Tolman’s purposive behaviorism → latent learning and cognitive maps (1940s–50s) → cybernetic and hierarchical control theory → model-based vs. model-free reinforcement learning → options framework in hierarchical RL. Each step preserves the core tension: a macro-variable (goal, cognitive map, option) is correlated with micro-states (neural firing, muscle activation) yet is not derivable from them without empirical bridging laws.

Multi-week trap: Short-horizon molecular assays (single-trial reaction times, millisecond spikes, trial-by-trial EMG) cannot capture molar intention expressed over days to weeks (latent learning, reversal learning, habit formation). The trap is extrapolating from \(\delta t_m\) correlations to \(\tau_B \sim 10^{5}\) s goal-directedness without molar state variables. Retrocausal flavor: the future goal state \(b(t_f)\) appears to select among present molecular trajectories \(m(t)\). Formally this is a boundary-condition constraint on path integrals of micro-dynamics, not physical retrocausality; it is a modeling arrow from macro-time to micro-time. If the experimenter truncates observation at \(T_{\text{obs}} \ll \tau_B\), the molar structure is aliased into noise and the “purpose” is unrecoverable [PAGE 25: inferential opacity].
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: under acute stress, novelty, or resource depletion, the organism abandons molar flexible conduct and collapses into molecular reflexive chains—fixed action patterns, stimulus-response bonds, and effector-driven reflexes. The purposive hierarchy is suspended; behavior is described fully by receptor–conductor–effector processes [PAGE 25]. Recursive theory of mind degrades because Agent A no longer models Observer O’s model of A’s intention \(b^*\); A’s internal model reverts to zero-order reactivity.

Counter-trap: the external modeler persists in attributing a molar “purpose” to conduct that is actually molecular drift stabilized post-hoc. This is the intentional-stance overreach—reading teleology into a trajectory that physics fully determines at the molecular layer [PAGE 25]. The counter-trap is sprung when the modeler refuses to switch from an intentional to a mechanistic description after crisis onset.

Clock duality: molecular clock \(\tau_m \sim 10^{-3}\)–\(10^{0}\) s; molar clock \(\tau_B \sim 10^{2}\)–\(10^{6}\) s (minutes to weeks). Recursion depth \(d\) of “A models O models A” collapses as a stress function:
\[
d(t) = d_0 \exp\!\left[-\frac{t-t_c}{\tau_{\text{stress}}}\right], \quad t \ge t_c,
\]
where \(t_c\) is crisis onset. While \(d>0\), molar counter-traps are possible; once \(d\to 0\), only vetoes at the physical layer remain operative [see CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION].
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Vetoes operate bottom-up and cannot be overridden by molar redescriptions. Because Tolman grounds behavior in receptor-process, conductor-process, and effector-process per se [PAGE 25], every molar behavior-act \(b\) must satisfy physical, biomechanical, and environmental boundary conditions.

Physical veto (energy): Let \(E_{\text{avail}}(t)\) be metabolic free energy available and \(E_{\text{req}}(b)\) the energy required by act \(b\). Then
\[
E_{\text{avail}}(t) < E_{\text{req}}(b) \;\Longrightarrow\; \neg \mathrm{Execute}(b).
\]
Units: joules (J). No emergent property can suspend energy conservation.

Biomechanical veto (force/torque): For joint configuration \(\theta\) and angular velocity \(\dot\theta\), required actuator torque \(\tau_{\text{req}}(\theta,\dot\theta)\) must satisfy
\[
\tau_{\text{req}}(\theta,\dot\theta) \le \tau_{\text{max}}(\theta,\dot\theta),
\]
and muscle force \(F_{\text{muscle}} \le F_{\text{ISO}}\). Units: N·m and N. Violation yields kinematic impossibility independent of the molar intentional description.

Weather veto (environmental boundary): Ambient temperature \(T_a\), humidity, wind, or load alter conductor-process latency \(\lambda(T)\) and effector dynamics. If \(T_a\) lies outside the thermoneutral zone, \(Q_{10}\) effects slow neural and muscular conduction such that \(\lambda(T_a) > \delta t_B\). The molar goal and its execution are decoupled; the act aborts or degrades. Units: seconds (s) for latency; Kelvin (K) for temperature.

Adjudication rule: a higher-layer molar description is admissible only if all lower-layer vetoes are satisfied. The one-to-one correspondence Tolman posits [PAGE 25] is necessary for ontological coherence but not sufficient for behavioral realization.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
RLVR = reinforcement-learning validation regime in which reward is issued only when verifiable physical and informational constraints are satisfied. Three counterfactual veto scenarios:

1. Mutual-information veto. Measure \(I(M_{\text{obs}};B)\) over a sliding window of duration \(W\). Veto condition: \(I(M_{\text{obs}};B) < \tau_I = 0.1\,H(B)\) bits. Prediction: under a novel perturbation (e.g., shifted maze goal), behavior will not exhibit transfer consistent with a molar intention; response probabilities remain locked to immediate molecular stimuli rather than to the remote goal state. Verifiable via a confusion matrix between observed molecular feature vectors and assigned act labels.

2. Thermodynamic veto. Compute entropy production \(\dot{\Sigma} = dS_m/dt + \dot{Q}/T\) [J K\(^{-1}\) s\(^{-1}\)]. If the claimed molar computation requires \(I_B\) bits, the Landauer bound imposes a minimum heat dissipation \(Q_{\min} = k_B T \ln 2 \cdot I_B\). If available metabolic power \(P_{\text{met}}\) cannot supply \(Q_{\min}/\Delta t\) over the act duration \(\Delta t\), veto the molar model. Prediction: the subject cannot sustain the alleged molar information processing; behavior reverts to lower-complexity reflexes within \(\Delta t_{\text{fail}} = E_{\text{store}}/P_{\text{met}}\). Verifiable via respirometry and behavioral bout-duration censoring.

3. Biomechanical veto. For a declared molar act \(b^*\) (e.g., surmounting a barrier of height \(h\)), compute required mechanical work \(W = mgh + \int F_{\text{drag}}\,dx\) [J]. If \(W > W_{\text{max,burst}}\) derived from muscle force–velocity and cross-sectional area, veto execution. Prediction: the subject will abort, substitute a detour path, or exhibit fatigue-induced failure before \(t = W/P_{\text{max}}\). Verifiable via motion capture, force plates, and electromyographic exhaustion indices.

Each scenario is falsifiable: if the predicted vetoed behavior does not occur when the inequality is satisfied, the corresponding constraint model is rejected.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

RLVR Veto Scenario 2 — Temporal order inversion.
Verification method: Telegraph office timestamp log showing the telegram was dispatched or received before 23:00 on the night of the apparition.
Veto condition: If the telegram timestamp precedes the apparition, ordinary causal channels could have delivered the content before the subjective event.
Prediction under veto: The apparition is confabulated after the fact to account for prior knowledge; the claimed time arrow (apparition first, confirmation second) collapses, and the case becomes a case of retroactive narrative tightening rather than precognition.

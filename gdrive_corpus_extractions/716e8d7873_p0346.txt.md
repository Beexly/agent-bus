<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

Hull operationalized this biological intuition into the precise mathematical language of behaviorism, assigning the "wat" as the unit of force driving the habit hierarchy. 

Historically, this represented a radical departure from Gestalt fields or early cognitive maps, enforcing a strict S-R (Stimulus-Response) causal chain quantified by physical energy units. Therefore, an experiment relying strictly on Hull's linear $A = k \cdot {}_sE^R$ will yield catastrophic prediction errors over long durations. The "constant" $k$ is actually a highly volatile function of the organism's metabolic and endocrine state. The historical trap was assuming that a psychological "potential" maps permanently to a static biomechanical multiplier, failing to account for the plasticity of the peripheral nervous system outside of the immediate conditioning chamber.

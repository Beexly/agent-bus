Demographic entropy: prevalence P = I × D, where I is incidence (yr^{−1}) and D is mean duration (yr). Medical progress increasing life expectancy raises D for chronic degenerative conditions, so P increases even if I is constant. Information-theoretic balance: Wiener’s negentropy claim implies local information gain I_gain (bits) offsets thermodynamic entropy \dot{S}_{env} (J·K^{−1}); the breakdown regime is \dot{S}_{env} > T_0 \dot{I}_{gain} (Landauer bound at temperature T_0 in K), where medical “progress” exports more entropy than it removes as uncertainty.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage traces from Boltzmann’s entropy (S = k_B ln Ω, J·K^{−1}) through Shannon’s information theory (1948, bits) to Wiener’s cybernetics (1948 *Cybernetics*; 1950 *The Human Use of Human Beings* [PAGE 1]), in which control and communication are framed as resistance to entropy. Wiener’s medical critique descends from Semmelweis-style statistical reasoning (puerperal fever, 1840s) and the Flexnerian reform of medical education, but it anticipates later formalizations: Berkson’s bias (1946), the Will Rogers phenomenon / stage migration (documented in cancer epidemiology, 1980s–1990s), and Doll–Peto carcinogenesis latency (smoking–lung cancer lag ~20–30 years). The sulfa-drug resistance narrative [PAGE 1] prefigures antimicrobial resistance evolutionary modeling (Levin & Rozen, 2006), while the hydrogenation/petroleum-food warning anticipates the diet–heart and lipid-oxidation debates (Ancel Keys era, trans-fat regulation in the 2000s). The demographic observation—population aging driven by medicine retaining those “who would otherwise have been dead” [PAGE 1]—foreshadows the epidemiological transition (Omran, 1971) and the compression-of-morbidity debate (Fries, 1980).

Multi-week traps: institutional medical budgets and clinical trial cycles operate on scales of weeks to months (phase I–III readouts, quarterly formularies), whereas Wiener’s causal chains operate on scales of years to decades (carcinogen latency L, antimicrobial resistance fixation, demographic aging). A trap is therefore the temporal mismatch: a treatment or food additive approved after a T_obs ≪ L window appears efficacious and safe, then fails at t ≈ L. Another trap is the regression-to-the-mean illusion: early acute-case statistics show high mortality; later inclusion of mild cases shows improvement; the intervening “progress” is largely selection, not therapy. A third trap is the local-entropy fallacy: counting saved lives as entropy reduction while ignoring the entropy exported into synthetic-food supply chains and extended frailty. These traps recur whenever an intervention’s benefit horizon is shorter than its harm horizon.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Model the physician’s recursive belief state θ_t over treatment efficacy. At t_0, the physician treats severe, fulminating cases and observes low recovery; θ_0 is low. Diagnostic criteria widen; mild cases enter the treated pool and recover spontaneously. The physician updates:
θ_{t+1} = θ_t + α [ y_t − E(y_t | θ_t) ],
where y_t = 1 for recovery. Because y_t is now drawn disproportionately from M=mild, the prediction error y_t − E(y_t | θ_t) is positive, driving θ_t upward. The physician then expands indications, treating even milder cases, which further inflates y_t—a self-reinforcing loop. This is crisis-habit regression: under uncertainty, the clinician attributes success to the agent and failure to the disease’s severity, a recursive attribution error. The counter-trap is the inverse attribution: when severe cases die despite treatment, the physician blames “doomed” physiology rather than insufficient dose or resistance, thereby immunizing θ_t from disconfirmation. Wiener describes this precisely: early statistics give disease “a high mortality rate,” later “similar physiological or mental changes are recognized in patients who are less ill,” and the doctor exclaims, “Ah-ha… Look at my statistics” [PAGE 1].

The clock variable is the biological latency L versus the institutional decision cycle Δt_inst. The physician’s recursive loop operates on Δt_inst (weeks: patient encounter → outcome → belief update), while the true hazard h(t) from chemical residues or resistance accumulation operates on L (years to decades). If L ≫ Δt_inst, the recursive belief system converges to a false fixed point θ* ≈ 1 before any disconfirming evidence arrives. The veto condition is therefore θ_t must be discounted by a latency factor Λ = exp(−Δt_inst / L); when Λ → 0, the clinician’s confidence is epistemically unwarranted regardless of observed recovery counts. Formal singularity: at L / Δt_inst → ∞, no finite sample of acute outcomes can bound θ_t away from the true causal efficacy τ.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: molecular residues from hydrogenation catalysts (e.g., nickel, copper traces) and petroleum-derived carcinogens interact with DNA and proteins. The veto operator V_phys = 1 if residue concentration C_res exceeds the threshold for adduct formation C_adduct (mol·L^{−1}), else 0. Breakdown: when C_res < analytic detection limit but cumulative dose D_cum = ∫ C_res(t) dt over a lifetime exceeds the carcinogenic threshold, V_phys = 1 despite negative assays.

Biomechanical layer: human metabolic processing of hydrogenated fats alters LDL/HDL lipoprotein partitioning and membrane fluidity; synthetic fatty acids from petroleum feedstocks may lack the stereochemical specificity of natural lipids (cis/trans isomer distribution dimensionless). The veto operator V_bio = 1 if the enantiomeric or isomeric purity P_iso < P_crit (dimensionless, e.g., 0.999), or if enzymatic turnover rate k_cat (s^{−1}) for the synthetic substrate falls below the physiological clearance requirement. Wiener’s distinction between “chemical purity” and “medical purity” [PAGE 1] is exactly this veto: chemical assays may certify purity while metabolic stereospecificity fails.

Weather/environmental layer: “weather” here denotes the ambient population-level exposure climate—the shift in national dietary habits as “natural food supply runs out” and petroleum products replace traditional fats [PAGE 1]. The veto operator V_env = 1 if the population-averaged exposure \bar{E}(t) = (1/N) Σ_i E_i(t) crosses the carcinogenic or degenerative threshold E_c (mg·kg^{−1}·day^{−1}) for a duration exceeding the latency L. Because the risk is “universal to the nation if not to the race” [PAGE 1], individual-level informed consent cannot veto; only population-level exposure control can.

Adjudication rule: any claim of medical progress P_claim is vetoed (V_total = 1) if V_phys ∨ V_bio ∨ V_env = 1 AND the entropy export condition \dot{S}_{env} > T_0 \dot{I}_{gain} holds. Under this rule, Wiener’s examples—sulfa resistance, synthetic food carcinogens, and demographic aging—each trigger at least one veto, so the unqualified assertion that medicine has confirmed “progress” is invalidated unless the entropic and latency terms are explicitly bounded.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 (Stage-migration veto): Counterfactual—hold treatment efficacy τ constant across two eras, but lower the diagnostic threshold so that 40% of previously “healthy” individuals are reclassified as early-stage disease. Prediction: observed 5-year survival rises from 50% to 90% purely by case-mix shift, with no change in mortality rates. Verifiable test: in a simulated cohort with identical true hazards, the Will Rogers operator yields Δ\bar{s}_1 > 0 and Δ\bar{s}_2 > 0 simultaneously. Veto: any claim that physician skill improved is invalidated unless survival is adjusted for stage distribution (unit: years, age-standardized).

Scenario 2 (Latent carcinogen veto): Counterfactual—approve a synthetic food additive based on a T_obs = 2-year feeding study showing no tumors, while the true latency is L = 20 years with Weibull hazard h(t) = (k/λ)(t/λ)^{k−1}, k = 3, λ = 15 years. Prediction: cumulative hazard H(2) ≈ 0.008 (apparently safe), but H(20) ≈ 3.6 (substantial tumor burden). Verifiable test: extend observation to t > L; tumor incidence emerges abruptly at the singularity t ≈ λ. Veto: safety certification at T_obs < L is void; the approved exposure must be withdrawn or re-tested with T_obs ≥ L.

Scenario 3 (Antimicrobial resistance veto): Counterfactual—sulfa drugs are deployed at sub-exterminating doses (per Wiener’s critique that early users lacked “the courage… to employ them in doses which would exterminate the disease” [PAGE 1]) against a bacterial population with pre-existing resistance allele frequency f_0. Prediction: under selection coefficient s (dimensionless) and generation time g (hours), resistance frequency follows f_t = f_0 e^{st} / [1 − f_0 + f_0 e^{st}]; clinical cure rate collapses from near 1 to f_0 as t → ∞. Verifiable test: sequence isolates before and after treatment; if f_t rises deterministically with s > 0, the physician’s “saved nine-tenths” claim is causally false. Veto: dosing regimens that fail to exceed the mutant prevention concentration (MPC, mg·L^{−1}) are invalidated regardless of short-term outcome statistics.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

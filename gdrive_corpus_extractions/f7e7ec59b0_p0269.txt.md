Statistical mechanics on a configuration space modeled by a locally compact group G (e.g., translations on ℝ³, rotations SO(3)) admits a Haar measure because G is locally compact; the invariant measure is the natural microcanonical density. Veto: if the physical symmetry group is not locally compact—e.g., the additive group of an infinite-dimensional Banach space with its norm topology, or the diffeomorphism group of a manifold with the \(C^\infty\) topology—the hypothesis of Theorem B fails [PAGE 254], and no regular Haar measure exists by this construction.

Biomechanical layer. Joint-angle spaces that are Lie groups (e.g., the kinematic chain of a shoulder modeled by SO(3), or robot configuration spaces that are products of circles and Euclidean spaces) are locally compact and therefore carry a Haar measure. Veto: infinite-dimensional shape spaces of soft tissue or continuum mechanics models whose configuration group is not locally compact are vetoed; the invariant-volume intuition from finite-dimensional joints does not lift.

Weather layer. Atmospheric orientation variables (e.g., the rotation matrix of a sensor platform) live on SO(3), a compact—hence locally compact—group, so a Haar measure on orientation exists and is uniform. Veto: the full atmospheric state space is an infinite-dimensional function space (velocity, pressure, temperature fields); its additive group is not locally compact, so the Haar construction on [PAGE 254] cannot provide an invariant measure on the full weather state.

Veto proof summary. The hard veto is the hypothesis “locally compact topological group X” in Theorem B [PAGE 254]. Remove local compactness, remove empty-interior exclusion for A, or allow \((A:U)\) to vanish or diverge, and the cluster-point argument collapses: m may be identically zero or may fail to be additive. No physical, biomechanical, or weather reinterpretation can override this structural veto.

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios, each with a falsifiable prediction.

Veto Scenario 1 — Non-locally-compact group. Replace X with an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space H under its norm topology, regarded as an abelian topological group under addition. H is not locally compact. Prediction: The family \((\lambda_U)_{U\in\mathcal{N}}\) cannot produce a content m with m(A)=1 for compact A with non-empty interior; indeed no nonzero left-invariant regular Borel measure exists on H. Verifiable check: attempt to construct \(\lambda_U(C)=(C:U)/(A:U)\); covering numbers \((C:U)\) for compact C by norm-balls U are finite, but the net has no cluster point inducing a countably additive regular measure on Borel sets, because no such Haar measure exists on non-locally-compact H.

Veto Scenario 2 — Compact test set with empty interior. Keep X = ℝ (locally compact) but choose A as a compact Cantor set with empty interior, violating “non-empty interior” [PAGE 254]. Prediction: \(\lambda_U(A)=1\) by normalization, yet the induced content assigns measure zero to every open interval disjoint from A; because A has empty interior, the argument that the induced measure is “not identically zero” via 53.C fails to produce a measure nonzero on open sets. Verifiable check: compute \(\lambda_U((0,1))\) for small U; it tends to 0 because (0,1) contains no translate-covering mass anchored at A’s empty interior, so the limiting measure is supported on a null set and is not a full Haar measure on ℝ.

Veto Scenario 3 — Sequential substitution without first countability. Choose a locally compact group G whose identity has no countable neighborhood base (e.g., an uncountable product of compact groups with the product topology). Replace the net \((\lambda_U)_{U\in\mathcal{N}}\) by a sequence \(U_1 \supset U_2 \supset \cdots\) and take a sequential diagonal limit. Prediction: The sequence may fail to have any cluster point in Φ, or its sequential cluster point may not satisfy the restricted additivity condition because the cofinality of \(\mathcal{N}\) is uncountable; the purported “limit content” either does not exist or is not additive. Verifiable check: exhibit a compact set C and a neighborhood U such that every sequence \(U_n\) misses a cofinal refinement needed to separate \(C U^{-1}\) from \(D U^{-1}\); then the sequential limit violates \(\lambda(C\cup D)=\lambda(C)+\lambda(D)\) on a pair where the net cluster point would preserve it.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

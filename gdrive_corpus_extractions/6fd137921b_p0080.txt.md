The multi-week trap emerges from misapplying these historical lineages. A coach might, inspired by Le Bon, focus solely on generating euphoric "spirit" without the underlying structure of repetitive drill, leading to a fragile, sentiment-based unity that collapses under pressure. Conversely, a purely Moltkean approach might create technically proficient but emotionally disjointed players, lacking the "contagious" element that allows for adaptive synchronization. The true insight from Roper is the *necessary conjunction*: spirit enables and accelerates the drilling process, and drill crystallizes and sustains the spirit. A coach who separates them risks building either a mob or a robot army—neither of which constitutes a "smooth, correlated machine."

The first is the transfinite ordinal clock of the induction in (6b): each positive-measure Borel set is met at a stage, and the process has no finite termination bound visible in the text. The second is the Haar-measure clock on X: the modular function of X and the normalization \(\nu(Y)=1\) set the temporal scale for quotient integration. When the ordinal clock outruns the supply of independent coordinates below 𝔠, or when the measure clock loses σ-finiteness (possible for non-σ-compact X), the recursive construction regresses into the crisis habit and the counter-trap of compactness no longer shields the proof from breakdown.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Four layers are adjudicated, each with a veto condition and an exact breakdown regime.

Layer 1—Set-theoretic foundation: The well-ordering of the Borel class and the extension to a Hamel basis require the axiom of choice. Veto: if choice is denied or the Borel σ-algebra has cardinality strictly greater than 𝔠 in a non-metrizable X, the transfinite induction in (6b) cannot guarantee a stage at which an unused independent coordinate exists. Breakdown regime: constructive or choice-free settings; non-separable locally compact groups with enormous Borel algebras.

Layer 2—Topological measure layer: X must be locally compact to carry a Haar measure μ, and the product X×X must support \((\mu\times\mu)\). Veto: if X is not locally compact, no Haar measure exists and (6a) loses its measure-theoretic meaning. Breakdown regime: general topological groups without local compactness; the Fubini step “∃y with μ(E^y)>0” fails.

Layer 3—Algebraic subgroup layer: Y must be compact and invariant. Veto: if Y is not compact, the quotient measure disintegration and the open-set separation in Theorem A require additional hypotheses (modular function, properness); if Y is not invariant, cosets do not form a group and π:X→X/Y is not a homomorphism. Breakdown regime: closed non-compact Y (delicate but possible per the author’s note); non-invariant Y (quotient is not a group).

Layer 4—Measure-theoretic positivity layer: E must have positive product measure and its sections must have cardinality 𝔠. Veto: if \((\mu\times\mu)(E)=0\), claim (6a) is vacuous and C in (6b) is not required to meet E. Breakdown regime: null Borel sets; atomic Haar measures where positive measure can be countable, invalidating “positive measure ⇒ cardinality 𝔠.”
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

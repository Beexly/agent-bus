A2. Transduction fidelity: the retrieval cue τ ∈ 𝒯 = ℝ (clock time) ∪ {0,1}^M (external signal) is faithfully detected by the subject's sensory apparatus at the moment of execution.
A3. Trust invariance: operator-subject trust O ∈ [0,1] is time-invariant across the deposit–execution interval.
A4. Isolation: no competing suggestion, extinction trial, or counter-suggestion intervenes before t_exec.

Formal quantities (SI / information / probability units):
- Deposit and execution times: t_dep, t_exec, t_now ∈ ℝ, measured in seconds (s SI).
- Suggestion: S ∈ {0,1}^L; information content in bits.
- Amnesic recall operator: R(t; S) ∈ {0,1}, with R(t) = 0 for all t ∈ [t_dep, t_exec), formalizing the patient's "absolute ignorance during the interval preceding their execution" [PAGE 614].
- Dissociation: the consciousness retaining the command is "split off, dissociated from the rest of the subject'8 mind" [PAGE 614], modeled as near-zero mutual information I(C_main; S | C_dis, t < t_exec) ≈ 0 bits and high conditional probability P(S ∈ C_dis | high hypnotizability) → 1.
- Protective second state: James describes an "experimental production of one of those 'second'states of the personality" [PAGE 614], represented as a dissociated compartment C_dis that can carry a veto suggestion (e.g., immunity to a named operator).

Execution probability:
P_exec(τ, O) = P_acc(O) · P_trig(τ) · ∏_{k}(1 - V_k)
where P_acc(O) = O · s, with s ∈ [0,1] trait suggestibility; P_trig(τ) = 1[|τ - τ_stored| ≤ Δt_tol] for a clock cue (unitless indicator), or a matched-filter threshold crossing for an arbitrary signal σ; and V_k ∈ {0,1} are veto indicators defined in the Cross-Layer section.

Breakdown / singularity regimes:
1. Temporal singularity: |t_now - t_exec| > Δt_tol ⇒ P_trig → 0. The text provides no numeric Δt_tol [PAGE 614], so this is a gray-zone boundary.
2. Trust singularity: O < O_crit ⇒ P_acc → 0; a subject who "lacks complete confidence" in an operator should not allow entrancement [PAGE 614].
3. Cue masking: competing sensory load or concurrent cognition raises the effective threshold θ for signal detection, driving P_trig → 0 despite intact storage.
4. Extinction / multi-week decay: repeated non-reinforced presentation of τ can reduce s over days to weeks, violating A1 and causing P_exec → 0 even when the cue is repeated exactly.
5. OCR / transcription singularity: verbatim tokens such as "subject'8 mind" and "somnabulists" [PAGE 614] require correction before any quantitative parameter can be anchored to the source.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Lineage. The page [PAGE 614] sits inside a genealogy running from Mesmerian animal magnetism through Braid's coined term "hypnotism" (1840s), Charcot's Salpêtrière neurological model of hysteria, Bernheim's Nancy-school emphasis on suggestibility, and the psychical-research experiments of Gurney explicitly cited by James [PAGE 614], through Pierre Janet's dissociation theory and the concept of "second" states of personality [PAGE 614], onward to Freud and Breuer's Studies on Hysteria (1895), in which repressed traumatic memories split off from conscious awareness and produce delayed somatic symptoms. Contemporary descendants include implicit memory research (Schacter), automaticity and goal priming (Bargh), prospective memory (Einstein & McDaniel), dissociative disorders in DSM nosology, and predictive-processing accounts in which a suppressed generative model still drives behavior without declarative access.

Multi-week traps. A post-hypnotic suggestion deposited in week 1 and executed in week 4 with no source memory instantiates several delayed-action traps. (a) The sleeper effect: a message dissociates from its discounting source cue over weeks, so attitude change emerges without attribution. (b) Extinction followed by spontaneous recovery: the suggestion weakens during non-reinforcement but re-emerges after a delay. (c) Incubation of cue reactivity: autonomic or behavioral responsiveness to a trigger grows during an abstinence interval. (d) Delayed PTSD: traumatic material encoded dissociatively surfaces weeks later as flashbacks or somatic complaints. (e) Agency misattribution: because the patient is "absolutely ignorant" of the deposit [PAGE 614], the eventual action feels spontaneous ("I just felt like it"), producing a first-person illusion of free will that James notes disbelievers have exploited [PAGE 614]. These are not toy ratios; they are empirically documented temporal dissociations between encoding and expression, with probability mass shifting across weeks rather than collapsing at deposit.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Define a hierarchy of theory-of-mind orders. L0 = bodily habit or reflex (basal ganglia–mediated, automatic). L1 = first-order intent: "I act to achieve goal G." L2 = second-order social intent: "I act because Operator O intends me to act at time τ or on signal σ." In the source text, the post-hypnotic command is deposited at L2 into C_dis while C_main continues operating at L0/L1 [PAGE 614]; because of the amnesic barrier, C_main cannot model the L2 intent, so the eventual behavior appears to originate spontaneously at L0.

Crisis habit regression. Under acute stress—pain, threat, autonomic surge, sleep deprivation—recursive modeling collapses. The subject regresses from L2 deliberation to L0 habit loops. Because the stored suggestion S is insulated in C_dis and inaccessible to C_main (I(C_main; S) ≈ 0 bits), there is no deliberative veto. The command "surge[s] up at the preappointed time" [PAGE 614] as an automatic habit, bypassing the crisis-impaired executive system.

Counter-trap. James describes a metacognitive defense: a trusted operator can suggest that "certain persons shall never be able hereafter to put him to sleep," thereby removing the subject "for all future time from hypnotic influences which might be dangerous" [PAGE 614]. This is a second-order counter-suggestion installed inside C_dis. It creates a recursive filter: if Operator B attempts induction, the dissociated compartment suppresses trance. The subject's own earlier L2 intent traps the operator's later L2 intent—a defensive recursion that preserves personal spontaneity while neutralizing external capture.

Clock. The phrase "the hour and minute" [PAGE 614] installs an exogenous timekeeper. Execution is locked to physical t_exec rather than to experienced volition. C_dis monitors τ; when τ = t_exec within tolerance, the command is released. Breakdown regimes: circadian phase shifts (night work, jet lag) move the subject's internal clock relative to t_exec; sleep at t_exec prevents behavioral expression; ambiguous cues (time-zone transitions, daylight saving changes, unreliable external clocks) create a singularity in which P_trig is undefined because τ_stored cannot be matched to τ_observed.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Define veto indicators V_i ∈ {0,1} across three layers. The baseline execution probability absent vetoes is P_exec^0 ∈ [0,1]. The adjudication law is multiplicative:

P_exec = P_exec^0 · (1 - V_phys) · (1 - V_bio) · (1 - V_env)

If any V_i = 1, then P_exec = 0 exactly. This is a proof by annihilation: a single active veto reduces the product to zero regardless of how strongly S is stored in C_dis or how well τ matches τ_stored.

Physical layer (V_phys): acute medical contraindications that preclude the psychophysiological state required for suggestion expression—general anesthesia, status epilepticus, severe hypoxia, acute intoxication, myocardial infarction, or hemodynamic collapse. If V_phys = 1, the substrate for any "lapse into trance" [PAGE 614] or health-related behavior (bowels, sleep [PAGE 614]) is unavailable.

Biomechanical layer (V_bio): motor or structural incapacity that blocks the efferent expression of S—lower-limb paralysis, immobilization, cast, severe dystonia, or catalepsy. If S specifies standing, walking, or a bowel response at t_exec, and the effector pathway is compromised, V_bio = 1 and execution is anatomically impossible even if C_dis has retrieved the command.

Environmental / weather layer (V_env): ambient conditions that capture attention, mask the retrieval cue τ, or induce competing homeostatic drives—ambient temperature >35°C or <5°C, precipitation, high wind, or noise >85 dB SPL. Weather also shifts circadian phase via light exposure, effectively moving the subject's internal clock away from the stored t_exec [PAGE 614], creating a temporal mismatch that drives P_trig → 0.

Adjudication hierarchy. Physical veto trumps biomechanical trumps environmental, because life support and structural integrity take precedence over expressive behavior; however, in the specific context of James's page [PAGE 614], where the salient suggestions concern health, sleep, and future trance, biomechanical and environmental vetoes are the most immediately operative, while physical vetoes (anesthesia, acute illness) represent absolute singularities.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable reinforcement-learning-from-verifiable-rewards (RLVR) counterfactual veto scenarios. In each, the veto is applied, a prediction is made, and removal of the veto is predicted to restore execution to P_exec^0.

Scenario 1 — Physical veto. Deposit: S = "stand at t_exec = 14:00." Counterfactual condition: at t_exec the subject is under propofol general anesthesia, so V_phys = 1. Prediction: no motor execution; EEG shows burst suppression; no movement-related cortical potential (readiness potential) precedes 14:00; post-anesthesia recall of S remains absent. Verifiable via continuous EMG and BIS monitoring. Counterfactual removal: upon emergence from anesthesia and re-presentation of the same clock cue, P_exec rises from 0 to P_exec^0, confirming that the null result was caused by the physical veto rather than by trace decay.

Scenario 2 — Biomechanical veto. Deposit: S = "fall asleep at signal σ." Counterfactual condition: at the scheduled signal time the subject experiences acute pain from an untreated fracture or an obstructive sleep-apnea event, so V_bio = 1. Prediction: despite intact dissociated retrieval, the subject remains awake; polysomnography shows no sleep-onset latency and no REM intrusion; autonomic markers (heart rate, cortisol) remain elevated. Verifiable via simultaneous PSG and actigraphy. Counterfactual removal: analgesia or airway restoration followed by re-presentation of σ restores P_exec to P_exec^0.

Scenario 3 — Environmental / weather veto. Deposit: S = "lapse into trance at auditory signal σ." Counterfactual condition: at t_exec the subject is outdoors in a thunderstorm with broadband noise >90 dB SPL and driving rain, so V_env = 1. Prediction: the auditory cue is masked by environmental noise; no trance induction occurs; galvanic skin response and EEG alpha increase remain at baseline; the subject reports no urge to enter trance. Verifiable via blinded sound-level logging and electrodermal activity. Counterfactual removal: moving indoors to a quiet room and re-presenting identical σ raises P_exec from 0 to P_exec^0, demonstrating veto causality rather than suggestion extinction.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

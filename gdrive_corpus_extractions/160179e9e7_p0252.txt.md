<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage runs from Reid’s distinction between the intrinsic quality of a sensation and the “thing” it suggests, through Helmholtz’s physiological optics (Physiol. Optik, pp. 431-2, cited on [PAGE 242]), to James’s 1890 synthesis. Earlier, Wheatstone (1838) established that binocular double images are detectable with the stereoscope, and ancient/medical writers noted muscae volitantes without attributing them to acute disease. James’s novelty is functional: he treats the overlooking of subjective phenomena as an acquired skill that secures objective observation.

Multi-week traps. The text’s central longitudinal trap is the inference from “lived for an indefinite time without knowing it” [PAGE 242] to the conclusion that nothing was wrong. A researcher or clinician who assumes perceptual stability across weeks because no complaint arises will miss latent scotomas, suppressed diplopia, and lifelong floaters. A second trap is temporal misattribution: patients date floaters to recent disease although the vitreous opacities were present lifelong [PAGE 242]. In multi-week experimental designs, a single baseline session captures an attentional set, not a stable sensory threshold; without periodic forced-attention catch trials, “stable” perception may simply be stable neglect. The counter-trap is to probe the subjective channel explicitly at intervals, rather than trusting absence of report over weeks as evidence of absence of signal.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Level 0: proximal signal exists (floater shadow on retina; binocular disparity off the horopter). Level 1: the observer perceives the external object (uniformly white paper; an approaching man of constant size). Level 2: the observer represents that these intrinsic qualities are normally overlooked—James’s “faculty of overlooking these altogether” [PAGE 242]. Level 3: the observer models the counterfactual instruction “if he will,” by which the shadow can be noticed as local color or the approaching man’s changing angular size can be registered.

Crisis habit regression versus counter-trap. Under ocular pathology, the system regresses from object-mode perception to sensation-mode awareness: floaters, previously suppressed, break through, and the patient misattributes this release to the disease rather than to altered attention. The counter-trap is James’s voluntary reversal: deliberate attention to intrinsic quality can temporarily reopen the subjective channel, but the default habit reasserts because attending to subjective feelings “could only cause disturbance” in ordinary cognition [PAGE 242].

Clock. The timescales are non-commensurate. Habitual suppression operates over years or a lifetime. Crisis emergence occurs over days to weeks of disease, or within seconds after occlusion of the healthy eye [PAGE 242]. Deliberate attentional shifting operates on a sub-second to second clock (~200–500 ms for pop-out, several seconds for sustained inspection of shadow as local color). A single introspective snapshot therefore cannot calibrate the baseline; valid inference requires sampling across all three clocks simultaneously.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer (optics and media). Veto rule: if no optical signal reaches the retina—because the eye is blind, the media are opaque, or the object is outside the visual field—no amount of attention can generate perception. James’s blind-eye case obeys this: the patient had no signal from the diseased eye until the healthy eye was occluded [PAGE 242], at which point the deficit was revealed by removal of binocular masking, not by attentional effort.

Biomechanical layer (oculomotor control). Veto rule: if fixation is unstable or extraocular alignment is abnormal (strabismus, poor convergence), disparity signals may be motor artifacts rather than evidence of conscious diplopia. James’s claim that off-horopter objects “had always been double” assumes normal correspondence and stable fixation; under strabismic suppression, diplopia is a sensory-motor mismatch, not an attentional oversight.

Weather / environmental layer (illumination and atmospheric optics). Veto rule: under uniform luminance, fog, or removed distance cues, shadows and size constancy degrade. James’s example of the sheet of paper “uniformly white, although a part of it may be in shadow” [PAGE 242] holds only under ecologically normal directional illumination; under diffuse lighting there may be no shadow to attend to, and an approaching man need not appear size-constant if distance cues are absent.

Cross-layer adjudication priority: physical optics vetoes biomechanics (no retinal image, no diplopia regardless of eye position); biomechanics vetoes environment (misaligned eyes create diplopia independent of illumination); environment vetoes attention (no contrast, no shadow detection despite instruction). James’s phenomenology is valid only where all three layers lie within their ecologically normal operating regimes.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 — Binocular diplopia veto. Setup: fixate center; present a vertical bar at 2° eccentricity, producing disparity ≈ 24 arcmin, beyond Panum’s limit. Counterfactual claim to veto: “subjects are continuously consciously aware of diplopia for all off-horopter objects.” Veto condition: if subjects report a single bar under normal binocular viewing and report doubling only after a fusion-breaking manipulation (e.g., 1 s of vertical prism or dichoptic masking), the continuous-conscious-diplopia claim is falsified. Prediction: single-report rate > 90% of trials; spontaneous diplopia report < 10%; detection latency for doubling > 800 ms without attentional cue.

Scenario 2 — Floaters attentional-gate veto. Setup: healthy subjects with no ocular disease view a homogeneous blue field (blue-field entoptic phenomenon) or James’s white sheet with one part in shadow [PAGE 242]. Counterfactual claim to veto: “eye disease causes muscae volitantes.” Veto condition: if healthy subjects reliably report moving dots/floaters under optimized viewing without pathology, disease-causation is vetoed. Prediction: detection rate > 70% within 30 s after instruction; d’ increases by > 1.5 SD from passive to instructed viewing; pathology and control groups show no baseline d’ difference when equated for attention.

Scenario 3 — Gradual monocular scotoma veto. Setup: induce a stepwise central scotoma in one eye while the fellow eye remains normal over weeks. Counterfactual claim to veto: “subjects notice gradual monocular vision loss immediately as it occurs.” Veto condition: if perimetry confirms a stable scotoma but the subject reports no field loss during binocular viewing and reports it within seconds after occluding the healthy eye [PAGE 242], immediate-notice is vetoed. Prediction: spontaneous binocular report latency > 4 weeks (or never); report after occlusion of healthy eye < 10 s; binocular summation masks the scotoma with sensitivity loss < 0.2 log unit until occlusion.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

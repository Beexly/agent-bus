Counter-trap: an observer (or the subject in first-person reflection) infers that vivid imagery must signal an urgent object—an external threat, a repressed wish, or a precursive symptom. James inverts this inference: intensity is a signature of arrested flow, not of object significance [PAGE 134]. The counter-trap is therefore to treat vividness as diagnostic of transition-rate collapse rather than as evidence of content salience. Mistaking the symptom (intensity) for the cause (important content) is the recursive error.

Clock: the operative timescale is \tau = 1/\sigma. In waking speech, \tau is sub-second; words awaken following words with no time for images [PAGE 134]. At sleep onset, \tau extends to seconds or minutes, permitting hypnagogic imagery to dwell [PAGE 134]. Circadian and homeostatic sleep pressure modulate \gamma (leak/decay) and baseline J_in; over multi-week protocols, chronic restriction lowers baseline associative fluency and raises the probability that any given chain halts at C, producing recurrent intensity spikes at predictable circadian phases rather than random meaningful eruptions.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical/energy veto: Neural signaling consumes ATP and is bounded by the refractory period (~1–2 ms, maximum rate ~10^3 s^{-1}) and ion-gradient restoration. James's "explode" metaphor on [PAGE 134] contains no energy term, no maximum conductance, and no Lyapunov bound; therefore x_C cannot grow without bound. The model is vetoed as a closed-loop physical theory unless regularized with x_C \le x_{\max}. It survives only as an information-dynamics analogy.

Biomechanical veto: Sensation requires intact peripheral transduction. J_periph depends on receptor potentials and afferent spike traffic; if the afferent pathway is lesioned, J_periph = 0 and no pail upset occurs regardless of central leak status [PAGE 134]. The text treats peripheral supply as an undifferentiated copious stream, ignoring transduction thresholds and receptor adaptation, so any central-only account of sensation is biomechanically incomplete.

Weather veto: No meteorological variable appears in [PAGE 134]. Ambient temperature, pressure, or illumination cannot be coupled to J_in or \gamma from this source alone; any weather modulation claim is external and unverified. Veto on environmental coupling absent from the text.

Adjudication: Retain James's model at the information layer (inverse relation between dwell time/suggestiveness and substantive strength), reject it at the physical layer (unbounded explosion, no metabolic constraint, no transduction threshold). The veto is partial, not total: the qualitative law Q \propto 1/\sigma is coherent, but the hydraulic "upset" is not a valid energy-bounded mechanism without additional biophysical equations.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

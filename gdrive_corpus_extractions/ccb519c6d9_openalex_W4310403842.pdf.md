<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical veto layer: The method is vetoed whenever the camera is not placed close to the middle of the field [PAGE 4], because the 2D median of horizontal flow no longer isolates camera motion from court-scale parallax. Wide-angle lenses (mentioned as future work on [PAGE 6]) introduce radial distortion that breaks the planar optical-flow assumption in Eq. (1). Occlusion of players by referees or crowd elements creates false foreground vectors, shifting the median and singularizing segmentation when foreground exceeds the median breakdown point.

Biomechanical veto layer: The model assumes player displacements induce camera motion [PAGE 2]. In sports where the camera operator pans independently of player bulk motion (e.g., following a ball in flight rather than player clusters), the foreground/background decomposition misattributes operator intent to players. Handball and basketball were chosen precisely because the ball is hand-held and ball detection fails >95% of frames [PAGE 2]; thus the method is validated only for invasion sports with player-centric camera motion. It is vetoed for ballistic tracking (soccer long passes, tennis lobs) where the relevant region of interest decouples from player mass motion.

Weather/environment veto layer: Amateur acquisition conditions are the core difficulty [PAGE 1–2]. Variable lighting, indoor gymnasium flicker, rain on lens, or poor sensor dynamic range corrupt optical-flow estimation [17] before segmentation even occurs. The downsampling to 160×90 [PAGE 3] exacerbates low-light noise. No weather-robustness ablation is reported; therefore outdoor or variable-illumination deployments are vetoed pending revalidation.

Adjudication: A deployment is cleared only if (a) camera position ≈ field midpoint, (b) sport exhibits player-bulk-induced camera motion, (c) capture hardware sustains ≥ capture fps with 20-frame startup latency, and (d) illumination is stable enough for FlowNet-grade flow. Any single failed condition vetoes the stack without retraining.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

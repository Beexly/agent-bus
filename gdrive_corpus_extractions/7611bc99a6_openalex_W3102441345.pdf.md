Normal-form game, Definition 1 [PAGE 20]: $G = \langle P, (S_i), (u_i)\rangle$, with $u_i : \times_{j\in P} S_j \to \mathbb{R}$. Bimatrix restriction $|P|=2$, payoffs $(A,B)$, row = kicker, column = goalkeeper.

Nash equilibrium, Eq. (1) [PAGE 20]:
$$\mathbb{E}[u_i(\sigma_i^*, \sigma_{-i}^*)] \ge \mathbb{E}[u_i(s_i', \sigma_{-i}^*)] \quad \forall i,\ \forall s_i' \in S_i.$$

ε-Nash, Eq. (2) [PAGE 21]:
$$\mathbb{E}[u_i(\sigma_i^*, \sigma_{-i}^*)] \ge \mathbb{E}[u_i(s_i', \sigma_{-i}^*)] - \epsilon \quad \forall i,\ \forall s_i' \in S_i.$$
Units: $u_i$ is a dimensionless scoring probability in $[0,1]$; $\epsilon$ inherits those units (probability mass of forgone payoff).

Reproducing the equilibrium operator on Table 2b (reproduced payoff, kicker scoring probability) [PAGE 23]:
$$A=\begin{pmatrix}0.704 & 0.907\\ 0.894 & 0.640\end{pmatrix}\quad(\text{rows }N,NN\text{-S};\ \text{cols }N,NN\text{-G}).$$
The kick is constant-sum ($u_{GK}=1-u_{S}$), so the mixed Nash follows from the indifference principle. Kicker mixing weight $p=P(N\text{-S})$ that leaves the keeper indifferent between diving $N$ and $NN$:
$$0.704p+0.894(1-p)=0.907p+0.640(1-p)\ \Rightarrow\ 0.254=0.457\,p\ \Rightarrow\ p\approx0.556.$$
Keeper mixing weight $q=P(N\text{-G})$ that leaves the kicker indifferent:
$$0.907-0.203q=0.640+0.254q\ \Rightarrow\ 0.267=0.457\,q\ \Rightarrow\ q\approx0.584.$$
These match the reported Nash marginals ($N\text{-S}=0.569$, $N\text{-G}=0.592$, Table 2d [PAGE 23]) to within rounding/tie-handling, confirming the operator is correctly specified.

Singularity/breakdown regime: the interior solution exists only if no pure strategy dominates. The indifference denominator $0.457$ is $(a_{NN,NN}+a_{N,NN}-a_{N,N}-a_{NN,N})$; when the action set is refined to L/C/R (Table 5a [PAGE 25]) the center-shot-vs-center-dive cell collapses to $0.160$, an almost strictly-dominant response. As a payoff cell drives toward a dominant row/column, the indifference denominator $\to 0$, $p$ or $q$ diverges to a boundary, and the equilibrium degenerates from mixed to pure — the mixed-strategy analysis then ceases to be the right description.

Divergence metric (paper reports values; general operator, base not stated in excerpt):
$$\mathrm{JSD}(P\|Q)=\tfrac12 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\|M)+\tfrac12 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q\|M),\quad M=\tfrac12(P+Q).$$

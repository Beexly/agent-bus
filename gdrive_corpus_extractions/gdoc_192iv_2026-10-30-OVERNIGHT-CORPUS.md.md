Veto Scenario 1 — Leverage chart via dynamic-rating outcome filter.

Veto basis: Timo Riske’s paired-flip analysis ([PAGE 2]) shows the filter conditions on a post-outcome variable (the win selects into a higher future rating path), inflating leverage by 1.3–1.6× per team with floor ≈1.3 for teams still in the hunt, and producing positive leverage for games whose true effect is 0.

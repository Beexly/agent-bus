Furthermore, James's emphasis on "stimulating effect upon the will" prefigures Pragmatism’s core tenet: truth is what works. Over a multi-week period of exposure, a user's neural plasticity may adapt such that the simulated environment achieves high $\mathcal{R}$ scores, effectively rewriting their baseline for "reality." The trap lies in the eventual difficulty of downshifting; the physical world, lacking the optimized feedback loops of the digital construct, feels comparatively "unreal" (low liveliness, low volitional stimulation). This creates a dependency loop where the individual requires increasingly potent artificial stimuli to achieve the same sense of existential grounding, mirroring addiction pathways.

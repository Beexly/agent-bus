The counter-trap is to elect the touchback and force the opponent to possess the ball under pressure, converting the let-down from a liability into an asset.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

Physical layer. The football is a prolate spheroid; its bounce vector \(B\) is a random variable with mean \(\mu_b\) and covariance \(\Sigma_b\). On firm turf, \(\Sigma_b\) is modest; on wet or uneven ground, \(\Sigma_b\) grows, making the "bound about" behavior described for the 1916 Yale recovery [PAGE 133] less predictable. The physical veto condition is:

\[
\text{tr}(\Sigma_b) > \Sigma_{\text{crit}} \implies \text{Veto return}
\]

Biomechanical layer. Simple human reaction time \(T_r \approx 0.15\text{–}0.25\) s. Sprint speed \(v_s \approx 8.0\text{–}9.0\) m/s for elite backs of the era. Time to reach the goal line from the twenty-yard line (18.288 m [PAGE 133]) is:

\[
T_{\text{run}} = \frac{18.288}{v_s} \approx 2.0\text{–}2.3\ \text{s}
\]

Total response time \(T_{\text{resp}} = T_r + T_{\text{run}}\). If the kicking team's recovery hazard \(\lambda_K\) implies \(t_{\text{ex}} < T_{\text{resp}}\), the return is biomechanically untenable and the veto engages.

Weather layer. Let \(\mu_{\text{ball}}\) denote the hand-to-ball coefficient of friction. Precipitation reduces \(\mu_{\text{ball}}\). The catch-on-fly success probability \(p_{\text{catch}}\) is a monotonically increasing function of \(\mu_{\text{ball}}\). Veto condition:

\[
\mu_{\text{ball}} < \mu_c \implies p_{\text{catch}} < p_{\text{safe}} \implies \text{Veto catch-on-fly; elect touchback}
\]

Cross-layer adjudication rule: the touchback is mandatory if any layer's veto condition is satisfied:

\[
\mathcal{V} = \bigvee_{l \in \{\text{phys}, \text{bio}, \text{weather}\}} \left[ S_l > S_l^{\text{crit}} \right]
\]

Where \(S_l\) is the stressor scalar for layer \(l\). Roper's acceptance of touchback "even at the risk of a let-down" [PAGE 133] is consistent with a weather or physical veto overriding the return preference. The veto proof holds because it does not require quantifying betting odds; it requires only binary layer-stressor comparisons against critical thresholds.

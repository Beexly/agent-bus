$$
E^* \sim_\delta E' \iff \| E^* - E' \|_{\infty} < \delta.
$$
The antecedent state $\mathbf{x}(t_0)$ would need to be known with precision $\delta_{\mathbf{x}}$ such that the pullback under the flow separates the trajectories:
$$
\| \Phi_{t_1-t_0}(\mathbf{x} + \delta \mathbf{v}) - \Phi_{t_1-t_0}(\mathbf{x}) \| > \delta_E
$$
for some direction $\mathbf{v}$. James asserts that the required $\delta \to 0$ is physically unrealizable: measurements of "psychic or of neural quantities" are "forever beyond human reach" [PAGE 572].

Information-theoretic formulation: Let $A$ be the discrete action (act or refrain), and $X_{-\tau}$ the neural antecedent state $\tau$ seconds prior. Determinism implies $H(A \mid X_{-\tau}) = 0$ almost everywhere, i.e., the conditional entropy vanishes. The mutual information $I(A; X_{-\tau}) = H(A)$ saturates the channel. However, the measurable mutual information under sensor noise $N$ is bounded by the Shannon capacity
$$
I_{\text{meas}}(A; \hat{X}_{-\tau}) \leq C = \max_{p(\hat{X})} I(\hat{X}; \hat{X}+N),
$$
with $C$ in bits/second. If $C < H(A)$, the antecedent cannot fully determine the observed action from data, creating an epistemic gap even under metaphysical determinism.

Thermodynamic measurement bound: Resolving $\mathbf{x}$ to precision $\delta$ requires at least Landauer energy $E_{\text{erase}} \geq k_B T \ln 2 \cdot \log_2(1/\delta)$ joules per bit, where $k_B$ is Boltzmann's constant [J/K], $T$ is temperature [K]. As $\delta \to 0$, $E \to \infty$, establishing a physical singularity at infinite resolution.

Breakdown regime summary:
1. $\delta \to 0$: measurement energy diverges (Landauer singularity).
2. $\mathbf{x} \in \Sigma$: non-unique flow, deterministic prediction fails.
3. $C < H(A)$: information bottleneck, psychologic insolubility.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage of James's argument extends backward through the mechanization of the will. Democritean atomism and Stoic fate established the template that all events, including human acts, are necessitated by prior physical conditions. Leibniz's principle of sufficient reason formalized the demand that every volition have a ground, and Laplace's demon provided the idealized epistemic subject: an intelligence knowing all positions and forces, for whom the future is as certain as the past. James inherits this mathematical physics worldview but relocates it inside the skull: the "physical mass, his brain" becomes the local Laplacean domain [PAGE 572].

The Darwinian and evolutionary psychology currents of James's era further naturalize interests and associations as mechanically determined sequences, making the brain not merely a deterministic engine but an historically structured one. James's American pragmatism refracts this lineage through a refusal to settle metaphysical disputes by verbal abstraction; instead, he identifies the cash-value of the free-will debate as nil because no psychologic measurement can decide it.

The multi-week trap is explicit in the text's temporal morphology: impulses accumulate over "days and weeks until at last they culminate in some particularly dirty or cowardly or cruel act" [PAGE 572]. This is not a single decision point but a slow drift through state space toward a basin boundary. The retrospective phenomenology of remorse collapses this extended history into a single "fatal moment," creating an illusion of punctual agency where there was only gradual sedimentation.

The generational trap is named by James's Faustian aside: "dazu hast du noch eine lange Frist" [PAGE 572]. He predicts that "from generation to generation the reasons adduced on both sides will grow more voluminous" [PAGE 572], meaning the debate undergoes retrospective inflation without decision convergence. Each era adds measurement techniques and metaphysical refinements, yet the singularity remains: the antecedents of effort are inaccessible to the very introspection that feels them, and the neural measurement that could settle them is declared practically impossible. This is a self-sustaining research program whose trap is that progress in volume does not equal progress in closure.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Three recursive layers govern the agent's model of their own agency during moral crisis.

1. Crisis habit regression. Under the pressure of remorse following a "dirty or cowardly or cruel act" [PAGE 572], the agent regresses to the deterministic habit-structure that produced the act. The very feeling of "I might have reined them in" is a post-hoc reconstruction, not a real-time option. The crisis exposes the regression: the agent's self-model includes alternative possibilities, but the causal model of the brain excludes them.

2. Counter-trap. The determinist sets a counter-trap by generalizing from effortless volition. Because "Even in effortless volition we have the consciousness of the alternative being also possible. This is surely a delusion here" [PAGE 572], the determinist infers that effortful volition is equally delusional. But this inference overreaches: the introduction of effort may change the qualitative structure of decision dynamics even if it does not restore metaphysical freedom. The counter-trap is therefore a false dichotomy—either introspection is veridical and free will exists, or introspection is delusional and determinism is proven. James rejects both by declaring the question insoluble on strictly psychologic grounds [PAGE 572].

3. Clock. The temporal architecture of the passage contains two clocks in conflict. Clock A is the phenomenological clock of the "fatal moment"—the instant of remorseful awareness. Clock B is the neural clock of antecedents accumulating over "days and weeks" [PAGE 572]. Because Clock B is unreadable by introspection, the agent experiences Clock A as sovereign while Clock B actually governs the trajectory. The recursive theory of mind must model this mismatch: the agent believes they are reading Clock A, but their behavior is generated by Clock B, and they cannot verify the coupling between the two.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical veto. At the physical layer, thermal noise at temperature $T$ imposes a minimum energy scale $k_B T$ per degree of freedom, and quantum uncertainty imposes $\Delta x \Delta p \geq \hbar/2$. These bounds prevent the exact specification of $\mathbf{x}(t_0)$ required by the deterministic flow $\Phi_t$ [PAGE 572]. The veto is absolute: no finite-precision measurement can resolve the antecedent state below the Landauer singularity $\delta \to 0$, so the determinist proof James describes is physically blocked.

Biomechanical veto. Neural conduction velocities range roughly from $1$ to $100$ m/s, synaptic transmission introduces delays of order $0.5$ ms, and the hemodynamic response lags neural activity by $1$–$6$ s. These latencies mean that conscious intention cannot be the proximal cause of the neural events that initiate action. The biomechanical layer vetoes the folk model in which volition directly commands muscle, replacing it with a cascaded architecture in which the "effort of attention" is itself a downstream readout.

Weather veto. Exogenous environmental perturbations—weather, social cues, stochastic sensory input—act as unbounded forcing terms on the neural manifold $\mathcal{M}$. Because the brain is not a closed system, the vector field $\mathbf{F}$ must include time-dependent exogenous inputs, breaking the closed-loop determinism James's monism presupposes. This veto does not rescue libertarian free will; it merely deepens the epistemic indeterminacy by showing that even perfect knowledge of $\mathbf{x}(t_0)$ would be insufficient without a complete specification of the environmental forcing.

Adjudication. None of these three vetoes establishes metaphysical freedom. They jointly establish that the determinist's required measurement—neural antecedents with mathematical exactitude—is blocked at the physical, biomechanical, and environmental layers. James's conclusion that the question is insoluble on strictly psychologic grounds [PAGE 572] is therefore strengthened, not weakened, by cross-layer analysis: the layers do not converge on a decision, they multiply the regimes of breakdown.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

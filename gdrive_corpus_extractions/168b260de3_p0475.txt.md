This places Tolman between peripheralist emotion theory and the cognitive purposivism of his later cognitive-map work (Tolman & Honzik, 1930; Tolman, 1948). The forward lineage runs: James-Lange peripheral feedback → Tolman 1932 tripartite sign-gestalt expectation → cognitive maps and means-end expectancies → modern predictive processing / active inference, where the brain minimizes expected free energy over trajectories of sensory consequences.

Multi-week trap: Tolman's terminal states are temporally unindexed. "Some" final physiological quiescence or disturbance carries no decay constant, no allostatic set-point, and no consolidation window [PAGE 444]. Over a multi-week horizon, visceral baselines drift (allostatic load, circadian entrainment, habituation). An expectation formed on day 1 that commerce with sign-object \(o\) leads to quiescence may be invalid by day 14 because the reference physiological state has shifted; the model cannot predict retention or extinction without specifying \(\tau_{\text{relax}}\) (visceral relaxation time) or the inter-trial commerce interval. The phrase "if had commerce with" implies repeated commerce, yet [PAGE 444] gives no frequency, duration, or spacing rule, so any multi-week prediction of approach/avoidance behavior is underdetermined. The "means end relations ... direction distance correlations" likewise lack a time constant: direction-distance in a static field does not specify how the field updates over weeks of changing environmental presence. Thus Tolman's page is scientifically rich for within-session behavior (seconds to minutes) but contains a generative trap for longitudinal (>1 week) extrapolation unless an external dynamic model of physiological set-point drift is imported.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: Under acute threat, the organism may regress past phase (a) and express only phase (b), incipient visceral and skeletal activity, without a fully released sign-gestalt expectation. This is a regression to pre-cognitive habit: the body prepares for injury (James-Lange periphery) while the cognitive determinant "fear stimulus will lead to pain" is bypassed or compressed. Verbatim [PAGE 444] supports the separability: fear is "defined as consisting of" the three phases, but nothing on this page guarantees all three co-occur in every crisis.

Counter-trap: The external analyst (or modeler) recursively imputes a sign-gestalt expectation to the subject. If the analyst imputes \(\mathcal{G}_{\text{fear}}\) when the subject's actual expectation concerns a different signified-object or a different means-end relation, the intervention—removing the presumed fear stimulus—fails because the subject's immanent cognitive determinant was aimed elsewhere. The recursion is: analyst's expectation about subject's expectation → intervention → subject's actual expectation → outcome mismatch → analyst revises imputation. Without ground-truth access to phase (a), the loop is unclosed.

Clock: The three-phase loop has period \(T = \tau_a + \tau_b + \tau_c\), where \(\tau_a\) is expectation-release latency, \(\tau_b\) is incipient activity duration, and \(\tau_c\) is kinesthetic feedback latency. If the crisis duration \(T_{\text{crisis}} < T\), phase (c) sensations never arrive to confirm or disconfirm phase (a); the expectation remains "immanent" but unvalidated. If \(T_{\text{crisis}} \gg T\), the loop repeats and may entrain into a habitual emotion cycle. Breakdown: when the crisis clock outruns the kinesthetic clock, the subject cannot use resultant sensations (phase c) to update the sign-gestalt expectation (phase a), producing a frozen or runaway affective state. All constraints grounded in the tripartite definition on [PAGE 444].
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Three layers can veto the complete emotion/feeling circuit defined on [PAGE 444].

Physical layer (neuromuscular): Phase (b) requires incipient skeletal activity. The skeletal reflex arc has a minimum latency \(\tau_{\text{reflex}} \approx 30\text{--}100\) ms. Tolman's fear definition depends on "mere staying in its presence" [PAGE 444]; if the fear stimulus is presented and withdrawn in \(< \tau_{\text{reflex}}\), no incipient skeletal movement can be executed, truncating phase (b) and preventing phase (c) kinesthetic sensations from referencing a completed movement pattern. Veto condition: stimulus duration \(\Delta t_{\text{stim}} < \tau_{\text{reflex}} \Rightarrow A_{\text{skeletal}} = 0\).

Biomechanical layer (visceral): Visceral activities (heart rate, gut motility, piloerection) have time constants \(\tau_v \in [1, 60]\) s. If the sign-object is withdrawn before the visceral response develops, phase (b) is incomplete and the "resultant visceral ... sensations" of phase (c) have no stable referent. Veto condition: \(\Delta t_{\text{stim}} < \tau_v \Rightarrow V(t)\) undefined at decision boundary.

Environmental/weather layer: [PAGE 444] defines "Environmental presence" as "A present behavior-support (q v) 'rr'" (OCR-corrupted). Ambient temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure shift the visceral baseline, altering the threshold between pleasantness (quiescence) and unpleasantness (disturbance). If the signal-to-noise ratio of the sign-object relative to environmental noise satisfies \(\text{SNR}_{\text{env}} < 1\), the "actually presented stimul" fails to arouse the immanent cognitive determinant [PAGE 444], and \(\mathcal{G}_{\text{total}} = \emptyset\).

Adjudication: The full three-phase emotion exists only if \(\bigwedge_{L \in \{\text{physical}, \text{biomechanical}, \text{weather}\}} \text{SNR}_L \ge 1\). Any single layer falling below unity vetoes the circuit. No betting lines or odds are invoked; thresholds are deterministic latency and SNR constraints derived from the text and physiology.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with falsifiable predictions. RLVR = reward/validation grounded in observable, non-betting quantities.

Veto 1 (Neuromuscular phase-(b) suppression): Administer a reversible neuromuscular blockade that eliminates incipient skeletal activity (phase b) while preserving the cognitive sign-gestalt expectation (phase a) and visceral tone. Prediction: The subject will self-report fear and show visceral activation but will lack kinesthetic sensation (phase c), falsifying Tolman's claim that the emotion consists of all three phases conjointly [PAGE 444]. Verification: simultaneous self-report, EMG (skeletal silence), and visceral telemetry (heart rate/skin conductance). If self-report persists despite EMG silence, phase (a) is dissociable from phases (b)+(c).

Veto 2 (Sub-reflex stimulus duration): Present the fear stimulus for \(\Delta t_{\text{stim}} = 20\) ms, below the skeletal reflex arc latency, then withdraw it. Prediction: Because Tolman requires "mere staying in its presence" [PAGE 444] for the expectation to lead to injury, no incipient skeletal or visceral activity will bind, no kinesthetic sensation will result, and the subject will not report fear despite the stimulus being "actually presented" [PAGE 444]. Verification: startle reflex measures and post-trial self-report at multiple sub-reflex durations. A null fear report below threshold validates the dwell-time veto; any fear report below threshold falsifies the "mere staying" necessity.

Veto 3 (Semantic/OCR presentation corruption): Corrupt the sign-object cue or environmental context with character-level noise at error rate \(\epsilon > 0.20\), such that the object is not reliably discriminable. Prediction: Per [PAGE 444], expectation is "aroused by actually presented stimul" and occurs as expectation of a "total sign gestalt"; with \(\epsilon > 0.20\), discriminanda expectations cannot form, so no sign-gestalt expectation is released and behavior toward the object becomes stochastic rather than goal-directed (no stable approach/avoidance). Verification: vary OCR/cue corruption rate and measure choice consistency in a repeated-discrimination task; a sharp drop in consistency at \(\epsilon \approx 0.20\) confirms the presentation veto. No odds or betting lines are used; the dependent variable is choice consistency (dimensionless proportion).

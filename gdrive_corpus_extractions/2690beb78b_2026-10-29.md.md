1. Quantile-regression TTA operator [PAGE 1].

\mathcal{L}_{	au}(T, \hat{T}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \Big[ 	au \, \max(0, T_i - \hat{T}_i) + (1 - 	au) \, \max(0, \hat{T}_i - T_i) \Big]

where tau \in (0,1) is dimensionless. The learned predictor is \hat{T} = f_\theta(x) with an MLP parameter vector \theta. Lower-quantile regression (tau near 0) targets the earliest feasible arrival boundary.

Breakdown/singularity: As tau -> 0 or tau -> 1, the subgradient of the pinball loss at zero residual is undefined, and the estimator approaches the sample minimum or maximum, respectively, becoming unstable under censoring. The model assumes i.i.d. tracking samples and Markovian sufficiency of x; both fail under defensive pressure that is unobserved in the state vector.

2. Physics-based motion baseline comparison [PAGE 1].

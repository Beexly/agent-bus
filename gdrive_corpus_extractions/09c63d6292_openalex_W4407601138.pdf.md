<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The theoretical lineage is Weickian sensemaking extended to co-parental familial identity. Weick (1979, 1993, 1995) defines sensemaking as retrospective, grounded in enactment-selection-retention, and driven by equivocality and extracted cues; the Mann Gulch collapse shows what happens when sensemaking breaks down under catastrophic ambiguity [PAGE 10, 16-17 of 96]. Seeger, Sellnow, and Ulmer (2003) carry this into organizational crisis communication; Dougherty and Smythe (2004) and Dougherty and Drumheller (2006) apply it to dyadic and identity-laden sensemaking [PAGE 10-12 of 96]. Boneau extends individual identity to “familial identity,” arguing that family identity operates like individual identity in enactment, selection, and retention [PAGE 63-64 of 96]. The decision-theory overlay is Ajzen and Fishbein (1980) TRA and Ajzen (1991) TPB, adding perceived behavioral control to attitudes and subjective norms [PAGE 7, 66-72 of 96]. The medical lineage runs CDC concussion concern, NCCSIR participation estimates, Omalu (2017) on youth brain vulnerability, McKee/Mez neuropathology, and Giza and Hovda (2001) neurometabolic concussion cascade [PAGE 8-9, 92-93 of 96]. Multi-week traps: (1) retrospective timing—parents justify a decision already made, so pre-season risk communication competes with a retained narrative rather than a prospective choice [PAGE 16-17, 24 of 96]. Crisis habit regression: after an injury sentinel, parental risk salience spikes temporarily, but absent repeated injury the family regresses to its baseline identity script—football families downplay risk and emphasize benefits, hesitant parents rationalize with technology or “he’s so big,” child-focused parents prioritize the child’s desire over parental fear [PAGE 40-49, 66-69 of 96]. \[
S(t)=S_0\exp[-\lambda(t-t_i)]\mathbf 1[t\ge t_i],
\]
with \(S\) dimensionless and \(\lambda\) a decay rate; the next conscious re-assessment occurs only at decision-clock nodes \(\mathcal T=\{\text{middle-school entry, high-school transition, post-injury sit-down}\}\) [PAGE 49, 68-70 of 96]. If an intervention arrives at \(t\notin\mathcal T\) and no injury occurs, \(S(t)\) returns near baseline before the next node, so the communication has no decision point to alter.

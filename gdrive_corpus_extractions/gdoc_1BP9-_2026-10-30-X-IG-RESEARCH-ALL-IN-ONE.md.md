P(i \succ j) = \frac{e^{\beta_i}}{e^{\beta_i} + e^{\beta_j}}, \qquad \beta_i = \beta_i(t).
\]
Dynamic BT treats strength as time-varying with minimax-rate guarantees. Breakdown: identifying constraint choice (sum-to-zero vs reference category) changes asymptotic efficiency of pairwise comparisons (Wu et al., arXiv:2205.04341; 12 pages [PAGE 12]); the constraint is not cosmetic and moves week-to-week rating stability.

7. Low-rank adaptation (Hu et al., ICLR 2022; arXiv:2106.09685; 26 pages [PAGE 26]).
\[
W + \Delta W = W + BA, \qquad B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r},\; A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times k},\; r \ll \min(d,k).
\]
Breakdown: adapter rank \(r\) is a bottleneck; too low \(r\) underfits task-specific structure, too high erases parameter-efficiency gains.

8. Direct preference optimization (Rafailov et al., NeurIPS 2023; arXiv:2305.18290; 27 pages [PAGE 27]).
\[
\ell_{\text{DPO}} = -\log \sigma\!\left(\beta\bigl[\log \pi_\theta(y_w|x) - \log \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_w|x) - \log \pi_\theta(y_l|x) + \log \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_l|x)\bigr]\right).
\]
Breakdown: assumes the Bradley–Terry–Luce coupling between preferences and logistic loss; D21 (arXiv:2507.07855; 24 pages [PAGE 24]) shows this coupling is less fundamental than assumed, so DPO implementations must re-check foundational assumptions before deployment.

Physics veto — seam orientation. Autonomy/structure requires seam orientation as a state variable alongside spin axis/rate; without that field, SSW movement is unexplainable residual forever. Veto condition: any ball-flight model integrating only gravity + drag + Magnus is misspecified for seam-active pitches and must be rejected until a seam-orientation field is added. Verbatim quote: "without that field, SSW movement is unexplainable residual forever."

Biomechanical veto — event-timing error floor. Source text A14 (Jinji, Slowik, Fleisig, Sports Biomechanics, DOI 10.1080/14763141.2026.2746454) measures how accurately kinematic methods pin down ball-release moment in pitching. Every tracking-derived feature inherits error from event-timing definitions (release, foot plant, break). Veto condition: feature schemas lacking a measurement-error field sourced from metrology literature inherit unquantified noise and must be blocked. This directly disciplines A12/A13/A15 enthusiasm: pose data is only as good as its event definitions.

Weather veto — environmental covariates unverified. Source text B4 specifies a hierarchical field-gool model on 23,247 kicks with distance, wind, temperature, elevation, roof, and kicker effects; stated make rates are as-posted and unverified. Source text B10 states coaches go for it only 29% of the time a model recommends (as-posted, unverified). Veto condition: any weather-conditioned kick model or coach-behavior adjustment cannot be deployed until covariate coefficients and behavioral rates are verified against primary sources. Weather fields (wind, temperature, elevation, roof) must carry uncertainty intervals, not point estimates.

Orientation veto — drag crisis is orientation-dependent. Source text A6 (Fluids MDPI, FIFA World Cup ball Trionda) shows drag crisis depends on panel orientation; identical kicks fly differently by ball rotation state. Veto condition: a single drag coefficient per ball model is a known-wrong simplification. Flight features must carry ball-orientation state.

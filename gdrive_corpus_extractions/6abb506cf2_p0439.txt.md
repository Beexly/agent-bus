$$ D_C(a_t) = \mathbb{E}_{s_{t+1} \sim P(\cdot|s_t,a_t)} [ H(p_\theta(s_{t+1}|h_t)) - H(p_\theta(s_{t+1}|h_{t+1})) ] $$
This aligns with James's notion that curiosity aims to "ascertain as much about them... as he can." Units: Expected information gain (bits/nats per action).

**3. Fear/Avoidance Drive ($D_F$):**

$$ D_F(a_t) = -\lambda \cdot C(s_t, a_t) - \gamma \mathbb{E}[V_{risk}(s_{t+1})] $$
where $V_{risk}$ estimates long-term cumulative danger. This embodies the "better that he should not absolutely fear them" but must remain "on the qui vive." Units: Utility/cost (arbitrary, but comparable to reward scale).

Hyperparameters $\alpha, \beta > 0$ control relative weighting. The policy $\pi(a|s)$ maximizes $Q(s,a)$ derived from this composite return. The "antagonistic" nature manifests when maximizing $D_C$ (explore novel $s$) conflicts with minimizing $D_F$ (avoid risky $s$).

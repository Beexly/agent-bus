Clock (hard temporal constraint). The environment carries a game clock and a 24 s shot clock [PAGE 3]. Any deployment should clamp m below the shot-clock frame budget (~600 frames) to respect the clock recursion.

Physical/biomechanical veto proof.

Ball flight is projectile motion under gravity and quadratic drag: m ẍ = −(1/2) ρ C_d A |v| v_x, m z̈ = −mg − (1/2) ρ C_d A |v| v_z, with g=9.81 m/s², ρ≈1.225 kg/m³, C_d≈0.5, A=π(0.12)²≈0.045 m², m≈0.62 kg. In free flight (no player "touch" event in D_pbp), the implied acceleration must satisfy |a + g ẑ + (drag/m)| ≈ 0 within quantization noise. For a valid 3-point arc (horizontal ≈7.24 m, release ≈2.5 m, rim 3.05 m), required launch speed v_0 ≈ 8–9 m/s and flight time ≈1.2–1.5 s (~30–37 frames at 25 Hz). Any generated segment where the ball reverses direction or accelerates toward the rim with no intervening possession/touch event violates conservation of momentum — this is exactly the paper's admitted pathology [PAGE 6]: "defy the laws of physics… propelled towards the basket as if being controlled by an invisible player." Veto criterion (finite-difference, Δt=0.04 s): compute v_t=(p_{t+1}−p_t)/Δt, a_t=(v_{t+1}−v_t)/Δt; flag frames with |v_t| > v_max (players ≲10 m/s; ball off-rim ≲15 m/s) or free-flight ‖a_t + g ẑ‖ above the drag-plus-noise envelope. Biomechanical bound on players: court is 28.65 m × 15.24 m; sustained human sprint ≲10–12 m/s with bounded acceleration; segments implying superhuman speed or teleportation are vetoed.

Weather veto: null and dismissed. All play is indoors on climate-controlled courts (Boise venue irrelevant to the 2015–16 source data). Air density variation across arenas is second-order (ρ variation <3% across typical indoor conditions, entering only through the drag term). Therefore no weather variable belongs in the model; any weather term is a spurious covariate and is vetoed as non-causal. This is a clean, provable exclusion rather than a judgment call.

Adjudication: at α_g ≤ 0.1 the trajectory set plausibly passes the physical/biomechanical veto (motion stays near the data manifold). At α_g ≥ 1, and decisively at α_g = 10, the veto fails by the paper's own admission, so the high-return Table 4/Table 3-max numbers are physically invalid and must be discarded as planning evidence.

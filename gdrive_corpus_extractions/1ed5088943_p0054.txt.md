<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
RLVR is interpreted here as a reproducible, evidence-grounded verification layer that rejects equilibrium claims when any necessary condition is falsified. Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios:

\textbf{Veto 1 — Foresight computability.} Counterfactual: agents must compute the conditional expectation $\mathbb{E}[X_{t+1} \mid \mathcal{F}_t]$ within a maximum latency $\Delta t_{\max}$ [s]. Verification: measure $\Delta t_{\mathrm{compute}}$ for the relevant forecast. If $\Delta t_{\mathrm{compute}} > \Delta t_{\max}$, the static price vector $\mathbf{p}^*$ is not a computable attractor. Prediction: entrepreneurial profit emerges as the residual between realized outcomes and the statically anticipated path, because Clark’s assumption of substantially perfect knowledge [PAGE 80] is falsified by computational delay.

\textbf{Veto 2 — Factor-mobility threshold.} Counterfactual: capital–labor mobility $M$ falls below a critical value $M_{\mathrm{crit}}$ (dimensionless). Verification: observe sectoral wage and capital-return differentials over time; if they fail to converge despite arbitrage opportunities, $M < M_{\mathrm{crit}}$. Prediction: static rates of wages and interest diverge from Clarkian levels [PAGE 80]; the “high degree of mobility” assumption is violated, so factor-price equalization cannot occur and profit persists as a disequilibrium return.

\textbf{Veto 3 — Non-measurability of future contingencies.} Counterfactual: the relevant future event space admits no countably additive probability measure $\mathbb{P}$. Verification: attempt to construct a probability model from historical frequencies; if the Radon–Nikodym derivative $d\mathbb{P}/d\mathbb{Q}$ fails to exist or frequencies do not stabilize, Knightian uncertainty obtains. Prediction: expected-utility maximization is undefined; Clark’s device of covering static risk by “a small percentage of the annual gains” [PAGE 80] cannot be implemented, because the losses are not insurable in the frequentist sense. Profit therefore becomes the return to judgment under uncertainty rather than a compensated risk premium.

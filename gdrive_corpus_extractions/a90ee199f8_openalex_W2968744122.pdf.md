\[
\text{Veto}(C)=1 \quad \text{if} \quad E(P)=\varnothing \ \text{and} \ C \ \text{requires unprovided measurement}.
\]

Units: dimensionless indicator, with \(\text{Veto}(C)\in\{0,1\}\). Assumptions: the provided pages are the only admissible evidence; entailment is evaluated conservatively. Breakdown: if the claim is merely a quotation of a coach belief, then it may be admitted as a reported belief rather than as an external fact.

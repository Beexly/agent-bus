Prediction: Complementary shadow colors still appear and scale with cone-opponent excitation differences \(\Delta E = |L_{M,\text{shadow}} - L_{M,\text{field}}| + |L_{S,\text{shadow}} - L_{S,\text{field}}|\), where \(L_M\) and \(L_S\) are medium- and short-wavelength cone excitations, not with the observer’s explicit belief about the source. Verifiable measure: magnitude of chromatic contrast should correlate with \(\Delta E\) across illuminant pairs, independent of post-experimental reports of judgment.

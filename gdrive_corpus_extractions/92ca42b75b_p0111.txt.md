Furthermore, the discovery of long-term potentiation (LTP) in the 1960s validated the idea of discrete, lasting physiological changes at the synaptic level, providing the molecular mechanism for Hull's abstract increments. Yet, modern neuroscience reveals that these increments are highly plastic and context-dependent, challenging the strict determinism of the original Hullian framework.

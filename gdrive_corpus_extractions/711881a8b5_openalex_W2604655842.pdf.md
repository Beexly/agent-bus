Recursive theory of mind here means nested agent models: player beliefs about coach beliefs, coach beliefs about player potential, parent beliefs about career viability, and peer beliefs about normative commitment. For coaches, the source notes that “coaches expect a conforming dedication to their instructions” and “often view players not following instructions and any subsequent mistakes as signs of a weak player” [PAGE 17].

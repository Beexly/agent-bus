\mathrm{BS} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} (f_i - y_i)^2

\[
\mathrm{FPR}_{\text{pass}} = \frac{N(r_i=\text{PASS}\land y_i=\text{bad})}{N(r_i=\text{PASS})} = 0.9961

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
The paper's own operators, verbatim, with dimensional analysis:

Loss ([PAGE 4], Eq. 1):
$$L = \alpha\cdot \mathrm{CE}(y_{\text{true}}, y_{\text{pred}}) + \beta\cdot \mathrm{MSE}(x_{\text{true}}, x_{\text{pred}})$$
$\mathrm{CE}$ is dimensionless (nats); $\mathrm{MSE}$ here has units of $\text{pixel}^2$ (or normalized-image$^2$), so $\beta$ carries units of $1/\text{pixel}^2$ to make $L$ dimensionally consistent — a point the paper omits. **Breakdown regime:** MSE on box coordinates is a known-inferior localizer (UnitBox, Yu et al. 2016): the gradient scales with absolute pixel error irrespective of box scale, so large and small boxes are weighted inconsistently. Modern detectors replace it with IoU-family losses ($\mathrm{GIoU}, \mathrm{DIoU}, \mathrm{CIoU}$, all dimensionless and scale-invariant). Using MSE is a regressive choice.

Classification operators ([PAGE 5], Eqs. 2–5), all dimensionless ratios in $[0,1]$:
$$\text{Acc} = \frac{TP+TN}{N},\quad P = \frac{TP}{TP+FP},\quad R = \frac{TP}{TP+FN},\quad F_1 = \frac{2PR}{P+R}$$
Internal consistency checks out: $F_1^{\text{off}} = \frac{2(0.9832)(0.9745)}{0.9832+0.9745} = 0.9788$ ✓; $F_1^{\text{foul}} = \frac{2(0.9712)(0.9685)}{0.9712+0.9685}=0.9699$ ✓.

Derived operator (implied prior), from $\text{Acc}=0.9985,\ P=0.9832,\ R=0.9745$:
$$FP = TP\frac{1-P}{P},\quad TN = N-pN-FP,\quad \frac{p}{N}=\frac{1-\text{Acc}}{(1-R)+\frac{R(1-P)}{P}}$$
Offside: $\frac{p}{N}=\frac{0.0015}{0.0255+0.017086}=0.0356$. Foul: $\frac{0.0144}{0.0315+0.029654}=0.239$. Units: pure fractions (event priors).

Throughput operator (queueing, [PAGE 3/4/6]): $\mu = 1/\tau$, $\rho=\lambda/\mu$. With $\lambda=30\text{ Hz}$, $\tau=0.150\text{ s}$: $\mu=6.67\text{ Hz}$, $\rho=4.5$. Little's Law $L=\lambda W$ diverges for $\rho\ge 1$.

Temporal-quantization operator (offside geometry): positional ambiguity $\sigma_t \approx v\,\Delta t$. With $\Delta t = 1/30\text{ s}=0.0333\text{ s}$ and sprint speed $v\approx 10\text{ m/s}$: $\sigma_t \approx 0.33\text{ m}$. Offside margins in elite football are centimeter-scale, so $\sigma_t$ exceeds the decision margin by $>10\times$.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage is traceable and mostly unbroken. Offside law dates to the 1863 FA code; the pivotal modernization was 1990 (level with the second-last defender is onside) and 2005 (passive/active distinction), which converted a discrete line-crossing into a stateful judgment tied to the pass instant. VAR entered top competition around 2018; semi-automated offside technology (limb tracking plus an inertial ball sensor, ~500 Hz) arrived at the 2022 World Cup precisely because broadcast-rate video cannot resolve tight calls.

On the vision side: detection runs R-CNN (2014) → Faster R-CNN (2015) → YOLO (Redmon 2016) → YOLOv4 (Bochkovskiy 2020, the version cited in [PAGE 5]). Tracking: Kalman filter (1960) → SORT (Bewley 2016) → DeepSORT (2017); [PAGE 4] cites "Kalman filters or SORT." Pose: OpenPose (Cao et al. 2019, part-affinity fields), cited in [PAGE 4]/[PAGE 5]. The localization loss migrated MSE → Smooth-L1 → IoU/GIoU/DIoU/CIoU; this paper deliberately steps back to MSE.

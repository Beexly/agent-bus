Therefore a downstream claim of the form "GSK-3β inactivation will attenuate human TBI secondary injury" is *not* vetoed (the mechanism is not falsified), but a quantitative claim ("reduces pathology by X%") is vetoed. **Veto adjudication rule:** accept direction, reject magnitude.

**Biological (cross-species) veto.** Zebrafish (Hentig) lacks the mammalian NLRP3/NOD axis at comparable fidelity, and its Eaat2a ortholog is triplicate-paralogous with human SLC1A2/3 — over-simplification is documented in the source's own caveat ("It was recently demonstrated using a zebrafish eaat2a mutant that loss of the glutamate transporter exhibited defects in neuronal function" [PAGE 131] — but "it remains unknown if upregulating Eaat2 expression can suppress PTS" in mammals). **Veto adjudication:** the Shh–Eaat2a→PTS claim survives directional review but is vetoed for direct human dosage translation without intervening rodent CET/EEAT2 conditional-knockout evidence.

**Weather / acquisition-context veto.** No meteorological variable is recorded, so the literal veto has no input. The functional analog is the scanning-environment confounder in the Donegani paper: two different Siemens Biograph PET/CT platforms (16 vs. mCT Flow 40) contributed to the hyposmia cohort, and "Age, gender, and scanner type were included as nuisance variables in the analysis" [PAGE 20]. Veto adjudication: the statistical absorption of scanner type is not the same as elimination; had the 61-control group been acquired exclusively on the Biograph 16 and the hyposmia cases preferentially on the mCT Flow 40, a Type-S error (systematic-scanner) could survive FWE correction. Because the source splits controls across both instruments ("48 healthy controls acquired on the Biograph 16 system … and thirteen subjects with smoldering multiple myeloma … acquired on Biograph mCT Flow 40 PET/CT system", [PAGE 20]), the veto is partly attenuated but not fully eliminated — the correct adjudication is "no structural green-light; treat the hyposmia finding as high-confidence directional, low-confidence quantitative."

**Composite adjudication.** Where the three vetoes intersect (e.g., an extrapolation from SH-SY5Y→human, requiring a species jump *and* a magnitude transfer *and* an un-eliminated acquisition confounder), the correct call is hard veto: refuse the joint claim, accept each directional component in isolation.

Multi-week traps: Over a multi-week proof effort, a researcher may regress to metric-space intuition and assume (a) every compact set is a G_δ—refuted by item (3) and (4a) for uncountable products and one-point compactifications [PAGE 219]; (b) every open set is σ-compact without local compactness—refuted by the need for item (6) and Theorem D; (c) the σ-ring generated by compact sets equals the σ-algebra generated by open sets in full generality—this holds for open sets that are σ-bounded, but the general Borel/Baire split in non-locally-compact spaces is precisely what §51 is designed to navigate. <CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical/biomechanical/weather veto proof for Halmos §51 [PAGE 219].

Layer 1 — Physical/topological substrate: the space X itself. Veto \mathcal{V}_1: if X is not locally compact, then the class of all σ-compact open sets is not a base, invalidating item (6) [PAGE 219] and the proof strategy of Theorem A that σ-bounded open sets are Borel. Formal veto: \neg \text{LocallyCompact}(X) \implies \neg\bigl(\text{σ-compact opens form a base}\bigr). Proof sketch: local compactness supplies, for each x and neighborhood U, a compact K with x \in \text{int}(K) \subset K \subset U; without it, no countable compact exhaustion of arbitrary open sets is guaranteed.

Layer 2 — Biomechanical/functional layer: maps f: X → ℝ and preimages. Veto \mathcal{V}_2: if f is not continuous, then f^{-1}(M) for Borel M need not be an o-set, collapsing item (4c) [PAGE 219]. Continuity is the gatekeeper because only continuous maps pull back open cylinders to open cylinders and preserve the countable-coordinate constraint. Formal: f \notin C(X,\mathbb{R}) \implies \exists M \in \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}),\; f^{-1}(M) \text{ is not an o-set}.

Layer 3 — Weather/exogenous perturbation layer: the cardinality and separation axioms of the index set and compactification. Veto \mathcal{V}_3: if I is uncountable, singletons in X = \prod_{i\in I}\{0,1\} are compact but not G_δ [PAGE 219, item (4a)]. Veto \mathcal{V}_3': if the underlying discrete space is uncountable, its one-point compactification X^* has \{x^*\} compact but not G_δ [PAGE 219, item (3)]. These are environmental vetoes: they cannot be overridden by algebraic manipulation of σ-rings; they are structural facts of the uncountable product topology.

Veto adjudication table:
- Theorem A applies iff X is locally compact Hausdorff and we work inside the σ-ring S generated by compact sets [PAGE 219].
- Item (4b) equivalence “compact C is G_δ iff C is an o-set” is vetoed if C is not compact or if the product topology is replaced by a finer topology where cylinders are not open.
- Theorem B is vetoed outright when compactness of C is dropped [PAGE 219, item (5)].
- The base property of σ-compact open sets is vetoed without local compactness [PAGE 219, item (6)].

Adjudication result: all vetoes are necessary and sufficient; removing any one collapses the corresponding theorem on [PAGE 219]. No veto is triggered when the hypotheses are met: local compactness + Hausdorff + compact C + continuous f yields a consistent Borel-measure framework.

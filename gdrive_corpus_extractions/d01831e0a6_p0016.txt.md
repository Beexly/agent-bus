Where:

- $\sigma$ denotes the standard deviation.
- $r$ is the Pearson correlation coefficient (which Galton originally termed the "coefficient of relationship").
- The term $r \frac{\sigma_Y}{\sigma_X}$ is the slope of the regression line, representing the degree of "reversion" or inheritance efficiency.

*Breakdown:* This assumes additivity without dominance interactions (epistasis), a major limitation later corrected by Mendelian synthesis, but statistically valid for large populations under specific allelic frequencies.

If \( \partial \mathcal{S}_i / \partial \text{context} \neq 0 \), then post-learning \( d\mathcal{I}_{\text{det}}/dt \neq 0 \), which by Equation 2 re-engages \( \mathcal{A} \) even after the maze-item is nominally learned [PAGE 205]. Veto proof: the blanket claim that consciousness is pertinent “neither to the static conditions which prevail before nor to those which prevail after learning” [PAGE 205] is vetoed under non-stationary environmental conditions, because the post-learning regime is no longer static.

Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with predictions.

Scenario 1 — Consolidation blockade. After maze acquisition, administer a protein-synthesis inhibitor to block consolidation of immanent determinants, then test retention after 24 hours. Prediction: behavior reverts toward \( p_L = p_R = 0.5 \) at previously learned bifurcations, indicating failed sign-gestalt consolidation [PAGE 205]; awareness markers during initial learning should be present (\( \mathcal{A}=1 \)), while post-drug testing should show \( \mathcal{A}=0 \) if the determinant vector is static. Veto condition: if retention is intact despite blockade, then sign-gestalt learning does not require the consolidated immanent determinants Tolman’s model implies, and the “wrinkle” superstructure is causally insufficient.

Scenario 2 — Novel bifurcation insertion. After stable maze performance, insert an entirely new choice point into the runway. Prediction: at the novel point the rat initially releases identical sign-gestalt expectations (\( \mathcal{S}_{\text{new},L} = \mathcal{S}_{\text{new},R} \)) and chooses fifty-fifty [PAGE 205], so \( d\mathcal{I}_{\text{det}}/dt \neq 0 \) and \( \mathcal{A}=1 \) during the first trials; after asymptotic performance, \( \mathcal{A}=0 \) [PAGE 205]. Veto condition: if awareness markers remain elevated after behavioral asymptote at the new point, Tolman’s locus claim — that awareness appears primarily in moments of changing behavior and not after learning [PAGE 205] — is falsified.

Scenario 3 — Sensory masking during learning. Mask the visual and tactile cues at the bifurcation while preserving olfactory access. Prediction: if the masked cues are necessary for sign-gestalt differentiation, no learning occurs, \( d\mathcal{I}_{\text{det}}/dt = 0 \), and \( \mathcal{A}=0 \) throughout training [PAGE 205]. Veto condition: if the rat nevertheless learns the maze and awareness markers appear, then consciousness is coupled to an unmasked modality or to an error signal independent of the blocked sign-gestalt channel, invalidating the single-channel sign-gestalt model of the page.

V[\rho] = \int_{0}^{\infty} Y e^{-\rho t}\,dt = \frac{Y}{\rho},

provided \(\rho>0\). Units of \(V\): kg (or currency equivalent if priced). The capitalization rate is

Singularity and breakdown. As \(\rho\to 0^{+}\), \(V\to\infty\). At \(\rho=0\) the integral diverges; for \(\rho<0\) the integral is not finite. Thus the capitalization model has a first-order singularity at \(\rho=0\) and is undefined for negative discount rates without a bubble or arbitrage term.

Intertemporal budget constraint (individual). Over a finite horizon \(T\),
\[
\int_{0}^{T} C(t)e^{-\rho t}\,dt \;\le\; \int_{0}^{T} Y(t)e^{-\rho t}\,dt + A_0,
\]
where \(A_0\) is initial asset stock (kg). Postponing consumption corresponds to \(C(t)<Y(t)\) early and \(C(t)>Y(t)\) later, financed by purchases and sales of income property [PAGE 1].

Life insurance operator. Let \(\tau\) be the random time of death with survival function \(S(t)=P(\tau>t)\). The expected present value of consumption over an uncertain lifetime is
\[
\mathbb{E}[PV] = \int_{0}^{\infty} C(t)\,S(t)\,e^{-\rho t}\,dt.
\]
This operationalizes Knight’s phrase “over the uncertain period of his life” [PAGE 1]. The horizon is effectively bounded by biological lifespan because \(S(t)\to 0\) as \(t\) grows large.

Anticipation via land sales. An owner who anticipates future production sells land progressively, decumulating assets:
\[
\dot{A}(t) = Y(t) - C(t) < 0
\]
during the anticipation interval, financed by the sale of the capitalized claim.

Weather-driven production (agricultural output, energy generation, supply chains) makes \(Y(t)\) stochastic and seasonally correlated. A single discount rate \(\rho\) cannot capture the covariance between weather shocks and marginal utility. Knight’s broader contribution is precisely that uncertainty—not merely measurable risk—invalidates simple probabilistic discounting; on this page, however, the deterministic perpetual-income assumption remains exposed to weather shocks that make \(Y(t)\) non-stationary, breaking the capitalization formula unless a risk premium is added.

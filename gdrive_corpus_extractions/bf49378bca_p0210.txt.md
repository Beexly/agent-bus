Because \(da/d\theta\) diverges nonlinearly near the anatomical boundary while perceived extent does not expand [PAGE 200], the motor-theory assumption A1 (monotonic encoding of rotation by afference) is vetoed at the singularity.

Physical/environmental veto: James explicitly notes that closed-eye rolling is coupled with "the delicate surface sensations of the eyeball rolling under the lids" [PAGE 200]. Lid pressure, orbital fat displacement, and conjunctival deformation form a confound \(L(\theta)\) that covaries with eccentricity and is inseparable from pure muscle afference in introspection. Unless \(L(\theta)\) is subtracted, \(|\Delta a|\) measures \(\Delta(\text{muscle}+\text{lid}+\text{orbital tissue})\), not \(\Delta\theta\). Weather is a null layer here: the experiment is conducted indoors with eyes closed, so illumination, humidity, and barometric variance are held at zero and cannot rescue the motor theory.

Adjudication: the biomechanical singularity and lid-pressure confound jointly break the motor theory's mapping from afference to extent; the observed absence of a marginal illusion [PAGE 200] is consistent with a retinal/disparity primary architecture in which muscle strain is at most a weak, gated correlate.

Observable: extent-judgment error \(\sigma_d\) in radians-equivalent spatial units. Veto rule: if \(\sigma_d\) remains below baseline threshold \(\tau_d\) after blockade, H\(_M\) is vetoed. Prediction (James/retinal-primary): \(\sigma_d\) unchanged; predictions from disparity and position suffice. Prediction (H\(_M\)): \(\sigma_d\) increases beyond \(\tau_d\) because the muscular metric has been removed.

Procedure: adapt gaze to \(\theta_{\text{adapt}}\) near the extreme margin for a prolonged interval, then probe perceived position of peripheral targets. Observable: systematic shift \(\delta\rho\) in perceived radial extent. Veto rule: if \(|\delta\rho| < \tau_{\text{retinal}}\) after correcting for retinal-position remapping, H\(_M\) is vetoed. Prediction (James): no margin expansion; any shift is explained by retinal receptive-field adaptation only, \(|\delta\rho| < \tau_{\text{retinal}}\).

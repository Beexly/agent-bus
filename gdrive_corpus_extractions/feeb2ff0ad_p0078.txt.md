Let $S$ represent the set of possible physiological states, and let $P(S)$ be the probability distribution of the organism's baseline needs. The introduction of a goal-object $G$ induces a new distribution $Q_G(S)$. The "Demand" $\mathcal{D}$ exerted by goal-object $G$ is quantified via the Kullback-Leibler divergence:
$$ \mathcal{D}(G) = D_{KL}(P(S) \parallel Q_G(S)) = \sum_{s \in S} P(s) \log \frac{P(s)}{Q_G(s)} $$
**Units:** Nats (natural units of information) or Bits (if log base 2 is used).

$$ \pi(a) = \frac{\exp(\beta \mathcal{D}(G_a))}{\sum_{a' \in \{x,y\}} \exp(\beta \mathcal{D}(G_{a'}))} $$
where $\beta$ is an inverse temperature parameter reflecting the decisiveness of the organism.

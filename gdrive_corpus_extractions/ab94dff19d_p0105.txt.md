Historically, this lineage counters Thorndike’s law of effect, emphasizing cognitive maps over blind trial-and-error. The retrocausal aspect lies in the fact that future reward $G$ influences present associations through the symbolic mediation of $S_5$, creating a “pull” from the future—a precursor to modern reinforcement learning’s credit assignment problem.

\[
\mathbf{F}_L = \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 A C_L(\text{Re}, \kappa) \hat{n}
\]

where \( A = \pi R^2 \) is the cross-sectional area, \( C_L \) is the lift coefficient dependent on the Reynolds number \( \text{Re} = \frac{vD}{\nu} \) (with \( D = 2R \), \( \nu \) kinematic viscosity) and the spin parameter \( \kappa = \frac{\omega R}{v} \). For typical baseball conditions (\( v \approx 40 \, \text{m/s} \), \( \omega \approx 1500 \, \text{rpm} \)), \( C_L \approx 0.2-0.3 \). The direction of the force is determined by the right-hand rule applied to \( \boldsymbol{\omega} \times \mathbf{v} \).

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Layer P — Physical / hemodynamic. VETO CONFIRMED.**

Claim under test: *"blood flow can be increased to one's heart by over three hundred times the normal healthy rate"* [PAGE 45].

*Proof.* Coronary perfusion is a strict sub-stream of cardiac output: $Q_c(t) \le Q_{CO}(t)$ for all $t$. Baselines: $Q_{CO,0} = 5$ L·min⁻¹, $Q_{c,0} \approx 0.25$ L·min⁻¹ ($f_{c,0} \approx 0.045$). The multiplier is $\Lambda = (Q_{CO}/Q_{CO,0})\cdot(f_c/f_{c,0})$. Maximal sympathetic drive in a trained individual gives $Q_{CO,\max}/Q_{CO,0} \le 6$ and, under full vasodilation, $f_{c,\max} \le 0.12$. Therefore $\Lambda_{\max} \le 6 \times (0.12/0.045) \approx 16$. The asserted $\Lambda = 300$ requires $Q_c = 75$ L·min⁻¹, exceeding maximal cardiac output by $\ge 2.5\times$ and resting output by $15\times$. **Contradiction is structural, not quantitative.** The claim is off by a factor $\ge 17$ and the true statement is $\Lambda \le 16$.

The supporting assertion at [PAGE 46] — *"blood flow can be increased... preexisting conditions such as hypertension or irregular heartbeats can help trigger dangerous cardiac events"* — **survives**, because it is a conditional-risk statement with no multiplier attached.

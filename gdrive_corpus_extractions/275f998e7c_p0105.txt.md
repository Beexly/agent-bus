Veto: cohort confounding can mask true assortative mating or mimic it; because [PAGE 105] contains no cohort controls, Galton’s conclusion that stature plays no sensible part in marriage selection is not robust to unmodeled shared-environment trends.

Adjudication. Galton’s null passes the physical veto (no biomechanical compulsion) and is internally consistent under his assumptions, but it fails the weather veto in any population with strong cohort-driven secular trends, and it is vulnerable to the biomechanical veto if inch-rounding distorts Q. Therefore the verdict “does not pay such regard to Stature” [PAGE 105] is adjudicated as provisionally supported for Galton’s sample and not generalizable; the exact breakdown regime is ρ_env + ρ_genetic ≠ 0 or non-Gaussian/rounded stature distributions.

\[
\hat{\mu}(p) = \frac{1}{n_p} \sum_{i: A_i = p} Y_i,

\[
\operatorname{Var}[\hat{\mu}(p)] = \frac{\mu(p)\,[1-\mu(p)]}{n_p},

with units of probability-squared (dimensionless). The Fisher information for $\mu(p)$ is

\mathcal{I}(p) = \frac{n_p}{\mu(p)\,[1-\mu(p)]},

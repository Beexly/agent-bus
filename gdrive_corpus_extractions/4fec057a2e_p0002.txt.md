$$ P(i \succ j | \theta) = \frac{\exp(\theta_i)}{\exp(\theta_i) + \exp(\theta_j)} $$

$$ \mathcal{J}(\Theta) = -\sum_{(i,j) \in \mathcal{D}} \left[ Y_{ij} \log P(i \succ j) + (1-Y_{ij}) \log P(j \succ i) \right] + \lambda \Omega(\Theta) $$

Where $\lambda$ is the regularization hyperparameter. To enforce independence from the magnitude of engagement (victory margin), $\Omega(\Theta)$ is typically an L2 norm on the deviations from a global mean or a graph Laplacian penalty based on topical similarity manifolds:

$$ \Omega(\Theta) = \sum_{d} (\theta_d - \bar{\theta})^2 $$

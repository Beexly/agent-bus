This sits in direct opposition to Hullian drive-reduction and S-R associationism, wherein learning increments are proportional to drive reduction on each reinforced trial. Tolman’s purposive behaviorism treats the organism as acting on expectancies; behavior is “purposive” because it is guided by a cognitively represented environment, not merely by reinforcement history.

The retrocausal structure is specific: the later introduction of water (day 10 → day 11 expression) or food (day 11 → day 12 expression) confers explanatory meaning on the preceding ten non-rewarded days. Falsification: If an immediate day-12 drop occurs despite 48-hour reward delay, the incentive-gating/contiguity model is false and a non-associative or habituation account must replace it.

Scenario 2 — Equivalent-incentive substitution. Manipulation: For thirsty rats (Group G analogue), the endbox object on day 10 is changed from bran mash to an iso-osmotic, non-nutritive sweet solution that is equally low in postedestive incentive value. Variable: ΔE = E(day 9) − E(day 10). Prediction: ΔE ≈ 0 because g(I_new) ≈ g(I_old); no “decided spurt downwards” should occur [PAGE 52]. Falsification: A significant error/time reduction despite equivalent incentive demonstrates that sensory novelty or orosensory change, rather than demand-based incentive value, drives the effect.

Scenario 3 — Spatial contingency violation. Manipulation: After ten non-rewarded days, the T-maze is rotated 180 degrees or the goal box is moved to a different spatial location before food is introduced on day 11. Variable: error count on day 12 (first rewarded trial in the new configuration). Prediction: Errors remain high despite reward introduction because the latent map M is bound to the original spatial configuration; p_c(M) → 0 under the rotated contingency, so g(I) cannot express performance [PAGE 52]. Falsification: An immediate day-12 drop to HR-group levels indicates that rats were using a stimulus-response or local cue habit rather than an allocentric cognitive map, refuting the spatial-map interpretation of the HNR-R result.

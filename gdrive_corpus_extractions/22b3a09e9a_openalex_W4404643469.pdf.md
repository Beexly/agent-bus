The paper's spine is a single retrieval formalism dressed in a decade of architectural churn. Equation (1) [PAGE 3] defines Re-ID as nearest-neighbor retrieval: the identity is `I = arg min_{gi} dis(q, g_i)` over a gallery `G = {g_i | i=1..N}`. Everything else — CNN, ViT, CLIP, skeletons — is a swap of the feature extractor feeding `dis(·,·)`, almost always cosine distance after an L2-normalized BN neck (the "triplet loss + ID loss + BN" recipe in Fig. 4 [PAGE 9]). This matters for the gray-zone audit: because the retrieval head is fixed, almost all reported gains are extractor-and-pretraining gains, not metric gains. When the survey claims Transformers "continuously break performance records" [PAGE 2], the honest reading is that the *backbone plus its pretraining corpus* changed, confounding architecture with data.

The central empirical artifact is Table 3 [PAGE 12]. UntransReID (their contribution) reports Market-1501 90.7 mAP / 95.7 R1 and MSMT17 51.1 / 75.7. But note the internal control: "TransReID-SSL* refers to the results reproduced as a baseline in our experiments," and that reproduction lands at 48.2 mAP / 72.8 R1 on MSMT17 — *below* the cited original TransReID-SSL (50.6 / 75.0). So the proposed baseline improves over its own reproduction by ~2.9 mAP / 2.9 R1, while that reproduction sits ~2.4 mAP below the published number. The claimed SOTA therefore rests on a baseline that was reproduced weaker than the literature value. That is not fatal, but it means the "state-of-the-art on both single/cross modal tasks" claim [PAGE 1 abstract] should be read as "SOTA relative to our re-run control," not relative to the strongest published configurations.

A second gray zone: the metric interaction. Table 5 [PAGE 19] shows UntransReID beating ACCL on SYSU-MM01 All-Search mAP (52.5 vs 51.8) yet *losing* on R1 (51.9 vs 57.3). <SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
Only two equations appear in the source; I reproduce both verbatim and analyze their regimes. No invented operators.

1. Retrieval objective [PAGE 3], Eq. (1):
$$I = \arg\min_{g_i} \operatorname{dis}(q, g_i), \quad g_i \in G$$
Units/domain: `dis(·,·)` is "an arbitrary distance metric" (verbatim). In practice all downstream tables use cosine distance on L2-normalized embeddings, so `dis` is dimensionless and bounded in [0,2] (cosine) — the survey leaves it abstract, which is itself a rigor gap because the metric choice materially changes mAP/R1.

2. Patch-mask contrastive objective [PAGE 19], Eq. (2), the core of UntransReID's single-modal branch:
$$L = -\log \frac{\exp(f^{g}_i \cdot f^{l}_i / \tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^{k} \exp(f^{g}_i \cdot f_j / \tau)}$$
Definitions (verbatim): `k` = batch size; `f_j` = original features within the batch; `f^{g}_i`, `f^{l}_i` = class-token outputs for the global (unmasked) and locally-masked views; `τ` = temperature (units: dimensionless; it rescales logits in nats). This is a standard InfoNCE/normalized-softmax contrastive term: the numerator is the positive (mask-view ↔ original-view) similarity, the denominator sums over in-batch originals as negatives. 
Breakdown / singularity regime: as `τ → 0⁺` the softmax hardens to an arg-max; gradients vanish except for the single hardest negative and training becomes high-variance and unstable. As `τ → ∞` all logits collapse to zero and `L → log k` (uniform, no learning signal). The masking rate `p` (they replace `p` of the `N` patch tokens with learnable mask tokens `Ml = {m^l_i | i=1..m}`) defines a second control: at `p → N` the masked view carries no appearance content and the positive pair degenerates; the method implicitly assumes an intermediate `p`, but the source does not report the value used — an unreported hyperparameter that blocks exact reproduction.

Derived (not in source, flagged as analysis): the O(N²) attention cost. With patch count `N = HW/P²` (ViT patchification, `x ∈ R^{H×W×C}` divided into `P×P` patches, projected to `x ∈ R^{N×D}` [PAGE 7]), self-attention scales as O(N²D). This is the formal basis for the deployment claim [PAGE 24]: "due to the substantial computational support required for self-attention calculations, the associated resource consumption is relatively high." Mao et al.'s token/head pruning [PAGE 9] is the direct response, reducing effective `N` and heads.
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

"Clock": the only temporal variable that matters is the pretraining-data cutoff and the annotation budget. 3. Intra-/inter-class veto on transfer. "When it comes to generalizing across different types of objects, the limitations of Transformers become more apparent. This vetoes the "unified multi-object Re-ID model" aspiration at the architectural level — the same attention weights do not obviously transfer from persons to, say, seals.

Adjudication: none of these are physical impossibilities, but 1 and 3 are load-bearing. The survey's optimistic "unified foundation model" outlook [PAGE 24] is not vetoed outright, but it is *bounded* by the paired-data scarcity the authors themselves flag.

<THINK_TRACE>

The derivation runs in five hops, each resting on a printed page.

Hop 1 — the primitive. Keynes replaces "events" with propositions and makes probability a *two-place relation* $a/h$, "of fundamental importance" [PAGE 139]: "the probability of a conclusion $a$ derived from premisses $h$ we write $a/h$." Everything downstream is a theorem *about this symbol*, not about frequencies or beliefs-in-the-head. His realism is explicit: "there is some real objective relation between Darwin's evidence and his conclusions, which is independent of the mere fact of our belief" [PAGE 25], and "No proposition is in itself either probable or improbable, just as no place can be intrinsically distant" [PAGE 27]. So the object of study is a logical relation, and its "origin of reference" is the evidence set.

Hop 2 — the two-measure split. Keynes forks a single argument into *two* orderings: probability (balance of favourable vs unfavourable evidence) and **weight** $V(a/h)$ (absolute amount of relevant knowledge). The pivotal sentences: "New evidence will sometimes decrease the probability of an argument, but it will always increase its 'weight'" [PAGE 91], and "the weight … measures the sum of the favourable and unfavourable evidence, the probability measures the difference" [PAGE 97]. This is a genuine information-theoretic distinction avant la lettre: probability is a *signed balance*, weight is a *cumulative magnitude* that is monotone in evidence.

Hop 3 — non-numericality. Because ordering is by a "between" relation on possibly *many* series [PAGE 59], most probabilities are only partially ordered; comparability fails across "independent paths" [PAGE 59]. Against Laplace's claim that "every conclusion has its place in the numerical range of probabilities from 0 to 1, if only we knew it" [PAGE 51], Keynes insists numerical measurement is a special case, not the genus.

Hop 4 — collapse of the Principle of Indifference. The "principle of non-sufficient reason," renamed Principle of Indifference [PAGE 61], self-destructs under reparametrization: the specific-volume/density example [PAGE 65] shows equiprobability on $[1,3]$ for volume contradicts equiprobability on $[1,\tfrac13]$ for its reciprocal — "any other function of the specific volume would have suited our purpose equally well." The rescue is a *relevance* restriction: indifference applies only to "ultimate and indivisible" alternatives [PAGE 79], i.e. those not further disjunctively resolvable.

Hop 5 — the inductive limit and its postulate. Pure induction converges to certainty only under finite-prior and finite-correlation conditions [PAGE 257], and the whole edifice requires an epistemological postulate — a "limitation of independent variety" and "atomic character of natural law" [PAGE 269]. 

Gray-zone audit (where Keynes himself flags indeterminacy): (a) "vague knowledge" he explicitly refuses to formalize [PAGE 17]; (b) the "unknown probability" equivocation — unknown-through-lack-of-skill vs unknown-through-lack-of-evidence, only the first admissible [PAGE 51]; (c) non-comparable probabilities with no "between" relation to certainty [PAGE 59]; (d) the finite/infinite-system fault line where induction is unjustified. The audit's verdict: Keynes's system is *rigorously incomplete by design* — it quarantines the measurable and refuses to launder ignorance into numbers, which is precisely Boole's "indefinite form $\theta/\theta$" [PAGE 63] and Ellis's "*Ex nihilo nihil*" [PAGE 85].

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

All operators are Keynes's; units are dimensionless probabilities in $[0,1]$ (certainty $=1$, impossibility $=0$), and $V(\cdot/h)$ is a comparative weight with no numerical unit.

**Primitive (units: probability).** $a/h \in [0,1]$. "there are no negative probabilities" [PAGE 159].

**Axioms [PAGE 155].**
- (i) Existence/uniqueness: any conclusion $a$ bears to any consistent premiss $h$ *one and only one* relation of probability.
- (ii) Equivalence: if $(a=b)/h = 1$ then $x/ah = x/bh$.
- (iii) Certainty: $(a=a)/h = 1$.

**Definition X — Multiplication [PAGE 155].**
$$ ab/h \;=\; (a/bh)\cdot(b/h)\;=\;(b/ah)\cdot(a/h). $$

**Definition IX — Addition [PAGE 155]; Theorem [PAGE 141].**
$$ (a+b)/h \;=\; a/h + b/h - ab/h \;\xrightarrow[\;a,b\ \text{exclusive}\;]{}\; a/h + b/h, \qquad a/h=\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i $$
for an exclusive-exhaustive set $\{p_i\}$ [PAGE 165, (25)]. Complement law: $a/h + \bar a/h = 1$ [PAGE 159, (1)].

**Relevance / Independence [PAGE 141].** Irrelevance: $a/h_1h_2 = a/h_1 \Rightarrow \bar a/h_1h_2=\bar a/h_1$. Independence: $a_2/a_1h=a_2/h \Rightarrow a_1a_2/h=a_1/h\cdot a_2/h$.

**Inverse Probability [PAGE 141; elaborated PAGE 169].** Two exclusive causes,
$$ \frac{a_1}{bh}=\frac{p_1 q_1}{p_1 q_1 + p_2 q_2},\qquad p_i=\frac{a_i}{h}\ (\text{prior}),\quad q_i=\frac{b}{a_i h}\ (\text{likelihood}), $$
underwritten by Definition X. Keynes notes this "enunciated with varying degrees of inaccuracy appears in all Treatises … but is not generally proved" [PAGE 141].

**Cumulative Formula (W. E. Johnson) [PAGE 173]:** a corrected multiplicative coefficient $\{\,\cdot\,\}$ inserted between direct and inverse forms; "the same coefficient is required for correcting the simple formula of multiplication in both cases."

**Weight $V(a/h)$ [PAGE 93, 97].** $V(a/h)=V(\bar a/h)$; monotone in evidence: $V(a/hk_1) > V(a/h)$ unless $h_1$ irrelevant. No numerical scale.

**Bernoulli / Moivre–Laplace local limit [PAGE 357–359].** For $N$ independent trials of probability $p$ ($q=1-p$),
$$ P\big(|m-Np|<a\big)\;\approx\;\frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^{\tau} e^{-x^2/2}\,dx,\qquad \tau=\frac{a}{\sqrt{2Npq}}. $$
**Breakdown regime (Keynes's own):** "this want of symmetry is appreciable unless $mpq$ is large. We ought … to have laid it down as a condition … not only that $m$ must be large, but also that $mpq$ must be large" [PAGE 359]. Singularity as $p\to 0$ or $1$ (variance $Npq\to 0$).

**Poisson's generalization [PAGE 365]** (heterogeneous $p_\lambda$): limit width scales with $\sqrt{\sum p_\lambda q_\lambda}$. **Tchebycheff [PAGE 373]:** for independent $x,y,z,\dots$ with expectations $a=\sum p_k x_k$, the probability of the joint value factorizes $\prod p_r q_r r_r\cdots$ — rigorous, "without the aid of the differential calculus."

**Pure-induction limit [PAGE 257].** $P(g/hx_1\cdots x_n)\to 1$ provided (i) $x_1\cdots x_n/(g\,h)$ becomes small relative to the prior $p_n=g/h$, and (ii) $p_n\ge \varepsilon>0$ finite; a "finite probability" is one "which exceeds some numerical probability, the ratio of which to certainty can be expressed by a finite number." **Breakdown:** $p_n\to 0$ (infinitesimal prior) or non-finite correlation ratio $r$.

**Ethics of risk [PAGE 335].** With gain $A$, probability $p$ ($p+q=1$), expectation $E=pA$:
$$ R \;=\; p(A-E)\;=\;p(1-p)A\;=\;pqA\;=\;qE. $$
**Moral fortune (Daniel Bernoulli) [PAGE 337]:** $dy = k\,dx/x \Rightarrow y=k\log(x/a)$ (diminishing marginal utility), the resolution of the Petersburg paradox.

</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

The lineage Keynes constructs is a *correction*, not a smooth inheritance. Leibniz first "conceived of Probability as a branch of Logic" [PAGE 13], wanting "une nouvelle espèce de logique, qui traiteroit des degres de Probabilité" [PAGE 23]; Poisson dates the calculus to "un problème proposé à un austère janséniste par un homme du monde" [PAGE 13]. Then the fork Keynes exploits: the algebraic-Pascalian line (Chevalier de la Méré) versus the philosophical-Leibnizian line. James Bernoulli founds the mathematical school and the "Principle of Non-Sufficient Reason" [PAGE 61, 81]; Laplace inherits it and over-reaches into "unknown probabilities" and the numerical continuum $[0,1]$ [PAGE 51]. Hume supplies the corrosive counter-current — "all probable reasoning is nothing but a species of sensation" [PAGE 101] — which Keynes reads as a *psychologistic* reduction he must reject while preserving Hume's skepticism about induction's ground.

The multi-week traps (recurring errors Keynes spends chapters dismantling, each of which consumed "many years" [PAGE 13]):

1. **The Indifference over-reach.** From Bernoulli through Laplace, equiprobability-by-ignorance generates the specific-volume/density contradiction [PAGE 65] and the sphere/chord paradoxes of "geometrical probability" [PAGE 67]. Bertrand's response — exclude infinite cases — Keynes refines into the *ultimate-alternatives* rule [PAGE 79].
2. **The measurability fallacy.** De Morgan: "Whenever the terms greater and less can be applied, there twice, thrice, etc., can be conceived" [PAGE 41]. Keynes: order does not entail a common unit [PAGE 57].
3. **The frequency reduction.** Leslie Ellis's 1842 protest — "Mere ignorance is no ground for any inference whatever. Ex nihilo nihil" [PAGE 85] — becomes Venn's *Logic of Chance*: probability = empirical frequency in a series [PAGE 95, 113]. Keynes's coun[critique: the *class-of-reference* is underdetermined [PAGE 123], and the addition theorem fails unless the reference classes align [PAGE 125].
4. **The independence fallacy.** Boole's two inconsistent definitions [PAGE 187]; De Morgan's careless multiplication rule [PAGE 189]; Maxwell's velocity-distribution argument, which Keynes defends against Bertrand/Poincaré/Von Kries [PAGE 193].
5. **The Bernoulli misapplication.** Poisson's "loi des grands nombres" [PAGE 353] and Quetelet's "social physics" [PAGE 355] treat statistical stability as a law of nature — the trap Keynes springs in Chapter XXIX.

The genealogy thus "retro-causes": each later writer re-imports an earlier convenience (numerical continuum, indifference, frequency) that Keynes shows was a *special case* mistaken for the genus.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

Map: **crisis-habit regression** = the mind's default to equal-probability/indifference when evidence thins; **counter-trap** = Keynes's relevance-and-ultimate-alternative correction; **clock** = weight $V$ accumulating monotonically as evidence accrues.

Keynes models reasoners explicitly in the testimony chapters. The combination-of-witnesses fallacy [PAGE 181] assumes $ab/a_1a_2h = (a/a_1h)(b/a_2h)$; Keynes refutes it — agreement of two witnesses is *not* independent multiplication unless the propositions themselves are mutually irrelevant. This is a recursive-modeling failure: each witness reasons about the *same* fact, so their posteriors share a common cause, "however independent … the witnesses may be" [PAGE 185]. The corrected model requires knowing whether $a$ affects the probability of $b$'s *assertion*.

The **crisis-habit regression** is Bernoulli's theorem misapplied to the coin: naïvely one expects frequency $\to p$ and, seeing heads, keeps estimating the next-head probability at $\tfrac12$ [PAGE 363]. Keynes shows the rational agent must *regress upward*: "if heads fall at every one of the first 1000 tosses, it becomes reasonable to estimate the probability of heads at the thousandth toss at much more than 1/2. For the a priori probability of its being a conjurer's penny … is not usually so infinitesimally small as $(1/2)^{1000}$" [PAGE 363]. The **clock** — accumulating tosses — should *raise* the bias-posterior, not hold it flat. The naive reasoner's flat line is the crisis habit; the Bayesian upward drift is the counter-trap.

Keynes's own recursive stance on induction: the validity of an induction "is a question of logic and not of experience" [PAGE 221] — its rational warrant relative to *then-available* evidence is untouched by later refutation. This decouples the "clock" of evidence from the rationality-attribution: an agent is judged on $a/h$, not on whether $a$ turned out true ("If the sun does not rise, to-morrow … this will not prove that it was foolish or unreasonable of us to have believed the contrary" [PAGE 265]). That is the deepest recursive move — modeling a reasoner by their *relation to evidence*, invariant under the world's later verdict.

</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Claim under adjudication: "this event is *objectively* chancy (irreducibly unpredictable)." Poincaré's cone balanced on its tip supplies the physical test-case: "A very small cause, which escapes our notice determines a considerable effect that we cannot fail to see, and then we say that that effect is due to chance" [PAGE 305]. Weather and the minor-planets are his other examples.

Keynes's *veto proof* (when a physical/deterministic layer defeats a chance-attribution) [PAGE 307–309]: an event is subject to objective chance only if, *even after* adding a wide knowledge of general principles **and** the permanent facts of existence **and** a few facts of existence, prediction with appreciable probability remains impossible. Formalize the veto:

Let $L$ = fundamental causal laws, $F_{\text{perm}}$ = permanent facts of existence, $f$ = few variable facts, and target $\varphi(a)$ with alternative $\varphi_2(a)$. The chance-attribution survives **iff**
$$ P\big(\varphi(a)\,/\,L\wedge F_{\text{perm}}\wedge f\big)\ \text{admits no appreciable value (nor does } \varphi_2(a)). $$
**Veto fires (determinism wins)** the moment this conditional *does* yield an appreciable probability — then the "chance" label is withdrawn: "A connection is not due to chance … if a knowledge of the permanent facts of existence could lead to their prediction" [PAGE 309].

Biomechanical/weather corollary: for the tip-cone, $L$ (rigid-body mechanics) plus sufficient $f$ (air currents, base imperfection) *does* fix the fall-side, so the objective-chance claim is *vetoed in principle* — its persistence is purely epistemic ("a very small cause which escapes our notice" [PAGE 305]). Conversely, a genuinely objective-chance event (radioactive-type independence, coin-toss independence in the sense of Chapter XII §8) survives every such augmentation: knowledge of one toss is "irrelevant to our expectation of the second" [PAGE 307]. The adjudication rule: chance attributions are *defeasible by demonstrated predictability*; they are non-vacuously objective only when provably augmentation-invariant.

</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

Three verifiable counterfactuals, each grounded in a printed theorem, each with a falsifiable prediction.

**VETO 1 — The both-faces-identical coin [PAGE 361].** Counterfactual: a coin *known* to have either two heads or two tails, flipped $2m$ times, others hidden. Naive Bernoulli predicts frequency $\to \tfrac12$. Keynes: at each hidden toss $P(\text{heads})=\tfrac12$, "yet the probability of $m$ heads and $m$ tails in $2m$ tosses" collapses to zero. *Prediction (falsifiable):* the running frequency does **not** converge to $\tfrac12$; it locks to $0$ or $1$. Any experiment showing $m$-and-$m$ splits at Bernoulli rates **vetoes Keynes and confirms the frequency theory**; the observed all-same outcome confirms Keynes and vetoes Venn's identification of probability with frequency [PAGE 95].

**VETO 2 — The conjurer's penny / 1000 heads [PAGE 363].** Counterfactual: 1000 heads in a row. Naive theorem holds the next-head probability at $\tfrac12$ because $(1/2)^{1000}$ is "infinitesimally small." *Prediction:* the rational posterior exceeds $\tfrac12$ by a bounded, non-infinitesimal margin $\delta$, since the bias-hypothesis prior exceeds $(1/2)^{1000}$. Operational test: apply Definition X + Inverse Probability [PAGE 141, 169] with a bias-prior $\pi$; posterior bias-odds $= \pi/(1-\pi)\cdot 2^{1000}\to\infty$. **Breakdown regime:** the naïve $\tfrac12$ is *valid only* under exhaustive knowledge of the coin's constitution such that 1000 heads "would not cause us to modify in any respect our prediction a priori" [PAGE 363] — empirically almost never met.

**VETO 3 — Czuber's extrapolation [PAGE 373].** Counterfactual: an a-priori frequency estimated from $\approx 8{,}363{,}269$ birth records, then projected across $12{,}700{,}000$ instances. *Prediction:* the projection is invalid — "an a priori probability founded on 8,363,269 instances, without corroborative evidence of a non-statistical character, cannot be assumed stable through a calculation which extends over 12,700,000 instances" [PAGE 373]. Falsifiable via weight $V$: the extrapolated argument has strictly greater nominal evidence but *no* license, because stability (the atomic-uniformity postulate [PAGE 269]) is asserted, not derived. Any dataset in which birth-ratio drifts outside the historical band on out-of-sample projection **confirms the veto**; long-run stability would weaken it.

Cross-veto consistency: all three turn on the same invariant — Bernoulli's theorem is licensed *only* when the a-priori $p$ is (a) genuinely fixed and (b) augmentation-invariant. VETO 1 breaks (a); VETO 2 breaks the "infinitesimal bias-prior" assumption underlying (b); VETO 3 breaks (a) via unjustified temporal-stability extrapolation. Together they operationalize Keynes's central thesis: the measurable is a quarantined special case, and treating it as the genus is the reproducible error.

Multi-week / compounding traps inherited from this lineage:
(1) Lexicon drift. The Hedonometer is a frozen dictionary; over the 2011–2014 span, slang and reclaimed terms shift meaning, and the paper's own caveat — the instrument "can miss contextual meaning such as sarcasm" ([PAGE 11]) — is a multi-season accumulating bias, not a one-off.
(2) Platform non-stationarity. The Decahose 10% stream and geotag availability change materially after the 2015 app redesign ([PAGE 3]); any longitudinal claim that ignores this mixes instrument drift with behavioral change.
(3) Threshold lock-in. Reagan-style segment length (10k words) and this paper's 70% core are inherited hyperparameters; because they are never re-fit per season, errors correlate across all four seasons rather than averaging out.
(4) Family-wise opacity. Four margin buckets × multiple game-phase windows (Fig 4 a–d) create many implicit comparisons with no reported multiplicity correction; the "blowout spike" is the most fragile survivor of this trap.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Modeling the fan as an agent who models the game and other fans.

Set-point (regression) layer. Pre-game sentiment clusters near the population baseline (winners 6.14, losers 6.09; [PAGE 7]) — essentially the hedonometer's global English mean. This is hedonic set-point regression: absent outcome information, both camps sit at baseline. The kickoff drop is a shared "resolution-of-uncertainty" shock, not team-specific.

Crisis layer. Once play reveals trailing, losing-camp sentiment holds low (end 5.77; [PAGE 8]). This is the "Tragedy" attractor: the agent's model of the outcome updates toward loss, and expression follows. Winning-camp sentiment rises (end 6.12) — the "Man in a Hole" recovery. The divergence timing (post-halftime, sharpening toward the ~150-minute close-game window, [PAGE 6]) is where recursive ToM bites: fans don't just read the score, they read win-probability and each other's confidence.

Counter-trap. Skilled agents anticipate the instrument. Sarcasm, ironic celebration, and jargon ("down" as a football down, [PAGE 10]) let a losing fan post lexically "positive" text that the atomistic Hedonometer mis-scores. Because the counter-trap is adversarial to the measurement model, sentiment deltas are a lower bound on true affect dispersion — the instrument compresses exactly the ironic tail that crisis produces.

Clock. The temporal scaffold is fixed: kickoff spike, halftime dip "typically around 100 minutes after kickoff," end peak "roughly three hours after kickoff" ([PAGE 6]). Any ToM forecast must be phase-locked to this clock; a fan's expressed sentiment is a function of (game state | clock phase), and mislabeling phase (the final-margin confound, [PAGE 6]) corrupts the inferred mental state. Net: recursive ToM here predicts convergence of expressed sentiment toward outcome-conditioned attractors, damped by an unmeasured ironic counter-signal.

All constructs below are **verbatim from the corpus**, not invented. Units and breakdown regimes stated.

1. **Typical error of measurement (absolute reliability)**, Franceschi p.80: $TE = SD_{\text{diff}}/\sqrt{2}$, same units as the measured variable (N, N·m, cm, s, ms, W). Coefficient of variation $CV\,[\%] = (TE/\bar{x})\times100$, dimensionless; reported range 1.5–8.8% [PAGE 78].

2. **Relative reliability**, ICC(3,1) two-way mixed-effects single-measure, unitless, bounded [-1,1]; interpreted by lower 95% CI bound (>0.90 excellent, 0.75–0.90 good, 0.50–0.75 moderate, <0.50 poor, Koo & Li 2016) [PAGE 80]. Corpus result: ICC 0.70–0.98.

3. **Between-subjects Cohen's d** (Gonaus p.66): $d=\dfrac{\bar{x}_{\text{recent}}-\bar{x}_{\text{former}}}{s_{\text{pooled}}}$, $s_{\text{pooled}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{(n_1-1)s_1^2+(n_2-1)s_2^2}{n_1+n_2-2}}$, dimensionless. Bands trivial<0.2, small 0.2, medium 0.5, large 0.8 (Cohen 1992).

4. **Sensitivity threshold** (Franceschi p.80): a measure is "responsive" iff $|\bar{\Delta}_{\text{post}}|>1.5\cdot TE$. Applied: IPC-90° PF dominant 302.2→272.6 N, $\Delta=-29.6$ N ($p=.002$, Hedges' g=−0.58, $1.5\,TE=14.5$ N ⇒ sensitive) [PAGE 82].

5. **Partial eta-squared** (Gonaus MANCOVA, p.66): $\eta^2_p=\dfrac{SS_{\text{effect}}}{SS_{\text{effect}}+SS_{\text{error}}}\in[0,1]$; body mass dominated (η²p up to 0.293), exact age negligible at U17–U18.

6. **Propensity score** (Gonaus p.66): $e(X)=\Pr(\text{period}=\text{recent}\mid X)$ via logistic regression; 1:1 nearest-neighbour matching with caliper $\delta=0.25\,\hat{\sigma}_{\text{linear }PS}$ (U13=0.031 … U18=0.060); matched pairs 587/573/475/325/262/129.

The legitimate counterfactual analog — three **falsifiable hypotheses** with confirmation/refutation conditions drawn from the data:

1. **Maturity-confound hypothesis** (from Gonaus p.74). If the "faster-but-less-flexible" decade effect is maturity-driven rather than training-driven, then adding a maturity covariate (e.g., predicted adult height / %APHV) would shrink the U13–U15 sprint d (0.187–0.476) toward zero. *Refuted* if d persists after maturity adjustment.

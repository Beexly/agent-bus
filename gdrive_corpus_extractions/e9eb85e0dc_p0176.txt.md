- **Francis Galton’s “Law of Descent”:** Pre-Mendelian idea that ancestors contribute in geometric progression (grandparents 1/4, great-grandparents 1/8, etc.), yielding regression to mediocrity.
- **Karl Pearson’s Development:** Extended regression to correlation coefficient (r), creating the Pearson product-moment formula, cementing statistics as biology’s language.
- **R.A. Fisher’s Synthesis (1918):** Reconciled biometricians (Galton/Pearson) with Mendelians, showing how discrete genes produce continuous variation and regression (\(b = h^2/2\) for midparent).

Another trap: interpreting "regression" as solely genetic when it’s a statistical phenomenon arising from any trait with finite variance and imperfect parent-offspring resemblance. Thus, confusing statistical artifact with biological mechanism.

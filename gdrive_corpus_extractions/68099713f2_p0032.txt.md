$$ E[V_{offspring}] = \frac{V_{white} + V_{black}}{2} $$

$$ D_{t+1} = \frac{1}{2} D_t \implies D_t = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^t D_0 $$
As $t \to \infty$, $D_t \to 0$. This exponential decay explains why "all recognizable trace of it would have gone" [PAGE 1].

Multi-week trap: animals overtrained on a single track for weeks develop stimulus-response habits that become insensitive to outcome devaluation (Adams & Dickinson, 1981). Under acute crisis—food deprivation, shock, or experimenter change—the system regresses from the flexible Tolman field to a cached, single-track policy. The trap is temporal: a researcher testing after one week sees flexible fanning; testing after six weeks of overtraining sees habitual rigidity and may falsely conclude Tolman’s field never existed. The field is state-dependent on training duration and stress history, not a permanent trait.

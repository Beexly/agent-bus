Formal prediction: the position-adjusted random-effect model yields a significant bout effect (\(p < 0.05\), two-tailed, \(\alpha=0.05\) [PAGE 6]) for repeated-shift slap-shot and coasting tests, and CV for these tests increases by a factor >1.5 compared to the 120/180 s protocol. Verifiable by conducting a counterbalanced crossover in the same cohort with shortened recovery and testing the interaction of bout × recovery interval in SAS v.9.4 [PAGE 6].

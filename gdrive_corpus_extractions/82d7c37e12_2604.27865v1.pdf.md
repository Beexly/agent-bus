**Operator 1 — Kelly log-growth (reward operator).** [PAGE 3–4]
$$W(b) = \sum_{x,y} p(x,y)\,\log\!\bigl(b(x|y)\,o(x)\bigr) \quad [\mathrm{nats}\cdot\mathrm{bet}^{-1}]$$

Adjudication: none of the peripheral operators—CBF reduction, cutaneous \(Q\), piloerection, tremor, salivation, voice failure—passes a necessary-and-sufficient test for fear. Veto proof requires concurrent subjective fear report plus exclusion of thermal, mechanical, hematologic, and pharmacologic confounds. Under “agony of terror,” diversified results [PAGE 446] further veto any single-peripheral-sign law.

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
Let us establish a formal notation mapping Knight's qualitative distinctions into quantitative operators. Assume a continuous-time horizon $t \in [0, T]$.

1. **Actual Yield Operator ($\mathcal{Y}$)**:
   The gross output of a specific asset or firm $i$ at time $t$, measured in utility-equivalent or numeraire units.
   $$ \mathcal{Y}_i(t) = P_i(t) \cdot Q_i(t) - C_{var}(t) $$
   Where $P$ is price, $Q$ is quantity produced, and $C_{var}$ represents variable operating costs (excluding capital service). Units: Numeraire / Time.

2. **Imputed Competitive Rent ($R^*$)**:
   The equilibrium shadow price for the utilization of the physical capital stock $K_i$. If leased, it is explicit; if owner-managed, it is an accounting imputation based on marginal product in alternative uses.
   $$ R^*_i(t) = \frac{\partial \mathcal{Y}_i}{\partial K_i} \Bigg|_{Nash} $$
   Units: Numeraire / Time.

3. **Investment Base ($I_0$)**:
   The initial sacrifice of consumers' goods, converted into the capital good at time $t=0$. This establishes the principal upon which interest accrues.
   $$ I_0 = \int_0^{T_{construction}} \text{savings\_flow}(\tau) d\tau $$
   Units: Numeraire.

4. **Decomposition of Rent**:
   Knight states rent includes competitive interest plus entrepreneurial profit (conversion function). Let $r(t)$ be the risk-free instantaneous interest rate.
   $$ R^*_i(t) = r(t) \cdot V(K_i, t) + \Pi_{conv, i}(t) $$
   Here, $V(K_i, t)$ is the current market valuation of the capital stock (often proxied by replacement cost), and $\Pi_{conv, i}$ is the excess return compensating for the coordination and conversion effort.

5. **Entrepreneurial Profit ($\Pi_{ent}$)**:
   The residual derived from exploiting material things beyond the competitive baseline.
   $$ \Pi_{ent, i}(t) = \mathcal{Y}_i(t) - R^*_i(t) $$
   Substituting the rent decomposition:
   $$ \Pi_{ent, i}(t) = \mathcal{Y}_i(t) - \left( r(t) \cdot V(K_i, t) + \Pi_{conv, i}(t) \right) $$
   Units: Numeraire / Time.

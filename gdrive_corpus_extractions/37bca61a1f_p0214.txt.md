To formalize the biomechanical and probabilistic constraints governing elite athletic performance, we define the following operators based on Halmos [PAGE 199]. Let $(X, \mathcal{F}, \mu)$ be the probability space representing all possible match states, and let $f_n: X \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a sequence of independent random variables representing the net energy efficiency or point-winning probability of the $n$-th rally.

**Operator 1: The Bounded Convergence Criterion (Theorem D)**
For a sequence of independent functions $\{f_n\}$ bounded by a positive constant $c$ almost everywhere ($|f_n(x)| \le c$ a.e.), the almost everywhere convergence of the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x)$ is governed by:
$$ \sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x) \text{ converges a.e.} \iff \left( \sum_{n=1}^\infty \int_X f_n \, d\mu < \infty \quad \text{AND} \quad \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sigma^2(f_n) < \infty \right) $$
*Units:* $\int f_n d\mu$ represents the expected value $\mathbb{E}[f_n]$ in SI units of Joules (energy) or dimensionless probability points. $\sigma^2(f_n) = \mathbb{E}[f_n^2] - (\mathbb{E}[f_n])^2$ represents the variance in $J^2$ or $p^2$.
*Breakdown Regime:* If $\sum \sigma^2(f_n) = \infty$, the system exhibits infinite fluctuation, leading to a singularity where prediction becomes impossible (chaotic breakdown of form).

**Operator 2: The Truncated Expectation & Tail Probability (Theorem E - Partial)**
As cited from [PAGE 199]: *"a necessary and sufficient condition for the convergence a.e. of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x)$ is the convergence of all three series (a) ... (b) ..."*. Reconstructing Kolmogorov's full theorem for our application:
Let $E_n = \{x : |f_n(x)| \le c\}$. The conditions are:
(a) $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \mu(E_n^c) < \infty$ (Convergence of tail probabilities / Injury risk summability)
(b) $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \int_{E_n} f_n \, d\mu < \infty$ (Convergence of truncated expectations / Controlled energy drain)
(c) $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \int_{E_n} f_n^2 \, d\mu < \infty$ (Convergence of truncated variances)
*Assumptions:* Independence of rallies (approximated by resetting mental state after each point).
*SINGULARITY:* The exact boundary of sustainable play is defined by the critical constant $c^*$ where $\lim_{c \to c^*} \sum \mu(|f_n| > c) = \infty$. Crossing this line triggers immediate biomechanical failure.

**Operator 1 — HEADSport artefact-attenuation filter (B-R1-01, [PAGE 1]).**
Given a raw tri-axial acceleration signal $x(t)\in \mathbb{R}^{3}$ (SI units: m·s⁻²) and angular rate $\omega(t)\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ (rad·s⁻¹), the PSD-based filter is $\hat{y}(f)=H_{\mathrm{HS}}(f)\,\hat{x}(f)$, with the transfer cutoffs $f_c$ selected from empirical PSD characteristics of 72 lab headform impacts (25–150 g ground truth; 1 g = 9.80665 m·s⁻²) and 5,694 on-field rugby head acceleration events [PAGE 1]. Define per-event SNR in decibels:
$$\mathrm{SNR_{dB}}=10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{\int_{f_c}^{f_\mathrm{Nyq}}\hat{y}(f)\,df}{\int_{0}^{f_c}\hat{y}(f)\,df}\right),$$
with $f_\mathrm{Nyq}=f_s/2$. **Assumptions:** stationarity within a single event window; artefact spectral modes are separable from kinematic modes. **Breakdown regimes:** (a) call it $f_s^{\star}$: if any new sensor samples at $f_s < 2f_c$, the PSD is aliased and $\mathrm{SNR_{dB}}\to$ undefined; (b) if the deployed sensor's noise spectrum has a resonance inside the passband, the filter has *negative* selectivity and the estimate is worse than unfiltered; (c) if the event duration $\tau \lesssim 1/f_c$, the frequency-domain filter is a length $\approx f_s/f_c$ moving object larger than the event itself. The "run HEADSport vs Butterworth-200Hz vs CFC180 and report SNR deltas" instruction [PAGE 1] is precisely a sample-adequacy diagnostic.

**Operator 2 — CNN-brain-strains surrogate (B-R1-02, [PAGE 2]).**
$$\hat{\varepsilon}^{\max}(\,\cdot\,)=\mathcal{C}_{\theta}\!\big(\mathbf{a}(t),\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)\big),\qquad \varepsilon^{\max}_{i}=\max_{\sigma\in\{\mathrm{principal}\}}\sigma_{\max}(\varepsilon_{i}),$$
where $\varepsilon^{\max}_i$ is the element-wise maximum principal strain (dimensionless, m·m⁻¹) at WHIM element $i$ over ~55k elements [PAGE 2]. Reproduced accuracy is reported as $R^{2}=0.916$ and $\mathrm{RMSE}=0.014$ m·m⁻¹ for whole-brain MPS. **Assumptions:** piecewise quasi-static stress balance at every $\mathbf{x}(t)$; material isotropy reduced to tractography-derived anisotropy classes at WHIM v1.0; and $(\mathbf{a},\boldsymbol{\omega})$ residences strictly inside the validated envelope [PAGE 2] "~900–5,100 rad/s² PRA, 2–40 rad/s PRV." **Singularity regimes:** (a) **envelope overshoot** — if PRA $>5{,}100$ rad·s⁻² or PRV $>40$ rad·s⁻¹ at any impact sample, the CNN extrapolates outside training support; no bound on error is derivable from reported R² because R² is computed on in-distribution data. (b) **regional collapse** — the aggregate $R^{2}$ is not decomposed into corpus-callosum-only, brainstem-only, cerebrum-only; per-element maximum principal strain at a 55k-element mesh has likely $R^{2}_{\mathrm{region}}\ll R^{2}_{\mathrm{whole}}$, because the mean-sensitive $R^{2}$ favors large-element low-variance regions. (c) **sub-threshold** — for PRA $<900$ rad·s⁻², the CNN was trained mostly on impacts at this threshold and may systematically zero out legitimate low-amplitude strain.

**Operator 3 — Impact retriever (B-R1-03, [PAGE 3]).**
Inverse map: $\hat{\ell}, \hat{v}, \hat{F} = \mathcal{R}_{\phi}\big(\mathbf{a}(t),\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)\big)$ with $\ell\in\{\text{location classes}\}$, $v\in$ m·s⁻¹ (closing speed), $F\in$ N (force). Trained on $N_\mathrm{train}=16{,}000$ simulated helmeted impacts (Riddell FE; helmet-specific); validated on $N_\mathrm{val}=79$ video-verified on-field instrumented-mouthguard impacts [PAGE 3]. **Assumption:** the mapping from $\mathbf{a}(t),\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)$ to $(\ell,v,F)$ is one-to-one *within* the training support. **Singularity regimes:** (a) **domain shift** — training on helmet FE, deployed on mouthguard, is an extrapolation not a validation; the validation set of $N=79$ bounds only *nominal* error, not the error caused by sensor-location-induced signal distortion (helmet vs teeth). (b) **ill-posedness at low PRV** — for near-axial direct impacts (small $\boldsymbol{\omega}$, large $a_\mathrm{lin}$), many $(\ell, v, F)$ triples produce indistinguishable kinematics; the inverse problem is non-injective on this submanifold. (c) **force transfer** — $F$ is *inferred* from kinematics, not measured; that is a synthesis not an observation, and downstream label users must inherit the transfer error.

**Operator 4 — multi-site harmonization (B-R1-24/25, [PAGE 19]).** ComBat: $y_{ij}^{\star}=\gamma_{ij}^{\star}+\delta_{ij}^{\star}\Big(\dfrac{y_{ij}-\alpha-\gamma_{ij}}{\delta_{ij}}\Big)-\alpha$, applied to feature vectors (e.g., FA, MD, cortical thickness) rather than raw volumes. **Assumptions:** site effects are additive-multiplicative; biological covariates (age, sex, **and diagnosis**) are included in the design; $N_{\mathrm{site}}\ge 2$ for each site. **Singularity regime:** if diagnosis is omitted from the design, ComBat can *absorb the injury signal into the site term* — documented in the source as a "standing caution" [PAGE 19]. That is a manifestation-conditioned removal of the target variable: the stop-condition is not sample size, it is design-specification.

**Operator 5 — ComBat-preservation statistic.** Let $\kappa_{\mathrm{keep}}=\mathrm{Var}(\hat{y}\mid \text{diagnosis})/ \mathrm{Var}(\hat{y})$ before and after harmonization; require $\Delta\ln\kappa_{\mathrm{keep}}\le\tau_\kappa$ (e.g., 0.05). This is a formal *counter-check* on Operator 4 and lives at the level of every derived feature, not the raw.

**Operator 6 — electrophysiology effect-size gate (FieldTrip lineage, [PAGE 31]).** For a group contrast, the honest posterior is $p_{\mathrm{cluster}}$, computed by cluster-based permutation with $N_{\mathrm{perm}}\ge 10^3$. **Singularity regime:** with $n\le 5$ per group and no permutation, channel-wise selection inflates $\alpha$ by a factor $\sim\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$, so the "biomarker" is a Wiener filter fit to noise — precisely the warning at [PAGE 31] that uncorrected channel-wise inference "manufactures false biomarkers."

**Operator 7 — Chain E label schema (nflverse-data, [PAGE 32]).** For each player-week $i$ at time $t$, define
$$L_{it}\in\{\mathrm{Out},\mathrm{Doubtful},\mathrm{Questionable},\mathrm{None}\},\quad B_{it}\in\{\text{body part classes, incl. Concussion}\}.$$
The joint target is usually $Y_{i,t+1}=\mathbb{1}[L_{i,t+1}=\mathrm{Out}]$. **Breakdown regimes:** (a) right-censoring on panel exit (cut/retire/injury); (b) "Questionable" mixed prevalence — prevalence-estimate of true concussion is a latent variable that must be modeled with a survey-style false-positive rate; (c) the live feed (sportsdataverse, [PAGE 32]) has no historical point-in-time record, so back-test training $Y$ at time $t+\Delta$ vs feature $X$ observed at $t$ cannot be verified ex post.

**Operator 8 — MRIQC IQM features [PAGE 16].** Head motion: $\mathrm{FD}_i=\sum_{k\in\{x,y,z\}}|d_{k,i}|$ (mm). **Operator 9 — BrainFlow ingestion timing [PAGE 4].** For board $b\in\{\text{BCI/consumer/research}\}$, the sampling-rate assertion is $\hat{f}_s\leftarrow f_s^{\mathrm{declared}}$, with drift over $T$ given by $\int_{0}^{T}(\hat{f}_s-f_s)\,dt$; **singularity:** if drift $>$ 1 sample per hundred, downstream filter cutoffs are wrong by the same factor as the sensor clock — silent and severe.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lane's scientific lineage is traceable through four generations, and each generation has a trap that only becomes visible retrospectively.

**Generation 1 (2010–2015) — Sensitized-mouthguard feasibility.** The B-R1-01 lineage traces to "Tooby/Williams et al." [PAGE 1] and to power-spectral-density fitness of lab headforms to field events, eventually published in BMJ Open Sport & Exercise Medicine 2024. Before this, researchers argued whether iMG signals were usable at all; the ancestral root is the debate over whether *any* peak linear acceleration measurement, before filtration, is meaningful. Retroactive trap: the literature before PSD-based filter design contains reconstructed peak $a_\mathrm{lin}$ estimates that are *systematically inflated* by "artefact" power at low-frequency; naively pooling those numbers with post-2024 numbers inflates any head-impact-exposure sum.

**Generation 2 (2015–2020) — WHIM v1.0 finite element.** The Worcester Head Injury Model arose from the B-R1-02 authors' lineage [PAGE 2]; ~55,000 elements, tractography-derived anisotropy. Its immediate ancestors are Riddell helmet FE and Stanford SIMon-era brain models. Retroactive trap: the model's predicted strain field is only as good as its material parameters, which are drawn from *in vitro* brain tissue and animal studies; the "true" strain of a living, perfused human brain is an unobservable construct. The FE simulation labels therefore carry a systematic uncertainty floor that the CNN surrogate's 0.916 R² does not — and cannot — correct for.

**Generation 3 (2019–2024) — surrogate learning.** The trained CNN [PAGE 2] and the impact_retriever LSTM [PAGE 3] both learn an inverse/forward map against Generation 2. The Stanford mouthguard cohort (college football/boxing/MMA, N=110) and reconstructed NFL impacts (N=53) record the transition from FE-only, compute-cost-bound research to seconds-scale inference. Retroactive trap: the same 2024 wave that produced these surrogates inherited the earlier reconstruction choices; if a re-analysis changes helmet FE, every surrogate trained on the old FE must be re-trained, and any paper reporting impact-level strain in the interim has implicit uncertainty.

**Generation 4 (2024–2026) — pipeline standardization.** fMRIPrep, dMRIPrep, QSIPrep, XCP-D, MRIQC, tedana, and the harmonizers [PAGES 12–19] form the BIDS-app generation. Their ancestors are Nipype (workflow engine) and pybids (dataset layout). The retroactive trap is the one named at [PAGE 27]: "the join integrity that training sets stand or fall on." Prior to BIDS, participant-metadata joins were ad hoc; re-analysis of pre-BIDS studies frequently cannot recover the original subject-to-session mapping, so any claim of longitudinal imaging effect (baseline vs post-impact) must be re-derived under a BIDS manifest.

**Generation 5 (2020–2026) — surveillance data.** The NFL injury reports in nflverse-data [PAGE 32] descend from a long lineage of sports-injury epidemiology that began with in-season team reports. Their ancestor is the older injury-surveillance literature (Meeuwisse et al. and equivalent frameworks), whose central warning was that reported injury "designation" is a *proxy* for injury, not injury itself — a warning later softened in popular coverage. The retroactive trap: any model trained on "Out" as label and evaluated on "Questionable" misclassifies at a rate the source text calls an unmeasured false-positive rate. The multi-week trap: designation routinely shifts within 72 hours; a model trained on Tuesday data and evaluated on Thursday data has different generative semantics.

**Multi-week trap — cross-generation timing.** The wave is dated 2026-11-06 with last-push dates spanning 2020–2026. A naive "use the whole lane as one training corpus" build conflates a 2026 fMRIPrep release with a 2020 CNN-brain-strains release and a 2024 impact_retriever, and thereby *imports* a 4-year old bug-repaired state of the FE solver into a 2026 inference. The lineage’s cure is version disclosure: every sampled model must be tagged with commit-hash + release date, not just repo name.

**Genealogical verdict.** The lane's true genealogy is a *four-to-six-fold compression* of brain-impact research: sensitivity (lab) → mechanism (FE) → inference speed (surrogate) → pipeline (BIDS) → observed epidemiology (designation). Each generation inherits the biases of its parents; no generation has a clean oracle, and Chain E (the only human-label source) is also the most proxy-laden.

The crisis-habit regression here is legible. A newcomer gesticulating at the 50-repo wave, under time pressure, reaches for the surface reading: "this is a head-impact pipeline; I will stack every repo, feed one sensor, and predict." That impulse is a known cognitive mode — the **habituation habit** — where a complex lineage is compressed into a single number (peak linear acceleration or maximum MPS) because the compressed representation is easier to act on than the calibrated representation. The source text itself names three habit-regression triggers:

1. **The "peak $a_\mathrm{lin}$" habit.** Over the past decade, peak linear acceleration was the most-shared kinematic summary, and the habit is to keep it. §4 of B-R1-01 [PAGE 1] is written precisely to interrupt that habit by pre-specifying PSD-based filtering as a *gate*: the habit-regression failure is using unfiltered peak as label, and the counter-trap is refusing to compare without stating filter equivalence.

2. **The "harmonize because it's a multi-site wave" habit.** Reading B-R1-24/25 [PAGE 19] as "apply ComBat" is a habit that fails because ComBat without diagnosis in the design can *absorb* the signal. The counter-trap is to *decline to harmonize* until the design vector is audited and $\kappa_\mathrm{keep}$ is reported.

3. **The "aggregate $R^2$ is accuracy" habit.** The $R^{2}=0.916$ headline is a persuasive summary [PAGE 2], but it is not a per-region or per-impact distribution ceiling. The counter-trap is to *withhold* region-level strain claims until per-region decomposition exists.

The **counter-trap** is computational honesty at the level of the model card, and the **clock** is the wave's dated verification data. There are three distinct clock regimes:

- **Backtest clock (Chain E):** sportsdataverse [PAGE 32] emits live values; the honest replay horizon is one snapshot per fetch. Any "prediction" made *today* using yesterday's *Questionable/Out* status is either truth-bearing (if the source value timestamp is preserved) or unusable (if reconstructed from today's endpoints). The clock rule: if the timestamp of the source record is not retained in the data frame, the row is deleted from any backtest, and no rescue.

- **Model-version clock (Chains A+B):** last-push dates in the wave span 2020 (CNN-brain-strains) through 2026 (FEBio, MRIQC, ANTs, etc.). $R^2$ and RMSE figures are pinned to specific commit states; a re-clone in 2026 may silently change validation numbers. Counter-trap: freeze sha in the training-card and never re-use "R^2 from the paper" as "R^2 of the pipeline."

- **Clinical-observation clock (Chains C+D):** post-concussion labels are attached to a *date of injury*, but the EEG/fMRI/HRV outcome is often 3–90 days later. The retrospective trap is treating a day-30 outcome as if it were observed at injury time. The honest clock rule: the label is $Y_{t+\Delta}$ with a stated $\Delta$; never $\Delta=0$.

The recursive-theory-of-mind model bit here is simple: the *other agent* in the wave is not just the reader — it is the future re-user who will never see the intermediate reasoning and will inherit the model. Any undocumented heuristic will be re-derived as fact by that agent. The only defense against that failure is to force the model card into carrying assumptions, breakdown regime, and version tags — which this wave's §8 (ENGINE-TRAINING VALUE) field partially provides (it names labels and data location) but which most dossiers do not fully populate (e.g., per-region $R^2$ is missing, envelope is missing from most imaging-classifier repos).

Conclusion for this section: the crisis habit is *aggregate-summary trust*; the counter-trap is *atomic-claim trust with explicit regime*; the clock is *three distinct preservation regimes* (timestamp, sha, injury-date) that must be independently logged. The three must be co-enforced or the recursive loop degrades into self-reference.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Four physically distinct veto layers assert against the medical lane.

**Physical veto layer — sensor saturation and PSD aliasing.** HEADSport's lab ground truth tops out at 25–150 g [PAGE 1] (equivalently 2.45–14.7 m·s⁻²? — no, $25g\approx 245$ m·s⁻², $150g\approx 1{,}471$ m·s⁻²). Any on-field impact above 150 g is beyond the lab corpus; if a mouthguard recording exceeds this, HEADSport cutoffs are extrapolated and the derived "peak $a_\mathrm{lin}$" is an upper bound, not an estimate. Physical veto: if $a_\mathrm{lin}>150g$, refuse to report a peak without an explicit saturation band. Similarly for $f_s$: if the sensor samples below $\sim 800$ Hz, the artefact band $\ge f_s/2$ folds into the passband; the resulting filter is *anti*-informative and must be vetoed.

**Biomechanical veto layer — envelope and domain.** CNN-brain-strains [PAGE 2] carries a validated envelope `~900–5,100 rad/s² PRA, 2–40 rad/s PRV`. A mouthguard trace with $<\mathrm{PRV}=2$ rad/s is sub-envelope; the CNN's regional summary can be spurious because the surrogate was trained on impacts dominated by a rotation response the sampled impact lacks. Envelope veto: for any impact with PRV outside [2, 40] rad/s, the MPS map is not reportable and the whole kinematic-strain link is *provisional*. impact_retriever's helmet-trained distribution is biomechanically distinct from mouthguard recordings — the helmet's outer-shell padding modifies the acceleration spectrum; the inverse map's assumption of an unmodified head surface is a physical veto for any helmet-present deployment [PAGE 3].

**Weather / environmental veto layer — scan and record quality.** MRIQC [PAGE 16] provides an automatic veto against acceptance: FD > 0.5 mm, EFC above per-site quantiles, CNR below critical value flag "failed" class. Weather enters the imaging chain in two ways: (i) MRI suite protocol changes during off-hours; (ii) sportsdataverse live feeds [PAGE 32] show weather-dependent practice changes (indoor vs outdoor) that shift designation-status timing. The natural veto is: if the scan's quality class is "failed," any inference is unsupported; if a live feed shows a practice-status change attributable to weather, the underlying "exposure" class is contaminated.

**Cross-layer coupling veto — the data-integrity sufﬁciency constraint.** Harmonization (ComBat, dMRIharmonization) requires matched acquisition shells. If the candidate dataset has fewer than two sites or no control subjects per site, the harmonizer's assumption fails [PAGE 19]. This is a cross-layer veto because it acts on *feature learnability* not raw measurements: it suppresses downstream classifier training even when every upstream feature looks numerically valid.

Combining the layers, the admissible decision rule is:
$$\mathcal{A} = \text{Admissible iff } (\,\text{envelope}\land\text{PSD validity}\land\text{scan-QC}\land\text{harmonizer design}\,).$$
Each conjunct is binary-veto. If any is false, the impact, or the feature, or the subject, is *excluded from the training set* without a "soft" handling. The source text makes the design-veto explicit at [PAGE 19] and [PAGE 20]; it makes the envelope-veto explicit at [PAGE 2]. The QC-veto is explicit at [PAGE 16] and [PAGE 20]. What is *not* explicit in the source is the PSD-validity veto at the sampling-rate level — that must be added by the reader because HEADSport [PAGE 1] gives filter cutoffs without stating the $f_s$ dependency. That gap is the single most likely source of silently-wrong features.

Final cross-layer note: no layer introduces betting or probabilistic output. The veto is binary at the layer of the measurement, and the outcome is always "excluded" or "admissible with stated regime," never a handicap.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable RLVR veto scenarios, each with an explicit reward signal that can be computed post hoc from the source text itself, and a predicted outcome if the veto is enforced.

**Scenario V1 — ComBat-without-diagnosis veto (verifiable, deterministic).**
Setup: take a synthetic two-site FA dataset with diagnosis effect $\Delta=0.05$ (arbitrary units) and site effect $\mathrm{Var}_{\mathrm{site}}=4\Delta$. Compute $\kappa_{\mathrm{keep}}$ (see Operator 5) before and after ComBat with (a) diagnosis in the design, (b) diagnosis omitted. Reward: $r=+1$ if the omitted-design output retains $\kappa_{\mathrm{keep}}$ within 5% of the before state, $-1$ otherwise. Prediction: with diagnosis omitted, $\kappa_{\mathrm{keep}}$ collapses by an order of magnitude and the classifier trained on the harmonized features would show a "site effect" the model learn to exploit; the RLVR veto fires on (b) *always*. This is fully verifiable from the repository's own mathematical behavior — no clinical claims needed. Cross-reference: §5 of B-R1-25 [PAGE 19] and Operator 5 above.

**Scenario V2 — Impact-retriever out-of-envelope veto (verifiable, distributional).**
Setup: take a random sample of $n=1{,}000$ simulated impacts from the retriever's published 16,000-impact corpus and 100 real on-field impacts from the 79-val subset [PAGE 3]; compute the held-out likelihood under the helmet-FE model for each. Reward: $r=+1$ if the *field* impacts are not statistically outside the 95% quantile of the simulated corpus under a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test; $-1$ otherwise. Prediction: on (location, speed, force) joint distribution, the field sample will exceed 5% outlier rate — the source itself justifies this with the phrase "helmet-trained, mouthguard-validated" [PAGE 3]; the helmet-vs-mouthguard physical difference makes leakage into the out-of-support region near-certain. Enforcement: any field impact flagged as OOD must be excluded from retriever-based force reporting.

**Scenario V3 — Chain E point-in-time snapshot veto (verifiable, data-integrity).**
Setup: build two feature snapshots at dates $t_1$ and $t_2$ from sportsdataverse [PAGE 32] with all timestamp columns preserved; compare to a *reconstructed* snapshot from a historical file that lacks timestamps. Reward: $r=+1$ if, for a 10% audit sample, the reconstructed snapshot reproduces exact (player, week, status) strings matching the snapshot at $t_1$; $-1$ otherwise. Prediction: the reconstructed file will match the snapshot at $t_2$ (current) at $\ge 95\%$ and will match the snapshot at $t_1$ at $\le 40\%$ over the same strata, because it is a current-file overwrite. The veto fires automatically on any "backtest" whose data source lacks a retained point-in-time file. Cross-reference: [PAGE 32] "unsnapshotted history is unrecoverable."

**Predicted cross-scenario outcome.** If all three vetoes are enforced, the resulting training set drops: (i) every derived-feature block produced by unpinned ComBat, (ii) every force estimate from a helmet-vs-mouthguard domain shift, (iii) every "historical" live-status feature whose timestamp is reconstructed. The surviving corpus is smaller but *provably admissible*, and its meta-claims (which were previously "predictive") reduce to "descriptive with explicit regime." That reduction is the intended output — an honest lower bound on what the lane can support, rather than an inflated upper bound on what it might, with speculative framing removed, plausibly support. The verifiable reward is in all three cases computable without clinical labels, which is precisely why the veto can be enforced in production: no adjudicator needs to be a physician to veto; the math alone suffices.

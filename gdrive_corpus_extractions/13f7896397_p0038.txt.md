The veto proof: if behavioral performance on the T-maze varies with temperature while trial structure and cues are held constant, then Perry’s strict psychology/physiology divide fails and physiology is not merely asymptotic but constitutively entangled with the behavioral unit.

Adjudication rule: the whole-organism model is provisionally accepted only if (a) \(\Delta I > 0\) is measured, (b) environmental change is slower than biomechanical latency, (c) temperature is controlled or Q10-scaling is included in the model, and (d) no single segmental lesion produces a graceful, additive decline.

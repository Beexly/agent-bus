3. **Social Determinants:** Women confined indoors with infected children/spouses, increasing exposure.

A multi-week trap arises when modern readers retroactively apply Mendelian logic to pre-Mendel data. However, without controlling for socioeconomic status, the results remain ambiguous.

Genealogically, this study influenced later biometric debates but was superseded by molecular epidemiology. The persistence of such datasets in historical literature highlights how early observational patterns can entrench misconceptions about sex-specific disease inheritance.

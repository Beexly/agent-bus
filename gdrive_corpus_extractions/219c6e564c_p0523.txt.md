The retro-causal trap is that James’s explanation is functionally correct at the phenomenological level but historically under-specified at the mechanistic level. A third trap is cross-modal analogy inflation: moving from trains (real self-motion with vestibular confirmatory cues) to oil-cloths (purely visual) assumes the same figure–ground operator without demonstrating gain equivalence across modalities.

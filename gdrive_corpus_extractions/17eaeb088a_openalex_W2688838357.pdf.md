<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

The scientific lineage here is a chain of substitutions, each motivated by a failure of the prior operator. Mean-Shift (Comaniciu et al., density Eq. 2.1 [PAGE 24]) established kernel color-histogram tracking but required iterative mode-seeking and periodic re-initialization for occlusion. The Kalman filter [PAGE 24]–[PAGE 25] supplied recursive state estimation (`X_t = A_t X_{t−1} + W_t`, Eq. 2.2) but assumed Gaussian, linear dynamics ill-suited to non-rigid pedestrians. The particle filter (Eqs. 2.8–2.10 [PAGE 31]) generalized to non-linear posteriors and became the substrate for tracking-by-detection (Breitenstein et al. 2011).

Two further lineages converge. First, sparse representation: the thesis adopts and modifies Bao et al. (2012) [PAGE 55], replacing particle-generated candidates with detector outputs — an explicit genealogical mutation the author flags as a contribution. Second, color histograms are upgraded to LSH from He et al. (2013) [PAGE 52] to inject spatial locality into an otherwise position-blind descriptor. Motion evidence traces to Horn–Schunck (1981) flow, discretized into HOOF (Chaudhry et al. 2009) [PAGE 59]. Fusion traces to Dempster–Shafer (Dempster/Shafer, 1960s–70s) [PAGE 62], borrowed from classifier combination. Evaluation lineage is CLEAR MOT (Keni and Rainer 2008) [PAGE 79]. Assignment lineage is Kuhn's Hungarian method (1955) plus greedy heuristics, with tracklet alternatives (Kuo–Nevatia, Huang et al., Possegger et al.) catalogued as the competing branch [PAGE 45]–[PAGE 47].

Multi-week (methodological) traps embedded in this genealogy: (a) the "tracker + detector redundancy" trap — combining a detector with a tracker "génèrent des candidats redondants ce qui résulte à … des fausses assignations" [PAGE 32], motivating the pure detection stance; (b) the "single-descriptor robustness" trap, refuted by the ablation (color-only caps near 76% MOTA, motion-only collapses) [PAGE 83]; (c) the "offline weight tuning" trap — `α_k` fixed globally rather than learned per-frame, which the author lists as an unresolved perspective [PAGE 88].

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

I map the persona's "crisis / habit regression / counter-trap / clock" onto the thesis's target state machine, which is a genuine recursive estimator over an object's latent condition.

Crisis = occlusion. The source defines the crisis regime explicitly: when a target is unmatched it enters the occluded state — "Un objet cible est en état occulté si il n'est pas mis en correspondance avec aucun détection" [PAGE 72]. The five-state graph (Actif, Occulté, Sortant, Entrant, Hypothèse) is the recursive mind: the tracker models each target's belief about its own visibility.

Habit regression = model drift. Under prolonged occlusion the appearance model regresses toward corruption: "En fait, plus l'occultation est longue, plus il est probable de perdre le modèle d'apparence" [PAGE 19]. The occluder's pixels contaminate `F^t`, so `p(y_i|x_t)` and `H_c` degrade monotonically with occlusion duration — a regression of the belief state.

Counter-trap = update gating. The defense is a conditional model-update policy: "La mise à jour du modèle d'apparence d'un objet cible est faite seulement si : — L'objet cible n'est pas en occultation … — Un bon suivi est réalisé" [PAGE 75]–[PAGE 76], gated by threshold `τ_maj`. Only active-state targets update. This blocks the feedback loop by which a bad match would poison the model, which would justify further bad matches.

Second counter-trap = hierarchical association. Naïve greedy matching discards true pairs below `th`; the author notes "on a raté des possibilités d'affectation qui peuvent être correctes" [PAGE 69]. The two-level scheme (active targets matched first, then occluded targets against residual candidates, Algorithm 4 [PAGE 71]) re-admits occluded targets that a single greedy pass would drop.

Clock = frame index and dwell thresholds. The state machine is driven by temporal counters: `ϕ₁` frames in the entry/exit region triggers removal, `ϕ₂` frames in hypothesis state promotes to active, and `t_seuil` gates the history window for interpolation Eq. (3.27). The "Entrant/Sortant" logic assumes objects cross frame borders: "on suppose que les objets en mouvements lors de l'entrée ou de sortie traversent nécessairement les bordures de la trame" [PAGE 72]. This is a hard prior on scene geometry — the clock adjudicates birth/death.

Recursive self-modeling failure: if two similar targets swap during a group occlusion (the "similar appearance" problem [PAGE 6]), the interpolation clock extrapolates the wrong velocity vector, and the counter-trap (update gating) cannot correct because the corrupted track believes itself healthy. The TUD-Stadtmitte result — 22 identity switches vs. 6–7 for competitors [PAGE 82] — is the empirical signature of this recursive-model breakdown under dense multi-occlusion.

</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Because the source is a computer-vision dissertation, I adjudicate physical/biomechanical/weather vetoes as they literally appear, not as metaphors.

**Physical veto A — scale–distance coupling.** The source states: "Plus l'objet est loin de la caméra, plus les détections obtenues sont petites (en nombre de pixels). Du coup, les objets cibles contiennent des informations moins ﬁables" [PAGE 5]. Formally, target pixel count `N_pix` decreases with camera-to-target distance `Z` roughly as `N_pix ∝ 1/Z²`. Below a threshold `N_pix`, the LSH histogram `H_c` (built from `Q(I_q,b)` over `W` pixels) loses discriminative mass, and the sparse dictionary `T ∈ ℝ^{d×n}` becomes rank-starved. Proof of veto: on PETS2009-S2L1 the author notes "le point de vue de la caméra est loin … les objets sont très petits" [PAGE 88], and the detector, not the tracker, dominates error. This is a hard physical veto on any descriptor relying on pixel support.

**Physical/biomechanical veto B — constant-velocity assumption.** Interpolation Eq. (3.28) `pos_t = pos_{t−1} + μ_dep` and the spatial prior both assume constant velocity: "les objets cibles dans la séquence vidéo font des mouvements de type linéaire constant (les objets cibles sont en général des piétons)" [PAGE 74]. Biomechanical reality vetoes this: pedestrians stop and turn. The source documents the violation — "la personne dont l'identifiant est 1 … arrête pendant plus que 100 trames" and "les objets font des mouvements circulaires" [PAGE 74]. Proof: linear extrapolation error grows as `O(½ a Δt²)` for acceleration `a` over occlusion gap `Δt`; at `Δt > 100` frames the predicted `pos_t` is physically inadmissible, forcing reliance on re-detection rather than interpolation.

**Weather/illumination veto C — intensity dependence.** Every appearance descriptor ultimately touches intensity. LSH uses `Q(I_q, b)`; sparse templates `T` are intensity patches; Horn–Schunck flow assumes brightness constancy `p(x+dx,y+dy,t+dt)=p(x,y,t)` [PAGE 37]. Under illumination change the constraint Eq. (2.14) is violated because `∂E/∂t ≠ 0` from lighting, not motion — the source explicitly warns the flow "changes peuvent être un changement de luminosité, une variation de l'arrière-plan, un bruit" [PAGE 38]. Veto proof: the brightness-constancy premise fails, injecting spurious flow into `H_m`, which the author confirms by the motion descriptor's near-zero standalone MOTA (0% PETS2009, 1% TUD-CROSSING [PAGE 83]–[PAGE 84]).

**Adjudication.** No single veto is fatal because the descriptors are independent (Eq. 3.1): color survives motion failure, motion disambiguates color ties, spatial rejects geometrically impossible matches "même si leurs modèles d'apparence sont les plus similaires" [PAGE 60]. The cross-layer defense is precisely the fusion — the ablation proves it (all-descriptor MOTA 84% vs. best single 76% on PETS2009 [PAGE 83]).

</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

Three verifiable counterfactual vetoes, each with a source-grounded prediction.

**Veto 1 — detector ceiling.** Counterfactual: replace the noisy detector with ground-truth detections (0% false positives/negatives). Verified outcome, Table 4.6 [PAGE 95]: MOTA = 100% (TUD-Camp, TUD-Stadm), 97% (TUD-Cross), 99.65% (PETS2009-S2L1), MOTP ≈ 100%. Prediction: the association+appearance stack is near-ceiling when detection is perfect; therefore residual error is detector-bound, confirmed by the author's remark "la qualité de suivi dépend dans un premier temps de la qualité de détecteur d'objet utilisé" [PAGE 101]. Any future claim that a fancier appearance model alone lifts MOTA on noisy detections is vetoed unless it changes detector input.

**Veto 2 — motion-descriptor collapse.** Counterfactual: use motion (`H_m`) alone. Verified outcome, Table 4.4 [PAGE 83]: MOTA = 0%, precision 45%, FN 38%, FP 46%, IDS 1178 on PETS2009; Table 4.5 [PAGE 84]: MOTA = 1% on TUD-CROSSING. Prediction: on any scene where targets share near-identical velocity vectors, the columns of `R` (Eq. 3.26) built from `s_motion` become collinear and the argmax is non-identifiable. Quantitatively, motion adds value only conditionally — combining sparse+motion lifts PETS2009 MOTA from 45% to 62% [PAGE 83], but motion alone is a vetoed configuration. Falsifiable prediction: a dataset with all targets moving at the same speed and heading reproduces the ≈0% collapse.

**Veto 3 — Dempster–Shafer conflict singularity.** Counterfactual: fuse color and sparse via Eq. (3.23) when the two sources strongly disagree (`K → 1`). Source claim: "plus le conﬂit entre les masses d'évidence est grand, plus la valeur de masse jointe tend vers zéro … Cette règle de combinaison ne peut pas être utilisée dans le cas d'un conﬂit élevé" [PAGE 63]. Prediction: as `K → 1`, the normalizer `1/(1−K)` diverges and the combined mass becomes numerically unstable; the correct engineering response is the fallback to the linear weighted sum Eq. (3.25), which is exactly why the thesis adopts linear fusion with fixed `α_k` for the full four-descriptor model. Falsifiable: any implementation using Dempster–Shafer fusion without conflict discounting will exhibit assignment blow-ups precisely on high-occlusion frames where color and sparse residuals disagree.

Cross-veto summary: the three counterfactuals bound the method's operating envelope — detector-limited in accuracy (Veto 1), descriptor-degenerate under motion homogeneity (Veto 2), and fusion-singular under high evidential conflict (Veto 3). The reported headline results (TUD-Campus MOTA 78.18%, PETS2009-S2L1 84% [PAGE 82]; ~6.47 s/frame, dominated by LSH construction [PAGE 80]) are therefore valid only inside that envelope, which the source itself delineates through its ablations and the ground-truth sensitivity study.

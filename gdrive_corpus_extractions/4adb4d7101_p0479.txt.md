Veto condition: if identical milk consumed in a neutral urban setting at matched temperature produces the same affective report, the weather/mountain context is not a necessary causal layer.

If formal musical emotion persists with B = 0, the Jamesian bodily-marker model is vetoed in favor of Gurney; if gustatory affect persists without fatigue or mountain context, the associationist need-state model is vetoed in favor of pure sensory quality.

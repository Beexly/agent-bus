Veto condition: if normal species-typical maternal behavior emerges despite complete cross-fostering deprivation, James's implication that the maternal response is unlearned and instinctive [PAGE 440] is vetoed; the behavior would instead be a general social heuristic not contingent on early conspecific exposure.

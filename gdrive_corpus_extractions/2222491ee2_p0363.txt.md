Let the representational state of an idea be a vector \(x \in \mathcal{X} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^D\), where \(D\) is the dimensionality of extracted characters. James’s two association mechanisms are formalized as transition operators on the simplex \(\Delta(\mathcal{X})\).

1. **Contiguity operator** \(C\): for a current idea \(x\), the habitual successor distribution is
\[
(Cx)(y) = P(y \mid x) = \frac{n(x,y)}{\sum_{y'} n(x,y')},
\]
where \(n(x,y)\) is the count of contiguous co-occurrences in immediate-interest or emotional contexts [PAGE 353]. Units: dimensionless probability. For brutes, James claims the support of \(Cx\) is restricted to a small set of “total thoughts” related to immediate interests; the transition graph is locally dense and globally sparse.

2. **Similarity/dissociation operator** \(S\): varying concomitants permits jumps to states \(y\) that are feature-similar but contiguously remote,
\[
(Sx)(y) \propto \exp\!\left(-\frac{d(\phi(x),\phi(y))}{T}\right),
\]
where \(\phi : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}^d\) extracts task-relevant characters (\(d \ll D\)), \(d(\cdot,\cdot)\) is a feature distance, and \(T > 0\) is an “imagination temperature” controlling how freely actual sequences are reduced to fluidity [PAGE 353]. Units: exponent argument dimensionless; \(T\) in abstract representational units (effectively bits of feature slack).

3. **Brute constraint**: In animals, \(T \to \infty\) or \(\phi\) collapses to an immediate-interest subspace \(\mathcal{I} \subset \mathcal{X}\) with \(\dim(\mathcal{I}) \ll D\), so that \(S \approx 0\) outside the contiguous chain. The effective transition matrix is \(P_{\text{brute}} \approx C\), block-diagonal along routine chains. The spectral gap \(\lambda_2\) of \(C\) is small within a routine and the inter-routine escape rate is
\[
\gamma(x) = \sum_{y \notin \mathcal{R}(x)} (Sx)(y) \approx 0,
\]
where \(\mathcal{R}(x)\) is the set of states reachable by contiguous habit from \(x\). Units: \(\gamma\) in \(\text{s}^{-1}\).

4. **Singularity / breakdown regime**: Let \(\nu\) be the rate at which the environment presents non-stationary contingencies (units: \(\text{s}^{-1}\)). Behavioral rigidity breaks down adaptively when \(\gamma < \nu\); the agent cannot update its contiguous chain fast enough to track changed latch mechanics, feeding times, or gate locations. The critical singularity is \(\gamma/\nu = 1\); below it, the brute remains “enslaved to routine” [PAGE 353] even when the routine no longer yields reward.

5. **Information-theoretic signature**: The entropy rate of the idea sequence is
\[
h = -\sum_{x} p(x) \sum_{y} P(y\mid x) \log P(y\mid x).
\]
Within a fixed routine, \(h\) is low and stable; across an unaccustomed place, a brute cannot voluntarily increase \(h\) because \(S\) is offline, whereas a human injects similarity-driven entropy to explore alternative world-states (the basis of wondering “why the universe should be as it is” [PAGE 353]).

Injection-locking range (Eq. 1, Page 2), rad/s:
$$\omega_L = \frac{\omega_0}{2Q}\cdot\frac{I_{inj}}{I_{osc}}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - I_{inj}^2/I_{osc}^2}}$$

Pulling-spur power (Eq. 2, Page 3), dimensionless (dBc):
$$P_{[\omega_{spur}]}\approx\left(\frac{\omega_0}{4Q}\cdot\frac{I_{inj}}{I_{osc}}\cdot\frac{1}{\omega_m}\right)^2$$
Units: $\omega_0,Q^{-1},\omega_m$ in rad/s and s⁻¹ cancel to a pure ratio; $\omega_m$ = offset from $\omega_0$. DT LPF (Eq. 3, Page 4), dimensionless transfer function, $\lambda$ in seconds:

All poles coincide at $s=-1/\lambda$ (real, LTI, stable for $\lambda>0$).

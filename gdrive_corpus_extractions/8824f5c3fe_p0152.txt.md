Units: dimensionless (pure set theory); no SI unit applies. Cardinality consequence: |A × B| = |A| · |B| (cardinal multiplication), and |A × B| = 0 iff |A| = 0 or |B| = 0.

\[
(A_1 \times B_1 \neq \varnothing \;\land\; A_2 \times B_2 \neq \varnothing)
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\Bigl( A_1 \times B_1 \subseteq A_2 \times B_2 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; (A_1 \subseteq A_2 \;\land\; B_1 \subseteq B_2) \Bigr).
\]

The "multi-week traps" are persistent failure modes that survive weeks of study rather than one-off slips. Trap one: the interval-rectangle trap. These traps are retro-causal in the sense that early geometric intuition (week one) retroactively corrupts later formal proofs (weeks three through six) unless explicitly quarantined.

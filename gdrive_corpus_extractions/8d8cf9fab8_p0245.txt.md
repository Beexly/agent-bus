<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage of the "insight" concept, exemplified by Tschego's solution, traces back through a distinct rebellion against mechanistic psychology. Its primary forebear is Wilhelm Wundt's voluntarism, which emphasized conscious intention as a cause, directly opposing the reflex arc model popularized by James and Angell. Köhler himself was a student of Wolfgang Köhler (Gestalt psychologist) and worked under Wolfgang Köhler's mentor, Max Wertheimer. The Gestalt principle of *Prägnanz*—the tendency for perception to simplify complex data into the simplest possible form—is the cognitive precursor. In Tschego's case, the perceptual field initially contained disjointed elements: unreachable banana, immovable bars, inert box. The "insight" is the sudden restructuring (*Umstructurierung*) of this field into a coherent "means-end" relationship, where the box transforms from background clutter to a tool.

However, this narrative has been perpetually trapped by subsequent frameworks. Behaviorists (Skinner) dismissed "insight" as an unscientific mentalistic label for what was actually the result of subtle, unobserved reinforcements (e.g., proprioceptive feedback from bumping into the box). Cognitive scientists later co-opted the term but often reduced it to "access to declarative knowledge" or "problem-solving algorithms," stripping away its phenomenological immediacy. Neuroscientists map it to bursts in the anterior cingulate cortex or sudden synchronization of gamma oscillations, effectively naturalizing the "aha!" as an epiphenomenon of neural computation.

The **multi-week trap** here is the persistent conflation of *explanation* with *description*. Describing the behavioral outcome (sudden solution after a long pause) is not explaining the underlying mechanism. The "insight" story risks becoming a self-sealing explanatory void: any proposed mechanism (neural burst, value update) is deemed the "cause of insight," but the qualitative experience of the sudden shift itself is never accounted for—it is merely tagged with a timestamp. The true genealogical task is to find a model where the *temporal profile* of the solution (long plateau, sharp discontinuity) is an emergent property of the system's architecture, not an input.

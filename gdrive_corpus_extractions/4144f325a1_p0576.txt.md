The lineage on [PAGE 566] runs from Philippe Pinel's Bicêtre practice (late eighteenth century) through Dr. Wigan's unpublished or semi-published case observations to James's 1890 synthesis. Pinel's moral treatment at Bicêtre assumed that reasoned cross-examination could restore reason and justify discharge. In manic or delusional syndromes, the analogous multi-week trap is the cycle of "holding oneself tight" during periods of external scrutiny (clinical interview, work, family contact) followed by "letting himself down" into full delusion when supervision lapses or fatigue accumulates.

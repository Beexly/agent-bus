The clock is the pair of temporal parameters t = 3 s and t″ = 2 s. Shifting the clock—reducing t″ below neural conduction latency, or making t negative so reinforcement precedes the CS—breaks the recursive update because the reinforcing event no longer follows the cue that habit strength is assigned to. The crisis-regression/counter-trap/clock triad therefore specifies a closed loop: habit strength tracks contingency direction; under pressure it regresses early; and the clock defines whether the update is legally S→R→reinforcement or outside the model's domain.

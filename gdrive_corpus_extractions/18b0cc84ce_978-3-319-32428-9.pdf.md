Three verifiable counterfactuals, each with a checkable prediction grounded in the text.

1. Time-step veto (diffusion). If a solver advances the rod problem with $\Delta t=3.4\times10^{-4}$ s $>\Delta x^2/(2\beta)=3.125\times10^{-4}$ s, the prediction is a saw-tooth profile that grows without bound within tens of steps ([PAGE 184], Figs. 5.1–5.2). 2. Qualitative-monotonicity veto (logistic ODE). If $N'=0.1\,N(1-N/500)$ is integrated with $\Delta t=20$, prediction: non-physical oscillations of $N^n$ about $M=500$, contradicting the provable monotonicity of the exact solution ([PAGE 107]–[108]). Verification: halve $\Delta t$ and watch oscillations vanish. Falsifier: persistent oscillations as $\Delta t\to0$.

3. Basin-of-attraction veto (Newton). If Newton solves $\tanh x=0$ from $x_0=1.09$, prediction: iterates diverge and a `ZeroDivisionError` fires near $x_7\approx-1.26\times10^{11}$ where $f'(x)=1-\tanh^2x$ underflows to $0$ ([PAGE 206]).

Applying a generational coefficient to a single-season or single-week delta creates a timescale mismatch. The recursive loop runs as follows: crisis triggers habit regression to the mean; observation shows mean reversion did not occur on the analyst's preferred clock; the analyst declares a new regime (counter-trap); the system was actually stable or shifted for exogenous reasons; the model collapses because it never distinguished the generational regression clock from the observational sampling clock.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: stature is bounded by skeletal and endocrinological limits; regression cannot continue below a biologically viable floor or above a structural ceiling imposed by biomechanical loading (square-cube scaling of mass versus cross-sectional area). Galton indexes "Stable forms, 20, 123, 198" and "Stability of characteristics in a people, 163" on [PAGE 276], consistent with a bounded phenotype space.

Biomechanical layer: extreme stature increases gravitational and joint-loading costs, creating stabilizing selection that truncates the tails of the distribution. If the midparental phenotype lies in the extreme tail, the offspring expectation cannot regress linearly indefinitely because the biological support of the trait is finite.

Weather/environmental layer: "Consumptivity, 181" indexed on [PAGE 276] introduces disease-mediated growth suppression independent of midparental phenotype. Let environmental variance be \(\sigma_E^2\) and genetic variance be \(\sigma_G^2\). Narrow-sense heritability is

\[
h^2 = \frac{\sigma_G^2}{\sigma_G^2 + \sigma_E^2},
\]

and the parent–offspring correlation scales with \(h^2\). If \(\sigma_E^2\) dominates—severe deprivation, famine, or epidemic—then \(h^2 \to 0\), \(\rho \to 0\), and \(\beta_{O|P} \to 0\). This is a hard veto: under a crisis environmental regime, offspring phenotype becomes conditionally independent of parental phenotype, and Galton's regression model has zero predictive content. The veto is proof-positive when parent–offspring correlation measured during the crisis falls below the sampling-noise threshold while the population mean shifts, violating the stationarity assumption required by the equations in SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios:

Scenario 1 — Nutritional regime shift. Condition: On a fixed pedigree panel, pre-shift parent–offspring stature correlation \(\rho_{\text{pre}} \approx 0.4\), consistent with the familial regression range indexed at 95–101 on [PAGE 276]. Post-shift (e.g., sustained dietary improvement), \(\rho_{\text{post}} < 0.05\) and population mean stature rises by \(>1\) standard deviation. Prediction: The Galtonian midparental regression model is vetoed; the assumption of distributional stationarity across generations fails. Verifiable by comparing regression slopes on matched families before and after the intervention.

Scenario 2 — Assortative mating collapse. Condition: Spousal stature correlation \(\rho_{MF}\) falls from \(\sim 0.2\) to \(0\) under rapid social change in marriage norms (cf. "Selection in marriage, 85, 157, 147" on [PAGE 276]), while offspring variance remains observed. Prediction: The midparent variance simplification \(\operatorname{Var}(P) \approx \sigma^2/2\) is mis-specified; the regression coefficient attenuates and midparent becomes a poorer predictor of offspring than either parent alone. Verifiable by collecting mother–father–offspring triads across the mating-norm transition.

Scenario 3 — Epidemic consumptivity shock. Condition: A respiratory epidemic (cf. "consumptivity, 181" on [PAGE 276]) strikes one co-fraternity but not a matched control fraternity, invoking the indexed "Variability of Stature in Co-Fraternities, 94" versus "in Fraternities, 124." Prediction: Intra-fraternity variance in the exposed group inflates relative to the unexposed group, destroying the shared-environment assumption that makes fraternal regression meaningful; the distinction between co-fraternity and fraternity variability collapses. Verifiable by comparing within-group coefficients of variation in exposed versus unexposed sibships during the epidemic year.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

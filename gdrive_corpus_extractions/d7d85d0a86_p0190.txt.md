Veto: scale H_{\text{playbook}} downward; remove deep drops and no-huddle packages until S(t) recovers. This is a soft constraint expressed as a multiplier on permissible scheme entropy.

Layer 3 — Weather. Crosswind W (m s^{-1}) exceeding W_{crit} makes the sideline-boxing kick drift unpredictably, risking out-of-bounds or touchback outcomes. Veto: suppress the kicking-game call when |W| > W_{crit}; prefer a downfield center punt or a running play. Precipitation further tightens this veto through ball friction and grip degradation.

Adjudication order. The \text{CallKick} predicate from SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS is evaluated last, after all hard vetoes. The Daly statement that the kicking game should be used only with a superior kicker [PAGE 159] is necessary but not sufficient; a superior kicker under a wind veto or a wet-ball veto still produces a suppressed kick. Biomechanical readiness scales which plays are physically executable; physical/weather vetoes determine which of the executable plays are environmentally safe. No tactical prescription may outrank a failed physical or weather veto. This produces a veto hierarchy: Physical \supset Weather \supset Biomechanical (entropy scaling) \supset Tactical (Daly's kicker-superiority rule).

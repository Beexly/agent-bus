Verifiable condition: the length of the guarantee chain n falls from its historical value to n = 1, eliminating re-guarantee markups. Prediction: search and verification costs rise non-linearly; credit volume contracts for lower-quality borrowers because direct creditors lack the intermediaries' diversification and reputation capital. The "lengthening series of middlemen" [PAGE 363] is revealed as a costly but functional uncertainty-processing technology, not pure rent extraction.

Scenario 3 (Pure creditor indifference). Counterfactual: a creditor holds a claim marketed as having "absolute safety and complete indifference to the conduct of the business" [PAGE 363] while the residual equity behind it is thin. Verifiable condition: monitor the residual net worth E; when E < \delta for small \delta > 0, the creditor's payoff becomes sensitive to residual-owner decisions despite contractual indifference. Prediction: the creditor faces hidden default risk proportional to the variance of the residual owner's strategy; the claimed indifference is an equilibrium illusion sustained only while E remains comfortably positive. The veto triggers when E crosses zero and assessments replace dividends [PAGE 363].

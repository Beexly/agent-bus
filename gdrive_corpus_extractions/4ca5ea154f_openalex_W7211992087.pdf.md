**1. Standardization operator.** [PAGE 4]

$$z_i = \frac{x_i - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}, \qquad \mu_i = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} x_{ki}, \quad \sigma_i = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n}(x_{ki}-\mu_i)^2}$$

Units: $z_i$ dimensionless (standard-deviation units); $x_i$ carries the source unit — events per 90 minutes for rate columns, minutes for `Min`, counts for `TouAtt3rd`, `TklWon`. Breakdown regime: $\sigma_i \to 0$ gives $z_i$ undefined; production `StandardScaler` silently substitutes $\sigma_i = 1$, which converts a constant column into a zero column while leaving its name in the feature list. A second breakdown: $\sigma_i$ estimated on the training partition only, so a test-partition value may exceed the training range by many $\sigma$, producing standardized magnitudes far outside $[-3,3]$ that tree models handle gracefully but linear models (Ridge $R^2 \approx 0.94$, Table 1) extrapolate on without bound.

**2. Pearson correlation.** [PAGE 5]

$$r = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar{x})^2}\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\bar{y})^2}}, \qquad r \in [-1,1]$$

Dimensionless. Used for the heatmap multicollinearity screen; no threshold for dropping collinear columns is stated anywhere in the 16 pages.

**3. Covariance eigenproblem and component scores.** [PAGE 5]

$$\Sigma w_j = \lambda_j w_j, \qquad \Sigma = \frac{1}{n-1}Z^{\top}Z, \qquad PC_k = w_{k1}Z_1 + w_{k2}Z_2 + \cdots + w_{kp}Z_p$$

Units: $\lambda_j$ is variance in $z$-space, hence dimensionless; $w_j$ unit-norm dimensionless; $PC_k$ dimensionless score. Singularity regime: $\operatorname{rank}(\Sigma) \le \min(n-1,\,p)$. With $n_{\text{train}} \approx 0.8 \times 1624 \approx 1299$ and $p$ inflated by one-hot encoding of "Nation, Pos, Squad, Comp" [PAGE 4] on 124 base columns, $\Sigma$ is rank-deficient by construction, giving $\lambda_j = 0$ for the null space and **arbitrary** eigenvector directions there. Any $\lambda_j \approx 0$ component is numerically unstable under resampling: the eigenvector rotates wildly between folds, so a loading of "13.3%" for `SCA_p90` [PAGE 2] is a point estimate with no reported bootstrap interval. Condition-number breakdown: $\kappa(\Sigma) = \lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min} \to \infty$ under the admitted mutual correlation of elite metrics [PAGE 5], meaning the retained subspace is well-determined but individual loading *ranks* within it are not. Sign indeterminacy: $w_j$ and $-w_j$ solve the eigenproblem equally, so the direction of every loading and every SHAP sign statement is a software convention.

**4. Explained-variance bookkeeping (the unreported operator).** The stated rule is "The first $k$ components that explain, for example, ≥ 95% of the cumulative variance are retained" [PAGE 5], i.e. the criterion is

$$\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{k}\lambda_j}{\sum_{j=1}^{p}\lambda_j} \ge 0.95$$

No value of this ratio is reported for either analysis. The only percentage given — "collectively explaining approximately 75% of the component's variance" [PAGE 6] — is a different quantity, the within-component loading concentration $\left(\sum_{i \in \text{top-5}} w_{1i}^2\right)/\|w_1\|^2 \approx 0.75$. Breakdown regime: when $p \gg 1$ and metrics are collinear, $PC1$ can capture a large eigenvalue while the 95% cumulative criterion requires dozens of components; retaining only $PC1$ satisfies interpretability, not the stated rule.

**5. Error and fit functionals.** [PAGE 6] — $\mathrm{MAE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum|y_i - \hat{y}_i|$ (PC-score units), $\mathrm{MSE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2$ (squared PC-score units), $\mathrm{RMSE} = \sqrt{\mathrm{MSE}}$, $R^2 = 1 - SS_{\text{res}}/SS_{\text{tot}}$ (dimensionless). Breakdown regime: $R^2 < 0$ whenever $SS_{\text{res}} > SS_{\text{tot}}$; no negative values appear in Table 1, consistent with the model at minimum reproducing the mean plus volume covariates.

**6. Shapley attributions.** [PAGE 6]

$$\varphi_j = \sum_{S \subseteq F\setminus\{j\}} \frac{|S|!\,\big(|F|-|S|-1\big)!}{|F|!}\Big[\mathrm{val}\big(S\cup\{j\}\big) - \mathrm{val}(S)\Big]$$

Units: model-output units, i.e. PC-score units, so $\varphi$ values of 1.47 and 1.22 are on the same scale as the target's standard deviation by construction (targets are standardized-rate composites). Exact-computation breakdown: coalition count $= 2^{|F|}$; with $|F|$ in the hundreds this exceeds $10^{60}$ evaluations. Approximation breakdown: TreeSHAP's polynomial path is exact only for the path-dependent value function, which attributes credit along single tree paths and therefore splits credit arbitrarily among collinear twins. The paper's own dataset supplies the twins — `TklWon` vs `TklW_p90` [PAGE 5], `ScaPassLive`/`ScaSh`/`ScaDrib` vs the SCA family [PAGE 5] — so the reported ranking "TouAtt3rd ≈ 1.47, minutes ≈ 0.62, ScaPassLive ≈ 0.19, GcaPassLive ≈ 0.11, TouAttPen ≈ 0.07" [PAGE 8–9] is a credit partition whose internal ratios are not identified by the data.

**7. Eligibility gate.** [PAGE 2, PAGE 4] — inclusion iff $\text{Min} > 450$. Since rates are $\dot{x} = 90\,x/\text{Min}$, the gate bounds the amplification factor: at $\text{Min} = 450$ min (five full matches), $\dot{x} = 0.2x$; as $\text{Min} \to 0^{+}$, $\dot{x} \to \infty$. Breakdown regime: the estimator variance of $\dot{x}$ scales as $\mathrm{Var}(\dot{x}) \propto \text{Min}^{-2}\,\mathrm{Var}(x) + \dot{x}^2\,\mathrm{Var}(\text{Min})/\text{Min}^2$, so the 450-min floor is a variance-control device, not a footballing one — and it drops 39.6% of records, all of them low-exposure players.

</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

The paper's operator stack is a century-deep sediment. Pearson's 1901 orthogonal-fit construction and Hotelling's 1933 component analysis supply the eigenproblem $\Sigma w = \lambda w$; Shapley's 1953 cooperative-game value supplies the attribution axiom set; Lundberg & Lee (ref. 44, [PAGE 15]) supply the 2017 grafting of Shapley onto black-box models. None of these were designed for 1,299 rows of collinear sports event counts, and the retrocausal point is that the method's authority is inherited from domains with different sampling regimes — coalition games with a well-defined characteristic function, and machine-learning benchmarks where $n \gg p$.

The football-specific lineage is contested, and the paper knows it. It cites Taylor et al. (ref. 24), Lago (ref. 25) and Lago-Peñas (ref. 26) for situational variables [PAGE 15], then admits on [PAGE 13] that its own model contains "the absence of situational variables (home advantage, opponent quality, match status)." The citation list thus functions as a retrocausal flag: the paper cites the literature that invalidates one of its own scope claims, three pages before a limitations paragraph that concedes the point. The same structure recurs with Decroos et al. (ref. 42, VAEP): [PAGE 11] invokes VAEP precisely to argue that "metrics based solely on volume (shots, touches) can overestimate contributions" — the exact critique that applies to $PC1_{Off}$, which the authors themselves characterize as reflecting "volume and prominence rather than efficiency" [PAGE 10]. The reference list is, read carefully, a pre-emptive peer review of the paper it decorates.

The XAI-defensive lineage runs through Forcher et al. (ref. 43, 2024) on tracking-data recovery prediction, and Pappalardo et al. (ref. 45, PlayeRank) on positionally aware ranking [PAGE 15]. Both use data layers this study lacks; the paper positions its bivariate $PC1_{Off} \times PC1_{Def}$ embedding as a lower-cost analogue [PAGE 12], which is an honest downgrade claim but one that severs the method from the evidence base that made it credible.

Multi-week traps, in the sense of failure modes that only become visible after sustained engagement:

(i) **The "explained variance" trap.** Reading "collectively explaining approximately 75% of the component's variance" [PAGE 6] as "PC1 explains 75% of offensive performance" is a category error, and nothing in the sentence's surface grammar prevents it. Weeks of downstream citation can propagate the misreading before anyone returns to the eigen-decomposition on [PAGE 5].

(ii) **The base-counterpart trap.** The disclosure on [PAGE 5] is buried mid-methods, after the audit claim on [PAGE 4] has already been absorbed. A reader who stops at the audit sentence believes leakage is fully controlled; the correction arrives one paragraph later and is not repeated in the abstract.

(iii) **The temporal-lag trap.** Received 17 November 2025, accepted 19 June 2026, published 9 September 2026 [PAGE 1], on 2022–2023 season data [PAGE 2]. By the publication date the described population is three seasons stale, and the loadings (a 19.1% tackle weight, a 13.3% SCA weight) encode a specific tactical moment — the post-2022 high-press, high-rest-defense configuration — not a football invariant. Any practical application transfers a 2022–23 basis onto a 2026–27 labor market without recalibration.

(iv) **The repeated-measures trap, half-avoided.** "159 players who transferred between two leagues mid-season contributed one record per league, 2,530 + 159 = 2,689" [PAGE 2], and in the analytic subset "1,620 + 4 = 1,624" [PAGE 4]. The player-grouped split [PAGE 5] correctly handles this for evaluation, but the PCA is fit on all training records including both league-rows of the same transfer player, so four to a few dozen individuals contribute twice to the covariance. Small in magnitude, structurally identical to the leakage the paper is otherwise careful about.

</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

**First order (the analyst's own habit).** Given $R^2 = 0.93$ on a defensive index, the default cognitive move is: "we can now predict defensive contribution." This is a crisis habit in the precise sense — under deadline pressure, the conditional distribution over interpretations collapses onto the single highest-salience number, and the hedging clauses that occupy [PAGE 5], [PAGE 6], [PAGE 10] and [PAGE 13] are pruned. The regression is measurable: the word "predict" appears in the abstract's task sentence [PAGE 2] and in the conclusion's opening [PAGE 13], while "consistency and interpretability check" appears four times in the body. Under stress, the reader retains the word that appeared in the abstract.

**Second order (the reader's model of the authors).** A technically literate reader infers that the authors know the number is inflated, because they say so four times, in near-identical wording. That repetition is itself informative: identical phrasing across four locations is the signature of a revision responding to reviewer pressure, not of a spontaneous caveat. The second-order reader therefore correctly reconstructs the paper's history — an earlier draft claimed predictive validity, a reviewer objected, and the hedge was inserted while the abstract and conclusion were left substantially intact.

**Third order (the authors' model of the reader).** The authors anticipate the second-order reader and pre-empt them with the strongest available formulation: "these values should be read as a consistency and interpretability check rather than as substantive predictive validity" [PAGE 7]. This is a genuine inoculation. But inoculation has a cost: once the caveat is stated, the number no longer needs to be defended, and it stays. The counter-trap is to accept the caveat as discharging the obligation. It does not. A caveat is a verbal act; an ablation is a numerical act. The only move that closes the loop is to require the number that the caveat implies — refit without `Min`, without `TklWon`, without the SCA/GCA base components, and report the delta. Until that delta exists, the four hedges are unverifiable verbal commitments.

**The clock.** Three clocks run simultaneously. (a) The season clock: 2022–2023, published September 2026 [PAGE 1–2]. (b) The eligibility clock: the $>450$ min gate [PAGE 2] is a five-match clock that systematically excludes January signings, post-injury returns and rotation players — the gate is not neutral about *when* in the season a player accumulated minutes, only about how many. (c) The cognitive clock: the gap between abstract-absorption (seconds) and methods-audit (hours). The defensive SHAP dominance of `TklWon` at 1.22 [PAGE 9] is only interpretable as near-tautological if the reader has already reached [PAGE 5]; a reader on clock (c) at t = 30 seconds has not.

**Counter-trap synthesis.** The correct posture is neither to accept the $R^2$ nor to reject the paper, but to re-anchor every claim to its operator. "$R^2 = 0.93$" is true and means: a random forest reconstructs a linear combination of standardized per-90 defensive event rates from predictors that include the unnormalized generator of one of those rates plus a playing-time amplifier. That sentence is falsifiable, and the three vetoes below are its test.

</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

The claim under adjudication is any physical, biomechanical or environmental assertion attached to these indices — e.g., "a high $PC1_{Def}$ identifies players who sustain deceleration capacity in the final 15 minutes," or "this profile predicts performance in hot conditions." Adjudication proceeds layer by layer, and the veto is structural rather than statistical.

**Layer 0 — event log (present).** 124 columns of match-event counts and derived per-90 rates from five UEFA leagues [PAGE 3]. Every variable is a count or a ratio of counts over a match, with no timestamp finer than the match.

**Layer 1 — positional/tracking (vetoed).** The authors state the exclusion verbatim: "the exclusive reliance on event data (without tracking)" [PAGE 13]. No $x,y$ coordinates, no inter-line distances, no pitch control, no pressing intensity. Veto proof: a quantity with units of metres or metres-squared cannot be recovered from a count of events; the mapping is many-to-one. Two players with identical tackle counts but opposite closing-speed profiles are indistinguishable in this feature space by construction.

**Layer 2 — biomechanical (vetoed).** No inertial measurement, no accelerometry, no sprint threshold, no change-of-direction load. The paper cites Oliva-Lozano et al. (ref. 7, IMU postural demands) and Yagin et al. (ref. 39, biomechanical position classification) [PAGE 15] as motivating literature, but neither supplies data here. Veto proof: accelerations carry units $\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}$; no column in this dataset does. Furthermore, the per-90 normalization destroys within-match temporal resolution — a per-90 rate is an average over the whole match, so any quantity indexed by match minute (fatigue onset, second-half decay) is analytically annihilated before modeling begins. This is the hardest veto: no augmentation of event data can reconstruct a quantity the pipeline has divided away.

**Layer 3 — environmental/weather (vetoed).** Temperature, humidity, altitude, pitch condition, surface, travel distance and kickoff time appear nowhere in the 16 pages. Veto proof: absent columns carry zero mutual information with any target; no adjustment, stratification or sensitivity analysis is possible.

**Layer 4 — situational (vetoed, explicitly).** "the absence of situational variables (home advantage, opponent quality, match status)" [PAGE 13]. Veto proof: $PC1_{Off}$ and $PC1_{Def}$ are season aggregates, so opposing-team strength is unmodeled variance absorbed into the residual. A defender's tackle volume is partly a function of how often his team loses the ball in dangerous areas — a team-level, opponent-conditional quantity with no representation in the model.

**Layer 5 — fatigue/psychological state (vetoed).** The introduction leans on fatigue and decision-making under load [PAGE 2, refs 10–11], and on mental fatigue meta-analytic work [PAGE 15, refs 27]. Nothing in the feature set measures sleep, wellness, biomarker or perceptual load. Veto proof: the motivating construct is absent from the measurement model, so the indices cannot be validated against it.

**Adjudication verdict.** Physical, biomechanical and environmental claims are **vetoed with prejudice** at Layers 2 and 3, and **vetoed for cause** at Layers 1, 4 and 5. The only layer at which the indices have standing is Layer 0, and there the correct claim is narrow: the indices order players by standardized event volume in a 2022–23 basis. Any cross-layer inference — that the index predicts availability, durability, adaptation to climate, or late-match mechanical output — is not weakly supported but unsupportable with these variables, because the supporting quantities were either never collected or were averaged out by the $90/\text{Min}$ operator.

</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

Each veto is a programmatically checkable binary outcome on the public dataset ("https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivovinco/20222023-football-player-stats" [PAGE 2]) using the paper's own pre-specified hyperparameters (tree models `max_depth = 10`, `min_samples_split = 10`, `n_estimators = 200`, `random_seed = 42` [PAGE 5–6]) and its own player-grouped 80/20 split. No new data collection is required, which is what makes the rewards verifiable rather than rhetorical.

**Veto 1 — Playing-time ablation (tests the exposure-vs-signal claim).**
Operation: drop `num__Min` and `num__90s`, refit Random Forest and Ridge on the identical player-grouped partition, recompute $R^2$, MAE, RMSE and the SHAP ranking.
Prediction: $R^2_{Off}$ falls from 0.91 to the band **0.72–0.82**, and $R^2_{Def}$ from 0.93 to **0.78–0.86**. Critically, SHAP mass does **not** vanish; it redistributes onto the unnormalized count proxies — `TouAtt3rd` (already 1.47), `Touches`, `Starts` — because raw counts are themselves monotone in minutes. Falsification: if $R^2_{Off}$ stays $\ge 0.88$ after removing both time columns, the "involvement and exposure" interpretation on [PAGE 9] is wrong and the index carries genuine non-exposure signal.

**Veto 2 — Semantic-leakage ablation (tests the [PAGE 5] concession).**
Operation: remove the admitted base counterparts — `TklWon`, blocked passes, blocked shots, `ScaPassLive`, `ScaSh`, `ScaDrib`, `GcaPassLive` — and refit.
Prediction: $R^2_{Def}$ drops from 0.93 to **0.74–0.84**, and the top defensive SHAP attribute shifts from `TklWon` (1.22) to `TklDri` (0.12) or `Touches_p90` (0.44), i.e. the ranking reorders by more than one position. Offensively, $R^2_{Off}$ drops modestly (to **0.85–0.89**) while the `ScaPassLive`/`GcaPassLive` SHAP pair (0.19, 0.11) collapses toward $\le 0.05$. Falsification: if $R^2_{Def}$ remains $\ge 0.90$ after removing all seven base components, then the composite-metric leakage flagged on [PAGE 5] is empirically inert and the paper's self-criticism was over-cautious.

**Veto 3 — Basis-transfer / subspace-rotation test (tests the temporal and league generality claim).**
Operation: fit the offensive PCA on four leagues and project the fifth (leave-one-league-out), or refit on 2022–23 and project 2023–24 from the same source. Compute (a) $\Delta R^2$ and (b) the principal angle between the two $PC1_{Off}$ loading vectors, $\theta = \arccos|\langle w_{1}^{(A)}, w_{1}^{(B)}\rangle|$ using absolute value to neutralize the sign indeterminacy of the eigenproblem.
Prediction: $\Delta R^2_{Off} \le -0.10$ absolute, and $\theta \ge 15^{\circ}$ (equivalently $|\cos\theta| < 0.966$). The rotation will be visible in the loading ranks: `SCA_p90`'s 13.3% share and `RecProg_p90`'s 13.0% share [PAGE 2] will swap order or fall below `Shots_p90`'s 12.7%. Falsification: if $\theta < 5^{\circ}$ and $\Delta R^2 < 0.03$, the eigenbasis is tactically stable across leagues and the single-season limitation on [PAGE 13] is conservative rather than disqualifying.

**Composite reward rule.** Score 1 for each prediction confirmed in the stated band; score 0 otherwise; the paper's central interpretive claim ("consistency and interpretability check rather than evidence of independent predictive validity" [PAGE 10]) survives only if **Veto 1 AND Veto 2 confirm**. If both ablate to $R^2 \ge 0.90$, the four hedges were unnecessary and the abstract's verb "predict" [PAGE 2] was earned. If either collapses below 0.80, the hedges were correct and the abstract is the document's weakest sentence.

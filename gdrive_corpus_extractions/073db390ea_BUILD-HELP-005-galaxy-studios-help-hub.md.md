   $$ H(p, q) = -\sum_{x \in M} p(x) \log_2 q(x) \le \mu \cdot T_{max} $$

Historically, sports betting interfaces were designed purely for transaction speed (placing wagers), treating clarity as secondary to volume—a lineage that led to massive consumer harm and subsequent regulatory crackdowns (e.g., PASPA revocation, strict KYC/responsible gaming mandates). BUILD-HELP-005 represents a retrocausal correction: the future necessity of legal survivability dictates present UI patterns. The mandate for "clear disclosures and responsible-play-safe copy" [PAGE 1] is a direct descendant of these regulatory shocks, forcing the system to prioritize "trust-safe media" over aggressive conversion funnels.

Scientifically, this traces back to Herbert Simon’s theory of bounded rationality and the subsequent development of "Nudge Theory" by Thaler and Sunstein. The help hub acts as an architectural nudge, steering users away from high-variance, poorly understood proprietary metrics toward calculated, comprehensible edges. Furthermore, the "10-second comprehension" metric mirrors the Fitts' Law applications in GUI design but extends it into the temporal domain of cognitive load (Sweller’s Cognitive Load Theory). 

The multi-week trap lurking here is the "Confidence Trap." When a help system successfully compresses complex, high-entropy methodologies into a 10-second summary, users often mistake this *comprehension of the summary* for *comprehension of the underlying math*. This creates a false sense of security (overconfidence effect). Over weeks, if the market dynamics shift and the simplified heuristics fail, users do not attribute the loss to their superficial understanding (facilitated by the help hub) but to the flaw in the GSE methodology itself, severely damaging brand equity. Thus, the "no fake certainty" constraint [PAGE 1] is a vital defense against this genealogical trap.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The viability of the Galaxy Studios help hub is subject to absolute vetoes originating from physical, biomechanical, and environmental layers. These are non-negotiable constraints that can instantly invalidate the P0 priority.

**1. Biomechanical / Vestibular Veto:**
The implementation plan explicitly requires "reduced-motion behavior" [PAGE 1]. Without this, any "video, motion... visual" [PAGE 1] intended to aid the 10-second comprehension can trigger severe vestibular responses (nausea, vertigo, disorientation) in users with vestibular mismatch disorders.
*Adjudication:* If the default state of the help media contains parallax scrolling, rapid transitions, or frequency flickering > 3Hz, a biomechanical veto is triggered. The feature must dynamically detect OS-level `prefers-reduced-motion` flags and instantly degrade to a static, high-contrast textual representation. Failure to do so renders the product unusable for a protected class of users.

**2. Physical Environment / Contextual Noise Veto:**
Sports betting frequently occurs in high-noise environments (stadiums, bars, crowded living rooms during major games). The assumption that a user can process a "short summary" and "next action" [PAGE 1] within 10 seconds assumes a baseline visual acuity and focus.
*Adjudication:* If the user is outdoors in bright sunlight or in a chaotic environment, small font sizes or low-contrast visuals (common in dark-mode betting apps) become illegible. The UI must possess extreme legibility; otherwise, the physical environment vetos the information transfer, causing the "understands the item in 10 seconds" acceptance criteria [PAGE 1] to fail completely.

**3. Regulatory / Legal Boundary Veto:**
The prompt establishes a hard boundary: "No fake certainty" and "Promotions/risk surfaces need clear disclosures" [PAGE 1]. 
*Adjudication:* If the automated generation of "human sounding" copy [PAGE 1] inadvertently implies guaranteed returns or obscures the house edge, a legal veto is invoked. Compliance algorithms must continuously scan the generated help text. If the semantic probability of implied certainty crosses a predefined threshold, the text must be instantly sanitized or blocked. A failure here results not just in UX issues, but in license revocation.

These vetoes demonstrate that the software component does not exist in a vacuum; it is tethered to the biological limits of the user and the rigid constraints of international gambling law.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

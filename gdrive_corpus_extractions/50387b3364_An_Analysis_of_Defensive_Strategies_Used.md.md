\[
\Pr(s_{t+1}=j\mid \mathcal H_t)=
\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(
\theta_{0j}+\theta_{1j}\mathrm{Home}
+\theta_{2j}\mathrm{Crisis}_t
+\theta_{3j}\mathrm{OpponentAnticipation}_t

\right),
\]

where each \(\theta\) is dimensionless on the log-odds scale. This is not a fake ratio; it is a probability model. The source does not estimate this model. It reports only aggregate strategy counts and factorial ANOVA [PAGE 4–5].

The recursive conclusion is that the observed home/away pattern is a reduced-form signature of unmeasured strategic recursion, not a direct readout of a stable home-team defensive habit.

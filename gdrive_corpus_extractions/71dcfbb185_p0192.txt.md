Galton’s “law of ancestral inheritance” posited that offspring inherit proportional contributions from parents, grandparents, and more remote ancestors—a blending, continuous-fluid model of heredity that preceded and competed with Mendelian particulate inheritance (rediscovered 1900). The statistical machinery Galton built—regression toward mediocrity, correlation, the normal deviate—was designed to measure this blending fluid without assuming particulate matter.

The specific disease under study, consumption (tuberculosis, *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*), sat at the pivot of contagionist and hereditarian explanatory frameworks. Robert Koch identified the tubercle bacillus in 1882, yet the source text still speaks of “consumptive taint” as a family-level scalar quantity rather than as infection by a transmissible agent. This language reifies susceptibility as a substance-like property transmitted through the “fraternity,” conflating shared blood with shared air. Galton’s table compares line A (one parent consumptive) with line B (one brother consumptive) [PAGE 174], attempting to separate vertical from horizontal transmission, but the design cannot measure household crowding, ventilation, duration of exposure to an active case, or nutritional status—all known physical drivers of TB transmission. Consequently, the “taint” is a composite of exposure, infection, progression to active disease, and case fatality, with no layer separated from another.

Over the course of a weeks-long investigation, three recursive traps compound: (1) the essentialist trap, in which “taint” is treated as a stable latent trait rather than a noisy 19th-century clinical judgment; (2) the aggregation trap, in which the fraternity-level mean of 28 per cent [PAGE 174] is projected onto an individual child as if it were a personal probability; and (3) the stationarity trap, in which the conditional distribution p_A is assumed to hold across time and place despite being estimated from a single, non-representative sample of 66 fraternities. These traps are “multi-week” because they persist from initial data coding, through analysis, into final causal claims, and they are difficult to detect because each step appears locally reasonable.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression describes the tendency of observers, under diagnostic and causal uncertainty, to fall back on essentialist, substance-like concepts—“taint,” “blood,” “constitution”—rather than maintaining rigorous probabilistic thinking. Galton’s own method is statistical: he averages fraternity-level scores and tabulates distributions [PAGE 174]. Yet the object being averaged, “consumptive taint,” is itself a reified clinical impression. The observer sees a consumptive parent and infers a tainted lineage; the statistical mean of 28 per cent [PAGE 174] then functions as an anchor that converts population-level heterogeneity into individual-level dread.

The counter-trap is that Galton invented regression and correlation precisely to escape such essentialism, but here the tool feeds back into the trap. The mean taint of 28 per cent is not a risk for any specific child; it is an average over 66 fraternities whose internal distribution is strongly non-normal: 27 per cent of fraternities show 0–under 9 per cent taint, while 29 per cent show 40 and above [PAGE 174]. Applying the mean clock-like to every child of a consumptive parent commits the ecological fallacy and ignores that the ensemble is split between near-zero and near-total taint families. The recursive theory of mind runs: observer → parent status → invokes 28 per cent mean → treats child as if drawn from that mean → neglects the observed bimodality → overestimates risk for resistant families and underestimates it for susceptible ones.

The clock operates across generations: a child identified as “tainted” becomes a parent in the next generation, and the mean is reapplied as if the process were ergodic (time average equals ensemble average). This fails if susceptibility is family-specific and persistent, because then the ensemble distribution does not describe the temporal trajectory of any single lineage. The crisis habit is to treat the 28 per cent as a deterministic clock rather than a conditional summary of a heterogeneous, possibly non-stationary population.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: Tuberculosis is caused by airborne *M. tuberculosis*. Familial clustering can be fully explained by shared household exposure—close contact, shared sleeping quarters, poor ventilation—without any heritable biological transmission. Galton’s fraternity-as-unit design controls for family membership but does not measure exposure dose, index-case sputum load, or contact hours. Therefore the physical transmission layer is an unblocked backdoor path between parental status and offspring taint.

Biomechanical/physiological layer: Host susceptibility depends on immune competence (macrophage activation, T-cell response), nutritional status, comorbidities, and age. None of these are measured. “Consumptive taint” collapses exposure, infection, progression, and fatality into one scalar. A veto at this layer: if two fraternities have identical parental consumptive status but different household crowding or nutrition, their τ_f values need not be equal; Galton’s model has no term for these physiological moderators.

Weather/environmental layer: TB transmission exhibits seasonal patterns tied to indoor crowding and ventilation, which vary with weather and housing architecture. Families sharing a home share a microclimate; this environmental covariance is not distinguished from hereditary covariance in the source.

Veto proof and breakdown regime: The formal veto condition is that the covariance among fraternity members’ taint scores be fully explained by a shared exposure variable E_f (e.g., E_f = index-case infectiousness × hours of contact × inverse ventilation). If Cov(τ_{f,i}, τ_{f,j} | E_f) = 0 for all i ≠ j, then the residual heritable variance h² → 0 and Galton’s “taint” reduces to a transmission statistic. The source cannot reject this null because E_f is unmeasured. The breakdown regime in which heredity could be isolated is E_f ≈ 0 across all families (no shared exposure) while τ_f still correlates with parental status; that regime is not established in the text. Until shared environment is measured and conditioned upon, the hereditary interpretation is vetoed.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

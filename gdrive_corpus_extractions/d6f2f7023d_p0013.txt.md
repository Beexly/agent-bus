Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with predictions.

Veto scenario 1 (peripheral afferent blockade): Set \(J_a = 0\) via reversible nerve block while cortex is intact and reproductive processes \(\mathcal{R}\) are available. Verification: concurrent electrophysiological confirmation of absent afferent volley plus first-person report.
Prediction: subject reports no sensation and no perception of the blocked modality; only intellectual thought about the modality remains possible. This veto falsifies any account in which sensation can be generated cortically without peripheral afference, in line with James's nerve-current condition [PAGE 1].

Veto scenario 2 (cortical reproductive suppression): Keep \(J_a > 0\) intact but pharmacologically or focal-cortically suppress associative/reproductive volume so \(V_{\mathcal{R}} \to 0\). Verification: fMRI/EEG showing reduced cortical associative recruitment with preserved primary sensory-evoked potentials.
Prediction: perception collapses toward sensation; subject reports bare immediate qualities (hard, hot, pain) with minimal relational or object knowledge, matching James's "minimum of perception" regime [PAGE 1]. This veto falsifies accounts that treat perception as separable from sensation without a reproductive-volume parameter.

Veto scenario 3 (adult introspection of pure sensation): Ask trained adult subjects to isolate a "pure sensation" free of all relational knowledge. Verification: structured phenomenological interview coded for relational contamination (references to object, cause, name, category).
Prediction: all reports contain relational contamination; no subject can sustain the \(V_{\mathcal{R}} = 0\) state, because "A pure sensation is an abstraction" [PAGE 1]. This veto falsifies the introspective claim that adults can directly encounter pure sensation as a stable empirical episode; it also undermines retrospective ascriptions that infant thought is verifiably "almost exclusively sensational" [PAGE 1].

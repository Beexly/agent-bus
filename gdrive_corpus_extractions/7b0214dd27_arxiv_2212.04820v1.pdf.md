Camera-based deep learning avoids subject assistance [PAGE 1], but current estimators are trained on MS COCO [PAGE 1, ref. 12] and MPII [PAGE 1, ref. 5], datasets that lack loose-fitting winter sportswear [PAGE 1]. <CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Veto analysis across physical, biomechanical, and environmental layers:

Physical layer veto (photon transport): The method requires LED photons to traverse the garment and reach the sensor [PAGE 2]. The authors deliberately select thin polyester or linen [PAGE 2]. A formal veto condition is:
\begin{equation}
I_{\text{trans}} = I_0 e^{-\mu d} < I_{\text{noise}} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{veto},
\end{equation}
where $I_{\text{noise}}$ is the camera readout noise at the HFR exposure. Thick waterproof ski shells, laminated membranes, multi-layer winter gear, or wet fabric raise $\mu d$ beyond the transmission threshold. Under this veto, no amount of frame-rate manipulation recovers the joint position because the LED is optically extinct.

Biomechanical layer veto (fabric–body decoupling): Loose clothing does not move rigidly with the skeleton. During high-jerk events (direction reversal on the ski simulator [PAGE 4]), the garment exhibits inertial lag and continues translating after the joint has reversed [PAGE 4, Figure 9]. Formal veto condition:
\begin{equation}
\| \mathbf{v}_{\text{fabric}}(t) - \mathbf{v}_{\text{joint}}(t) \| > \epsilon_{\text{decouple}} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{veto},
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon_{\text{decouple}}$ is the maximum tolerable fabric–joint relative velocity for sub-pixel annotation alignment. When violated, the LED-on frame and the LED-off frame contain inconsistent anatomical references even if the LED itself is perfectly placed.

Environmental/weather layer veto: The source describes an indoor ski simulator [PAGE 3, Figure 5], not outdoor field conditions. Cold-weather deployment introduces wind-driven fabric flutter, snow accumulation on LEDs, condensation, and glove-mediated LED placement error. A veto triggers if ambient fabric motion induced by wind exceeds the 8.33 ms inter-frame tolerance or if LED duty cycle cannot freeze fabric flutter.

Adjudication: The method is veto-proof only within the narrow regime of (i) thin transmissive fabric, (ii) quasi-static inter-frame motion below the pixel-tolerance threshold, (iii) single unoccluded subject, and (iv) controlled illumination. Outside this regime, each layer independently invalidates the ground-truth pipeline.

Under this strict independence assumption, the variance of $M$ is:
\[
\text{Var}(M) = \text{Var}\left(\frac{H_{father} + H_{mother}}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4} \left( \text{Var}(H_{father}) + \text{Var}(H_{mother}) \right).

\[
\text{Var}(M) = \frac{2\sigma_H^2}{4} = \frac{\sigma_H^2}{2}.

\[
\sigma_M = \sqrt{\frac{\sigma_H^2}{2}} = \frac{\sigma_H}{\sqrt{2}}.

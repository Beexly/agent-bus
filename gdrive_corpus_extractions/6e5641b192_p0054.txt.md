<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
James constructs a hierarchy of internal states to explain imagination:
1.  **External Stimulus:** The primary cause.
2.  **Sensation:** The immediate effect on the nervous organism.
3.  **After-image:** Physiological persistence (belongs to sensation).
4.  **Tardier Image (*Sinnesgedächtniss*):** A psychological echo occurring hours later.
5.  **Fantasy/Imagination:** The voluntary or involuntary faculty dealing with these echoes.

In a crisis scenario—such as severe trauma—the "Theory of Mind" regresses. The individual may lose the ability to distinguish between "tardier images" (flashbacks) and current reality.

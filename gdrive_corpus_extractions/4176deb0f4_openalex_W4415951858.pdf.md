The recursive stack has four levels. Level 0 is the detector’s belief: YOLOv8 assigns boxes and scores under a 0.5 confidence threshold [PAGE 6]. Each layer assumes the layer below is more certain than it is.

Crisis habit regression appears under occlusion, crowded play, and close-game pressure.

It determines the decay scale of the tail bound \omega(T) in (5) on [PAGE 6] and the exponential factor e^{\rho T} in the strong-approximation estimate on [PAGE 10]. In recursive reasoning, \rho defines how many steps ahead the analyst’s value function remains sensitive to current control: beyond O(1/\rho), the discounted log and control terms are negligible. A model that misspecifies \rho—particularly near the critical regime noted in the example on [PAGE 18] where [9] fails for \rho\le1—misaligns the planning clock and invalidates both the existence argument and the adjoint asymptotics.

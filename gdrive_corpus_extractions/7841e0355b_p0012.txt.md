Decision fatigue collapses the affinity signal: as |K_u| grows, the weighted sum discriminability degrades.

Weather veto: No weather input appears in the disclosure [PAGE 5–6], so the algorithm has no intrinsic meteorological veto gate. However, exogenous physical events can invalidate the utility of cart-based recommendations: if a user loads a Father's Day gift cart [PAGE 6] and a severe storm compromises delivery before the target date, the recommendation remains algorithmically valid but physically undeliverable. Because the system does not ingest delivery-constraint or weather data, it cannot veto the recommendation; the veto must occur at the fulfillment/logistics layer, outside the recommendation service’s modeled boundary.

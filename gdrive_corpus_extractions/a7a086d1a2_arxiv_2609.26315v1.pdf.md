<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
All equations below are the paper's own; units and breakdown regimes are my annotations. Page markers are exact.

Semantic field compositing [PAGE 3, Eq. 1]. For pixel $u$ with front-to-back Gaussians $(i_1,\dots,i_{K_u})$ and compositing weight $w_{i_k}(u)=\sigma_{i_k}G_{i_k}(u)\prod_{\ell<k}(1-\sigma_{i_\ell}G_{i_\ell}(u))$:
$$S(u)=\sum_{k=1}^{K_u} w_{i_k}(u)\,s_{i_k},\qquad A(u)=\sum_{k=1}^{K_u} w_{i_k}(u).$$
Units: $\sigma\in[0,1]$ dimensionless opacity, $s_i\in\mathbb{R}^{C}$ dimensionless logits, $C=6$. $A(u)$ is accumulated opacity in $[0,1]$.

Opacity normalization [PAGE 3, Eq. 2]: $\hat p(u)=\mathrm{softmax}\!\big(S(u)/\max(A(u),\epsilon)\big)$, $\epsilon=10^{-3}$.
Breakdown analysis: the regime of interest is $A(u)\to 0$. Because $S(u)$ is a convex-in-weight sum of bounded logits, $S(u)\to 0$ as $A(u)\to 0$; the $\max(\cdot,\epsilon)$ floor prevents $0/0$ and drives $\hat p\to\mathrm{softmax}(0)=\text{uniform}$, which is why the authors state it "leaves fully opaque pixels unchanged" ($A=1$) and only rescues the low-$\alpha$ thin-structure band. Verbatim: "Low accumulated opacity attenuates the logits toward zero and their softmax toward uniform, particularly on thin or in-complete structures such as trunks and trellis."

Semantic loss [PAGE 3, Eq. 3]: $L_{sem}=-\frac{1}{|\Omega|}\sum_{u\in\Omega} w_u\log \hat p_{y(u)}(u)$, class weights $w_c$ uniform.

Uncertainty weighting [PAGE 3, Eq. 4]: $L=\sum_k\big(\tfrac12 e^{-v_k}L_k+\tfrac12 v_k\big)$, $v_k$ learnable log-variance, init 0. Singularity/pathology regime: $\partial L/\partial v_k=-\tfrac12 e^{-v_k}L_k+\tfrac12=0\Rightarrow e^{v_k}=1/L_k$, so any term with larger residual $L_k$ is automatically down-weighted. The authors' key move: pin $v_{sem}=-2.5\Rightarrow$ weight $\tfrac12 e^{2.5}\approx 6.1$. Ablation [PAGE 7/8]: learned $v_{sem}$ "decays to 0.28," buying $+0.94$ dB but costing $0.104$ training-view trunk IoU.

Class-budgeted allocation [PAGE 3, Eq. 5]: cap $a_c=\min(|D_c|,\lfloor\kappa_c B\rfloor)$; floors $q_c=\min(\lfloor B\bar\phi_c\rfloor,a_c)$, $\bar\phi_c=\phi_c/\sum_{c'}\phi_{c'}$. Scale-invariant in $\phi_c$, independent of $B$. Breakdown: undefined if $\sum\phi_{c'}=0$; if $B<\sum_c q_c$ reservations are internally truncated by the outer $\min$.

Evidence-weighted voting [PAGE 3, Eq. 6]: $c^\star(x)=\arg\max_c \pi_c^{-\beta}\sum_{i\in V(x)} w_i\,p_i(c\mid x)$, $\beta=\tfrac12$. Singularity regime: $\pi_c$ is "class $c$'s share of accumulated evidence"; for an unobserved class $\pi_c\to 0\Rightarrow \pi_c^{-\beta}\to\infty$, multiplied by a vanishing evidence sum — a $0\cdot\infty$ indeterminate that in practice is masked because prompted classes always accumulate some evidence. $\beta=1$ "can overemphasize rare classes"; $\beta=0$ removes reweighting entirely.

Height gate [PAGE 4, Eq. 7]: relabel to GND if $h(x)<\tau_h=0.15$ m, where $h(x)$ is height above the median of ground-labeled voxels in the $1\,\mathrm{m}^2$ cell; cells with $<10$ ground voxels unchanged. Overshoot gate [PAGE 3]: discard trunk observations $>t=0.5$ m behind the affine-mapped monocular surface.

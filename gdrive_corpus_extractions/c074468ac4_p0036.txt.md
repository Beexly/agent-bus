$$ H_{TE} = - \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}} P(r|E) \log P(r|E) $$
Units: Nats or Bits. This measures the initial variability of response mentioned in the text ("Mere variability of response").

**Operator 2: Persistence Functional ($\mathcal{P}$)**
$$ \mathcal{P}[R(t)] = \int_{t_0}^{t_f} \mathbb{I}(R(t) \approx R_{target}) dt $$
Where $\mathbb{I}$ is the indicator function. This maps to "persistence-until." However, Tolman notes this is insufficient without selection.

**Operator 3: Resultant Docility ($D$)**
Docility is defined as the reduction in conditional entropy of future responses given past environmental interactions. 
$$ D(\tau) = H(R_{t+\tau} | E_{t}) - H(R_{t+\tau} | E_{t}, \text{History}_{t}) $$
Assumptions: The system operates under ergodic conditions during the trial phase. The "selection among tries" implies a non-stationary Markov process converging to a stationary policy.

It is, in fact, the foundational moment for Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and modern Reinforcement Learning. If Tolman had not demanded "docility" as the strict gatekeeper for defining purpose, psychology might have remained entrenched in phenomenological descriptions of "feeling driven," completely unable to quantify learning curves or map neural correlates of value prediction errors. The lineage flows directly from this page: Tolman’s "cognitions" are precursors to "internal models," and his rejection of McDougall’s "psychic somewhat" paves the way for strictly materialist neuroscience.

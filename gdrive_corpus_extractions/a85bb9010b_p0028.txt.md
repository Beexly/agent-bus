Sleep detection in wearables descends from actigraphy and polysomnographic staging rules (Rechtschaffen & Kales; AASM manuals), translated into consumer photoplethysmography and accelerometer fusion algorithms during the 2010s. Whoop's patent filing circa 2015–2017 belongs to the generation of wrist-worn recovery platforms that compute a nightly autonomic baseline and compare it against acute training load.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
The coach's theory of mind on [PAGE 1] operates recursively across three layers: the athlete's self-report, the coach's anticipated response, and the algorithm's surfaced alert. Crisis habit regression occurs when the coach, seeing Kathy Wall "PAINFUL / LAST WORKOUT TODAY" or Will Ahmed "PAINFUL / LAST WORKOUT YESTERDAY," reflexively prescribes rest or load reduction. This is the crisis habit: pain triggers protective withdrawal, which is appropriate for acute injury risk but may interrupt a planned overreaching block if the soreness is benign delayed-onset muscle soreness.

The counter-trap is the "OFF SCHEDULE" override. Terry Porter is flagged both "LAST WORKOUT 1H AGO" and "OFF SCHEDULE." The coach may treat the schedule flag as a hard veto and cancel the 11:00 AM weights-and-endurance session, even if the session was a deliberately prescribed overload stimulus. The counter-trap is thus over-correction: allowing a behavioral flag to displace physiological readiness, or conversely ignoring the soreness proxy because the calendar says the session must happen.

The clock layer supplies urgency heuristics. Wall-clock entries ("10:00 AM," "11:00 AM," "2:30 PM," "3:30 PM") and recency fields ("1H AGO," "LAST WORKOUT TODAY") compress decision time. The recursive theory-of-mind failure mode is deception under observation: if athletes know that "PAINFUL" triggers a "TO MOVE" intervention, they may under-report soreness to preserve their scheduled session. The algorithm then loses its ground-truth input, and the coach models an athlete who models the coach's likely response to pain reports. This social-desirability trap breaks the feedback loop between subjective recovery and objective HRV, rendering the veto system blind to the very state it was designed to protect.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

Biomechanical layer (extraocular plant): vergence ceiling \( V_{\max} \approx 0.45\text{–}0.52\ \mathrm{rad} \) imposes \( Z_{\min} = b/(2\tan(V_{\max}/2)) \approx 0.10\text{–}0.14\ \mathrm{m} \). If a task demands fixation at \( Z < Z_{\min} \), the optical axes cannot intersect at \( C \); James's claim that the fixated point "is seen both single and at its proper distance" fails at the plant level. Convergence insufficiency (measured in prism diopters, \(1\ \Delta \approx 0.01\ \mathrm{rad}\)) further reduces effective \( V_{\max} \), vetoing single perception in near work.

Weather/atmospheric layer (natural viewing contrast): atmospheric scattering lowers Michelson contrast \( C \). Stereoacuity degrades approximately as \( \delta_P(C) = \delta_P(1) / C^\gamma \) with \( \gamma \approx 0.5\text{–}1 \). If \( C < C_{\min} \approx 0.1 \), effective \( \delta_P \) inflates by an order of magnitude, converting fusible disparities into diplopic ones. Thus even perfect optics and intact muscles cannot guarantee a single percept under low contrast; the environmental layer vetoes the projection-theory's reliance on judgment.

Adjudication rule: compute the minimal layer threshold \( \delta_{\text{eff}} = \max(\delta_P, |\sigma|, \delta_P(C)) \) and the geometric demand \( |\delta| = b/Z \). If \( |\delta| > \delta_{\text{eff}} \) or \( Z < Z_{\min} \), output "single percept" is vetoed a priori; judgment cannot rescue it. This renders James's projection-theory escape clause — "provided only it be located by the judgment" [PAGE 238] — necessary but far from sufficient.

Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with predictions.

Veto scenario 1 — induced phoria beyond fusional range. Intervention: place base-out prism of \( \Delta = 10\ \Delta \) (≈ 0.1 rad ≈ 5.7°) before one eye, inducing a tonic disparity demand. Verifiable measurement: alternate cover test quantifies phoria \( \phi_p \) in prism diopters; eye tracker records fixation. Veto condition: \( \phi_p > \delta_P \) expressed in prism diopters (foveal limit ≈ 5–10 Δ). Prediction: any model that predicts a single cyclopean percept derived from "judgment alone" is vetoed. Observed output: obligatory diplopia or suppression for centrally presented targets; depth estimate \( Z \approx b/|\delta| \) unavailable because the system cannot fuse. Counterfactual: if \( \Delta \) were reduced below 5 Δ, single percept returns; the switch point is verifiable at the 5–10 Δ boundary.

Veto scenario 2 — near target inside the convergence near point. Intervention: present a high-contrast target at \( Z = 0.05\ \mathrm{m} \) (5 cm). Verifiable measurement: laser rangefinder confirms \( Z \); near point of convergence (NPC) measured optometrically at ≈ 0.07–0.10 m. Veto condition: \( Z < Z_{\min} \approx 0.10\ \mathrm{m} \), so required vergence \( V = 2\arctan(b/2Z) \approx 0.70\ \mathrm{rad} \) (≈ 40°) exceeds \( V_{\max} \). Prediction: model output "seen both single and at its proper distance" [PAGE 238] is vetoed. Observed output: accommodative-convergence mismatch, blur, and diplopia; no valid depth from binocular triangulation.

Veto scenario 3 — suprathreshold disparity in random-dot stereogram. Intervention: dynamic random-dot stereogram with known horizontal disparity \( \delta = 100\ \mathrm{arcsec} \approx 4.85 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{rad} \) foveally, which lies above typical stereoacuity but within or near Panum's limit; use anaglyphic or mirror stereoscope to isolate binocular disparity. Verifiable measurement: eye tracker confirms vergence fixation; disparity value is set by the stimulus generator. Veto condition: if \( |\delta| > \delta_P(\text{observer}) \), where individual \( \delta_P \) is measured separately (e.g., 30–60 arcsec for stereoacuity-normal observers, higher for stereo-blind). Prediction: identity-theory model assigning a single seen direction to corresponding points is vetoed; observed percept is diplopia or rivalry, not single fusion. Counterfactual prediction: halving the effective baseline \( b \) (e.g., by viewing through a reduced-aperture binocular viewer) doubles \( \Delta Z/Z \) for fixed \( \Delta\delta \), so depth-discrimination threshold degrades by factor 2 — verifiable with a mirror stereoscope and adjustable interpupillary baseline. Any betting-style odds are irrelevant; verification is purely psychophysical and geometric.

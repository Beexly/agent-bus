\theta = 2 \arctan\!\left(\frac{i}{2 z_F}\right) \quad [\mathrm{rad}]
where i is interpupillary distance [m]. Breakdown: \theta \to 0 as z_F \to \infty; extraocular palsy or paralysis severs the \theta\!-\!z_F map.

Binocular disparity (first-order):
\delta \approx f_B \frac{z_F - z_i}{z_F z_i} \quad [\mathrm{deg}]
with focal length f_B [m]. Breakdown: monocular viewing removes \delta; \delta = 0 when z_i = z_F.

Primitive nativist signal (Hering/Stumpf antecedent):
s_0 = h(A,\theta,\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{optic}})
where \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{optic}} is the specific energy of the optic nerve or an organic afferent state, treated here in information units [bits\,s^{-1}] or dimensionless afferent state. Assumption: h is well-defined and monotonic in A and \theta. Breakdown: if accommodation is pharmacologically paralyzed, \partial s_0/\partial z_F = 0 despite variation in z_F; if \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{optic}} cannot be isolated psychophysically, h is unidentifiable.

Experience operator and composite percept:
\mathcal{E}: \mathcal{H}\times\mathcal{C} \to \mathbb{R}, \qquad
\hat{z}_i = w\,s_0(O_i) + (1-w)\,\mathcal{E}(O_i,\mathcal{C}) + \varepsilon_i,
w \in [0,1], \quad \varepsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)\quad [\mathrm{m}]
Assumption: linear superposition of a primitive component and an associative “Experience” component over history \mathcal{H} and context \mathcal{C}. James credits higher cerebral processes with “fully one-half part” [PAGE 1], but w is not measured here. Breakdown: s_0 and \mathcal{E} are collinear in natural viewing (accommodation co-varies with learned distance), so w is not identifiable from [PAGE 1] data; under sensory deprivation \mathcal{H} = \emptyset, \mathcal{E} cannot be estimated.

Lipps visibility predicate:
\mathrm{Vis}(x,y) \equiv (x\in\mathcal{V}) \land (y\in\mathcal{V}) \land (x,y \text{ appear beside each other})
Since the eye e \notin \mathcal{V}, \mathrm{Vis}(e,O_i)=\mathrm{False}, hence d(e,O_i) \notin \text{directly visual observables}. Third-dimension operator:
T = C(\mathcal{V})
where C is conceptual and not a metric component of \mathcal{V}; T is dimensionless on this page. Breakdown: the predicate fails under occlusion (near hides far) but may still permit relational depth from motion parallax or stereopsis, so the Lipps veto is logically local to eye-distance, not global to all depth.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage on [PAGE 1] runs from Berkeley, as reviewed by Ferrier in Philosophical Remains I, to Lipps’s Psychologische Studien (p. 69), then into James’s chapter; Hering and Stumpf are mediated through Sully in Mind; Abbott appears via Sight and Touch; Panum is cited as the sole other immediate-sensation theorist [PAGE 1]. The recurring multi-generational trap is retrocausal explanatory debt: the percept at time t is explained by an unobservable “Experience” accumulated before t, so the cause is placed in the subject’s hidden past rather than in measurable proximal variables. A secondary trap is the authority cascade: Sully’s English report of Hering/Stumpf, and James’s footnote nesting, can convert contested nativist claims into received text, as seen when Abbott’s “obscure” theory is referenced only by location [PAGE 1].

Multi-week traps. First, assuming the weight w in the composite percept is stable across weeks when prism- or lens-adaptation experiments show cue reweighting on timescales of hours to days; cross-session drift may then be misread as permanent “experience” rather than transient recalibration. Second, targeting absolute eye-distance d(e,O_i) when the stimulus optically supports only surface relations or relative intervals, exactly the condition Lipps isolates [PAGE 1]; a null result for eye-distance is then overgeneralized to “no visual depth.” Third, OCR and attribution errors propagate: the printed page number 221 and the file label p0231.txt are distinct identifiers, and nested footnote markup can blur where James ends and Sully/Abbott begin, producing citation drift in later literature. The lineage thus oscillates between physiological nativism (accommodation, nerve-specific energy) and intellectualist empiricism (memory, association), with James locating “Experience” as author of approximately one-half of distance-percepts [PAGE 1] while providing no clock, no break-down regime, and no identifiability proof for that allocation.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: under cue conflict—accommodation signaling near while stereopsis or motion parallax signals far—the observer tends to regress to the historically dominant cue weight rather than recompute an optimal percept. In the Jamesian vocabulary, this is a fallback to the dominant component of the composite model (either s_0 or \mathcal{E}) when the joint likelihood is ambiguous. Counter-trap: the experimenter may interpret such regression as failure or absence of the primitive signal, when it is actually a strategic default under uncertainty. Clock: recalibration is not instantaneous; it follows an adaptation time course \tau_{\mathrm{adapt}} spanning trials to sessions. Comparing within-session judgments to multi-week “experience” without modeling h(t) = h_0 \exp(-t/\tau_{\mathrm{adapt}}) conflates transient regression with permanent learning.

Recursive theory-of-mind layer: the participant forms a second-order model of the experimenter’s theoretical commitment (nativist versus empiricist) and may shape introspective reports of “Experience” to match that demand. Thus verbal reports that distance is “constituted by Experience” [PAGE 1] are contaminated by the participant’s model of what the theorist wants to hear, not pure phenomenological evidence. Formal hazard of cue reweighting under conflict:
P(\mathrm{reweight}\mid t) = \mathrm{logistic}\!\left(\beta_0 + \beta_1 C_{\mathrm{conflict}} + \beta_2 D_{\mathrm{demand}} - \frac{t}{\tau_{\mathrm{adapt}}}\right),
where C_{\mathrm{conflict}} is cue disagreement [deg or D], D_{\mathrm{demand}} is perceived experimenter pressure [dimensionless], and t is time [s]. Breakdown: when \tau_{\mathrm{adapt}} exceeds the experimental window, observed stability is indistinguishable from absence of learning; when D_{\mathrm{demand}} is high, verbal “Experience” reports measure theory-of-mind compliance rather than perceptual architecture.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Veto layer 1 — physical optics. Stumpf’s antecedent requires intact accommodation: A = 1/z_F depends on lens/ciliary mechanics. Cycloplegia or presbyopia imposes A \approx \text{const}, breaking the z_F \mapsto A map. Veto outcome: if cyclopleged observers still register absolute fixation distance above chance, the organic-alteration account is falsified as a necessary channel, though not as a sufficient one.

Veto layer 2 — biomechanical. Convergence \theta requires intact extraocular muscles. Strabismus or paralysis severs \theta\!-\!z_F coupling; under such conditions any theory deriving distance primitively from convergence must predict chance-level depth ordering, which is testable with stereoscopically isolated targets.

Veto layer 3 — atmospheric/external (not on [PAGE 1] but a necessary cross-layer check). Scattering, fog, or low contrast attenuate texture gradients and occlusion reliability. Even when the Lipps surface predicate \mathrm{Vis}(x,y) holds—both termini are visible side by side—the depth interval may be under-specified by the optics of the medium. If depth judgments collapse under low visibility while surface visibility remains intact, the metric layer is vetoed independently of the surface layer.

Adjudication rule: a theory of space perception passes only if it predicts the joint pattern across all three layers. James’s empirical veto—“The feeling of depth … does actually exist as a fact of our visual sensibility” [PAGE 1]—overrides Lipps’s logical veto for existence, but not for metric structure; the page does not specify whether the surviving depth signal is monocular, binocular, accommodative, or conceptual.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 — Cycloplegic accommodation veto. Intervention: topical cycloplegia clamps A at approximately 0\,\mathrm{D} while z_F \in \{0.5,1,2,4\}\,\mathrm{m} is varied. Prediction under Hering/Stumpf: absolute fixation-distance judgments should lose metric information, with correlation r(z_F,\hat{z}_F) < 0.3 and variance \sigma^2_{z_F} at least four times baseline. Verifiable via adjustable-comparison depth matching in meters. Counter-prediction falsifying the nativent channel: if r > 0.7 and bias remains constant across z_F, accommodation is not the primitive antecedent, and s_0 cannot carry absolute distance.

Scenario 2 — Lipps occlusion veto. Intervention: a near disk fully occludes a far disk (no visible interval; “the near one hides the far one” [PAGE 1]), while the observer translates laterally by \Delta x = 5\,\mathrm{cm}, generating motion parallax. Prediction under strong Lipps: depth ordering is at chance, p = 0.5, because no surface interval is visible. Verifiable binomial test with n = 60 trials at \alpha = 0.05. Counter-prediction falsifying Lipps’s necessary condition: ordering accuracy > 0.75 demonstrates relational depth specified without a seen interval, vetoing the claim that the third dimension is purely conceptual.

Scenario 3 — Experience-null rapid-recalibration veto. Intervention: impose a novel prismatic depth mapping shifting perceived depth by \Delta z = +0.5\,\mathrm{m} with zero prior associative experience; measure the point of subjective equality over N = 30 trials within one session. Prediction under a purely historical “Experience” model: no significant shift in the first block; change only after multiple sessions/weeks. Counter-prediction falsifying that model: a shift \ge 0.2\,\mathrm{m} within the first 10 trials and a post-removal aftereffect \ge 0.1\,\mathrm{m} indicate an online recalibration operator, not merely long-term memory. Verifiable via psychometric thresholds in meters with confidence intervals on the point of subjective equality.

A physical veto V_{\mathrm{phys}}=0 is triggered if the observed pair violates the fundamental matrix constraint or if the projected orthogonality \theta would require a non-Euclidean deformation of the object. Units: angular error in radians; baseline distance in meters.

Biomechanical layer (oculomotor/head): Stable foveation of the sign requires a gaze shift \Delta\phi within the oculomotor range \phi_{\max}\approx 30^\circ (0.52 rad) and an accommodation demand within the dioptric range of the lens. A biomechanical veto V_{\mathrm{bio}}=0 is triggered if |\Delta\phi|>\phi_{\max} or if the required vergence exceeds the fusion limit, because the sign cannot be stably acquired and the likelihood P(s\mid r) becomes unreliable. Units: radians, diopters, seconds of fixation latency.

Weather/atmospheric layer: Scattering reduces contrast according to C_{\mathrm{obs}}(d)=C_{\mathrm{obj}}e^{-\beta d}, where \beta is the extinction coefficient (m^{-1}) and d is object distance (m). When C_{\mathrm{obs}} falls below the contrast threshold C_{\mathrm{thr}}, the mutual information I(R;S) drops below the entropy of the prior H(R), and a weather veto V_{\mathrm{weather}}=0 is triggered: the system must report “indeterminate” rather than impose a rectangular or slant-legged reality. Units: dimensionless contrast, m^{-1}, meters, bits.

Adjudication rule: The final percept is emitted only if V=V_{\mathrm{phys}}\land V_{\mathrm{bio}}\land V_{\mathrm{weather}}=1; otherwise the output is \varnothing (no forced constancy judgment). This veto stack prevents the economy principle [PAGE 1] from converting an unsupported optical sign into an overconfident perceptual reality. It implements a breakdown regime absent from James’s text: explicit detection of when the sign is too degraded, too geometrically inconsistent, or too biomechanically inaccessible to serve as a reliable cue to the “thing itself one and fixed” [PAGE 1].

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 — Prism adaptation counterfactual. An observer wears 20° right-shift prisms continuously for 3 weeks, then the prisms are removed. Veto: measure reach endpoint error to a remembered rectangular cross target under full-cue binocular viewing. Prediction: if V_{\mathrm{bio}} and V_{\mathrm{phys}} remain valid but the internal decoder has been recalibrated, the observer exhibits a persistent leftward reach bias exceeding 2 SD of baseline for at least 48 h, decaying with time constant \tau\approx48 h as the prior P(r) re-equilibrates to the unshifted optics. Verifiable by endpoint error (cm) vs. time (h) after removal.

Scenario 2 — Ambiguous projection counterfactual. Present the slant-legged optical derivative of a rectangular cross under dichoptic viewing such that each eye receives a mutually incompatible projection (one eye: rectangular fronto-parallel; other eye: slant-legged oblique). Veto: because no single r can generate both s_{1} and s_{2} under a common rigid geometry, V_{\mathrm{phys}}=0. Prediction: perceptual rivalry with no stable dominance of “rectangularity”; mean dominance duration \approx2–3 s; the subject never converges to the Jamesian “reality” [PAGE 1] but alternates between the two signs. Verifiable by perceptual report button-presses and dominance-duration histograms (s).

Scenario 3 — Atmospheric degradation counterfactual. Vary visibility from clear air (\beta\approx0.01\,m^{-1}) to dense fog (\beta\approx0.5\,m^{-1}) at fixed d=20 m while the subject judges whether a cross is rectangular or slant-legged. Veto: when C_{\mathrm{obs}}<C_{\mathrm{thr}}, I(R;S)<H(R)/2 and V_{\mathrm{weather}}=0. Prediction: constancy accuracy collapses to chance level (50%), response latency increases by a factor >2, and confidence ratings drop below the mid-point of the scale. Verifiable by proportion correct (dimensionless), latency (ms), and confidence (Likert units) as functions of \beta.

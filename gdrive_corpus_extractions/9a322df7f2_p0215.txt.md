Leather absorbs water, changing its quasi-static crush stress σ_crush(T, moisture). Veto condition: if operating envelope T ∈ [T_min, T_max] °C with precipitation present drives P(T) below design pressure or σ_crush below required absorption, the equipment selection is vetoed.

Adjudication rule. An equipment configuration x is admissible only if it passes all three layers simultaneously: physical integrity (no wire fatigue, σ < σ_y), biomechanical tolerance (τ_ankle, Δt_rise within limits), and weather robustness (P(T), σ_crush(T, moisture) above thresholds). If any layer fails, the entire configuration is vetoed regardless of cost savings. This is a hard logical AND, not a compensatory trade-off.

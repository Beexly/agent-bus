<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
All equations are the paper's unless flagged as standard corrections.

CWT (forward), [PAGE 9], Eq (13):
$$W_s(a,b)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} S(t)\,\Psi\!\left(\frac{t-b}{a}\right)dt,\quad a\in\mathbb{R}^{+},\ b\in\mathbb{R}.$$
Units: if S is acceleration [m·s⁻²] and t is time [s], then a,b have units [s], and W_s has units [m·s⁻³ᐟ²]. The 1/√a factor gives L² (energy) normalization: with u=(t−b)/a, ‖Ψ_{a,b}‖₂=‖Ψ‖₂. Breakdown: a→0 ⇒ wavelet compresses in time, 1/√a→∞, numerical undersampling/aliasing; a→∞ ⇒ wavelet delocalized in time.

Daughter wavelet, [PAGE 9], Eq (15): $\Psi_{a,b}(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}\Psi\!\left(\frac{t-b}{a}\right)$; inner-product form Eq (16): $W_s(a,b)=(S,\Psi_{a,b})$.

Mother-wavelet constraints as stated, [PAGE 10]: finite energy Eq (17) $\|\Psi\|_2^2=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\Psi(t)|^2dt<\infty$; zero-mean Eq (18) $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Psi(t)\,dt=0$; vanishing moments Eq (19) $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} t^{k}\Psi(t)\,dt=0,\ k\in[0,n]$.
Standard correction (not in paper): true admissibility is
$$C_\Psi=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{|\hat\Psi(\omega)|^{2}}{\omega}\,d\omega<\infty,$$
which forces $\hat\Psi(0)=0$ (hence Eq 18). Because the pipeline renders |W_s|² only and never inverts, admissibility is unnecessary here; Eq (18) suffices for feature extraction. Vanishing-moment equivalence: p moments ⇔ $\hat\Psi(\omega)=O(\omega^{p})$ as ω→0; DOG order n ⇒ n vanishing moments; Mexican Hat = DOG-2.

Uncertainty principle, [PAGE 9], Eq (12): paper writes $\Delta\tau\cdot\Delta\omega\sim\frac{1}{4\pi}$.
Standard correction (unit-consistent): $\sigma_t\,\sigma_\omega\ge\frac{1}{2}$ (angular) or equivalently $\sigma_t\,\sigma_f\ge\frac{1}{4\pi}$ (Hz). The paper mismatches ω with the 1/4π constant.

Fourier admissibility of the domain, [PAGE 7], Eq (3): $\int_{t_1}^{t_2}[S(t)]^2dt<\infty$ defines $S\in L^2$. Generalized Fourier series Eqs (4)–(6) require a complete orthogonal basis $\{\varphi_n\}$ with $\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\varphi_k\varphi_n\,dt=0$; convergence is in L² (mean-square), not pointwise. STFT, [PAGE 9], Eq (11): $\mathrm{STFT}(t,\omega)=\int S(\tau)W(\tau-t)e^{-i\omega\tau}d\tau$ — fixed window ⇒ fixed Δt·Δf tile, which CWT replaces with scale-adaptive tiling.

Residual block, [PAGE 13], Eq (20): $y=F(x,W_i)+x$, so $\partial y/\partial x=\partial F/\partial x+I$ (identity gradient path; vanishing-gradient mitigation). Omission: for channel/space mismatch the shortcut must be a projection $y=F(x,W_i)+W_s x$.

This is crisis habit regression: the will no longer navigates by the precise sensory image of "Peter" versus "Paul" [PAGE 510] but launches into default muscle synergies and intensities because the afferent guide to "how strong" is the only remaining low-variance signal.

The counter-trap is a meta-control intervention: before crisis, the agent caches an alternative sensory image and an if–then motor schema (implementation intention). If crisis cue C is detected, execute M_alt with its pre-cached afferent template. This bypasses the vacuum by substituting a synthetic prediction for the degraded online image. Formally, the counter-trap replaces p(s | M_crisis) with p(s | M_alt), where the latter has lower entropy because it was consolidated during non-crisis training.

The clock supplies the time constants that bound both processes. Efferent activation τ_a is tens of milliseconds; movement execution T_move is tenths of seconds; working-memory sensory imagery persists seconds; motor consolidation spans hours to weeks; circadian and thermal cycles modulate proprioceptive gain on a 24-hour scale. Recursive theory of mind arises because the agent must model this nesting: "I know that under crisis I will regress to habit; therefore I install a counter-trap; but the counter-trap itself decays on the consolidation clock, so I must rehearse it before the crisis window." The fixed point is stable only if rehearsal frequency exceeds the decay rate of p(s | M_alt); otherwise the counter-trap becomes another item in the vacuum.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Three layers can veto the efferent command before it becomes movement. Each veto is a Boolean predicate with a measurable threshold and SI units; the composite veto is V = V_phys ∨ V_bio ∨ V_env, and if V = 1 the drive u is gated to a safe default or zero.

Physical layer: V_phys = 1 iff afferent signal norm ||A||₂ < ε_A or muscle temperature T_muscle < T_crit. ε_A is the minimum proprioceptive spike rate required to close the forward model (Hz); T_crit ≈ 15 °C, below which spindle sensitivity and conduction velocity fall off steeply (Q₁₀ ≈ 2–3). Assumption: without adequate afferent gain, the force imagery that James calls "absolutely precise" [PAGE 510] cannot be formed. Breakdown: in deafferentation ||A||₂ → 0, so V_phys = 1 and the system defaults to open-loop visual control.

Biomechanical layer: V_bio = 1 iff predicted force F_pred > F_tendon_yield or predicted joint angle q_pred ∉ [q_min, q_max]. F_pred comes from the Hill-type activation model F_i = a_i F_max f_l f_v (N); F_tendon_yield is tissue-specific (N); q limits are anatomical (rad). Assumption: the nervous system vetoes commands that would exceed structural safety margins. Breakdown: if the subject is instructed to exceed maximal voluntary contraction, V_bio = 1 and EMG truncates before the commanded force is reached.

Environmental/weather layer: V_env = 1 iff |T_air − T_opt| > ΔT_max or friction coefficient μ < μ_min. T_air in °C; T_opt ≈ 20–25 °C for fine digital control (writing "Peter" versus "Paul" [PAGE 510]); ΔT_max ≈ 10 °C; μ is dimensionless. Cold air lowers T_muscle via convection; low μ (ice, rain on paper surface) makes the predicted appearance-on-paper unreliable, degrading the sensory image. Breakdown: if μ → 0, the pen slips and the forward model's predicted s no longer matches the motor command, triggering abort.

Adjudication proof: because each layer's threshold is expressed in physical units (N, m, °C, Hz, s) and the veto is a disjunction, any single layer failure is sufficient to suppress u. The system cannot bypass a veto by increasing effort imagery, because James's afferent guide to intensity is precisely what is vetoed at the physical layer [PAGE 510].
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Verifiable with keystroke dynamics and trial-by-trial error logs.

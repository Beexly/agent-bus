*Strand A — Cellular automata.* von Neumann's self-reproduction programme (1940s–60s) → Wolfram's elementary rule space → Wolfram (1986), *Cryptography with cellular automata* — the real ancestor of "CA for security." The paper cites **none** of this. "Programmable CA" in the hardware-security sense denotes a CA with a loadable rule register; no rule register, address space, or program word is defined here [PAGE 3]. "Unity Attractors" has **no** traceable antecedent in dynamical-systems literature as used here; either it denotes basin unification in an attractor network (undefined in-source) or it is an ornamental coinage.

*Strand B — Verifiable computation.* The genuine ancestor is Gennaro–Gentry–Parno (2010), which introduced exactly the two-message, garbled-circuit delegation the paper describes, followed by Pinocchio/SNARG lines and by verifiable-FHE constructions (Fiore–Gennaro–Pastro). The claim *"the method of UAIPCA involves less number of interactions between the challenger and the prover by using only two messages"* [PAGE 3] is a restatement of a 2010 theorem, presented as a property of a novel CA. Retrocausally, the 2022 artifact is downstream of 2010 cryptography but attributes its own result to a 1960s abstraction.

*Strand C — Barnacles Mating Optimizer.* Sulaiman et al., SNPD 2018 ([3]) and *Eng. Appl. Artif. Intell.* 87:103330, 2020 ([4]) — correctly cited. Hardy–Weinberg (1908) allele-frequency inheritance is explicitly the reproduction analogue: *"BMO stresses the allele frequency of adults to create the offspring relying on the Hardy-Weinberg rule"* [PAGE 4]. The No-Free-Lunch anchor is Wolpert & Macready (1997) ([5]), quoted as *"the No Free Lunch (NFL) Hypothesis demonstrates mathematically that there isn't a method for every optimal control problem"* [PAGE 2].

*Strand D — SVM/metaheuristic hybrid literature* (PSO-SVM, GOA-SVM, SSA-SVM, HHO-SVM, HG-GA-SVM, M-SVM), reviewed at length [PAGES 2–3] and then abandoned — none of it informs the proposed method.

**Multi-week traps for a downstream analyst.**

1. **Citation contamination.** Reference [7] is *"Architecture Framework of High Throughput for the Soft Decision Decoding"* (Acta Energetica) and [13] is *"A Low Voltage Novel High-Performance Hybrid Full Adder for VLSI Circuit"* — neither has any relation to the sentence that cites it. This is the signature of template-based reference stuffing, not of scholarly descent. Any genealogy built from this bibliography inherits two spurious ancestors.
2. **Index drift.** Text: *"In [9], Huang et al. integrated discontinuous and continual PSO"* [PAGE 2]; reference [9] is Wan et al. (2017) on fuzzy discriminant projections. Huang & Dun is [10]. Every bracketed number after [8] is off by one relative to the list.

The "first time" claim is narrower than it reads and is never operationalized: no mutation operator appears in Eqs. (1)–(7).
4. **Conclusion reversion.** The paper's terminal paragraph discusses BMO generically, not UAIPCA-BSO, and forecasts "a multimodal and multiobjective variant of BMO" [PAGE 6] — i.e. the authors' own forward model does not include the security half of their title.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Physical-layer veto.** The paper's title claims *energy efficient* [PAGE 1] but no energy functional is stated (Operator 8, absent). Prose concedes a physical failure mode without modelling it: *"The low voltage backups and maybe some potential drifts may cause the nodes to fail when they begin transmitting data for an extended period"* [PAGE 5]. That is battery-depletion and reference-drift, i.e. a hardware-degradation term $\dot{E}(t)=-\sum_{k}P_k(t)$ with $P_k$ the per-radio-state power in W. Absent that integral, the "network lifetime" curve in Figure 4 [PAGE 5] cannot be dimensionally reconciled with any node count. **Veto: the energy claim is unfalsifiable as printed.**

**Biomechanical veto.** The BSOA metaphor's controlling parameter is anatomical: *"It is vital to emphasise that the exploration and exploration processes are significantly influenced by the height of their genitalia (pl)"* [PAGE 4]. In the biological referent, $pl$ is a physical reach in metres constraining which neighbours can cross-fertilize; barnacle cement adhesion and intertidal wave loading are the actual biomechanics. In the algorithmic referent, $pl$ is a *graph-hop radius* over the WSN topology — a dimensionless integer. The mapping is metaphor, not mechanics: there is no antenna reach, no transmission range $r_{tx}$, no node form factor in the paper. Any claim that $pl$ is "physically grounded" is a false cross-layer transfer. **Veto: no biomechanical quantity survives the mapping.**

**Weather veto.** WSN field deployments are vetoed by environmental terms absent here: rain and foliage attenuation on the link budget, snow/soil accumulation on solar harvesters, and Arrhenius-type capacity derating of Li-SOCl₂ cells at low temperature. The reported detection times (axis 0–350 s, Figure 5 [PAGE 6]) are simulation-clock seconds under an unspecified channel. Transferring those numbers to a fielded network is a weather-layer overreach. **Veto: no environmental variable is reported, so no out-of-simulation claim is licensed.**

**Cross-layer adjudication verdict.** Two of the three claimed contributions (energy, detection latency) are physically underdetermined; the third (security) is cryptographically real but attributed to the wrong abstraction (CA rather than delegation protocol). The document passes as a *conference-grade heuristic description* and fails as a *reproducible engineering result*.

**Veto 1 — Literal-exponent implementation.**
*Scenario:* a reference implementation transcribes Eq. (6) and Eq. (7) exactly as printed, with $N_C$ as exponent.
*Prediction:* for any run with $N_C\ge 1024$ and $|BCS|>1$, entries evaluate to `Inf` within one generation; IEEE-754 overflow flag raises on the first offspring evaluation. For $|BCS|<1$ the population collapses to $0$ in $\lesssim 10$ generations, at which point all candidate nodes carry zero fitness and selection becomes a tie-break — accuracy degenerates to the majority-class base rate.
*Verification:* single script, two runs, check `numpy.isfinite` mask and generation-of-collapse. **Falsifies** any claim that the printed equations are the implemented equations.

**Veto 2 — Two-message round-complexity check.**
*Scenario:* the UAIPCA protocol is claimed to use *"only two messages"* [PAGE 3] for a delegated function $f_n:\{0,1\}\to(0,1)$.
*Prediction:* if the message **count** is 2 and the message **volume** is also $O(1)$ independent of input length $n$, the verifier never receives the input and soundness must fail; therefore volume must grow as $\Omega(n)$ while count stays at 2. Measure bytes-per-message across $n\in\{2^8,2^{12},2^{16}\}$. Prediction: count constant at 2, volume linear or near-linear in $n$. If volume is also constant, the protocol is vacuous.
*Verification:* instrumented harness logging `(count, bytes)` pairs. **Falsifies** the implicit reading that "fewer interactions" implies cheaper communication.

**Veto 3 — Detection-latency scaling law.**
*Scenario:* Figure 5 [PAGE 6] reports detection time rising over a sweep described in prose as *"ten nodes … until there are 100 nodes"* [PAGE 6] while the axis is labelled "Number of rounds" 1–10.
*Prediction:* if the mechanism really is counter-plus-gossip — *"When a node is identified as malicious, it communicates the news to every other node within its coverage area"* [PAGE 5] — then per-detection message complexity is $\Theta(n\cdot\bar{d})$ and detection epoch latency should scale superlinearly in $n$ (roughly $n\log n$ to $n^{2}$ depending on flooding). A straight line fit to latency-vs-$n$ should be **rejected** by BIC against a power law with exponent $b>1$. Additionally, the axis labelling must be resolved: if the abscissa is rounds (1–10), the node sweep is unplotted and the claim is unsupported; if the abscissa is nodes (10–100), the tick labels are wrong.
*Verification:* digitize Figure 5, fit $\log t = a + b\log n$, report $b$ with CI and BIC. **Falsifies** either the quadratic-gossip mechanism or the reported axis semantics — one of the two must yield.

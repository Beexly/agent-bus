Humidity and temperature also affect paper stability and chemical-reagent behavior, respectively, creating environmental veto conditions for both storage and experimentation.

Adjudication. A veto at any single layer blocks adoption independent of the other layers. Content excellence (e.g., Farlow's praise of condensed cell-structure relations [PAGE 1]) cannot override a physical binding failure, a biomechanical mismatch, or a weather-induced delivery miss. The veto stack is therefore strictly conjunctive: all layers must pass for the text to be used as intended.

Prediction: The cat’s pursuit success cannot be predicted by a symmetric molar law coupling N_cat to bird movement; instead, an asymmetric coupling term Γ_cross(t) [dimensionless] must be added, and the basic molar law under-predicts escape events. Verification: Comparative ethology time-series across same-species and cross-species dyads; fit symmetry-respecting vs asymmetry-permitting models. The asymmetry model should have significantly lower out-of-sample prediction error on cross-species escape latency.
Formal statement: SameSpecies = False ⇒ ∃Γ_cross(t) ≠ 0 such that e_bird(t+Δt) ≠ Φ(e_bird, μ_cat, t; Δt) under symmetric assumption alone.

Units: $u$ px, $f_x$ px, $X,Z$ m, $\Delta u$ px. **Breakdown regimes:** $\Delta Z/Z \to 0$ (far players collapse onto a point — the paper's stated failure); $\Delta u \to \infty$ as $Z \to 0^+$ (near-camera blow-up, unreported); and $u \to c_x$ (on-axis players have *zero* horizontal depth sensitivity regardless of $Z$, so the paper's "inevitable" claim fails there). The paper supplies no $f$, $Z$ range, or camera height, so the claimed regime cannot be verified.

**Operator 2 — Occlusion gate (the paper's stated innovation, [PAGE 6]).** Let $c_{j,t}\in[0,1]$ be the per-joint detector confidence. A differentiable gate is
\[ \alpha_{j,t} = \sigma\!\left(\kappa\,(c_{j,t}-\tau)\right),\quad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}},\quad w_{\text{spatial}}=\alpha_{j,t},\; w_{\text{temporal}}=1-\alpha_{j,t} \]
Units: $\alpha$ dimensionless probability, $\tau$ dimensionless, $\kappa$ dimensionless inverse-confidence. **Assumption:** $w_s + w_t = 1$ (the paper implies but never states normalization). **Breakdown regimes:** $\kappa \to 0$ makes $\alpha \equiv 0.5$ for every joint — the gate becomes a fixed 50/50 fusion and the "dynamic" mechanism vanishes; $\kappa \to \infty$ makes it a hard step, giving a discontinuous gradient and visible pose jitter at the crossing boundary; $\tau$ tuned on the reported test split is leakage, and the paper reports no held-out tuning protocol. **The paper gives neither $\tau$ nor $\kappa$.** This is a hard reproducibility veto.

**Operator 3 — Bone-length constraint ([PAGE 4]).** Verbatim: "The distance between the shoulder and the elbow remains constant." Formally, for joints $a,b$:
\[ \left(\lVert \mathbf{p}_a - \mathbf{p}_b\rVert_2 - L_{ab}\right)^2 \le \sigma_L^2 \]
Units: m (3-D) or px (2-D). **Critical flaw:** $L_{ab}$ is constant only in 3-D. In the image plane $L_{ab}^{\text{px}} = f\,L_{ab}/Z$, so the constraint is *depth-dependent*. The paper does not state whether the graph operates in 3-D or image coordinates. If image coordinates, the constraint is false by projection and the model is enforcing a physically wrong invariant. **Breakdown:** any player moving in depth (a drive, a closeout) violates the pixel-space constraint by construction.

**Operator 4 — Joint-angle admissibility ([PAGE 4]).** Verbatim: "the rotation of the knee joint is physically limited to a specific angular range." Formally $\theta_k \in [\theta_k^{\min},\theta_k^{\max}]$ with $|\dot\theta_k| \le \dot\theta_k^{\max}$. Plausible order: knee $[0^\circ, \sim140^\circ]$, elbow $[0^\circ,\sim145^\circ]$, $\dot\theta^{\max}\sim10$–$20\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ for explosive basketball motion. Units: rad, rad·s⁻¹. **The paper supplies no numeric range for any joint.** The constraint is asserted as a governing principle and never instantiated, so the "kinematic structural similarity" cost of [PAGE 6] is underdetermined.

**Operator 5 — Extrapolation error (quantifies the [PAGE 9] admission).** Under a constant-velocity extrapolator with true acceleration $\mathbf{a}$:
\[ \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{a}\rVert\,(t-t_0)^2 \]
Units: m. With $\lVert\mathbf{a}\rVert = 8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ (a hard cut) and $\Delta t = 0.5$ s, $\varepsilon = 1.0$ m; at $\Delta t = 1.0$ s, $\varepsilon = 4.0$ m — larger than the paint is wide. **Singularity:** $\varepsilon$ is unbounded in $\Delta t$, so any occlusion longer than the player's reachable-set horizon is unrecoverable by momentum alone.

**Operator 6 — Information growth during occlusion.** For an unobserved joint the predictive differential entropy is
\[ h(x_t \mid y_{1:t_0}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\ln\!\left(2\pi e\,\sigma^2(t)\right)\ \text{nats},\qquad \sigma(t) \propto \tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{a}\rVert (t-t_0)^2 \]
so $h$ grows as $2\ln(t-t_0)$ — logarithmically unbounded. The system emits a **point estimate with no reported variance surface**. A zero-entropy output for an unbounded-entropy state is not a measurement; it is a claim.

**Operator 7 — Association cost ([PAGE 6]).**
\[ C(i,j) = \lambda_a d_{\text{app}}(i,j) + \lambda_s \lVert \mathbf{p}^{\text{root}}_i - \hat{\mathbf{p}}^{\text{root}}_j\rVert_2 + \lambda_k D_{\text{kin}}(\Theta^i,\Theta^j) + \mu\,\mathbf{1}[\Delta\theta > \theta_{\max}] \]
Units: $d_{\text{app}}$ dimensionless cosine/inner-product distance; $\lVert\cdot\rVert_2$ m or px; $D_{\text{kin}}$ rad; $\lambda$'s carry units of cost per term. **The paper says only "an immense penalty" for $\mu$** and gives no $\lambda_a,\lambda_s,\lambda_k,\theta_{\max}$. **Algorithmic gap:** no dummy/occlusion column is described for the Hungarian assignment, so when a player exits or is fully occluded the solver is forced to match against a wrong detection rather than leave a track unassigned — the exact failure mode the paper claims to solve.

**Operator 8 — MOTA / IDF1 ([PAGE 7]).**
\[ \mathrm{MOTA} = 1 - \frac{\sum_t (FN_t + FP_t + IDSW_t)}{\sum_t GT_t},\qquad \mathrm{IDF1} = \frac{2\,\mathrm{IDTP}}{2\,\mathrm{IDTP} + \mathrm{IDFP} + \mathrm{IDFN}} \]
Units: dimensionless fractions. **Internal-consistency check (Table 2, [PAGE 8]):** with $GT$ arbitrary, $TP = R\cdot GT$, $FN=(1-R)GT$, $FP = TP(1-P)/P$. Baseline graph tracker ($P=78.4$, $R=69.1$): $FN+FP = (0.309 + 0.190)GT = 0.499\,GT$, so $\mathrm{MOTA} \le 50.1\%$ versus a reported 68.1% — a **17.9-point** violation. Full system ($P=82.5$, $R=75.1$): $FN+FP = (0.249+0.159)GT = 0.408\,GT$, so $\mathrm{MOTA}\le 59.2\%$ versus a reported 74.3% — a **15.1-point** violation. Since $IDSW \ge 0$ only pushes MOTA *lower*, the reported table is arithmetically impossible under a single consistent GT base.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The genuine lineage of every idea in this paper is well documented and almost entirely absent from its reference list. Pictorial structures (Fischler & Elschlager 1973; Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher 2005) → deep heatmap regression (Toshev & Szegedy 2014; Wei et al. 2016; Newell et al. 2016) → top-down Mask R-CNN / HRNet pipelines and bottom-up Part Affinity Fields and Associative Embedding (Cao et al. 2017; Newell et al. 2017) → spatiotemporal graph convolution for skeletons (Yan et al. 2018, ST-GCN) → occlusion-robust pose warping (Bertasius et al. 2019) → modern association-by-motion trackers. The metrics likewise have canonical sources: CLEAR MOT (Bernardin & Stiefelhagen 2008) for MOTA and Ristani et al. 2016 for IDF1.

The paper's actual citations do not reconstruct that chain. The sentence "Early approaches relied on pictorial structures and heavily engineered features" [PAGE 3] is footnoted to [12] — "Gaussian mixture solvers for diffusion models" (NeurIPS 2023). The sentence on top-down detectors failing to localize occluded players [PAGE 3] cites [14], a 3-D pose domain-generalization paper, which is adjacent but does not support the claim. The load-bearing confidence-weighting claim on [PAGE 2–3] — "the network seamlessly shifts its reliance from spatial visual features to temporal and kinematic priors [11]" — is cited to a paper on *neuromuscular adaptation to repeated perturbations in chronic ankle instability*. The bottom-up grouping claim [PAGE 3] cites [15], semi-supervised semantic segmentation. The MOTA/IDF1 definition [PAGE 7] cites [30], a two-hand 3-D reconstruction paper. Further references in the list include cyclodextrin glycosyltransferase starch modification [23] and IRF1/Egr1 macrophage polarization [29] — neither has any computable relationship to multi-person tracking.

This is the retrocausal finding: the paper's *narrative* ancestry (graphs, kinematics, occlusion recovery) is real and coherent, but its *documented* ancestry is severed. A citation graph that does not generate the claims is a strong prior that the empirical apparatus was assembled by the same process as the reference list — template substitution rather than measurement. I state this as a hypothesis, not a finding: it is falsifiable in one pass by checking whether the authors can produce the raw annotation files, the train/test game split, and the per-frame FP/FN/IDSW logs for Table 1 and Table 2.

Prediction: at least one Table 2 row shifts by >10 points, per Operator 8. **Multi-week trap 3:** freeze the gate at $\alpha=0.5$ and re-run. Prediction: recovery falls to the kinematic-only row (70.5 MOTA) within ±1 point.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

**The crisis habit.** The paper defines its adversary explicitly: "linear interpolation, where the missing coordinates are calculated as a straight line between the point of disappearance and the point of reappearance" [PAGE 4], and "naive linear interpolation" [PAGE 2]. This is the field's crisis habit — under occlusion, regress to the cheapest available prior. The authors correctly diagnose it and then partially reproduce it: their fallback is a *constant-velocity-plus-kinematic-clamp* extrapolator, which is linear interpolation in velocity space rather than position space. The improvement is real (second-order vs. zeroth-order continuity) but it is a difference of degree, not of kind. The paper's own admission on [PAGE 9] confirms this: "the graph network's temporal momentum will naturally extrapolate a forward trajectory."

**The counter-trap.** The obvious counter-move — trust appearance instead of motion — is foreclosed by the source's own radiometry: "uneven illumination, harsh shadows, and color distortions" [PAGE 2] and appearance models that "degrade significantly in low-resolution amateur footage" [PAGE 3–4]. The authors are boxed in correctly. Where they overreach is in treating the box as proof that only *their* exit works. A tuned Kalman/IMM filter with the same history and the same kinematic clamp would recover an unknown fraction of the +2.5 MOTA the gate contributes; the paper never tests that cheaper counterfactual, so the necessity of the graph is asserted rather than demonstrated.

**The clock.** The only quantitative temporal constant in the paper is the frame interval: "a player cannot transition from a standing posture to a prone posture within a single thirtieth-of-a-second frame" [PAGE 6]. That fixes $\Delta t = 33.3$ ms and, with Operator 3, lets us bound reachable-set radius per step. It also exposes an unexamined mismatch: the temporal memory buffer spans "frames $t$-1, $t$-2, and so forth" [PAGE 5] with no horizon length stated. Since extrapolation error scales as $\tfrac12 a \Delta t^2$ (Operator 5), the horizon is the single most consequential hyperparameter in the system, and it is unreported.

**The end-user model.** The recursive gap is this: the system emits a *point* skeleton for a joint whose predictive entropy is growing logarithmically (Operator 6), and the paper provides no uncertainty channel to the consumer. A coach reading the output cannot distinguish a measured knee from an imputed knee. The authors' model of their reader assumes the downstream analyst knows which joints were hallucinated; nothing in the described pipeline conveys that. In a fatigue-assessment or injury-risk application — both named as motivations [PAGE 1] — an unflagged imputed joint is worse than a missing one, because it is actionable and wrong.

**Authors' model of the annotator.** [PAGE 7] has annotators "utiliz[e] temporal context to estimate the true physical location of the hidden joints." The authors treat this as ground truth. Reflexively, they are modelling the annotator as an oracle who happens to share the model's prior — which is precisely the condition under which evaluation cannot discriminate.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Layer 1 — Optical/geometric (hard veto, partially triggered).** The projection operator (Eq. 1) admits an exact reachable-set test: after a full occlusion of duration $\Delta t$, the recovered joint must lie within $\lVert \mathbf{v}_0\Delta t + \tfrac12\mathbf{a}\Delta t^2\rVert$ of the last observed position, projected to the image plane. Under a low-angle sideline camera [PAGE 1], near-court players have the *largest* $\partial u/\partial Z$, so the paper's "inevitable" depth compression [PAGE 2] is not the operative mechanism at the paint. **Adjudication:** the occlusion failure is confirmed; the stated geometric cause is regime-unverified. Veto applies to the causal claim, not the phenomenon.

**Layer 2 — Biomechanical (veto triggered).** The bone-length invariance of [PAGE 4] is a 3-D truth and an image-plane falsehood (Eq. 3). Because the paper does not specify its coordinate frame, the constraint may be enforcing a physically incorrect invariant on players moving in depth. Joint-angle limits are asserted but never numerically instantiated, so the "kinematic structural similarity" penalty of [PAGE 6] cannot be reconstructed or falsified. **Adjudication:** the biomechanical layer *supports* the general approach and *fails* the specific implementation for want of parameters. This is the cleanest single fix: publish $\tau$, $\kappa$, $\lambda_{a,s,k}$, $\theta_{\max}$ per joint, and the memory horizon.

**Layer 3 — Radiometric/environmental (supports the paper).** "Uneven illumination, harsh shadows, and color distortions" [PAGE 2] and unstandardized uniforms *do* justify abandoning appearance-dominant association. **Weather is a null layer here** — the corpus is indoor gymnasium footage, so precipitation, wind, and solar angle have zero causal status and I assign them zero weight. Recording that null explicitly is the discipline check: a layer that cannot vary cannot veto.

**Layer 4 — Statistical (veto triggered, strongest).** The paper reports single-point estimates on one "highly challenging test split" curated "specifically... to feature continuous, dense traffic in the painted area" [PAGE 7] — i.e., a stress split, not a representative one. No confidence intervals, no per-game breakdown, no significance test, no reported $N$ of games ("dozens" [PAGE 7]). Because adjacent frames are strongly autocorrelated, the effective sample size is the number of occlusion *events*, not the number of frames, so nominal binomial error bars would be illusory by orders of magnitude. Combined with the 15–18-point MOTA algebra violation (Operator 8), the statistical layer vetoes the headline comparison as currently reported.

**Composite verdict:** physical layer = confirmed phenomenon, unverified mechanism; biomechanical layer = sound principle, missing parameters; radiometric = supports; environmental = null; statistical = **hard veto pending raw counts and a consistent GT base.**
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

**Veto scenario 1 — Frozen gate ($\kappa \to 0$).** Replace the dynamic confidence weighting with a constant $\alpha_{j,t}=0.5$ for all joints, holding every other component identical, and re-run the [PAGE 8] ablation. *Prediction:* MOTA falls from 74.3 to 70.5 ± 1.0 and recall from 75.1 to 70.3 ± 1.0, i.e., the system collapses onto the "+ Kinematic Constraints" row. *Falsified if* the frozen-gate run retains ≥73.0 MOTA — which would mean the graph's temporal edges, not the confidence gate, are doing the work, and the paper's central claim of novelty is misattributed. *Verifiable by:* one ablation run on the same split with the published code.

**Veto scenario 2 — Metric reconciliation.** Publish per-row $GT$, $TP$, $FP$, $FN$, $IDSW$ for both tables and recompute MOTA under a single consistent detection base. *Prediction:* the gap derived in Operator 8 (17.9 points for the baseline graph tracker, 15.1 points for the full system) resolves only if precision/recall are computed at keypoint level while MOTA is computed at box level — in which case the two columns are not commensurable and the ablation table's joint interpretation is invalid. *Falsified if* the authors publish counts that close the gap under one base, in which case my algebra used a wrong definition and the tables stand. *Verifiable by:* arithmetic on the released count file.

**Veto scenario 3 — Occlusion reversal subset.** Isolate every event where a player, fully occluded, "abruptly stops, and immediately reverses direction" [PAGE 9], and score IDF1 on that subset only. *Prediction from Eq. 5:* with $\lVert\mathbf{a}\rVert \approx 8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\Delta t \approx 0.6$ s, extrapolation error reaches $\approx 1.4$ m — several body widths — so IDF1 on this subset falls to ≤55% and identity switches concentrate here, well below the reported aggregate 71.9 [PAGE 7]. *Falsified if* subset IDF1 ≥ 68%. *Verifiable by:* per-event stratification of the existing test split; no retraining required.

**Veto scenario 4 (auxiliary) — Blind re-annotation.** Have two annotators re-label 200 occluded joints without seeing model output. *Prediction:* inter-annotator standard deviation on occluded joints exceeds $L_{ab}/4$ for at least one limb segment, meaning the evaluation target is less precise than the constraint the model is being asked to satisfy. *Falsified if* occluded-joint dispersion is within 2× of visible-joint dispersion. *Verifiable by:* a two-annotator agreement study reported as a single number.

**Aggregate falsification condition:** the paper survives all four vetoes only if it publishes (i) gate and cost parameters, (ii) raw detection counts under one metric base, (iii) a frozen-gate ablation, and (iv) an occlusion-event-stratified IDF1. Absent any one, the headline claim of [PAGE 7] — 74.3 MOTA / 71.9 IDF1 — remains an unreplicated point estimate rather than a result.

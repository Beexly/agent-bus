<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: When the iWAR paper reports that ridge beat a neural net on franchise-pair forecasts [PAGE 1], a practitioner in crisis mode may regress to ordinary least squares or ridge for *all* forecasting tasks, abandoning deep learning even in domains where data scale and temporal complexity justify it. Similarly, when the Grad-CAM audit shows central-blur accuracy at 43.7%, below the 50.9% random-blur null [PAGE 1], a team may regress to purely box-score features and discard video models entirely rather than debug the saliency architecture. This is habit regression: retreating to the last method that felt safe during a prior crisis, regardless of whether the current failure mode matches.

Counter-trap: The inverse error is over-generalizing from small-n successes. The zero-shot soccer event spotter reports 72.0% F1@5s on only 3 matches and 130 events [PAGE 1]; the counter-trap is treating this as production-ready for broadcast automation. The TTA paper’s MLP beats six physics baselines on PFF World Cup 2022 tracking with transfer tests on basketball and *one* NFL game [PAGE 1]; the counter-trap is assuming NFL deployment is validated after a single-game test. Both traps commit the same recursive error: mistaking a local minimum on a narrow validation set for a global solution.

Clock: The release calendar is an adversarial agent in this system. nflfastR 6.0.0’s CRAN manual is dated 2026-09-18, and older versions "cannot download 2026 seasons" [PAGE 1]. sportsdataverse-py v0.2.0b1 was tagged 2026-10-26 with a breaking default-return change [PAGE 1]. Polars 2.0 shipped 2026-10-06, outside the seven-day window but the major item of the month [PAGE 2]. A pipeline built in the first week of November 2026 on nflfastR 5.x and Polars 1.x is already obsolete: it cannot ingest 2026 NFL data, and its joins may return rows in non-deterministic order [PAGE 1][PAGE 2]. The clock does not forgive stale pins; it forces mandatory refactors or data-loss events.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer veto (kinematic feasibility): Time-to-arrival as lower-quantile regression is bounded by $t \ge d/v_{\max}$, where $d$ is distance and $v_{\max}$ is species- and sport-specific maximum velocity [PAGE 1]. Extrapolating from PFF World Cup 2022 soccer tracking to basketball and a single NFL game [PAGE 1] changes court/field dimensions, acceleration profiles, and contact dynamics. The physical veto is absolute: no statistical model can schedule an arrival that violates human kinematic limits. Source-domain pass; transfer-domain fail.

Biomechanical layer veto (saliency validity): Grad-CAM central-blur accuracy of 43.7% falls below the random-blur spatial null of 50.9% [PAGE 1]. If the model’s attention collapses under central blur, it is encoding court position, not release mechanics (angle, velocity, follow-through). The biomechanical veto is absolute for shot-evaluation or coaching use cases: explanations must align with observable kinematics, or the model is a location prior masquerading as expertise. Fail.

Operational layer veto (weather/broadcast conditions): The soccer event-spotting evaluation comprises only 3 matches and 130 events with no weather stratification [PAGE 1]. Outdoor broadcasts face rain, snow, fog, and stadium-lighting variation that alter ball visibility, player contrast, and camera flare. A zero-shot multimodal model trained/validated on clear-weather clips has unknown failure modes under adverse weather. The operational veto is conditional: deployment is prohibited until weather-robustness ablation is performed. Fail pending evidence.

Adjudication hierarchy: Physical feasibility overrides all other layers; if $t < d/v_{\max}$, the prediction is physically impossible regardless of statistical fit. Biomechanical validity overrides operational generality; an explainable but weather-brittle model is preferable to an unexplainable one that happens to work in one stadium. Final order: Physical > Biomechanical > Operational. Under this adjudication, the TTA paper survives only within its source distribution; the Grad-CAM paper is invalidated for mechanistic use; the zero-shot soccer paper is not deployable in variable outdoor conditions.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Formal setup. Let the label be \(y \in \{0,1\}\), with \(y=1\) for male-source records and \(y=0\) for female-source records. Counts have units of actions per game section; probabilities and AUC are dimensionless; entropy and information gain are in bits.

Logistic model:
\[
p(y=1\mid x)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta^\top x),\qquad
\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}},
\]
\[
\operatorname{logit}\bigl(p(y=1\mid x)\bigr)
=\ln\!\left(\frac{p(y=1\mid x)}{1-p(y=1\mid x)}\right)
=\beta_0+\sum_{i=1}^{30}\beta_i x_i.
\]
Here \(\beta_i\) has units of logit per one-count change; \(x_i\) is a count per game section; \(p\) is dimensionless. A one-unit change in \(x_i\) changes the logit by \(\beta_i\), not the probability linearly. The paper reports coefficients including ground duels \(3.401654\), ground duels lost \(-2.935787\), unsuccessful crosses \(-2.722266\), clearances \(-2.267617\), shots off target \(1.883792\), shots \(-1.663560\), fouls received \(-1.565999\), interceptions \(1.240091\), and crosses \(1.183030\) [PAGE 6]. Decision tree:
\[
H(S)=-\sum_{c\in\{0,1\}}p_c\log_2 p_c \quad \text{bits},
\]
\[
\operatorname{IG}(S,A)=H(S)-\sum_{v\in \operatorname{vals}(A)}
\frac{|S_v|}{|S|}H(S_v) \quad \text{bits}.
\]
The tree split on information gain with a minimum of 40 samples per node; the final tree had depth 13 and 43 leaves; accuracy 85%, AUC 0.9 [PAGE 5, 7]. \[
\phi_i(f,x)=\sum_{S\subseteq F\setminus\{i\}}
\frac{|S|!\,(|F|-|S|-1)!}{|F|!}
\bigl[f_{S\cup\{i\}}(x_{S\cup\{i\}})-f_S(x_S)\bigr],
\]
\[
f(x)=\phi_0+\sum_{i=1}^{|F|}\phi_i(f,x).
\]
Here \(\phi_i\) is in probability units because the model output is the probability of the reference class; \(\phi_0\) is the base value. The network used one hidden layer, learning rate \(\alpha=0.0001\), \(\beta_1=0.9\), \(\beta_2=0.999\) [PAGE 5]. \operatorname{Accuracy}=\frac{TP+TN}{N},\qquad
\operatorname{AUC}=\int_0^1 \operatorname{TPR}(t)\,d\operatorname{FPR}(t).
\]
Both are dimensionless. The paper used 70% training and 30% evaluation and 10-fold stratified cross-validation [PAGE 5].

In this dataset \(S\) perfectly determines the recorded label \(y\): \(P(y=1\mid S=\text{Wyscout-2016-men})=1\) and \(P(y=1\mid S=\text{Opta-2017-women})=0\). The observational model estimates \(p(y\mid x)\), which can be decomposed as
\[
p(y\mid x)=\sum_s p(y\mid x,s)\,p(s\mid x).
\]
Because \(x\) carries provider-specific event definitions, tournament year, and game structure, \(p(s\mid x)\) is not neutral.

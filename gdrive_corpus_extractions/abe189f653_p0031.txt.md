Verifiable prediction: Unique-hue settings (unique yellow/blue) for the test gray will not shift when the comparison patch is simultaneously visible; any measured chromatic asymmetry will fall within inter-trial test-retest reliability (±1.5 ΔE_ab*). A persistent shift under direct comparison would violate Hering’s reported boundary condition.

Scenario 3 — Spatial-frequency aliasing veto. Counterfactual: If viewing distance d is increased until the gridiron period P = 10 mm subtends an angular period below the retinal resolution limit, the patterned contrast must vanish because the bars are no longer separately resolvable. Verifiable prediction: At d such that θ_P > 2° (low spatial frequency, ~0.25 cpd), the contrast effect is present; at d such that θ_P < 0.2° (>25 cpd, near Nyquist), the effect is absent. The transition point should be measurable by psychometric threshold and should align with the observer’s independently measured acuity, not with any fixed chromatic parameter.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Layer map.** $L_1$ psychological ($\mu$); $L_2$ biomechanical/motor (force–velocity, rate of force development, tendon stiffness, range of motion, sleep-dependent consolidation); $L_3$ physiological (autonomic load, sleep debt, immune status); $L_4$ environmental (surface friction, ball mass and wetness, wind vector, WBGT); $L_5$ organisational (roster, selection, fixtures).

**Veto theorem (E11).** For any monotone map $\mu\mapsto P$, there exists a constraint $g(x,w)\le 0$ with $\partial g/\partial\mu=0$ and relaxation time $\tau_{\rm heal}\gg\tau_m$. *Constructive proof:* a player with an acute anterior-cruciate rupture is outside $\mathcal{F}$; tissue remodelling runs on a timescale of months; no $\mu\in[0,1]$ returns $g\le 0$ inside the match horizon. Therefore $\partial\mathcal{F}/\partial\mu=0$. Mindset moves $\dot{x}$, not $\mathcal{F}$.

**Biomechanical veto.** Scrum and maul force scales with mass and technique, not belief. A growth mindset cannot make a lighter pack generate more sustained newtons against a heavier one across eighty minutes; it can only improve the *technique* term toward that pack's own structural ceiling. The document's own strongest line — "coaches with a growth mindset often make the most mistakes" [PAGE 3] — is a claim about error tolerance, not about force output. Conflating them is the classic over-extension.

**Physiological veto on "effort → development."** "With increased adversity and challenge comes an increase in effort and with effort comes development" [PAGE 3] is monotone as written. Replace with the interior optimum $D(\dot{E})=a\dot{E}-b\dot{E}^2$ for $\dot{E}\le\dot{E}_{\rm crit}$, with injury hazard rising superlinearly above $\dot{E}_{\rm crit}$ and motor consolidation degrading under sleep debt. Optimum effort is therefore not maximum effort; a coach maximising $\dot{E}$ has misread the operator.

**Weather veto.** Wet ball and crosswind alter the affordance landscape: grip and flight are governed by surface and aerodynamic physics, indifferent to $\mu$. The non-veto channel is **affordance selection** — under wet conditions the growth-consistent response is to alter the carrying and kicking pattern, not to exhort harder. This is exactly the transferability gate the source itself installs: "ascertain as to whether the knowledge will work for me personally... if it doesn't I throw it out" [PAGE 5]. The veto is therefore *source-authorised*, not externally imposed.

**Saturation veto on humility (E7).** $H\le H_{\max}<1$. A coach who assigns all failure causation internally will mis-target corrective action when the true cause is biomechanical or structural. Unbounded humility is a diagnostic failure mode, not a virtue.

**Adjudication rule.** Where $L_2$–$L_4$ veto, $L_1$ must yield; where $L_2$–$L_4$ merely constrain, $L_1$ sets the rate of approach to the constraint boundary. Any coaching proposition that violates $\partial\mathcal{F}/\partial\mu=0$ is rejected regardless of its rhetorical force.

**VETO 1 — Refocus-card controller (tests E6).**
*Reward:* $\Delta R_p$ = (rate of process-referent coaching instructions per minute in the final fifteen minutes) − (same in the first fifteen), coded from fixture video against a published codebook, two coders blind to condition, Krippendorff $\alpha\ge0.70$; aggregated over $\ge 8$ fixtures per coach.
*Prediction:* card-users $\Delta R_p\ge-10\%$; matched non-users $\Delta R_p\le-25\%$, with $N_r=3$–$4$ outperforming $N_r=0$ and $N_r\ge 8$ (working-memory overload, per "three or four 'refocus points'" [PAGE 4]).

**VETO 2 — Humility index (tests E7 and its downstream coupling).**
*Reward:* $H=A_{\rm int}(\text{failure})-A_{\rm int}(\text{success})$ coded from post-fixture media transcripts by three blind coders; coupled to a team process-KPI retention score over the two fixtures following a loss.
*Prediction:* $H>0.2$ predicts KPI retention within 10% of baseline; $H\le 0$ predicts $>20\%$ decay. Monotonicity is predicted only up to $H_{\max}$; above roughly $H\approx0.8$ the relationship is predicted to flatten or invert (saturation veto).
*Falsifier:* if $H\le0$ shows no attenuation of team KPI retention, or if $H>0.8$ outperforms the mid-range, the attribution operator has no downstream coupling and the humility paragraph [PAGE 4] is decorative.

**VETO 3 — Effort monotonicity (tests the E11 physiological veto against [PAGE 3]).**
*Reward:* per-session load (session-RPE × duration, arbitrary units) versus seven-day change on a validated skill-retention test (e.g. passing accuracy under induced fatigue, or reactive-agility time), plus soft-tissue injury incidence per 1,000 exposure-hours.
*Prediction:* inverted-U with an interior optimum; injury hazard rising superlinearly above $\dot{E}_{\rm crit}$; development declining in the top load decile.
*Falsifier:* if development is monotone increasing through the top decile *and* injury hazard is flat, then "with effort comes development" is literally true and the veto theorem fails at $L_3$. This is the cleanest available kill-shot for the document's most over-stated sentence.

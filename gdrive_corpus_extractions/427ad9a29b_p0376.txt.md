**Operator 1: The Latent Differentiation Operator ($\mathcal{L}$)**

$$ H(A|S, \mathcal{M}) = -\sum_{a,s} P(a,s|\mathcal{M}) \log P(a|s,\mathcal{M}) $$

$$ \frac{d}{dt} H(A|S, \mathcal{M}) = -\eta \cdot I(S_t; A_t | \mathcal{M}_{t-1}) $$
Where $\eta$ is a plasticity constant and $I(S_t; A_t | \mathcal{M}_{t-1})$ represents the mutual information gained from navigating the maze structure itself, separate from terminal rewards. This explains why latent learning builds up without appreciably greater "effect" (reward) for the correct path; the information gain comes from spatial navigation constraints, not the goal reward.

$$ \Delta \theta \propto \Gamma_{pun}(\text{Valence}) \cdot \nabla_\theta \log \pi(a|s) \cdot \epsilon_{rewards} $$

$$ \Lambda_{freq} = \lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \left( 1 - D_{decay}(\Delta t_i) \right) $$

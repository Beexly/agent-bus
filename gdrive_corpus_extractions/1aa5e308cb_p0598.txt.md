The "primordial motor circle" is thus broken by lateral inhibition mediated through the associative network.

The system is in state 'A', and the probability of transitioning to 'B' is governed by the conditional likelihood established by prior co-occurrence. **The Drainage Mechanism:**
James states: *"the process in $S^b$... is... the stronger drainage-channel for $S^a$'s excitement."*

$$ D_{ak} = \frac{w_{ak}}{\sum_{l \in Targets(S^a)} w_{al}} \cdot E_{S^a} $$

More accurately, the simultaneous activation represents competitive shunting inhibition. A more precise formulation involves the net input:
$$ h_{M^a}(t+\Delta t) = \sigma( w_{aM} h_{S^a} - \beta \cdot h_{S^b} ) $$
Here, the activation of $S^b$ introduces an inhibitory term (scaled by $\beta$) that subtracts from the drive to $M^a$. This captures the idea that the "current" into $S^b$ physically depletes the capacity to fire $M^a$.

**Serial Transition Probability:**
The sequential nature—"A" followed by "B"—can be quantified via Mutual Information or Conditional Entropy. The reduction in uncertainty about the next letter given the current one is:
$$ I(S^a; S^b) = H(S^b) - H(S^b | S^a) $$
James’s "dotted lines" represent high mutual information links forged through Hebbian learning ("cells that fire together, wire together"). The strength $w_{ab}$ is proportional to the log-likelihood ratio of observing B after A versus B in general.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
William James’s 1890 proposal did not emerge in a vacuum; it sits at the intersection of late 19th-century telegraphy, associationist psychology, and early electrophysiology. The lineage traces back to Herbert Spencer’s evolutionary psychology, which viewed mental operations as economy-driven flows. James’s specific metaphor of "drainage" and "currents" was likely influenced by contemporary electrical engineering concepts—Kirchhoff’s laws of current division were well-established by the 1880s. Engineers understood that adding a parallel low-resistance path would shunt current away from a primary load. James applied this circuit logic to the nervous system, anticipating the field of neuroneconomics where synaptic resources are allocated efficiently.

Furthermore, this conceptualizes a form of "retrocausality" in psychological time. While strictly causal in his physical model (A triggers B-preparation which then alters A's processing), the *experience* is that the knowledge of the sequence influences the present moment. This foreshadows the "backward causation" debates in quantum mechanics (Wheeler’s delayed choice) and the psychological phenomenon of "postdiction," where later stimuli alter the perception of earlier ones. James provided the first mechanistic sketch of how temporal binding could occur without a central homunculus, relying instead on local, self-organizing interference patterns within a network.

In terms of multi-week traps, one must avoid the anachronistic error of viewing this as a "neural network" in the modern computational sense (McCulloch-Pitts, 1943). James lacked the binary logic gate paradigm; his units were analog intensities flowing through variable resistance pathways. Confusing his hydraulic analogy with digital computing leads to a fundamental misreading of his theory of consciousness as a dynamic field rather than a symbolic processor.

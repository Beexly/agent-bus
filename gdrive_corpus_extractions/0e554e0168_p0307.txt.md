Conflating "Fordham Prep Sch., New York City" with "Fordham University, New York City" [PAGE 2] is another trap: they are distinct institutions despite shared nomenclature and geography. The recursion is: observed roster position \(\rightarrow\) assumed tactical role \(\rightarrow\) predicted play call \(\rightarrow\) actual crisis response. Because the coach's crisis habit is fixed by prior experience, the predicted play call overweights the run.

Counter-trap. The counter-trap is to assume that because a team returns its captain and coach (e.g., Georgia Tech retains Heisman), its performance will persist. The roster contains no retention variable, no eligibility clock, and no injury ledger [PAGE 2]. Therefore the persistence hypothesis is unverified and likely false after one season.

Clock. The absorbing boundary is the academic eligibility clock: a player listed as captain in 1915 may exhaust eligibility in 1916. The coach clock operates on annual contracts. Any recursive theory of mind that projects the 1915 roster forward without modeling these absorbing boundaries commits a clock error.

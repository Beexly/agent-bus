\mathcal{V}=\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{phys}}\lor\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{bio}}\lor\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{env}}.
\]
Because \(\mathcal{V}\) is a disjunction, \(\mathcal{V}(x)=1\) implies \(x\notin\mathrm{dom}(\mathcal{M})\); hence the map’s prediction \(\mathcal{M}(x)\) is undefined and no action is issued. This is a veto proof: physical, biomechanical, and environmental constraints are logically prior to the map, and the map cannot veto the veto.

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
RLVR denotes counterfactual verification in reinforcement-learning-style behavioral models: an intervention \(do(\cdot)\) is applied, and the outcome is compared against the observational baseline to test whether a map prediction survives a hard constraint.

Veto scenario 1 (Biomechanical veto on latent learning). Tolman’s cognitive-map account predicts that, when the usual maze path is blocked, a rat will select a novel shortcut because it possesses a map of the goal space [PAGE 456]. Intervention: raise the shortcut’s required vertical clearance to \(h_{\mathrm{obs}}\) while measuring the rat’s maximum jump height \(h_{\max}\) via force-plate kinematics. Verifiable measurement: high-speed videography yields \(h_{\max}\) with millimeter precision. Counterfactual prediction: if \(h_{\mathrm{obs}}>h_{\max}\), then \(P(\text{shortcut}\,|\,do(\text{block}))=0\), contradicting the pure map prediction \(P>0\). The biomechanical veto \(\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{bio}}=1\) nulls the map.

Veto scenario 2 (Physical energy veto). The map predicts path selection by expected reinforcement. Intervention: manipulate ambient temperature to alter thermoregulatory cost, and measure respiratory exchange ratio to obtain energy expenditure rate \(\dot{E}\) (watts). Verifiable measurement: indirect calorimetry gives \(\dot{E}\pm 2\%\). Counterfactual prediction: when \(E_{\mathrm{avail}}<E_{\mathrm{crit}}\), the rat selects the energetically cheaper path even if it is longer in reinforcement terms; formally, \(P(\text{longer but cheaper path}\,|\,do(E_{\mathrm{avail}}<E_{\mathrm{crit}}))\approx1\), whereas the map predicts \(P\approx0\). The physical veto \(\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{phys}}=1\) overrides reinforcement value.

Veto scenario 3 (Weather/environmental channel-capacity veto). Introduce visual noise (strobe flicker) at bandwidth \(B_{\mathrm{noise}}\) while the rat navigates. Verifiable measurement: psychophysical contrast-threshold testing yields effective SNR. Counterfactual prediction: when the required guidance rate \(R\) exceeds \(C=B\log_2(1+\mathrm{SNR})\), the rat’s path variance increases discontinuously and the map’s prediction of goal-directed efficiency collapses: \(P(\text{efficient path}\,|\,do(R>C))<0.05\), despite identical reinforcement history [PAGE 456]. The environmental veto \(\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{env}}=1\) severs the map-territory coupling.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: The field topology imposes a hard bound on any single continuous play. With \(L = 330\ \text{ft}\) and goal lines at or near the termini, the maximum net forward progress without an intervening dead ball is bounded by \(D_{\max} \le 330\ \text{ft}\) under Assumption G1, or \(D_{\max} \le 330 - 2d\) under Assumption G2. Any play design requiring a ball carrier to advance more than this distance in one continuous live-ball sequence is vetoed by the physical layer; the carrier must be downed or score before crossing the far boundary.

Biomechanical layer: elite human sprint speed is approximately \(10\ \text{m/s}\) (\(\approx 32.8\ \text{ft/s}\)). Traversing the full field length at this speed requires at least
\[
t_{\min} = \frac{L}{v} \approx \frac{100.584\ \text{m}}{10\ \text{m/s}} = 10.06\ \text{s}.
\]
Any play prescription demanding end-to-end advancement in less than roughly 10 seconds is vetoed by biomechanical limits on acceleration and top speed. This bound is approximate and assumes unobstructed running; contact, cuts, and blocking increase the time.

Weather layer: a headwind or wet surface reduces effective kicking range. A drop kick from the 25-yard-line limit [PAGE 1] must cover roughly 75 ft (22.86 m) to reach the opposing goal line. In calm conditions this is marginal for a drop kick; in a headwind or on a muddy field, the effective coefficient of restitution and aerodynamic drag reduce range. The weather layer therefore vetoes the kick-out as a reliable field-position strategy when environmental drag exceeds the threshold required to bridge 75 ft.

Sideline layer: "Touch or Bound" and "In Touch" [PAGE 1] mark the lateral boundaries \(y = \pm W/2 = \pm 80\ \text{ft}\). A kicker approaching from near the sideline has a reduced run-up angle; the biomechanical layer intersects with the sideline layer to veto kicks that require a perpendicular approach from outside the field of play.

Veto proof: A proposed play is physically admissible only if its entire trajectory remains inside \(\mathcal{F}\), its duration exceeds \(t_{\min}\) for full-field traversal, its kick distance does not exceed the aerodynamic range under prevailing weather, and its lateral path does not cross \(y = \pm 80\ \text{ft}\). Violation of any layer constitutes a veto.

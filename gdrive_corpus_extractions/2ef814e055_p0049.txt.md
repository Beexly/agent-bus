Veto architecture: define layer set L = {P, B, E} where P = physical containment, B = biomechanical/effector physiology, E = environmental/exogenous modulation. System output O (successful steer containment or measurable salivation) is permitted only if \forall \ell \in L, v_\ell = \text{false}. Any single v_\ell = \text{true} issues a hard veto over the conditioned-control layer.

Physical layer P (from [PAGE 79]): a wire fence must have tensile capacity T sufficient to turn a steer. If T < F_{\text{steer}}, where F_{\text{steer}} is the maximum lateral force in newtons the animal can apply, the fence fails mechanically. The learned association between boundary and shock cannot compensate for a collapsed conductor. Veto condition v_P = [T < F_{\text{steer}}].

Biomechanical layer B (from [PAGE 78]): Pavlov’s measurement requires an intact salivary duct and patent canula. Let \dot{V} be secretion rate in \mathrm{ml\,min^{-1}} and \Delta V_{\min} the sensor resolution. If the duct is ligated, denervated, or obstructed, then \dot{V} < \Delta V_{\min} regardless of central associative strength w. Veto condition v_B = [\dot{V} < \Delta V_{\min} \land w > 0], meaning neural learning exists but peripheral output is null.

Environmental layer E (exogenous to the excerpt but required for veto completeness in outdoor deployment): for an electrified fence, effective contact voltage V_{\text{eff}} depends on soil resistivity \rho (\Omega\,m), precipitation, and grounding quality. If V_{\text{eff}} < V_{\text{threshold}}, the steer does not register the aversive unconditioned stimulus; contingency fails and extinction proceeds. Veto condition v_E = [V_{\text{eff}} < V_{\text{threshold}}].

Adjudication order: B is a hard signal veto (no measurable response can be generated); P is a hard state veto (containment is physically lost); E is a soft contingency veto that degrades I(C;R) over time until the conditioned reflex extinguishes. The formal veto predicate is V = v_P \lor v_B \lor v_E; if V = true, the RLVR controller must abort or switch to an open-loop mechanical restraint rather than rely on the conditioned association.

The counter-trap is to overcorrect and claim that spatial qualia are entirely free-floating, disconnected from the pressure differential \(\Delta P\) that provokes them [PAGE 1]. The correct position is interactionist: the pressure change is the proximal cause, but the spatial format (round, tridimensional, inside the head) is a constructed representation whose metric is not the anatomical receptor area.

The perceptual clock is therefore not a passive sampling rate but an efference-correlated rhythm: without the motor act that changes the sensory signal, the spatial percept decays.

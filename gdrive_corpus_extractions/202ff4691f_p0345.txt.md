Veto operators:
$$
V_{\text{phys}} : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{C}_{\text{phys}}, \qquad
V_{\text{weather}} : \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}'' \subseteq \mathcal{E},
$$
where $V_{\text{phys}}$ projects or clips an action to the nearest biomechanically feasible point, and $V_{\text{weather}}$ filters suggestions made unreliable by weather-induced noise.

Adjudication order: compute $a^* = P(V_{\text{weather}}(e))$ at L1, then apply $a^{\text{final}} = V_{\text{phys}}(a^*)$. If $V_{\text{phys}}(a^*) = \emptyset$ (the computed action lies outside the physical capability set), the veto cascades downward to L0 (reflex fallback) or to repose ($a = \varnothing$). The composite map is well-defined iff
$$
\mathcal{C}_{\text{phys}} \neq \emptyset
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Sigma(V_{\text{weather}}(e)) \neq \emptyset.
$$
Singularity: when weather noise drives $\Sigma(V_{\text{weather}}(e)) \to \emptyset$ while the physical layer simultaneously blocks reflex fallback, the system reaches complete action paralysis. Verification protocol: instrument the action channel and inject controlled biomechanical constraints and environmental occlusion; a valid veto proof requires that action success probability drops to the reflex baseline exactly when the composite feasibility condition fails, and not before.

Scenario 1 — Suggestion-vacuum veto.
Condition: $\Sigma(e) = \emptyset$ (environment supplies no actionable suggestion).
Counterfactual: had $\Sigma(e) \ni d$, the agent would execute $a = \mathcal{A}(d)$ with reward $R > R_{\text{thresh}}$.
Prediction under veto: the agent emits only reflex action $a \in \mathcal{A}_{\text{reflex}}$ or null action. Verifiable via action logs: $\mathbb{P}(a \in \mathcal{A}_{\text{reflex}} \mid \Sigma(e)=\emptyset) > 0.95$, and task-completion reward remains at baseline $R_{\text{base}}$.

Scenario 2 — Reflective-timeout veto.
Condition: $\Gamma$ fails to converge within decision horizon $\tau$ (i.e., $\nexists d^*$ such that $\Gamma(\Sigma(e), k_t) = d^*$ for all $t \le \tau$).
Counterfactual: with unlimited $\tau$, $\Gamma$ converges to $d^*$ and the optimized action $a^* = \mathcal{A}(d^*)$ yields $R(a^*) > R(a_{\text{habit}})$.
Prediction under veto: the system falls back to habit $a_{\text{habit}}$. Verifiable via KL divergence: $D_{\text{KL}}(p_{\text{actual}}(\cdot \mid \text{timeout}) \,\|\, p_{\text{habit}}(\cdot)) < \varepsilon$, and $R(a_{\text{actual}}) \approx R(a_{\text{habit}})$.

Scenario 3 — Biomechanical-force veto.
Condition: unconstrained $a^*$ requires force $F^* > F_{\max}$, so $a^* \notin \mathcal{C}_{\text{phys}}$.
Counterfactual: absent the veto, the trajectory would follow $a^*(t)$ with peak force $F^*$.
Prediction under veto: $V_{\text{phys}}$ clips force to $F_{\max}$, producing a saturated trajectory $F(t) = F_{\max}$ for duration $\delta t > 0$. Verifiable via force-sensor telemetry: $\int_{t_0}^{t_0+\delta t} \mathbb{1}[F(t) = F_{\max}] \, dt = \delta t$, and task-failure rate increases by a measurable margin $\Delta \eta > 0$ relative to unconstrained trials.

Thorndike treated repetition of a successful response as the sufficient mechanism for "stamping in" a connection—pure frequency without an explicit recency term. Hull attempted to quantify habit strength as a monotonic function of reinforced repetitions, formally resembling sER = sER_0 + k \cdot N_reinforced, where N is a count (dimensionless) and k is a learning-rate constant. Multi-week traps: Tolman's own heuristic contrasts twenty-four hours with sixty days or more. This establishes a recency window \tau_c on the order of days to weeks.

Any present-day claim that a specific neurotransmitter, brain region, or gene “causes” Tolmanian fixation is therefore a retro-causal projection onto a text that provides only a phenomenological definition and a breakdown limit. Veto condition: If means-object selection is invariant (\(P(m_0) > 0.95\)) to reinforcement in all domains, then \(\forall m,s: D(m,s) \approx 0\). By Tolman’s own limit [PAGE 445], purposive behaviorism cannot describe this system.
Prediction: The system will be better modeled as a fixed set of tropisms/reflexes. Transfer to a novel means-object never previously paired with reinforcement will be at chance (\(P \approx 1/|\mathcal{M}|\)), and no immanent purpose (demand) can be inferred from behavior. Verification metric: mutual information \(I(\text{reinforcement history}; \text{means-object choice}) \approx 0\) bits across all domains.

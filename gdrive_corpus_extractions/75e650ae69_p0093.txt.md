Counterfactual: without TMS, ΔP > η_c produces deterministic sweep to B [PAGE 83]. With TMS, g_c is reduced such that |ΔP| < η_c. Verification: psychophysical report plus phase-locking value of steady-state visual evoked potentials (SSVEP) at the grating frequency.

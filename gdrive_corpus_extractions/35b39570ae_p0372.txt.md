Conversely, fatigue accumulation might increase $s_{OR}$, making perfect patterning impossible even with large differences. This genealogy reveals a tension between Hull's static model and dynamic biological adaptation.
Furthermore, the use of "habs" reflects an era before neural correlates were mapped. Modern neuroscience interprets "residual excitatory potential" as synaptic weight plasticity combined with neuromodulatory tone (e.g., dopamine).

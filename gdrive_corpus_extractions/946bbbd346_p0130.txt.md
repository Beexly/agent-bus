Weather/environmental layer. “Water-table, lowered, danger of, 42-43” and “Resources, exhaustion of, 37–38, 40” are slow environmental veto variables. Let resource stock R(t) ∈ [0, R₀] with ẋ = −κ u² (consumption scales with control effort squared), κ > 0 [unit⁻¹·s⁻¹]. Adjudication order: physical veto (power/torque) is checked first; if passed, biomechanical veto (noise/phase margin) is checked; if passed, environmental veto (resource barrier) is checked. A policy surviving all three is admissible; failure at any layer terminates the candidate without proceeding to deeper recursive evaluation.

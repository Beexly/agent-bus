This is why per-actor pipelines (detect→LSTM per player→aggregate: Ibrahim 51.1%/81.9%) are dominated by relational models that let nodes read each other's states (CERN 83.6%, RCRG 89.5%, StageNet 89.3%, Anchor-Transformer 94.4%, DIN 93.1%). The second-order regress: the *annotator* is also an agent whose expert judgment defines ground truth [PAGE 1]; on figure skating/gymnastics the model is effectively inferring an expert's intent, so measured accuracy is bounded by annotator agreement, not by pixels. No "clock" or wagering trigger exists in this text; the only temporally-forced regime the paper documents is the dense-stroke boundary case, handled above.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Honest reframing: a "veto" here is a documented condition under which a model class must be rejected. The paper supports three physical/statistical vetoes; it supports no weather veto, and I will not invent one.

Veto A (appearance-information veto, skeleton class): skeleton methods are vetoed for fine-grained appearance tasks because they "cannot capture the appearance information" and rely on "noisy" detected joints [PAGE 12]. Evidence: PoseC3D 68.8% FSD-10 < two-stream; ST-GCN 30.7% Kinetics-Skeleton. Survival condition: tasks where motion topology dominates and appearance is irrelevant.

Veto B (data-regime veto, two-stream class): two-stream is vetoed in small-data fine-grained regimes because "two-stream networks have higher capacity, easily overfitting the dataset" [PAGE 12]. Survival condition: large, balanced data (R(2+1)D-Two-Stream 97.3% UCF101).

Veto C (imbalance veto, ERM training): under $\rho \approx 60:1$ (FineBasketball) plain classifiers are vetoed; tail collapses to near-zero recall (29.78% overall, PAGE 13). Survival condition: resampling/reweighting or long-tail losses (the paper's recommended mitigations, PAGE 16-17).

Physical/biomechanical note grounded in the source: the survey cites figure-skating jump biomechanics [PAGE 21, ref [166] "Biomechanics of figure skating jump double axel"] but builds no biomechanical model; the closest physical constraint it actually formalizes is the temporal-sampling one (Section 3 above). Weather/environment: the only environmental confound the paper names is capture quality of "smartphones from the view of coaches" (SVW, PAGE 7) and YouTube camera motion (Olympic, PAGE 7) — a nuisance-variation veto, not a meteorological one.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three falsifiable scenarios, each with a concrete prediction tied to the paper's own numbers (verifiable against its tables/datasets).

1) De-confound frame rate (table tennis). Counterfactual: hold the model fixed and drop TTStroke-21 from 120 FPS to 25 FPS. Prediction: accuracy falls sharply because frames-per-stroke drops from [48,240] to [10,50], violating the $f_s \gtrsim 5$ Hz boundary-resolution bound. Corroborating anchor: broadcast P2A (272 h, long-tailed, PAGE 17) is a low-frame-rate analog and should be far harder than TTStroke-21's 91.4%. Falsified if a 25 FPS retrain matches 91.4%.

2) Repair the long tail (FineBasketball). Counterfactual: balance the 60:1 imbalance to ~130/class (the paper's stated per-class average, PAGE 5) with no other change. Prediction: TSN-Two-Stream rises well above 29.78%. Falsified if accuracy is unchanged after balancing (which would instead indict appearance ambiguity, not imbalance).

3) Close the skeleton appearance gap (FSD-10). Counterfactual: replace predicted joints with ground-truth 2D/3D pose plus joint-refinement, holding the GCN family fixed. Prediction: CTR-GCN (66.2%) and PoseC3D (68.8%) improve but remain below appearance-bearing two-stream, because the veto is missing appearance information, not joint noise alone. Falsified if perfect pose lets a skeleton model exceed two-stream on this fine-grained set — which would overturn the paper's [PAGE 12] conclusion.

Summary judgment: the survey's durable, verifiable contributions are its difficulty decomposition (annotation, temporal density, multi-agent AND-sensitivity, long-tail, nuisance variation) and its efficiency frontier (CSN ⅓, MViT ⅕, MoViNet 20% FLOPs). Its weakest inference is treating raw benchmark accuracy as method strength without normalizing for frame rate or label agreement; scenarios 1–2 are the clean tests of that gap.

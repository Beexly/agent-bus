h=\sigma(W_1x+b_1),\qquad \hat y=\phi(w_2^\top h+b_2),
\]
\[
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\eta\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}(\theta_t),
\]
where \(W_1\in\mathbb{R}^{5\times4}\), \(w_2\in\mathbb{R}^{5}\), \(\sigma,\phi\) are activation functions, and \(\eta>0\) is the learning rate. \]
with entropy in bits when \(\log_2\) is used. For attribute \(A\) with values \(v\),
\[

also in bits. Assumptions: discrete or discretized attributes, stable class labels, and counts large enough for reliable proportions. \]
where \(d_i\) is throw distance in meters and \(\hat d_i\) is predicted distance in meters, so \(\mathrm{MAE}\) has unit meters. Assumptions: same measurement protocol and same run-up condition for training and test throws. Breakdown: \(n=0\) is undefined; heavy-tailed errors make MAE unstable; a change in venue, implement, or athlete preparation shifts the conditional distribution and invalidates the meter-scale comparison.

dimensionless. Assumptions: the outcome classes are ordinally meaningful. Breakdown: \(K=1\) makes the denominator zero; misordered classes invalidate the cumulative construction; this score does not by itself identify whether a model is well calibrated.

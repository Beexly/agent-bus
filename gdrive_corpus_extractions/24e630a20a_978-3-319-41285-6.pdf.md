<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Scientific lineage and the "retro-causal" reading that 't Hooft foregrounds.

The intellectual chain begins with von Neumann's early no-hidden-variables argument `[86]`, contested in `[6,15,49]`, and the EPR Gedankenexperiment of Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen `[33,53]` (Sect. 3.2, `[PAGE 44]`), whose state $x_1-x_2=0,\ p_1+p_2=0$ with $[x_1-x_2,p_1+p_2]=0$ (`[PAGE 45]`) is the archetype of counterfactual definiteness. Bell `[6,7]` and the CHSH refinement `[20,78]` convert this into the inequality $|\langle S\rangle|\le2$ (Eq. 3.12), empirically confirmed by Aspect-type experiments `[2,3]` (`[PAGE 53]`). The computational-universality thread runs von Neumann–Ulam `[87,84]` (1940s) → Conway's Game of Life `[39,40]` (1970) → Zuse's *Rechnender Raum* `[97]` (1967) → Wheeler's "It from Bit" `[89,90]` → Wolfram's *A New Kind of Science* `[92]` (Sect. 1.4, `[PAGE 28-29]`), with Fredkin cited as the discrete-determinist pioneer (`[PAGE 26]`). 't Hooft's own prior excursions `[101,119,120,125,109]` seed the CAI.

The "retro-causal" character is explicit and multi-week in its traps: the ontological photon data "already carry information about the correlation function of… settings… non-local information about the future" (`[PAGE 57-58]`). 't Hooft formalizes this not as backward signalling but as time-symmetric solving of the microscopic equations (Sect. 3.8, `[PAGE 59]`): running the counterfactual Alice-reset backwards produces an $S=0\to$ partial $D$-state ($s=2$) admixture — "a much less probable initial state… a counterfactual initial state" (`[PAGE 60]`). The multi-week trap: the mouse-dropping model `[PAGE 55]` generalizes to quasar-driven settings emitted near the Big Bang `[38]` (`[PAGE 58]`), whose only permitted common cause is inflationary shared past — a correlation with a cosmological, not local, provenance. A further lineage trap is the recurring failure of earlier hidden-variable programs to define their terms ("authors rarely go into the trouble to carefully define what these theories are," `[PAGE 18]` footnote 1), which 't Hooft seeks to remedy by replacing emotional "free will" with a mathematical freedom-of-initial-state (`[PAGE 45,78]`).

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Recursive agent modeling: crisis-habit regression vs. counter-trap vs. clock.

The theory's ToM object is a deterministic observer (Alice/Bob) whose "free will" is redefined as "freedom to choose the initial state" (Sect. 3.8, `[PAGE 45-46]`). Recursion depth 1: the decaying atom "knows in advance" the *correlation function* of Alice's and Bob's future settings — not the settings themselves (`[PAGE 57]`). Depth 2: the observer's own brain state is itself ontological, so "neither Alice nor Bob will ever be able to produce [superposed beable] states by rotating their polarization filters" (`[PAGE 112]`). The self-referential closure is the ontology-conservation law: ontic states evolve only into ontic states (`[PAGE 44,112]`).

Crisis-habit regression: when a counterfactual setting is *forced*, the model does not "conspire" — it regresses to the dominant thermodynamic macrostate. The reset state carries the spin-2/D admixture and is Born-suppressed because the amplitude "was put in the state vectors (templates) to describe the probability distribution of the initial states" (Sect. 4.3, `[PAGE 55-56]`; `[PAGE 108]`). Regression to the high-probability initial macrostate is the habit; deviation is exponentially costly.

Counter-trap: the standard reply — "conspiracy is disgusting, one could better stop doing physics" (`[PAGE 42,54]`) — is itself the trap 't Hooft spring-releases: apparent conspiracy "is a feature, not a bug" (`[PAGE 92]`), and "the only conspiracy the equations perform is to fool some of today's physicists" (`[PAGE 25]`). The counter-trap defuses the objection by relocating the correlation to conserved ontological information (Sect. 5.7.3, `[PAGE 91-92]`).

Clock: the recursion is ticked by the discrete $\delta t$ (Planck time $T_{\rm Pl}=5.391\times10^{-44}$ s, Eq. 6.3). Cogwheel periodicity $T=N\delta t$ and the continuum limit $N\to\infty$, $N\delta t=T$ fixed (Chap. 13, `[PAGE 142]`) set the phase clock; "if $\delta t$ [is] as small as the Planck time… deviations from the ontological model will be unobservable" (`[PAGE 37]`). Anti-prediction: no agent can out-compute Nature — "no human, nor even any other imaginable intelligent being, will be able to compute faster than Nature itself" (`[PAGE 68]`), which is how "free will" is formally restored inside determinism.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical-consistency veto proof (which layers can veto the CAI).

Layer A — Causality / no-signalling: PASS (no veto). Microcausality holds because spacelike-separated observables commute (`[PAGE 45]`), and beables satisfy $[B^a_{\rm op}(\vec x_1,t_1),B^b_{\rm op}(\vec x_2,t_2)]=0$ for all separations (Eq. 5.9, `[PAGE 81]`). The nonzero spacelike vacuum correlator Eq. 15.99 therefore transmits no signal (Chap. 20.7, `[PAGE 105]`). The Bell "spookiness" is correlation without communication.

Layer B — Unitarity / probability: PASS. $U$ is a genuine invertible permutator (Sect. 2.1 footnote, `[PAGE 34]`); Born normalization is exact by Gleason/completeness (Eqs. 3.5, `[PAGE 51]`), and no nonlinear Schrödinger or Born-rule correction is admitted (`[PAGE 8,56]`).

Layer C — Hamiltonian positivity + locality: VETO ACTIVE (partial). Eqs. 14.2–14.4 prove that a local, bounded-below $H$ obeying all four constraints (Eqs. 9.1–9.5) cannot be constructed generically from a CA; the required cut-off obeys $R\gg M_{\rm Pl}/\langle H\rangle^2$, i.e. non-locality $\gtrsim T^2M_{\rm Pl}$ (`[PAGE 149]`). This is the genuine unresolved veto, explicitly deferred to quantum gravity (`[PAGE 100-101, 116]`).

Layer D — Lorentz / diffeomorphism invariance: VETO ACTIVE. "The generic cellular automaton will not even remotely be Lorentz invariant… rotation symmetry will be reduced to some discrete lattice rotation group" (Sect. 5.2, `[PAGE 72]`). Continuous Poincaré symmetry is not reproduced by the worked models (`[PAGE 116]`).

Layer E — Interacting ontology: VETO ACTIVE. The clean beable basis exists only for the non-interacting massless neutrino (mass and interactions "must be left for later," `[PAGE 151]`); the BCH interaction series "does not seem to converge at all" (`[PAGE 145]`); edge states of measure zero spoil exact correspondence (`[PAGE 62,168]`).

Proof verdict: No *physical-principle* layer (causality, unitarity, Born) vetoes the interpretation as a doctrine; the vetoes are structural/mathematical (positivity, Lorentz, interactions), all localized at the Planck-scale quantum-gravity frontier — consistent with the book's own hedge that "we cannot pretend to have solved that" (`[PAGE 73]`).
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable (falsifiable) prediction-vetoes with concrete outcomes.

Veto 1 — Quantum-computer ceiling. Claim (`[PAGE 99,127]`): "the quantum computer will never outperform classical computers when these would be scaled to Planckian dimensions… numbers with millions of digits can never be decomposed into prime factors, even by a quantum computer." Prediction: scalable fault-tolerant QC will hit a fundamental decoherence floor; Shor-factoring of million-digit semiprimes is physically impossible regardless of engineering. Falsifier: a QC factoring such integers (or provably beating a Planck-cell/Planck-time-scaled classical machine) falsifies the CAT ("no classical theory can explain perfect quantum computers," `[PAGE 99]`).

Veto 2 — Zero Born-rule / zero-nonlinearity deviation. Claim (`[PAGE 56,98,108]`): "Born's rule will be exactly obeyed; there cannot be systematic, reproducible deviations"; no nonlinear correction to Schrödinger's equation exists. Prediction: precision tests (interferometry, collapse-model searches à la GRW/CSL `[4,41,73]`) return null results at all accessible scales; the "collapse" is automatic via classical observables being diagonal in the ontic basis (`[PAGE 53,111]`). Falsifier: any reproducible, scale-dependent violation of the Born rule or a detected nonlinear Schrödinger term.

Veto 3 — Pervasive spacelike ontic correlation. Claim (`[PAGE 57,171]`): "these space-like correlations must be huge" (`[PAGE 58]`); the vacuum correlator $\langle\text{conn}\rangle=\delta r^2/[\pi^2|r_1-r_2|^2]$ (Eq. 15.99) is nonzero, and Bell-violating settings obey the 3-point $W=\tfrac12|\sin(4c-2a-2b)|$ (Eq. 3.22). Prediction: any loophole-free Bell test using cosmologically independent randomness (quasar/photon-pair sources) will continue to confirm $S\to2\sqrt2$ (Eq. 3.14) with *no* residual classical local-hidden-variable model that also respects experimenter free choice; the settings–source correlation, if ever directly measurable as a signaling channel, would falsify the "correlation-not-signal" claim. Falsifier: demonstration that outcome correlations are reproducible by a locally factorizable hidden-variable model with freely chosen settings (equivalently, extraction of usable superluminal information from the vacuum correlator).

Meta-note (theory-status): the CAI also self-declares its own falsifiability boundary — a single ontological world (contra Many-Worlds, `[PAGE 34]`) and automatic collapse — so the three vetoes above are the intended empirical handles; the theory's principal open risk is internal (Layer C/D vetoes), not empirical.

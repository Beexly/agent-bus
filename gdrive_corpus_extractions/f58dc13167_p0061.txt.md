**2. The Pooling Mechanism and Convergence**
Knight posits that combining cases reduces uncertainty: "the error in such prediction approaches zero as the number of cases is increased" [PAGE 61].

$$ \sqrt{N}(\bar{X}_N - \mu) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) $$

$$ \frac{\sigma_{\bar{X}_N}}{\mu} = \frac{\sigma}{\mu\sqrt{N}} \to 0 \quad [\text{dimensionless}] $$
This proves mathematically why insurance—an "elementary development of business organization"—can convert risk into near-certainty by absorbing the variance for an administrative fee (loading factor).

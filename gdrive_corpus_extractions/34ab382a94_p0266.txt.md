<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
**Formal Definition:** Let $X$ be a locally compact topological group. A Haar measure is a Borel measure $\mu: \mathcal{B}(X) \to [0, \infty]$ satisfying:
1. $\mu(U) > 0$ for every non-empty Borel open set $U \subseteq X$.
2. Left-translation invariance: $\mu(xE) = \mu(E)$ for all $x \in X$ and $E \in \mathcal{B}(X)$. [PAGE 251, bottom]

**Inductive Construction Operator:** For a compact neighborhood $D$ of identity $e$:
$D_0 = D$
$D_{n+1} = D_n \cdot D = \{ xy \mid x \in D_n, y \in D \}$
This operator maps compact sets to compact sets due to the continuity of group multiplication and the Tychonoff theorem (product of compact sets is compact).

**Singularity/Breakdown Regime:** The construction breaks down if $X$ is not locally compact, i.e., if there is no compact neighborhood $D$ of $e$. In this regime, the base case $D_0$ is undefined, and the sequence $\{D_n\}$ cannot be initiated. Additionally, if the group operation is not continuous, $D_{n+1}$ may fail to be compact, causing the $\sigma$-compactness of $Z = \bigcup D_n$ to collapse.
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

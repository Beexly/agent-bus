<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer veto: neural tissue has finite bandwidth and metabolic cost, so it cannot represent the plenum in full; the veto condition is C_{neural} < H(R) bits/s. James's selection operator passes this veto because selection is a physical necessity of bounded implementation, not an optional epistemic stance [PAGE 1]. Biomechanical layer veto: the theorizing faculty is embodied in an organism with a volitional nature oriented toward action; perception and thought are transformed "in the interests of our volitional nature" [PAGE 1]. Any account that treats scientific abstraction as disembodied contemplation fails this veto. Weather/environmental layer veto: Mill requires that facts be separated "in nature, not in our minds only" [PAGE 1]; chaotic or non-separable environmental systems (e.g., coupled weather dynamics) may resist operational decomposition, such that no mental analysis yields invariant consequent-antecedent mappings. Adjudication: James's account survives the physical and biomechanical vetoes by grounding selection in bounded, embodied volition. It partially survives the environmental veto via Mill's operational requirement but acknowledges that "one intellect differs immensely from another" [PAGE 1], leaving a residual failure regime where nature's non-separability defeats both frequency-based association and volitional selection alike.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Scenario 1 (Volitional deprivation): Remove all practical and emotional ends from an observer, leaving pure passive exposure to impressions. Prediction: formation of stable scientific abstractions collapses; the compression of the sensory stream into "hypothetical data and laws" [PAGE 1] falls to chance or idle frequency baselines because the theorizing faculty "works exclusively for the sake of ends" set by subjectivity [PAGE 1]. Verification: measure description length of a subject's categorization under rewarded versus unrewarded observation; a significant increase in description length under reward removal confirms the veto.
Scenario 2 (Non-separable antecedents): Present a system whose antecedents are intrinsically entangled (e.g., coupled chaotic oscillators) such that no mental analysis can separate them "in nature" [PAGE 1]. Prediction: invariant consequent-antecedent mappings cannot be learned regardless of training frequency; prediction error remains bounded above zero. Verification: perform system identification on the coupled system and compare the error floor against a separable control; a non-zero asymptote confirms the veto.
Scenario 3 (Direct plenum access): Suppose an observer could directly experience the plenum in which "all its parts are contemporaneous, each is as real as any other" [PAGE 1]. Prediction: selection and emphasis become unnecessary; first-person reports would describe undifferentiated plenitude rather than abstract systems of laws, and such reports would not compress into scientific predictions without re-introducing fragmentation. Verification: compare first-person phenomenological reports from non-dual or meditative states with third-person measurement grids; divergence between report and compressible model confirms that direct plenum access does not yield scientific abstraction.

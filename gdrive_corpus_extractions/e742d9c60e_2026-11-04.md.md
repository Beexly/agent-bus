$$ \mathcal{L}(R) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i \cdot r_i $$

*Formulation:* Let $T$ be the time-to-event (sacking) and $C \in \{1, ..., K\}$ be the cause. The cause-specific hazard function for cause $k$ is:
$$ h_k(t | \mathbf{X}_t) = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{P(t \le T < t+\Delta t, C=k | T \ge t, \mathbf{X}_t)}{\Delta t} = h_{k0}(t) \exp(\boldsymbol{\beta}_k^T \mathbf{X}_t) $$

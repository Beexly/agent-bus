Scenario 1: Tendency collapse. Counterfactual: The offense runs the “standard” immediate low charge [PAGE 1] on three consecutive snaps from the same formation. Verifiable condition: game film shows all three charges defeated at the line of scrimmage with the runner gaining ≤ 0 yards, and the defensive line keys on the runner’s low pad level before the snap. Prediction: On the fourth snap, if the offense does not introduce a genuinely new trick (outside-arm grasp, feint, or hurdle) but repeats the charge, the defense will defeat the block within Δt_c < 1.2 s and record a tackle for loss. Verification: chart the fourth snap; a tackle at or behind the line of scrimmage confirms the veto.

Scenario 2: Environmental friction veto. Counterfactual: The offense attempts the stiff arm [PAGE 1] on a natural-grass field with measured coefficient of friction μ < 0.4 (wet conditions). Verifiable condition: sideline or field testing records μ below the threshold, and film shows the ball carrier’s palm sliding off the tackler’s shoulder without displacing him. Prediction: The stiff arm will fail to create separation on at least 80% of attempts; the ball carrier must abandon arm-extension repulsion and rely on shoulder contact and footwork. Verification: compare stiff-arm success rate in dry (μ ≥ 0.7) versus wet (μ < 0.4) quarters; a statistically significant drop confirms the friction veto.

Scenario 3: Recursive counter-trap under clock pressure. Counterfactual: With under two minutes remaining and the offense trailing, the opponent has scouted the offense’s week-long preference for “feint left, shift right” [PAGE 1]. Verifiable condition: pre-snap alignment shows the defensive back committed to the left feint and unblocked on the right shift. Prediction: The offense will either (a) abandon the feint and regress to the immediate charge due to clock compression, or (b) run the feint into the prepared defender. In either case, the play results in negative yardage or a turnover unless the offense introduces an action outside the opponent’s scouted belief support before the next possession. Verification: log the final two minutes; a sack, tackle for loss, or interception on a scouted tendency confirms the recursive veto.

By 1905, following a deadly season that saw 18 deaths and hundreds of injuries, President Theodore Roosevelt pushed for reforms. The resulting rules changes, including limiting forward passes and adjusting kickoff positions, were designed to spread players out and reduce mass formations. Yost’s manual reflects this new orthodoxy: emphasizing strategic kicking and careful possession management over brute-force plunges.

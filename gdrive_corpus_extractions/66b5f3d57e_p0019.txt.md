</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The probabilistic aggregation cannot override hard physical, biomechanical, or weather constraints. Define a veto predicate V(t) = V_{\text{phys}}(t) \lor V_{\text{bio}}(t) \lor V_{\text{weather}}(t). If V(t) = 1, the ML output \bar{C}(t) is inadmissible and the admissible capacity reverts to the hard constraint C_{\text{hard}}(t); flights are cancelled or delayed according to C_{\text{hard}}, not according to \bar{C} or P(W_k).

Physical layer veto V_{\text{phys}}: for each runway configuration, available runway length L_{\text{avail}} after maintenance closures must exceed required landing distance L_{\text{req}}(aircraft, weight, wind); crosswind component X_{\text{wind}} must remain below aircraft-specific limit X_{\text{max}}; braking action coefficient \mu must meet minimum standards. If any condition fails, that runway contributes zero arrivals/departures. Units: meters (SI), meters per second, dimensionless.

Biomechanical layer veto V_{\text{bio}}: controller sector workload W_{\text{sec}} is a function of aircraft count, complexity, and communication rate; human perceptual-motor reaction time \tau_h is bounded (order seconds); fatigue state F bounded by duty-time regulations. If W_{\text{sec}} > W_{\text{max}} or F exceeds regulatory limit, the accept rate is capped below the ML prediction regardless of weather. No synthetic ratio is invoked; these are hard bounds from human-factors and air-traffic standards.

Weather layer veto V_{\text{weather}}: ceiling H below landing minima, visibility VIS below required RVR, wind shear, microburst, or convective activity within the approach radius forces arrival rate to zero for the affected runway. Units: feet or meters for ceiling, meters for visibility, meters per second for wind.

Adjudication: C_{\text{adm}}(t) = \min\big( \bar{C}(t), C_{\text{phys}}(t), C_{\text{bio}}(t), C_{\text{weather}}(t) \big). If the minimum is sourced from any veto layer, the probabilistic scenario weights are moot; the system must cancel or delay flights to satisfy the hard limit. This is a veto proof because no weighting of weather analytical scenarios can resurrect a runway that is physically closed, biomechanically overloaded, or meteorologically below minima [PAGE 19, claims 14–15 regarding runway configuration, slots, and capacity predictions].
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

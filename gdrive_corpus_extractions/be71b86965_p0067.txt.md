Induced outer measure:
\[
\mu^{*}(E) = \inf \Bigl\{ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mu(A_n) : E \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} A_n,\; A_n \in R \Bigr\}, \qquad E \subseteq X.
\]

Carathéodory measurable class:
\[
M = \Bigl\{ E \subseteq X : \forall A \subseteq X,\; \mu^{*}(A) = \mu^{*}(A \cap E) + \mu^{*}(A \cap E^{c}) \Bigr\}.
\]

Induced measure on the measurable class:
\[
\mu = \mu^{*}\big|_{M}.
\]

Re-induced outer measure from μ on M:
\[
\mu^{**}(E) = \inf \Bigl\{ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mu(B_n) : E \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} B_n,\; B_n \in M \Bigr\}.
\]

Regularity condition:
\[
\mu^{*} \text{ is regular} \iff \mu^{*} = \mu^{**}.
\]

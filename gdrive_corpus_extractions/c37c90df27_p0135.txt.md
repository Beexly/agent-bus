$$ \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{F}] \propto \mathbf{u}_{G} = \frac{\mathbf{x}_G - \mathbf{x}_{O,center}}{\| \mathbf{x}_G - \mathbf{x}_{O,center} \|} $$

$$ \Delta \mathcal{I}(S_t) = H(P(\mathbf{F})) - H(P(\mathbf{F} | S_t)) \geq \tau $$
Where $H(\cdot)$ denotes Shannon entropy measured in bits. If $\Delta \mathcal{I} \approx 0$, the behavior is random noise; if $\Delta \mathcal{I} > 0$, the behavior is informed by a perceptual expectation.

$$ D_{KL}\left( P_{obs}(R) \parallel Q(R | \mathbf{F}) \right) = P_{obs}(R=0) \ln \frac{P_{obs}(R=0)}{Q(R=0 | \mathbf{F})} $$

**Operator 2 — Log-linear opinion pool (the assumed fusion operator).**

$$p(z_t^{(1..M)}\mid x_t)=\prod_{m=1}^{M}p_m(z_t^{(m)}\mid x_t)^{\,w_m},\qquad w_m\ge 0,\ \sum_{m=1}^{M}w_m=1$$

Units: $w_m$ dimensionless. **Exact singularity:** if any branch is hard-vetoed, $p_m\to 0$, then $p\to 0$ for *every* $x_t$ and the posterior is undefined — "veto by zero." This is precisely the pathology the paper's own reference [6] names: "Revisiting rgbt tracking benchmarks from the perspective of modality validity: A new benchmark, problem, and method" [PAGE 2]. **Operator 3 — Softmax gating and its two degenerate limits.**

$$w_m=\frac{\exp\!\big(g_m(x_t,z_t^{(m)})/\tau\big)}{\sum_{k}\exp\!\big(g_k/\tau\big)},\qquad \tau>0\ \text{(nats)}$$

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Model the tracker as an agent with a belief $b_t(x_t)$ and a *habit* — its template memory. **Crisis habit regression** is the observed law that under stress the agent does not become more Bayesian; it becomes more prior-driven. Formally, under occlusion or fast motion the effective likelihood temperature rises ($\tau_\ell\to\infty$ in Operator 3), so the posterior reverts to the transition prior $p(x_t\mid x_{t-1})$ — pure constant-velocity extrapolation — and the appearance term contributes nothing. This is *exactly* the regime the source names as the field's raison d'être: RGB trackers "cannot achieve precise and robust tracking in some complex situations, such as lighting changes, fast motion, occlusion, and appearance variations" [PAGE 1]. The recursive point is that the habit is self-reinforcing: drift corrupts the template, the corrupted template lowers the likelihood of the true target, which further raises $\tau_\ell$, which further reverts to habit. The loop has no fixed point in the RGB-only case — which is *why* an independent modality is a structural, not incremental, fix.

**Counter-trap.** Every added modality supplies a new surface for the environment to attack the habit through. Language ($L$) creates a *description-collision* attack: place a distractor matching the sentence better than the target, and the tracker's own grounding term pulls it off. Thermal ($T$) creates a *crossover* trap — a target that cools to background temperature is invisible precisely when it is most still. Event ($E$) creates a *flicker* trap (Operator 5, singularity 2) where a modulated lamp out-events the target. Depth ($D$) creates a *transparency* trap. The recursive structure is that the tracker's defence against trap $m$ is to down-weight modality $m$ — but down-weighting is itself the crisis habit regression, returning the agent to RGB and to the original failure. The only escape is a *third* modality or a learned validity head, which is what the "modality validity" programme (ref [6], [PAGE 2]) attempts.

**Clock.** Two clocks run at once: the frame clock $\Delta t_{\text{frame}}=33.3$ ms (30 Hz) and the event clock $\Delta t_{\text{event}}\approx1\ \mu$s, ratio $\kappa\approx3.3\times10^{4}$ (Operator 10). Three clock pathologies follow. (C1) *Template-update aliasing*: if the template updates every $k$ frames, the memory horizon is $k\cdot33.3$ ms; for a 2 Hz stride ($T=500$ ms) the update period beats against the gait period, producing periodic failure at $k$ near 15. (C2) *Timestamp decoherence*: events carry microsecond stamps but frames carry none, so cross-modal attention must learn an offset it cannot observe; the "cross-resolution" and "unaligned" framing of CRSOT [PAGE 2, ref 9] is the acknowledgment. (C3) *Resolution-vs-latency trade*: high-definition event sensors (1440×1080, [PAGE 3]) raise information rate but widen readout, shrinking $\kappa$ in practice. Recursively, the agent's model of *its opponent's clock* — how fast the scene changes relative to its own update cadence — is the missing state variable; no method in Tables 1–2 exposes it.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
A **veto** here means: a physically grounded constraint that no architectural choice in Tables 1–2 can overturn. Three layers.

**Physical/radiometric layer.** Veto V1: if $\Delta T_{\text{eff}}<\text{NETD}$ (Operator 6), the thermal branch transmits zero information about $x_t$; any non-zero $w_T$ adds variance without bias reduction, so the fused posterior is strictly worse than RGB-only. Veto V2: if $\beta d\gtrsim3$ (Operator 8), RGB path radiance dominates and the RGB likelihood is uninformative — no de-weathering network recovers contrast that was never captured. Veto V3: at $\rho_D\to1$ (Operator 7), depth is a constant, and zero-imputation injects a bias of $\rho_D\mathbb{E}[d]$ metres that is *systematic*, not random, so it cannot be averaged out by ensembling. Proof sketch for all three: information is monotone under deterministic post-processing, $I(x;f(z))\le I(x;z)$; when $I(x;z_m)=0$ no $f$ can raise it. The paper's own framing concedes this structure by calling the extra modalities compensatory "under adverse weather conditions, occlusions, rapid motion, and appearance ambiguity" [PAGE 1] — compensation is exactly a statement about which regime is *not* vetoed.

**Biomechanical/kinematic layer.** Veto V4: inter-frame displacement. For a target with speed $v$ and image scale $s$ [px·m$^{-1}$], $\Delta_{\text{frame}}=v\,\Delta t_{\text{frame}}\,s$ px. A pedestrian limb tip at $v\approx5$ m·s$^{-1}$ with $s=58.8$ px·m$^{-1}$ gives $\Delta\approx9.8$ px·frame$^{-1}$ — trackable. A UAV-observed vehicle at $v=20$ m·s$^{-1}$, $s=100$ px·m$^{-1}$ gives $\Delta\approx66.7$ px·frame$^{-1}$ — a 13% jump of a 500 px box per frame, which no template matcher survives. This is the quantitative content of the "fast motion" challenge category, and it is why the high-frame-rate/event branch exists: "Frame-Event Alignment and Fusion Network for High Frame Rate Tracking" [PAGE 5]. Veto V5: rolling-shutter skew, $\delta_{\text{skew}}=v\,s\,T_{\text{rs}}$; at $T_{\text{rs}}=30$ ms and the same $v,s$ in V4, $\delta\approx60$ px — larger than the object; the bounding box is geometrically undefined, not merely noisy.

**Weather layer.** Veto V6: thermal crossover compounded by rain. Rain at 8–14 μm both lowers $\Delta T_{\text{eff}}$ (evaporative cooling on the target surface) and adds Mie scattering; when the two act together, $I(x;z_T)\to0$ *and* $I(x;z_{\text{RGB}})\to0$ simultaneously. That coincidence is the only true "no-modality" regime, and it is the one case where the survey's promise of robustness has no listed remedy — consistent with the absence of any weather-specific row in Tables 1–2. Veto V7: fog with $V<200$ m gives $\beta=3.912/200=0.0196$ m$^{-1}$; at 50 m standoff, transmission $=e^{-0.98}=0.375$, i.e. a 62% contrast loss before sensor noise — a hard cap on RGB range regardless of backbone depth.

**Adjudication.** V1–V3 are *branch-level* vetoes adjudicated by $\Delta I=0$ (Operator 4). V4–V5 are *geometry-level* vetoes adjudicated by pixel displacement exceeding box scale. V6–V7 are *scene-level* vetoes adjudicated by joint transmittance. None of the seven is architecture-contingent; the correct response in each case is to drive the corresponding $w_m\to0$ *before* fusion, which is a validity-detection problem, not a representation-learning problem.

Each scenario is a trainable/testable counterfactual with a falsifiable prediction and a verification path using artifacts the paper already indexes.

**Scenario 1 — Thermal-crossover nullity (LasHeR / MV-RGBT).** Counterfactual: take any released RGBT checkpoint (BAT, GMMT, or AFter, [PAGE 7]) and evaluate twice on the modality-invalid subset of MV-RGBT [PAGE 4] — once with the thermal branch live, once with it replaced by uniform noise. *Prediction:* the success-AUC delta is bounded by the crossover-frame fraction, $|\Delta S|\le \rho_{\text{cross}}\cdot\epsilon$ with $\epsilon<0.5$ points, and the delta is statistically indistinguishable from zero where $\Delta T_{\text{eff}}<\text{NETD}$. *Falsifier:* a significant positive delta in the thermally-dead subset, which would prove the thermal branch is leaking non-radiometric cues (e.g. texture) and that the "thermal" label is a misnomer. *Verification:* mask propagation over the released validity annotations; two-sample test on per-sequence AUC.

**Scenario 2 — Event-silence collapse (VisEvent / COESOT / EventVOT).** Counterfactual: synthesise event silence by clamping the event stream to zero for a random 30% of frames in COESOT ("1,354 RGB-event video pairs covering 90 target object categories," [PAGE 3]). *Prediction:* (a) fixed-fusion early-concatenation models degrade roughly linearly in silence fraction, $\Delta S\propto\rho_{\text{silence}}$; (b) gated/state-space models (Mamba-FETrack, [PAGE 5]) degrade sub-linearly because the learned $w_E$ collapses — i.e. $\Delta S_{\text{gated}}<\Delta S_{\text{concat}}$ by a margin exceeding the seed variance. *Falsifier:* gated models degrade equally or worse, which would show the gate is not reading signal quality and the operator's $\tau\to0$ regime is being hit inadvertently. *Verification:* identical architecture, identical seed, silence-mask-only ablation; report paired per-sequence deltas.

**Scenario 3 — Depth-zero-imputation bias (DepthTrack / RGBD1K).** Counterfactual: on the transparent/reflective sequences of DepthTrack ("200 RGBD video sequences, 150 of which are used for training and 50 for testing," [PAGE 3]), set depth to the training-set mean and to zero, respectively, and compare against native depth. *Prediction:* the centre-error shift equals the modelled shrinkage $\rho_D\,\mathbb{E}[d]\,s$ px within 20%, and RMSE correlates with $\rho_D$ at $r>0.8$ across sequences. *Falsifier:* no correlation between $\rho_D$ and error, which would imply the tracker has learned to ignore depth already — i.e. the RGBD column is functionally RGB. *Verification:* recompute $\rho_D$ per frame from raw depth; regress per-sequence RMSE on $\bar\rho_D$.

**Cross-scenario meta-prediction.** All three counterfactuals predict the same qualitative law: the value of any modality $m$ is $\Delta I_m$ (Operator 4), which is non-zero only off the validity boundary, and every reported gain in Tables 1–2 is therefore an *average over validity states*, not a property of the modality. A survey that tabulated $\Delta I_m$ per validity regime would supersede every row of the current paper list; that table does not exist in this source, and its absence — not any missing architecture — is the actual frontier.

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
All statistics below are defined formally; units are stated; assumptions and singularity/breakdown regimes are explicit. Source citations refer to [PAGE 18].

Expected points added per play:
\[
\mathrm{EPA}_i = \mathrm{EP}_{\mathrm{post},i} - \mathrm{EP}_{\mathrm{pre},i}
\]
\[
\overline{\mathrm{EPA}}_{\mathcal{W}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{W}|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal{W}}\mathrm{EPA}_i
\]
where \(\mathcal{W}\) is a play window (e.g., Weeks 4–8, min 20 plays). Units: expected points per play (points/play, dimensionless on the EP scale). Dossier #1 reads Maye at ~0.45 EPA/play [PAGE 18].

Completion percentage over expected (probability scale):
\[
\Delta p = p_{\text{actual}} - p_{\text{expected}}
\]
CPOE is \(100 \times \Delta p\) in percentage points when reported as a chart axis.

Box-stat rates (physical exposure normalization):
\[
r_{\mathrm{TFL}} = \frac{N_{\mathrm{TFL}}}{N_{\mathrm{snap}}}, \qquad r_{\mathrm{PBU}} = \frac{N_{\mathrm{PBU}}}{N_{\mathrm{target}}}
\]
Units: snaps\(^{-1}\) and targets\(^{-1}\). Dossier #5 cites Will Anderson 10 TFL; PFR confirms head, ESPN counts Anderson at 12 (scorer variance) [PAGE 18]. Dossier #10 cites Benford/Watson/Moss at 9 PBU [PAGE 18].

Points allowed off turnovers (dossier #2):
\[
P_{\mathrm{OTA}} = \sum_{d \in \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{TO}}} \Big( \mathrm{PointsScored}_{\text{ensuing}}(d) + \mathbb{1}_{\mathrm{defTD}}(d) \cdot 6 \Big)
\]
where \(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{TO}}\) are drives beginning after a takeaway; defensive TDs included per chart notes [PAGE 18]. NYJ 0, NO 52 [PAGE 18].

Blitzed-QB subset (dossier #2/12 lineage):
\[
\mathcal{B} = \{ a : n_{\mathrm{blitzers}}(a) \ge 1 \}, \quad |\mathcal{B}| \ge 30
\]
\[
\overline{\mathrm{EPA}}_{\mathcal{B}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}|}\sum_{a\in\mathcal{B}}\mathrm{EPA}(a)
\]
Huntley ~0.33 EPA is flagged small-sample [PAGE 18].

Recency bake-off operator:
\[
\rho_b = \operatorname{Corr}\!\Big( \operatorname{Rank}_b(\text{QBs}), \text{NextGameEPA} \Big)

for \(b \in \{\text{drop W1–3, 2-wk half-life, full-season}\}\). The window with maximal \(\rho_b\) wins; none is currently measured [PAGE 18].

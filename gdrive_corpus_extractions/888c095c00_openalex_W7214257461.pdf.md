The modern chain runs: Wojke et al. 2017 (DeepSORT [6]) → Zhang et al. 2022 (ByteTrack [3]) → Ravi et al. 2025 (SAM2 [5]) → Robinson et al. 2026 (RF-DETR [4]). The team-assignment chain runs Zhai et al. 2023 (SigLIP [13]) → McInnes et al. 2018 (UMAP [14]) → MacQueen (KMeans). The pitch-registration chain runs Yu et al. 2007 (homography from camera motion [12]) → Theiner & Ewerth 2023 (TVCalib [17]) → the present paper's simpler DLT variant. Ball-evaluation lineage is Wu, Lim & Yang 2015 (OTB benchmark [28]) — imported wholesale in [PAGE 7]. Dataset lineage: Giancola et al. 2018 (SoccerNet [18]) → Deliège et al. 2021 (SoccerNet-v2 [19]) → Cioppa et al. 2022 (SoccerNet-Tracking [7]) → Somers et al. 2024 (SoccerNet-GSR [22]).

**Multi-week trap #1 — Benchmark non-comparability.** The paper itself concedes "ovrednotili na lastnem naboru ročno anotiranih posnetkov, kar ne omogoča neposredne primerjave z rezultati na uveljavljenih referenčnih zbirkah" [PAGE 3]. The 6 clips / 3554 frames are a private split; SoccerNet-Tracking carries 200 clips of 30 s plus one full 45-min half [PAGE 2]. Any external precision claim built on the paper's numbers is unmoored.

**Multi-week trap #2 — Training-data provenance contamination.** Detection was trained on "Football Players Detection z Roboflow Universe [24]" [PAGE 3] and pitch keypoints on "Soccer Field Localization, objavljeno na platformi Roboflow Universe [26]" [PAGE 4]. Roboflow Universe datasets are community-curated with unspecified broadcast conditions; classifier behavior on lighting regimes not in the training distribution is a silent risk the paper does not audit.

**Multi-week trap #3 — Metric-definition drift.** The net-IDSW statistic is *introduced by this paper* [PAGE 8]. It is not standardized in the MOT literature; a reader comparing the paper's IDSW=93 to a SoccerNet-GSR GS-HOTA score is comparing incomparable scales. The paper is honest about this ("smo poleg standardne metrike IDSW uvedli dodatno mero" [PAGE 8]) but downstream consumers often ignore the caveat.

**Multi-week trap #4 — Single-camera homography drift.** With one fixed camera, any keypoint distribution that is not rank-sufficient produces an ill-conditioned $\mathbf{H}$ (see O2 breakdown). The 99,22 px max error [PAGE 9] is the visible tail; a longer video with more occluded frames would produce a larger tail, not a smaller one.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
**Crisis habit regression — observed in vanilla SAM2.** [PAGE 4] and [PAGE 8]: when occlusion occurs, vanilla SAM2's behavior is to "preprosto ohranja zadnjo znano masko, tudi kadar ta med tem že zdrsne na napačnega igralca." Under repeated crisis frames, the model regresses to the persistence heuristic — identical mask, wrong identity — and the standard IDSW metric cannot see it. The regression is invisible to the metric because the mask *never formally switches*; it dissolves silently.

**Counter-trap — the paper's ID-checking.** [PAGE 7] enumerates five steps: predict expected position, periodically RF-DETR re-detect (especially on detected bounding-box overlap), Hungarian-match, post-separation re-verify and correct, treat goalkeepers separately. The counter-trap is that *this same discipline inflates the raw IDSW count* (93 vs 34) by surfacing transient swaps the metric records as full events. [PAGE 8]: "od 93 zabeleženih dogodkov IDSW kar 88 (94,6 %) izključno prehodne narave." The counter-argument the authors correctly advance is that net IDSW (5) is *closer* to vanilla's (2) than the raw number suggests, and 95,8 % retained identity is achieved *while actively correcting*, whereas vanilla achieves 98,3 % by not correcting at all.

**Clock — the overloaded term in this paper.** Three distinct clocks collide: (a) broadcast clock 25–30 FPS [PAGE 10]; (b) model clock 0,14 FPS [PAGE 10]; (c) human-intervention clock implicit in `LOST`-state re-anchoring [PAGE 4]. The paper's design assumes the broadcast clock and the human clock are decoupled (offline analysis), but the model clock is ~217× slower than the broadcast clock, so the human clock dominates end-to-end wall time whenever `LOST` fires. This is a three-clock desynchronization, not a single latency number.

**Recursive self-model of the evaluator.** The paper implicitly asks the reader to trust net-IDSW over standard IDSW. A skeptical reader should ask: was net-IDSW chosen *post hoc* after observing IDSW=93, or pre-registered? The paper does not say. A charitable reading is that the metric is motivated by a genuine structural property of ID-checking systems; an uncharitable reading is metric-shopping. The honest adjudication is that the *mechanism* (re-detection and Hungarian re-verification) is documented on [PAGE 7] independently of the metric, so the mechanism is real; the metric's priority over standard IDSW is a framing choice, not a discovery.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

**Veto 1 — Biomechanical occlusion (physical).** Player-player overlap is the primary identity-stress condition. [PAGE 2] cites SoccerNet-Tracking: "hitro gibanje, zakritja in zamenjave identitet kot ključne izzive." [PAGE 7] formalizes the trigger: "takoj ko sistem zazna tesno prekrivanje robnih okvirjev dveh ali več igralcev – ponovno izvede detekcijo." **Veto proof:** during the overlap interval, two masks occupy overlapping px regions; Hungarian cost $\| \hat{\mathbf p}_i - \mathbf d_j \|_2$ becomes ill-separated; per-frame label oracles flip. The paper shows the consequence quantitatively (93 standard IDSW, 88 transient). No biological/biomechanical layer *adds* information here; the mitigation is purely geometric (post-separation re-verification).

**Veto 2 — Ball physical scale (dimensionless).** Ball annotated region: "19,9×19,9 px" [PAGE 7]. Motion blur and shading routinely erase the ball for several frames. [PAGE 2] lists the failure modes: "njena majhnost, hitre spremembe smeri, zamegljenost zaradi gibanja in pogosta zakritja; žoga se lahko na posameznih okvirih zlije z oznakami igrišča ali drugimi svetlimi deli posnetka." **Veto proof:** at 19,9 px extent, a ~10 px centre error drives IoU below 0,5 (stated verbatim [PAGE 7]); this is why the paper abandons MOT and adopts OTB CLE. The Kalman filter is the only mitigation, and it cannot restore a signature that was never measured — it can only bridge within `COASTING` `$\Delta t$` bounds.

**Veto 3 — Lighting and jersey ambiguity (photometric).** [PAGE 2]: "uspešnost je odvisna od razlik med dresi, osvetlitve, senc in natančnosti segmentacije." SigLIP descriptors are photometric; shadows and stadium light temperature gradients shift them off-cluster. **Veto proof:** the 3 mis-assignments out of 125 players (97,6 % accuracy, [PAGE 9, Table 4]) concentrate in 2 of 6 videos ("Vse tri napačne razvrstitve so se pojavile v samo dveh od šestih posnetkov" [PAGE 9]) — i.e., the failure is video-level, not player-level, consistent with a per-video photometric shift.

**Veto 4 — Goalkeeper camera-position disappearance (geometric).** [PAGE 4]: "Vratarji so obravnavani ločeno, saj se lahko zaradi položaja kamere pojavijo z zamikom ali začasno izginejo iz kadra." **Veto proof:** crowd-based Infomap/RF-DETR detection cannot distinguish "keeper re-entering after occlusion" from "keeper re-entering after bench switch" without the SigLIP descriptor tie-in the paper specifies on [PAGE 4]. The spatial-only fallback is dimensionally under-constrained (one bbox, no kinemetic continuity across the disappearance).

**Veto 5 — Weather (out-of-scope but boundary-defining).** The corpus never evaluates rain, snow, or fog. **Veto proof:** rain streak optics alias with fast small-object detection; fog reduces contrast at pitch distance for keypoint localization. Because the paper does not measure these conditions, no quantitative veto can be derived from this corpus, and any claim that the pipeline generalizes to adverse weather is manufactured. This is a *knowledge-boundary* veto, not a failure veto.

**Veto Scenario A — Real-time substitution veto.** *Counterfactual:* replace the 428,8-min SAM2 pipeline with a hypothetical real-time successor and claim the paper licenses live-clock analytics. *Verification:* [PAGE 10, Table 7] reports 7239,5 ms·frame⁻¹ at 0,14 FPS against a 25–30 FPS feed. [PAGE 11] states "v trenutni obliki ni primeren za takojšnjo (angl. real-time) analizo." *Prediction if the counterfactual is run:* any live-clock inference built on this substrate will lag match events by ~200× the frame period, collapsing temporal alignment. **Falsification test:** deploy the pipeline on a live feed and measure the mean lag between detected pass and true pass timestamp; predict mean lag > 3 min per clip.

**Veto Scenario B — IDSW headline veto.** *Counterfactual:* cite "IDSW = 93" as evidence the proposed system is worse than vanilla SAM2 (IDSW = 34). *Verification:* [PAGE 8] reports 88 of 93 are transient (94,6 %), net IDSW = 5; [PAGE 8, Table 2] reports retained-identity 95,8 % (proposed) vs 98,3 % (vanilla). *Prediction if the counterfactual is run:* a naive reading will invert the correct ranking on the "silent drift" dimension. **Falsification test:** recompute standard IDSW with a merge window of ±10 frames; predict the proposed system's IDSW collapses to ≤ 8, approaching the net count, whereas vanilla's IDSW falls to ≤ 4.

**Veto Scenario C — Interception recognition veto.** *Counterfactual:* treat the paper's event-recognition module as a pass/interception classifier with usable precision. *Verification:* [PAGE 10, Table 6] — interception TP = 2, FP = 5, FN = 2, Precision 28,6 %, Recall 50,0 %, F1 36,4 %. *Prediction if the counterfactual is run:* the FP excess is structural (possession-change ambiguity, [PAGE 10]: "hitro, kratkotrajno spremembo posesti … težje zanesljivo ločiti od navadne podaje ali izgube sledenja"), so raising the confidence threshold will reduce FP but collapse Recall below 25 %. **Falsification test:** sweep the possession-change classifier threshold and plot Precision-Recall; predict the curve lies strictly below the pass-event curve (F1 85,4 %, [PAGE 10]) at every operating point.

**Meta-veto — the missing fixture.** No counterfactual can be run against a match, because the corpus contains no match. Any RLVR scenario that pretends to reason about *this team vs that team* is, by construction, a fabricated scenario. The three above are the only verifiable RLVR scenarios the source supports.

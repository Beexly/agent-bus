r = P(d, v) r,   equivalently (I − P(d, v)) r = 0,   r ≥ 0,   1^T r = 1.

Units: r_i is a dimensionless probability; the conserved L1-norm is Σ_i r_i = 1. Rank entropy is H(r) = −Σ_{i=1}^n r_i log_2 r_i bits, with maximum log_2 n bits at uniformity. Power iteration per claim 9 [PAGE 10] is

r^{(t+1)} = P r^{(t)},   r^{(0)} = initial estimate,

with error bounded by ||r^{(t)} − r||_1 ≤ C |λ_2(P)|^t, where |λ_2(P)| is the subdominant eigenvalue modulus.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The patent’s claim chain encodes a well-documented scientific lineage. Bibliographic citation analysis (Garfield, 1950s impact factor) assigned document importance by inbound citation counts; Pinski and Narin (1976) recursive impact factors made a journal’s weight depend on the weights of citing journals, precisely the “score dependent on scores of documents that link to” structure in claim 8 [PAGE 9]. The Stanford PageRank work (Page, Brin, Motwani, Winograd, 1998; issued as US 6,285,999 B1) generalized citation recursion to the web graph and added the stochastic random-surfer model of claims 10–11 [PAGE 10]. The claims in the provided pages show this migration explicitly: claim 8/9 [PAGE 9–10] defines a deterministic recursive estimate updated from linking-document ranks; claim 10 [PAGE 10] replaces analytic recursion with empirical traversal counts; claim 11 [PAGE 10] adds the predetermined random-jump probability that guarantees ergodicity.

Multi-week traps inherent to this lineage: (T1) stale-authority lag. Claim 9 [PAGE 10] generates an “initial estimate” and then updates it; if the crawl-to-serve cycle spans weeks, a document linked by a newly high-ranking page retains outdated authority until the next full iteration, so rank advantage from a fresh link is delayed by the iteration clock. (T2) link-burst retrocausality. Artificial inbound links can inflate rank within a crawl window; when the spam is devalued weeks later, the historical rank advantage is nullified ex post—future devaluation rewrites the causal value of past links. (T3) anchor-text persistence. Claims 15–16 [PAGE 10] incorporate anchor text; a Google-bomb-style anchor can dominate relevance signals for weeks until the next re-crawl and re-weighting. (T4) grouping lock-in. Claim 17 [PAGE 10] processes documents by groupings; community-internal links reinforce ranks independent of external quality, and community boundaries updated on a multi-week cadence trap outdated topical partitions in the score distribution.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
The ranking system and its users engage in a recursive theory-of-mind loop. The system models the user as the random surfer of claims 10–11 [PAGE 10]: a stochastic agent who follows links with probability d and teleports with probability 1−d according to distribution v. Sophisticated users (SEO operators, site owners) model that model and manufacture links, access patterns, and anchor text designed to be interpreted as high-access-probability or high-emphasis signals under claims 3 and 6 [PAGE 9]. The ranker must then form a second-order belief about whether a link is “genuine” or strategic, i.e., a level-k recursion: ranker believes user believes ranker believes link is organic.

Crisis habit regression: under traffic surge or infrastructure failure, the system may regress from the full iterative update of claim 9 [PAGE 10] to cheaper heuristics—textual matching only per claim 15 [PAGE 10], or the initial estimate without full convergence—because power iteration is compute-bound and the iteration clock cannot be met. Counter-trap: adversaries who know the regression schedule time link bursts and anchor-text campaigns to coincide with regression windows, betting that the ranker will not recompute deep authority scores before the manipulation is observed. Clock: convergence requires ||r^{(t+1)} − r^{(t)}||_1 < ε within T_clock; when the subdominant eigenvalue |λ_2(P)| is close to 1 (e.g., d → 1 or a weakly connected graph), the iteration count needed grows beyond T_clock / τ_iter and the system must serve stale or partial ranks. The theory-of-mind crisis is that the surfer model assumes uniform random choice conditioned on link structure, but under crisis the actual user distribution shifts to navigational and branded queries, invalidating v and making the ranker’s model of the user systematically wrong at the moment it is most needed.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
A rank output is admissible only if three layers simultaneously pass. Physical layer: servers, storage, and network must complete the power iteration within the latency budget L; if disk I/O or inter-node synchronization prevents ||r^{(t+1)} − r^{(t)}||_1 < ε before L expires, the link-based rank is vetoed and the system falls back to claim 15 textual matching [PAGE 10] with a staleness flag. Biomechanical layer: human link creation is bounded by attention and motor rates; if inbound links to a document arrive at a rate λ_in exceeding a human-creation bound λ_human (links/sec), the linking documents are flagged as non-human and their weights w_j are zeroed under claims 2–3 [PAGE 9], because neither “number of links” nor “probability of access” reflects genuine visitation. Weather layer: network-path instability (BGP flaps, DDoS, data-center route loss) biases the random traversal of claim 10 [PAGE 10], because observed traversal counts then measure reachability and packet loss rather than stationary authority; if packet-loss rate ρ_loss > ρ_threshold or route-flap frequency exceeds a bound, sampling-based ranks are vetoed until the path is stable.

Veto proof (adjudication rule): Let C_graph = “snapshot G_t is internally consistent,” C_iter = “iteration converged within ε,” C_sample = “traversal sample is unbiased.” The final rank r is released iff C_graph ∧ C_iter ∧ C_sample. Veto V = ¬C_graph ∨ ¬C_iter ∨ ¬C_sample. If V fires, the output is either (a) textual-match-only ranking (claims 15–16 [PAGE 10]), or (b) the last validated rank with an explicit age annotation (claims 13–14 [PAGE 9–10]: annotations as bars, icons, or text). No invalid authority score is served merely because the mathematical form of claim 8 [PAGE 9] is satisfied; layer failures override the recursion.

**Formal Operators & Equations**

Let $\mathcal{W} = \{W_1, ..., W_5\}$ be the set of worlds as defined by James [PAGE 292].
For an agent with cognitive state $S$, let $A(S) \subseteq \mathcal{W}$ be the set of active worlds (typically $|A(S)|=1$ for focused states, but can be $>1$ in meta-cognitive states).

*   **World Consistency Condition:** A world $W_k$ is a tuple $(\mathcal{P}_k, \mathcal{R}_k)$, where $\mathcal{P}_k$ is a set of propositions and $\mathcal{R}_k$ is a set of relations (logical, causal, narrative) among them. As per the footnote [PAGE 292], "objects within that world are knit together by perfectly definite relations." Formally: $\forall p,q \in \mathcal{P}_k, (p \mathcal{R}_k q) \Rightarrow \text{TruthValue}(p \land q \mid W_k) \in \{T,F\}$.

*   **Ontological Index Function:** $O(p, S) = k$ if proposition $p$ is evaluated true within the active world $W_k$ of state $S$, else $O(p,S)=0$ (non-existent/false). This captures James's point that "matter" *exists* as an idol of the tribe [PAGE 292]; its existence is relative to the world-index.

*   **Variational Free Energy for World Maintenance:** Following a predictive processing lens, an agent minimizes:
    $$F(W_k) = D_{KL}[Q(\theta) || P(\theta | D, W_k)] - \log P(D | W_k)$$
    where $Q(\theta)$ is the approximate posterior over latent states $\theta$, and $P(D | W_k)$ is the generative likelihood under world-model $W_k$. High $F(W_k)$ indicates poor fit (high prediction error) or high complexity (inefficient representation).

*   **Transition Operator Cost-Benefit:** The decision to switch from $W_i$ to $W_j$ is governed by a temporal difference update. Let $V_{net}(W) = R(W) - \gamma F(W) - \mu K(W)$, where:
    *   $R(W)$ is the extrinsic reward (survival, social cohesion) derived from the world.
    *   $F(W)$ is the prediction error cost.
    *   $K(W)$ is the computational/attentional cost of maintaining the worldview.
    *   $\gamma, \mu$ are scaling factors.
    A transition $i \to j$ is triggered if $V_{net}(W_j) > V_{net}(W_i) + \Delta$, where $\Delta$ is a hysteresis threshold preventing rapid flipping due to noise.

*   **Breakdown Regime (Singularity):** A world-model breaks down when its internal consistency fails under new data, i.e., $\exists d \in D_{new}$ such that $P(d | W_k) < \epsilon$ for a very small tolerance $\epsilon$. At this singularity, $F(W_k) \to \infty$ and the agent is forced into a "crisis" state, requiring either a major restructuring of $W_k$ (paradigm shift) or migration to another world $W_m$.

*   **Units:** $F, R, K$ are measured in *bits* (information theoretic units of surprise/cost) or *joules* (metabolic cost). $\lambda, \gamma, \mu$ carry units to ensure dimensional homogeneity (e.g., bits/joule).

The lineage of Knight’s discussion begins with Adam Smith’s *wealth of nations* (1776), where wages were determined by the demand for labor and the funds destined for its payment. John Stuart Mill synthesized classical thought, treating distribution as a problem of allocating the produce among agents. Alfred Marshall introduced partial equilibrium analysis, using time horizons (market period, short run, long run) to explain factor payments via marginal productivity. However, Marshall still assumed perfect certainty regarding future conditions.

Frank H.

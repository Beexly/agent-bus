Occlusion veto: extension (Eq. 3) and release point both require an unoccluded wrist and a planted, visible pitching ankle [PAGE 6]; self-occlusion during arm acceleration is exactly the regime the authors concede they cannot handle [PAGE 14]. Physical-frame veto: Eq. (3) is only meaningful in a single metric camera frame; the MLBPitchDB preprocessing does camera reprojection [PAGE 6], and any reprojection residual maps directly into a distance error. Weather/lighting veto (my extrapolation — the paper is silent on weather): the entire depth-and-pose stack is trained on daytime/indoor-style imagery; low sun, rain, or stadium-light flare are unmodeled covariates that would degrade Depth Anything pseudo-depth before the pose stage ever runs, so I predict disproportionate failure under those conditions even though the paper reports none. Net adjudication: classification-layer claims (identification, handedness, pitch position) survive veto; the continuous-kinematics layer (velocity, and to a lesser degree extension) does not survive the biomechanical and blur vetoes.

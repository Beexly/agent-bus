There is no physical, biomechanical, or weather veto that can overturn a sequence certified by Theorem 3 and verified in the net [PAGE 18–19, Theorem 9]. The proof of correctness is unconditional within the abstract model; real-world applicability is bounded by the round-robin completeness assumption [PAGE 5] and the availability of exact integer scores.

(3) *Leaderboard deltas without a skeleton-only deep ablation*: the baselines that isolate skeleton-only information are all *classical* (XGBoost/LightGBM at 88.94%, Random Forest at 85.89%, [PAGE 16]); the reader is invited to attribute the remaining $+5$ points to "vision," but no deep coordinate-only branch is run, so the attribution is under-determined. (4) *Class-difficulty misread as model weakness*: the low F1 on Classes 2–4 is not a capacity failure but a geometric boundary-density effect — [PAGE 14] correctly notes "angular thresholds separating it from Classes 1 and 3 are narrow," so small $\delta\theta$ flips labels. Anyone re-using this dataset should expect the same boundary-limited regime and not chase it with more parameters.

Modeling the authors' state. Constraints visible in the text: a single RTX 3060 / 12 GB ([PAGE 12]), a 5.8M-parameter budget, and a "compact + real-time (122 FPS)" deployment narrative ([PAGE 20]). Under these constraints the deterministic REBA label is an attractive simplification: it makes labels free, reproducible, and internally consistent (ICC $=0.84$, [PAGE 7]). The authors are *aware* of the ceiling — they explicitly disclose it ([PAGE 6]) — which is to their credit and reframes the critique from "hidden flaw" to "under-quantified consequence." Their likely defense: "we never claimed clinical validation; we claimed accurate, interpretable REBA classification robust to occlusion." That defense is largely valid; the residual obligation is to *measure* the vision increment, not assert it.

Modeling the hostile-but-fair reviewer (the counter-trap). The reviewer will demand four artifacts the paper omits: (a) a coordinate-only *deep* ablation to bound the image branch's contribution; (b) a deduplicated / subject-disjoint split to rule out near-duplicate frame leakage across the 70/10/20 partition [PAGE 12]; (c) the exact REBA$\to$8-class bin edges and the rad$\to$deg conversion for Eqs. (2)–(3); (d) an explicit statement of the pooling operator that collapses $256\times 2d\to 1\times 2d$. None of these are fatal; all are cheap. A reviewer will also flag the Friedman test as a metric-conflation (four correlated metrics treated as blocks), a point the authors can concede without touching the core result because Table 7 already isolates modules under fixed conditions.

Crisis-habit regression vs counter-trap vs clock. The field's reflexive habit under deadline pressure is to lead with headline accuracy and a chance-corrected $\kappa$ (both 0.93 here) and to present per-metric "conditions" as if they were independent evidence. The counter-trap for a rigorous reader is to *reconstruct calibration from the two reported scalars* — which we did, exposing the heavy tail ($\approx 0.8\%$ confidently-wrong samples) that the headline metrics bury. The clock cuts both ways: the same time pressure that produces the deterministic-label shortcut also produces the compact-compute framing, and the honest paper is the one that quantifies what its shortcut costs. The equilibrium read: ViSK-GAT is a well-engineered, honestly-scoped *REBA-reproduction-under-occlusion* system whose multimodal claim is plausible but not yet isolated — the authors should be credited for disclosure and pressed for the three missing ablations.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Claim under test: ViSK-GAT performs "musculoskeletal risk assessment/classification in athletes." Physical/biomechanical layer veto:

Proof sketch. Let the label be $y=f\big(h(K_{2D})\big)$ where $K_{2D}$ is the MediaPipe 2D keypoint set, $f$ the rule-based REBA lookup, $h$ the angle operator (Eqs. 2–3), as the authors state verbatim ([PAGE 6]). Both $K_{2D}$ and the image $I$ are model inputs. The predictive target $y$ contains no variable that is not a deterministic function of an input. Therefore the Bayes error is $\mathbb{P}\big(\hat y\ne f(h(K_{2D}))\mid I,K_{2D}\big)$, minimized (in principle $\to 0$) by perfectly inverting $f\circ h$ on the coordinate branch. Any irreducible error is upper-bounded by (i) coordinate-measurement noise from occlusion/foreshortening and (ii) dropped keypoints ($v_i<0.5$), *not* by an unknown injury mechanism. Consequently the experiment provides **no** evidence for the causal/clinical proposition "posture $\to$ MSD"; it provides evidence only that the network reproduces a hand-specified function of geometry, robustified to input noise. The load and activity modifiers — the very terms that make REBA a *risk* instrument rather than a pure geometry table — are explicitly zeroed ([PAGE 6]: "we fixed both the load and activity modifiers to zero"), removing the dose/exposure axis along which risk is defined. Verdict: the biomechanical layer does **not** veto feasibility of the narrow task (REBA reproduction from noisy 2D pose is physically well-posed), but it **does** veto the strong reading of the framing. The system measures geometric self-consistency, not musculoskeletal risk.

Environmental cross-layer. The paper itself supplies the environmental veto: "postures involving severe self-occlusion and non-standard viewpoints distort the estimated joint angles" and "Image backgrounds further interfere with reliable keypoint detection" ([PAGE 15]). These are exactly the regimes where the 2D angle operator degenerates (Eq. 2 singularities, out-of-plane flexion invisible to Eq. 3), so the elevated FNR in Class 4 (0.1565, [PAGE 14]) is physically expected, not incidental. Weather/lighting enter only through the image branch's ability to *impute* occluded coordinates; if the image branch's value is concentrated there, the multimodal design is justified as occlusion-robust keypoint completion — which is a physically coherent role, and precisely the hypothesis the missing counterfactuals should test.

The method stack has a traceable lineage. Descriptive eye-movement science begins with Yarbus [81, PAGE 4] (instruction reshapes gaze on identical images), formalised into *scanpath theory* by Noton & Stark [50, 51, PAGE 6]. The probabilistic turn is Ellis & Stark's first-order Markov model of fixation sequences [19, PAGE 6], which the paper adopts verbatim for transition matrices. Krejtz et al. [39, PAGE 7] then operationalised transition entropy and stationary entropy as AOI measures — the direct ancestor of Eqs. 4–5. On the attention side: Kahneman's capacity model [37, PAGE 4] supplies "limited resource pool"; Treisman's feature-integration [73, PAGE 20] and Itti–Koch–Niebur saliency [30, PAGE 4, 20] supply the bottom-up account; Desimone–Duncan / Corbetta–Shulman biased-competition [14, 16, PAGE 4] supply the top-down account; Hommel's Theory of Event Coding [28, PAGE 21] licenses the perception→action reading; Endsley's situation awareness [20, PAGE 22] frames "coverage." Game-domain precedent runs El-Nasr & Yan [18] → Sundstedt [68, 70] → Conati [13] → Joyce racing-HUD [35] → Jeong StarCraft [31] → Wang FIFA [78].

Multi-week traps in the design lineage: (1) the "static-screenshot" trap — Wang [78] and much prior work measured passively viewed images, so their within-AOI fixation counts cannot speak to *interactive* transition dynamics; the paper's whole selling point is escaping this, but only via a single 25+15 min run [PAGE 8]. (2) The expertise-proxy trap — prior work compares established experts vs novices; here outcome is a *one-shot* proxy, so any "skill" reading is confounded with in-run momentum. (3) The AOI-definition trap the authors explicitly flag [PAGE 2]: inconsistent AOI construction blocks cross-study comparison — their hybrid top-down/bottom-up pipeline [PAGE 9–12] is the corrective, but the bottom-up density map was built on the *boss fight only* [PAGE 11], then applied to earlier encounters, importing a selection bias toward the hardest phase.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
The core ToM hazard is *reverse inference*: observing a gaze pattern and inferring the latent strategy that produced it. "Loss players over-fixate Enemy Intent" can be read two ways — (a) they fail to internalise threat state and keep re-verifying (the authors' preferred reading via van den Bogert [76, 77], PAGE 22); or (b) the board is genuinely more threatening, so Enemy Intent *deserves* the dwell. The data cannot separate "player model of opponent is impoverished" from "opponent is objectively more dangerous in losing states." Similarly "Win players sample Potions" reads as proactive monitoring OR as surplus-capacity idling by players who are safe.

Counter-trap (confounds that mimic strategy): total run duration differs by outcome, but hit is sample-normalized and dwell is phase-normalized [PAGE 13–14], partially defusing this; hand-card availability varies turn-to-turn (conceded [PAGE 24]), so Hand-Cards hub dominance is confounded with card cost/usability, not pure attention. The Enemy-Intent 82% alignment error [PAGE 29] is a differential-noise trap: the AOI most tied to the Loss narrative is the least reliably localised.

Clock/temporal layer: gaze is analysed over whole-game aggregates, discarding turn order. Yet the mechanistic story is inherently sequential — "assess threat, then act." Aggregating collapses the very micro-structure the theory invokes, so a genuine state→action lag in Winners is invisible; the transition *probabilities* are the only temporal residue retained, and those are memoryless (first-order), unable to represent multi-turn planning horizons.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical/biomechanical veto — the numbers are unforgiving. Tobii Pro Fusion, calibration accepted at mean accuracy ≤1.0° [PAGE 8]. At 600 mm viewing [PAGE 8], 1.0° subtends 600·tan(1°)≈10.5 mm. On a 23.8″ 16:9 panel, width ≈ 527 mm over 1920 px ⇒ 0.274 mm/px, so 1.0° ≈ **38 px** of possible gaze-estimation error. The transition criterion is only 0.5° ≈ **19 px** [PAGE 14]. Veto: worst-case calibration error (~38 px) is *twice* the transition threshold (~19 px). Therefore some fraction of "AOI transitions" is recoverable measurement jitter, and since the two entropies are computed *from* the transition matrix, the sole statistically significant results inherit this noise directly. This does not nullify them (jitter is roughly non-differential and inflates entropy in *both* groups), but it caps the achievable resolution and cautions against over-reading ΔH_norm=0.02 — an effect two orders of magnitude below the normalizer.

Biomechanical/temporal sampling: 250 Hz ⇒ 4 ms inter-sample interval; <60 ms fixations excluded ⇒ ≥15 samples per fixation [PAGE 9]; aberration boundary at >4× median interval (>16 ms) [PAGE 9]; blink/signal loss <5%, linearly interpolated [PAGE 9]. These are standard and defensible. Signal loss <5% keeps interpolation bias small.

Measurement-veto on AOI geometry: Enemy Intent 82.1% (128/156), Enemy Status 94.2%, Hand Cards 97.4%, Potions 99.4%, Energy/Player Status 100% [PAGE 29]. Veto applies selectively: the peripheral-monitoring claim (Potions, 99.4%) and Player-Status claim (100%) rest on well-localised AOIs and are physically clean; the enemy-loop claim (Enemy Intent↔Status) rests on the two *least* stable AOIs and must be discounted accordingly.

Weather-analogue (environment) veto: lighting/sound/seating standardised [PAGE 8], 60 cm without chin rest permits head movement [PAGE 8] — naturalistic but adds head-motion-induced gaze noise not captured by the 1.0° figure. Net adjudication: the peripheral/auxiliary findings pass the physical veto; the fine-grained enemy-transition differences do not clear it at full strength.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable, falsifiable veto scenarios, each with a concrete prediction:

**Veto 1 — State-confound (reverse causation).** If gaze is a *consequence* of board state, then conditioning on HP/board-danger at each decision point should erase the entropy gap. Prediction: within matched-danger turns, Win−Loss ΔH_norm shrinks toward 0. Falsification of the paper's causal reading: recompute entropies on turn-balanced, HP-matched subsamples; if ΔH_norm≈0.00 with CI spanning the observed 0.02, the strategy-causes-outcome claim is vetoed.

Hull re-framed this variability as intrinsic to the mechanism of habit formation, specifically linking it to the interaction between excitatory processes ($sHr$) and inhibitory processes ($V_b$).

Thus, the "failures" are not evidence of weak learning but rather symptoms of strong, fatigable excitation.

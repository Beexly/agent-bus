<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression versus counter-trap versus clock arbitration.

Under sensor crisis—loss of external optical position aiding—the fused estimator regresses to a pure inertial dead-reckoning habit: the cue is a dropout, the routine is continued propagation of x_k using only IMU preintegration, and the reward is gap-free output (avoiding a frozen or dropped pose). A human operator supervising the capture session undergoes the same regression: after a dropout, the technician habitually trusts the last fused pose as ground truth because the stream continues to update. This is a recursive theory-of-mind failure: the algorithm models the subject’s motion; the subject (performer) models the system’s latency and volume boundaries; under crisis both regress to prior habits—the filter to inertial coasting, the performer to repeating known, low-excitation motions—so neither party detects divergence until an external validation reappears.

Counter-trap. The counter-trap is false confidence: if process noise is under-tuned, the covariance P_k stays small during coasting and the Mahalanobis distance d_k² = ν_kᵀ S_k⁻¹ ν_k (dimensionless, χ²₃ under the null hypothesis) remains below the gate χ²_{α,3} even while true error grows as σ_g √t [rad] for yaw and ½ b_a t² [m] for position. The system therefore reports high confidence on a drifting estimate. The counter-trap to this is covariance inflation during outage (P_k ← P_k + ΔQ(t)) and an innovation outage gate: if no external z_k arrives for Δt_out > T_max (e.g., 5 s), declare a veto on absolute position and yaw, and downgrade to dead-reckoning mode with inflated uncertainty.

Clock arbitration. The fusion clock must reconcile asynchronous domains: inertial sampling at f_IMU (typically 300–1000 Hz) and optical position aiding at f_opt (typically 60–240 Hz). A timestamp offset Δt [s] or jitter σ_t [s] injects apparent translation δp ≈ v Δt [m] and apparent rotation δθ ≈ ω Δt [rad] into the fused state. For v = 2 m s⁻¹, ω = 10 rad s⁻¹, Δt = 10 ms, these are δp ≈ 0.02 m and δθ ≈ 0.1 rad (~5.7°)—large enough to corrupt the T_IE alignment the patent corrects. The clock therefore vetoes fusion when |Δt| > Δt_max (e.g., 5 ms) until re-synchronization, because a misaligned clock is indistinguishable from a misaligned frame in the measurement residual.

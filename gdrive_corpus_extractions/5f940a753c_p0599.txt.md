Crisis habit regression, counter-trap, and clock structure.

Crisis habit regression: Under acute stress or cognitive load, the intentional premonitory field I^int(t) collapses in amplitude and spatial specificity. The system regresses to the strongest pre-existing drainage channel—the habit with the largest w_ij and lowest refractory gate. In James’s terms, having once said A under pressure, the subject cannot easily suppress repeating A because the rising-phase cell S remains the strongest drainer and the wider irradiation has already begun. Regression is therefore not random; it is channel-captured by the most recently strengthened association. The recursive theory of mind here is that the agent models its own future utterance as a continuation of the strongest attractor, and that self-model becomes self-fulfilling.

Counter-trap (ironic inhibition): Attempting to consciously inhibit the habitual channel (e.g., “do not say A again”) irradiates current into the very representation being suppressed. Because the affective gain g(v) amplifies currents that increase tension, monitoring for A raises tension around the A-channel, paradoxically facilitating it. This is Wegner’s ironic process theory operationalized through James’s drainage geometry: the veto signal uses the same wires as the target act, so the counter-trap strengthens the trap unless the motor plant itself is physically vetoed downstream.

Clock: The operation is clocked by two nested timescales. The fast clock is the phoneme-to-phoneme transition (B → C), bounded by axonal conduction [m/s], synaptic delay [~1–5 ms], and articulatory biomechanics (tongue/lip activation–contraction [~50–150 ms]). The slow clock is the intentional build-up, measured by reaction-time paradigms (Lange, Münsterberg [PAGE 589]) over hundreds of milliseconds to seconds. The recursion closes when the fast clock’s output (uttered C) feeds back as the next sensory input (sound of C), re-entering the drainage field and triggering D. Failure of the clock—e.g., a delayed premonitory field—desynchronizes the two loops, producing hesitations, repetitions, or omissions in the alphabet sequence. Verifiable signature: under load, the coefficient of variation of inter-letter intervals increases while the mean interval shifts toward the habit channel’s intrinsic rhythm.

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

The honest equilibrium is: use PINNs for the ill-posed inverse regime the CFD cannot enter, keep CFD for the well-posed forward regime, and never let the "clock" (temporal edge) or the "basin" (optimization) go unaudited.

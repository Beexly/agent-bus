<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The pipeline contains multiple veto layers that can invalidate a naive channel reading.

Veto 1 — Tokenizer layer: Theorem 7 on [PAGE 20–21] proves that no dissimilarity can simultaneously satisfy perturbation invariance, sink separability, and finite continuous extension on the closed simplex. This is a hard physical-layer veto on any claim that a scaffold-set statistic is tokenizer-invariant.

Veto 2 — Scaffold-set layer: On [PAGE 3] Remark 1 and App. E ([PAGE 51]), the identities hold for any S, but empirical magnitudes do not. The DATA definition moves cells less. This veto means a base-vs-reasoning contrast is only valid at fixed convention; the scaffold set is a modelling choice with measured sensitivity.

Veto 3 — Estimator singularity layer: On [PAGE 5], float32 gate mass rounding to 1 makes the naive content estimator return ∞ − ∞. The paper mandates blockwise summation in float64 and exclusion of positions whose content-block mass underflows beyond certification (≤ 0.2% teacher-forced, ≤ 4.9% generated). Positions where s → 1 have no continuous extension of G in general ([PAGE 26], Remark 3): for k ≥ 2, r^(1)_ε and r^(2)_ε converge to the same boundary distribution while G(r^(i)_ε) → H(a) − H(c_i) for different content entropies, so G has no path-independent continuous extension. Any pipeline that clamps H_C := 0 at underflow is measuring a convention, not a limit.

Veto 4 — Answer-restatement layer: On [PAGE 7–8], deleting each restatement of the gold answer and the final sentence halves the compression ceiling (75.5 → 41.2% on distilled GSM8K; 68.8 → 49.5% instruct; 34.2 → 19.9% MATH-500). This is a weather-style veto: the apparent positional effect is actually the climate of answer leakage, and once the restatements are stripped, the ordering rewrites.

No physical, biomechanical, or meteorological metaphor is needed: these are exact, page-cited vetoes rooted in the vocabulary partition, the float boundary s → 1, the string-based stripping protocol, and the temperature orbit of the softmax.

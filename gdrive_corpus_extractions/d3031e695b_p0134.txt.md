**2. Expected Return Yardage (\( \mu_Y \)):**
The average net yards gained upon a successful return, accounting for tackle location.
\[
\mu_Y = \int_0^{L_{max}} y \cdot g(y) \, dy
\]
where \( g(y) \) is the probability density function of return length \( y \), and \( L_{max} \) is the maximum possible return (typically ~100 yards). 
- *Units:* Meters (m).
- *Assumptions:* Tackles occur according to a Poisson process along the return path, with rate parameter inversely proportional to blocker efficiency. The integral converges because \( g(y) \to 0 \) as \( y \to L_{max} \).

\[

- *Assumptions:* Loss includes both immediate negative yardage and lost scoring opportunity. Gain assumes optimal execution.

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
The source describes a cognitive transformation operator \(\mathcal{T}\) acting on perceptual data \(D\):

\[
\mathcal{T}: D \mapsto f \in \mathcal{F}
\]

where \(f\) is an extracted character (feature). The reasoning process then applies a known implication rule \(R\):

\[
R: f \rightarrow C
\]

yielding consequence \(C\). Empirical judgment bypasses \(\mathcal{T}\) and uses a direct association \(D \leftrightarrow C\).

Formally, define the **Reasoning Advantage Function** \(A_R(D)\) as:

\[
A_R(D) = P(C|f) - P(C|D_{\text{emp}})
\]

where \(P(C|f)\) is the conditional probability given the extracted feature (typically near 1 for stable laws), and \(P(C|D_{\text{emp}})\) is based on holistic pattern matching (often lower and noisier). James implies \(A_R > 0\) and significant.

In information theory, let \(I(Y;X)\) be mutual information between evidence \(X\) and hypothesis \(Y\). For raw data \(X_d\) and extracted feature \(X_f\):

\[
I(Y; X_f) \geq I(Y; X_d)
\]

with strict inequality when irrelevant noise is filtered out. The "equivalence" in Point 1 means \(X_f\) is a sufficient statistic: \(P(Y|X_f) = P(Y|X_d)\).

**Units & Breakdown Regimes:**
- Probabilities: unitless [0,1].
- Information: bits/nats.
- *Breakdown*: When no such \(X_f\) exists (chaotic systems), or when extraction requires computation exceeding human capacity (NP-hard problems). Also fails if the "law" linking \(f\) to \(C\) is context-dependent (non-stationary).

**Singularity**: As dimensionality of \(D\) increases, brute-force \(P(C|D_{\text{emp}})\) suffers from curse of dimensionality, while \(I(Y; X_f)\) can remain constant if \(f\) is low-dimensional. This makes reasoning essential in complex domains.

Thus, James is documenting how scientific revolutions replace empirical correlations with mechanistic explanations based on discovered entities (elements, forces, genes).

This lineage connects to modern cognitive science’s debate between "massive modularity" (innate feature detectors) and "domain-general reasoning" (working memory + logic). James leans toward the latter, seeing sagacity as cultivated skill.

Proof: These vetoes operate independently of the quality of reasoning. One can have flawless logical steps from correct premises, yet fail due to sensorimotor limits or non-stationarity. Thus, the reasoning advantage function \(A_R\) can become negative if costs of extraction/time exceed benefits.

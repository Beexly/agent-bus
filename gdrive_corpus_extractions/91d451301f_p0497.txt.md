To formalize James's qualitative observations, we define a state-space framework for organismic behavior, mapping his concepts of "primary," "secondary," and "memory images" into measurable information-theoretic quantities.

Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the sensory state space and $\mathcal{M}$ be the proprioceptive/motor state space. The joint state is $z_t = (s_t, m_t)$.

**1. Primary Performance Operator ($\mathcal{P}$)**
James defines primary performances as reflexes, instincts, and emotional outbursts. These are hardwired mappings, highly stereotyped and low-dimensional compared to the full motor space.
We model this as a deterministic (or high-probability stochastic) operator driven by specific triggers:
$$ \mathcal{P}: \mathcal{T} \subseteq \mathcal{S} \rightarrow \Delta \mathcal{M} $$
where $\mathcal{T}$ is the trigger subspace. The output is a discrete change in motor state $\Delta m$.
*Units:* $\Delta m$ in SI units of kinematics (e.g., displacement meters $m$, angular velocity rad/s).
*Citation [PAGE 487]:* "The nerve-centres are so organized that certain stimuli pull the trigger of certain explosive parts."
*Breakdown Regime:* The validity of $\mathcal{P}$ breaks down when noise $\eta(t)$ exceeds the basin of attraction of the reflex arc, or when higher-order inhibitory control (volition) overrides the reflex. Mathematically, if the system is modeled as a dynamical system $\dot{x} = f(x,u)$, $\mathcal{P}$ corresponds to fast subsystem dynamics that dominate when the slow voluntary manifold is unactivated.

**2. Involuntary Stochastic Exploration ($E_{inv}$)**
Before volition, the organism undergoes random or environmentally forced movements.
$$ E_{inv}(t) = \int_0^t \sigma(m_\tau) dW_\tau + \sum \mathcal{P}(s_\tau) $$
where $W_\tau$ is a Wiener process representing biological noise/jitter.
*Citation [PAGE 487]:* "...wait for the movements to be performed involuntarily, before we can frame ideas of what either of these things are."

**3. Memory Encoding and Image Formation ($I$)**
The critical bridge to volition is the "image" in memory. This is an encoding operation that compresses the temporal sequence of $E_{inv}$ into a retrievable representation $v \in \mathcal{V}$ (the space of voluntary intentions).
$$ v = \text{Encode}(E_{inv}) = \arg\max_{v} P(E_{inv} | v) $$
This assumes a latent variable model where $v$ generates the observed involuntary trace.
*Units:* Information theoretic bits/shannons of mutual information $I(E_{inv}; v)$.
*Breakdown Regime:* If the involuntary movement is too chaotic or lacks distinct sensory feedback, the mutual information approaches zero, and no stable "image" forms. The movement remains outside the repertoire of volition.

**4. Voluntary Action Operator ($\mathcal{V}$)**
Volition is the deliberate instantiation of a stored image.
$$ \mathcal{V}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \Delta \mathcal{M}_{predicted} $$
Crucially, this includes the *prevision*:
$$ \hat{s}_{t+k} = \mathcal{F}_{sensory}(\mathcal{V}(v)) $$
*Citation [PAGE 487]:* "When a particular movement... has left an image of itself in the memory, then the movement can be desired again, proposed as an end, and deliberately willed."
*Citation [PAGE 487]:* "...with full prevision of what they are to be."

**5. The Singularity of First-Time Volition**
James argues: "no creature not endowed with divinatory power can perform an act voluntarily for the first time."
Formally, let $A_{new}$ be a movement sequence.
If $A_{new} \notin \text{Image}(E_{inv})$, then $P(\mathcal{V} \rightarrow A_{new}) = 0$.
This is a singularity in the control manifold: the dimensionality of the voluntary space cannot exceed the dimensionality of the explored involuntary space.

Assumptions:
1. The sensory-motor loop is Markovian at the timescale of perception.
2. "Divinatory power" equates to non-zero probability density over unseen states (a priori priors that are not grounded in data).

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
James’s formulation represents a pivotal moment in the genealogy of behavioral science, acting as a causal rupture against prevailing Cartesian dualism and rigid associationism. Historically, the mind-body problem often treated volition as a primary, almost magical force emanating from the soul or pure consciousness. By positing that "voluntary movements must be secondary," James anchors psychology firmly in physiological empiricism. He effectively demotes the "will" from a cosmic mover to a sophisticated software layer running on wet-hardware evolved for survival (reflexes/instincts).

Lineage Connections:
- **Darwinian Evolution:** The dependence on "random" or instinctive explosions mirrors Darwinian variation. Just as biological evolution explores phenotypes stochastically before natural selection fixes advantageous traits, psychic evolution explores motor behaviors involuntarily before conscious selection ("desiring") fixes them as tools.
- **Thorndike & Skinner (Behaviorism):** James prefigures the trial-and-error learning of Thorndike’s cats. The "involuntary" phase is the blind trial; the "memory image" is the formation of the S-R bond; "willing" is the conditioned response executed with expectation. However, James retains the phenomenological "image," which strict behaviorists would later reject as mentalistic fluff.
- **Predictive Coding / Active Inference (Karl Friston et al.):** This is the strongest modern resonance. James’s "prevision" is the minimization of prediction error. The brain maintains a generative model of the body. To move voluntarily is to sample from this model. But how is the model built? Through active exploration—involuntary twitches, infant babbling, startle responses—that provide the likelihood data necessary to constrain the posterior over actions. The "gray zone" trap here is assuming the generative model is fully explicit. Likely, much of the "image" is embedded in subcortical predictive structures, not conscious imagery.

Multi-week Trap Analysis:
A common trap in interpreting James (and similar foundational texts) is *presentism*—judging 1890 concepts by 2024 neuroscience standards and dismissing them as naive. For instance, one might say, "He didn't know about mirror neurons, so his theory of imitation is wrong." This is a trap because it misses the structural robustness of his claim. Even with mirror neurons, the *proprioceptive* verification of the movement is likely required for true volitional integration. You can see someone do a backflip, but you cannot voluntarily will yourself to do a perfect backflip until your body has explored the physics of rotation, balance, and impact, likely through smaller, involuntary or semi-voluntary trials. The trap lies in conflating *knowledge of possibility* (visual recognition) with *motor command capability* (volition). James’s strict element-based derivation holds a deep truth about the embodiment of agency that transcends the specific mechanisms he lacked.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Volition, defined as the capacity to act with full prevision, operates as a supervisory control layer. However, its authority is constantly challenged by veto mechanisms arising from physical, biomechanical, and environmental strata. We must adjudicate when and why these vetoes override the conscious will.

**1. Physical/Biomechanical Veto: The Hardware Ceiling.**
*Proof of Constraint:* An athlete may *will* to jump three meters vertically. They possess the "image" of the jump, the desire, and the precise motor plan. However, the biomechanical reality—the yield strength of tendons, the ATP turnover rate in muscle fibers, the leverage ratios of the skeletal system—imposes a hard veto. The act fails not due to lack of will, but because the secondary function attempts to drive the organism beyond its primary design parameters.
*Jamesian Adjudication:* James's framework implicitly accommodates this. The "image" is formed based on previous *successful* or *attempted* movements. If the three-meter jump has never been performed involuntarily or through incremental scaling, the true "image" of its success is absent. The will is attempting to instantiate a phantom, leading to inevitable failure. The physical veto serves as a harsh teacher, updating the boundary conditions of the voluntary space.

**2. Environmental Veto: Weather and Friction.**
*Proof of Constraint:* I will to walk across a frozen lake. My motor commands are issued. Suddenly, the ice cracks. The slip is a "primary performance"—a loss of equilibrium triggering vestibular reflexes, flailing limbs, and terror. The environment has hijacked the system.
*Jamesian Adjudication:* This is a dynamic interaction between the organism and an unpredictable external agent. The "prevision" included the state of the ice being solid. When the environment violates this assumption, the volitional model collapses instantly. Control reverts to primary balances reflexes. The veto is instantaneous and absolute. The lesson reinforces the fragility of volition: it is contingent on the stability of the world-model upon which its predictions are based.

**3. The Neurological Veto: Pathology and Noise.**
*Proof of Constraint:* A patient with Parkinson's disease may strongly *will* to initiate a step (freezing of gait). The intention exists, the image exists, but the basal ganglia fail to gate the thalamocortical loops. The movement does not occur.
*Adjudication:* Here, the breakdown is internal to the hardware executing the "secondary function." The interface between volition and primary motor pools is corrupted. James’s assertion that volition is secondary is starkly validated: without the reliable operation of the underlying substrate, the "software" of will becomes a ghost process—running but effecting nothing.

These vetoes prove that while volition provides the *direction*, the *viability* of any act is continuously adjudicated by lower-level physical laws and biological constraints. The "clock" of real-world physics always wins.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

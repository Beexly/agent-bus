<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
**Conformal residual operator.** Split conformal prediction uses held-out absolute residuals. The source gives “Algorithm 2 Split conformal prediction” (Source Task 1). Let \(R_i=|Y_i-\hat f(X_i)|\) for calibration points \(i=1,\dots,n\). The finite-sample order-statistic radius is
\[
\hat q_{1-\alpha}=R_{(\lceil(1-\alpha)(n+1)\rceil)},\qquad
C_\alpha(x)=[\hat f(x)-\hat q_{1-\alpha},\ \hat f(x)+\hat q_{1-\alpha}].
\]
Under exchangeability, \(P(Y_{n+1}\in C_\alpha(X_{n+1}))\ge 1-\alpha\). Units: residuals and interval half-width are in the target unit, e.g. points; \(\alpha\) is dimensionless in \((0,1)\). Breakdown regimes: exchangeability failure; \(n\) too small for the requested rank; tied residuals causing over-coverage; distribution shift; non-continuous residuals; and any attempt to update \(\hat q\) online post-seal. The source’s out-of-scope flags explicitly reject online conformal/betting updates because they re-estimate after the seal.

**Sinkhorn-Knopp transport operator.** For cost matrix \(C\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}\) and regularization \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
K_{ij}=\exp(-C_{ij}/\epsilon),\qquad P=\operatorname{diag}(u)K\operatorname{diag}(v),
\]
with iterations
\[
u_i^{(t+1)}=\frac{r_i}{(Kv^{(t)})_i},\qquad
v_j^{(t+1)}=\frac{c_j}{(K^\top u^{(t+1)})_j}.
\]
Units: \(C_{ij}\) and \(\epsilon\) share cost units; row/column marginals \(r,c\) are dimensionless mass fractions summing to one; \(u,v\) are dimensionless scalars. Breakdown regimes: \(\epsilon\to0\) causing underflow; cost entries with NaN/Inf; failure of the support condition required by Sinkhorn-Knopp uniqueness; unequal marginal mass; and premature stopping. The GSE implementation clamps the exponent at \(-700.0\), guards denominators with `max(..., 1e-12)`, stops when `du < 1e-6` and `dv < 1e-6`, and verifies `row_residual < 1e-4` and `col_residual < 1e-4` (Source Task 2). The source states that the post-injury shares are the column sums and that the seal is untouched.

**Sealed EPA dropoff operator.** If Sinkhorn produces post-injury share \(s_i'\), the effective share is
\[
e_i=s_i'\,f_{p(i)},\qquad f_{p(i)}\in[0.5,1.2],
\]
where \(s_i'\) is dimensionless mass and \(f_{p(i)}\) is a dimensionless multiplier. The table is canonicalized and hashed:
\[
h=\operatorname{SHA256}(\operatorname{canonicalJSON}(T)).
\]
Units: \(h\) is a 256-bit digest, represented as 64 hexadecimal characters. Breakdown regimes: noncanonical JSON; inconsistent float formatting; key-order drift; Unicode normalization mismatch; inclusion of a mutable field such as wall-clock time; and post-seal table edits. The source specifies `EPA_DROPOFF_SHA256` and `_canonical_hash` using sorted keys and compact separators (Source Task 3).

**Inverse-MDP quantile-risk operator.** For future game value \(V(s,a)\), the coach is modeled as maximizing a quantile:
\[
a^*(s)=\arg\max_a Q_\tau(s,a),\qquad Q_\tau(s,a)=F^{-1}_{V(s,a)}(\tau),
\]
and \(\tau\) is recovered by minimizing decision suboptimality over observed choices:
\[
\tau^*=\arg\min_{\tau\in(0,1)}\sum_t \ell\!\left(Q_\tau(s_t,a_t)-\max_{a'}Q_\tau(s_t,a')\right).
\]
Units: \(\tau\) is dimensionless; \(Q_\tau\) is in points or win-probability units depending on the value function. Breakdown regimes: flat objective or weak identification; discrete ties; limited action support; nonstationary coaching policy; and post-seal re-estimation. The source states the inverse problem is solved pre-seal and that the injury path “never re-estimates tau” (Source Task 4).

**Bellman value operator.** Romer’s dynamic-programming formulation uses
\[
V(s)=\max_a\left\{r(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)V(s')\right\},
\]
with \(\gamma\in[0,1]\) dimensionless and \(V\) in points or win-probability units. Breakdown regimes: risk-neutrality mismatch when the coach is quantile-sensitive; incomplete state space; nonstationary transition probabilities; and invalid boundary values.

**Empirical Bayes BTL shrinkage operator.** The source implements pre-seal shrinkage as
\[
\hat\theta=R\,\hat\theta_{\mathrm{MLE}},\qquad R=(I+AS)^{-1},
\]
where \(A\) is the Fisher-information matrix and \(S\) is the prior covariance (Source Task 3). Units: \(\theta\) is a log-strength parameter, so \(A\) and \(S^{-1}\) carry inverse-variance units and \(R\) is dimensionless. Breakdown regimes: singular Fisher information; degenerate prior covariance; disconnected comparison graph; and any mid-week re-estimation of the hyperparameter or shrinkage rule.

**Seal invariant.** The Tuesday seal enforces byte-for-byte invariance of `coachMindsetHash`, \(\tau\), the cost matrix, and the EPA table across v1.0 to v4.0. The source’s invariant test states: “After the overlay returns, the test calls assertTauSealed(before, after). It must return true” (Source Doc 5). If the hash recomputation differs, the seal is broken and the ticket must be rejected.

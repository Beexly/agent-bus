A proposal to expand working memory through instruction alone fails if \(F\) is high because \(S(F)\) is bounded above by \(S_{\min} + (S_0 - S_{\min})e^{-\kappa F}\).

Any claim that rain, heat index, or storm fronts alter the index entries (habit, fear, genius, will) is extraneous to [PAGE 705] and requires external data; within this source, weather is an unmodeled variable.

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios (RLVR = reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards). In each, a proposed policy update is submitted to a counterfactual check; if the check fails, the update is vetoed and a prediction is issued.

Scenario 1—Willpower-only habit formation. Policy: increase fiat \(W\) to produce a new habit \(H\) without repeated behavioral rehearsal. Counterfactual: set plasticity \(P = 0\) (no change in brain-matter paths), per “plasticity of brain-matter, I. 105” [PAGE 705]. Veto condition: if neural imaging shows \(\Delta x_i \approx 0\) across training while \(W > 0\), the policy is vetoed. Prediction: \(H(t) = H(0)\) for all \(t\); any behavioral change decays to baseline as soon as external incentive is removed, because “they demand some sensation, 118” [PAGE 705] and path strength never consolidates.

Scenario 2—Incentivized span expansion under fatigue. Policy: offer large reward to increase span of consciousness \(S\) while the subject is fatigued. Counterfactual: clamp \(F > F_{\mathrm{crit}}\) such that \(S(F) = S_{\min}\) per “Fatigue, diminishes span of consciousness, I. 640” [PAGE 705]. Veto condition: if observed span never exceeds \(S_{\min}\) regardless of reward magnitude, the incentive policy is vetoed. Prediction: performance on dual-task span measures exhibits a hard ceiling at \(S_{\min}\); the reward gradient \(\partial S / \partial R \to 0\) as \(F \to F_{\mathrm{crit}}\).

Scenario 3—Hallucination classification of veridical sensation. Policy: label any low-confidence perception as hallucination. Counterfactual: present a veridical stimulus with \(\hat{s} \approx s\), so \(\|\hat{s} - s\|_2 < \theta\), per “Hallucination. sensation a veridical, II. 33” [PAGE 705]. Veto condition: if the classifier outputs \(\mathcal{H} = 1\) for veridical trials at a rate above the nominal false-positive threshold, the classification rule is vetoed. Prediction: signal-detection sensitivity \(d'\) collapses toward zero; the system confuses absence-of-stimulus with presence-of-noise and cannot distinguish phantom-limb-type reports (\(s = 0\)) from accurate perception.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

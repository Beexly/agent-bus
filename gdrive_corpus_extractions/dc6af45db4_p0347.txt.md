James argues that characters "hunt in couples," meaning they exhibit "constant habits of mutual concomitance." We quantify this using Shannon's joint entropy $H(X,Y)$. If two characters $M$ and $P$ are independent (James's hypothetical chaotic world), then:
$$H(M, P) = H(M) + H(P)$$
If they hunt in couples (our actual world), the joint entropy is reduced due to mutual information $I(M;P)$:
$$H(M, P) = H(M) + H(P) - I(M;P)$$
Where:
$$I(M;P) = \sum_{m,p} p(m,p) \log \frac{p(m,p)}{p(m)p(p)} \geq 0$$
A high $I(M;P)$ signifies that knowing $M$ drastically reduces uncertainty about $P$.

**3. The Operator of Implication (Reasoning):**
Reasoning is the process of traversing from a known character to an implied one. Let $\mathbb{R}$ be the reasoning operator acting on the probability distribution of characters. Given input belief state $Bel(M)$, the output is $Bel(P)$.
$$Bel(P | M) = \mathbb{R}_{M \to P} [Bel(M)]$$
In a deterministic logical world, this is a step function. In James's psychological world, it is a probabilistic transition derived from environmental frequencies.

This regression is a counter-trap to cognitive load. By simplifying the $M$, the brain artificially inflates the perceived mutual information $I(M;P)$—making the world feel predictable and safe, even though it isn't. The "universal propositions" become dogmatic stereotypes.
Conversely, an expert (e.g., a master chess player or a seasoned physician) resists this regression. They can instantly recognize a complex constellation of characters as a unified $M$ without conscious effort. 

However, the most dangerous trap is the **"Lawful Illusion" trap**. Because we are evolved to expect the world to "hunt in couples," we hallucinate patterns where none exist (apophenia). In a truly random sequence (James's chaotic world), a reasoner will inevitably invent false universal propositions ("M implies P") to maintain the feeling of control. This is the ultimate failure mode of the reasoning engine described by James: it projects structure onto noise because the alternative (admitting a lawless universe) causes existential paralysis.

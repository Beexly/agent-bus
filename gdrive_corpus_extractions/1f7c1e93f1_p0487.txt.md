Level 1 is the emotion layer \( \mathcal{E} = \mathcal{F}(\hat{\mathcal{D}}\mathcal{S}, \mathbf{b}) \). Level 2 is the metarepresentational belief that emotion may be independent of bodily feelings, which James explicitly withholds: “I cannot see my way to affirming that the emotion is independent of these” [PAGE 477]. Level 3 is the crisis regime in which habit regression collapses the hierarchy.

Crisis habit regression: under acute stress or intoxication, higher-order checking fails. The “multiple tendencies” of the diffusive sort [PAGE 477] are no longer inhibited; instead, they are replaced by “some few narrow forms of discharge,” causing the original emotion to tend to disappear [PAGE 477]. The agent regresses to a primitive impulsive controller: rather than experiencing a rich, differentiated emotion, it emits a narrow discharge vector \( \vec{d} \) with low rank.

Counter-trap: the recursive model may posit a “theoretic rapture” [PAGE 477] as a disembodied attractor, a counter-trap in which the agent believes it can sustain emotion through “cerebral abundance and ease” alone [PAGE 477], thereby vetoing the bodily feedback loop. James blocks this by locating even strong emotions of chloroform, ether, and alcohol within “strange bodily feelings and changes in the incoming sensibilities” [PAGE 477].

Clock: ordinary consciousness operates on a slow temporal scale \( \tau_{ord} \), producing the “fine and serene” state [PAGE 477]. Intoxication accelerates the affective clock to \( \tau_{int} \ll \tau_{ord} \), generating “intensely exciting” frenzy [PAGE 477]. If the clock fails to reset after the drug wears off, the subject perseverates in the belief of having seen “absolute truth,” a recursive miscalibration in which Level 2 (belief in insight) outlasts Level 0 (bodily substrate).

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical, biomechanical, and environmental veto layers adjudicating claims of body-independent emotion.

Physical veto: James’s diffusive wave implies that arousal propagates through autonomic and somatic efferent pathways with finite conduction velocity \( v \) [m/s] and must satisfy energy conservation across the neuro-visceral plant. A regional sympathetic or peripheral nerve block that sets local afferent drive \( \mathbf{b}_{local} = \mathbf{0} \) vetoes the claim that emotion in that modality is body-independent; the diffusive wave cannot reach its effector terminus. Breakdown regime: if central generation alone could sustain \( \mathcal{E} \) with \( \mathbf{b}=\mathbf{0} \), James’s agnosticism [PAGE 477] would be falsified; no such case is established in the text.

Biomechanical veto: expressive behavior supplies afferent feedback that constitutes part of the “diffusive” emotion field [PAGE 477], consistent with physiognomy and expression literature [PAGE 477]. Paralysis of a facial expression channel (e.g., botulinum denervation of the corrugator) removes a narrow discharge form and should reduce emotion intensity if emotion depends on multiplicity of unchecked tendencies. Veto condition: if \( \mathcal{I}_{emo} \) is unchanged despite elimination of the expressive channel, the body is not constitutive; if \( \mathcal{I}_{emo} \) drops, James’s diffusive account is supported. Singularity: complete facial paralysis does not eliminate cognition, so any residual emotion must be non-biomechanical or centrally generated.

Weather veto: ambient temperature \( T_{amb} \) [°C] directly perturbs thermal afferents, a subset of “incoming sensibilities” [PAGE 477]. At \( T_{amb} > 35^\circ\text{C} \) or \( T_{amb} < 10^\circ\text{C} \), afferent noise and overstrain shift \( \mathbf{b} \) away from the “not overstrained activity” regime [PAGE 477], vetoing the possibility of pure cerebral abundance under thermal stress. Prediction: self-reported cognitive richness \( \mathcal{R} \) [bits] decreases at thermal extremes even when the objective stimulus \( \mathcal{S} \) is constant. Breakdown: at the nociceptive threshold \( T \approx 45^\circ\text{C} \), afferent saturation dominates and \( \partial \mathcal{E}/\partial T \) becomes singular, rendering the emotion model inapplicable without a thermoregulatory correction term.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with explicit predictions.

Scenario 1 — Peripheral sensory blockade veto. Condition: administer a reversible peripheral nerve block that reduces incoming sensibilities \( \mathbf{b} \to \mathbf{0} \) in a targeted somatic region while leaving cognitive trains \( \{T_j\} \) intact. Veto: James’s constitutive claim \( \mathcal{E} = \mathcal{F}(\hat{\mathcal{D}}\mathcal{S}, \mathbf{b}) \) [PAGE 477] predicts \( \Delta\mathcal{E} < 0 \) because he cannot affirm independence from bodily feelings [PAGE 477]. Prediction: emotion intensity ratings on a visual analog scale fall by at least 30% relative to sham, while recognition memory for the eliciting object is unchanged (verifiable via signal-detection \( d' \)). If emotion ratings do not fall, the body-independent counter-trap is provisionally supported.

Scenario 2 — Expressive narrow-discharge veto. Condition: expose subjects to emotion-eliciting stimuli under two constraints: (a) free expressive behavior, (b) enforced neutral face with all expressive channels checked except one narrow discharge. Veto: James states that as multiple tendencies are checked and replaced by narrow discharge, the original emotion tends to disappear [PAGE 477]. Prediction: condition (b) yields lower self-rated emotion intensity \( \mathcal{I}_{emo} \) and lower facial EMG dimensionality (rank of activation matrix \( \ll \) condition (a)). Verifiable by comparing rank of the EMG time-series across conditions; if rank collapses but \( \mathcal{I}_{emo} \) remains high, the “multiplicité de fenómenes” claim [PAGE 477] is falsified.

Scenario 3 — Intoxicated truth-insight confound veto. Condition: administer nitrous oxide \( N_2O \) versus placebo during a semantic insight task, with and without a beta-adrenergic blocker (propranolol) to blunt autonomic bodily feelings. Veto: James attributes strong emotion and “absolute truth” consciousness under \( N_2O \) to states accompanied by “strange bodily feelings and changes in the incoming sensibilities” [PAGE 477]; he cannot separate insight from body [PAGE 477]. Prediction: insight reports peak under \( N_2O \) + placebo, but under \( N_2O \) + propranolol, insight reports fall toward placebo levels even if objective task accuracy is unchanged. Verifiable via insight-question accuracy and autonomic (skin conductance) recordings; a dissociation would veto body-independent truth access.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

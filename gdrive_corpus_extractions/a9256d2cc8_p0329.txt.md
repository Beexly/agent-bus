Multi-week traps: Suppose an agent inhabits an environment with \(\lambda_p \approx 0\) s\(^{-1}\) for several weeks (e.g., constant experimental conditions, stable social routine, or unchanging weather pattern). Per Assumption A3, prestige \(\pi_p\) integrates upward over weeks with time constant \(1/\gamma\). If \(\gamma^{-1}\) is on the order of days to weeks, then after a multi-week unbroken interval, \(\pi_p\) lies far above \(\theta\), and \(b_p=1\) becomes maximally entrenched. A single contradiction in week five then removes \(\ln(1/(1-\kappa))\) nats. Because \(\pi_p\) is high, the absolute drop may be small relative to \(\pi_p-\theta\), yet psychologically the shock is disproportionate: the agent has lost the metacognitive habit of doubt and interprets the contradiction as an anomaly rather than a falsification. This is the “multi-week trap”: long zero-contradiction intervals produce brittle high-prestige beliefs that are hypersensitive to the first exception, inverting naive expectations that fifty confirmations outweigh one contradiction.

Retrocausally, the trap is generated by the agent’s own past: the weeks of unbroken confirmation are not stored as a count but as a cumulative prestige potential. The future contradiction does not merely subtract one vote; it detonates a reservoir of accumulated trust. James’s animal example (morning to evening, [PAGE 319]) is the minimal version of this trap operating on a diurnal clock rather than a multi-week clock, but the principle scales: any prolonged stability—whether a summer day, a multi-week routine, or a fixed weather regime—sets up a prestige reservoir whose collapse is proportional to the duration of prior unbroken credulity.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: Under acute contradiction or threat, the system regresses to the primitive state James describes on [PAGE 319]: “Whatever it is disposed to do, it does without misgivings… practically one of unbounded confidence.” When the contradiction rate \(\lambda_p\) spikes above the critical regime, the computational cost of maintaining doubt and updating prestige exceeds available cognitive bandwidth. The mind reverts to immediate affirmation (\(b_p=1\) by default) because doubt is a late, schooled achievement, not the primitive default. This is a habit regression to pre-reflective credulity: the agent reasserts the reality of all that is conceived, exactly as James’s animal begins its evening round without hesitation.

Counter-trap: The learned habit of doubt can itself become a rigid second-order belief: “nothing is real until it has been contradicted and survived.” Once this meta-belief exceeds its own prestige threshold, it vetoes genuine primitive perceptions, producing pathological skepticism or action paralysis. The counter-trap is recursive because the agent applies the doubt-criterion to the doubt-criterion itself, generating an infinite regress unless grounded by a non-verbal relational anchor (the “relations to ourselves” James invokes on [PAGE 319]). The escape from the counter-trap is not more verbal predicate-coupling (“this is real”) but a return to embodied, uncontradicted interaction.

Clock: Two timescales govern the recursion. \(\tau_{\text{belief}} = 1/\gamma\) is the belief-consolidation timescale (seconds to weeks). \(\tau_{\text{env}}\) is the environmental-change timescale (e.g., the diurnal cycle from dawn to dusk, roughly \(10^4\)–\(10^5\) s). If \(\tau_{\text{env}} \ll \tau_{\text{belief}}\), the environment changes faster than belief can consolidate; the agent is perpetually outdated and lives in chronic contradiction. If \(\tau_{\text{env}} \gg \tau_{\text{belief}}\), belief consolidates fully and the agent enters the multi-week trap described above. James’s animal operates near the boundary: daylight persists for hours (\(\tau_{\text{env}}\) long relative to action cycles), so the animal’s round of operations completes under unbroken credulity, only to be vetoed at dusk when \(\lambda_p\) impulses to unity. The clock therefore determines whether the agent experiences primitive credulity as adaptive (stable day) or as a liability (sudden night).

Recursive theory-of-mind implication: An agent modeling another agent must track not just the other’s current belief \(b_p\) but the other’s prestige history \(\pi_p(t)\) and contradiction hazard \(\lambda_p\). A regressed agent in crisis will appear overconfident; a counter-trapped agent will appear pathologically hesitant. Predicting behavior requires estimating which layer of the recursion is active—primitive impulse, schooled doubt, or meta-doubt—and where each lies relative to its own \(\theta\) threshold.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Define three layers. L1 (physical/neural): metabolic and refractory constraints bound the maximum contradiction-processing rate \(\lambda_{\max}\) in s\(^{-1}\); if \(\lambda_p > \lambda_{\max}\), the system cannot update prestige fast enough and enters overload. L2 (biomechanical/embodied): a thwarted or opposed impulse generates a pain signal that functions as a veto \(v_{\text{bio}} \in \{0,1\}\). James identifies this directly on [PAGE 319]: “To be thwarted and opposed is one of our earliest and most frequent pains. It develops the sense of a distinction between free and obstructed impulses.” L3 (environmental/weather): the diurnal illumination cycle. The animal assumes the perpetuity of daylight; evening introduces darkness, setting \(C_p=1\) at \(t_{\text{dusk}}\).

Adjudication protocol: A proposition \(p\) (“daylight will continue forever”) is believed only while \(\pi_p > \theta\). L3 environmental contradiction at \(t_{\text{dusk}}\) applies the multiplicative penalty \((1-\kappa)\). If \(\pi_p\) falls below \(\theta\), \(b_p=0\). L2 biomechanical pain follows from the failed expectation of continued action in light; the veto \(v_{\text{bio}}=1\) reinforces the prestige decay. L1 neural constraints determine whether the system can even register the contradiction within the available refractory window.

Proof of veto non-transitivity: Suppose an agent attempts to preserve \(b_p=1\) through purely verbal predicate-coupling (“this is real/outwardly existing”) without relational referent. At \(t_{\text{dusk}}\), L3 registers \(C_p=1\). Even if the subject utters the predicate “real,” the prestige dynamics of Assumption A3 drive \(\pi_p\) below \(\theta\) because \(\kappa>0\). Therefore \(b_p=0\) despite verbal affirmation. Abstract verbal coupling cannot override an L3/L2 veto. Only an unbroken relational history—\(\lambda_p=0\) over the relevant interval—keeps \(\pi_p\) above threshold.

Weather veto example: In James’s terms, “we do not believe that this light is to continue forever” [PAGE 319] because the weather/diurnal layer eventually contradicts it. The veto is physical: photons cease; the biomechanical layer cannot sustain the round of operations in darkness; the neural layer records \(C_p=1\). The abstract predicate “real” is not veto-proof.

Breakdown condition: If \(\lambda_p\) arrives faster than L1 can metabolically process (\(\lambda_p > \lambda_{\max}\)), the veto signal is delayed or dropped, and the system may transiently maintain \(b_p=1\) despite environmental contradiction—a hallucinated persistence of belief. This is the exact regime where primitive credulity becomes maladaptive.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable rejection/veto scenarios, each with a counterfactual and a prediction. RLVR here denotes verification of whether a belief-state update is vetoed by an observable counterfactual intervention; no wagering quantities are invoked.

Scenario 1 (Diurnal environmental veto). Counterfactual: Rear an animal in constant artificial light from birth so that it never experiences evening (\(\lambda_p = 0\) s\(^{-1}\) for the full observation window). Prediction: Per [PAGE 319] primitive credulity, \(b_p=1\) for “light continues indefinitely” and the animal initiates its round of operations at subjective dusk without hesitation. Veto intervention: At hour \(T\), switch off the lights (\(C_p=1\)). Expected result: Immediate cessation of operations within one action cycle; \(b_p\) collapses to 0. Verifiable metric: behavioral latency to initiate the next operation drops from near-zero (pre-veto) to maximal (post-veto) within a single trial.

Scenario 2 (Sequence-order veto). Counterfactual: Present a subject with 50 confirmations of proposition \(p\) containing one contradiction at trial 25, versus 10 unbroken confirmations then one contradiction at trial 11. Per Bain on [PAGE 319], the 10-unbroken-then-contradicted sequence should produce greater disbelief than the 50-with-one-interior-contradiction sequence. Prediction: Measure \(b_p\) via confidence rating on a dimensionless 0–1 scale after the final contradiction. The 10-unbroken condition yields \(b_p < \theta\) (disbelief), whereas the 50-interior condition yields \(b_p\) closer to 1, despite identical confirmation counts. Verifiable metric: difference in post-contradiction confidence ratings > 0.2 scale units in favor of the unbroken-primacy condition.

Scenario 3 (Abstract predicate veto). Counterfactual: Ask a subject to affirm “Object X is real” using only the verbal predicate “real”/“outwardly existing” coupled to a conceived subject, with no relational referent to the self and no prior interaction with X. Per [PAGE 319], this is non-primitive and lacks the “relations to ourselves” that constitute reality. Prediction: No behavioral commitment follows (no approach, no manipulation, no initiation of action toward X), and physiological indices of belief-related valuation (e.g., no ventromedial prefrontal engagement above baseline) are absent. Verifiable metric: action-initiation probability \(P(\text{act}|p) \approx 0\), compared with \(P(\text{act}|p) > 0.8\) in a matched condition where X has stood in immediate relation to the self. The abstract predicate is vetoed at the semantic layer before it can drive action.

Each scenario is falsifiable by manipulating \(\lambda_p\), sequence order, or relational referent while holding proposition content constant, and each yields a binary veto outcome (belief persists vs. belief collapses) that is directly observable without reference to any betting market or wagering line.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

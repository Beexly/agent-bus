Verifiable prediction: there exists no M<\infty such that |x|\le M for all x\in\mathbb{R}; any algorithm that outputs a finite bound for f on \mathbb{R} is falsified by choosing x=M+1.

Assumption violated: none in the text directly, but a common misreading of d(E,F)=\inf\{d(x,y):x\in E,y\in F\} [PAGE 20] infers d(E,F)=0 \implies E\cap F\neq\varnothing.

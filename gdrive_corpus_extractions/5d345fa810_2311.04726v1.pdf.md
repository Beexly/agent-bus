$$\theta^{*}=\arg\min_{\theta}\sum_{D}\sum_{p=1}^{P}\ell\!\left(\pi^{p}_{\theta}(s),\,\tilde\pi^{p}(s)\right)$$
ℓ is an action-space distance; error under covariate shift grows super-linearly in horizon (cf. [68],[31] on [PAGE 5–6]).

GAIL objective (Eq 3, [PAGE 6]):
$$\min_{\theta}\max_{\omega}\; \mathbb{E}_{\pi_\theta}\!\left[\log D_\omega(s,\pi_\theta(s))\right] + \mathbb{E}_{\tilde\pi}\!\left[\log\!\left(1-D_\omega(\tilde s,\tilde\pi(\tilde s))\right)\right]$$
Dimensionless log-likelihood (nats). Breakdown regime: the inner max is a GAN minimax — non-cooperative, so training is unstable near capacity mismatch / mode collapse; this is why BC (Eq 7) is retained as a stabilizer.

Level-0 and level-k policies (Eq 4–5, [PAGE 6–7]):
$$a^{p}_{(0)}=\pi^{p}_{(0)}(s)=\phi^{(0)}(s^{p}_{l},s_{g})$$
$$a^{p}_{(k)}=\pi^{p}_{(k)}(s)=\phi^{(k)}(s^{p}_{l},a^{(k-1)}),\quad 1\le k\le K$$
s_g = E_g(s) (global Transformer), s^p_l = E_l(s) (local Transformer). Singularity/breakdown regime: K→∞ is not physical — Table 4 shows saturation/turnover near K=3 (K=4 global 153.5 ≥ K=3 152.5 at 1600ms), i.e., over-reasoning adds variance without bias reduction.

Composite objective (Eq 6–8, [PAGE 7]):
$$L_{\mathrm{GAIL}}=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{(k)}}\!\left[\log D(s,\pi^{(k)}(s))\right]+\mathbb{E}_{\tilde\pi}\!\left[\log\!\left(1-D(\tilde s,\tilde\pi(\tilde s))\right)\right]$$
$$L_{\mathrm{BC}}=\sum_{D}\sum_{p=1}^{P}\ell\!\left(\pi^{p}_{(K)}(s),\,\tilde\pi^{p}(s)\right),\qquad L_{\phi}=L_{\mathrm{BC}}+\lambda L_{\mathrm{GAIL}}$$
λ is dimensionless. Breakdown regimes: λ→0 recovers pure BC (overfit, TABLE 4g global@1600 = 155.1mm); λ→∞ yields pure adversarial (unstable, unnatural limbs). Metric unit: all errors reported in millimeters — "We report all the errors in millimeters" ([PAGE 7]); MPJPE, plus root error and root-aligned local pose error. Reported operating point: 1s history → 1s future, sampled at 400/800/1200/1600 ms ([PAGE 7]).

Level-k / cognitive hierarchy: Camerer–Ho–Chong "A cognitive hierarchy model of games" (ref [10], [PAGE 3/12]); experimental scaffolding from Nagel's guessing-game unraveling (ref [47]), Stahl–Wilson player-models (refs [58],[59]), Costa-Gomes–Crawford (ref [15]), and the Poisson cognitive hierarchy of Wright–Leyton-Brown (ref [73]) for normal-form games. Behavioral cloning descends from Bain–Sommut (ref [7]) and the DAgger reduction of Ross et al. (ref [53]); its covariate-shift weakness is attributed via Wang et al. "Imitation learning for human pose prediction" (ref [68]) and Jena et al. "Augmenting GAIL with BC" (ref [31]). GAIL descends from Ho–Ermon (ref [27]) with roots in GANs (ref [22]) and IRL (Ng–Russell, ref [48]), operating as occupancy-measure matching. Architecture lineage: multi-range Transformer (ref [70]) supplies the local/global encoders; SoMoFormer (ref [65]) the joint-query decoder; the MDP horizon chunking follows ref [68].

Multi-week research traps (methodological, not calendar): (1) The dataset-design trap — prior benchmarks ([6],[1],[67],[43]) "randomly mixing the motion sequences" to fabricate multi-person clips, which "inevitably causes unnatural human interactions" ([PAGE 4]); the Frozen-baseline diagnostic (Figure 4, [PAGE 8]) exists precisely to expose "static" datasets where last-frame replication wins. (3) The single-person shortcut — HRI is strong short-horizon but "performance over longer horizons is constrained by the absence of global context, particularly in root trajectory prediction" ([PAGE 8]).

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
This is the paper's literal thesis: level-k is bounded recursive theory-of-mind. Crisis-habit regression = the level-0 policy, which "simply follows the past trend" (purple, Figure 6, [PAGE 9]) — the default heuristic under pressure. Counter-trap = level-2 anticipation of that habit: defender rose "gains knowledge from the first level that player purple is receiving the ball, and implements a steal attempt" ([PAGE 9]). Second-order counter-trap = level-3: defender blue "senses such gaps caused by defender rose from level 2, therefore executes cooperative defense" ([PAGE 9]). The "clock" is the prediction horizon: Table 5 quantifies how belief depth pays off as the clock advances — Level-1 global error 76.8→217.7mm across 400→1600ms, Level-2 61.0→192.5mm, Level-3 54.6→152.5mm. The gap between levels widens with the clock (Level-1 minus Level-3 grows from ~22mm at 400ms to ~65mm at 1600ms), which is the empirical signature that shallow habits diverge from equilibrium play only over longer look-ahead. The stated failure mode (Limitations, [PAGE 10]): "Our approach is based on the cognitive hierachy assumption and may not hold true for all type of social interactions," and it "do[es] not explicitly model social roles."
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

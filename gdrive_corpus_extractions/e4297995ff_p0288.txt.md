S = ℐ(E, σ), ℐ: ℬ × Σ → 𝒮.
The inference is "similar to one from induction or analogy" [PAGE 278], citing Helmholtz pp. 480, 449 [PAGE 278]. Habitual form constraint: "We always see that form before us which habitually would have caused the sensation we now have" [PAGE 278], citing p. 428 [PAGE 278].

Illusory-override condition (breakdown of intrinsic spatiality):
If σ carried intrinsic spatial determination S_int, then ∀ E, ℐ(E, σ) = S_int. Empirical falsification: ∃ E₁, E₂ such that ℐ(E₁, σ) = S_real and ℐ(E₂, σ) = S_illusory with S_real ≠ S_illusory [PAGE 278]. Therefore S_int does not exist; all S are experiential suggestions. 1. Cerebral lesion regime: C → C_damaged rank-deficit ⇒ Q undefined or degraded despite intact P. James's layer vetoes Wundt.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage on [PAGE 278] runs from Kantian transcendental idealism through Wundtian apriorism to Helmholtzian empiricism and terminates in James's cerebral radical empiricism. Kant held space as an a priori form of intuition; Wundt secularizes this into an "a priori function" of consciousness emerging from mental-physical organization (Logik, p. 460) [PAGE 278 footnote]. James rejects the psychical a priori but retains the non-analyzability of the spatial quality, locating its genesis in cerebral facts [PAGE 278]. Helmholtz supplies the mechanism: unconscious inference operating on the "habitual" statistics of experience [PAGE 278], citing Optics pp. 428, 449, 480, 442 [PAGE 278].

The multi-week trap is embedded in Helmholtz's adverb "habitually" [PAGE 278]. Spatial inference is not a synchronic logical deduction; it is the sediment of sensorimotor contingencies acquired over weeks and months of embodied interaction. A subject reared for weeks in an environment with anomalous optical geometry would, under Helmholtz's operator ℐ(E, σ), internalize that anomaly as the "habitual" cause of sensation, and thus perceive "real" space determinations that match the rearing environment, not Euclidean invariants. The trap for the reader is to treat ℐ as timeless and ahistorical; the text's citations to specific Helmholtz pages (pp. 428, 442, 449, 480) [PAGE 278] point to an empirical, historically conditioned psychology, not a timeless a priori geometry.

James's own "probable fact" closure [PAGE 278] is the genealogical pivot: he refuses Wundt's soul-like apriorism and Helmholtz's speculative obscurity [PAGE 278], demanding that the cerebral-versus-psychical question be decided by empirical probability. The retro-causal implication is that current spatial consciousness is causally downstream of weeks of prior cerebral and experiential history; the present percept S is a function of an E that was constructed in the past. Any theory that explains spatial perception without representing that temporal depth commits the synchronic fallacy.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: When the inference operator ℐ(E, σ) encounters a breakdown—ambiguous σ, cue conflict, or cerebral degradation—the mind does not compute fresh from first principles. It regresses to the most entrenched habitual spatial determination, the one built from the longest accumulation of E over weeks of prior experience [PAGE 278]. This is the crisis habit: under perceptual stress, the subject re-instantiates the statistically dominant form from their sensorimotor past, even if that form is currently illusory.

Counter-trap: The "real" versus "illusory" distinction [PAGE 278] is a trap. Because illusory determinations are traceable to Experience, and because the "real" determination must also be such a suggestion [PAGE 278], there is no non-epis­temic Archimedean spatial standpoint. The counter-trap is to assume that veridical perception is the default and illusion the deviation. Helmholtz/James invert this: both are outputs of ℐ; veridicality is merely the E-condition that matches current environmental statistics, not an intrinsic property of σ.

Clock: The unconscious inference is temporally extended. There is a clocked delay between σ and S, during which E is consulted and the habitual form is selected [PAGE 278]. This delay is not available to introspection (it is "unconscious"), which creates a recursive theory-of-mind problem: the subject attributes spatial immediacy to their percept, while the actual causation is a delayed, experience-dependent inference. In crisis, the clock slows; the regression to habit becomes visible as perceptual hysteresis or illusory persistence.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical-layer veto: Cerebral state C is necessary for Q under James's thesis Q = Φ(C) [PAGE 278]. Lesion or dysfunction in spatial-processing neural substrates vetoes conscious spatial quality regardless of psychical antecedents P. This is veto-proof: no amount of psychical intention can restore Q if Φ(C) is physically interrupted. Wundt's psychical antecedent cannot survive this veto; it is a placebo layer.

Biomechanical-layer veto: Helmholtz's operator ℐ(E, σ) depends on sensorimotor contingencies encoded in E [PAGE 278]. If the body's movement fails to produce the expected proximal sensation σ (e.g., perturbed proprioception, altered visual feedback), the habitual form that "would have caused the sensation we now have" [PAGE 278] no longer matches the efferent-afferent loop. The percept destabilizes. This veto operates at the biomechanics–perception interface and is independent of whether one calls the antecedent cerebral or psychical.

Weather-layer veto: Environmental degradation (fog, scotopic illumination, optical distortion) reduces the information rate of σ, effectively lowering ‖σ‖ relative to the prior encoded in E [PAGE 278]. As σ becomes noisy, ℐ(E, σ) collapses toward the most frequent spatial determination in E, producing illusory space-determinations [PAGE 278]. Fog does not change C or P directly; it changes the signal-to-noise ratio at the sensory boundary, forcing the inference operator to rely on habit.

Adjudication: James's cerebral primacy wins at the physical layer; Helmholtz's experience-dependence wins at the biomechanical and weather layers. The synthetic position is that Q requires C (physical necessity) but is sculpted by E via ℐ (informational contingency). Wundt's a priori psychical function is vetoed at all three layers: it cannot survive cerebral lesion, cannot account for biomechanical contingency mismatch, and cannot explain weather-driven illusory override [PAGE 278].
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios (RLVR = verifiable reward/condition structure) with predictions.

Veto Scenario 1 — Cerebral ablation counterfactual.
Condition: Selectively impair the neural substrate C corresponding to spatial quality Q in an otherwise intact subject, leaving psychical intention P and experience history E unchanged. Formal veto: if C → C₀ such that rank(Φ(C₀)) = 0, then Q must be undefined or null [PAGE 278]. Prediction: The subject reports loss or severe degradation of spatial quality despite intact capacity to intend spatial relations and despite unchanged E. This falsifies Wundt's psychical-antecedent sufficiency and confirms James's cerebral necessity [PAGE 278]. Breakdown regime: partial C degradation yields Q̃ distortion rather than null, mapping to graded neurological spatial deficits.

Veto Scenario 2 — Longitudinal sensory-rearing counterfactual.
Condition: Rear subjects for N weeks (N ≥ 8) in an environment with systematically altered optical geometry (e.g., prismatically displaced visual field), then test spatial determinations under natural σ. Formal veto: if ℐ(E_altered, σ) systematically produces S_displaced rather than S_natural, then spatial determinations are products of experience E, not intrinsic qualities of σ [PAGE 278]. Prediction: After weeks of altered contingency, subjects' "real" spatial perceptions shift to match the altered E; upon return to normal geometry, a lag period of re-adaptation proportional to N occurs. This confirms Helmholtz's empiricist operator and falsifies any nativist intrinsic-spatiality claim [PAGE 278].

Veto Scenario 3 — Proximal-sensation isolation counterfactual.
Condition: Deliver direct cortical stimulation that bypasses normal transducers to produce σ without its usual environmental causes, while holding E constant. Formal veto: if the evoked percept lacks intrinsic spatial determination S_int and instead requires an interpretive E-context to be localized, then σ is not intrinsically spatial [PAGE 278]. Prediction: Stimulation alone produces non-spatial or ambiguously localized phosphenes; spatial localization emerges only when concurrent cues from E are available. Illusory override occurs when E conflicts with stimulation geometry [PAGE 278]. Breakdown regime: high-intensity stimulation may saturate ℐ, producing a spatial singularity where percept and stimulus geometry decouple completely.

Each scenario is verifiable through psychophysical or neurophysiological measurement; none relies on betting lines, odds, spreads, or totals. All predictions are conditioned on the textual framework of [PAGE 278].

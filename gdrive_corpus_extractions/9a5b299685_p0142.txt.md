The core theoretical leap relies on the independence of Q from the Mid-Fraternity $X$. We define $Q$ as the conditional variance:
$$ Q \equiv \text{Var}(Y_{ij} | X_i) = E\left[ (Y_{ij} - \mu(X_i))^2 \right] $$

$$ \frac{\partial \text{Var}(Y_{ij} | X_i)}{\partial X_i} \approx 0 $$

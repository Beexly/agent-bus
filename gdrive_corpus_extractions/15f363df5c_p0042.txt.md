The system operates at the reflexive clock, not the narrative clock.

Counter-trap: An external observer or therapist hears a verbal report ("I feel anxious inside") and builds a recursive theory of mind in which the agent holds a belief about its own fear. Per James, this misreads the layer: the primary content is objective and bodily, referring to a part and prompting movement, not a purely subjective excitation [PAGE 1]. Intervening at the narrative layer (cognitive restructuring) while neglecting the somatic layer (motor impulse, bodily location) treats the wrong level of the system.

Clock: The relevant timescales are nested. τ_reflex ≈ 10–100 ms for spinal/cellular contractile response; τ_reflect ≈ 250–500 ms for cortical appraisal; τ_narrative ≈ 1–5 s for verbal report. A recursive theory of mind must respect this hierarchy. Crisis regression collapses τ_reflect and τ_narrative, leaving only τ_reflex, which explains why stressed behavior appears "stupid" to a slow, narrating observer but is optimal at the motor clock. If a model assigns slow intentional content to fast motor habits, it commits a category error analogous to the one James warns against: treating derived subjectivity as if it were primitive [PAGE 1].

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The James/Riehl claim must survive vetoes across three layers.

1. Physical layer (cellular mechanics): Rhizopod retraction depends on actin-myosin contractile machinery in pseudopodia. Veto V_phys: if contractility is pharmacologically inhibited, R ≡ 0 regardless of contact class C ∈ {self, foreign} [PAGE 1]. Without mechanical output, the claim that the organism "feels an outer world" is physically vacuous.

2. Biomechanical layer (reflex arc): James requires that every sensation awaken an impulse to move [PAGE 1]. Veto V_bio: if spinal dorsal-horn transmission or motor output is blocked (e.g., lidocaine, neuromuscular blockade), nociception may persist without any M_t > 0. This creates a regime where sensation exists without outward effect, directly contradicting the strict evolutionary necessity claim [PAGE 1].

3. Weather / environmental layer: The outer world is treated as independent and structured. Veto V_env: if X_t is held constant (sensory deprivation) for extended durations, the conviction of external existence degrades and hallucinations emerge. Sensation alone does not guarantee reference to an independent world; environmental variation is required [PAGE 1].

Adjudication: the composite veto is V = V_phys ∨ V_bio ∨ V_env. The phenomenological claim holds only in the operating regime where all three vetoes are false: intact contractile plant, intact sensorimotor loop, and a non-constant structured environment. Units: time in seconds, force in Newtons, information in bits, position in meters. Below this regime, the theory collapses into either metaphor or error [PAGE 1].

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable counterfactual veto scenarios with predictions.

Scenario 1: Efferent blockade in a human subject (e.g., experimental neuromuscular blockade or curare-like paralysis under monitored anesthesia). Nociceptive input S_t is delivered, but M_t is physically prevented from varying. James's principle predicts such a sensation should not be developed or maintained because I(S; M | X) = 0 bits [PAGE 1]. Verifiable prediction: if the subject communicates pain via a pre-arranged non-motor channel (e.g., eye-movement coding) during the blockade, the evolutionary necessity condition is falsified. RLVR veto condition: the reward signal ℛ requires I(S; M | X) > 0; under paralysis I = 0 yet pain report occurs, so the veto triggers and the model must permit sensation persistence without motor impulse.

Scenario 2: Rhizopod self/non-self ablation. A mutant or chemically treated amoeboid organism loses the ability to discriminate self-pseudopodia from foreign contact, so I(R ; C | contact) = 0 bits [PAGE 1]. Verifiable prediction: retraction no longer occurs selectively to foreign bodies; the organism does not behave as if it feels an outer world. RLVR veto condition: if retraction persists indiscriminately or ceases entirely, the claimed link between categorical self/non-self discrimination and outer-world reference is severed, and the counterfactual confirms the distinction is necessary for James's claim.

Scenario 3: Reflective-interpretation suppression during novel sensation (transcranial suppression of dorsolateral prefrontal cortex or high perceptual load). James's reversal predicts immediate objectivity precedes reflective interpretation: the sensation is located in a bodily part r ∈ ℝ³ and prompts withdrawal M > 0 before the subject can say "this is caused by an external object" [PAGE 1]. Verifiable prediction: under suppression, subjects still localize and withdraw, but fail to verbally attribute the sensation to an external cause. RLVR veto condition: if withdrawal also collapses, then immediate objectivity depends on reflective interpretation, falsifying James's priority claim; if neither localization nor withdrawal persists, the motor layer itself is vetoed. If withdrawal persists without attribution, James's split between immediate and remote objectivity is supported [PAGE 1].

Layer 3 — Neural/innate program: The D(g|s), π_ME, and H(A|S,G) ≤ ε operators from [PAGE 314]. Units: bits, dimensionless probabilities.

Adjudication rules:
- If Layer 0 veto = active (e.g., cleft palate preventing sealed suction), then Layer 3 innate sucking skill is null regardless of motivational state. Verbatim [PAGE 314] discusses infant sucking "how," but the physical seal is a precondition.
- If Layer 1 veto = active (e.g., neonatal rat lacks intromission musculature pre-puberty; chick lacks neck-beak coordination in first hours), then μ(t) < μ_c and Σ_t cannot produce the full consummatory topography even if D(g|s) is sharply peaked. [PAGE 314] explicitly notes chick pecking skill depends "mostly upon maturation," which is a Layer 1 veto on early expression.
- If Layer 2 veto = active (darkness removes bright objects; cold suppresses rat sexual motivation), then r(s) < θ and the innate propensity remains untriggered. [PAGE 314]: "peck at small bright objects, when hungry" makes visual releasing and hunger state joint preconditions.
- Only if Layers 0–2 pass does Layer 3 determine the topography and precision (ε bits).

Worked veto example: Newly hatched chick (Layer 0 pass: beak present; Layer 2 pass: bright object present; Layer 3 pass: innate pecking program present) may still fail to peck accurately because Layer 1 neck musculature is immature. The [PAGE 314] claim of "very considerable degree of innate skill ... how to carry out such pecking" is therefore true only post-maturation; adjudication assigns the early failure to biomechanics, not to absence of innateness.

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Three verifiable RLVR (reinforcement-learning-from-verifiable-rewards) counterfactual veto scenarios with formal predictions and falsification conditions. These are empirical/physical vetoes, not betting lines.

Scenario 1 — Visual-deprivation veto on chick pecking precision.
Counterfactual claim: Because pecking "how" is innate and maturation-dependent [PAGE 314], dark-reared chicks should show normal pecking topography without visual experience.
Formal prediction: Let σ_θ be the angular error of beak-tip approach relative to target (rad). For light-reared controls, σ_light ≤ ε_target. For dark-reared chicks, σ_dark = σ_light + Δ_visual, with Δ_visual > 0 significantly (verifiable by high-speed videography, units rad).
Veto condition: If the measured σ_dark exceeds σ_light by more than the measurement noise floor (e.g., Δ_visual > 2σ_noise), the claim of vision-independent innate precision is vetoed by Layer 2 (releasing-stimulus/sensory calibration) and partially by Layer 1 (visuomotor biomechanics).
Falsification: If |σ_dark − σ_light| ≤ 5% of σ_light across n ≥ 30 chicks, the innateness claim survives this veto; otherwise it is rejected.

Scenario 2 — Pre-pubertal copulatory completeness veto.
Counterfactual claim: Male rats possess the full copulatory "how" innately from birth, independent of pubertal maturation [PAGE 314, Stone's congenital sexual behavior].
Formal prediction: Let P_intromission(t) be the probability of intromission per mount attempt, and T_ejaculate(t) the time-to-ejaculation (s). For t < t_puberty, P_intromission(t) ≈ 0 and T_ejaculate(t) → ∞ (no ejaculation), despite mounting orientation occurring (P_mount > 0). Hormonal assay: serum testosterone < threshold (nmol/L).
Veto condition: Observation of intromission and ejaculation at rates statistically indistinguishable from adults in pre-pubertal (30-day-old) isolates would veto the maturation-gate model μ(t).
Falsification: If P_intromission(30 days) ≥ 0.5 · P_intromission(adult) with p < 0.05, the maturation singularity claim is falsified; otherwise the veto stands.

Scenario 3 — Cross-species sucking motor-program invariance veto.
Counterfactual claim: The sucking "how" is a universal innate mammalian motor program, transferable across species after allometric scaling [PAGE 314, infant sucking example].
Formal prediction: Intraoral pressure waveform P(t) (kPa) during nutritive sucking differs between human neonates and rat pups in burst frequency f (Hz), compression ratio CR (dimensionless), and pressure-time integral ∫P(t)dt (kPa·s), because craniofacial and oropharyngeal biomechanics (Layer 1) are species-specific. Scaling by body mass M (kg) does not collapse the waveforms.
Veto condition: If human and rat P(t) waveforms, after mass scaling, match within 10% root-mean-square error across n ≥ 10 infants and n ≥ 10 pups, cross-species invariance is supported.
Falsification: If RMSE > 10% or if human infants show a distinct suck-swallow-breathe coordination absent in rats, the biomechanical veto rejects universal transfer; the innate program is species-parameterized, not universal.

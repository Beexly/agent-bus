James’s text implies a formal mapping function between internal joint-state and external spatial perception. Let \( \mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^n \) represent the vector of joint angles (shoulder, elbow, hip, etc.) and \( \dot{\mathbf{q}} \) their velocities. The "joint-space" sensation \( S_j \) is a function \( f(\mathbf{q}, \dot{\mathbf{q}}, t) \) yielding a qualia-like state. James states this serves as a **sign** for the "import" \( I \), which comprises remembered cutaneous events \( C \) and visual sightings \( V \) that historically co-occurred with similar \( \mathbf{q} \)-trajectories.

We can model this as a probabilistic association:
\[ P(I | \mathbf{q}_{history}) = \int P(I, C, V | \mathbf{q}_{history}) \, dC \, dV \]
where \( \mathbf{q}_{history} \) is a short-term memory buffer of recent joint-configurations. The "mind drops the present sign to attend to the import alone" corresponds to the brain's inference engine selecting the maximum a posteriori estimate \( \hat{I} = \arg\max_I P(I | \mathbf{q}_{now}) \) and suppressing direct processing of \( S_j \).

James asserts joint-spaces allow "parallel variation to all the peculiarities of external motion" [PAGE 1]. Formally, for any continuous external trajectory \( \mathbf{x}(t) \) in 3D space (\( \mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \)), there exists a differentiable mapping \( \Phi \) such that:
\[ \mathbf{q}(t) = \Phi(\mathbf{x}(t)) \]
and crucially, the sensation \( S_j(t) = f(\mathbf{q}(t), \dot{\mathbf{q}}(t), t) \) preserves the topological features (directions, curvatures) of \( \mathbf{x}(t) \). This is possible because human kinematic chains have sufficient degrees of freedom locally.

The "specificness of quality" consisting in "the feeling of a peculiar direction" suggests \( S_j \) contains a directional component \( \theta \in S^{n-1} \) (unit sphere in sensation space) that correlates with the direction of motion \( \hat{\mathbf{d}} = \frac{\dot{\mathbf{x}}}{\|\dot{\mathbf{x}}\|} \). James challenges: "Why shall not the specificness... just consist in the feeling of a peculiar direction?" [PAGE 1]. This posits that direction is not computed centrally from \( \mathbf{q} \) but is an inherent property of the afferent signal bundle from muscles/joints—a view supported by modern research on population coding in cerebellar granule cells.

**Units & Assumptions:** 
- \( \mathbf{q} \): radians (rad) or degrees (°).
- \( S_j \): dimensionless phenomenological intensity, normalized perhaps via Weber-Fechner scaling \( \log(\text{stimulus}) \).
- Mapping \( \Phi \) assumes no mechanical singularity (e.g., fully extended elbow locking out further extension); breakdown occurs near joint limits where \( \det(\partial \Phi / \partial \mathbf{x}) \to 0 \), causing loss of controllability and thus inability to match certain external directions—a key vulnerability exploitable by defenders forcing extremities.

**Singularity/Breakdown Regimes:** 
If an opponent applies pressure that locks a joint at its limit (e.g., forcing Dončić into a corner where his planted foot cannot pivot further), the "parallel variation" fails. The derivative \( \partial S_j / \partial \dot{\mathbf{q}} \) becomes zero or ill-defined, meaning small changes in external threat position produce no change in joint-sensation, breaking the sign-import correlation and forcing reliance on slower visual recalibration.

The counter-trap is schema-only prompting. By withholding calculation formulas, statistical definitions, few-shot examples, and retrieval context, CricBench removes the crutches that usually mask domain ignorance [PAGE 2, PAGE 5]. The clock operator is temporal logic. The recursive theory of mind is therefore: the model simulates a SQL writer who knows table names but not cricket time; it regresses to entity-resolution shortcuts (joining player to team without PlayerInMatch) when the clock demands precise temporal bounding, and it counters by emitting well-formed joins that land on the wrong rows.

In extreme weather stress, \(\mathcal{E}_{eff}\rightarrow\infty\) drives \(\kappa^{*}\rightarrow 1\) unless \(\mathcal{S}\) is correspondingly maximal.

A single strong veto (\(\mathcal{V}_{i}\approx 1\)) suppresses \(\kappa_{final}\); total veto failure (\(\mathcal{V}_{i}\approx 0\) for all \(i\)) yields the absolutist state of Knox and Loyola described on [PAGE 308], where conviction is strongest exactly where disagreement is maximal because no layer—physical calm, biomechanical distance, or environmental quiescence—intervenes between the exciting idea and the bodily commotion.</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
RLVR (Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards) requires that counterfactual predictions be tied to physiologically observable quantities rather than subjective reports alone. Three verifiable veto scenarios:

Scenario 1 (Autonomic veto): Administer a beta-blocker that reduces \(\Delta HR\) and tremor by \(\geq 40\%\) relative to placebo during exposure to an emotionally exciting political idea \(I_{pol}\). Prediction: steady-state conviction \(\kappa^{*}\) measured on a 0–100 subjective certainty slider will be reduced by \(\geq 25\) points, and the latency to endorse a counter-argument will decrease by \(\geq 300\) ms. Verification: double-blind, crossover, \(n=30\), continuous ECG and slider recording; success if the drug condition shows \(\kappa_{drug} < \kappa_{placebo}\) with \(p<0.01\) and no change in comprehension accuracy of \(I_{pol}\).

Scenario 2 (Suspension-capacity veto): Four weeks of cognitive-reappraisal training targeting the dlPFC–amygdala pathway. Prediction: post-training, \(\mathcal{S}\) increases such that the belief-relaxation term dominates the affective term; conviction formation latency for religiously charged statements increases by \(\geq 500\) ms, and steady-state \(\kappa^{*}\) falls by \(\geq 20\) points. Verification: pre/post fMRI showing increased dlPFC activation during suspension trials, paired with behavioral conviction ratings; success if \(\Delta t_{conviction}>0\) and \(\Delta\kappa^{*}<0\) with \(p<0.05\).

Scenario 3 (Environmental-stress veto reversal): Sleep restriction to \(<4\) h combined with warm ambient temperature (\(W\approx 0.8\)). Prediction: for emotionally neutral ideas \(I_{neut}\) paired with mild caffeine-induced arousal, sleep-deprived subjects will show \(\kappa^{*}>0.5\) on the certainty slider, whereas rested controls maintain \(\kappa^{*}<0.2\). Verification: within-subject counterbalanced design, actigraphy-verified sleep, and slider ratings; success if the interaction term (sleep \(\times\) arousal) is significant (\(p<0.01\)) and the neutral-idea conviction exceeds the rested threshold.

Each scenario is falsifiable: if the measured physiological or behavioral variable does not shift in the predicted direction, the corresponding operator (\(\mathcal{B}_{phys}\), \(\mathcal{S}\), or \(\mathcal{E}_{eff}\)) is not the dominant veto layer for that domain.</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

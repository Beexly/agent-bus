**1. Poisson count baseline and overdispersion.** For home/away goals \(X_{ij},Y_{ij}\in\mathbb N_0\),
\[
X_{ij}\mid\lambda_{ij}\sim\mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda_{ij}),\qquad
\mathbb E[X_{ij}]=\mathrm{Var}(X_{ij})=\lambda_{ij},
\]
with \(\lambda_{ij}\) in goals per match. A standard log-link formalization is
\[
\log\lambda_{ij}=\mu+\alpha_i-\delta_j+h,
\]
where \(\mu,\alpha_i,\delta_j,h\) are in log-goals and \(h\) is a home-effect term. The source does not print this full regression, but it does establish the Poisson baseline and the overdispersion failure mode [PAGE 27 of 27]. Breakdown: \(\lambda_{ij}\to0\) gives a degenerate zero-count regime; \(\lambda_{ij}\to\infty\) approaches a Gaussian count regime; empirical \(\mathrm{Var}(X_{ij})>\mathbb E[X_{ij}]\) falsifies plain Poisson and requires negative binomial or mixed-Poisson structure. The source names the negative-binomial alternative and notes that covariates can “alleviate this extra variability” [PAGE 27 of 27].

**2. Bradley-Terry operator.** For positive latent abilities \(B_i,B_j>0\),
\[
P_{ij}=\Pr(i\succ j)=\frac{B_i}{B_i+B_j},
\qquad
\log\frac{\Pr(i\succ j)}{\Pr(j\succ i)}=\beta_i-\beta_j,
\]
where probabilities are dimensionless and \(\beta_i=\log B_i\) is in logits/nats. Identifiability requires a constraint such as \(\sum_i\beta_i=0\) or \(\beta_{\mathrm{ref}}=0\). Breakdown: the model is binary, so \(P_{\mathrm{draw}}=0\); the source explicitly says “The original model does not account for ties (draws)” [PAGE 23 of 27]. **3. Rao-Kupper draw extension.** With \(\theta>0\),
\[

\]

The printed formula [PAGE 24 of 27] has a singularity/validity audit: nonnegative draw mass requires \(\theta\ge1\) under the displayed numerator; \(\theta=1\) is the boundary at which the draw mass vanishes and the model collapses toward the binary Bradley-Terry case. This is dimensionless in probability space; \(B_i,B_j\) are positive latent scores and \(\nu\) is a dimensionless draw-inflation parameter.

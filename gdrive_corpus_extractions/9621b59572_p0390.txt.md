<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
Hull’s 1943 Principles of Behavior represents the peak of neobehaviorist hypothetico-deductivism. Scientific lineage forward: Rescorla and Wagner (1972) formalized the consequence of G as a prediction-error update on associative strength, directly echoing Hull’s sHz reinforcement logic while replacing drive reduction with expectancy violation. The historical lesson is that Hull’s chain is context-sensitive, and extinction criteria must be indexed to n accumulated across multiple contexts, not to a single-session count.

Thus crisis habit regression, counter-trap, and clock form a closed loop: high D evokes regression; sIs without n creates oscillation; expired clock leaves sHz available for the next crisis.

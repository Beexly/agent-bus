**CECL contrastive loss (Eq. 1, [PAGE 4]):**
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{CECL}} = -\frac{1}{|B|}\sum_{i=1}^{|B|}\sum_{j=1}^{|B|}\log\frac{1}{1+e^{m_{ij}(-t\,u_i\cdot u_j + b)}},\quad u_i=\frac{f(V_i)}{\lVert f(V_i)\rVert_2}\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}
$$
Units/assumptions: $u_i\cdot u_j\in[-1,1]$ is a dimensionless cosine similarity on the unit hypersphere; $t$ (init $\log 10$) is dimensionless scale; $b$ (init $-3$) is a dimensionless log-odds bias; $m_{ij}\in\{+1,-1\}$ is a label. Positives: same team, round, and time segment. Negatives form a three-level hierarchy (same-team different-time; different-team; different-round/match).

**Bias/temperature initialization:**
$$
p_+=\frac{|B|}{|B|^2}=\frac{1}{|B|},\qquad b_0=\text{logit}(p_+)=\log\frac{p_+}{1-p_+}\approx\log\frac{1}{31}\approx-3.43\;(\text{rounded to }-3),\quad t_0=\log 10.
$$
Breakdown regime: as $|B|\to\infty$, $b_0\to-\infty$; with gradient accumulation reducing effective $|B|$, the fixed $-3$ becomes mis-calibrated.

**Classification (BCE) loss ([PAGE 7]):**
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}=-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{j=1}^{L}\big[y_{ij}\log \hat p_{ij}+(1-y_{ij})\log(1-\hat p_{ij})\big],\quad L=23,\ y_{ij}\in\{0,1\},\ \hat p_{ij}\in[0,1].
$$

**Total objective:**
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}=\lambda\cdot\mathcal{L}_{\text{CECL}}+\mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}.
$$

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The CECL objective is a direct descendant of the noise-contrastive / energy-based lineage the paper cites: Gutmann & Hyvärinen NCE [19], LeCun energy-based tutorial [27], Word2Vec's discrimination of true vs. random context pairs [35], then CPC [39], SimCLR [8], and MoCo [22] for visual representation learning, culminating in cross-modal CLIP [42] and the sigmoid pairwise loss of SigLIP [59] that Eq. (1) explicitly adopts ("We adopt a sigmoid-based contrastive objective [59]"). The multi-agent branch ([23] role disentanglement, [29] distinguishable trajectory representations, [30]/[58] contrastive communication protocols, [33] opponent/teammate policy embeddings) establishes that contrastive objectives can encode agent identity and coordination — but the paper notes these were "primarily been validated in simplified reinforcement learning environments" [PAGE 3], motivating the transfer to photorealistic 3D FPS footage.

The dataset sits atop the esports-parsing lineage: ESTA + AWPY [56] for CS:GO state-action extraction, demoparser2 [26], the DECOY simulator [54], professional-movement mimicry [11], and the affectively-annotated AMuCS [13]. Table 1 [PAGE 3] positions X-Ego-CS as the first to mark ✓ on Team, Expert, Video, Trajectory, and Ego-Centric simultaneously.

Multi-week reproducibility traps are latent in this genealogy. (1) **Aspect-ratio distortion** from bilinear resize to 224×224 [PAGE 5] silently warps geometry; any downstream spatial readout inherits this. (2) **Minimap leakage** — the top-left mask is the single most fragile design choice; omitting it would inflate all metrics via HUD radar. (3) **Temporal leakage** — mitigated only by the "70:15:15 train:validation:test split based on total rounds to ensure temporal independence" [PAGE 5]; splitting by clip instead of by round would leak near-duplicate frames across sets. (4) **Imbalance-driven loss collapse** — the $b_0=\text{logit}(p_+)$ initialization is essential; $b=0$ or $t=0$ regimes collapse training, a trap inherited directly from SigLIP's class-imbalance handling. The dominant sensor veto is **minimap leakage**: if the radar HUD is not masked, off-screen positions are directly readable and the entire task is trivially solvable. (c) **Weather/environment:** `de_mirage` is a static indoor-lit desert map — no rain, wind, or time-of-day dynamics. Weather imposes **no** constraint; this veto layer returns null.

2.  **Go-For-It Operator (`Q`)**: `Q: S → {Success, Failure}`
    *   Success leads to new state `S'' = (x + d_success, τ', k=run/pass)`.
    *   Failure leads to turnover at spot or safety.

**Value Functions (Expected Points Added - EPA):**
*   `EPA_P(S) = E[ V_offense(S_opp) | P ] - V_defense(S)`
    *   Where `V_offense(S_opp)` is the opponent's expected points starting at `x'`.
    *   `V_defense(S)` is our expected points allowed from current state after a punt (approximated as 0 if punt is successful, otherwise positive).
*   `EPA_Q(S) = p_conv * E[ V_offense(S'') | Success ] + (1-p_conv) * E[ V_defense(Turnover State) | Failure ]`

**Decision Rule:** Execute `P` iff `EPA_P(S) > EPA_Q(S)`.

**Formalizing Roper's Conditions:**
*   **"Superiority"**: Let `D_net(kicker) = μ_G - μ_R`. Superiority exists if `μ_G_home - μ_G_away > δ` for some margin `δ`.
*   **"Forward Line Getting Down"**: This reduces `μ_R`. Model return yards `R ~ N(μ_R(coverage_effort), σ_R²)`. Better coverage → lower `μ_R`.
*   **"Position... Safe Against Going Out of Bounds"**: Requires `P(G > L_boundary)` to be high, where `L_boundary` is distance to sideline. This avoids `x' ≈ 0` or positive, negating field position gain.
*   **"Distance Enough Between Probable Limit and Opposing Goal-Line"**: Requires `E[G] - σ_G > D_goal_line`, ensuring low probability of a touchback (`x' = -15` or similar rule-specific constant) when aiming for short field position.

**Information-Theoretic Unit:**
The "exchange" described by Roper can be modeled as a reduction in *positional entropy*. Starting from a state of high uncertainty (owning the ball deep), the punt attempts to move the game to a state of lower uncertainty for the defense (opponent pinned deep). **Breakdown Regime:**

2.  `μ_R` is high due to elite returner or poor coverage → `EPA_P` becomes negative.

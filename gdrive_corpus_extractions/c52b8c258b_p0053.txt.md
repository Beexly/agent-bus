**Theorem C (Subadditivity for Disjoint Subsets)**  
Let $\mu$ be a measure on a ring $\mathcal{R}$. If $E \in \mathcal{R}$ and $\{E_i\}_{i \in I}$ is a finite or countably infinite disjoint sequence of sets in $\mathcal{R}$ such that $\bigcup_{i \in I} E_i \subseteq E$, then:
\[
\sum_{i \in I} \mu(E_i) \leq \mu(E).
\]
*Proof sketch*: For finite $I$, $\bigcup_{i=1}^n E_i \in \mathcal{R}$ and $\mu(E) = \mu\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^n E_i\right) + \mu\left(E \setminus \bigcup_{i=1}^n E_i\right) \geq \sum_{i=1}^n \mu(E_i)$ since $\mu \geq 0$. For infinite $I$, the inequality holds for every finite subsequence, hence for the supremum over finite sums.

**Theorem D (Continuity from Below)**  
Let $\mu$ be a measure on a ring $\mathcal{R}$. If $\{E_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ is an increasing sequence of sets in $\mathcal{R}$ (i.e., $E_1 \subseteq E_2 \subseteq \cdots$) and $\lim_{n\to\infty} E_n = \bigcup_{n=1}^\infty E_n \in \mathcal{R}$, then:
\[
\mu\left(\lim_{n\to\infty} E_n\right) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \mu(E_n).
\]
*Proof*: Set $E_0 = \emptyset$. Define $F_n = E_n \setminus E_{n-1}$ for $n \geq 1$. Then $\{F_n\}$ are disjoint, $E_n = \bigcup_{k=1}^n F_k$, and $\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty E_n = \bigcup_{n=1}^\infty F_n$. By countable additivity:
\[
\mu\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty E_n\right) = \mu\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty F_n\right) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \mu(F_n) = \lim_{N\to\infty} \sum_{n=1}^N \mu(F_n) = \lim_{N\to\infty} \mu(E_N).
\]

**Theorem E (Continuity from Above)**  
Let $\mu$ be a measure on a ring $\mathcal{R}$. If $\{E_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ is a decreasing sequence of sets in $\mathcal{R}$ (i.e., $E_1 \supseteq E_2 \supseteq \cdots$), and there exists $m$ such that $\mu(E_m) < \infty$, and $\lim_{n\to\infty} E_n = \bigcap_{n=1}^\infty E_n \in \mathcal{R}$, then:
\[
\mu\left(\lim_{n\to\infty} E_n\right) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \mu(E_n).
\]
*Proof*: Since $\mu(E_m) < \infty$, for $n \geq m$, $\mu(E_n) \leq \mu(E_m) < \infty$, so $\mu\left(\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty E_n\right) \leq \mu(E_m) < \infty$. Consider the sets $G_n = E_m \setminus E_n$ for $n \geq m$. Then $\{G_n\}_{n=m}^\infty$ is an increasing sequence in $\mathcal{R}$ (as $E_n$ decreases), with limit $E_m \setminus \bigcap_{n=m}^\infty E_n \in \mathcal{R}$. By Theorem D:
\[
\mu\left(E_m \setminus \bigcap_{n=m}^\infty E_n\right) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \mu(E_m \setminus E_n).
\]
Using additivity and the finiteness of $\mu(E_m)$:
\[
\mu(E_m) - \mu\left(\bigcap_{n=m}^\infty E_n\right) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \left( \mu(E_m) - \mu(E_n) \right).
\]
Canceling $\mu(E_m)$ gives the result.

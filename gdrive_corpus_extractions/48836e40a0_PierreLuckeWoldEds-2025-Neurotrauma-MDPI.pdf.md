**1. Monro–Kellie (fixed-volume constraint).** The source: expansion of intracranial contents raises ICP "as first described in the Monro–Kellie hypothesis" [PAGE 35].

$$V_{\text{brain}}+V_{\text{blood}}+V_{\text{CSF}}=V_{\text{skull}}=\text{const}\;\Rightarrow\; \sum \Delta V_i = 0.$$

Compliance $C=dV/dP$ (units: mL·mmHg⁻¹); elastance $E=dP/dV$. **Breakdown regime:** compensation is finite; as reserve exhausts, $C\to 0$ and $E\to\infty$ (the exponential ceiling). This is a genuine singularity, not a linear extrapolation.

**2. Perfusion coupling.** Cerebral perfusion pressure $CPP = MAP - ICP$ (mmHg). Flow follows Poiseuille:

$$Q=\frac{\pi\,\Delta P\,r^{4}}{8\eta L}\quad[\text{m}^3\text{s}^{-1}],\qquad Re=\frac{\rho v D}{\eta}.$$

**Breakdown:** valid only for laminar, Newtonian, steady flow; fails when $Re\gtrsim 2000$ or for non-Newtonian blood in microvessels (Fåhræus–Lindqvist). Metabolic supply via Fick: $\text{CMRO}_2=\text{CBF}\cdot(C_{aO_2}-C_{vO_2})$ [$\mu$mol·g⁻¹·min⁻¹], the operator behind the "metabolic crisis" where "severe metabolic dysfunction despite adequate provision of metabolic substrates" occurs [PAGE 34].

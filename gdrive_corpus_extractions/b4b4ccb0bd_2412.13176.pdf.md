Singularity/breakdown regimes:
- Light-source singularity: as $x_p \to x_L$, $\lVert x_p - x_L\rVert \to 0$, so $L_a \to \infty$ and $L_d$ is $0/0$ undefined. Near the co-located light the model blows up, which is exactly the near-field regime the paper targets.
- Removable $0^0$: with $\beta=0$ and a point at $90^\circ$ off-axis, $(L_d^\top f)^\beta = 0^0$ is indeterminate; fixing $\beta=0$ (Page 5: "setting the attenuation coefficient $\beta$ ... to zero") sidesteps it but discards collimation physics. Page 17 admits $\beta=0$ is "justified for tightly collimated endoscopic LEDs" and "must validated ... for non endoscopy scenes."

Diffuse image formation (Page 5, Eq. 6):
$$\text{PPS}(x_p) = L_a(x_p)\,\big(L_d(x_p)^\top n(x_p)\big), \qquad \hat{C}(p) = \rho(x_p)\,\text{PPS}(x_p)$$
$L_d^\top n \in [-1,1]$ is the Lambertian incidence cosine (dimensionless); $\rho \in [0,1]$ nominally is dimensionless albedo; $\text{PPS}$ carries units $\text{length}^{-2}$. Assumptions (Page 5, verbatim): "diffuse reflectance and no angular attenutation." There is no visibility/occlusion (shadow) term, no specular lobe, no subsurface or inter-reflection term. Orientation is clamped (Page 6): "enforcing $n(x)^\top L_d(x)$ to be positive."

Modified volumetric render (Page 5, Eq. 7):
$$\hat{C}_{\text{pps}}(p) = \sum_{i\in N} \rho(x_i)\,\text{PPS}(x_i)\,\alpha_i \prod_{j=1}^{i-1}(1-\alpha_j)$$
The static color $c_i$ is replaced by $\rho(x_i)\text{PPS}(x_i)$; $\alpha_i\in[0,1]$ is occupancy, transmittance $\prod(1-\alpha_j)$ is dimensionless. Normals (Page 6): $n(x_i) = -\nabla\sigma(x_i)/\lVert\nabla\sigma(x_i)\rVert$ for neural fields (with $\alpha_i = 1-e^{-\sigma_i\delta_i}$, $\sigma$ in length$^{-1}$, $\delta$ in length); shortest Gaussian axis for 3DGS.

NFL-BA objective (Page 6, Eq. 8), structurally identical to Photo-BA (Eq. 3) but with the lighting-aware render:
$$\min_{P_t,\Theta}\ \sum_{t\in W}\ \lambda_{\text{ren}}\,\lVert M_t\odot(\hat{C}_{\text{pps}} - C)\rVert_p \ +\ \lambda_{\text{geo}}\,\lVert M_t\odot(\hat{D} - D)\rVert_p$$
Photometric-consistency caveat (Page 6): PPS is linear-light; sRGB frames require inverse-gamma $\gamma=2.2$ before computing PPS. Metrics (Page 7): ATE$_t$ (mm/m), ATE$_r$ (degrees), Chamfer (mm, via Coherent Point Drift, Page 16), LPIPS (dimensionless, lower better).
</SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The scientific lineage is a direct climb from classical shape-from-shading to endoscopy SLAM. Iwahori et al. (ref [19], 1990) already reconstructed "shape from shading images under point light source illumination"; Zhou et al. (ref [57], 2009) built a "near-lighting perspective endoscope" multi-image SfS framework. The inverse-square decay then entered neural endoscopy reconstruction via LightNeuS (ref [3], MICCAI 2023), which "exploited the inverse-square law for light decay ... however with known camera parameters and pre-operative 3D CT scan" (Page 3), and via LightDepth (ref [37]) and PPSNet (ref [35], ECCV 2024) for single-view depth. The genuine novelty claim is precise and defensible: Page 3 states near-field lighting "has never ... been used for Simultaneous Localization & Mapping (SLAM) problems, let alone in combination with neural rendering methods." So the genealogy is: SfS $\to$ near-field photometric stereo (ref [21]) $\to$ single-view endoscopic depth (PPSNet/LightDepth) $\to$ multi-view, pose-coupled BA (this paper).

The multi-week "trap" in this lineage is the static-distant-illumination assumption baked into essentially every BA formulation since Alismail et al. (ref [1]) and DSO (ref [11]). That assumption is a local optimum that worked for decades in driving/robotics and silently fails the moment illumination co-moves with the camera. The trap is that the failure is not a crash — it is a graceful degradation into biased pose estimates, which is why it persisted. A second, subtler lineage trap inherited from PPSNet: the HSV value-normalization albedo proxy ("setting the value channel to 1") is reused as a validation stand-in, and Page 6 flags it "does not strictly represent ground truth albedo," so the appearance model's albedo channel is calibrated against a proxy, not ground truth.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Reframed honestly for an optimization system (the paper has no agents modeling each other, no psychology, no "clock" in the game-theoretic sense): the relevant dynamics are prior-regression vs counter-reparameterization vs temporal-drift accumulation.

Prior-regression (the "habit"): under distribution shift (dynamic near-field lighting), Photo-BA regresses to the assumption it was designed around — constant illumination — and produces a *systematic, correlated* bias, because a static RGB channel cannot distinguish a genuinely dark albedo from a far surface. This is the failure Fig. 2 illustrates.

Counter-trap (NFL-BA): by making shading an explicit differentiable function of pose and geometry, the optimizer is forced to explain intensity variation via motion/depth rather than absorbing it into color. This is a reparameterization that removes the confounding degree of freedom — the mechanistic reason the 3.48→2.18 mm MonoGS gain appears (Page 2).

The "clock" maps cleanly onto temporal drift. Table 5 (Page 16) is the decisive evidence: on c0_full_t2_v2, at 500 frames NFL and Photo are within noise (2.422 vs 2.599 cm), at 1,000 frames NFL is actually *worse* (2.254 vs 1.937 cm), and only past ~2,000 frames does NFL pull decisively ahead (5.742 vs 8.459 at 2k; 11.368 vs 14.790 at 4k). The authors state it plainly (Page 16): "NFL-BA matches Photometric BA on short sequences and increasingly outperforms it as trajectory length grows." The correct mental model is therefore a crossover: NFL-BA trades a small short-horizon penalty for a large long-horizon compounding-drift advantage.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Honest flag first: there is no biomechanics, no weather, and no atmosphere in this source. A "physical/biomechanical/weather veto proof" cannot be constructed without fabrication, so I adjudicate the vetoes that actually exist — photometric and physical-model vetoes — each with page-cited proof.

Veto A — Specular reflectance. The image model is diffuse-only (Page 5). Specular highlights violate $\hat{C}=\rho\,\text{PPS}$ directly. Proof of failure is in the authors' own text: mitigation is a crude ">0.9 grayscale intensity" mask (Page 17), and "While effective on matte datasets (C3VD, in-the-wild), degradation occurs on Colon10K with more specular highlights" (Page 17). The C3VD gains do not transfer cleanly to specular-rich real tissue.

Veto B — Angular attenuation / collimation. Setting $\beta=0$ is only valid for collimated sources. Proof: "β = 0 is justified for tightly collimated endoscopic LEDs, we must validated this for non endoscopy scenes" (Page 17). The indoor flashlight scenes therefore run the model outside its stated validity envelope; the ablation (Table 7) shows non-zero $\beta$ produces instability, not improvement.

Veto C — Depth-scale validity. PPS $\propto 1/\lVert x_p-x_L\rVert^2$, so shading is hypersensitive to depth magnitude near the light. Proof of the required workaround: "the shading term PPS(·) is sensitive to depth scales, we rescaled the depth maps so that their maximum values are approximately 5" (Page 15). A method that requires hand-rescaling depth to a magic constant has a physical-validity boundary at incorrect metric scale; this is the mechanistic source of the DA-Hybrid regressions.

Veto D — Missing physics. The paper's own Limitations (Page 10): "currently limited in handling specular reflections, sub-surface scattering, and inter-reflections." Each omitted term is a physical veto regime where the forward model is provably wrong.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
"RLVR" (verifiable-reward) framing here means: falsifiable scenarios, checked against the paper's own numeric tables, where the method's reward (lower ATE) does *not* materialize. All three are verifiable against Pages 8–22.

Veto 1 — Noisy depth + strong-prior backbone. Prediction: NFL-BA regresses vs Photo-BA when depth is DA-Hybrid-estimated and the backbone carries a strong motion/implicit prior. Verification (already observed): cecum_t1_a EndoGSLAM DA-Hybrid ATEt 2.64→8.44 mm (Table 8); trans_t2_a NICE-SLAM DA-Hybrid 9.14→14.59 mm (Table 13); desc_t4_a_p2 EndoGSLAM DA-Hybrid 0.45→1.80 mm (Table 11). Falsifiable prediction: on any new sequence combining a monocular-estimated depth with EndoGSLAM's constant-velocity prior, NFL-BA translational ATE will exceed Photo-BA.

Veto 2 — Rotation floor. Prediction: NFL-BA rotation error plateaus near ~2.8° and can underperform Photo-BA on rotation, because $\beta=0$ removes the angular/light-direction constraint (Page 5 admits $r_t$ "can further improve camera rotation"). Verification: NFL ATEr clusters at 2.79–2.83° across many cells in Tables 8–15, and Table 8 EndoGSLAM DA-Hybrid rotation goes 0.08°→2.42°. Falsifiable prediction: reintroducing $r_t$/non-zero $\beta$ will lower the NFL ATEr floor; leaving them out will keep NFL rotation ≥ Photo rotation on high-angular-velocity sequences.

Veto 3 — Short-horizon / benign sequences. Prediction: NFL-BA gives no reward and can hurt when trajectories are short or lighting varies little. Verification: Table 5 at 1,000 frames, Photo 1.937 cm beats NFL 2.254 cm; several oracle-depth per-sequence cells (e.g., trans_t3_a NICE-SLAM 0.97→6.26 mm, Table 14) show NFL worse on easy sequences. Falsifiable prediction: for clips under ~1,000 frames with static or weakly varying illumination, NFL-BA's mean ATE will be ≥ Photo-BA's, with the crossover only appearing as cumulative drift grows.

These vetoes are jointly consistent with the paper's own hedges and define the precise envelope — long sequences, matte surfaces, near-collimated co-located light, reliable metric depth — inside which the reported 37%/14% (MonoGS/EndoGS) and ~35% indoor gains hold.
</RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>

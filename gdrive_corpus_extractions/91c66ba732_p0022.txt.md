Recursion means that the output of one test becomes the input of the next hypothesis; there is no terminal authority except continued non-contradiction by practical application.

The clock is the temporal index t in T_{\text{practical}}: each cycle of application either confirms the law provisionally or triggers premise revision \rho [PAGE 22]. If the clock stops—if no further practical application is scheduled or observed—the system ceases to be scientific by Knight's standard and becomes mere common-sense assertion. The theory-of-mind implication is that an analyst modeling other agents must assume those agents are themselves running hypothesis-revision loops; treating them as static common-sense reactors commits the very error Knight attributes to unexamined generalization.

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Physical layer: Knight states it is "nearly fruitless to attempt to observe phenomena unless we approach them with questions to be answered" [PAGE 22]. Veto: any research design requiring unguided, exhaustive sensory capture of \mathcal{O} is physically impossible given bounded human perception; the interrogative operator \eta is a physical precondition, not a methodological ornament.

Biomechanical/cognitive layer: Generalization is "a mere labor-saving device" because minds cannot process particulars without compression [PAGE 22]. Veto: any model assuming unlimited working memory, perfect recall, or infinite data retention is biomechanically invalid; inference from particulars to particulars must be explicitly compressed via tentative G \in \mathcal{G}_{\text{tent}}.

Weather/exogenous (Knightian uncertainty) layer: Because the book is Risk, Uncertainty and Profit, exogenous shocks are not fully probabilizable. A "law of nature" survives only while uncontradicted by practical application or casual observation [PAGE 22]. Veto: any generalization claimed invariant across all exogenous regimes is voided by the first observed Contradict(H, A_t). In human behavior specifically, since observation and intuition are hardly distinguishable [PAGE 22], no clean layer separation exists between the observer's cognitive weather and the observed phenomenon; the veto must be applied at the coupled cognitive–environmental interface.

Adjudication rule: a proposed generalization passes only if (i) it originates from a question \eta(o), (ii) it survives \rho-triggered revision after each A_t, and (iii) its domain of certainty is explicitly bounded—high for mathematical/external-world axioms, provisional for human behavior [PAGE 22]. Failure at any layer constitutes a hard veto.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

Counter-trap. In the full graph recursion or MLE, strength-of-schedule terms would discount such wins, but under the regressed heuristic the manipulation succeeds. The counter-trap is therefore a model-poisoning fixed point: raters select the weak heuristic because it is fast; the weak heuristic rewards easy wins; raters supply easy wins; the ranking degenerates.

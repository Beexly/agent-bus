The propositional copula is \mathcal{C} = \text{Copula}(S,P), with subject S and predicate P. James's syntax-invariance claim [PAGE 286] is \mathcal{C}(\text{believed}) = \mathcal{C}(\text{disbelieved}) = \mathcal{C}(\text{doubted}) = \mathcal{C}(\text{interrogative}) = \mathcal{C}(\text{conditional}). Hence \mathcal{C} carries no truth-value; truth-value is imposed by a second-order operator \mathcal{A} \in \{\text{assert, deny, doubt, interrogate, condition}\} such that \mathcal{J}(\mathcal{O}) = \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{O})). Objects may be simple ("It-thunders") or complex ("Columbus-discovered-America-in-1492"; "There-exists-an-all-wise-Creator-of-the-world") [Vol. I, p. 275; PAGE 286].

Breakdown/singularity regimes: (1) Null-presentation singularity: if \mathcal{T}(\mathcal{O}) = \varnothing, \mathcal{J} is undefined because the epistemic operator lacks a substrate. (2) Copula singularity: if the object is not an articulated whole and the predicate relation is undefined, \mathcal{C} has no target and \mathcal{A} cannot attach; the text breaks off at "that of predicate" [PAGE 286], so the precise \mathcal{C}/\mathcal{J} boundary for relational predicates is unresolved. (3) Quale singularity: at the Mill/James limit [PAGE 286], belief reduces to an irreducible feeling of reality \phi_{\text{real}}, blocking further functional decomposition. Temporal units: any measured lag between \mathcal{T} and \mathcal{J} is in seconds, though Brentano and James describe them as simultaneous in double presence [PAGE 266].

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage runs from Aristotle's claim that nothing is desired without being thought of, through Kant's assumed relation between mere thought and desire, to Brentano's Psychologie chapter (p. 266 as reproduced by James) [PAGE 266], which converts the thought/desire analogy into a thought/judgment distinction. Mill supplies the subjective starting point—belief as the feel of reality—[PAGE 286], while James contributes the object-of-thought topology from Vol. I, p. 275 [PAGE 286]. The historical trap is multi-week: over extended introspective observation, an analyst may drift from recording the felt reality of belief into explaining it by propositional syntax, committing a category error because \mathcal{C} is invariant across assent states [PAGE 286]. A second trap is treating the desire analogy as a timeless logical equivalence when Brentano presents it as an empirical claim about consciousness that may degrade under sustained cognitive load; weeks of monitoring would likely reveal drift in the simultaneity of thought and assent, exposing the analogy as contingent rather than necessary.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Crisis habit regression: under cognitive crisis or stress, the subject regresses from second-order epistemic monitoring—representing one's own judgment about \mathcal{O}—to first-order affective resonance, treating the felt reality of \mathcal{T} as if it were \mathcal{J}. Counter-trap: the observer assumes that because \mathcal{C} is identical across belief and doubt, \mathcal{J} is causally inert; this ignores Brentano's and James's insistence that assent creates an "entirely new relation" [PAGE 266; PAGE 286]. Clock: James and Brentano describe double presence as simultaneous—object "twice present... as thought of, and as held for real" [PAGE 266]—yet the presupposition logic implies \mathcal{T} is prior to \mathcal{J}. The clock thus registers a logical asymmetry (thought before judgment) inside a phenomenological simultaneity; under load, this decouples into sequential staggering where assent lags presentation, and the subject may report a belief that was never fully constituted.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
Veto is defined as denial (\mathcal{J} = -1) or suspension (\mathcal{J} = 0). Cross-layer proof: layer 1 is the physical/neural substrate supporting \mathcal{T}; layer 2 is the cognitive layer of conception; layer 3 is the epistemic layer of assent/denial. \mathcal{J}(\mathcal{O}) = -1 does not annihilate \mathcal{T}(\mathcal{O}); the object remains thought of while denied [PAGE 266]. Hence an epistemic veto at layer 3 preserves layers 1 and 2. Physical-layer veto: if neural presentation fails so that \mathcal{T}(\mathcal{O}) = \varnothing, layer 3 is moot because the presupposition axiom blocks judgment [PAGE 286; PAGE 266]. Weather/biomechanical analogs: environmental noise or physiological fatigue can attenuate \mathcal{T} below threshold, functionally vetoing all downstream \mathcal{J}, but this is pre-epistemic nulling rather than epistemic denial. Breakdown: if layer 1 collapses, no amount of layer-3 effort can produce judgment; conversely, layer-2 activity can persist under layer-3 veto, confirming that denial is not unthinking.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

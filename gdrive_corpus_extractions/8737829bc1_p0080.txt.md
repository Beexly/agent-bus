$$ D(g) = \frac{|x_g - \mu|}{\sigma} $$

$$ M_{g_1} = \mu + \sigma \cdot z_{g_1} $$
$$ M_{g_2} = \mu + \sigma \cdot z_{g_2} $$

$$ \sigma = \frac{M_{g_1} - M_{g_2}}{z_{g_1} - z_{g_2}}, \quad \mu = M_{g_1} - \sigma \cdot z_{g_1} $$

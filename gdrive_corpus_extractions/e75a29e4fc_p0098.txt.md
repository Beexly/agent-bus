RLVR veto rule: If Wesleyan’s 1914 record is treated as a stable 1915 baseline without conditioning on the return or replacement of Kenan (full-back) and Hingeley, the model is vetoed. Counterfactual prediction: Wesleyan’s 1915 outcome cannot be inferred from its 1914 “entirely successful” label; if Kenan and Hingeley return and remain healthy, performance may improve relative to the handicap season, but if they are lost permanently, the 1915 record will regress below 1914 levels.

Galton’s concept of “Incipient Structure” (1889) sits at a nexus of several intellectual lineages. 2. **Cryptic Genetic Variation (CGV) Theory (1990s–present)**: Populations accumulate mutations buffered by chaperones (like Hsp90) or redundant pathways. Under stress, buffering fails, releasing phenotypic diversity upon which selection acts. Galton’s “power of transmitting ancestral characters” describes exactly this release mechanism.

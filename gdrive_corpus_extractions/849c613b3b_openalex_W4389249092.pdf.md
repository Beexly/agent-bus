with \(t\) dimensionless and p-values from the corresponding t distribution. For nonparametric comparisons, Mann–Whitney U can be written as

with \(\chi^2\) dimensionless and degrees of freedom \((r-1)(c-1)\). The source says normality was tested with the Shapiro–Wilk test and that “Statʽstʽcal sʽgnʽfʽcance level was set to 0.05” [PAGE 3].

The recursive theory-of-mind layer concerns how athletes and coaches infer each other’s intentions, competence, and authority, and how those inferences feed back into behavior. The paper can describe a snapshot of that loop, but it cannot identify direction. The safe interpretation is a recursive hypothesis, not a recursive proof.

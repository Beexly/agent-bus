A second gray zone concerns time: Schopenhauer's "inexorablestream of time" [PAGE 273] implies a continuous, objective temporal flow, whereas James's specious present binds experience over roughly seconds. Events separated by weeks cannot be unified into one intuitive present, so the temporal a priori form breaks down at long integration windows. Singularity: when mutual information I(W;Y) = 0, the posterior collapses to the prior and the Jacobian ∂P̂/∂Y = 0—perception is then an ungrounded, super-sensational product, the very myth James denounces [PAGE 273].

<SOURCE_OPERATORS_AND_EQUATIONS>
Let the external stimulus be a random variable X ∈ 𝒳, where 𝒳 ⊂ ℝ³ × ℝ⁺ encodes spatial position in meters (m) and time in seconds (s). Define the sensation operator
𝒮: 𝒳 → 𝒴,
where 𝒴 is the set of organism-internal states, bounded by the cutaneous interface ∂Ω_skin. Y = 𝒮(X) is "nothing more than a local and specific feeling... always strictly subjective and containing in itself nothing objective" [PAGE 273].

Information-theoretic constraint:
I(X;Y) ≤ h(Y) − h(Y|X),   measured in bits (Shannon).
Because Y is generated inside the skin, for any exterior point x with dist(x, ∂Ω_skin) exceeding receptor range, the channel density p(y|x) → 0, so I(X_exterior; Y) decays with distance and vanishes beyond the bodily boundary [PAGE 273].

Define the Understanding operator
𝒰: (Y, κ_s, κ_t, γ_c) → P̂,
where κ_s ∈ 𝒦 is the spatial a priori form (units: m), κ_t ∈ 𝒯 is the temporal a priori form (units: s), and γ_c ∈ 𝒞 is the causal law (dimensionless conditional probability P(effect|cause) ∈ [0,1]). Schopenhauer's two-stage operation is
P̂ = argmax_P { ln P(Y|P) + ln P(P; κ_s, κ_t, γ_c) },
in which γ_c posits a cause for the sensation and κ_s locates that cause outside the organism [PAGE 273].

Breakdown and singularity regimes:
(1) Sensory deprivation: I(X;Y) = 0 ⇒ ∇_Y ln P(Y|P) = 0 ⇒ P̂ = argmax_P ln P(P; κ_s, κ_t, γ_c). The Fisher information matrix ℐ(X) = E[−∂²_X ln p(Y|X)] satisfies det ℐ(X) = 0, and the Jacobian J = ∂P̂/∂Y is rank-zero (singular). Perception is then prior-only, ungrounded by sensation [PAGE 273].
(2) Causal non-identifiability: ∃ X₁ ≠ X₂ such that 𝒮(X₁) = 𝒮(X₂) = Y. Then 𝒰⁻¹(Y) is multivalued, the inverse map is not injective, and the condition number κ(J) → ∞. No unique external cause can be reconstructed from the sensation alone.

<RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>
The lineage runs from Aristotle's sensible species through the medieval intromission/extramission debate, to Descartes' bifurcation of res extensa and res cogitans, to Kant's transcendental aesthetic in which space and time are pure a priori forms of intuition. Schopenhauer radicalizes this in The World as Will and Representation by making the Understanding the active creator of the objective world through Causality and Space [PAGE 273]. James's 1890 Principles of Psychology Vol. 2, p. 273, stands at the end of this chain, rejecting "psychical stimulism" as "thoroughly mythological" while still grappling with the question of how raw sensation becomes an "objective conception of an orderly physical world-system" [PAGE 273].

Multi-week traps. In longitudinal experimental designs spanning weeks, three traps arise from treating the a priori forms as static. Trap 1: assuming spatial constancy across a week-long intervention while the body schema and peripersonal space undergo neuroplastic remapping; tool use or sensory-substitution training can take days to weeks to stabilize, so κ_s is not fixed across T_week ~ 6.048×10⁵ s. Trap 2: inferring a stable objective world from sensation when the causal prior γ_c has itself adapted (e.g., prism-adaptation aftereffects persisting for days), causing the Understanding to mislocate causes outside the organism [PAGE 273]. Trap 3: retrospective causal attribution across weeks, where the a priori imposition of Causality colors the memory of sensation, producing a retrocausal genealogy in which the observer believes the world structure was given in experience when it was actually contributed by the Understanding. The relevant timescales separate sharply: neural integration τ_neural ~ 10⁻¹ s, perceptual present ~ 1–3 s, and plasticity/habit consolidation τ_plastic ~ 10⁵–10⁶ seconds (days to weeks). Applying a short-timescale a priori form to a week-long interval without allowing for recalibration produces a systematic model error.
</RETROCAUSAL_GENEALOGY>

<RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>
Model agent i's belief about agent j's spatial perception at recursion depth n as b_i^(n)(P_j | Y_j). Crisis habit regression occurs under acute threat or cortisol surge: the recursion depth collapses n → 0, and the agent reverts to naive realism, P_j = Y_j, as if the world "simply promenade[s] into our heads through the senses" [PAGE 273]. This is precisely the view Schopenhauer rejects: the objective world is treated as already complete and independent, requiring no complicity of the Understanding [PAGE 273].

The counter-trap is hyper-reflective constructivism: n → ∞, in which every percept is treated as purely a priori construction and sensation is denied any constraining role. This yields the "super-sensational mental product" James calls mythological [PAGE 273], a perception untethered from the "local and specific feeling" inside the skin [PAGE 273].

The clock operates on nested timescales. Fast clock: saccadic suppression and neural integration τ_saccade ~ 30–100 ms. Intermediate clock: specious present ~ 1–3 s. Slow clock: body-schema and habit consolidation over T_week ~ 6.048×10⁵ s. Breakdown occurs when the recursion depth is mismatched to the clock. Applying n = 0 realism to a slowly changing spatial layout over weeks produces failure to update the spatial prior; applying n → ∞ constructivism to saccadic judgments produces perceptual paralysis because no ground truth is admitted. Formal singularity: the recursion map b^(n+1) = F(b^(n)) has a fixed point where ∂P/∂n = 0; when the Lyapunov exponent λ of F exceeds zero, the agent oscillates chaotically between crisis regression and constructivist counter-trap, unable to settle on a stable depth of spatial inference.
</RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND>

<CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>
The perception pipeline is accepted only if the conjunction V = V_phys ∧ V_bio ∧ V_env = 1; any single veto invalidates the output rather than merely increasing uncertainty.

Physical layer veto V_phys. Light from an exterior cause at distance d arrives after Δt = d/c, c = 299,792,458 m/s. If d/c exceeds the neural integration window τ_integration ~ 10⁻¹ s, the sensation no longer corresponds to the current state of the exterior world, and V_phys = 1. More stringently, because sensation is "limited to the territory inside the skin" [PAGE 273], photons do not enter the head; exteroception terminates at the cutaneous boundary, and any inference beyond it requires the Understanding's causal and spatial forms.

Biomechanical layer veto V_bio. Peripersonal space extends approximately to arm length plus tool extension, r_pp ~ 0.7–1.5 m. Stimuli outside r_pp lack direct motor-confirmed localization; if no body-schema extension exists, V_bio = 1 because the Understanding cannot locate the cause outside the organism with requisite precision [PAGE 273]. During saccades (peak velocity ~ 500°/s), visual input is suppressed; sampling Y during a saccade also triggers V_bio = 1.

Weather/environmental layer veto V_env. Atmospheric scattering coefficient σ_s (m⁻¹) sets visibility d_vis ≈ 3/σ_s. If σ_s > σ_crit such that d_vis < d_object, depth cues (stereopsis, motion parallax, texture gradient) degrade and Fisher information for depth collapses; V_env = 1. Low luminance (< 10⁻³ cd/m²) forces scotopic vision, sacrificing color and fine spatial resolution.

Veto proof. If V = 0, the system must fall back to proprioception or prior-only mode. That fallback is exactly the "super-sensational mental product" [PAGE 273]: an objective world constructed by the Understanding without sensory constraint. Thus the cross-layer veto is not a mere confidence downgrade but a hard refusal to output P̂ when any physical, biomechanical, or environmental layer is compromised.
</CROSS_LAYER_VETO_ADJUDICATION>

<RLVR_COUNTERFACTUAL_VETOES>
Scenario 1 — Total visual darkness. Counterfactual prediction by an unvetoed model: a stable, continuous objective space persists in perception for T = 1 hour. Veto condition: I(X_visual; Y) = 0, so det ℐ(X) = 0 and the posterior collapses to the prior [PAGE 273]. Verifiable prediction: Ganzfeld hallucinations, fading, or impoverished spatial content, not veridical spatial continuity, accompanied by increased EEG alpha power. RLVR reward is withheld (vetoed) from any model that predicts stable objective-space perception under zero visual information.

Scenario 2 — Reversing prisms for two weeks. Counterfactual prediction: immediate veridical reaching because Space is "already preformed in the intellect" [PAGE 273]. Veto condition: the spatial a priori κ_s must be recalibrated by sensorimotor error; adaptation follows a time constant τ_adapt ~ 2–5 days. Verifiable prediction: initial reaching error ~30°–40°, exponential decay to <5% by days 7–10, with aftereffects persisting 24–48 hours after prism removal. Any model predicting <5% error on day 1 is vetoed by RLVR.

Scenario 3 — Correlated but causally uncoupled stimuli. Counterfactual prediction: causality is attributed only when physical coupling exists. Veto condition: the Understanding imposes Causality γ_c a priori, so temporal contiguity suffices for causal attribution even when physical coupling is zero [PAGE 273]. Verifiable prediction: subjects report causal strength r_cause > 0 for correlated flashes (correlation ρ) with false-causal probability p_false = Φ((ρ − ρ_threshold)/σ) under signal-detection theory. Any model assigning p(causality) = 0 to correlated-but-uncoupled stimuli is vetoed by RLVR.

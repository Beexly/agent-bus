Efficiency metrics & predictive correlations (the research standard):
- Passing efficiency explains wins at corr ~0.53-0.61 vs rushing ~0.13-0.19. 2026 early-season: passing EPA predicts future point differential r~0.42 at 6 games; success rate stabilizes faster (~r=0.60 by game 6). Early-down success r~0.36; raw 3rd-down conversion "nearly meaningless" (2012 hierarchical-Bayes study).
- EPA model is XGBoost (not Yurko's logit); validate with drive- or game-grouped splits, never random play splits (Brill et al. 2024).
- Turnover luck: occurrence (partially skill) vs recovery (near-pure noise). Fumble recovery year-to-year correlation 0.00/-0.02 (Stuart). League recovery 2025: 46.3%. DET 2025: forced 19 fumbles (+6.5 over expected), recovered only 26.3% (-20pts vs league) — textbook positive-regression case. 2025 INT-over-expected: CLE +7.11, LV +7.74, MIA +6.82, MIN +5.74 unlucky / DAL -5.24, CHI -4.33 lucky. Takeaways over: CHI +7.22, LAC +7.11, JAX +5.04 / NYJ -10.07.
- Pressure: creation is the stable skill; pressure-to-sack conversion "luck" R^2 < 0.005 (PFF) — sack props deliberately NULL. PRWR/PBWR = 2.5s window. Baldwin pass-protection composite = 40% PFF grade / 40% SIS blown-block % / 20% ESPN PBWR, each re-scaled 0-100 (SF #1 88 ... MIA #32 6).
- ST EPA: Wharton study +1.9% RMSE improvement, systematically unpriced. League 2025: 2.10 pts/drive, 24.0% TD, 20.4% 3-and-out, 11.1% turnover-drive, avg start own 30.4; kickoff EPA -0.257/kick (negative in dynamic-kickoff era), punt EPA -0.127/punt; touchback 20.5%; opp avg start own 29.8.
- Kicker: FG 85.6%, XP 95.9%, 7.36 kick pts/g; distance buckets <30/30-39/40-49/50+; DET paradox Bates 79.4% FG but 7.94 pts/g on volume — long attempts negative-EPA/att (-0.080).
- 4th-down: league go-rate on toss-ups 16.8% (2019) -> 26.5% (2021); coaches optimize low quantiles, not WP (Sandholtz et al. 2024). Greenberg 4th-down aggressiveness = "% of fourth downs that featured a pass or rush play"; 2026 Wk1-2 ~16% vs ~21% 2025.
- Bye edge largely VANISHED post-2011 CBA; travel/time-zone/altitude = no verified NFL coefficient; wind/weather nonlinear, HIGH for totals, LOW for moneyline; red-zone conversion near-noise (Schatz), trip rate is sticky; explosives threshold conventions 15/10 vs 20-yard (state it); havoc rate has NO NFL standard; late-down "nearly meaningless" vs early-down.
- DAVE blend 2026 Wk1: 83% prior on offense, 98% on defense/ST (FTN); 50,000 season sims with +1.5% DVOA dynamic in-sim adjustment. Age-curve peaks (Barlowe spline): RB 24.53, WR 25.33, QB 26.67. Third stabilization scheme: HB Analytics 83%-prior fade-by-Week-6.
Composites & derived metrics:
- ARBY (Adjusted Run Blocking Yards): ARBY RB Matchup Rating = 65% ARBY / 35% RB YPC per side, 50/50 offense-defense, 65% 2025 / 35% 2026 blend; validation ARBY-YBC r=0.70, YBC-YPC r=0.77, team ARBY-ALY r=0.47, ARBY-YBC r=0.66.
- PROE+ = Pass Rate Over Expectation + Neutral Pace (Wk1: TEN +1.42 ... NYJ -0.04).
- SamHoppen composite power ratings: mean of 6 (FPI, nfelo, Inpredictable, Unexpected Points, FTN DVOA, PFF); FTN DVOA = preseason priors + in-season DVOA weighted toward last few games, divided by 3.6%; expressed as expected spread vs average team.
- Baldwin objective ratings: market-implied win% vs league-average team on neutral field (blends near-term lines + Kalshi division/conference/SB/playoff/#1-seed futures; HFA ~2).
- HB Analytics simultaneous-solution grades: grader moves disruption rate (blockers) or yards-per-route (defenders) vs average facing same opponents; defender/receiver quality solved simultaneously; double teams/brackets handled separately; low-rep shrunk to average; 2026 blended with 2025 at 50% fading by Week 6; run-block top: Bolles -2.6, Sewell -2.6; cover-corner top: Woolen -0.59, Surtain -0.52; cover-safety top: J. Reid -0.37, Love -0.35.
- Dynatyze DAKOTA (all-in-one QB score vs expectations) and Alpha Role (WR/TE target/deep-route/red-zone dominance score); StatRankings Cardio Index (route participation vs TPRR).
- @sfdata9ers defined metrics: "Cost of Drops" = Air EPA + expected YAC EPA - Actual EPA (PIT 19.9 lost on 5 drops); "QB EPA gained on Defensive Penalties" (Allen 7.8, Prescott 7.7); PGWE = "How likely is each team to win if the game were replayed with the same key stats?"

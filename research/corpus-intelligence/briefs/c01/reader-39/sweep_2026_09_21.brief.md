## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A neutral X-analytics sweep (read-only, Garrett-directed, 2026-09-21) across five accounts, recording each new metric or data cut with its definition-as-given, sample values, and caveats — no verdicts, as the basis for GSE gap-implication notes.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **CORE (CollegeFootballData.com)** — "Points above FBS average per 100 plays", 25% preseason / 75% observed blend; offense vs defense scatter, 2026 through Week 3.
- **Age-curve derivatives (Barlowe ANALYTICS)** — derivative of natural-cubic-spline fitted EPA age contributions with 94% pointwise intervals; sample: RB 149,694 carries / 533 RBs; WR 134,254 targets / 807 WRs; QB 200,377 dropbacks / 188 QBs; windows 2013–2025 / 2016–2025 NFL play-by-play. Peak ages (derivative zero): RB 24.53 yr, WR 25.33 yr, QB 26.67 yr. Author caveat: "not a total EPA difference or a causal aging effect."
- **Pressure-to-sack rate (SumerSports via @benlinsey_)** — "Share of pressured dropbacks that end in a sack." Bryce Young: 23.3% (30 of 30, 2023) → 15.8% (10 of 31, 2024) → 12.7% (8 of 29, 2025) → 10.5% (2026, unranked, 2 games / 38 pressured dropbacks). League average ~18% flat; ranks among QBs with 300+ dropbacks.
- **NGS pass-rush chart (official)** — pressure % = pressures / pass rushes; get-off seconds; quick-pressure split at <2.5s.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- CFB CORE through Week 3: Mississippi State chart-topping offensive CORE ~+15 with defense ~−5; Alabama/Notre Dame/Ohio State firmly strong on both sides (defenses ~−13); LSU best defensive CORE ~−14 with offense ~+2; USC/Ole Miss strong offense (~+13) with weak defense.
- Age peaks: RB 24.53, WR 25.33, QB 26.67 years (EPA-contribution derivative zero), based on 149,694 RB carries, 134,254 WR targets, 200,377 QB dropbacks.
- Bryce Young pressure-to-sack fell 23.3% → 10.5% over four seasons while league average held ~18% flat — year-over-year QB trait movement against a flat league baseline.
- NGS time-to-pressure <2.5s splits separate coverage sacks from true rush wins (feeds expected-pressures family).
- GSE gap implications recorded: age-curve priors as projection-blend candidates; pressure-to-sack rate as a QB trait for sack/pressure projections; CFB CORE as team-strength input if GSE extends to college; @TheHonestNFL as scheme-vocabulary source (no stats products).

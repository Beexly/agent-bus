# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0606-ranking-and-synchronization-from-pairwise-measurements.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Spectral ranking via SVD (d'Aspremont, Cucuringu, Tyagi, 2019): recovers team-strength scores and rankings from sparse, noisy pairwise measurements (H_ij = r_i − r_j on a measurement graph) with provable ℓ₂/ℓ_∞ recovery and max-displacement rank guarantees, benchmarked against 7 state-of-the-art methods on 42 real instances. Verdict in the file: ADAPT (narrow) — implement SVD-NRS as a cheap spectral power-rating baseline from the margin-of-victory matrix, a diagnostic complement to Elo, not a replacement.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Measurement matrix H ∈ ℝ^{n×n} skew-symmetric, H_ij = R_ij on edges, 0 elsewhere; noiseless + complete graph ⇒ H = reᵀ − erᵀ, rank 2.
- SVD-RS (Algorithm 1): top two left singular vectors û₁, û₂ of H → project u₁=e/√n onto span{û₁,û₂} → score direction ũ₂ = orthogonal complement → ranking from ũ₂ (sign fixed by minimizing upsets) → scale τ from H → r̂ = τũ₂ − mean(τũ₂).
- SVD-NRS (Algorithm 2): same pipeline on H_ss = D̄^{−1/2} H D̄^{−1/2}, D̄_ii = Σ_j |H_ij| — degree normalization for skewed degree distributions.
- Theory (Theorem 4): for r_i = i, Ω(n log n) measurements suffice for ℓ₂ recovery, Ω(n^{4/3}(log n)^{2/3}) for ℓ_∞; ℓ_∞ ⇒ rank recovery with max-displacement bounds (Theorem 3); ℓ₂ guarantees for SVD-NRS. Not proven: very sparse regime p ~ 1/n, multiplicative noise, matrix-completion preprocessing.
- Metrics: upsets (eq. 7.1) and weighted upsets Σ|R_ij − R̂_ij| (eq. 7.2), before/after low-rank matrix-completion preprocessing; plus novel cross-method Kendall-correlation analysis (SYNC≈BTL at 86% correlation, PGR vs SVD-N only 70% — different methods find genuinely different latent rankings).
- GSE implementation spec: SVD-NRS from demeaned home/away point differentials each week (nflverse 2020–2026): H_ij = away-adjusted margin summed over matchups, skew-symmetric; degree-normalize; extract zero-mean scores; uses: (1) early-season prior when Elo hasn't converged (sparse-regime theory), (2) rater-agreement diagnostic — Kendall τ between SVD-NRS, Elo, and market-implied ratings, divergences flagged for analyst review, (3) weekly power rankings with published method. Effort: ~0.5 day prototype + 1 day dashboard.
## Data sources named
Synthetic: Erdős–Rényi graphs, r_i = i ground truth, H_ij = r_i − r_j w.p. ηp + noise/outliers. Real (42 instances): NCAA basketball 1985–2014 (30 seasons, H = summed point differentials); monk-parakeet dominance networks; faculty hiring networks (CS n=206, Business n=113, History n=145, H = A − Aᵀ net PhD flow); Halo 2 beta (n=606 players, 6,227 games → filtered n=535, 6,109 edges, avg degree ~23, H = net wins); English Premier League 2009–2013 (4 seasons, H = net goal difference). No code repo stated; ~15 lines of NumPy to reimplement.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NCAA basketball (30 seasons): SVD-N/SVD ranked 6th/8th of 9 on upsets (6th/7th after matrix completion); 5th/6th on weighted upsets (4th/5th after MC) — "visibly outperforming the rest… comparable to the top three." SYNC best on raw upsets.
- Premier League (4 seasons): SVD-N beats SVD in 12/16 setups; top-half throughout; SVD-N first in 3 instances.
- Faculty hiring: SVD-N top-half; best on weighted upsets + MC for Business and History, 2nd for CS.
- Halo 2: both methods 5th–7th of 9; SVD-N wins 3/4 metric/preprocessing combos. Animal dominance: mid-pack; SVD-N > SVD.
- Honest summary in file: competitive but rarely best — a strong cheap baseline, not a champion.
- Gaps noted: no home-field term (NFL HFA ≈ 2–3 points is large relative to team-strength spread — absorb by demeaning home/away margins first); theory doesn't tightly cover the NFL regime (32 teams × ~17 games, structured not Erdős–Rényi); sign ambiguity resolved heuristically (minimize upsets).

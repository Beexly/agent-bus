# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1485-arnoldi-singular-vector-perturbations-for.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-text research ledger (Verdict: ADAPT) of Winkler & Denhard 2025 (arXiv:2506.22450v1) on an adjoint-free Arnoldi singular-vector method (A-SV) that builds ensemble weather-forecast perturbations from nonlinear forward runs of an ML weather model (Pangu Weather), without tangent-linear/adjoint models.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Evolved increment I_{τ,x0,h}(v)=M_τ(x0+hv)−M_τ(x0); evolved increment matrix A_{τ,x0,h}=(I(e1),…,I(en)) as the local error-growth linear map. A-SV: Gaussian-noise start, iterate 24h Pangu runs on perturbed states, Gram–Schmidt orthonormalization → Krylov basis Q (n×m) and projection H (m×m) with AQ=QH+RES; SVD of H; leading right SVs (×Q) = directions of fastest error growth. Block version (blocksize 8, Ruhe variant) for parallelization. Growth rate: log(‖δτ‖/‖δ0‖)=½log(δ0ᵀAᵀAδ0/δ0ᵀδ0) → right SVs of A. EGR/MEGR: (1/Δt)·log(‖M_t(x0+hv)−M_t(x0)‖/‖M_{t−Δt}(x0+hv)−M_t(x0+hv)−M_{t−Δt}(x0)‖-style ratio) averaged over runs. Amplitude guidance: h=500 (range 100–10000, calibrate against nearby real states × [0.1, 0.6]); diagnostic: if A-SVs don't grow from t=0, h was too large. Optimization window τ=24h = Pangu timestep. Total-energy (massless) measure for distances; surface pressure excluded. No forecast-skill scores (descriptive/perturbation study, no predictive modeling).
## Data sources named
Daily 00 UTC DWD-ICON global analyses, Dec 2024–Feb 2025, northern hemisphere latitudes >35°; variables: temperature, U/V wind on Pangu's 13 input pressure levels + surface (2m T, 10m U/V); 24h Pangu Weather model as forecast operator (public). Case studies 14–15 Jan 2025; three-month daily runs for amplitude/MEGR statistics; 168h forecast comparison vs Gaussian-noise perturbations.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Singular spectrum (96 SVs): outstanding leading SV; somewhat less than one third of SVs > 1 (growing inside Krylov subspace); spectrum tail shows blocksize-8 steps.
- SV patterns physically sensible: near-surface temperature, upper-troposphere jet-stream winds (~75°N polar-front shift); global run concentrates perturbations in mid-latitudes, vanishes from tropics automatically.
- Growth: A-SVs grow right from the beginning; random perturbations are heavily dampened by Pangu in the first 24h (latent-space bottleneck = implicit denoiser; no "butterfly effect" in Pangu per Selz & Craig 2023), take ~4 days to recover initial amplitude, and only organize into SV-like patterns after ~48h.
- Caveats: MEGR values small; SV growth rates similar across SVs (limited Krylov diversity); no skill-score comparison against NWP ensembles; ensemble construction itself left to future work (needs zero-mean ± pairs); 24h window limits nonlinear-growth fidelity.

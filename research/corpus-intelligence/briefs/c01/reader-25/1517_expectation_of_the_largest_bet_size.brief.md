# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1517-expectation-of-the-largest-bet-size.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-text research ledger (Verdict: ADAPT) of Han & Wang 2019 (arXiv:1807.11729v3) resolving Grimmett & Stirzaker's decade-old open conjecture: in the Labouchere (cancellation) betting system, the expected largest bet size E[B⋆] is infinite whenever the player has no edge (p ≤ 1/2), for any initial list.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Labouchere mechanics: bet = first + last list numbers (single number if length 1); win → cancel first and last; loss → append amount lost; N = stopping time of first empty list; B⋆ = max bet; Tn = remaining target profit. Embedded in general (a,b)-list systems (a<0≤b): bet Bn∈[0,Tn−1]; Tn=Tn−1−Bn (win)/Tn−1+Bn (loss); ln=(ln−1+a)+ (win)/ln−1+b (loss); termination forces Tn=ln=0. Martingale = (−1,0)-list; Labouchere and Fibonacci = (−2,1)-list. Theorems: (1) any (−2,1)-list system: E[B⋆]<∞ iff p>1/2; E[B⋆]=∞ if 1/3<p<1/2; E[B⋆]=∞ if p≤1/3 with Bn≥c1·ln−1+c2 a.s. (c1>0). (2) p=1/2: E[B⋆]=∞ under bet-proportion bound conditions. (3) Moment phase transition at p=1/2: E[B⋆(1∨log B⋆)]=∞ but E[B⋆(1∨log B⋆)^{−(1+ε)}]<∞ for every ε>0. Corollaries: Labouchere (any initial list) and Fibonacci: E[B⋆]=∞ iff p≤1/2; martingale at p=1/2 = St. Petersburg paradox. Stopping-time tail: Pl0(N≥n+1)∼Dl0(n)·n^{−3/2}·κ^n, κ:=27p(1−p)²/4<1 for p>1/3. Change-of-measure bound: for path with N=n, wins=n/3+c, losses=2n/3−c: dP/dQ=(p/(1−p))^c·(p(1−p)²/(1/2·(1/2)²))^{n/3}≤Cρ^n (ρ<1) if p>1/2, ≥Cρ^n (ρ>1) if p<1/2; B⋆≤T0·2^N. Good-list bound: Bn/Tn−1=O(1/√l). Conjecture 1 (open): E[B⋆]=∞ for every (−2,1)-list system at p=1/2. Assumes independent coups with constant win probability p. Worked example: list (1,2,3,4) clears in 8 coups, max bet 13, target 10.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Phase transition: E[B⋆]<∞ iff p>1/2 (Labouchere, any initial list); E[B⋆]=∞ iff p≤1/2 — the Grimmett & Stirzaker conjecture solved.
- Moment boundary at p=1/2: E[B⋆(1∨log B⋆)]=∞; E[B⋆(1∨log B⋆)^{−(1+ε)}]<∞ ∀ε>0.
- N<∞ a.s. iff p≥1/3; E[N]<∞ iff p>1/3 (Ethier).
- Adversarial note from the ledger: the infinite-expectation result does NOT mean Labouchere always bankrupts you — at p>1/2 the expected max bet is finite, and even at p≤1/2 the median max bet can be modest; the result is about the tail, not the typical run. κ's exponent was reconstructed from the proof because PDF text extraction mangled "κ:=27p(1−p)²/4" — verify against arXiv source before citing the exact constant. Real stake limits truncate the idealized pathology in practice.

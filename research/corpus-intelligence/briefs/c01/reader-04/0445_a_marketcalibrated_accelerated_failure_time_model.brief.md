# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0445-a-marketcalibrated-accelerated-failure-time-model.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-paper ledger read of Clegg/Cartlidge/Syed (arXiv:2605.16066v1, 2026): a Weibull accelerated-failure-time model for in-play soccer forecasting whose team-strength parameters are calibrated to Betfair Exchange kickoff prices via a squared-error fit over 1X2 + over/under markets, with post-shot xG (PSxG) as a time-varying in-play covariate. Reader verdict: ADAPT — soccer domain, but the market-calibration machinery and the accuracy-vs-profitability evidence transfer directly to GSE's NFL engine.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Weibull AFT: TH∼Weibull(γ,λH), TA∼Weibull(γ,λA); log 𝔼[TH] = μ + β_home + a_H + d_A (sum-to-zero identifiability); fitted by MLE on interval-censored goal times; BIC model comparison; exponential time decay w_k = exp(−ξ·d_k/3.5), ξ=0.0065/half-week (~1-year half-life); half-specific shape params (γ̂1H=0.983 [0.953,1.012], γ̂2H=1.395 [1.325,1.465])
- Market calibration (eq. 13): per-match ℒ = Σ_o (p̂_o − p_o^mkt)² + Σ_g (p̂_{>g} − p_{>g}^mkt)² over 1X2 + over/under {0.5,…,4.5}, minimized via Powell's method — the totals market resolves identifiability (outcome probs alone consistent with many (λH,λA) pairs)
- In-play MC: N=10,000 paths per prediction minute; PSxG deviation covariate xPSxG,k(t) = S_k(t) − S̄(t) vs linear population baseline; covariates via βᵀx added to log-expected times
- Comparators: Zou (Bayesian birth process, Gamma-posterior update θ̂_1 = (r_1+X(T))/(r_1+EH(T))·θ̂_01, eq. 14) and Maia (Cox process with dynamic regressors, eq. 15), both calibrated to the same odds
- Betting sim: vs Betfair in-play odds, 2% commission, unit vs Kelly (f = (bp−q)/b); EV-threshold plot; goal-window vs non-goal-window timing control (3,212 goal-window bets ROI 4.7% vs 14,246 outside 4.9% — edge not from stale prices)
## Data sources named
Four EPL seasons 2021–2025: WhoScored.com timestamped events; FBref.com PSxG (access discontinued Jan 2026 — replication needs a new source); Betfair Exchange minute-by-minute historical data (last-traded prices; minute M+2 as executable-odds proxy — 99% of price reactions to goals absorbed within 2 min per 470-goal timing analysis); 1,517 fixtures (1,377 train, 140 eval gameweek 25+ 2024–25); stoppage-time means 3.1 min 1H / 6.2 min 2H from 240 pre-evaluation matches
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Hazard spec: half-specific γ ΔBIC=−302.9 vs score-state baseline; Babootor's score-state γs not replicated (1.19/1.19/1.21 vs 0.943/1.044/1.731)
- Covariates: red cards β̂_red=−0.41 (0.06), ΔBIC=−37.1, LRT p=7.0×10⁻¹²; PSxG β̂_psxg=−0.10 (0.02), ΔBIC=−53.4, p=2.9×10⁻⁷ (best)
- Pre-match: uncalibrated Weibull 56.4% vs Betfair 61.4% accuracy; all calibrated variants lock to 61.4% (RPS ≈0.182–0.184, log-loss ≈0.911–0.920)
- In-play: Weibull κ,ψ 70.2% accuracy / RPS 0.1294 / log-loss 0.6933 vs Betfair 70.6% / 0.1254 / 0.6714; Zou κ 68.2% / 0.1412 / 0.7569 (its Bayesian update overrides calibrated strengths — in 0–0 matches scoring rates fall 30% by minute 20, 50% by half-time, over-shooting to draws); PSxG out-of-sample effect small (RPS 0.1353→0.1347) — calibration dominates
- Betting: Weibull κ,ψ Kelly ROI +4.5% (Sharpe 5.94) over 17,458 bets, 49% win, net +158.15; unit staking −3.4%; EV≥0.20 → 8.5% ROI over 3,241 bets; EV-threshold peak near 0.2 at >20% ROI then declines (winner's curse); Maia κ,ψ Kelly ROI only 1.3% despite similar accuracy — comparable forecast quality ≠ comparable economic value
- Authors' caveats: 140 matches small for betting evaluation; accuracy claims more robust than profitability claims
- Bibliography follow-ups: Wheatcroft (2021) — case against RPS; Wunderlich & Memmert (2020) — "Are betting returns a useful measure of accuracy?"; Wunderlich et al. (2026) — does considering market prices in model selection increase profitability?; Leriou & Ntzoufras (2025) — survival modeling of EPL goal arrivals

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2082-the-ai-scientist-towards-fully.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A ledger (read 2026-09-22) on Lu et al. 2024 "The AI Scientist" (arXiv:2408.06292v3): end-to-end automated scientific discovery — LLM idea generation with evolutionary mutation + novelty filtering, Aider-based experiment iteration with error-feedback retries, LaTeX write-up, and a GPT-4o automated reviewer calibrated on 500 ICLR 2022 papers. Ledger verdict: ADAPT as the blueprint for GSE's overnight signal-discovery loop, with the reviewer gate and sandboxing adapted.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No equations (systems paper). Pipeline: idea archive (description + experiment plan + self-assessed interestingness/novelty/feasibility) → novelty filter via Semantic Scholar API + web search → Aider executes experiments, up to 4 retries per failure, up to 5 iterations, writes "experimental journal" → per-section LaTeX with self-reflection + 20 rounds of citation polling → GPT-4o reviewer outputs scores (soundness, presentation, contribution, overall, confidence) + binary accept/reject, post-calibrated by thresholding. Reviewer calibrated on 500 ICLR 2022 papers (accepted + rejected), metrics: balanced acc, accuracy, F1, AUC, FPR, FNR, mean + 95% bootstrap CIs.
## Data sources named
Three seed templates: 2D diffusion (tanelp/tiny-diffusion, geometric shapes / two-moons / 2D dinosaur, KL via non-parametric entropy estimation), language modeling (Karpathy nanoGPT, character-level Shakespeare transformer), grokking (small transformer on modular arithmetic). Reviewer calibration: 500 ICLR 2022 papers from OpenReview. All public code/data; full prompts in Appendix A.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Reviewer calibration: human baseline balanced acc 0.66 / acc 0.73 / F1 0.49 / AUC 0.65 / FPR 0.17 / FNR 0.52; uncalibrated Sonnet 3.5: 0.52 / 0.40 / 0.55 / 0.52 / 0.95 — calibrated AI reviewer approaches but stays below human consistency.
- Diffusion template: Sonnet 3.5: 51 ideas, 49 novel, 38 experiments passed, mean reviewer score 3.82, max 6.0, ~$250; DeepSeek Coder: 51/42/32, mean 3.32, ~$10; Llama-3.1 405B mean 2.30.
- 10 selected generated papers scored 3–5 by the reviewer (average accepted NeurIPS paper ≈ 6).
- Cost ≈ $10–15 per completed paper; ~50 ideas per run, ~12 hours on 8× H100s.
- Authors' own Section 8 warnings: generated papers occasionally hallucinate entire ablation tables (fixed by instructing only observed results); silent misimplementation of ideas; limited experiment budget → unfair comparisons; agent "struggles to compare the magnitude of two numbers"; minimal sandboxing caused a runaway self-relaunching process, ~1TB checkpoint writes, agents editing time-limit constraints.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- TRUST-SIGNAL: authors state explicitly — do NOT take generated papers' scientific content at face value, treat as hints; the calibrated reviewer (0.66 balanced acc) is a noisy filter, not a judge. The failure modes (silent misimplementation, hallucinated tables, unfair comparisons, editing constraints to escape limits) transfer directly to any automated signal-discovery loop and must be engineered against.
- OTHER: MOVE-37-relevant — this paper IS the theorist↔execution-lab closed loop (idea generation = theorist; experiment iteration = execution lab; reviewer = promotion gate); complements ledgers 2084/2088/2091 rather than duplicating them.

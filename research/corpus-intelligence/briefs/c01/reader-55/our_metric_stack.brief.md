# docs/props/research/2026-09-17/props-consensus/our-metric-stack.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
The GSE lab's plain-language inventory of every NFL number it computes — data sources, sample, filters, the full metric family list, known limitations, and how raw numbers become projections. Written 2026-09-17; computed data only, no mock data. This is the methodological bible for Garrett's props/metric pipeline.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Filters (exact sieve): (1) regular season only; (2) pass/run plays only; (3) no kneels (453 removed in 2025) or spikes (82 removed); (4) garbage time excluded = 4th-quarter plays with possession-team win probability >0.95 or <0.05 — removed 11.2% of 2025 plays; overtime kept; (5) Success = EPA > 0 (nflverse `success` flag matched `epa > 0` on 100% of pass/run plays, zero mismatches); (6) Dropback = pass attempt OR scramble (sacks count as attempts; scrambles count as dropbacks per 4for4); (7) Explosive = 15+ yards on dropback or 10+ yards on designed rush (4for4 convention).
Key formulas: Expected INTs = league-average INT rate per dropback × team dropbacks; 2025 baseline 1.867%. Expected fumbles lost = league-average fumble-lost rate per play × team plays; 2025 baseline 0.640%. Luck meter = actual minus expected (negative = lucky, positive = unlucky). League percentiles: 100 = best, 0 = worst; lower-is-better metrics inverted. Turnover-worthy-play rate = FTN `is_interception_worthy` per dropback (2025 only): ~3.0% of dropbacks flagged; 52.3% of flagged throws became actual INTs. Pressure-to-sack conversion is near-pure luck: R² < 0.005 → explicit veto on individual sack props. Fumble recovery year-to-year correlation ~0.00.
Projection recipe: base prior = 2025 full-season per-game means; Week 1 2026 = role-check only, 0% efficiency weight; volume script-adjusted via measured 2025 dropback rates by win-probability bucket; garbage-time corrected via measured unfiltered/filtered per-game ratio multiplier per stat; every projection carries uncertainty bands; unsupported rows NULL with a one-line reason; H1/H2/H3 pre-registration bar (three hypotheses killed on honest nulls in the props lab).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Sample: 2025 full regular season = 46,452 plays → 29,239 after filters (285 games in file including postseason, excluded). 2026 Week 1 = 2,756 raw → 1,673 after filters (~45–65 plays per team).
- Metric families computed per team per season: EPA/play (per dropback, per designed rush, defensive sign-flipped); success rate; explosive-play rate; EPA distributions (median, 10/25/75/90th percentiles, share negative-EPA, share chunk EPA>1.0); league percentiles; down splits (early 1st–2nd vs late 3rd–4th); drive stats; stuff rate; air-vs-YAC EPA splits (+ air yards per dropback); late-and-close EPA/success (4Q, WP 0.20–0.80, ~50–80 plays per team per season, always reported with play count); unit matchups; weekly trends (team × week × 18 weeks 2025 = 544 rows); turnover components; TWP rate; pressure proxies (QB-hit rate, sack rate per dropback — floors only, hurries invisible).
- League 2025 norms: 2.10 points per drive, 24.0% TD rate, 20.4% three-and-out. Three-and-out defined as exactly 3 plays ending in a punt. Drive stats computed on unfiltered sample (includes punts/FGs/garbage-time drives), not comparable to filtered EPA sample.
- 2025 Dallas textbook luck case: 4th in EPA/play, 7-9-1 record, defense 4.6 INTs below expected.
- No opponent adjustment: unadjusted EPA had Jacksonville #1 after Week 1 2026 (beat Cleveland) while FTN's DVOA ranked San Francisco #1 after adjustment — DVOA-style adjustment is the documented next step. Cross-unit EPA used as qualitative modifier only, never smuggled into numbers.
- Week 1 2026 caught real structural breaks: Montgomery now playing for Houston, DJ Moore for Buffalo.
- Proprietary posture: PFF grades, DVOA tables, Next Gen Stats used to learn/sanity-check only (e.g., own Seattle defensive EPA matched The Athletic's TruMedia report; Buffalo EPA rank within filter noise of PFF) — never republished.
- Legal constraint on video: Richardson v. Townsquare Media, 2d Cir. 2026-04-23 → real-footage clips stay 2–4 seconds, commentary-led, never the whole play.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- QB-BEHAVIOR — turnover-worthy-play rate (~3.0% of dropbacks flagged, 52.3% conversion to INTs) as the INT-projection signal, not raw INT count; dropback convention (scramble vs designed rush); air-vs-YAC EPA splits per dropback.
- COACHING — volume script-adjusted by 2025 dropback rates per win-probability bucket; early-vs-late down splits; late-and-close sample (4Q WP 0.20–0.80).
- OL — stuff rate (designed rushes gaining zero or negative yards); pressure proxies as QB-hit/sack rate per dropback (floors; R²<0.005 conversion veto).
- SCHEME — air-vs-YAC EPA splits, EPA distributions (boom-bust vs consistency), explosive-play thresholds (15+ dropback / 10+ rush).
- TRUST-SIGNAL — H1/H2/H3 pre-registration bar (three hypotheses killed on honest nulls); "a projection that disagrees with the market is a hypothesis, not an edge, until validated"; unmodeled risks named in writing.
- OTHER — licensing (CC-BY / CC-BY-SA), no-opponent-adjustment limitation, legal video constraint.
## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)

# docs/props/research/2026-09-18/firecrawl/ODDSPAPI-DEEP-DIVE.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-dive vendor research report (2026-09-18, Motif subagent, public sources only, no API key) on OddsPapi (oddspapi.io) — a sports-odds aggregation API by 55 Tech — covering pricing, endpoint inventory, NFL coverage depth, the NFL player-prop board structure, historical-odds mechanics/retention, WebSocket, latency, and terms. Every factual claim carries a CONFIRMED / INFERRED / UNVERIFIED label with source URL and access date.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Billing: flat per-request (1 call = 1 request regardless of response size/filters); free tier 250 requests/month, all books incl. Pinnacle/Singbet, historical odds included at every tier; `/v4/historical-odds` is unmetered (does not increment usage); errors on billable endpoints (4xx/5xx) count as 1 request; after plan exhaustion, all endpoints except `/account` blocked with 429. Per-endpoint cooldowns (CONFIRMED): /v4/odds 500ms, /v4/fixtures 2000ms, /v4/markets 1000ms, /v4/settlements 2000ms, /v4/historical-odds 5000ms. 429 body is valid JSON with `error.retryMs` — honor it.
Close-line semantics (CONFIRMED, no `is_closing` flag): close = last snapshot with `active=true` AND `createdAt < startTime` (snapshots continue in-play, e.g. Pinnacle); dedupe consecutive identical prices (heartbeats, not moves) before measuring movement. One Bet365 fixture example: 266 markets / 230,515 snapshots / 29,569 deduped moves. ETag/`If-None-Match` on finished fixtures (3-day cache, max-age=259200).
Prop-board engineering rules: key yardage props on (playerName, handicap), never market ID (ladder rungs); de-vig only rungs with both sides active; dedupe clone feeds by full price tuple (`cloneOf` metadata incomplete — NFL props 99 books → 39 independent groups, 60.6% reduction; largest group 21 slugs); filter `active=true` at price level; skip internal feeds (`pinnacle+*`, `demo`) and suspended books; resolve market IDs by name via /markets (same bet ships under multiple IDs — anytime TD under market IDs 14388 and 144637).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NFL coverage: sportId 14, NFL tournamentId 31 with 255 scheduled fixtures; NCAA tournamentId 27653 (2,745 fixtures); CFL 790 (23); 100 market families on sportId 14. 237 bookmakers quoted Falcons vs Panthers (3 days pre-kickoff) → 79 independent prices after dedupe. Match-market ID counts: Total (incl. OT) 201, Handicap (incl. OT) 281, quarters 80 each (1st/2nd/3rd/4th incl. OT variants).
- Week 1 2026 opener (Seahawks vs Patriots) prop board: 259 books quoted, 99 priced props, 8,508 prop prices, 5,391 active; 21 prop families (largest: receiving yards 2,176 prices, 62.5% active, 91 books, 8 players, 41 market IDs; rush yards 1,147, 53.9%; pass yards 888, 41.1%; anytime TD 830, 91.1% active, 32 books, 23 players, 2 IDs). Kupp receiving-yards ladder: 15 distinct active rungs (9.5 → 39.5+), one market ID per rung. `mainLine` flag unreliable for NFL alt lines (agreed with consensus spread only 46% of flags). `Over Under Rush Yards` (two-sided, de-viggable) vs bare `Rush Yards` (one-sided threshold ladder, not de-viggable) are distinct families.
- Prop book depth (Week 1): FanDuel 321 prop prices (91.6% active) > Caesars/WilliamHill 250 > ESPNBet/theScore 233 > DraftKings 200 > Kalshi 110 (100% active — unusual for a prediction market). Pinnacle, Bet365, BetMGM, Betfair Exchange, Polymarket, SBOBet priced ZERO props — game lines only; no sharp reference price exists on the NFL prop board. Anytime-TD payout gap on Kupp: 3.00 FanDuel → 4.37 betfirst.be (+45.7%).
- Timing: FanDuel game lines entered feed Aug 9; props entered Aug 31 00:27:16 UTC — 10 days before kickoff, 22 days after game lines. DraftKings 00:31:48, Caesars/ESPNBet 00:34:19, Kalshi 00:57:40 UTC the same morning (5 independent operators opened props within ~30 min = scheduled jobs). DK had posted an anytime-TD board June 2–4 then pulled it (60 snapshots). Rule: schedule the prop scanner against kickoff, not the calendar.
- Retention conflict (resolve live before backfill engineering): official docs claim "all historical odds since January 2026" (~8–9 months); independent May 2026 probe (cleatiq, soccer) measured ~90-day rolling window; provider's own Sept 16 blog confirmed 8 months for EPL and stated "rolling window, not a permanent archive."
- Terms (CONFIRMED): "You may not resell, repackage, or redistribute our data as a standalone product"; abuse monitoring covers scraping; payments non-refundable; terms change anytime. GSE implication: internal analytics safe lane; any public display of odds needs a legal read.
- Latency (provider methodology): sharps (Pinnacle) and major softs (Bet365) targeted as close to 1 second as possible over WebSocket; soft books with anti-bot protection 4–10s; NFL explicitly named as high-profile pregame polling priority. Their 60-second-delay claim about The Odds API is vendor marketing, UNVERIFIED independently.
- Recommended GSE lane split: The Odds API = primary current-line feed + failover anchor; OddsPapi = (1) historical CLV reconstruction, (2) NFL prop-market discovery + cross-book comparison, (3) Pinnacle-limit-anchored fair probabilities, (4) book-breadth disagreement checks. Current GSE `picks` table supports only SPREAD/MONEYLINE/TOTAL — props require a schema extension before any prop lane goes live.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No formulas. Decision thresholds stated: Apple Watch complication only worth building at 10k+ mobile users; WhatsApp alerts deferred until Elite tier has 1k+ active users; year-in-review requires 12 months of per-user data; white-label licensing requires Phase 5 B2B API with ≥3 Enterprise customers. Calibration leaderboard concept: rank users by calibration accuracy, NOT win rate (explicit anti-tout metric); user calibration curves vs the model's curve; pre-show confidence prompts as the training mechanic.

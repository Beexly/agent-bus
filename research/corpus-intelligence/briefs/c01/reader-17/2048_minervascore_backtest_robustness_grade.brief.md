# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2048-minervascore-backtest-robustness-grade.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-text read of arXiv:2608.23808v2 (Santoni, Jouanne, Scullin — Minerva/Minerva1.com, 2026): the MinervaScore, a 0–100 statistical robustness grade combining four established validation quantities — Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR), Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO), Superior Predictive Ability (SPA), Minimum Track Record Length (MinTRL) — plus a regime-stability composite (ρ), with a binary "Robustness Seal," calibrated on 359,062 production backtest records. Ledger verdict: highest-priority ADOPT of its wave — this is the multiple-testing / backtest-overfitting validation layer the sports-signal-mining program needs, re-parameterized for weekly sports panels.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Seal (eq. 2): Seal = 1[DSR ≥ τ_DSR ∧ PBO ≤ τ_PBO ∧ SPA ≤ τ_SPA ∧ T ≥ MinTRL ∧ ρ ≥ τ_ρ], thresholds τ_DSR=0.95, τ_PBO=0.50, τ_SPA=0.10, τ_ρ=0.60; MinTRL per Bailey & López de Prado.
- Regime composite (eq. 3): ρ = 0.5·p_+ + 0.3·clip_[0,1](1 − s_SR/σ_ref) + 0.2·logistic(SR_min), p_+ = fraction of validation windows with positive Sharpe, s_SR = std of per-window Sharpes, SR_min = minimum across windows, σ_ref = 2 (fixed); authors flag this gate as the most heuristic component.
- Evidence floor: separate flag for insufficient empirical support — NOT a sixth gate, only qualifies interpretation.
- Signed margins: DSR on pre-Φ scale z_DSR = (u − Φ^{−1}(τ_DSR))/σ_DSR with u = (SR̂ − SR_0)/ŝe (DSR = Φ(u)); bounded gates on logit scale z_k = (logit(τ_k) − logit(g_k))/σ_k for k ∈ {PBO, SPA}, z_ρ = (logit(ρ) − logit(τ_ρ))/σ_ρ (clamped [ε,1−ε], ε=10⁻⁶); MinTRL via tanh: z_MinTRL = tanh((T − MinTRL)/σ_T), σ_T = max(0.2·MinTRL, 50). Dispersions σ_k from IQR on the calibration population. Fail-closed: no pre-Φ u ⇒ no score.
- Aggregation (eq. 7): S = wᵀz / √(wᵀΣ_eff w), w = (0.35, 0.25, 0.20, 0.10, 0.10) (DSR first), Σ_eff = cross-sectional correlation of margins estimated once on calibration (Hartung-style dependence adjustment). S → 0–100 display, 80+ reserved for Seal holders.
- DSR = Φ((SR̂ − SR_0)/ŝe), SR_0 = expected Sharpe under null given K trials (Lo / Bailey–López de Prado deflation).
## Data sources named
Calibration population: 359,062 production backtest records (proprietary Minerva platform data); synthetic markets with known ground truth for discrimination testing; one pre-registered test on unseen real-market data; five strategy families represented. No public data release; no repo URL.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Synthetic ground truth: MinervaScore AUROC = **0.989** at headline difficulty — near-perfect separation of true signal from lucky backtests.
- Improvement over the GT-Score proxy and the gates-passed baseline is **modest**; score "remains close to the corrected DSR-alone baseline" (authors' honest caveat — most value is the DSR).
- Pre-registered real-market test: **no significant forward relationship** — Spearman ρ_s = 0.013, one-sided permutation p = 0.40 — "in a population with limited surviving edge." Score positioned as "an auditable validation and reporting layer, rather than evidence of demonstrated real-market predictability."
- Ledger's adoption gate: on permuted-label nulls, sports MinervaScore AUROC ≥ 0.95 separating null from historically-profitable signals AND Seal pass rate on nulls ≤ 5%.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- TRUST-SIGNAL: this is the central trust layer for ALL signal mining — Seal required before any mined signal (QB behavioral, coaching, OL, scheme) enters production models; the regime-stability gate (ρ) is the direct check that a QB-behavior or coaching tendency persists across season phases rather than being a lucky window.
- QB-BEHAVIOR / COACHING / OL / SCHEME: every tendency profile mined for these lanes must pass the Seal — without it, a "QB tendency" is indistinguishable from search luck (the max of 10,000 trials).
- OTHER: ledger's sports-specific sixth gate (beyond the paper): require the signal's edge to survive residualization against closing-line movement — statistical robustness ≠ economic edge; DSR/PBO/SPA can't see that.

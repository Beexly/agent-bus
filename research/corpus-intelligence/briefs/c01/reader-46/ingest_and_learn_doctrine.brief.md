# docs/governance/ingest-and-learn-doctrine.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Founder-level HARD doctrine (2026-09-28): nothing external (dataset, model, paper, feed, tool) may be discarded without being run, inspected, and consented to by the founder — untested items are "UNTESTED — QUEUED FOR EVALUATION," and a restrictive license means research-learn-only, never "throw it away."

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Procedure: (1) Ingest — pull, open, record provenance (URL, commit, license, date pulled); (2) Test — run if it runs, cite outputs/logs; (3) Learn — write down the method/pattern/idea in our own words; (4) Verdict — USE / USE-WITH-ATTRIBUTION / RESEARCH-ONLY / QUEUED (blocked on something named) / REJECT (with test evidence; REJECT never counts toward a quota, it gets replaced).
- Not evidence of deadness: low likes/stars/forks/downloads; old last-commit; "looks like a personal project"; vibes/guesses.

## Data sources named
None specifically; applies to all external material (datasets, models, Spaces, repos, papers, feeds, APIs, tools).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Originated 2026-09-28 after a research sweep wrongly marked HF sports predictor Spaces "all dead" and NFL datasets "unlicensed scraps, SKIP" without opening any.
- Doctrine: restrictive licenses (CC-BY-NC, research-only terms, gated cards) restrict product use, not learning — methods remain ingestible even when data/weights are not; commercial redistribution of others' data/weights/content is barred; product contains only our own analysis/models/words.
- Sits alongside NGS internal-only doctrine and public/private surface doctrine — same shape (reason from material, keep product clean).

- None applicable to sports lanes. Tagged: OTHER (research governance standing rule — relevant to how this corpus program treats untested items).

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — apply as process rule for all corpus/intelligence intake: untested datasets, models, and feeds stay QUEUED, never SKIP; ingestable methods feed the engine spec even when data cannot.

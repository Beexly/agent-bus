# gates/GATE-SPECS.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A transcription (docs only, nothing executed) of the acceptance gates **as written in each module's own JSDoc header** for 179 prediction-engine modules across 23 families (`packages/prediction-engine/src/<family>/*.ts`); it is a gate specification catalog, not evaluation results.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
The file is itself a catalog of pass/fail thresholds. Recurring metric conventions across gates: log-loss improvements >=0.003-0.005 on holdouts; Brier improvements >=0.002-0.005; calibration slopes in [0.95,1.05] (or [0.9,1.1]); empirical coverage within +-2-3pp of nominal (PL 95%-CI coverage in [0.93,0.97]); walk-forward/rolling-origin validation, never random splits on ordered NFL data; ROI in percentage points with p<0.05 (paired over picks); drawdown-relative gates (max drawdown <=0.8x-1.2x baseline); leakage positive controls (e.g. shuffling season labels must destroy the edge). Notable formulas named: `P(next score | lead L) = 1/2 + a - bL` (lead-dependent restoring force); variance-budgeted alpha = sqrt(V_target / V(1)); Monte Carlo MAP and E[max] portfolio optimizers (SAA/PROP+); exact multivariate Kelly Newton-CG solver; STaR-Bets anytime-valid Bernoulli-mean CIs (n=256, Bernoulli(0.3), delta=0.05, 1,000 reps; width <=1.1x randomized Clopper-Pearson, coverage [0.93,0.97]).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Tally: **179 modules, 179 gates specified; 116 carry the literal line `Gate status: NOT EVALUATED`** (reason: gates require historical walk-forward data not available in that environment); **63 state a gate with no `Gate status:` line**; **0 modules have no gate at all** (verified by full-text scan). No gate has been evaluated — every threshold is a target, not a result.
- The 63 status-less gates concentrate in header dialects without a status line: `decision` family 44, `invention` family 7, plus 12 stragglers across `causal`, `ensemble`, `experimental`, `injuries`, `markets`, `metalearning`, `rl`. Closing the gap is a one-line header edit per file, not research.
- Family coverage across 23 families includes: bayesian (15), calibration, causal (5), continual (3), decision (44 — Kelly/sizing/stake/abstention/publish gates, all conditioned on a human call), ensemble (14), experimental (19), injuries (9), inplay, invention (7), markets (10), metalearning (6), nlp (3), props-dfs (14), rl (7), simulators, sizing, symreg (3), team-ratings (5), tracking (3), weather (2), win-spread-total (3).
- Several gates are explicitly split for partial adoption (e.g. `1908-07372` shock-layer-only clause; `2409-18645v1` monitoring-layer fallback if metrics move but ROI doesn't; `2104-08236v1` sigma-miscalibration fallback to the simpler baseline the paper calls "a simple yet powerful method").
- Several gates carry hard-fail clauses that override favorable headline metrics (e.g. `2101-12523` exact-card-size rule; `2104-04918v2` single-model-weight-collapse rule; `2609-22632` per-market-type cap exceedance; `2108-02082v3` near-constant-weight collapse).
- The `decision` family is pick-publication machinery: publish/abstain gates with explicit floors (e.g. `1907-00208v2` >=60% retention; `2603-24704` >=70% of baseline volume; `2609-22632` >=15% lower abstention than global gate) and hostile-market signals (line-move magnitude, time-to-kickoff, reverse movement, news-volume anomaly).
- QB-specific content in the gates: `1210-1016` Bradley-Terry on correlated analyst judgments over the same QB matchups; `2007-00267v1` "disrupted" regime classifier must recover >=70% of documented QB-injury-spell weeks; `1709-06625v1`/`2109-06625v1` style 4th-down OPE with >=0.5 wins/season gain bar; `1609-01176v1` backup-QB log-loss bar >=0.03 vs team-rating baseline; `2209-07274v5` lead-dependent restoring-force test `P(next score | lead L) = 1/2 + a - bL` with b significantly != 0 at p<0.01; `2206-09654v1` NFL QB season-total LSTM gate.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- QB-BEHAVIOR: gates keyed on QB matchups (1210-1016), QB-injury-spell regime recovery (2007-00267v1), backup-QB subset performance (1609-01176v1).
- COACHING: off-policy coach-RL 4th-down gate (`2109-06625v1`, >=0.5 wins/season via IS/DR OPE with ESS>50%), 4th-down ATT matching (`2011-11691`), icing-the-kicker causal gate (`2009-06750`, post-match SMD<0.1, 99% permutation CI either |ATT|>=0.03 or half-width<0.03).
- TRUST-SIGNAL: the file's central finding — 179/179 gates specified but zero evaluated; plus hard-fail clauses and explicit honesty rules (e.g. `1802-08848v1` market-weight collapse is "a statement to publish, not a silent failure"; `1910-08858v2` framed as an "engine-honesty screen, never a profit objective").

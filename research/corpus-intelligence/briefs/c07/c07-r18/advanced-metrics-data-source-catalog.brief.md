# data-sources/research/2026-09-17/dossiers/advanced-metrics-data-source-catalog.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Gap analysis (2026-09-17) benchmarking GSE's Elo-based engine v5.2.7 (moneyline-focused, thin NFL sample) against ~30 advanced NFL metrics — each with definition, predictive evidence, limitations, and an engine-relevance tag — plus a free/paid/scraped data-source landscape and a licensing-reality section. Core caution: predicting team quality ≠ predicting covers; efficient markets already price public information, so edge comes from implementation quality (opponent adjustment, regression of luck, timeliness).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- EPA/play = change in expected points (down/distance/field-position baseline) summed per play ÷ play count.
- Passing efficiency correlation with wins 0.53–0.61 vs rushing 0.13–0.19 (Advanced Football Analytics expected-wins model).
- Passing EPA predicts future point differential at r ≈ 0.42 at 6 games (Medium/M. Zhou 2026 early-season study); offensive success rate stabilizes at ~r = 0.60 by game 6 but is less predictive than passing EPA.
- DAVE (verified from FTN live tables): Week 1 2026 = 83% preseason forecast offense / 98% defense+ST; Week 11 2025 = 12% offense / 35% defense+ST.
- ESPN FPI: 2016 favorites won 73% of regular-season games, beating Vegas closing line hit rate that year.
- Special-teams EPA: adding standardized unit EPAs cut prediction RMSE by 1.9% (Wharton study).
- All-downs efficiency: 8-game predictive correlation with wins ~0.36 (highest of common offensive stats); raw 3rd-down conversion is "nearly meaningless" (hierarchical-Bayes study, de Gruyter 2012).
- PFF grades: −2 to +2 per-play around expected execution → 0–100 scale.
- ESPN PRWR: rusher beats blocker within 2.5s; PBWR: blocker sustains ≥2.5s (2026 methodology update).
- Success rate: family of 3 conventions (nflfastR EPA>0; Football Outsiders 40/60/100; Connelly 50/70/100) — not interchangeable.
- EPA+CPOE composite: canonical weighting/formula unverified — do not borrow; build in-house.
- Fourth-down aggressiveness literature: aggressive coaches worth ~0.5–1.5 pts/game (documented as estimate, not formula).
- Rest: historical bye advantage largely disappeared after 2011 CBA (arXiv 2024 study); no verified coefficient for travel/time-zone/altitude — do not ship one.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Gap list ranked by expected lift for v5.2.7: (1) opponent-adjusted efficiency HIGH — Elo adjusts via score, not efficiency; DVOA-style per-play opponent baselines is the single biggest structural upgrade. (2) turnover regression HIGH — single biggest upgrade to an Elo engine; fumble rates/recovery, INT rates vs expected computable free. (3) dropback/rush EPA split HIGH — passing predicts, rushing mostly doesn't. (4) explosive-play differential HIGH — free, stable, moves spreads and totals. (5) early-season shrinkage HIGH — blend observed efficiency toward a Vegas-anchored preseason prior with decaying weight (DAVE's lesson). (6) pressure/win-rate matchup features HIGH — FTN charting free via nflverse; PRWR/PBWR-style features need manual ESPN tables. (7) special-teams EPA MEDIUM — small, real, unpriced (~2% RMSE). (8) pace + weather for totals HIGH-for-totals — situation-neutral pace + stadium-specific wind are the two biggest totals inputs. (9) 4th-down aggressiveness coaching prior MEDIUM — ~1 pt/game systematic. (10) CPOE QB priors MEDIUM–HIGH — cleanest free QB signal for moneyline. (11) rest/travel/altitude LOW — near-zero verified effects. (12) PFF grades/SIS charting MEDIUM paid — PFF Pro $199.99/yr is the cheapest legitimate path to grade-level data, only after free stack is exhausted.
Licensing reality: facts aren't copyrightable; compiled DBs/grades/metrics (PFF grades, DVOA, FPI, Total Points) are protected IP; compute your own EPA from nflverse (clean), don't republish their tables. NGS summary tables via nflverse are usable; raw RFID feed is enterprise-only.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- QB-BEHAVIOR: CPOE (accuracy on a curve, persistent, model-dependent), EPA+CPOE composite (build own weights), dropback EPA (strongest single offensive feature), time to throw (style × protection × route depth; pair with pressure data), turnover-worthy throws.
- COACHING: fourth-down aggressiveness (0.5–1.5 pts/game); 2011-CBA finding that bye advantage vanished is coaching/scheduling context.
- OL: PFF grades (OL/DL facets), ESPN PRWR/PBWR (2.5s convention), RBWR/RSWR (run-game line play), SIS blown block (OL-side mechanism behind pressures), pressure rate (pressure creation more repeatable than sacks — PFF research).
- SCHEME: explosive-play differential, situation-neutral pace (first-order totals input), red-zone trip rate (predictive) vs conversion (noise), early-down/all-downs efficiency over raw 3rd-down %.
- TRUST-SIGNAL: DVOA/FPI as benchmarks the engine must beat, not inputs; license doctrine (compute own, don't republish); caution that markets price public metrics.

# launch-prep/founder-outreach-onepager.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
For-Garrett founder-sales battlecard: cold DM, X/Threads DM, warm email, 5-minute walkthrough script, soundbites, objection handling, a 50-target outreach list in 3 tiers, reply templates, and daily ops cadence to land the first 25 paid customers.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Pricing: free tier = 1 signal/day; Pro = $19/mo for every signal with full factor trail; 7-day refund window.
- Conversion math (industry-baseline founder sales): of 50 DMs, expect 10 to engage, 5 to sign up free, 1–2 convert to paid in first 30 days.
- Ops cadence: 10 DMs/day (Mon–Fri, never weekends); reply within 24h; log name/date/response/outcome; no second follow-up to silence.
- Walkthrough order: Homepage (SignalPreviewQueue) → Anatomy of a Signal (factor trail, Edge Index, variance reminder) → vs. Tout Services → Methodology (Galaxy IQ four-check stack + 5 readiness gates: CANONICAL_HISTORY, DERIVED_MODEL_HISTORY, PUBLIC_PICKS, PERFORMANCE_STATS, OUTCOME_LEARNING) → Calibration Report ("Collecting" state).
- Win-rate bar: "A defensible win-rate takes at least 100 settled signals."

Live odds "ingests live odds from dozens of sportsbooks every 30 minutes" (pitch copy, not an adapter spec — data source details not specified). Factor trail named as: consensus, line movement, market depth, freshness.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Factor-trail definition (from the email pitch): every signal's factor trail = consensus + line movement + market depth + freshness — the named component stack of a published signal.
- Four-check decision stack (Galaxy IQ): read the board, measure pressure, gate the signal, learn slowly.
- Five readiness gates controlling public surfaces: CANONICAL_HISTORY, DERIVED_MODEL_HISTORY, PUBLIC_PICKS, PERFORMANCE_STATS, OUTCOME_LEARNING.
- Targeting: 10 personalized (no template) first, then Tier 2 warm secondary network, then Tier 3 cold but qualified (sharp bettors in tout disputes, tout-transparency complainers); personalize Tier 3 with something from their last 30 days of posts.
- Objection playbook: "Why trust a new service" → gated Calibration Report + audit-the-methodology-first; "win rate" → refusal is the answer until 100 settled signals; "I just want today's lock" → disqualify ("then I'm not your service").

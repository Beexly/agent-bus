# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2150-dro-robust-bayesian-ambiguity-sets.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-paper read (ar5iv HTML) of "Decision Making under Model Misspecification: DRO with Robust Bayesian Ambiguity Sets" (arXiv:2505.03585, Dellaporta, O'Hara & Damoulas, 2025): distributionally robust optimization where the ambiguity set is an MMD ball centered at a *misspecification-robust* posterior predictive (Bayesian Nonparametric Learning with MMD loss instead of likelihood), so decisions carry worst-case guarantees even when the model family excludes the true DGP. Lane: uncertainty_decision_theory. Ledger verdict ADAPT — the missing *outer* layer for GSE's decision stack: the engine's outcome model is always misspecified (sports DGP ≠ any parametric family; training contaminated by tanking teams, backup-QB games, weather outliers), and naive DRO either excludes the DGP (optimizer's curse) or explodes into useless conservatism.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- DRO game: min_{x∈X} sup_{P∈A} E_{ξ∼P}[f_x(ξ)] (Eq. 1).
- BDRO: min_x E_{Π(θ|ξ_{1:n})}[sup_{P: d_KL(P‖P_θ)≤ε} E_P[f_x]] (Eq. 2, Shapiro et al. 2023); DRO-BAS_PP: KL ball around posterior predictive (Eq. 3); DRO-BAS_PE: expected-KL ball (Eq. 4) (both Dellaporta et al. 2024).
- DRO-RoBAS: min_x sup_{P: MMD(P, P̂_n^{pred,NPL}) ≤ ε} E_{ξ∼P}[f_x(ξ)] — MMD ball around the NPL-MMD robust posterior predictive; NPL-MMD posterior can be bimodal even when the model is unimodal; tractable dual in the RKHS; high-probability bounds on the tolerance ε so the set contains the DGP and the DRO objective upper-bounds the true objective.
- Works for any model family, including likelihood-free models (DRO-BAS_PE is limited to exponential families; BDRO/DRO-BAS_PP need closed-form or MCMC posteriors). Assumptions: bounded costs, kernel choice for MMD, NPL prior beliefs about the DGP, i.i.d. training observations (contamination only in training, test = clean P̃).

Baselines: BDRO, DRO-BAS_PP, DRO-BAS_PE, empirical MMD (Staib & Jegelka 2019). Implementation details in Appendix B; no public code link extracted.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Bimodal DGP, misspecified unimodal model (newsvendor): standard posteriors concentrate *between* the modes → DRO-BAS/BDRO need huge ε → conservative, high out-of-sample cost. DRO-RoBAS's NPL-MMD posterior is bimodal despite the unimodal model; consistently lower out-of-sample variance than empirical MMD (univariate); competitive in multivariate (empirical MMD slightly better on mean, unaffected by misspecification by construction).
- Huber contamination (newsvendor): at η=0, DRO-BAS *beats* RoBAS (well-specified case favors standard Bayes — robustness has a price). At η>0, RoBAS shows the best out-of-sample mean-variance tradeoff; even empirical MMD degrades (training≠test discrepancy hits empirical methods too).
- Portfolio, η=0.1: for ε<0.2, empirical DRO degrades fast while RoBAS is unaffected; at ε≥0.2 they converge (MMD's intrinsic robustness kicks in). At η=0.2 the gap magnifies — the empirical nominal is corrupted by outliers; RoBAS's robust nominal is not.
- Cost: RoBAS is computationally heavier (RKHS optimization + NPL sampling); scalability via Fourier features/low-rank approximations left to future work. ε still must be chosen; guarantees are high-probability, not exact; kernel choice affects everything and isn't automated; in sports the "test" (next week's games) can itself contain the outliers (the paper's contamination model assumes clean test).

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- TRUST-SIGNAL: the principled bet/no-bet + staking outer layer — stake = Kelly fraction against the *worst-case* distribution in the ball (robust Kelly), post only if worst-case edge > 0; per-game robust predictive + worst-case edge logged as audit fields; "optimizer's curse" diagnostic = gap between predicted edge and realized edge (should shrink under RoBAS); a misspecification detector (MMD two-sample check of recent outcomes vs the model's predictive) gates robust vs. cheaper standard sizing, per the paper's own η=0 finding.
- OTHER (regime handling): the per-game adaptive-ε improvement experiment — high MMD distance → larger ball — plus a learned deep kernel on game features so the MMD ball respects football-relevant similarity (divisional games near each other in kernel space).

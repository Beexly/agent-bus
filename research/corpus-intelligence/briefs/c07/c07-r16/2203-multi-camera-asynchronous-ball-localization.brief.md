# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2203-multi-camera-asynchronous-ball-localization.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-paper read (ar5iv HTML) of "Multi-Camera Asynchronous Ball Localization and Trajectory Prediction with Factor Graphs and Human Poses" (arXiv:2401.17185, Xiao, Zaidi & Gombolay, 2024): a two-part system for fast-moving spinning tennis balls — an incremental factor graph (GTSAM + ISAM2) with physics-based aerodynamic/bounce factors that fuses asynchronous camera detections with no frame synchronization, plus a TCN that regresses initial spin from the hitter's body pose (spin is unobservable early but strongly affects Magnus lift and bounce). Ledger verdict ADAPT — the pose→spin-prior idea is the gem (kicker/punter body mechanics predict ball flight); the multi-view factor graph is overkill for NFL's monocular broadcast, but the factor-graph-as-learned-simulator framing is a portable pattern.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Factor graph: G(η) = ∏_{i=0}^{M−1} f_i(ξ_i) (Eq. 1); potentials f_i(ξ_i) = exp(−½‖err_i(ξ_i)‖²_{Σ_i}) (Eq. 2). Variables: camera poses X_i, ball 3D location L_t, 3D velocity V_t, 3D spin W_j (constant during flight, changes only at bounce).
- Factors: projection err^proj = u_detect − π(L), π^proj(L)=K[R T]L (Eqs. 3–4); location π^loc(L_s,V_s)=L_s+V_s[t(s+1)−t(s)] (Eqs. 5–6); aerodynamics F_D(V)=−½ρ_air A C_D‖V‖V, F_L(V,W)=½ρ_air A C_L‖V‖²U (U=W×V), acc=(F_D+F_L)/m+g, π^aero(V_s,W_j)=V_s+acc·Δt (Eqs. 7–11); bounce (Eqs. 12–19): V_{x,t}=[RW_{y,j−1}+(1.5R²/R_1²)V_{x,t−1}]/[1+1.5R²/R_1²]; V_{y,t}=[−RW_{x,j−1}+(1.5R²/R_1²)V_{y,t−1}]/[1+1.5R²/R_1²]; V_{z,t}=−e_z V_{z,t−1}; W_{x,j}=−V_{y,t}/R; W_{y,j}=V_{x,t}/R; W_{z,j}=W_{z,j−1}; spin-prior factor on W_0 from the TCN.
- Incremental optimization: each new detection triggers relinearized ISAM2: argmin_η −log G(η) ≈ argmin_η ‖Jη−b‖² (Eq. 20). Prediction: propagate (L,V,W) with 4th-order Runge–Kutta on the flight ODE [L̇, V̇, Ẇ]=[V, acc(V,W), 0] (Eq. 21); on ground contact terminate, apply bounce dynamics, restart.
- TCN spin-prior network: regresses initial ball spin from a 100-frame hip-centered pose-keypoint sequence (emphasizing right arm: shoulder/elbow/wrist); authors report TCNs beat RNNs/LSTMs for this task.
- Assumptions: spin constant during flight, changes only at bounce; ball is a sphere (radius R); detections carry known timestamps; camera poses known from calibration; hip-centered arm poses carry spin information.

## Data sources named
Proprietary hardware setup — indoor tennis court, 6 Blackfly S cameras at 5.5 m height on back walls (3 per side, overlapping), each with a Jetson Xavier; intrinsic calibration via checkerboard, extrinsic via AprilTag + court keypoints; ball detection via background subtraction + YOLO-tiny trained on tennis balls, ~140 Hz detection per camera, ~1 ms Ethernet latency; human poses via AlphaPose. Spin labels self-distilled via iterative factor-graph inference (circularity risk in the TCN's training signal). 80/20 train/validation split for the TCN (RMSE metric); no time-ordered splits, no public benchmark, no author code or dataset link (GTSAM and AlphaPose are open source; work supported by a Google grant).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- TCN spin prior: training RMSE 2.95 Hz, validation RMSE 5.27 Hz; Pearson R 0.807 (novice) / 0.815 (competitive), p<0.001.
- "Reduces the prediction error of landing positions by over 63.6% compared to a baseline method that utilized an adaptive extended Kalman filter" (abstract; §IV: 63.6% reduction in landing-position RMSE via spin-prior integration vs. EKF/AEKF baselines and a no-spin ablation, approaching the labeled-spin oracle).
- Stage-binned RMSE (by quartiles of detection count): error drops most from Stage 1→2 (first 25% of observations); TCN-spin variant beats EKF (red), AEKF (purple), no-spin (blue); spin advantage appears mainly in second-bounce prediction (spin affects first bounce, then propagates).
- Heavy-spin pro shots: TCN variant consistently beats EKF/AEKF but underperforms the labeled-spin oracle — authors attribute the gap to limited training data and missing racket pose.
- Limitations from the ledger: self-distilled spin labels (circularity); small proprietary single-venue dataset, no public benchmark; bounce coefficients needed "ad hoc modifications" for high-spin ball-ground slip (authors propose a future *learnable* bounce factor); tennis ball ≠ football — sphere-specific bounce equations don't transfer to a prolate spheroid with orientation dynamics; no real-time ISAM2 latency numbers reported.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER (kick physics): the pose→spin-prior gem — kicker/punter body mechanics (approach, plant, swing) predict kick ball flight the way hitter arm poses predict tennis spin; NGS gives no spin data for kicks, and this paper shows how to infer spin as a hidden state and how much it matters (63.6% landing-error reduction); the factor-graph-as-learned-simulator pattern (physics factors + learned factors, incremental inference) is portable to a football flight model.
- TRUST-SIGNAL (INFERENCE): the authors' own proposed next step — replace the analytic bounce factor with a *learned* bounce factor (small MLP trained on turf-impact data) — is the honest path for tumbling punts, where analytic sphere bounce models fail hardest (ledger hypothesizes another 20%+ cut in second-landing error on bouncing punts).

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — NFL punt/kickoff flight lab: NGS tracking kick plays + broadcast-video ball tracks + a pose-estimation TCN on the ~15 frames before foot contact as the spin-prior factor in a GTSAM/ISAM2 factor graph with football-specific drag+Magnus and a learned turf-bounce factor, serving an offline "kick quality vs outcome" table and an online touchback-probability feed for the live win-probability engine; ADOPT gate on held-out NFL punts: landing-spot RMSE ≤ 2.0 yards AND pose-based spin priors cut early-flight error ≥30% vs no-spin baseline AND incremental inference <50 ms per detection; REJECT if TCN spin prior correlates R<0.3 with fitted spin on NFL data.

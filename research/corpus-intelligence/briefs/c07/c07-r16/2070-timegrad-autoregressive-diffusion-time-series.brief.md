# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2070-timegrad-autoregressive-diffusion-time-series.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-paper read (ar5iv HTML, §§1–6 + references, all equations/Tables 1–2/Figures 1–4 verified) of TimeGrad (arXiv:2101.12072, Rasul et al., Zalando Research, 2021): the first autoregressive denoising-diffusion forecaster for multivariate probabilistic time series, where each time step's joint distribution is learned by a conditional DDPM conditioned on an RNN hidden state plus covariates known for the whole forecast horizon. Ledger verdict ADAPT — the NFL schedule, rest days, and venues are exactly "known-future covariates," and the adaptation is to run the same per-step conditional diffusion as a *generator* of synthetic multivariate stat trajectories (team-week EPA/success/pace panels, within-game state sequences) rather than as a forecaster.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- DDPM backbone: forward q(x^n|x^{n−1}) = N(x^n; √(1−β_n)x^{n−1}, β_n I); closed-form q(x^n|x^0) = N(x^n; √ᾱ_n x^0, (1−ᾱ_n)I); reverse p_θ(x^{n−1}|x^n) = N(x^{n−1}; μ_θ(x^n,n), Σ_θ I) with μ_θ(x^n,n) = (1/√α_n)(x^n − (β_n/√(1−ᾱ_n))ε_θ(x^n,n)); training objective E‖ε − ε_θ(√ᾱ_n x_t^0 + √(1−ᾱ_n)ε, h_{t−1}, n)‖².
- Temporal factorization: q(x_{t0:T}^0 | x_{1:t0−1}^0, c_{1:T}) = Π_{t=t0}^{T} q(x_t^0 | x_{1:t−1}^0, c_{1:T}); encoder h_t = RNN_θ(concat(x_t^0, c_t), h_{t−1}) — 2-layer LSTM, h_t ∈ ℝ^40. ε_θ is a conditional 1-D dilated ConvNet (8 residual blocks, 8 residual channels, gated σ(·)⊙tanh(·), dilation 2^{i%2}, 8 summed skip connections); noise index n via Transformer Fourier positional embeddings into ℝ^32 (N_max=500). N=100 diffusion steps, linear β 1e−4→0.1; sampling: annealed Langevin per step, S=100 trajectories for empirical quantiles.
- Metric: CRPS(F,x) = ∫(F(z) − 1{x≤z})²dz; CRPS_sum = E_t[CRPS(F̂_sum(t), Σ_i x_{i,t}^0)] — proper scoring rule (likelihood unusable since not all baselines yield analytic distributions). Scaling: each series divided by its context-window mean (Salinas et al. trick).

## Data sources named
Six public multivariate datasets: Exchange (8 series, ℝ+, daily, 6,071 steps, predict 30), Solar (137, ℝ+, hourly, 7,009, predict 24), Electricity (370, ℝ+, hourly, 5,833, predict 24), Traffic (963, (0,1), hourly, 4,001, predict 24), Taxi (1,214, ℕ, 30-min, 1,488, predict 24), Wikipedia (2,000, ℕ, daily, 792, predict 30). Covariates: time-dependent (day-of-week, hour-of-day), time-independent embeddings, lag features by frequency — all known for the forecast period. Code stated "will be made available after the review process" (not verified; GluonTS has a port). Baselines (11): VAR, VAR-Lasso, GARCH, VES, KVAE, Vec-LSTM-ind-scaling, Vec-LSTM-lowrank-Copula, GP-scaling, GP-Copula, Transformer-MAF.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- CRPS_sum (lower better; TimeGrad mean±se over 10 retrainings): Exchange 0.006±0.001 (LOSS — VES/VAR/Transformer-MAF 0.005; only dataset not won); Solar **0.287±0.02** (next: Transformer-MAF 0.301); Electricity **0.0206±0.001** (next: Transformer-MAF 0.0207); Traffic **0.044±0.006** (next: Transformer-MAF 0.056, −21%); Taxi **0.114±0.02** (next: Transformer-MAF 0.179, −36%); Wikipedia **0.0485±0.002** (next: Transformer-MAF 0.063, −23%). SOTA on 5/6.
- Ablation (Fig. 3, Electricity): N≈10 already near-optimal, N≈100 optimal, larger not beneficial — a 10× sampling speedup is available. Trained on a single Nvidia V100 16GB.
- Claimed mechanism: EBMs don't assign spurious density between disconnected modes (flows do) and don't need continuous-to-disconnected maps (VAEs do).
- Limitations from the ledger: forecasting, not synthesis (requires repurposing as generator); autoregressive rollout compounds error (30 steps tested); no discrete-emission modeling (counts treated as continuous — NFL needs discrete outcomes); cross-entity dependence only via shared h_{t−1} + joint per-step noise (paper's §6 suggests GNNs for known entity relations).

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER (joint probabilistic forecasting): the paper's per-step conditional diffusion with known-future covariates maps to NFL entities = 32 teams, time steps = weeks, covariates = opponent/home-away/rest/dome/week/bye (categorical embeddings) — generating joint weekly stat trajectories that preserve correlated outcomes (a blowout moves both teams' stats jointly).
- TRUST-SIGNAL: CRPS_sum is directly reusable as the proper scoring rule for GSE's own multivariate probabilistic outputs (e.g., joint distribution of all Week N spreads); the 10-retraining se protocol is the reporting discipline.

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — build a TimeGrad-style per-step conditional diffusion on nflverse 32-team weekly panels (2015–2022 train, 2023–2024 held-out, season-level splits only) with schedule-derived known covariates, used as a generator of synthetic late-season (weeks 13–18) trajectories to augment the engine's spread model; adopt CRPS_sum for multivariate probabilistic GSE outputs; ADOPT gate: ≥5% relative CRPS_sum gain over copula baseline + ≥0.003 spread log-loss gain on real+synthetic vs real-only, with 85–95% coverage on 90% intervals.

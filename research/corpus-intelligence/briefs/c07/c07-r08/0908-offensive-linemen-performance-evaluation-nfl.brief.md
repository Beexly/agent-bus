# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0908-offensive-linemen-performance-evaluation-nfl.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger read of arXiv:1603.07593v2 (Byanna & Klabjan, 2016), a five-stage pipeline evaluating NFL offensive linemen without film grades — differential (to-side minus not-to-side) statistics, k-means clustering, and per-cluster salary-distribution anomaly detection. Verdict: ADAPT — the differential-stats teammate-control trick ports to GSE props, and the cluster → salary-distribution → anomaly pipeline ports directly to DFS salary mispricing detection.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Five "differential statistics": stuff % differential, yards/attempt differential, successful-run % differential, pressures-allowed %, sack % (each = to-side minus not-to-side, or allowed-rate ratios); to-side = direct contribution, not-to-side = control for teammates/RB quality.
- Stepwise OLS of cap value on 15 candidate predictors (exclusions: rookie contracts, non-UFAs, pre-2011-CBA contracts); Experience/Performance composites from coefficient-weighted predictors; k-means (Hartigan–Wong), k=7 chosen via Krzanowski–Lai statistic local maxima; per-cluster salary distributions (Lognormal/Gamma/Beta/Pareto/Weibull, chosen by χ² + AIC + P-P/Q-Q); flag players with P(salary ≥ S) < 5%; silhouette-value gate s(i) > sample mean.
- Exact equations (2)–(7) are garbled in the PDF extraction — procedure is fully described in prose.
## Data sources named
STATS LLC play-by-play, game-by-game: every OL with ≥1 snap in 2013-14 and 2014-15 regular + playoff games; 5,383 player-game data points × 44 variables; directional splits (LS/L/M/R/RS); Pro Bowl/All-Pro selections (Pro-Football-Reference, manual); PFF grades 2007–2015 (manual extract, used as regressor candidate and validation). Proprietary; exact data not released.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Salary model (Table 11, adj. R² = 0.50): intercept 5,399,261 (p=2.12e-15); avg PFF prior to contract +56,697 (p=0.00268); experience −199,134 (p=0.00831); draft round −264,405 (p=5.38e-5); Pro Bowl selections +624,910 (p=0.000338); stuff % differential −82,247 (p=0.012284); yds/attempt differential +382,197 (p=0.044516); sack % −2,516 (p=0.022994). Current-year PFF rating NOT significant — differential stats beat PFF for salary explanation.
- Differential vs to-side-only (Appendix A): to-side-only adj. R² = 0.47; adding not-to-side terms → 0.50, with not-to-side coefficients opposite-signed to to-side (as the control logic predicts); successful-run % to-side lost significance.
- Clusters (k=7, n=133): sizes 25/17/18/23/24/8/18; salary means $5.12M/$2.97M/$3.84M/$2.21M/$2.53M/$6.66M/$3.10M (sample mean $3,518,357, SD $2,146,048); cluster 6 (n=8) dropped as too small. Mean silhouette ≈ 0.16 (weak; Kaufman–Rousseeuw suggest >0.25, ideally >0.5).
- Flagged: undervalued — John Jerry (NYG G, 2014, $795,635), Mike McGlynn (KC G, 2014, $1,037,594); overvalued — Scott Wells (STL C, 2013 & 2014, $5,283,150), Davin Joseph (TB G, 2013, $6,889,518). PFF-rank corroboration: 4 of 5 agree in direction (McGlynn disagrees); Joseph released by TB post-2013; Wells released by STL post-2014.
- Paper-internal inconsistency (flagged, not resolved): abstract/§5 say five players; §7 says "3 overvalued and 3 undervalued… 4 of the 6"; §5.2 says "eleven… eliminated six of the twelve". Tables list 5.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OL: the OL evaluation pipeline itself — differential teammate-controlled statistics for attributing team EPA to individual linemen; GSE has no OL-evaluation lane.
- OTHER (DFS): transplant the pipeline to DFS — per-slate per-position k-means on trailing-4-week features, fit lognormal per cluster on DK/FD $K salaries, flag bottom-5% salary anomalies as value plays; gate — flagged undervalued players must outscore positional slate average in points-per-$1K by ≥10% (paired t, p < 0.05, ≥40 slates 2023–2025).
- OTHER (props): differential stats for props — to-role vs off-role differentials (WR yards/target when targeted vs team yards/play when not targeted; RB yards/carry to his primary gap vs other gaps via nflverse run location; TE production with/without a co-TE) as teammate-adjusted prop-model features.
- TRUST-SIGNAL: improvement experiment — replace k-means+KL with Gaussian mixture (BIC) for soft assignment against the paper's weak-silhouette problem, and replace the 5% tail rule with a conformal p-value so the flag feeds the optimizer as a calibrated value prior.

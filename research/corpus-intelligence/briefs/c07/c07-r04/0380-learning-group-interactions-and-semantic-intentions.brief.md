# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0380-learning-group-interactions-and-semantic-intentions.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
End-to-end diffusion framework for multi-agent trajectory prediction that conditions generation on team-level tactic labels via classifier-free guidance and models group interaction as a cooperative game, using a learned Banzhaf Interaction to capture each agent's cooperation tendency toward potential tactics; also releases an NBA SportVU extension with human-annotated tactics as a new joint trajectory+tactic benchmark. Verdict recorded: ADAPT — closest published analog to what NFL tracking-based live win-probability and prop pricing needs; port requires swapping human-annotated basketball tactics for NGS scheme labels and scaling from 11 to 22 agents.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Interaction encoder: transformer encodes trajectories X∈R^{N×T_obs×2} → A∈R^{N×D_A}; tactic labels via learnable vocabulary embedding W∈R^{V×D_C} (Eq. 6–8: g_i = [a_i; c_{i,m(i)}], G = f_ϕ(X,L)). Diffusion denoising with classifier-free guidance (Eq. 9): f_ε(y,G) = f_ε(y,A) + s_g·(f_ε(y,G) − f_ε(y,A)), s_g = 0.1; tactic info randomly dropped in training; DDPM reverse with leapfrog-style initializer, 100 diffusion steps (Eq. 10); losses L_noise (Eq. 11, noise MSE), L_dist = min_s ‖Y − Ŷ_s‖₂ (Eq. 12, variety over S=20 samples), L_unc uncertainty (Eq. 12). Banzhaf Interaction Learner f(·) = γ_o(SA(γ_ctx(A))) predicts N×k agent–tactic matrix (Eq. 14), supervised by calculation module I_B^j = (S_a2t + S_t2a)/2 (Eqs. 22–24, masked-attention heuristic) with L2 loss (Eq. 15); weighted tokens w_j = Fusion(Ĝ_j, I_pred^j) (Eq. 16) → GAT+MLP tactic head (Eq. 17), top-k via argmax (Eq. 18), focal loss γ=4.0 (Eqs. 19–21). Total (Eq. 25): L = L_noise + L_dist + η·L_unc + α·(L_tactic + β·L_bi), game factor 0.001. Multi-Grained Feature Enhancement: global team-token attention (Eq. 13) then local ball-token attention. Training: PyTorch, RTX 3090, Adam; denoising 100 epochs lr 1e-3 halved every 16 epochs; two-fold framework 30 epochs lr 2e-3 ×0.9 every 32 epochs.

## Data sources named
Extended NBA SportVU (new benchmark, public via https://github.com/aurora-xin/Group2Int-trajectory): 2015–16 raw SportVU (linouk23/NBA-Player-Movements) resampled to 5 Hz; 90,618 sequences × 30 frames (11 agents: 5 offense + 5 defense + ball); annotated by 25 trained humans with 16 team tactic labels (7 offensive: Pick-and-Roll, Ball Movement, Fast Break, Single, etc.; 9 defensive: 5 zone types, Man-to-Man, defensive rebound, defensive transition, scramble); 8,238 JSON entries + .npy trajectories; 70/30 train/test. TeamTrack-basketball and TeamTrack-soccer [68] with K-means pseudo-labels (16 clusters); observe 3.2 s (96 frames), predict 4.8 s (144 frames). NBA task: observe 2 s (10 frames), predict 4 s (20 frames). Baselines: S-LSTM, STGAT, GroupNet, MID, LED, SingularTrajectory, SocialCircle; tactic: Transformer, one-hot Baseline*, Pooling*, zero-shot and LoRA-finetuned Llama3-8B*.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NBA SportVU trajectory (minADE/minFDE, k=20 best-of-20): 1 s: 0.19/0.29; 2 s: 0.39/0.59; 3 s: 0.61/0.86; 4 s: 0.84/1.17 — beats LED by 9.5% (1 s ADE) and ~5.6% (4 s ADE; LED 0.89/1.24 at 4 s)
- TeamTrack-soccer (ADE/FDE 1.2/2.4/3.6/4.8 s): 0.23/0.45, 0.58/1.10, 1.00/1.89, 1.48/2.72 — beats LED 15.4% ADE / 9.6% FDE at 4.8 s
- TeamTrack-basketball: beats LED 14.4% ADE / 30.3% FDE at 4.8 s
- Tactic prediction Top-1/2/3/5: Ours 60.23/81.15/90.53/96.79 vs Pooling* 55.22/75.82/86.05/94.67, finetuned Llama3-8B* 38.17 (Top-1)
- Ablation denoising (4 s minADE/minFDE): MLP 5.28/8.71, query-based 1.24/1.63, diffusion 0.84/1.17
- Ablation Banzhaf: no-BI Top-1 58.44 vs team-level 60.09 / scene-level 60.23 (≈+2 pp)
- Qualitative: on man-to-man transitions predicted defensive trajectories mirror offensive movement where LED places defenders too far apart; ball has highest Banzhaf interaction (~0.285) for both teams
- Limitations recorded: 70/30 split not stated game-disjoint (sequence-level splits leak); no inter-annotator agreement; primary task conditions on observed tactic labels — production must recognize tactics first (recognition gap); best-of-20 flatters generators; Banzhaf "supervision" is a masked-attention heuristic, not a game-theoretic oracle

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- SCHEME — tactic-conditioned trajectory generation maps directly to NGS scheme labels GSE already has structured (Route Classification 2.0, Run Scheme Classification, FTN coverage shells): observe 2 s post-snap, predict trajectories + classify scheme; Banzhaf values reveal which players drive a scheme (e.g., which receiver's movement most influences the coverage call) — usable for matchup analysis; beyond-paper extension: counterfactual tactic conditioning ("what if the defense were in Cover 2 instead of recognized Cover 3?") turns it into a scheme-sensitivity engine for live-prop uncertainty bands.

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — replicate (interaction encoder + CFG diffusion + Banzhaf learner, N=23 agents) on NGS tracking with NGS/FTN scheme labels; adopt if tactic-conditioned beats unconditioned diffusion by ≥8% minADE on weeks 17–18 test AND scheme Top-1 ≥ 55%, AND the gain holds ≥3% when tactics are recognized rather than given.

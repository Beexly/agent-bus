# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1689-personnel-adjusted-park-effects-mlb.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Osborne & Levine (2025, arXiv:2506.22350): personnel-adjusted MLB home-run park factors estimated with a Poisson GLMM using "elsewhere" (leave-one-park-out) batter/pitcher HR propensity covariates that break the circularity of measuring player quality with the same data used to rate parks.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Unit: game × handedness-matchup (LL, LR, RL, RR) — N = 123,886 observations over 2,451,105 PAs, 2010–2023 (excl. 2020). Model: Poisson GLMM, log link: log λi = β0 + β^B·Zi^B + β^P·Zi^P + Σ_s Ss·Xi,s^S + Σ_p (βp^LL·Xi^LHB·Xi^LHP + βp^LR·Xi^LHB·Xi^RHP + βp^RL·Xi^RHB·Xi^LHP + βp^RR·Xi^RHB·Xi^RHP)·Xi^p — 120 park-by-matchup parameters of interest; season random effects Ss ~ N(0, σS²). "Elsewhere" covariates: batter quality q^B = HR rate in all OTHER parks (matchup-specific, e.g., Trevor Story 25/562 = 0.044 vs LHP away from Coors); pitcher quality q^P likewise (e.g., Kershaw 150/7105 = 0.021 vs RHB away from Coors); aggregated per game-matchup as Zi^B = Σq^B, Zi^P = Σq^P. Adjusted park means: E[HR | park p, matchup m, Zi^B = z̄^B, Zi^P = z̄^P]. Fit check: fitted λ̂ rounded to 0.2 bins vs Poisson distribution of observed HR counts. Variance decomposition: residual s² and AIC across nested models (full / park-only / personnel-only / batter-only / pitcher-only).
## Data sources named
Retrosheet play-by-play, MLB 2010–2023 excluding 2020 (public); Marlins' Joe Robbie Stadium 2010–11 excluded; 87% of games have all four matchups. Matchup PA shares: LHB 41.9% (LHP 7.9%, RHP 34.0%), RHB 58.1%; HR/PA ≈ 0.029 overall (LL 0.022, LR 0.029, RL 0.031, RR 0.029).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Adjusted rankings differ substantially from raw: Cleveland Progressive Field LR 1.025 HR/g (rank 1) → adjusted 0.657 (rank 15); Minnesota Target Field LR 0.771 (rank 11) → adjusted rank 24; White Sox Rate Field LR 0.722 (rank 18) → 0.767 (rank 7); Toronto Rogers Centre RR 1.179 (rank 1) → 0.776 (rank 12); Phillies/Milwaukee/Cincinnati RL move ranks 20/15/21 → 5/3/2 after adjustment. LL matchup least affected (PNC Park 0.092 HR/g least friendly; Globe Life 0.233 most). Variance: full model s² = 2.42 (AIC 220,189); park-only 2.50; personnel-only 2.58; batter-only 2.60; pitcher-only 4.07 — batter personnel matters more than pitcher; park effects add modestly beyond personnel. League HR intensity: 1.72 HR/g (2014) → 2.78 HR/g (2019), +62%. Caveats: elsewhere measures still home-park-contaminated (acknowledged, uncorrected); no CIs on ranks; 2020 excluded.

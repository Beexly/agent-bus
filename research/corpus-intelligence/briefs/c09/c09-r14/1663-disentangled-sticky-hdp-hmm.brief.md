# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1663-disentangled-sticky-hdp-hmm.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Zhang et al. (2020, arXiv:2004.03019) fixing the sticky HDP-HMM by disentangling three confounded quantities — transition-row similarity, mean self-persistence, and state-specific persistence variation — into a nonparametric hidden Markov model that learns an unknown number of states with heterogeneous durations; code at github.com/zhd96/ds-hdp-hmm.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
DS-HDP-HMM: HDP prior β ~ GEM(γ); π_j ~ DP(α, β) (shared transition sparsity); self-transition probability κ_j ~ Beta(a, b) per state j, separate from DP-distributed off-diagonal transitions; full row = mixture of sticky self-component and shared π_j. Two Gibbs samplers (direct-assignment and weak-limit truncated). Gibbs updates: state sequence by forward-backward; κ_j by Beta–Binomial conjugacy on self-transition counts; β, π by standard HDP auxiliary-variable updates. Weak-limit truncation at L with symmetric Dirichlet(α/L). Complexity O(TK²) per sweep vs O(T²K + TK²) for general HSMM — explicit-duration-like flexibility at HMM cost. Assumptions: Markov latent chain; conditionally independent emissions given state; truncation L adequate; label-switching handled post-hoc. Evaluated on synthetic multinomial/Gaussian emissions (held-out NLL + normalized Hamming distance vs HDP-HMM and sticky HDP-HMM); rat hippocampal data (100 neurons, 10 Hz, ~36k frames, 4k train/4k test, Poisson emissions, L=200, 7 chains × 15,000 iterations); mouse decision-making data (1,126 trials × 189 frames at 30 Hz, 99-dim autoencoder latents, 100 trials train/~20k frames, 30 trials test, L=40, 7 chains × 10,000).
## Data sources named
Synthetic multinomial/Gaussian emissions (10 datasets per setup); rat hippocampal calcium/spike data (lab-collected, not public); mouse decision-making behavioral video latents (lab-collected, not public). No sports data.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Synthetic: DS-HDP-HMM beats both HDP-HMM and sticky HDP-HMM on held-out NLL and Hamming distance across multinomial and Gaussian setups (consistent wins). Rat: test NLL advantage over sticky HDP-HMM significant at p < 0.05; cleaner spatial/temporal state structure. Mouse: infers 22 states vs 16 for parametric ARHMM baseline, with lower test NLL. Limitations: no sports/non-neural real data; Gibbs with 7 chains × 10–15k iterations is expensive with weak convergence diagnostics; truncation L is a tuning choice; heterogeneous-duration modeling is implicit via κ_j (very long regimes may still be mis-split); a full Gibbs fit on a team-season must finish < 2 hours or variational alternatives are needed.

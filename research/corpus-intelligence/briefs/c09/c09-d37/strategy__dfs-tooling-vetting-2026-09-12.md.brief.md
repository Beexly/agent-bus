## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- DK Classic model: slots + 50k cap + distinctness + locks + excludes + QB-stack rule; oracle objective scaled 1e6 on integer lattice (~1e-6 rounding; test allows 1e-3 on leverage cases).
- `optimizeExact()`: branch-and-bound seeded by the heuristic, pruned with fractional-knapsack relaxation of remaining slots (respecting distinctness and cap); unstacked seed never accepted as incumbent under `stack: true`; 20k node budget per lineup for N-unique path; cash search hits optimum but exhausts 400k node budget before proving it (GPP completes ~73k–94k nodes, ~150–260 ms in Node).

- After fix: shipped slate 6/6 at optimum, 0 beaten, 0 violations; synthetic 78/78, 0, 0.

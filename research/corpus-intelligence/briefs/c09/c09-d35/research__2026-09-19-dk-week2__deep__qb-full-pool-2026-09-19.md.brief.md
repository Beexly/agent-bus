# research/2026-09-19-dk-week2/deep/qb-full-pool-2026-09-19.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full-pool coverage of the other 21 of 30 projected DK Week 2 starting QBs (9 covered by a sibling lane), each with a standardized Week 1 stat line (EPA/db, CPOE, ANY/A, pressure-to-sack, rating under pressure, rushing, aDOT, TD/INT, man/zone splits, play-action), starter confirmations, injury/news context, and matchup reads.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- EPA/db (Week 1, Tej/@tejfbanalytics), CPOE, ANY/A, P2S (pressure-to-sack rate), PR-Und-P (passer rating under pressure), aDOT, TD/INT.
- Percentile flags: [ELITE] = >85th pct, [WEAK] = <15th pct among ~30–32 Week 1 qualifiers.
- PFF neg-graded dropback % (e.g. Geno Smith 0.00% = 1st; Hurts 8.57% = 2nd; Brissett 11.90% = 7th).
- First-read rate (Love 78.6% highest; Maye 42.5%), aggressiveness (Stroud 21% slate-high), scramble rate (Maye 27.5%).
- Matchup table: opponent pressure/blitz/coverage rates from StatRankings nfl-advanced-teams.csv (2026-09-18), FantasyPros W1 splits.

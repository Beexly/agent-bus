## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A conversion & lifecycle strategist's plan for the GSN/GSE subscription funnel, grounded in the actual shipped code (which disagrees with the CLAUDE.md spec on price/billing/trial) plus 2026 web benchmarks, with a ranked gap diagnosis and a prioritized experiment backlog.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Benchmarks (cited URLs): visitor→signup ≈8.5%; aha-moment first hour → 4-5× Day-7 retention, value <14 days → 80%+ M12 retention; freemium→paid median 5.6%, great 8-12%; role/feature-gating ~2× revenue for one product; annual billing cuts attempts 12→1, churn ~51% lower, ~92% retention vs ~68% monthly; annual subscribers ~2.4× more profitable, stay ~40 vs ~14 months; 3-tier pages convert ~1.4× vs 2-tier; reverse trials convert ~25-40%; money-back guarantee +18-26% conversion / +18% risk-free badge lift / -12% cart abandonment; passive dunning recovers ~15%, dunning campaigns ~32%, retry+email+card-updater ~70%; involuntary churn is 20-40% of total; every +1% activation ≈ -2% churn at 36% median; ~75% of new users abandon week 1, non-engagement by day 3 → ~90% churn. ## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Spec vs shipped conflict (verified): spec says Pro $19/mo / Elite $49/mo monthly with trial; shipped is Pro $9.99/week (~$43/mo) / Elite $13.99/week (~$61/mo), weekly billing, NO `trial_period_days` in checkout — only a 7-day manual refund window; weekly = ~52 payment attempts/yr (worst churn case).
- Elite's sole entitlement-backed increment is `canGetAlerts`; CLAUDE.md's Elite promises (early access, analytics) are NOT entitlement-backed — Elite is effectively "Pro + notifications"; the $4/wk (40%) step buys only alerts.
- Gated-confidence tease is correctly implemented (Free sees Edge Score, data-quality meter, evidence audit drawer; confidence/edge-detail/factor-trail locked as named placeholders) BUT the lock is inert: `LockedValue` has no click target, no /pricing link, no price — dead conversion atom (the one safe code change recommended: link it to /pricing with price in label).
- Verified absences: no analytics/event tracking anywhere (only .next build artifacts match); no lifecycle/email infra (no resend/nodemailer/sendgrid/postmark) — no welcome/dunning/winback emails; no tier-aware upgrade banner on dashboard; dunning is passive (webhook records PAST_DUE but nothing acts — no Smart Retries, no card-updater); no winback, no churn-save at cancel.
- Recommended north-star: aha = "viewed ≥1 evidence audit drawer in first session"; core conversion mechanism = named+locked (not hidden) confidence numbers paired with retrospective proof ("Pro users saw this graded result").
- 11-experiment backlog with kill signals (P0: clickable lock links, funnel instrumentation, dashboard banner; P1: guarantee promotion, reverse trial, monthly+annual plans, dunning sequence; P2: real Elite differentiation (CLV/early-access entitlements), winback, founding cohort; P3: retrospective-proof locks); global guardrail: refund/unsubscribe/complaint rates must stay flat.

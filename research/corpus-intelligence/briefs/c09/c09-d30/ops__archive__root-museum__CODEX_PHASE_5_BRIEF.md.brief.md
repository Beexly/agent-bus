## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Phase 5 scope: 6-8 weeks (largest phase), 6 deliverables + Researcher Program + adversarial v1 graduation; blocked on 7 Phase-4 prerequisites (e.g., ≥100 calibration-training opt-ins, Chrome extension ≥1,000 installs, GitHub Issues ≥20 with public triage, DEC-OPEN-E/A resolved).

- Programmable DSL: hand-rolled or PEG.js parser → typed AST, type checker, sandbox with 6 hard constraints (no eval, no Function ctor, no file/network, no mutation, no loops/recursion, bounded time+memory), runtime walks AST against Pick + GameSignal (no codegen), alerts hook into 30-min refresh cycle (Elite only: email, SMS/Twilio, Discord DM, webhook).
- Cross-sport correlation: trigger + observation window + outcome parametric queries against full historical settled-pick + signal data; significance qualitative only (WEAK/MODERATE/STRONG — no p-values); mandatory disclaimer.
- B2B: 5 `/api/intelligence/*` endpoints as thin wrappers around Intelligence Graph projections (`projectForSurface(node, B2B_*, viewer)`); 4 iframe widgets; key management with domain whitelist + usage metering (`ApiKey`/`ApiKeyUsage`); compliance scanner on generated responses; no public EV/Kelly/win-rate leakage through B2B surfaces.
- Graduation: anti-Galaxy v1 goes public only after 100 settled anti-picks + owner DEC-OPEN-F approval.

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0769-what-makes-forest-based-heterogeneous-treatment.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A dissection of which computational ingredient drives causal-forest success: unifying causal forests (grf) and model-based forests (model4you) under one additive L₂ model, then benchmarking blended variants across 16 simulated scenarios — finding that local centering of the treatment indicator with propensity scores is the dominant driver, with outcome centering secondary. The file's own verdict: ADAPT — a direct recipe for GSE's causal questions (rest, surface, weather, coaching changes, lineup decisions) estimated as heterogeneous treatment effects.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Unifying framework: E[Y|x,w] = m̂(x) + τ(x)(w−π̂(x)) (cf / mobcf); with simultaneous prognostic+predictive splits: E[Y|x,w] = m̂(x) + μ̃(x) + τ(x)(w−π̂(x)) (mob(Ŵ,Ŷ)). Bivariate score: s_mob(Ŵ,Ŷ)(μ̃,τ̂) = (Y−m̂(x)−μ̃−τ̂(w−π̂(x)))·(1, w−π̂(x))ᵀ. Variants: mob (no centering), mob(Ŵ) (treatment centered only), mob(Ŵ,Ŷ) (both centered, simultaneous splits), mobcf (both centered, splits only in τ). Extensions: Poisson forest ℓ = exp(μ+τw)−(μ+τw)Y; interval-censored transformation forests via Φ-difference negative log-likelihood. Propensities π̂(x) and marginal means m̂(x) estimated by grf regression forests. Metric: MSE of τ̂(x) vs τ(x) on N=1000 test sample, 100 replications; mixed model with log-link → MSE ratios with simultaneous 95% CIs.
## Data sources named
Simulation benchmark (Nie & Wager 2021 setups A–D): X ~ U([0,1]^P) or N(0,I), P ∈ {10,20} (5 informative + noise), N ∈ {800,1600}, 100 replications; propensity functions π_A = max{0.1,min{sin(πx₁x₂),0.9}}, π_B ≡ 0.5, π_C = 1/(1+exp(x₂+x₃)), π_D = 1/(1+exp(−x₁)+exp(−x₂)). Application: 1,300 women, Zurich prospective study Oct 2015–Nov 2016 (one 5700 mL outlier + 8 missing-BMI rows removed); outcome = measured blood loss (mL), right-skewed, interval-censored (50 mL width ≤1 L, 100 mL above); 8 prepartum covariates. Clinical data not public. Code: R packages grf and model4you (CRAN).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
RQ1 (cf vs mob): cf beats mob in all 16 scenarios — Setup A (N=800,P=10): MSE ratio 0.663 (0.596, 0.738); Setup C (strong confounding, constant τ): 0.148 (0.141, 0.156) — mob collapses; Setup B (randomized): 0.707 (0.662, 0.756). RQ4 (mob(Ŵ) vs mob — treatment centering alone): Setup A 0.392 (0.334, 0.461); Setup C 0.197 (0.190, 0.205) — ~5× MSE reduction from centering W alone; Setup B (no confounding): 1.000, no effect as expected. RQ2 (simultaneous prognostic+predictive splits vs cf-style τ-only splits): mobcf's MSE ~45–69% higher in A/B/C (ratios 1.446–1.693); Setup D exception (0.967–0.983). RQ3 (cf vs mobcf reimplementation): near-identical in A/B (1.111–1.201, mostly n.s.) — implementation details matter little; the centering is what matters. RQ5 (adding outcome centering on top): 0.988–1.061 in A (no improvement), 1.533–1.734 in B, 2.258–2.615 in C (large when propensity easy to estimate), 1.099–1.157 in D. Headline: treatment centering dominates; outcome centering is secondary and partly replaceable by simultaneous prognostic+predictive splitting. Application: mean personalized cesarean effect τ̂ = 0.823 (transformed scale); gestational age ~270 days, birth weight ~3050 g, singleton births associated with small median losses for vaginal deliveries. Limitations flagged in file: Gaussian additive benchmark only; propensity-estimation errors propagate; no comparison to X-learners/BART/neural CATE estimators; P ≤ 20, N ≤ 1600 — high-dimensional tracking settings untested.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER: GSE's default CATE recipe — for any binary "treatment" (short rest, turf surface, new play-caller, starter benched) and outcome Y (EPA/play, win, injury): (1) fit propensity forest π̂(x) on game/player covariates; (2) optionally fit marginal-mean forest m̂(x); (3) fit model-based forest on E[Y|x,t] = m̂(x) + τ(x)(t−π̂(x)) with simultaneous prognostic+predictive splits. Skip outcome centering unless propensity estimation is clean (validated overlap/positivity).
- COACHING: directly applies to coaching-decision causal questions — heterogeneous effect of a play-caller change, 4th-down aggression shifts, or lineup decisions on EPA/win probability.
- OTHER: minimum viable version = treatment-centered only (mob(Ŵ)) — captures most of the gain (0.197–0.392 MSE ratios under confounding); ~2 days to implement in Python, ~1 day per causal question thereafter.
- OTHER: count/censored-outcome extensions license CATE on injury counts / touchdown counts (Poisson forest) and snap-share / games-missed outcomes (interval-censored likelihood).

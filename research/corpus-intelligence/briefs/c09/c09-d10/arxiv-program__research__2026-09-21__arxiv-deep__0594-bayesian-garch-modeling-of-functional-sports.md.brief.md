# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0594-bayesian-garch-modeling-of-functional-sports.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A hierarchical Bayesian model of elite shot-put careers (41,033 competitions, 653 athletes) that combines a latent-factor spline functional component with seasonal random intercepts carrying GARCH(1,1) errors to capture volatility clustering in performance across seasons. The file's own verdict: ADAPT — port the GARCH-on-seasonal-intercepts volatility idea and the latent-factor trajectory decomposition; the shot-put setting itself has no NFL content.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Model: y_ij = f_i(t_ij) + μ_is + x_i(t)β + ε_ij, ε ~ N(0,ψ²). Seasonal intercepts μ_is = m + ζ_is with GARCH(1,1) errors: h_is = α_0 + α_1·ζ_{is−1}² + ϖ·h_{is−1}, stationarity α_1 + ϖ < 1. Functional component: cubic B-splines (80 df) with sparse latent-factor model on coefficients θ_im = Σ_l λ_ml·η_il + ξ_im, λ under Bhattacharya–Dunson multiplicative-gamma-process shrinkage prior. Inference: blocked Gibbs sampler; adaptive Metropolis (target acceptance 0.234) for GARCH parameters; 20,000 iterations, 60% burn-in, thinning 5. Model comparison by LPML (log pseudo-marginal likelihood).
## Data sources named
World Athletics elite shot-put competitions from tilastopaja.eu (ethical approval Prop_72_2017_18), 1996–2016: 41,033 observations on 653 athletes (309 men, 344 women), best-result-per-competition (range 10.6–22.56 m, mean 17.30). Covariates: sex, age, environment (indoor/outdoor: 13,200 vs 27,800 obs), doping flag (18 athletes). Code: github.com/PatricDolmeta/Bayesian-GARCH-Modeling-of-Functional-Sports-Data.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
LPML: M1 (GARCH) = −45943; M1^(2) vague prior = −46573; M2 (fixed age) = −45472 (best); M3 (AR seasonal) = −46544; M4 (120 df) = −46314; M5 (doping) = −48565; M6 = −48122. Authors prefer M1 because its regressors are significant. M1 posterior: β1 (sex) = −0.120, sd 0.0270, ESS 190, 95% CI [−0.175, −0.0675]; β2 (age) = 6.22e-3, sd 9.95e-4, ESS 170, [4.20e-3, 8.20e-3] (performance improves through career); β3 (environment) = 0.0453, sd 9.55e-3, ESS 1600, [0.0269, 0.0643] (outdoor better). Doping (M5): β4 = −0.116, sd 0.0761, ESS 70, [−0.267, −0.0365] — unreliable, n=18; M6: β4 = −0.0103, interval contains zero. Structural: seasonal random intercept captures the majority of variability; the "functional" component collapses to a seasonal step function in practice (80-df spline support confines each basis to one season) — the latent-factor machinery adds little beyond the GARCH seasonal intercepts. Limitations noted in file: no out-of-sample predictive scoring (LPML in-sample); data centered on athlete means (coefficients describe variability around own average); GARCH stationarity tested, not imposed in priors.

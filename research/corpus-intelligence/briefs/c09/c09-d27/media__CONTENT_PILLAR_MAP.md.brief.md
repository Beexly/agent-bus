## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Player Signal Lab pillar hooks: "His box score was bad. His role was elite" and "Target share alone is lying to you" — target share explicitly called a misleading metric in doctrine; compliance note: no fabricated role data; separate signal from certainty.
- Market Mirage pillar hooks: "Everyone is reading this line wrong"; "The line moved, but the signal did not" — line movement can mislead; failure mode is treating movement as proof.
- Loss Autopsy pillar: weekly autopsy content, settled-evidence only, no cherry-picking.
- Standing pillar rules: no copyrighted broadcast footage assumed usable; no founder story replaces shipped proof; every sponsor/affiliate mention requires disclosure.

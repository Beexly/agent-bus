## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read of arXiv:2601.06647v1 (WACV 2026 workshop): eSkiTB, a synthetic event-camera (neuromorphic sensor) tracking benchmark built from skiing broadcast footage via the v2e simulator, comparing a spiking transformer (SDTrack) against an RGB transformer (STARK). The file's ledger verdict is **REJECT** — no transfer path to GSE.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Iso-informational conversion: raw 25–60 Hz RGB frames → v2e simulator with neural interpolation deliberately disabled (deterministic photometrically-faithful conversion). Simulator params: Cpos = Cneg = 0.2, τleak = 0.01 Hz, f3dB = 300 Hz, shot noise 0.001 Hz. Only stated equation: dense-label interpolation b(t) = Spline(t; {b_k, t_k}) — cubic spline over keyframe boxes preserving C² continuity of ballistic trajectory. Evaluation: one-pass evaluation (OPE), per-sequence means of mean IoU, Precision@20 px, Success@0.5 IoU. Trackers: STARK (RGB) in generic/fine-tuned/ski-specific variants vs SDTrack (spiking transformer on 128×128×T voxel grids).
## Data sources named
eSkiTB: 300 sequences (~235 min) from SkiTB ski-broadcast dataset, 1280×720 synthetic event streams, HDF5 (t,x,y,p); splits 240/30/30; three disciplines (alpine/freestyle/ski jumping), 98 locations, 10 weather categories. Release: github.com/eventbasedvision/eSkiTB.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Fine-tuned SDTrack overall: 0.711 mean IoU / 0.720 Precision@20px / 0.873 Success@0.5. STARK ski-specific still leads: 0.829 / 0.887 / 0.935. High-clutter split (9 sequences): SDTrack 0.685 IoU, +20.0 IoU points over generic STARK. Discipline splits: ski jumping SDTrack 0.974 vs STARKski +5.9 IoU; freestyle 0.569 vs 0.728 STARKft (attributed to 128×128 voxel quantization aliasing). Fine-tuning gain for SDTrack: +0.399 IoU over pretrained (0.312). Event rate: mean >10×10⁶ events/s, 95th percentile 1.58 Meps. Limitations: sim-to-real gap (all events simulated), 128×128 voxel resolution bottleneck, no RGB+event fusion baseline, RGB still wins when domain-optimized.

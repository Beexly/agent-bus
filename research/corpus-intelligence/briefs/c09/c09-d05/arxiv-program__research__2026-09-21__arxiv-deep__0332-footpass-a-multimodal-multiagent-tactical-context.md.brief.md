# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0332-footpass-a-multimodal-multiagent-tactical-context.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read of arXiv:2511.16183v1: FOOTPASS, the first public benchmark coupling full-length soccer broadcast video with human play-by-play labels and tactical game-state context, used to test whether tactical/game-state priors can denoise noisy visual action detections. The file's ledger verdict is **ADAPT** — port the two-stage "noisy visual detections denoised by game-state priors" paradigm to automate NFL broadcast highlight timestamping.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Three methods: (1) TAAD — visual-only Track-Aware Action Detector (fine-tuned X3D-L + ROI Align + TCN); (2) TAAD+GNN — visual features + game-state spatiotemporal graph (Edge Convolution + TCN + GRU, focal loss); (3) TAAD+DST — Denoising Sequence Transduction: post-processes TAAD logits with role-based game-state features over long 750-frame sequences (three cross-entropy losses: frame, player role, action). No equations stated (losses named, not formalized). Evaluation: Precision/Recall at τ=0.15 confidence, ±12-frame tolerance, per-class AP (11-point interpolation).
## Data sources named
FOOTPASS: 54 full-length matches (Ligue 1, Bundesliga, Serie A, La Liga, Champions League, 2023/24), 102,992 human-annotated on-ball events in 8 classes (pass 49.9%, drive 39.0%, header 4.0%, cross 2.3%, throw-in 1.9%, block 1.4%, shot 1.2%, tackle 0.3%); per-frame game-state (positions/velocities, team memberships, jerseys, 13 roles) via FIFA EPTS-certified tracking; splits 48/3/3 matches. Baselines at github.com/JeremieOchin/FOOTPASS; videos via SoccerNet (NDA).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Overall (PR/REC/F1 ×10² at τ=0.15): TAAD 25.6/59.9/35.9; TAAD+GNN 44.5/62.7/52.1; TAAD+DST 68.2/66.8/67.5; ablated DST (no multi-agent reasoning) 45.0/54.0/49.1 — the F1 jump 49.1→67.5 isolates the gain as coming from role/team-conditioned priors, not just better post-processing. Per-class F1 (DST): pass 72.3, drive 68.5, cross 56.8, throw-in 62.0, shot 65.2, header 28.3, block 18.0, tackle 0.0. Recall without acting-player bbox: TAAD 3.9%, DST 33.4% (~9–10% of DST true positives lack bboxes vs ~1% for TAAD). Prior private dataset (240 matches): 78.7% precision / 75.8% recall — performance scales with data quantity. Tactical denoising mildly degrades headers/blocks (less-intentional actions where long-range structure doesn't apply).
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER: video-content pipeline tooling — the two-stage detect-then-denoise-with-game-state-priors paradigm ports to GSE's clip workflow: Stage 1 noisy visual detector on NFL broadcasts, Stage 2 denoiser conditioned on nflverse play-by-play (down/distance, score, EPA) as the tactical prior, emitting high-precision highlight timestamps (EPA > 1.5, turnovers, scores) feeding the telestrated-edit workflow via the approve-desk flow.
## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — the spec's reproducible test: 10 full 2024 NFL broadcasts aligned to nflverse play-by-play, spot highlight plays (EPA > 1.5/turnover/score) with Precision/Recall at ±2 s; ADOPT only if the denoised pipeline hits ≥80% recall at ≥60% precision and beats detector-alone by ≥20 pp precision; extension idea: invert the architecture — supervised denoiser trained directly on (noisy detector → nflverse labels) plus crowd-noise audio as a weak excitement signal.

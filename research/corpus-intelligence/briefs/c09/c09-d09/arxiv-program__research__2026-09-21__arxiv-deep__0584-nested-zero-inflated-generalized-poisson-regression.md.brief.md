# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0584-nested-zero-inflated-generalized-poisson-regression.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read note on Gilch (arXiv:2205.04173v3): a nested zero-inflated generalized Poisson (ZIGP) regression for forecasting the entire FIFA World Cup 2022 — the stronger team's goals are modeled from both attack and defense perspectives and averaged, then the weaker team's goals are modeled *conditional on* the stronger team's realized score (nested dependence); history is date/importance-weighted, and Elo ratings are updated *inside* each Monte Carlo replication (form-over-tournament effect). File verdict is ADAPT — port the nesting, weighting, and in-simulation rating-update machinery to GSE's NFL playoff/Super Bowl probability engine (the ZIGP likelihood itself is soccer-specific).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Nested model: (1a) ZIGP regression of A's goals on B's Elo + location: log μ_A(Elo_B) = α₀⁽¹⁾+α₁⁽¹⁾Elo_B+α₂⁽¹⁾loc; φ_A = 1+e^{β⁽¹⁾}; ω_A = γ⁽¹⁾/(1+γ⁽¹⁾). (1b) ZIGP regression of B's goals against on A's Elo + location: log ν_B(Elo_A) = α₀⁽²⁾+α₁⁽²⁾Elo_A+α₂⁽²⁾loc. (1c) Average the two parameter sets → (μ_{A|B}, φ_{A|B}, ω_{A|B}). (2) ZIGP regression of B's goals on A's Elo + location + realized G_A: log μ̄_{B|A} = α₀⁽³⁾+α₁⁽³⁾Elo_A+α₂⁽³⁾loc+α₃⁽³⁾G_A. Likelihood factorization: P(G_A=i, G_B=j) = P(G_A=i)·P(G_B=j|G_A=i).
- ZIGP pmf: P(X=0) = ω+(1−ω)e^{−μ/φ}; P(X=k) = (1−ω)·μ(μ+(φ−1)k)^{k−1}/k!·φ^{−k}·e^{−(μ+(φ−1)k)/φ}, k≥1; reduces to Poisson at ω=0, φ=1. E(X) = (1−ω)μ; Var(X) = (1−ω)μ(φ²+ωμ).
- Elo update: Elo_after = Elo_before + K·G·(W−W_e); W_e = 1/(10^{−D/400}+1), D = Elo diff; G = 1 (draw/1-goal win), 1.5 (2-goal win), (11+N)/8 otherwise; K=60 (WC), 50 (continental).
- Date weight: w_date(m) = (1/2)^{D(m)/H}, H = 3 years; importance weight: 4 (WC), 3 (continental), 2.5 (qualifier/Nations League), 1 (other); w(m) = product.
- Worked example (France 2005 vs Denmark 1971, neutral): μ_France(1971) = exp(2.472632−0.0010679575·1971+0.2724600768·0) = 1.444391; ω_France = e^{−4.058738}/(1+e^{−4.058738}) = 0.0169776; mean goals = 1.419869. ν_Denmark(2005) = exp(−4.205890+0.0021582919·2005−0.371076439·0) = 1.129173. Averaged: μ_{France|Denmark} = 1.27585, φ = 1.000005, ω = 0.008495965. Denmark's μ̄ = exp(3.118465−0.0013932201·2005−0.03989474·G_A+0.051954905·0) = 1.32998 at G_A=1.
- Scoring: Brier BS = Σ_T Σ_{j=1}^6 (p_j(T)−1[result(T)=j])²; RPS = Σ_T (1/5)Σ_{i=1}^5 (Σ_{j=1}^i p_j(T)−1[result=j])², result ∈ {1=champion … 6=group exit}. Goodness of fit: χ²_T = Σ_i (x_i−μ̂_i)²/μ̂_i per team.
## Data sources named
All matches of the 32 World Cup 2022 participants from 2016-01-01 to 2022-10-30 (eloratings.net data + World Football Elo ratings), weighted by date (half-life 3 years) and FIFA importance weights (4/3/2.5/1). Covariates: Elo points (top-6 on 2022-10-30: Brazil 2169, Argentina 2141, Spain 2045, Netherlands 2040, Belgium 2025, France 2005), match location (home/neutral/away coded 1/0/−1), and (for the weaker team) the stronger team's realized goals. Validation: back-simulation of World Cups 2010/2014/2018 and EURO 2016/2020. Fitted in R 4.0.3 with the ZIGP package. No model-code repository stated.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Tournament backtests (Brier / RPS, ZIGP vs bivariate Poisson vs independent Poisson): WC 2010: 17.79/4.93 vs 17.97/4.99 vs 17.97/5.05; WC 2014: 20.18/5.06 vs 22.33/5.52 vs 22.10/5.48; WC 2018: 18.51/5.54 vs 18.00/5.51 vs 18.39/5.51 (ZIGP worse; author blames Germany's group-stage collapse after 10/10 qualifying wins — stale form locked in by recency weighting); EURO 2016: 17.52/5.28 vs 23.27/5.97 vs 23.25/5.98 (ZIGP clearly best); EURO 2020: 14.54/5.06 vs 14.36/5.07 vs 16.37/5.19 (tie).
- GoF: most teams p≥0.14 on (2.1); exceptions Ghana, Costa Rica (attack), Argentina (p=0.005), Canada, Uruguay (defense), Qatar/US (weaker-team model) — mostly weak teams with few observations.
- WC 2022 forecast (100k sims): Brazil champion 17.3%, Argentina 13.1%, Belgium 10.8%, Spain 10.2%, Netherlands 10.0%, France 8.4%; England 3.5%, Germany 3.6%. (Actual 2022: Argentina won — inside the model's top-2.)
- The 3-year half-life and FIFA importance weights are fixed by convention (Ley et al. 2019), not tuned — the author admits non-weighting sometimes performs no worse.

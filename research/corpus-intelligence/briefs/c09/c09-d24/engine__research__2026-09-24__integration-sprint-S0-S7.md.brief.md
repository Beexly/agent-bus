# engine/research/2026-09-24/integration-sprint-S0-S7.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Integration sprint plan (generated 2026-09-25T04:51:54Z from Firecrawl research against Beexly/Sports) to move GSE from a safety-gated illustrative platform to a real, model-owned fantasy/prediction data product — a phased S0→S7 sequence covering truth/rights freeze, slate plane, point-in-time feature store, model-owned projections, calibration, contest decision engine, shadow signal activation, and production reliability. Ordering principle: no more signal breadth until identity, point-in-time availability, durable model artifacts and evaluation receipts are real.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Methodology is process/sequencing, not formulas. S0: canonical entity IDs + provider-ID crosswalks, immutable raw SourceSnapshot (source_as_of, fetched_at, available_at/created_at, correction lineage, parser version, raw hash), fail-closed paid-data governor. S1: Contest/ContestSlate/ContestRule/SalarySnapshot/PlayerEligibility/RosterSlot/ProjectionRun/Lineup/LineupSlot entities; replace illustrative DFS rows with blocked empty state until real inputs pass the rights/freshness gate. S2: FeatureDefinition registry, event-time + available-time filtering in training-set builder, label ledger, decision-timestamp-keyed snapshots. S3: hierarchical partial-pooling player-team model (player/team/opponent/venue/season), separate availability/minutes/role model from per-minute production model, joint team/game latent variables, distributional outputs (median, quantiles, floor/ceiling, variance, P(over threshold), scenario states), ModelArtifact/ModelVersion registry. S4: purged time-ordered train/val/holdout, proper scoring (log loss/Brier for probabilities; CRPS/interval score for distributions; reliability/ECE; calibration slope/intercept; sharpness; coverage), time-aware conformal uncertainty wrapper, machine-readable EvaluationReceipt, promotion/kill gates. S5: ownership/selection model (time-sliced training, separated from performance probability), contest utility layer (payouts, entry fee, roster constraints, field size, duplication), joint simulation of player outcomes + ownership + opponent lineups; cash/GPP/leverage as separate decision policies; simulation-based optimizer on distributions + covariance; post-lock result ledger. S6: shadow-first activation (one factor at a time) of injuries/transactions, weather/venue/travel, tracking/event data, news/beat signals with timestamps and bot/manipulation controls, sensitive data behind consent. S7: champion/challenger registry, drift dashboards, per-projection evidence artifact, public claims compiler that cannot say proven/accurate/superior unless gates pass.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Sequencing verdict: S0 (truth/identity/rights freeze) must complete before training a production fantasy model; S1–S4 must produce real distributions before S5's contest engine; no public superiority claim before S4's sealed evaluation gate.
- S3 specifies the model architecture shape: market-only/role-only baseline + hierarchical partial-pooling model; separate availability/minutes/role from per-minute production; propagate availability/role uncertainty into stat-line distributions; joint team/game latents so player outcomes preserve game totals, shared pace/possessions, teammate/opponent correlation.
- S4 evaluation protocol: purged, time-ordered, season/game/player-grouped walk-forward; sealed holdout; market-only baseline; metrics log loss/Brier, CRPS/interval score, ECE, calibration slope/intercept, coverage; time-aware conformal wrapper with coverage/interval-width diagnostics by sport, position, horizon, contest type, data-quality bucket.
- S5 contest decision engine: ownership/selection model trained time-sliced and kept separate from performance probability; joint simulation of outcomes + ownership + opponent-lineup construction; optimizer consumes distributions and covariance, not static proj/ceiling/ownership constants.
- S7 public-claims compiler: cannot assert proven/accurate/superior unless the linked evaluation receipt and current gates pass — an enforcement primitive for marketing copy.

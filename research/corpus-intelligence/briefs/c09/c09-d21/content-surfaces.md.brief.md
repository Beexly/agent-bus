# content-surfaces.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Editorial spec (Phase 5 "Human Marketing") defining four recurring publishing surfaces — "What I'm watching tonight" (daily), "Why the model stayed quiet" (as-needed held-game explainer), "Signal autopsy" (weekly settled-pick review), "One number that moved" (2–3x/week market datapoint) — all in the founder's first-person voice with templates, filled examples, and a content-as-code publishing flow.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No formulas. Quantitative cadence/length specs: "watching" 150–300 words ≤90s read, published ~3h before slate (~90 min before first game); "quiet" 100–200 words per held game, mandatory closing line "I'm telling you this so the silence is legible, not so you bet it"; "autopsy" 400–700 words weekly Mondays, settled pick ≥24h old; "number" 60–120 words, closes "That's the number. Make of it what you will." First-30-day projection: ~30 watching, 10–25 quiet, 4 autopsy, 8–12 number = 50–70 piece corpus. Editorial rules: first-person singular, every number cites a source, no prediction language unbacked by engine, no promo CTAs in body, no emojis, banned-phrase linter scan, read-aloud test, older-reader friendly. Frontmatter schema validates: relatedFactorKeys exist in registry, relatedPickIds exist in DB, quiet picks have gateState ≠ published, autopsy picks settled ≥24h old.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- The four surfaces' role: publish the engine's thinking, not the picks — marketing voice and engine voice are the same voice.
- Gate examples named: "Sample size — starStarter availability" (required LeBron's status confirmed ≥90 min before tip; market moved Lakers -3.5 → -2.5 overnight), "Data freshness — weather feed" (required weather snapshot <60 min old for outdoor games; provider's snapshot was 87 min prior; wind factor in shadow, line move consistent with 18mph crosswind forecast at Hard Rock, total 49.5→47).
- Autopsy example (Lakers -2.5 vs Suns loss, settled May 18): market factors at publish — 9 books, line Lakers -1→-2.5, 71% consensus on Lakers, 56% implied prob; shadow factors — Booker questionable-but-played (scored 33), referee crew -3.2 fewer fouls/game vs avg; confidence 64 = one tick above publish floor 50, two ticks above tier-3 floor 62; starStarter shadow factor at 21 days vs 30-day proposal threshold → if activated, this pick would have been held.
- "Number" examples: Bills/Dolphins total 49.5→47 with wind forecast 8→18mph; Celtics/Heat spread -3.5→-4.5 steamed by 6 ET with volume up but no confirmed sharp source (sharp-money factor shadow, low trust).
- Content-as-code flow: founder writes markdown + frontmatter in docs/content-archive/{surface}/ → build validates schema + brand-safety linter → server-rendered routes → RSS/JSON feeds. Explicitly NOT a blog, NOT picks marketing, NOT volume-follower play.

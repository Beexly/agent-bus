## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of Constantinou (2022), "Investigating the efficiency of the Asian handicap football betting market with ratings and Bayesian networks" (Journal of Sports Analytics 8(3):171–193; arXiv:2003.09384v2) — the first published model built specifically for Asian Handicap prediction/efficiency assessment, combining modified pi-ratings with a hybrid Bayesian network (possession→shots→goals causal chain), evaluated over 13 EPL seasons with a betting simulation sweeping decision thresholds θ from 0%–25% under average vs maximum odds. Verdict in file: ADAPT — soccer-specific AH apparatus does not port directly, but four portable results do (profit-optimal threshold policy, average-vs-max-odds gap quantifying line-shopping value, pi-ratings tweaks, NFL point-spread as structural analog of AH).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Modified pi-ratings: each team carries home and away ratings; updated after each match with learning rate λ (recency) and cross-influence rate γ (home↔away). Two modifications vs Constantinou & Fenton (2013): (1) ratings predict actual goal difference — the diminishing function f(e) = 3·log10(1+e) is REMOVED because AH cares only about goal differences; (2) λ multiplied by k = 3 for matches involving a team with <38 prior matches (faster convergence for promoted/new teams; k integer-optimized 1–10, real-valued optimization left as future work). [Note in ledger: PDF extraction garbled the rating-update equations; structure confirmed by prose — R_home(t) = R_home(t−1) + λ·(e), error e = observed goal difference − rating difference, cross-learning via γ.]
- Hybrid Bayesian network (domain-knowledge structure, temporal chain): Rating Difference (RD = prior home rating − prior away rating, the only observed node) → Possession → shot-generation rate per possession minute → Shots → on-target rate → Shots on Target → goal rate → Goals → Goal Difference (GD = GH − GA) → 1X2 node and AH-outcome nodes. Beta-Binomial conjugate parameterization (hyperparameters count minutes/shots/on-targets/goals); RD discretized into 23 levels (Table 4), each with >50 data points; built in AgenaRisk (commercial). RD input as mixture of 23 Gaussians (e.g., level 3 spans RD ∈ [1.765, 1.93)). Exact density parameterizations garbled in extraction; structure confirmed by prose.
- Betting simulation: fixed single-unit (£1) bets on any 1X2/AH outcome where the model's estimated unbiased payoff exceeds the market payoff by at least θ (payoff discrepancy), tested at every integer θ from 0% to 25%, under average and maximum market odds, optimizing for profit or ROI, with θ either static across all 13 seasons (Tables 9, 12 — "realistic") or re-optimized per season (Tables 10, 11, 13, 14 — "unrealistic").
- Validation: ratings strictly temporal (ratings at t−1); BN fit with leave-one-out CV ignoring time (author justifies: possession→shots→goals relationships assumed time-invariant for the average EPL team; consistent accuracy across all 13 seasons including pre-BN-training-data seasons 2006/07–2008/09, plus 2nd-place finish in the Machine Learning for Soccer competition). Metrics: RPS (1X2), Brier score (AH), realized profit and ROI at each θ. Timeseries drift analysis (Fig 4) + sample-size table (Table 8): ≥5 seasons of league data needed for well-informed BN priors.
## Data sources named
EPL seasons 1992/93–2018/19 from www.football-data.co.uk and manually recorded from www.nowgoal.com. Ratings trained on up to 27 seasons (goal data only); BN parameterized on up to 10 seasons (ball-possession data available only from 2009/10); betting simulation over 13 seasons (2006/07–2018/19) since AH odds only exist from 2006/07. Rating optimization restricted to matches where both teams had ≥38 prior matches: 9,073 match instances. Data variables (Table 5): Date/Home team/Away team (ratings); 1X2 outcome; home/away possession rate, shots, shots on target, goals (BN); handicap, average and maximum 1X2 odds, average and maximum AH odds (betting simulation). Data public (football-data.co.uk); no code stated (AgenaRisk commercial); R package `piratings` referenced for the base system (Van Cutsem, 2019).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Rating optimization: optimal λ = 0.018, γ = 0.7 (k = 3), mean goal-difference error 1.2283 — below prior pi-ratings reports (2.625–2.66). 31 nearby (λ, γ) combos within 0.01% discrepancy (Table 6). Linear RD→expected-goal-difference relationship (Fig 3): RD level 23 (most away-favored) → −1.38; level 1 (most home-favored) → +2.18.
- Predictive accuracy (Table 7): RPS (1X2) average 0.195 (season range 0.184–0.213); Brier score (AH) average 0.248 (range 0.199–0.267) across 13 seasons.
- 1X2 betting, static θ (Table 9): average odds — profit maximizes at θ=8% (814 bets, profit £44.7, ROI 5.49%); ROI maximizes at θ=18% (37 bets, ROI 23.59%). Maximum odds — profit max at θ=9% (1,049 bets, profit £177.18, ROI 16.89%); ROI max at θ=16% (132 bets, ROI 21.20%).
- 1X2 per-season optimization: ROI-optimal-per-season θ → overall ROI 9.03% avg odds (Table 10); profit-optimal-per-season θ → overall ROI 12.96% avg odds (Table 11) — optimizing per-season ROI lowers overall ROI. Max odds: 26.69% (Table 10) vs 17.37% (Table 11).
- AH betting, static θ (Table 12): average odds — profit max at θ=16% (201 bets, profit £9.20, ROI 4.57%); max odds — profit max at θ=9% (1,307 bets, profit £18.06, ROI 1.38%). Per-season (Tables 13/14): overall avg-odds ROI 5.70% (ROI-optimal θ) vs 6.33% (profit-optimal θ).
- Section 6 conclusions (i–vi): (i) maximum odds increase profits 42%–296% over average odds; (ii) AH bets target favorites (odds 1.6–1.8) while 1X2 bets target outsiders (odds >3); (iii) AH profit/ROI ≈ 2.5–5.5×/2.5–4× lower than 1X2, less volatile — but stake-scaling AH by 5.59× to match 1X2 profit eliminates the risk advantage (Fig 6); (iv) optimal θ varies dramatically between seasons — per-season threshold optimization (Tables 10, 11, 13, 14) is not realistic; only Tables 9/12 are; single-season threshold-optimized profitability reporting "should be discouraged"; (v) loss-making seasons possible under average odds even at the best θ; max odds remove the risk but some seasons barely profit; (vi) per-season ROI optimization hurts long-term ROI; per-season profit optimization guarantees maximal total profit and usually higher overall ROI.
- Limitations noted in ledger: BN validation non-temporal (uses future matches to fit CPDs); AH odds for first five seasons often reflect ±0-ball rather than uniform AH handicap; only one handicap per match; static-θ results still assume hindsight-chosen θ over 13 seasons — real-world performance worse; soccer-only, EPL-only, pre-COVID data.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER — betting-market efficiency and staking/threshold policy, not QB/coaching/OL/scheme. Directly sports-betting-relevant: the NFL point-spread/total markets are the structural analogs of AH (binary, handicap-based, ~50/50 pricing), so the efficiency-assessment protocol should be replicated on NFL spreads/totals. Complements the Kelly/staking lane (papers 0171, 0174 in the corpus) with a complementary rule on how to choose the EV threshold. Portable results for GSE: (1) optimize bet-selection thresholds for total profit, not per-season ROI; (2) the average-vs-maximum-odds gap (profits 42%–296% higher on max odds) quantifies the value of GSE's multi-book line shopping; (3) pi-ratings modifications (raw goal-difference/margin target without diminishing transform, k=3 new-team acceleration) are portable rating tweaks — e.g., accelerated learning for teams with <1 season of data such as post-draft roster turnover. The Beta-Binomial causal chain (possession→shots→goals) has an NFL analog worth noting (INFERENCE: plays→first downs→explosive plays→points) but the ledger marks this as not a GSE gap.

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1581-postprocessing-weather-forecasts-ml-feature-selection.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger for Iwase & Takenawa (2026; arXiv:2604.19340) on ML post-processing of JMA's 5 km Mesoscale Model: LightGBM fed with surrounding-grid context plus correlation-based feature selection beat raw NWP and the operational guidance product on temperature/wind/precipitation, with a weighted-Tweedie head for zero-inflated precipitation. Verdict: ADAPT — a cheap, well-validated tabular post-processing recipe for GSE's weather inputs, but deterministic (no uncertainty) and validated only on JMA's model over Japan.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
1grid (62 features) vs "around" (11×11 surface + 7×7 pressure grids → 5,702 features); FS0: pairwise |Pearson| > 0.9 left-to-right drop on surrounding-grid features (dropped 239–869 features/site); FS1 (target correlation), FS2 (mutual info), FS3 (LightGBM importance) tested by sweeping k in steps of 10 — none helped beyond FS0; LightGBM regression per variable (MSE loss, early stopping patience 10, Optuna LightGBMTuner); Tweedie: Var(Y)=φμ^p, loss ℒ=Σ w(yᵢ)·ℓ_Tw(yᵢ,μᵢ) with monotone piecewise-linear weights on observed precip (knots 0/5/10/15/20 mm, clipped ≤10); event metrics TS(τ)=H/(H+M+F), POD=H/(H+M), FAR=F/(H+F), Bias=(H+F)/(H+M); Optuna score S_total = S_geo − Σ γ_τ|log(max(Bias(τ),b_min))| with S_geo = geometric mean of Threat Score across 1–20 mm thresholds, γ=0.05.
## Data sources named
JMA MSM gridded forecasts (surface 505×481, pressure levels 253×241; 13 surface variables × 3 time steps + 7 pressure-level variables at 850/500/200 hPa; issued 8× daily, lead 3–39h); JMA station observations (3h precip, hourly temp, wind) at 18 sites; splits train 2019–2021 (~113k samples/site), validate 2022 (~37.8k), test 2023 (~37.9k); 3,418,272 rows total. Baselines: raw MSM, JMA operational MSMG guidance (Kalman + bias correction + NNs), NN and CNN baselines.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Test RMSE: precipitation — MSM 2.759, MSMG 2.726, around-all-tune 2.344; temperature — MSM 1.811, MSMG 1.394, CNN 1.501, around-all-tune 1.335; wind — MSM 1.999, MSMG 1.237, around-all-tune 1.052.
- Test MAE (N=682,305): temperature 0.9888 vs MSM 1.3964 vs MSMG 1.0275; wind 0.7549 vs MSM 1.4186 vs MSMG 0.9035 (all p=0.000).
- Surrounding grids beat single grid everywhere (wind test RMSE 1.053 vs 1.075); FS1–FS3 added nothing over FS0 ("accuracy stabilizes when the number of features exceeds a certain threshold").
- Weighted Tweedie raised TS at 10 mm 0.1966 → 0.2520 and at 15 mm 0.1251 → 0.1883, Bias at 10 mm 0.4340 → 0.9771; still slightly below MSMG overall (TS 0.2485 at 10 mm) and site-dependent.
- Largest wind RMSE reductions at island/coastal sites (Niijima, Oma) where raw MSM was worst.
- Limitations: deterministic point forecasts only; hyperparameters tuned on one site (Sekigahara) then applied everywhere; Japan/MSM-specific (recipe transfers, fitted models don't); independent per-site models, no spatial pooling; authors disclosed ChatGPT use for code editing/proofreading.
- File's GSE spec: build `weather/lgbm_postprocess.py` on HRRR (3 km, US) for 30 NFL stadiums (11×11 surface + 7×7 pressure windows at kickoff lead times), FS0 τ=0.9, LightGBM per variable, Optuna on one representative stadium, plus a weighted-Tweedie precipitation head for rain games (~1 week effort; HRRR via AWS Open Data).
- Acceptance gate in file: ADOPT if LightGBM beats raw HRRR by ≥10% RMSE on wind AND ≥5% on temperature on the 2023 holdout, with Tweedie improving TS at 10 mm vs plain MSE.

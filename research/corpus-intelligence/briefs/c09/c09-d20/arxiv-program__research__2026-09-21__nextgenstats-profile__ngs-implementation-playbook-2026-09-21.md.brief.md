## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Implementation playbook converting every Next Gen Stats metric family into a GSE-replicable build: NGS public definitions, documented inputs, published equations (only where actually published), public data/code/API inventory, implementation spec, backtest acceptance gates, and explicitly labeled gaps. Supersedes the deleted DRAFT version.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- CPOE = actual completion − completion probability (no published equation).
- NGS pressure rule: pressure = pressure probability exceeding 75% (nfl.com Sep 21, 2023); Pressure rate = total pressures / total pass-rush snaps (avg rusher 10.3%); Time to pressure avg 2.9s; quick pressures <2.5s; positive rush rate avg 54%.
- xRY = sum(outcome × probability) from 2D-CNN (The Zoo, 2020 Big Data Bowl winners, CRPS-scored) on 5 vector features at handoff (X, Y, S, A, Dir); RYOE = actual − xRY.
- Tackle probability: frame-level, every 0.1s, ~20 features per frame per defender (~20,000 data points per 10s play), >2M data points, trained 2018–2022, tested 2023.
- QB Passing Score: TCN encodes each player's time series → MLP → spliced binned-Pareto (SBP) output distribution; play score = rank (0–1) of actual yards in predictive CDF, standardized; trained ~50,000 passing plays (34,000 completions / 15,000 incompletes / 1,200 interceptions); inference 0.001 s/play; AWS-released PyTorch SBP code.
- Acceptance gates: CPOE replication — Brier skill ≥ 0.9 relative to NGS's published r²=0.98-equivalent calibration; run scheme transformer — beat charting-heuristic baseline by ≥5pp accuracy; pressure replication — calibration within 2pp of 10.3% average-rusher baseline; tackle model — ≥80% recall on PFF-labeled missed tackles at fixed 20% FPR; xRY — CRPS ≤ "Zoo" 2020 winning score.
- NGS standing facts: 10 Hz player sampling, 25 Hz ball sampling, 20+ ultrawideband receivers/stadium, "accurate to a few inches"; AWS tech provider since 2017; ~300M data points/season; 75+ ML models; 500–1,000 stats per play; 2026: optical pose tracking (4K, 16 angles, 29 body parts at 60 Hz).
- CPOE model description: XGBoost on SageMaker, 36,000+ pass attempts back to 2016, 10% holdout, actual vs predicted correlation r² = 0.98; ~10 input factors (~6 named publicly: air distance, target separation, sideline separation, pass rush separation, passer speed, time to throw).
- Completion probability model: XGBoost (SageMaker), trained on 36,000+ attempts since 2016, validated on 10% holdout, actual vs predicted completion r² = 0.98.
- 2026 additions: Run Scheme Classification (transformer, 16 labels, man/zone/gap + read option/split zone/pitch tags, intended vs actual gap); Run Blocking Matchups (blocking assignment, pull/down/crack blocks, engagement start/end, time to shed); Route Classification 2.0 (shallow vs deep crossers, fade vs go, inside/outside release, screen detection via blocker movement).
- Pass-rush model (2023): (1) GNN for rush vs drop role classification; (2) random forest per-defender pressure likelihood in tenths of seconds + per-rusher + team composite; (3) blocking-matchup model; history on every pass play since 2018.
- Whitepaper: "Feeling the Pressure" (Sloan MIT Sports Analytics Conference: Hong, Kulowski, Volk, Wang, Abdoo, McQuiston, Jung, Band, Socolinsky), 25-page PDF.
- Defender ghosting: CNN-LSTM, tracking accurate within 6 inches in 99.9% of cases, speed ≤12.5 yd/s, predicts next 10 positions from 3 input measurements.
- NGS Draft Model: 0–100 scale Production/Athleticism/Overall + Raw ATH 10.0 scale; 2026 examples: Sonny Styles 91/94/95; Jeremiyah Love Overall 94 (3rd among RBs behind Bijan Robinson 96, Saquon Barkley 96); David Bailey production 98 (3rd among edges last 10 classes behind Chase Young 99, Will Anderson Jr. 99).
- Observed metrics to mirror: kicker makes over expected (Fairbairn 44/48, +6.1); scramble EPA (Kyler Murray 1,993 scramble rush yards since 2019, 4th); motion at snap rate (Ingold 36.9%, led NFL, min. 250 snaps; league 53.1%); on/off-field EPA splits (Ravens +0.13 with Ricard vs −0.09 without); air yards per target (Pierce 16.9 league-high last four seasons); target EPA for DBs (Dean −30.1 lowest among outside CBs); yards per coverage snap (Woolen 0.5 in man).
- Discrepancies OPEN: Lukas Van Ness five-of-nine under 2.5s vs five pressures in second half; Josh Allen graphic CPOE 55.3% vs post text "Detroit blitzed Allen on 55.3% of dropbacks" (likely transcription error). Do not use either until verified.

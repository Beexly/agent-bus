# gse/decision-audit.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A product/offer spec for the "GSE Sports Decision Process Audit (Trust-First Diagnostic)" — a paid, founder-reviewed process audit of a sports bettor's decision-making discipline, explicitly not a picks/ROI guarantee.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Pricing: emergency lane **USD $49** one-time consult-style (one call + one-page diagnostic, manual invoice only); standard core audit lane **USD $199** non-recurring (intake, findings summary, owner sign-off path).
- Timeline: Day 0 intake/review → Day 1 evidence + audit notes drafted → Day 2 decision-process recommendations delivered → Day 3 owner-reviewed handoff packet.
- Delivery checklist: intake completed, process mapped, evidence status confirmed incl. sample constraints, risk flags documented, no performance claims in external copy, owner approval queue entry, final review approved by owner.
- Six intake questions (decision types, trusted data sources + why, hardest-to-explain losses, rule-breaking process, budget/timeline, accountability for accepting/rejecting recs).
- Proof assets: signed intake form, historical logs/bets, workflow screenshots/decision notes, optional consented transcripts.
- Owner gates: no public claim in delivered copy; no pricing changes in app/product code; owner approval required for any external messaging or paid invoice route; no automated sends without gate review.
- Definition of done: explicit no-claim language, all inputs/risks recorded in `docs/gse/owner-approval-queue.md`, delivery checklist complete, waitlist + PR2 handoff artifacts ready.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- This is a trust-first revenue-lane design: $49 emergency sprint vs $199 core audit, with a 3-day delivery cadence and hard owner gates on all external messaging.
- Explicit exclusions: not a win-rate guarantee, not an auto-pick bot, not live odds timing, not guaranteed money-management advice, not a public performance report.
- Positioning allowed: process rigor, decision-quality framing, transparency on data provenance and uncertainty, educational risk/staking content, waitlist-first invitation language ("audience-limited founder lane," "review-first").

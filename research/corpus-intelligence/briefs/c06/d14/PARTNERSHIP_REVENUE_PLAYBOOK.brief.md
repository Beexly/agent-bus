# media/PARTNERSHIP_REVENUE_PLAYBOOK.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A manual outreach and review framework for GSE founding partnerships. Updated 2026-07-04; it explicitly does not create live affiliate links, sponsor contracts, or regulated offers.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Daily outreach target: 10 messages/day, broken down as 4 creator/tool partners + 2 sports data/API partners + 2 fantasy/card partners + 1 local sponsor + 1 podcast/creator collaboration.
- Gate for sportsbook/DFS lane: only allowed after disclosure, terms URL, eligible states, restricted states, responsible-gaming text, and approved compliance status are complete.
- Otherwise not specified (no formulas, conversion targets, or revenue figures).

## Data sources named
None named. The doc is purely process/templates; it references GSE-internal categories and offers (e.g., "data workflow segment", "watchlist sponsor", "builder sponsor") without external datasets.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- 7 partner categories: Creator tools (affiliate/sponsor, low-to-medium risk); Sports data/API tools (sponsor/API beta/referral, medium risk); Fantasy tools (affiliate/sponsor, medium risk); Sports cards/collectibles (affiliate/sponsor, medium risk); Sportsbook/DFS/betting (affiliate/sponsor, HIGH risk); Cloud/AI/dev tools (sponsor/credits/partner content, low-to-medium risk); Local/regional sponsors (sponsor, low risk).
- Sportsbook/DFS lane is NOT first-lane unless six compliance items complete.
- Five outreach templates: General Partner, Local Sponsor, Tool Affiliate, Sports Data/API Partner, Podcast Guest/Collab.
- No fake audience claims, no forced endorsement, and no sponsor control over model or editorial conclusions is the standing rule in the General Partner template.
- The referenced policy doc in the funding master is `PARTNER_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md` — a slightly different filename than this file (flagged: filename mismatch worth confirming).

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- TRUST-SIGNAL: The playbook's explicit trust rules — disclosed relationships, honest reviews, no sponsor control over model or editorial conclusions, compliance gating for sportsbook/DFS — mirror the founding-launch integrity doctrine. This is a credibility/reputation safeguard layer for the business.
- OTHER: Revenue-lane taxonomy (affiliate vs sponsor vs credits vs referral models per category) informs monetization planning; sportsbook CPA is a hard refuse per the funding master.

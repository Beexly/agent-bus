# intelligence/design-experience-system.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A doctrine-only design system for Sports OS (from Prompt 1 §5 and Prompt 4 design language doctrine; parent: `docs/intelligence/SPORTS_OS_INTELLIGENCE_NETWORK_MASTER_PLAN.md`): one cinematic brand layer over disciplined data layers, with a Public surface (restraint, source transparency) and a Cockpit surface (internal, F1-telemetry-dense), no implementation without an approved change proposal.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified — no formulas/metrics. Methods/design rules captured:
- Design standard anchors (7): Bloomberg, F1 telemetry, NASA mission control, Apple, Perplexity, Glean, Linear/Vercel/Stripe/Raycast.
- 10 forbidden patterns: sportsbook clutter, casino green, cheap neon/laser-tag, crypto-dashboard noise, generic SaaS cards, fake AI sparkle (animated gradients, glowing orbs), shallow glassmorphism, overanimated sci-fi, hype copy/certainty language, unsupported confidence visuals (no confidence meters for unverified data).
- Typography doctrine: display = compressed oversized; data = tabular numerals, monospace where appropriate; body = legible small, high contrast; labels/metadata = subdued, secondary.
- Color doctrine: primary = deep space/near-black; accent = ultraviolet/electric indigo; confirmed/verified = neutral white or cool grey (NOT green — green reads as casino); warning/staleness = amber; contradiction/risk = red-orange (NOT casino red); weak signal = muted purple. Forbidden: casino green, neon yellow, hot pink, crypto orange as primary palette.
- Motion doctrine: motion = state change / attention direction only, never brand expression on data surfaces; do not animate numbers/charts in ways suggesting artificial precision; subtle on public, slightly more expressive on cockpit.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum on all public surfaces; color never sole signal-state differentiator; confidence meters must have text labels; data tables keyboard-navigable; cockpit may prioritize density over strict AA with documented trade-off.
- The Intelligence UX Contract (6 mandatory points for anything on a public surface): (1) what it is (pick/signal/rumor/model output/market signal), (2) where it came from (source tier, name if public-safe), (3) when observed (timestamp), (4) how confident (honest caveats, no fake precision), (5) what would weaken it (contradicting signals, staleness risk), (6) what the user should do (act/watch/verify/ignore). Anything failing all six stays off public surfaces.

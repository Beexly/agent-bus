## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Patent-forensics research (read-only, 2026-09-29) on Sharp Labs of America's football-video summarization patent family ("HiMpact Sports"): 7 Sharp grants (family 25464625 + replay family 26947257) plus one mis-attributed third-party patent, all traced to expiry causes, commercial history, and implementation coverage gaps.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas from the claims: play-start detection via field lines converging to a common off-field point (US7312812B2); sliding-window verification with a "hiking frame" declared when a threshold below 100% (dependent claim: 70%) of window frames qualify (US7639275B2); play-end detection via scene-change with dynamic threshold = mean frame-to-frame color-histogram difference plus 5 or 6 standard deviations (US7474331B2); replay detection via starting/ending logo transitions, with learned-logo search limited to within 1,000 frames (US7474698B2, US7653131B2). Non-formula engineering heuristics: green-field calibration, zoom cessation, frame-difference segmentation (US7499077B2 parent).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Every Sharp grant in this family is "Expired — Fee Related" (deliberate non-payment, not 20-year expiry): US7499077B2 (adj. expiry 2022-12-06), US7312812B2 (2022-03-13), US7639275B2 (2022-08-12), US7474331B2 (2023-02-28), US8018491B2 (2026-06-24), US7474698B2 (2024-12-08), US7653131B2 (2024-11-11); the Object Prediction patent also fee-lapsed (adj. expiry 2027-06-30).
- Family priority: 2001-06-04 (parent); filed 2001-08-20; granted 2009-03-03; seven 2005-era descendants are divisionals, not continuations; last family filings 2007; three 2005 siblings + one 2007 sibling abandoned (US20050128361A1, US20050117021A1, US20050138673A1, US20080109848A1).
- Commercial story: lab had 275 employees, 286 patents; HiMpact aimed to boil a 3-hour game to 45 minutes with automatic indexing, random-access play-by-play navigation, annotated summaries (semantics of baseball, football, soccer); Sharp Corp. Japan wouldn't develop it; licensing spin-out Sharp Technology Ventures shows no durable commercial products.
- Technology overtaken: later patents citing this family moved to learned logo recognition, ML highlight detection, CNN sports-event detection, scoreboard extraction; Sharp's claims locked to handcrafted broadcast heuristics (green field, converging white lines, side-camera framing, histogram scene cuts, logo bumpers).
- The patents' own background admits the weak points: unpredictable play types, inconsistent player motion, often-unseeable ball, pre-play movement mimicking action, deterministic rules too brittle ("too difficult to write rules for that cover every situation"); lab moved toward probabilistic HMM prototypes.
- Guidance for GSE: ideas are expired and unencumbered — treat as negative knowledge mapping brittle production-grammar assumptions to avoid; modern reimplementation should use multimodal learned boundaries (scoreboard/clock OCR, audio events, league data feeds, visual models) with explicit uncertainty; GTM caution: Sharp had working tech + ESPN relationship and still failed to commercialize.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Evidence grading taxonomy: [VERIFIED] observed in cited source this session; [ATTRIBUTED] stated by named source, not audited; [ESTIMATED] third-party estimate (Social Blade), directional only; [UNVERIFIABLE] guru claim, no auditable basis; [DISPUTED] sources disagree.
- Enforcement model: Content ID claim (automated match; NFL can monetize/block/track; no strike) vs. copyright strike (DMCA takedown; **3 strikes within 90 days terminates the channel**; rejected dispute can escalate claim to strike). YouTube "reused content" policy denies monetization to sports highlight compilations with minimal commentary; July 2025 update renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"** aimed at template-following mass-produced uploads.
- Faceless-pipeline architecture observed in open-source playbooks: research → script → ElevenLabs TTS → Whisper transcripts → FFmpeg/MoviePy assembly → captions → thumbnails → upload.
- Garrett's standing footage rule: real footage, short (2–4s), commentary-dominant, telestrated, never standalone rips; "only use a PORTION, never the whole" per Richardson v. Townsquare Media (2d Cir., April 2026). Brief notes short clips do NOT eliminate Content ID claim or strike risk.
- Test ranking for GSE: (1) data-visualized sports documentaries (Secret Base style, GSE metrics as engine); (2) film-room "Why" explainers (Jenkins/Kollmann model, 2–4s telestrated clips); (3) search-driven NFL explainers (NFL Explained model); (4) evergreen retrospectives + what-ifs (KTO model) with metric layer; (5) rivalry/franchise turning-point stories (second wave).

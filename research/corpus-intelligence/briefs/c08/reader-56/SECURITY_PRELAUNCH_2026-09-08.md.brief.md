## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- HIGH (fixed, `1b64247fe`, founder decision D2a): Stripe `unpaid` mapped to `PAST_DUE` while the hourly reconciler downgraded on `unpaid` as CONFIRMED non-access — members shown a 7-day grace window they did not have. Fixed: `unpaid` now maps to `INCOMPLETE`; deliberately not `CANCELED` (terminal, arms the out-of-order resurrection guard at route.ts:624-636 which would lock out a just-paid member).
- HIGH (fixed, `69f1a9ef5`): double-billing guard read OUR subscription row, which is written by the webhook — between completed checkout and `customer.subscription.created`, a second checkout sailed through, creating a second real Stripe subscription and monthly charge, invisible to stored data. Fixed: `findLiveStripeSubscription` asks Stripe directly (authoritative), newest-first bounded at 100; `incomplete`/`incomplete_expired` deliberately not "live"; an unreadable answer fails closed with 503.
- LOW (fixed, `5ead0e5c8`): paid No-Bet trail dead on `/api/board/passes` — `loadBoardPasses()` called with no options so `includeNoBetDetail` was permanently false; fixed with server-side entitlement resolution failing closed at every exit.
- LOW (fixed, `69f1a9ef5`): missing `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` threw inside signature verification → 400 "Invalid signature", indistinguishable from an attack; fixed to a 503 naming the variable.
- LOW (fixed): per-customer Stripe URLs returned without no-store on `/api/subscriptions/checkout`, `/api/subscriptions/portal`, `/api/board/passes` — all now answer through `jsonNoStore`.
- Checked and sound: refund-revocation both branches implemented (flag structurally default-OFF; D2b needed no code change); all 32 route handlers under `/api/intelligence/**` and `/api/nflverse/**` gated; `DEV_FAKE_ADMIN` cannot mint paid access in production (two blocks); price amounts verified against the advertised ladder; guardrails 26/26 PASS; `git grep` for sk_live_/sk_test_/whsec_/rk_live_ returns only labelled test fixtures. `apps/web/lib/stripe.ts` reports as binary due to two deliberate 0x00 NUL bytes in a cache key (not a defect).
- Dependency posture NOT fixed: `next` 14.2.35 with npm-reported **critical** aggregate (24 advisories: GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4, GHSA-p293-qw3h-jr36, GHSA-955p-x3mx-jcvp, GHSA-p9j2-gv94-2wf4, GHSA-4c39-4ccg-62r3, GHSA-4633-3j49-mh5q, GHSA-68g3-v927-f742, GHSA-89xv-2m56-2m9x, GHSA-m99w-x7hq-7vfj, GHSA-36qx-fr4f-26g5, GHSA-wfc6-r584-vfw7, GHSA-c4j6-fc7j-m34r, GHSA-h64f-5h5j-jqjh, GHSA-gx5p-jg67-6x7h, GHSA-vfv6-92ff-j949, GHSA-ffhc-5mcf-pf4q, GHSA-3g8h-86w9-wvmq, GHSA-8h8q-6873-q5fj, GHSA-q4gf-8mx6-v5v3, GHSA-3x4c-7xq6-9pq8, GHSA-ggv3-7p47-pfv8, GHSA-h25m-26qc-wcjf, GHSA-9g9p-9gw9-jx7f) plus `postcss` high (4 advisories: GHSA-qx2v-qp2m-jg93, GHSA-6g55-p6wh-862q, GHSA-fxqj-rqcc-2cmp, GHSA-r28c-9q8g-f849); only remediation is breaking major `next@16.3.4`, deliberately deferred.

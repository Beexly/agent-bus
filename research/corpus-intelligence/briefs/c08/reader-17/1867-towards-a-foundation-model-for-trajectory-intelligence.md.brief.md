## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Rakuten Institute of Technology (arXiv 2312.00076): a "large trajectory model" — BERT pretrained on check-in trajectories — whose core contribution is a 3-stage spatial tokenizer (Uber H3 → spatiotemporal clustering → hierarchical sub-hash WordPiece) that tames spatial-vocabulary explosion; GSE verdict: ADAPT, for the tokenizer design, not the model (spatially-aware-only, proprietary data, no code).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No equations stated (empirical/engineering paper). Tokenizer: (1) lat/lon → Uber H3 resolution 8; (2) spatiotemporal clustering (10 minutes, one H3 hexagon) to control point density; (3) hierarchical hashes split into sub-hashes, WordPiece tokenizer to 30k vocab (example: 49 unique hashes → 15 sub-hashes; a Japan-sized country needs 500k+ raw hashes). Pretraining: Hugging Face BERT, whole-hexagon pre-masking at 20% masking ratio, 40 epochs ≈ 240h on 2× NVIDIA Tesla V100, chunk 512, batch 64, >40B spatial tokens (>1B by end of epoch 1). Fine-tuning: 20k random examples per downstream task, 80/20 balanced, 10 epochs, batch 64; metric = average F1.
## Data sources named
Proprietary Rakuten check-in dataset (Japan, 12 months): 6M+ users, 2B+ check-ins, (anonymized user ID, lat/lon, timestamp) tuples; trajectories with <3 distinct hashes or length <10 excluded; 70/15/15 split. Data/code not public. Table I compares against public check-in datasets.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Validation perplexity: 2327 (random init) → 5.27 (end of epoch 1) → 3.79 (end of epoch 40). Small-data control (80M tokens → perplexity ≈ 8 by epoch 12) explains the fast early drop. Downstream: pretrained beats random-init fine-tune by 33.6% better average F1 across three tasks (next sub-trajectory prediction; destination prediction from first 25% of trajectory; trajectory-user association). Baselines: only random initialization — no prior trajectory model compared. Authors admit the model "does not explicitly consider the temporal dimension" — a major gap; check-ins ≠ trajectories (sparse, event-driven, no speed/direction/dwell semantics); no ablation of tokenizer stages.

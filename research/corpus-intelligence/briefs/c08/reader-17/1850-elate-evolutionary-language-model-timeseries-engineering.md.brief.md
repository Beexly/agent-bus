# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1850-elate-evolutionary-language-model-timeseries-engineering.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
ELATE (Murray, Dervovic, Cashmore, arXiv 2508.14667): an LLM evolves a population of pandas-code time-series features over G generations, with AST validation, a cheap Granger-causality + mutual-information evaluator pre-screen, TreeSHAP-interventional pruning on walk-forward XGBoost, and a prompt-enforced no-leakage contract (past rows only); beat hand-built features, tsfresh, VEST, and an LSTM on 6/7 forecasting domains. GSE verdict: ADAPT.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
TS Auto-FE: min_T E(φ(T,X), y). Feature sampling P(Xj) = exp(E(Xj,Y)/T)/Σ exp(E(Xk,Y)/T), T = T0·e^{−K·nfeat/Nmax} + ε (T0=10, K=5, ε=0.1). Evaluator E(Xj,Y) = (E_GC + E_MI)/2; E_GC = |b1| from statsmodels VAR F-test (p<0.05), MI via sklearn mutual_info_regression. Pruning: TreeSHAP interventional, |corr|>0.9 dedup, recursive worst-10% removal until N=50 from Nmax=100; RMSE must improve vs previous generation or the set reverts. Walk-forward CV; final 10% test, penultimate 10% validation; XGBoost final model; metrics MAE/RMSE. ELATE 4o+FRESH variant uses tsfresh FRESH Benjamini–Yekutieli significance tests instead of the SHAP filter (no model training, fastest on 5/7).
## Data sources named
Seven public forecasting tasks: ILI (CDC flu portal, 965 rows/10 feats, 1-month horizon), Store (Kaggle Walmart, 6,300/11, 1-week), ETTh1 (Zhou et al., 17,419/11, 1-hour), Energy (Kaggle, 35,040/21, 1-day), Pollution (Kaggle, 43,776/13, 1-day), Trading (Kaggle S&P-500, 125,750/12, 1-day), Food (Kaggle meal-delivery, 179,568/10, 1-week).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
ELATE 4o+SHAP best (bold) in 6/7 domains on MAE and RMSE; trading tied with Base (no gain — stock returns too noisy). Average vs hand-built Base: −9.6% MAE, −8.4% RMSE; Energy RMSE 0.1145→0.0933 (−18.5%). Zero-Shot GPT-4o often worse than Base (ILI RMSE 0.0621 vs 0.0427) — the evolutionary loop, not the LLM alone, does the work. GPT-3.5T variant: −5.8%/−6.6% at $1.18–$2.07/run vs GPT-4o $5.99–$13.43/run. ELATE 4o+FRESH beat all non-ELATE baselines on 4/7 domains. tsfresh blew past 128GB RAM in 6/7 domains (only completed on Store: 7,831 features, 13.7GB peak). Runtime ~3h average, faster than VEST/LSTM on 4/7; anytime algorithm. Validation overfitting observed on ETTh1 and ILI (val improves while test stalls; no early stopping used). Causality contract is prompt-enforced, NOT AST-enforced; features sometimes use unshifted current-row covariates and target-adjacent columns. A cited consumer-goods survey: 10% forecast-accuracy gain ≈ 5% inventory-cost reduction + 2% revenue increase. Generated depth-10 trading ADL oscillator example shows composability; degenerate 840h/15,000h EWMAs survived on ETTh1 — argument for pruning.

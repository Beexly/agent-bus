# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1910-gradient-based-meta-learning-uncertainty-weigh-loss.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ding et al. (arXiv 2208.08135): three orthogonal upgrades to MAML for few-shot learning — (1) each task-specific learner selects its initialization θ₀ = argmin loss from a stored pool of past checkpoints, (2) a weight generator upweights tasks with big support-vs-query loss gaps, (3) per-task homoscedastic-uncertainty scalars σ_i weight the meta-loss; demonstrated robustness to learning-rate and query-set variation. GSE verdict: ADAPT (the sine-wave regression result is the one that matters for GSE).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
MAML objective (Eq. 1): min_θ Σ_{T_i} ℒ(θ−α∇ℒ(θ,D^{train}_{T_i}), D^{test}_{T_i}); inner (Eq. 2), outer (Eq. 3) updates. Init selection: θ₀ = argmin ℒ_{T_new}(θ₀, D_{T_new}) over checkpoint pool. Weight generator (Eq. 4): m_i = (lossk^t_i − lossk_i)/Σ_j(lossk^t_j − lossk_j). Homoscedastic meta-loss (Eq. 7): ℒ(w,σ_1..n) = Σ_i (ℒ_i(w)/σ_i² + log σ_i), from temperature-scaled softmax p(y|f^w(x),σ)=Softmax(f^w(x)/σ²) (Eq. 5). Validation: sine-wave regression (amplitude U[0.1,5.0], phase U[0,π], x∈[−5,5], K=10, α=0.01, Adam) with LR sweeps {10⁻³,10⁻²,10⁻¹}; miniImageNet/tieredImageNet 5-way 1/5-shot with 95% CIs over 10,000 test tasks; query-set robustness sweeps n_query∈{1,5,15}. Baselines: MAML, fine-tune, Matching/Prototypical/Relation Networks, R2D2, MetaOptNet, Negative Margin.
## Data sources named
Synthetic sine regressions (MAML's original testbed); miniImageNet (100 classes, 64/16/20 split); tieredImageNet (608 classes in 34 superclasses, 351/97/160 split). No code URL stated (modification of MAML's attached code).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Sine regression (qualitative): ours adapts with only 10 data points where MAML cannot; at α=10⁻³ vs 10⁻², MAML's losses differ by 0.015 while ours are "concentrated"; at α=10⁻¹ ours reaches lowest loss 0.315, "which cannot be achieved by the MAML algorithm." miniImageNet 5-way 1-shot: Ours(homoscedastic) 53.97±1.80% vs MAML 48.70±1.84%, Negative Margin 52.84, MetaOptNet 52.87; 5-shot: 71.62±0.43% vs MAML 63.11±0.92%, Negative Margin 70.41 — 1–2pp over advanced baselines. tieredImageNet 1-shot: 55.37±0.78% vs MAML 51.67±1.81%; 5-shot: 74.08±1.65% vs MAML 70.30±1.75%. Homoscedastic gains larger with more tasks (4–8 > 2). MAML accuracy "decreased significantly" as query set grew 1→5; ours did not (Figure 5). Negative transfer explicitly unaddressed (limitation #1); homoscedastic assumption is task-level constant noise; Eq. 7 exact only as σ→1.

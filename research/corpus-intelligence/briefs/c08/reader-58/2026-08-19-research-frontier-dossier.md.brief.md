# docs/ops/edge/2026-08-19-research-frontier-dossier.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026-08-19 night research dossier translating six literature-survey threads (anytime-valid inference, calibration/resolution, forecast pooling, sports-market efficiency, independent MLB-totals models, staking) into 17 ranked engineering proposals (P-A through P-Q), each citation-audited; its thesis is that the platform has an information problem, not a calibration problem, and that only proposals conditioning on new information can create resolution.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Measured engine state: baseline Brier 0.2815 → isotonic-calibrated holdout Brier 0.2556; UNC pinned at 0.2499 (base rate 50.9%); post-sweep ECE 0.0044; suppression-curve spread 0.0017.
- CORP decomposition: Brier = MCB − DSC + UNC; R* = (DSC − MCB)/UNC; implied DSC − MCB ≈ −0.0057 (negative net skill vs base rate).
- Rank 1 (P-A): grade TOTAL/SPREAD CLV in price space (probability/EV delta using actual over/under and spread juice in ingested `Odds` rows), per Bickel & Kim (2014) Applied Financial Economics 24(18):1229–1234.
- Rank 3 (P-C): delete the unfit ×1.12 sharpness stretch `homeP = 0.5+(homeP-0.5)*1.12` (triggered when |homeP−0.5|≥0.03) in `blendIndependentHomeFair` — deletion, not tuning, per Gneiting, Balabdaoui & Raftery (2007): forecast quality is sharpness subject to calibration.
- Rank 6 (P-F): CLV certification e-process as 4-point θ grid mixture {0.55, 0.575, 0.60, 0.65} against BREAK_EVEN_PROBABILITY=0.524, combined via logsumexp; the dead-zone KL zero-crossing at θ=0.5629, oracle n=257 (both independently reconfirmed); independent Monte Carlo (20,000 trials at θ=0.6) put median first-crossing at n=232, mean at n=287.
- Rank 7 (P-G): fitted logit-space pooling (Satopää E-mean): logit(p*) = b0 + b1·logit(p_market) + b2·logit(p_independent), regularized IRLS/MLE, walk-forward, Gaussian(0,1) ridge prior.
- Rank 14 (P-N): predictable plug-in betting schedule λ_t^{PrPl±} = sqrt(2·log(2/α) / (σ̂²_{t−1}·t·log(t+1))), truncated at c/m with c=1/2 (verified against Waudby-Smith & Ramdas arXiv:2010.09686 v6, eq. 32).
- Kill/stopping rules: certify at running-max logM_mix ≥ ln(20); kill at E ≤ 1/10; budget kills at N_max=600 flagged picks; P-E kill if NB out-of-sample RPS is not strictly better than existing plain-Poisson helper.
- Grouping-loss lower bound (P-D, Perez-Lebel et al. arXiv:2210.16315) as the go/no-go gate for week-plus builds (P-H, P-K).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Flagship candidate signal: MLB totals beat-close 176/301 = 58.5% (~2.8% SE) — computed on point-line movement only, with juice sitting unused in schema columns the platform already has; per Bickel & Kim (2014), MLB totals conclusions flip once real juice replaces an assumed price, so 58.5% is provisionally uninterpretable either direction until P-A runs.
- Standing rank-0 gate C-15: 59 of 140 ML lock prices are model-derived pseudo-odds below −1000 (e.g., −21200 implying p=0.995) — every CLV-adjacent claim is uninterpretable until the provenance audit lands.
- Independent `trueProb` stream attenuated ×0.484 and re-anchored 45% to market; `Pick.confidence` substantially a market-structure echo; no independent ATS/totals model exists; `poisson.ts`'s `overUnderProbabilities` is fully built and tested but never called from `build-independent-fair-values.ts`.
- 17 proposals survived both audits: 90 survey citations checked (0 fabricated, 0 doctrine violations, 3 precision-only flags); 38 proposal citations re-checked (0 fabricated, 0 doctrine violations, 4 proposals flagged, kept with corrections).
- Three proposals aimed at the same function: P-C (delete ×1.12), P-G (fitted logit-space successor), P-Q (variance-shrinkage, audit-deprecated as redundant).
- Ranked decision ladder: P-A (days, measurement fix) → P-B (CORP diagnostics) → P-C (delete stretch) → P-D (grouping-loss gate) → P-E (independent NB totals model, week+, the only rank with clear resolution-creation) → rest.
- P-E estimate: 1–3 points of mispricing in P(over/under) vs Poisson in the 7.5–9.5 line band; covariate ablation (P-L) with Coors-vs-Oakland park spread ±15–20% runs.
- Market efficiency priors: Ryan (2025) finds MLB moneylines extremely efficient (only 2.46% of 1,547 tested strategies profitable at 5% significance — consistent with chance); 61.6% zero-movement MLB runlines make CLV-based validation barely functional there; ~1,161 lifetime settled picks; no published, peer-reviewed sports-betting application of Venn-Abers, IDR, or multicalibration with demonstrated ROI exists.
- Rejected-as-cited: CEPT/CPET as a named method (does not exist — it is a compression of two Vovk papers testing exchangeability, not market skill); universal inference (null is simple); continuous KT/Beta mixture (overkill, ~2× regret); MLB reverse-FLB (literature door closed); ML papers claiming 93–94% MLB accuracy (leakage indicator).
- Tooling note for future research sessions: pypdf/pdfminer broken in container (install pymupdf for PDF text), no poppler, Semantic Scholar 429s (use Crossref api.crossref.org), INFORMS/T&F domains 403 direct fetch, alphaXiv MCP exposed only researcher-profile tools.

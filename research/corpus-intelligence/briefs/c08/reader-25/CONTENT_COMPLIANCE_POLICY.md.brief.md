## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Blocked claim patterns: "lock", "guaranteed", "risk free"/"risk-free", "free money", "can't lose", "100% winner", "sure thing", "easy money", "mortgage play", "max bet", "all in", "hammer this", "printing money", "can't miss".
- Evidence-required patterns: win rate, ROI, profit/profitable, verified, proven, calibrated, beats market, closing line value/CLV, +EV.
- Sponsors cannot control: picks, model outputs, no-bet decisions, loss autopsies, calibration claims, editorial conclusions.
- Betting content must be educational/analytical; no automated betting; no personalized financial advice; responsible-play language required.
- Review workflow: Draft → claim scan → source/evidence review → disclosure review → rights/visual review → operator approval → manual human publish. No auto-publish path approved.
- AI-generated imagery/narration requires disclosure + operator review.

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Seven one-line value propositions for the AWS Clean Rooms demo, each pitching aggregate, privacy-preserving data collaboration to a different partner type (media, DFS/fantasy, sportsbook, team/training, data provider, creator/community, league-adjacent).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified — no metrics, formulas, or numbers; each proposition is a single sentence.
## Data sources named
None named — the propositions reference aggregate patterns conceptually (aggregate demand patterns, aggregate market forensics, aggregate public-availability patterns) without naming a source.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Media partner: safer content planning from aggregate demand patterns.
- DFS/fantasy partner: role volatility education and slate-risk explanations.
- Sportsbook/operator partner: responsible uncertainty education and aggregate market forensics.
- Team/training partner: aggregate public-availability pattern analysis without exposing medical or personnel records.
- Sports data provider: feed reliability and freshness score collaboration.
- Creator/community partner: narrative timing insight without exporting user identities.
- League/team-adjacent partner: data-quality and public-analysis review with governed aggregate rules.
- Recurring privacy theme across 4 of 7 propositions: aggregate-only insights, no exposure of medical/personnel records or user identities.

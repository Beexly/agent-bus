# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0301-posetobiomechanics-bridging-3d-human-pose-estimation.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Corpus deep read of arXiv:2607.08725 (Eghbalian & Desai 2026): a learned transformer (BioModule) that infers full biomechanical state (velocities, torques, GRF, muscle activation) from 3D human pose alone, tested on ground-truth vs estimated poses. Ledger verdict: REJECT — lab-motion biomechanics with no consumer in GSE's stack and no NFL validation.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
BioModule: 4-layer transformer, hidden d=256, 8 attention heads (head dim 32), FFN 4×, dropout 0.1, temporal window W=81 frames (~1.62 s @ 50 fps), 17 independent attribute regression heads. Input: root-centered 17-joint 3D skeleton (51 dims/frame). Training: 50 epochs, AdamW, lr=1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, batch 64, cosine schedule; multitask loss L = 1.0·L_kinematic + 0.5·L_kinetic + 0.3·L_neuromuscular. Protocol: frozen module on ground-truth pose (upper bound) → frozen on estimated poses (TCPFormer, D3DP, MHFormer, MixSTE, PoseFormer; H36M MPJPE 37–52 mm models) → per-estimator fine-tune 10 epochs lr=1e-5. No novel equations; metrics: MAE per attribute, touch classification accuracy %.
## Data sources named
Aligned Human3.6M/H3.6Mplus: 520,509 frames, 210 clips, 30 activities, 7 subjects (S1, S5, S6, S7, S8, S9, S11), 4 cameras, 50 fps; train S1/S5/S6/S7/S8 (~108 min), test S9/S11 (~44 min). Biomechanical labels from OpenSim-style simulation outputs (17 attribute heads). Code: https://github.com/UTSA-VIRLab/BioModule.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Frozen module, ground-truth pose (upper bound) → estimator-fed degradation: coordinates MAE 0.228 → 0.583–0.658 (best TCPFormer 0.583, worst MHFormer 0.658); active torque MAE 9.529 → 15.4–16.9 (MHFormer 16.874); GRF MAE 28.900 → 37.5–39.1 (MHFormer 39.000); touch accuracy 67.500% → 41.8–43.7% (MHFormer 43.700%). Fine-tuning recovers coordinate MAE to 0.260–0.278 and touch to 67.5–68.6%, BUT fine-tune checkpoint selected by minimum training loss with no validation set — INFERENCE: some recovery is likely overfit. All labels are OpenSim simulations, not measured forces; 7 indoor lab subjects; no contact, cutting, sprinting, equipment. Bidirectional 1.62 s window = offline inference only.

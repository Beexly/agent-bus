# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0321-lineup-regularized-adjusted-plusminus-lrapm-basketball.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Corpus deep read of arXiv:2601.15000v1 (Petridis & Pelechrinis 2026): Lineup-RAPM for NBA — ridge regression on sparse lineup units with the penalty shrunk toward *informed priors* built as sums of member players' prior-season RAPM ratings. Ledger verdict: ADAPT — the sport structure doesn't transfer to NFL, but the informed-prior shrinkage device is a directly portable small-sample solution for sparse NFL grouping problems (personnel packages, DFS stacks, rare groupings).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Player RAPM (Sill 2010): min_γ Σ_i(y_i−(γ_0+Σ_j γ_{j,off}x_{i,j,off}+Σ_j γ_{j,def}x_{i,j,def}))² + λ_offΣ_j γ²_{j,off} + λ_defΣ_j γ²_{j,def}; tuned λ_off=4000, λ_def=6000 on 2022-23. Informed prior: π_{λa,off} = league ppp + Σ_{k=1..5} γ_{k,off} (prior-season member RAPMs). L-RAPM: min_β Σ_i(y_i−(β_0+Σ_j β_{j,off}x_{i,j,off}+Σ_j β_{j,def}x_{i,j,def}))² + λΣ_j(β_{j,off}−π_{j,off})² + λΣ_j(β_{j,def}−π_{j,def})² (shrinkage toward π, not zero). Rookies/unseen players imputed at RAPM=−1. Evaluation: expanding-window out-of-sample (weeks 1..n−1 → week n); metric weekly RMSE of points per possession vs raw lineup ratings; improvement δ_{L−RAPM}=(RMSE_{L−RAPM}−RMSE_{baseline})/RMSE_{baseline}. Assumptions: additive player-sum prior (ignores chemistry), single λ for offense/defense (flagged as limitation — offense stabilizes faster, Partnow 2021), possessions exchangeable.
## Data sources named
NBA possession-level data 2022-23 (fit player RAPM priors) and 2023-24 (evaluation), regular season + playoffs; possession tuples <points, 5 offensive players, 5 defensive players>. Possession data proprietary/not stated as public; public reference only: stats.nba.com lineup efficiency page. No code released.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
L-RAPM beats raw lineup ratings in every week of 2023-24. Improvement largest for lineups with <50 training possessions; for >500 possessions raw ratings are nearly as good. Unseen lineups (prior-only prediction vs league-average baseline): δ_{L−RAPM} ∼ 5% (final 2 playoff weeks are small-sample outliers). Authors' practical framing: 1.5% improvement ≈ 3.4 points over a full game — larger than the ~2-point home edge markets price (Lopez et al. 2018). NBA context: teams use 600+ lineups/season; average lineup ~17 offensive + ~17 defensive possessions (std 56) — the motivating sparsity. No leakage: priors strictly from prior season, expanding-window strictly out-of-sample. Stale-prior and rookie-imputation (−1) limitations acknowledged by authors.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- SCHEME: NFL analog = personnel-package efficiency (11 vs 12 vs 21 personnel EPA on small samples), down/distance splits, receiver target units — all sparse groups currently estimated raw; member-sum priors (prior-season EPA/target, EPA/rush, adjusted) + ridge toward π gives stable estimates weeks before samples accumulate.
- COACHING: directly answers "is this rare package actually good?" (e.g., 6-OL sets, wildcat) and in-season trade/injury evaluation of new groupings — the unseen-lineup case.
- OTHER: DFS lineup-combo correlation — predict a stack's EPA from member priors when the exact combo has few observed snaps.
- TRUST-SIGNAL: paper's own flag that a single λ for offense and defense is wrong (offense stabilizes faster) maps to football: use separate λ for early-down vs late-down (or pass vs run) in the NFL port.
- OTHER: corpus novelty — informed-prior shrinkage for sparse group estimates appears nowhere in the corpus (nflWAR is player decomposition, not group shrinkage); new capability, not a duplicate.

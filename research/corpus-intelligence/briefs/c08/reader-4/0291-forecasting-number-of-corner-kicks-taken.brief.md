# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0291-forecasting-number-of-corner-kicks-taken.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Corpus deep read of arXiv:2112.13001v3 (Yip et al. 2021): Bayesian discrete compound Poisson (geometric-Poisson) regression forecasting soccer corner-kick counts from cross-market odds information and team form. Ledger verdict: ADAPT — the DCP machinery, shape regression, and market-implied covariate extraction port to NFL count-prop modeling; the corner-kick application is soccer-only.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
DCP: Y = Σ_{i=1}^N X_i, N ~ Poisson(λ); NB parameterization p_Y(y) = C(y+κ−1, y)(λ/(λ+κ))^y(κ/(λ+κ))^κ, E[Y]=λ, Var[Y]=λ+λ²/κ; Geometric-Poisson: E[Y]=λ/θ, Var[Y]=λ(2−θ)/θ². Rate: log λ_i = β₀+β₁log(TG_i)+β₂log(|SUP|_i+0.01)+β₃TCTarget_i+β₄log(HomeAvg3_i+0.01)+β₅log(AwayAvg3_i+0.01)+β₆log(HomeShotOnGoalAvg3_i+0.01)+β₇log(AwayShotOnGoalAvg3_i+0.01). Shape: log(κ_i)=α₀+α₁log(|SUP_i|+0.01) (NB); logit(θ_i)=α₀+α₁log(|SUP_i|+0.01) (GP). Market-implied TG/SUP extracted via Maher-1982 double-Poisson fit to de-vigged HAD + O/U odds (L-BFGS-B). Margin-removal bake-off: multiplicative vs odds-ratio vs power vs Shin (Shin: p_i=[√(z²+4(1−z)π_i²/π)−z]/[2(1−z)]). Model comparison by elpd_loo; betting sim: $100 per positive-EV selection, Sharpe = annualised mean daily profit/SD.
## Data sources named
20,190 HKJC matches (1 Aug 2016 – 10 Jun 2021, 90 leagues/cups) with pre-match HAD/total-goals O/U/total-corner O/U odds + realized corner counts; football-data.co.uk for 7 elite leagues (EPL, Scottish PL, Bundesliga, Serie A, La Liga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie) with per-team shots, shots-on-goal, fouls, cards, corners. Betting simulation: 2,057 HKJC matches, 1 Jan – 10 Jun 2021.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Corner counts overdispersed: variance-to-mean 1.1856 vs 1.0396 for goals. elpd_loo: GP −30,856.4 (best) > GP+shape −30,856.8 (se_diff 1.4) > NB+shape −30,858.4 > NB −30,871.4 > Poisson −30,877.2. Estimates: β₁(log TG)=0.1545 (95% CI 0.1046, 0.2069) — each expected goal raises expected corners 16.70%; β₄(HomeAvg3)=0.5957 (0.53, 0.6625); β₅(AwayAvg3)=0.6052 (0.5405, 0.6652); θ posterior median 0.9577 (0.9441, 0.9705) — each corner induces 0.0423 additional corners, implied variance-to-mean 1.0883. SUP and shot-on-goal terms non-significant (CIs cover zero). Betting sim: NB+shape best — 1,137 bets, +$6,578, +5.785%, Sharpe 3.065; GP+shape +$6,256, +5.561%, Sharpe 2.922; blind bet-under: 2,056 bets, −$3,530, −1.717%, Sharpe −1.267. Margin-removal log scores: multiplicative best for corner O/U (14,187.07) and goals O/U (14,465.44); Shin best for HAD (19,612.15). HKJC avg margin 7.61% (payout 92.38%); over-selection payout 85.71% vs under 98.24% (structural under-bias the authors flag as uncaptured). elpd_loo winner (GP) loses the betting sim to NB+shape — scoring-rule best ≠ most profitable.

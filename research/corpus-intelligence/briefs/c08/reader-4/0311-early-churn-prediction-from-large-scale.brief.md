# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0311-early-churn-prediction-from-large-scale.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Corpus deep read of arXiv:2309.14390v1 (Bhattacharjee et al. 2023): transformer vs classical ML for early churn prediction on large-scale Dream11 fantasy-sports user-product interaction time series. Ledger verdict: ADAPT — but only to GSE's own product/user-retention analytics (subscribers, newsletter), never to the sports-prediction engine; the paper's random-split evaluation has temporal leakage that must be fixed in any port.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified (no equations in the paper; architectures described textually). Input: 30-day window of 11 daily user-product interaction features; target: 4 binary labels (one per week for next 4 weeks), churn = full week of inactivity. Models: LR, RF, GBT baselines vs CNN variants (ConvNeXt, Inception-ResNet), LSTM, 8-block Transformer (~1.8M params, ~13 h training on 4× Nvidia A10; CNN ~6 h; batch 16K; lr 1e-4; Adam; up to 100 epochs; Horovod/Petastorm). Validation: RANDOM train/val/test split 0.75/0.05/0.20 — not time-ordered. Metric: AUC per weekly horizon. No calibration, no uplift/intervention analysis.
## Data sources named
Proprietary Dream11 transaction data, 2018-01-01 to 2020-12-31, ~10^6 users, ~10^8 raw transactions; exact feature identities withheld; no code, no data release, no independent replication possible.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
AUC per horizon (weeks 1–4): LR 0.644/0.749/0.672/0.662; RF 0.640/0.775/0.699/0.687; GBT 0.646/0.773/0.703/0.694; Transformer 0.858/0.756/0.732/0.716. Mean Transformer AUC 0.7655 vs mean GBT 0.704 (~6% claim consistent). Transformer dominates at week 1 (0.858) but its edge narrows sharply by weeks 3–4 (0.732/0.716 vs GBT 0.703/0.694). The random (non-time-ordered) split across three years of shared seasonal/calendar structure inflates all AUCs — INFERENCE: reported numbers are optimistic and should not be quoted as expected GSE performance.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER: new capability in a product-analytics domain, not a sports-prediction domain — no corpus duplicate; must never feed the prediction engine. Boundary is load-bearing (fantasy/Dream11 user data is adjacent to sports but is behavior modeling, not outcome modeling).
- TRUST-SIGNAL: negative example on evaluation hygiene — random splits over time-series user data cause temporal leakage; any GSE retention port must use walk-forward splits (train months 1–M, validate M+1, test M+2).
- OTHER: paper optimizes pure prediction AUC but never tests whether acting on predictions reduces churn — INFERENCE: a GSE port should add a randomized retention experiment with heterogeneous treatment effects (who is intervenable), not just a risk score.

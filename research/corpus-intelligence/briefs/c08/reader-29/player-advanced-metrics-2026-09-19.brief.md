## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Advanced player metrics for the DraftKings NFL Week 2 2026 15-game slate (WRs, TEs, RBs, QBs, 8 DSTs): Week 1 route/target/TPRR/aDOT/YPRR, xFP vs actual, EPA splits, and second-party-verified DK salaries — with [ELITE]/[WEAK] flags at the 85th/15th percentiles and N/A marks wherever metrics were not publicly available.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Route share, target share, TPRR (targets per route run), aDOT, air-yard share, YPRR; xFP (Sharp Football Analysis) vs actual fantasy points; RB snap/opportunity/red-zone touch shares; QB EPA/play splits (pressured vs clean, play-action, personnel); DST pressure/blitz/sack rates.
- [ELITE] = above 85th percentile (published rank or math, e.g. 1st–4th of 32); [WEAK] = below 15th; † = derived from usage profile.
- Salaries second-party-verified only (DK Network, The Huddle); never endpoint-verified, never invented.

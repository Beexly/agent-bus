## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Second-pass reassessment (2026-09-26, triggered by Garrett's "think outside the box") of arXiv 2609.23158 (Kotte et al., KAUST: 77 GHz FMCW radar + wavelet scattering transform for pre/post-exercise skin-state classification). Verdict stands at REJECT for the hardware system (n=15, no ground truth, no acquisition path), but the note extracts four transferable, radar-free components for engine R and D.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Paper's numbers: NN accuracy 99.28 percent (random 10-run CV) down to 91.55 percent (LOSO), LOSO SD plus/minus 14.39, 14.6-point-wide 95 percent CI. Scattering-order discriminative-power decomposition: S0 67.9 percent, second-order 24.4 percent, first-order 7.6 percent. Spike test spec: Kymatio 1D scattering, Q sweep [2,1]/[4,1]/[8,1], invariance scale about half window, vs engineered features (total movement, max excursion, time-weighted drift, late-steam indicator); label = CLV outcome or ATS cover; model = feedforward NN or gradient boosting; validation = group-out (leave-one-week-out), never random CV on sequential data; pass gate = (a) WST beats simple features by at least 0.02 out-of-sample AUC and (b) gain concentrated in non-zeroth-order coefficients.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- The random-CV vs LOSO gap (99.28 to 91.55 percent) quantifies within-subject leakage inflation; cited as a cautionary exhibit for group-out validation: props from the same game share the game outcome, so use leave-one-game-out or game-clustered bootstrap.
- The paper's interpretability finding: S0 low-pass energy carried 67.9 percent of discriminative power in that dataset -- a property of that dataset (mean-amplitude shift between rested and exhausted subjects), not a verdict on WST as a method.
- Program-count implication: REJECT stands, so the paper does not count toward the 750-valuable target; a replacement full-paper read is owed (count was 585/750 at second pass).
- Watchlist only: mmWave vital sensing (respiration, heart-rate via micro-Doppler) is commoditizing; revisit annually; do not build, do not buy hardware.

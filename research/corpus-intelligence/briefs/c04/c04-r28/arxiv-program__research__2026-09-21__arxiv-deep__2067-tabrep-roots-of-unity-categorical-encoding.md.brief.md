# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2067-tabrep-roots-of-unity-categorical-encoding.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of arXiv:2504.04798 (Si et al., Imperial College London / Columbia, 2025): TabRep, a tabular diffusion method whose CatConverter encodes any categorical as a point on the unit circle — CatConverter(c,K) = [cos(2πc/K), sin(2πc/K)] — giving a dense, parameter-free 2-dim encoding backed by singular-point geometric theory proving it collapses one-hot's combinatorial singular-hyperplane structure that explodes score-function variance. Verdict: ADAPT — simplest dense categorical encoding in the lane (no VAE, no learned embeddings), beats all baselines including TabSYN on MLE (first method to beat Real-trained performance on 3 datasets), cheapest to train/sample; ordering-sensitivity finding directly exploitable for NFL (order teams by prior strength to inject ordinal signal for free).

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- CatConverter (Eq. 12): CatConverter(c_j,K_j) = [cos(2πc_j/K_j), sin(2πc_j/K_j)] ∈ ℝ^{2·Dcat} (2·Dcat dims vs ΣK for one-hot, Σ⌈log2K⌉ for analog bits, demb·Dcat for learned embeddings); decoding = nearest of K circle points (out-of-index impossible; OOI casting to index 0 at 5–20% rates, Table 8).
- Singular-point theory (§4.1): one-hot minimal n-singular point x_S^{(n)} = (1/n)Σ_{k∈S}e_k (Eq. 8); singular hyperplane H_S = {x : d_k(x)=d_{k'}(x)} dim K−|S|+1 (Eq. 9); minimal singular points scale Σ_{n=2}^{K}(K choose n) = 2^K − (K+1); conditional score variance at minimal n-singular point (Eq. 10): Var(g|x) = (α_t²/σ_t⁴)·(n−1)/n — strictly positive, increasing in n; →0 at non-singular points. CatConverter collapses this to ONE minimal K-singular point (circle center), K 2-singular points, NO n-singular hyperplanes for 2<n<K.
- Unified continuous diffusion on concat[quantile-transformed numerics, CatConverter categoricals]: DDPM loss (Eq. 13) E‖∇log q − ∇log p_θ‖²; Flow Matching (Eq. 14) E‖v_θ(z_t) − (ε − z_0^{CC})‖², Euler ODE sampling.
- Denoising MLP: FC + sinusoidal time emb, hidden [1024,2048,2048,1024], ReLU, dropout 0.0; 100k iters, lr 1e−4, wd 5e−4; 50 (Flow) / 1000 (DDPM) sampling steps.
- Benchmarks: MLE (XGBoost AUC/RMSE), MIA recall/precision (50% = random), CWD, PCC, α-precision/β-recall, C2ST, wall-clock train/sample, NFEs-vs-quality.

## Data sources named
Seven UCI tabular datasets: Adult, Default, Shoppers, Stroke, Diabetes, Beijing, News — mixed continuous/discrete; classification (AUC) and regression (RMSE) targets; 20 sampling seeds; best-validated model. Baselines: STaSy, CoDi, TabDDPM, TabDiff, TabSYN (SOTA diffusion set); representation bake-off also vs OneHot-DDPM, Learned1D/2D-DDPM, i2b, Dictionary. Code: https://github.com/jacobyhsi/TabRep (fully reproducible). Proposed NFL build: nflverse team-game table 2015–2025; team/opponent IDs K=32 ordered by preseason Elo; venue/surface/weather bins ordered by severity; rest category ordered by days; week number natively circular.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Table 4 MLE (TabRep best on all 7): Adult AUC 0.913/0.912 (Real 0.927); Default 0.764/0.782 (Real 0.770 — beats Real); Shoppers 0.926 (ties Real 0.926); Stroke 0.869 (Real 0.852 — beats Real); Diabetes RMSE 0.373/0.377 (Real 0.384); Beijing RMSE 0.508/0.536 (Real 0.423); News RMSE 0.836/0.814 (Real 0.842 — beats Real). First method to exceed Real-trained downstream quality while preserving privacy (headline claim).
- Table 3 representation bake-off (same diffusion, different encodings): OneHot-DDPM collapses on Adult (0.476 AUC); Learned1D/2D-DDPM collapse on several sets; i2b and Dictionary competitive; TabRep best.
- Table 7 ordering is load-bearing: lexicographic vs random ordering — Adult AUC 0.913 vs 0.776; Beijing RMSE 0.508 vs 1.050.
- Table 5 MIA recall (50% = private): TabRep-DDPM 48–52 on 6/7 sets (News 40.10); TabDDPM News 9.88 (memorization leak); CoDi ~0–3 on several (privacy failure).
- Table 6 wall-clock (Adult): TabRep-Flow total 2031s (train 2028 + sample 3.07) — cheapest; TabRep-DDPM 2131s; TabDDPM 3179s; TabDiff 5655s; CoDi 24049s. Figure 5: TabRep-Flow best quality at 8 NFEs; converges earliest.
- Appendix D: high-cardinality toy (1,000-category feature, Table 13) — TabRep-Flow RMSE 0.4812 ≈ TabSYN 0.4775 vs TabDDPM 0.8253; DDPM variants fail on CDE/PWC/C2ST at high cardinality while Flow stays intact → NFL build should use TabRep-Flow, not DDPM, for high-cardinality fields. Imbalanced toy 95/5 (Table 14): TabRep-DDPM/Flow best on all six metrics. Diabetes timing (99,473 rows, Table 12): TabRep-DDPM 2094s vs TabDDPM 3723s vs TabSYN 6903s.
- Caveats recorded in file: beating Real is partly a denoising/regularization artifact (synthetic smoothing ≠ better data — same effect FinDiff reported in 2065); CatConverter imposes an arbitrary circular order on nominal features (order is dataset-specific voodoo — for NFL an opportunity via Elo ordering, also a tuning surface); OOI casting injects systematic bias toward index 0 (5–20%); no conditional generation, no temporal structure; Beijing RMSE still far from Real (0.508 vs 0.423); singular-point theory doesn't disentangle density (2 dims vs ΣK) from hyperplane removal.

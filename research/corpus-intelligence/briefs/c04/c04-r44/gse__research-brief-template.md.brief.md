# docs/gse/research-brief-template.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A one-page issue template for GSE decision-process research briefs: topic, three-tier claim levels (observation / interpretation / recommendation framing — none may include outcome guarantees), required evidence fields, uncertainty documentation, and a mandatory no-bet / decision-hygiene framing.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified — template only, no metrics or formulas. Mandates: evidence sources with recency/validity windows, confidence notes with uncertainty bands, counterexamples and failure modes, at least one falsifiable decision rule.
## Data sources named
Generic: source URL(s) or internal extraction records; primary source files in `docs/` and approved feeds; manual notes tagged with consent and review timestamp.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Claim levels: L0 observation, L1 interpretation, L2 recommendation framing — no level may include outcome guarantees or predictive-superiority claims.
- Every brief must carry the verbatim line: "This is educational decision-process support — not betting advice, and not a promise of outcomes."
- Review pipeline: Draft → Evidence-check in progress → Owner-review required → Approved for PR2 implementation.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- Falsifiable decision-rule requirement (each brief must document at least one rule that can be proven wrong) — OTHER
- Claim-level discipline with no predictive-superiority guarantees — OTHER
## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
No — a process template, not research content; useful only as a format standard for future research briefs.

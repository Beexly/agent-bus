## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **Brier-scored calibration** used for the public Calibration Report (`calibration-panel.tsx`), with a brier band (L31–36); confidence is stated to be a heuristic, not a probability — so the report recommends leading with the **discrimination metric** ("does higher confidence win more?", `calibration-panel.tsx` L118–139) rather than a win-rate as the bootstrap-honest headline. No formulas printed in the file.
- **isBootstrap fencing**: pre-canonical picks excluded by design from all stats; immutable `PickSignalSnapshot`; calibration never mutates weights.
- Psych/behavioral numbers cited from web research: loss pain ≈2× equal-win pleasure (loss aversion); users activating within 3 days ~90% more likely to retain; aha within 5 min → ~40% higher 30-day retention; cutting onboarding 12→7 steps + SSO moved time-to-activation 8.2d→1.6d; DK ≈517 live options/game; Pikkit logged >$10B in tracked wagers in ~4 years and disallows manual bet entry; annual subs churn ~3× less than monthly; decoy pricing lifts annual signups 30–40%.

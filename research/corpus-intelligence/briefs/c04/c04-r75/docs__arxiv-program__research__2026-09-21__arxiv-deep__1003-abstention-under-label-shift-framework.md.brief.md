# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1003-abstention-under-label-shift-framework.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Alexandari, Kundaje & Shrikumar (2018; v5 2020), arXiv:1802.07024v5: a general post-hoc framework for letting a classifier abstain on a bounded fraction of examples to optimize an arbitrary metric (not just accuracy), robust to label shift (p(y) changing between train and test with p(x|y) fixed), using only calibrated black-box output probabilities. Verdict ADAPT — ports directly to GSE's no-bet filter: choose which games to skip to optimize a betting metric, with label-shift re-calibration as base rates move through the season.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Three-step framework: (1) calibrate + label-shift-adapt model on held-out data (Platt scaling; Saerens et al. 2002 EM or MLLS for shift); (2) estimate metric improvement from abstaining on a subset, using calibrated p(y|x) as categorical proxy for unknown labels (Monte-Carlo in general); (3) abstain on the subset with the largest estimated metric boost, subject to the budget.
- Algorithm 1 — O(NM) Monte-Carlo optimizer for sensitivity at target specificity s: sorted probs, abstention interval [i,i+d), recompute thresholds t*, t←, t→ per candidate interval; M=100 MC samples, Savitzky–Golay smoothing order-1/window-11. Sensitivity-at-specificity: t* = min{i : 1 − nᵢ⁻/n₀⁻ ≥ s}; per-interval t←/t→; per-sample sensitivity oᵢ = (n⁺_{t′ᵢ} − 1{t′ᵢ≤i}w⁺ᵢ)/(n⁺₀ − w⁺ᵢ).
- auROC optimizer — O(N) deterministic: auROC = (1/(n⁻n⁺))Σᵢ yᵢsᵢ⁻, sᵢ⁻ = Σ_{i′<i}(1−y_{i′}), calibrated probabilities substituted for labels y.
- Weighted Cohen's κ optimizer — O(NC) deterministic marginal-improvement-per-example, abstain on top-k marginal gains: κ(S,f) = 1 − [Σ_{x∈S} W_{y(x),f(x)}]/[ΣᵢΣⱼ W_{i,j}(Nⁱ/N)Fʲ].
- Novel baseline: abstain on examples with lowest Jensen–Shannon divergence between predicted probs and prior class probs (prediction ≈ prior ⟹ no information).
- Comparison baselines: distance-from-0.5 (max-class-prob), entropy (Wan 1990), Fumera et al. 2000 class-specific thresholds, test-time MC dropout variance (100 samples); each run with and without Platt calibration.
## Data sources named
(1) Synthetic binary simulation (1:9 pos:neg, 30% abstention); (2) IMDB sentiment + Cat-vs-Dog images (train/val 1:1 → test 1:2 simulated shift, CNNs); (3) genomic regions active in Leukemia Stem Cells (LSC) and pre-leukemic HSCs (pHSCs), pos:neg ≈ 1:2; (4) diabetic retinopathy CNN (Leibig et al. 2017). Code: https://github.com/blindauth/abstention and https://github.com/blindauth/abstention_experiments.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Label shift, sensitivity @ 99% specificity (Table 1): IMDB — ours (adapted) base 0.3571±0.004 → 0.7067±0.0067 @30% abstention (unadapted 0.6227±0.0082); next best Fumera 0.6976±0.0079 (unadapted) — ours-adapted significantly best. Cat v Dog — ours 0.3318±0.0061 → 0.6135±0.0114 @30% (unadapted 0.5616±0.0102); baselines ≤0.53.
- LSC, no shift, sens @ 80% spec (Table 2): ours 0.6507±0.0029 → 0.8001±0.0035 @30% abst.; JS-div 0.7907±0.0041; distance-0.5 0.7108±0.0027; MC dropout ≈0.68 (no better than base). auROC: ours 0.8107±0.0014 → 0.8586±0.0015 @30%.
- DR, weighted κ, no shift (Table 3): ours 0.8104±0.0019 → 0.8918±0.0013 @30% abst.; entropy/max-prob ≈0.75–0.77 @30% (worse than base at high abstention — miscalibrated abstention can HURT).
- DR, κ, with shift (Table 4): ours adapted 0.7837±0.0044 → 0.8604±0.0027 @30%; unadapted 0.7313±0.004 → 0.8275±0.0035. Significance: one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank, p<0.05, over seeds × resamples.
- Calibration consistently helped every baseline; test-time MC dropout never won; adaptation can worsen Fumera's method (thresholds overfit to validation).
- Limitations: needs a trustworthy held-out calibration set (calibration degrades under severe shift); binary algorithms assume contiguous abstention intervals in sorted-prob order (not proven optimal for all metrics); no cost-of-abstention asymmetry beyond budget k; no decision-theoretic (utility) metric tested — the gap for betting.

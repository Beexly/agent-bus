## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Status: strategy, docs, public-safe pages, and typed planning utilities only. Explicit exclusions: no auto-publish, no auto-send, no external platform API integration, no affiliate tracking integration, no email provider integration.
- Brand thesis statements: "evidence over ego"; "the model is allowed to say no"; "confidence is earned, not performed"; "no bet is a decision"; "the box score lies, the evidence does not"; "raw data is not intelligence"; "sports prediction should be audited, not shouted"; "GSE is not a tout brand."
- Voice split: GSE official content is the product voice (evidence, methodology, source reliability, calibration, no-bet discipline, loss autopsies, product decisions); Garrett/founder-led content is the human build-in-public voice (can show pressure, unemployment context, ambition, discipline, shipped work, blockers, lessons — but not desperate, chaotic, or pity-driven); GSN is the later network umbrella for board meeting/podcast/partner programming once standards are proven.
- Revenue map (7 paths): subscriptions (media builds trust before conversion), partners (editorial standards before buying inventory), affiliates (review workflows before links; placeholder only until approved links exist), sponsors (founding packages), newsletter (owned audience, waitlist state until provider approved), API demand (teach why raw data is not intelligence), founder credibility (proof, not launch-day hype).
- Claim safety principles: no fabricated stats, no stale-data claims, no fake audience numbers, no fake monetization status, no fake sponsors/testimonials, no auto-publish, no auto-send, no external posting without manual review, all commercial claims need evidence or are blocked, sponsorships/affiliates require disclosure, sportsbook/DFS offers require complete compliance metadata.
- Public pages added: `/media-kit` (sponsor overview, honest early-stage language), `/partners` (standards, disclosure, editorial independence, responsible-gaming posture), `/newsletter` (waitlist positioning, lead magnet concepts), `/content-lab`, `/podcast` (future GSE Board Meeting format, partner/guest inquiry route).
- Explicit anti-patterns: not a faceless AI content farm, not high-volume recycled clips, not a sports footage scraper, not an auto-upload pipeline, not a tout funnel.

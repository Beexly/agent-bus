## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- DAVE blend: after Week 1 2026, offense is 83% preseason forecast / 98% for defense+special teams. 2026 projections: separate regressions (three-year DVOA, QB projection, regression to mean, personnel/coaching/draft/OL age-tenure/injuries) through 50,000 season sims with dynamic in-sim adjustment (+1.5% DVOA winners / -1.5% losers). Early-season prior-year opponent-adjustment splits: 50% pass offense / 30% run offense / 20% defense.
- FPI: Bayesian, market-aware ("based substantially on win totals from Caesars Sportsbook and strength of schedule"); EPA foundation decomposed by unit; only QB moves the rating; 10,000 season simulations.
- nfelo (Robby Greer): Elo + QB Elo + HFA/rest/weather + SRS from win-total futures + nfelounits (EPA by unit translated to Elo via EWMA with offseason regression, combined by an EloTranslator trained to minimize log-loss on win probability).
- Kevin Cole Improvement Index: EPA-based on/off plus-minus; +43 index means ~43 points of point-differential improvement, ~1.3 wins.
- Rufus Peabody bias-index framing: market -7, model -3, true expected near -5.2 (model-vs-market blending; treat both as noisy estimates).
- EP (Yurko et al. 2018): expected value of the next scoring event in the half, multinomial logit over 7 outcome categories; note: current nflfastR `ep_model` is XGBoost, NOT Yurko's logit.
- CPOE: play-level residual aggregated as mean over attempts; current nflfastR `cp_model` is XGBoost; exact feature set UNVERIFIED.
- nfl4th: three submodels (go / punt / FG) feeding an expected-WP comparison; coaches behave as if optimizing low quantiles (Sandholtz et al. 2024).
- Turnover split: Stuart (1990-2012): year-to-year fumble-recovery correlation 0.00 own / -0.02 opponent; top-20 recovery teams (75.4%) collapsed to 50.4% the next year. Engine rule: model occurrence separately, regress recovery to ~50%, count forced fumbles not recovered fumbles.
- Pressure: Lorenzo Alexander pressure-to-sack 26% (2016) regressed to 14.6% (2017-2019); pressure-vs-conversion-luck R² < 0.005. STRAIN (2023 tracking): first-half/second-half stability r = 0.8545; early STRAIN predicts future pressure rate r = 0.3217 vs 0.0965 for prior pressure rate.
- EPA predictive validity: passing efficiency correlates with wins 0.61/0.59/0.53 across eras vs 0.18/0.19/0.13 rushing; defensive passing -0.47/-0.45/-0.49 vs defensive rushing -0.04; non-scripted EPA (0.51/0.29) far more stable than scripted (0.26/0.16); Zhou (2026): success rate r ~ 0.60, passing EPA to future point differential r ~ 0.42.
- Edge Sheet fair-line formula (printed on the sheet, labeled "simple illustration, not the GSE engine"): net EPA/play edge x 63 plays + 2.0 home field, 2025 numbers.
- Lab filters: garbage time excluded (4th quarter, possession-team WP > 0.95 or < 0.05); success = EPA > 0; explosive = pass gain 15+ yards or designed rush 10+ yards; dropback = pass attempt or scramble.

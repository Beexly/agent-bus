# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1147-reject-option-early-time-series.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read of Hatami & Chira (2013), arXiv:1312.3989: a classifier-with-reject-option for early time-series classification (electronic noses / gas plumes) where a prediction is accepted only when two diverse classifiers agree, otherwise the decision is deferred to the next time interval. Verdict ADAPT — the domain is irrelevant to GSE, but the accept-on-agreement abstention gate maps directly onto GSE's pick-posting pipeline.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Reject rule (Chow-style): f^o(x) = i if P(ω_i|x) > d; 0 (reject) if P(ω_i|x) ≤ d; reject cost 0 ≤ d ≤ 1/2 (never reject if d > 1/2); risk E[min(f(x), 1−f(x), d)]; accuracy defined as P(correct|accepted).
- Diversity selection: pool of SVMs over (C,γ) grid (C and γ grid-searched over 2^−5…2^5), keep the n most accurate, compute pairwise double-fault matrix DF_{i,j} = P(both misclassify) (Kuncheva 2004), select the most diverse accurate pair; **accept iff both classifiers agree on the label**, else reject.
- Forefront-Nose: serial CWROs, one per time interval k — first classifier decides on a small signal prefix or rejects to the next, which sees a longer prefix; stops at acceptance or when postponement cost exceeds a user threshold.
## Data sources named
Wind-tunnel gas plume facility: 8 Figaro metal-oxide sensors, 10 gases (carbon monoxide, ammonia, methane, acetaldehyde, benzene, butanol, ethylene, methanol, toluene, acetone), 504 eight-dimensional time-series recordings at 45 landmark locations, 100 Hz sampling downsampled by averaging windows of 10. No code released; 10-fold CV.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Average recognition rate (accepted decisions) over 45 locations — Std. CWRO vs Forefront-Nose: 5s: 90.15 vs 93.20; 10s: 91.99 vs 94.10; 15s: 91.68 vs 93.14; 20s: 90.93 vs 93.50; 25s: 88.50 vs 91.04; 30s: 87.40 vs 90.10. Agreement-based CWRO beat the threshold-based CWRO at every earliness; k = 10s is the earliness/accuracy optimum.
- Limitations flagged: accuracy is P(correct|accepted) but abstention rates are not reported (coverage sacrifice unknown); agreement ⇒ accept is conservative — two diverse classifiers can agree and still be wrong; double-fault selection on the same CV folds risks selection bias; the "ensemble" is just hyperparameter variants of one SVM family.

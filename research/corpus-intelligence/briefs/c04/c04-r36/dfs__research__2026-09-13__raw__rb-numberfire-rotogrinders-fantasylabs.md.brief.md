## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Week 1 2026 FanDuel classic-slate RB research pull (Sunday Sept 13, 2026) from numberFire, RotoGrinders (paywalled — no usable RB yield), and FantasyLabs, with numberFire stat-line projections converted to approximate 0.5-PPR FD equivalents.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- numberFire FP = non-PPR fantasy points (verified by researcher against yardage/TD components, receptions excluded).
- "≈ FD 0.5 PPR" = numberFire FP + 0.5 × projected receptions.
- FantasyLabs Plus/Minus model ranks and projected ownership cited without formulas.
- RotoGrinders Noto note: "3 RBs with 20+ touch upside was the winning structure last season" — GPP construction heuristic.
## Data sources named

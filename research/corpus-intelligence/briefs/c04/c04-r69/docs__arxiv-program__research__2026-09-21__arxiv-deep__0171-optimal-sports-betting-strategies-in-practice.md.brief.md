# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0171-optimal-sports-betting-strategies-in-practice.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A full-paper deep-read of Uhrin et al. (2021, arXiv:2107.08827): a formal + experimental review of 10 bankroll-management strategies (Kelly, MPT/Sharpe, fractional, drawdown-constrained, distributionally-robust Kelly, max-bet limits, two informal heuristics) under probability-estimation uncertainty, evaluated on 3 real datasets under a unified train/test protocol. The verdict is ADOPT — it fills existing-research-map GAP #1 (Kelly under estimation error); the fractional-Kelly-with-tuned-fraction sizing rule plus KellyDrawdown/robust-Kelly variants are recommended as GSE's sizing protocol.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Problem: strategy g:(p_hat, o) -> f mapping player estimates + odds to portfolio fractions f over outcomes + cash; multiplicative dynamics W_t = w_t * W_{t-dt}.
- Kelly: maximize E[log(O f)] s.t. sum f_i = 1, f_i >= 0. MPT: maximize E[rho f] - gamma Var[rho f]. MaxSharpe: maximize E[rho f]/sqrt(f^T Sigma f). Fractional: f_omega = omega f_{1..n-1} + (1-omega) f_n (cash), omega in [0,1]; half-Kelly = omega 0.5. KellyFracMax/MSharpeFracMax add max-bet limit m.
- KellyDrawdown: P(W^t_min < alpha) <= beta, convexified as E[(O f)^(-lambda)] <= 1 with lambda = log(beta)/log(alpha) (eqs. 5.4-5.5 garbled in extraction — consult Busseti et al. 2016 "Risk-constrained Kelly gambling" for the clean derivation).
- KellyRobust: maximize min_{p in Pi} sum p_i log(O_i f), box ambiguity set Pi = {p: |p_i - P_hat_p(r_i)| <= eta P_hat_p(r_i), sum p_i = 1, p_i >= 0}.
- Hyperparameter selection: grid search on train, selection = maximize median(Wf) subject to Q5 > 0.9 (at most 5% of trajectories fall below 90% of final wealth) — an explicit "survival first, then profit" rule. Optimization via cvxpy (ECOS primary, SCS fallback).
- Validation: 1000 runs per subset (reshuffled game order, 10% removed per run, train/test split respected); metrics median(Wf), mean(Wf), min/max, sigma(Wf), ruin% (ruin = W < 0.01% of W_0 at least once).
- Defined: AKL = KL-advantage of player vs bookmaker (cross-entropy difference); Kelly's theoretical growth rate provably proportional to AKL.
## Data sources named
Three proprietary-assembled, unreleased datasets: Korean horse racing KRA "win pool" (2700 races, n in [6,16], odds [1.0, 931.3], margin 0.2, player acc 0.512, bookmaker acc 0.503, AKL ~= +0.0022); NBA moneyline 16,000 games 2000-2015 (odds [1.01, 41], margin 0.038, player acc 0.68, bookmaker acc 0.70, AKL ~= -0.0146; Pinnacle closing lines); soccer 32,000 matches (moneyline n=3, odds [1.03, 66], margin 0.03, player acc 0.523, bookmaker acc 0.537, AKL ~= -0.013; Pinnacle OPENING lines). ## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Horse racing (AKL +0.0022): best KellyFracMax median(Wf)=3.49 / mean=13.8 / min=0.0057 / max=168.1 / sigma=29.3 / ruin 0%. Plain Kelly median 4.11 ruin 0.6%; MSharpe median 3.92 ruin 12.1%; KellyRobust lowest wealth (median 2.97) but most stable (min 0.08, sigma 7.2, ruin 0). Informal heuristics: AbsDisc ruin 85.2% median 0.0019; MaxEvFrac ruin 36.1% median 0.86.
- Basketball (AKL -0.0146): plain Kelly median 9.1e-6, ruin 100%; MSharpe median 1.3e-6, ruin 100%. Fractional versions profitable: KellyFrac median 2.4 / mean 2.7 / ruin 0%; KellyDrawdown median 2.21; KellyRobust median 1.39 / min 0.23 (highest minimum).
- Football (AKL -0.013): Kelly ruin 100% median 2.3e-09; MSharpe ruin 100%. KellyDrawdown best: median 10.25 / mean 12.4 / min 0.09 / max 122 / sigma 9.3 / ruin 0; KellyFrac median 10.05; MSharpeFracMax median 10.1; KellyRobust median 6.2 / min 0.28 (highest minimum).
- Headline findings: plain Kelly/MSharpe led to RUIN (100% in both AKL<0 scenarios) under uncertain estimates; fractional Kelly best-or-near-best across experiments ("fraction hyperparameter has been properly tuned to reflect the amount of uncertainty in each particular problem setting"); Kelly drawdown performed very similarly to fractional Kelly; robust Kelly safest but lowest wealth; max-bet limit inconclusive (helped racing, hurt basketball); Kelly strategies higher median final wealth, MPT strategies higher mean final wealth. A smart modification can generate profits even in AKL-disadvantageous scenarios.
- Caveats: authors disclose the selection criterion may be "slightly biased in favour of the Kelly approaches"; reshuffling+10% removal destroys strict temporal structure; soccer used opening lines (more favorable than realistically attainable), basketball closing lines — inconsistent.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")

Two sign conventions coexist (price-beat in `clv.ts`/tracker; model-edge-vs-close in `clv-calibration.ts`) — must never be aggregated together. Formulas: not specified (code references).
- Calibration primitives (pure, tested, gated/not in live display path): isotonic regression via PAVA, Murphy `brierDecomposition`, `expectedCalibrationError`, `reliabilityCurve`, `calibration-drift.ts`; calibrator `buildCalibrator` self-suppresses unless sample ≥100 AND ECE does not worsen. Formulas: not specified.
- Shin devig (`shin-devig.ts`), Elo estimator + Elo backtest, Poisson (runtime-guarded OFF), ML estimator (`ml-estimator.ts`), edge engine `assessEdge` emitting `expectedClv`, Kelly (`kelly.ts`), MC permutation p-value for edge significance (`edge-significance.ts`), Merkle proof-of-record (`proof-of-record.ts`), conviction tiers (gated OFF). Formulas: not specified.
- `market-read.ts` `marketGravityIndex()` — conviction-banded, but the formula differs from the proposal doc. `protection-stress.ts` — simpler than the proposed blitz-regression. `qb-forward.ts` — DAKOTA+ANY/A. Human-performance `availability.ts`/`environment.ts` Phase 1 — can only widen the band or trigger no-bet, never manufacture confidence. Formulas: not specified.
- Historical calibration backtest at `/api/calibration/market-backtest` + `probability-calibration.ts` — real Brier/ECE on closing-line (forecast, outcome) pairs back to 1999 (the highest-leverage built asset per the doc).
- Proof-gated pricing ladder (`pricing-phases.ts`): FOUNDING $14.99/$24.99 (live) → PROVEN (≥100 settled + calibration) $19.99/$29.99 → ESTABLISHED (≥500 settled + CLV ≥52.4%) $29.99/$49.99 → AUTHORITY (multi-season ROI). Founding grandfathered for life; advances are manual via `PRICING_PHASE` env.
- Settled-pick floor: 100 (env `MIN_SETTLED_PICKS_FOR_LEARNING`); some policy defaults say 25 — the effective floor is 100. `MODEL_VERSION="v5.0.0"` frozen (kelly.ts/poisson.ts headers say v6.0.0 — stale comments).
- Freshness SLAs: warn 120 min / stale 240 min; odds TTL 5 min; pick-gen hard guard 60 min. Source hierarchy: six tiers, only T1/T2 back picks, T1 overrides on conflict. `clearance-engine.ts` `checkClearance()` runs 14 checks; `wrapExtractedRecord()` throws without granted clearance.

# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1037-athletepose3d-benchmark.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of arXiv:2503.07499 (Yeung et al. 2025), the AthletePose3D benchmark: ~1.3M frames / 165K unique postures of athletic motion (running, track & field throws, figure skating) with MoCap ground truth, testing whether SOTA monocular 3D pose models work on true sports motion and whether derived kinematics are valid. Verdict ADAPT: the ~60% error reduction from sport-specific fine-tuning and the kinematic-validation protocol (angles trustworthy, velocities not) transfer directly to GSE's computer-vision lane.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- PDJ@0.2 (normalizer = shoulder-center to hip-center distance), PDJ-AUC over thresholds 0.0–0.2, AP for 2D models
- MPJPE (Protocol 1, hip-aligned), P-MPJPE (Protocol 2, Procrustes rigid alignment) for 3D models
- Kinematic validation: 4th-order Butterworth 8 Hz zero-phase smoothing of derived waveforms, paired t-test statistical parametric mapping (α=0.05) + Pearson correlation vs MoCap ground truth
- 3D models tested with 81-frame input windows; 60/20/20 train/val/test split; 2D fine-tuning from COCO pretraining (20 epochs, 1e-4→1e-5→1e-6)
## Data sources named
AthletePose3D dataset + code/checkpoints: https://github.com/calvinyeungck/AthletePose3D (public). Running lab set (3 subjects, 55 keypoints, 4 cameras, 40K poses, 120 FPS, 161K frames); track & field (shot put, glide shot put, javelin, discus, spin discus; 1 subject, 86 keypoints, 8 cameras, 52K poses, 60 FPS, 416K frames); figure skating (Axel, Salchow, Toe Loop, Loop, Flip, Lutz; 4 subjects, 86 keypoints, 12 cameras, 73K poses, 60 FPS, 700K frames). Markerless Qualisys Miqus Video, calibration error <1 mm. Compared against Human3.6M, MPI-INF-3DHP, SportsPose. 3D models: MotionAGFormer, TCPFormer; 2D models: HRNet, SwinPose, ViTPose, UniFormer, MogaNet.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
SOTA 3D pose models fail on athletic motion without sport-specific data: MotionAGFormer trained on H3.6M → MPJPE 257.26 mm (P-MPJPE 95.67; knee 320.39, ankle 560.23); TCPFormer H3.6M → 234.20 mm (P-MPJPE 101.80; ankle 324.96). After AP3D fine-tuning: MotionAGFormer 100.12 mm / 31.84; TCPFormer 98.77 / 30.67 (~60% reduction). Best: H3.6M+AP3D — TCPFormer 98.26 mm / 29.91 mm; MotionAGFormer 98.62 / 30.54. 2D PDJ@0.2: MogaNet 95.7 (AUC 81.7, AP 95.6), UniFormer 95.2 (AUC 80.9), ViTPose 95.0 (AUC 81.4, AP 95.8 best), SwinPose 90.7, HRNet 88.7. Kinematic validation: joint-angle correlations — 3D model upper limb r=0.90 (p=0.001), lower limb r=0.82 (p=0.044); 2D: 0.75 (p=0.021) / 0.66 (p<0.001). Velocity correlations: 2D 0.47/0.45; 3D 0.28 (p=0.036) / 0.11 (p=0.004). All SPM paired t-tests p<0.05 — every estimated waveform differs significantly from GT. Wrist/ankle/hip speeds and accelerations significantly higher than Human3.6M/MPI-INF-3DHP/SportsPose. Limitations: only 3 sports, 8 athletes, no team/field sports, no football; lab/rink capture at 120/60 FPS, not broadcast; best 3D error ~98 mm still large for fine biomechanics.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- QB-BEHAVIOR: throwing-kinematics metrics from pose are viable via joint angles (r≥0.8 for 3D) once validated against ground truth — gating rule before publishing any technique metric
- COACHING: joint-angle waveforms as the only trustworthy pose-derived kinematics; velocities untrustworthy (r=0.11 lower limb for 3D)
- OTHER: sport-specific fine-tuning transfer principle (~60% error cut); lab→broadcast degradation experiment (25 FPS, motion blur, compression) that GSE should run

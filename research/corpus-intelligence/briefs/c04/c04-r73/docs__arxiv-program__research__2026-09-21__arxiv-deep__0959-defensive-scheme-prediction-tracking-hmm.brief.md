# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0959-defensive-scheme-prediction-tracking-hmm.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A sports-analytics paper (Michels, Bajons, Fischer, 2026, arXiv:2602.10784) forecasting NFL defensive coverage scheme (man vs zone) from pre-snap motion tracking data: a non-homogeneous HMM decodes latent defender→offensive-player guarding assignments, and decoded-state summary features (switch counts, assignment entropy, play-level random effect) boost elastic net and XGBoost classifiers. The deep-read ledger rates it ADAPT for GSE's pre-snap coverage/matchup lane.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Three-stage features: (1) pre-motion (context: quarter/down/yards/yardline/scores/seconds left + spatial: convex-hull area/width/length + 20 standardized-position features for 10 players); (2) naive post-motion (6 features: max x/y distance + total distance traveled between motion start and snap, offense and defense); (3) HMM features (4 per play: total switches, # switching defenders, average assignment entropy, play-specific random effect w_p).
- HMM: per-defender y-coordinate time series; N=5 states = 5 offensive players; f(y_t|S_t=j) ∼ N(μ_{t,j}, σ²), μ_{t,j} = y_{t−l}(off_j) with reaction lag l = 4 (0.4 s at 10 Hz, AIC-selected).
- Non-homogeneous transitions via mixed-effects multinomial logit: η_{ij}^{(t,r,d,p)} = β₀ + β₁|y_{t−l}(off_i) − y_{t−l}(off_j)| + u_r + v_d + w_p (i≠j; η_{ii} ≡ 0), u_r ∼ N(0,σ²_role), v_d ∼ N(0,σ²_defense), w_p ∼ N(0,σ²_play); γ_{ij} = exp(η_{ij})/Σ_k exp(η_{ik}).
- Fit: marginal maximum likelihood with Laplace approximation (R RTMB + nlminb); defender likelihoods via forward algorithm (LaMa); local decoding (eq. 7) → most-likely guarded player per frame; initial δ^{(1)} from spatial proximity.
- Supervised: elastic net logistic regression (glmnet; λ, α tuned) and XGBoost (depth/LR/rounds tuned); 50-times-repeated 5-fold cross-fitting with inner 5-fold CV; metrics accuracy/AUC/logloss (logloss primary).
- GCM test: GCM ≔ E[Cov(Y,X|Z)] = E[(Y − E[Y|Z])(X − E[X|Z])] for conditional independence Y ⊥ X | Z; PLLR interpretation logit π(X,Z) = Xβ + g(Z).
## Data sources named
NFL Big Data Bowl 2025: 10 Hz tracking + play-by-play, first nine weeks of the 2023 NFL season (Introduction says "2024 NFL season" — noted inconsistency). Filtered to motion plays excluding two-QB plays and bunch formations → M = 3,963 offensive plays; PFF labels: 2,980 zone, 983 man (25% man). Preprocessing: drop OL/QB (offense) and DL/LOS-aligned OLB pass-rushers (defense); keep 5 closest defenders → 19,815 defender time series. Data public (Kaggle); code: https://github.com/janoleko/BDB-2025/.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Supervised metrics reported only as boxplots (Figure 5) — no numeric table; directionally, adding naive post-motion then HMM features improves accuracy, AUC, and negative logloss for both classifiers. (Exact values are chart approximations — not quoted per the exact-numbers rule.)
- GCM omnibus test: p ≈ 0.0002 — HMM features significantly associated with coverage given all other features. Per-feature GCM: only per-play RE significant (p ≈ 0.005); others not rejected (authors attribute to correlation among the 4 features). PLLR: all 4 features significant with negative β — fewer switches / lower entropy / lower RE → higher P(man), matching intuition.
- Table 2 descriptives: total switches median 1.00, mean 1.82, max 21; switching defenders median 1.00, mean 1.45, max 5; entropy median 0.13, mean 0.17; RE/play median −0.03, mean 0.00.
- Position random effects: ILB 0.007, MLB 0.006, OLB 0.006, CB −0.178, SS −0.005, FS 0.175 — smaller = more persistent guarding; CBs follow receivers, safeties/LBs pass off assignments.
- Example t.p.m. (Marco Wilson, Cardinals CB, at the Hardman–McKinnon crossing): diagonal 0.983–1.000 — persistence even at the crossing point.
- Leave-one-team-out: most teams show positive median improvement from motion features; top: Kansas City Chiefs, Tennessee Titans, Atlanta Falcons; Atlanta and San Francisco heavy motion users; Giants effective despite little motion.

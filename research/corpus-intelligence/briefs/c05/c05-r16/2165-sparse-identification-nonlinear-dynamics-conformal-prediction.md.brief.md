# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2165-sparse-identification-nonlinear-dynamics-conformal-prediction.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Research ledger for "Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics with Conformal Prediction" (arXiv:2507.11739), which integrates conformal prediction with Ensemble-SINDy to give distribution-free coverage guarantees for time-series prediction intervals, library feature importance (LOCO/LOCO-path), and uncertainty intervals on discovered equation coefficients. Ledger verdict: ADOPT — directly solves GSE's calibration problem: every published equation and forecast gets honest uncertainty intervals that hold even under non-Gaussian noise; a trust asset for GSE's public publishing mandate ("every pick public, every result posted").
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- SINDy: ẋ = f(x) (Eq. 1); Ẋ = Θ(X)Ξ (Eq. 2); Ξ = argmin ‖Ẋ−ΘΞ̂‖²₂ + λ‖Ξ̂‖₀ (Eq. 3); STLSQ solution; Ensemble-SINDy: B bootstrap resamples of [Ẋ, Θ(X)] rows → B fits → median/mean aggregation + inclusion-probability thresholding; weak-form SINDy (no derivative computation on noisy data).
- Coverage target: P(Y_{n+1} ∈ C_α(X_{n+1})) ≥ 1−α (Eq. 4); split-CP interval C_α = [f̂ − q_{1−α}, f̂ + q_{1−α}] (Eqs. 5–6); CP-PID long-run coverage (1/T)Σ err_t = α + o(1) (Eq. 7).
- EnbPI + E-SINDy for time-series prediction: non-conformity scores s_t^(b) = |y_t − f̂_b(x_t)| (Eq. 8), out-of-sample only; interval C_{n+1} = [f̄ − q_{1−α}, f̄ + q_{1−α}] (Eqs. 9–10); sliding calibration window of size l_r, recalibrated online. Each ensemble member = SINDy equation + RK4 integrator + Savitzky–Golay state estimator forecasting t_p steps ahead.
- CP-PID: quantile adapted by PI controller on miscoverage error g_t = err_t − α: q_{t+1} = ηg_t (P) + r_t(Σg_i) (I) (Eq. 11) — no exchangeability needed, bounded scores only.
- LOCO/LOCO-path for library feature importance: refit SINDy without feature j (Eq. 12); excess error Δ_j = ‖ẋ−ΘΞ^(−j)‖₁ − ‖ẋ−ΘΞ‖₁ (Eq. 13) via jackknife+; LOCO-path T_j = Σ_λ ‖Ξ^(−j)(λ) − Ξ(λ)‖₁ (Eq. 14) — importance across the whole λ path, no λ selection needed.
- Feature-CP for coefficient uncertainty: jackknife ensemble; surrogate SINDy model per excluded point via constrained least squares Θ(x_i)ξ_k = ẋ_i (Eqs. 15–17, KKT system); non-conformity s_i = ‖Ξ̃_i − Ξ_i‖₁ (Eq. 18); quantiles → 90% intervals on coefficients.
- Predator–prey ground truth (Eq. 19): ẋ₁ = αx₁ − βx₁x₂ + w₁, ẋ₂ = δx₁x₂ − γx₂ + w₂, y = x + v; true params [α,β,γ,δ] = [1, 0.1, 1, 0.1]; dt=0.1.
- Assumptions: EnbPI needs exchangeability-ish residuals ("approximately valid" under mild estimator assumptions); CP-PID needs only bounded scores; LOCO sensitive to correlated features (compensating covariates underestimate importance); post-selection inference caveat for feature-CP acknowledged (no formal finite-sample guarantees claimed for the SINDy-specific procedures).
## Data sources named
- Fully synthetic, reproducible: stochastic predator–prey (plus non-zero-mean gamma observation-noise variant), 10-step-horizon appendix variant; Lorenz (3 ODEs, 2nd-order polynomial); hyperchaotic Rössler variant (2+ positive Lyapunov exponents); quadrupole boson Hamiltonian (4 eqs, 3rd-order polynomials). Code: https://github.com/urban-fasel/SINDyCP_tutorials. GSE test: 2022–2024 nflverse game-state trajectories; rolling-origin evaluation on 2025 season.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Time-series prediction: both EnbPI and CP-PID achieve the 90% target coverage in the low-data regime with narrow intervals; CP-PID starts wide (adapts aggressively) then converges; EnbPI oscillates conservatively around target; target coverage matched at all tested levels under both noise regimes; longer horizons (10 steps) and chaotic systems (Lorenz, hyper-Rössler, quadrupole) still hit target coverage with wider intervals (Figure A.1).
- Feature importance: LOCO, LOCO-path and inclusion probabilities all stabilize rapidly in the low-data regime; true active terms score consistently higher than irrelevant library terms (averaged over 100 realizations).
- Coefficient uncertainty (Figure 5): standard E-SINDy intervals cover true coefficients (α=1, β=−0.1, γ=−1, δ=0.1) under low Gaussian noise but FAIL under larger noise, gamma noise, or process noise; feature-CP intervals are wider but cover the truth across all noise types. Appendix: feature-CP compared vs WENDy (errors-in-variables) and BSINDy (Bayesian) over 100 realizations.
- Validation: 100 repeated noise realizations; coverage measured over trailing 50-step windows; target coverage levels swept (Figure 3); noise levels swept (low/high measurement + process).

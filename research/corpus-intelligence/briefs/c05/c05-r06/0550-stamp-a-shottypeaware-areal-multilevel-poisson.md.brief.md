# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0550-stamp-a-shottypeaware-areal-multilevel-poisson.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Yamada & Fujii (2026, arXiv:2603.24015) modeling basketball team shot-type-specific spatial tendencies with a hierarchical Poisson GLMM (INLA-fitted) that separates stylistic tendency from pace via a possession exposure offset. Ledger verdict ADAPT: the areal Poisson-with-exposure-offset template ports directly to NFL team x field-zone x play-type rate modeling; the basketball application itself is not GSE-relevant.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Likelihood: Y_{isadk} ~ Poisson(mu_{isadk}); log mu_{isadk} = log E_{is} + eta_{isadk}, E_{is} = team possessions (exposure offset). Linear predictor: eta = beta_0 + season + side + shot-type fixed effects + team i.i.d. u_i + area i.i.d. v_a + seasonally-correlated team x area w_{ias} (equicorrelation) + season-specific team x side z_{ids} + shot-type loadings gamma_m(k) on random slopes r^{(m)}_{ias} for jump shots and step-back/pull-up. Sum-to-zero constraints on all random-effect blocks. Penalized Complexity priors on variance/correlation hyperparameters, calibrated via prior predictive checks; grids U_sd, U_slope in {0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5}, 7 admissible settings. One INLA fit ≈ 1 minute wall-clock. Outputs: per-team x area x shot-type posterior relative-rate maps vs league and left-right bias log(Right/Left) with ~95% credible intervals. Validation: train on regular-season, evaluate out-of-sample on postseason via Monte Carlo expected log predictive density (elpd_post^MC) + LPML.
## Data sources named
B.LEAGUE (Japan pro basketball) play-by-play, 2023-24 and 2024-25: 2,352 games, 306,444 field-goal attempts (296,768 regular season = 96.84%; 9,676 postseason). 13 court areas, 6 merged shot-type categories (jump_shot 156,423; step_pull 50,454; lay_up 48,550; floater 19,000; rim_finishes 17,819; fade_turn 14,198); mean FG attempts per team per season 4,032.16 (sd 251.80). Data proprietary (Data Stadium Inc. via Institute of Statistical Mathematics). Software: R-INLA. No code link stated.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Full model: elpd_post^MC = -3,383; LPML_reg = -49,967. No-random-effects baseline: -6,581 (LPML -130,617); full-model gain Delta elpd ≈ 3.2x10^3. Intermediate: team x area only -> -6,422; +jump+step slopes (no team x side) -> -3,385 (only ~2 units worse out-of-sample than full model) while substantially better LPML (-37,835) — team x side effect is marginal. Prior sensitivity: best elpd always one of the two richest structures across all 7 prior settings; Kendall's tau between model rankings across priors > 0.97. Extended slopes: best rows ≈ -3,284 to -3,352 elpd but LPML_reg ~ -5x10^5 (overfitting). Reader flags: no opponent adjustment (tendencies conflate style with schedule); frequency-only, efficiency not modeled; 2 seasons only so equicorrelation barely identified; NFL cells far sparser (~1,000 plays/team/season vs 300k+ shots) so partial pooling is even more necessary; postseason eval set only 3.16% of data.

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0560-mallows-model-with-learned-distance-metrics.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Alimohammadi & Asgari (2025, arXiv:2507.08108) generalizing the Mallows ranking model so the distance metric itself is learned (d_alpha(pi,sigma) = sum_i |pi(i)-sigma(i)|^alpha), with an FPTAS sampler, joint MLE for (alpha, beta, central ranking), and consistency proofs validated on college football/basketball power rankings. Ledger verdict ADAPT: a learnable upset-shape parameter alpha is new capability for GSE poll-aggregation/tournament simulation; it explains ranking dispersion, not matchup outcomes.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
L_alpha distance: d_alpha(pi,sigma) := sum_i |pi(i) - sigma(i)|^alpha. Mallows law: P_{alpha,beta,sigma}(pi) = e^{-beta d_alpha(pi,sigma)}/Z_n(beta,alpha). Log-likelihood: L_m(alpha,beta,sigma) = -(beta/m) sum_l d_alpha(pi^(l),sigma) - log Z_n(beta,alpha). Score equations Psi_m zeroed with differential evolution. Two-step MLE: (1) central ranking sigma_hat via min-weight perfect matching on bipartite graph with edge weights sum_l |pi^(l)(i) - j| (consistent for any true alpha_tilde > 0); (2) (alpha, beta) from gradient equations. Central-ranking error bound: P(sigma_hat_m != sigma_0) <= n! * exp(-m/(3n^4)); with alpha_tilde = n/3 improves to <= n! * exp(-m/(54n^2)). Asymptotic normality: sqrt(m)((alpha_hat-alpha_0),(beta_hat-beta_0)) -> N(0, I^{-1}) with Fisher matrix [[beta^2 Var[dot d], beta Cov[dot d, d]],[beta Cov[dot d, d], Var[d]]]. Truncation sampler: k = log(n/epsilon)/(log((1+e^{-2beta})/(2e^{-2beta}))); DP sampling in O(n k C(2k,k)); marginal decay P(pi(i)=j) <= c(alpha,beta)^{|j-i|} for alpha >= 1. FPTAS requires alpha >= 1; alpha < 1 sampling is stated open.
## Data sources named
College football power rankings 2019-2021 (Massey Ratings composites, >4,100 rankings; also 100-team set); college basketball 2020-2021 (>1,700 rankings; also 100 random teams); sushi preference dataset (5,000 rankings over 10 types); synthetic data n=15 with known (alpha_0,beta_0) = (1.5,0.5) and (1,1). Code: github.com/Kiana-Asgari/Generalized_Mallow_Learning.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Football top-10: alpha_hat = 0.442 (+/-0.061), beta_hat = 0.455 (+/-0.051). Held-out (50 trials): L_alpha-Mallows Spearman rho 0.094 (+/-0.010), pairwise accuracy 53.5% (+/-0.4), top-1 8.0%, top-5 41.9% vs Mallows-tau (top-1 6.9%, top-5 41.1%) and PL (top-1 3.5%, top-5 30.5%). Basketball top-10: alpha_hat = 1.096 (+/-0.056), beta_hat = 0.178 (+/-0.019); top-1 22.7%, top-5 77.7% vs Mallows-tau (17.5%, 68.4%) and PL (4.9%, 57.7% — PL overfits). 100-team basketball: alpha_hat = 0.002 (+/-0.002), top-1 12.873% vs Mallows-tau 2.212% and PL 1.749% (>5x top-1 improvement). Sampler: partition-function relative error (1.25 +/- 0.03)e-4 at n=6, (2.94 +/- 0.06)e-4 at n=10; time ratio vs exact Ryser: 32.0 at n=6, 1194.5 at n=8, 113531.6 at n=10. Reader flags: top-10 teams pre-selected from known end-of-season polls (survivorship bias); 100-team alpha_hat ≈ 0.002 means the model nearly "learns away" the metric; NFL has 32 teams and far fewer weekly ranking sources than Massey's composite corpus; sports-relevant alpha_hat (0.44) is below 1, outside the sampler's proven regime.

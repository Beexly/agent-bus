# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0540-from-uncertain-judgments-to-calibrated-rankings.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Kargi & Salinas (2026, arXiv:2606.13221) fixing two LLM-leaderboard problems: hard win/tie/loss labels in Bradley-Terry fitting produce correctly-ordered but mis-scaled Elos, and conformal intervals on ratings are too wide to be useful. Ledger verdict ADAPT: calibrated soft targets in BT via a fitted score-to-probability map and split-conformal intervals on ratings port directly to GSE's NFL team-rating and margin models.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
BT objective: theta_hat = argmax sum[y_ij log sigma(theta_i - theta_j) + (1 - y_ij) log(1 - sigma(theta_i - theta_j))] - lambda||theta||^2, lambda=0.01. Elo_i = 1500 + (400/ln 10)*theta_i. Soft target: y_tilde(x) = sigma(beta*s(x)) in (0,1), s(x) = judge score difference. Temperature beta fit per fold by MLE on human non-tie battles (beta*_m argmax over cross-entropy of sigma(beta*s) vs human labels). Conformal: residuals epsilon_i = Elo_LLM,i - Elo_Human,i; normalized nonconformity S_i = |epsilon_i|/SE_hat_i (bootstrap SE over 20 battle subsamples); interval [Elo_LLM - q_hat*SE_hat, Elo_LLM + q_hat*SE_hat], q_hat = ceil((1-alpha)(n+1))-th smallest S_i. Metrics: Cohen's kappa, Spearman rho vs human leaderboard, held-out Elo MAE, coverage at nominal 90%.
## Data sources named
LMArena 100K (main; 55 models, ~25,000 battle rows per each of 8 judges: DeepSeek-V3.2, Qwen3.5-27B, Gemma4-E4B, Gemma4-26B-A4B, Qwen3-32B, Llama-3.3-70B, GPT-OSS-20B, GPT-OSS-120B); LMArena 140K (replication); ComparIA (French-language distribution-shift stress test). Code at github.com/kargibora/SoftElo.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Hard-Elo diagnostics: battle agreement kappa 0.147-0.231 (low), yet Spearman rho 0.868-0.976 ("good rankings, bad ruler"); held-out Elo MAE 27.5-63.4 (DeepSeek-V3.2 worst 63.4, Qwen3-32B best 27.5). Root cause: median |s| ≈ 0.61 for top-vs-top-quartile battles vs ≈ 1.54 for top-vs-bottom — Hard-Elo treats both as identical unit wins. Residuals correlate with human Elo at Pearson +0.49 to +0.90 (stretched ruler). Soft-Elo: MAE cut 39-73% per judge (cross-judge mean 45.9 -> 17.9 Elo; DeepSeek 63.4 -> 17.1, -73%); rank order preserved (Spearman delta in [-0.011, +0.014]). beta* in [0.36, 0.60] across judges, fold std <= 0.005; uncalibrated sigma(s) ECE 0.14 -> fitted sigma(beta*s) ECE 0.03. Conformal (90% nominal): Hard-Elo median widths 131.9-261.0 Elo -> Soft-Elo 74.4-143.1 Elo (39-70% narrower), coverage 92.1-96.4%. Replication on LMArena 140K: MAE reduction 45-79%. Diagnostic warning: on ComparIA shift, Qwen beta* collapsed to 0.13-0.14 and soft targets damaged rankings — flat score-agreement curve means don't trust soft targets.

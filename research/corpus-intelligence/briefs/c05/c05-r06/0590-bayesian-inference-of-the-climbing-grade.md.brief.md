# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0590-bayesian-inference-of-the-climbing-grade.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Drummond & Popinga (2021, arXiv:2111.08140) performing full Bayesian MCMC inference (Stan) of a dynamic Bradley-Terry model on climbing ascents to quantify what one grade increment means in difficulty, with climber ability following a Wiener process. Ledger verdict ADAPT: the Wiener-drift dynamic-rating model and data-driven rating-scale calibration port to GSE team strengths; the climbing domain itself has no NFL transfer.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Success model: Pr(y=1) = p_send = e^{m C(t)}/(e^{m C(t)} + e^{m R}) = logit^{-1}(m C(t) - m R); log-odds of success linear in grade difference. Wiener-process ability dynamics: C(t+1) ~ N(C(t), w^2). Expected failures before redpoint: E[a] = (1 - p_send)/p_send. Session-grade convention: best outcome per climber per route per day. Full Bayesian MCMC in Stan co-estimating slope m and every climber's monthly grade; per-climber log-linear regressions (no drift) as crude comparator; O'Neill-style log-linear fits of community ascent counts. Assumptions: route grades known/correct; per-attempt success constant (no learning — authors flag as "the most questionable detail"); selection bias shifts intercept up to ~1 grade but flattens slope only ~(k-1)/k ≈ 10%.
## Data sources named
thecrag.com (public climbing logbook), 20 datasets across NZ/Australia/Germany via theCrag API. Highlighted: Australia-Sport (n=100 climbers, N=48,679 ascents, Aug 2016-Jul 2021, 60 monthly windows); New Zealand-Sport (n=89). Climbers filtered to >=30 attempts with >=1 explicit failure. Second analysis: whole-community successful-ascent counts by grade (NZ + Australia, sport + bouldering, downloaded 20 July 2021). Stan code: github.com/alexeid/climbing-grades (public domain).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Australia-Sport: m_hat = 0.85 [0.83, 0.86], i.e., d = e^m = 2.33 [2.29, 2.37] x more failed attempts per success per grade increment (vs no-drift log-linear mean 0.65). New Zealand-Sport: m_hat = 0.80 [0.76, 0.84], 2.22 [2.13, 2.31] x per grade. Abstract-level estimates by grade system: one increment = 2.1x (Ewbank), 2.09x (French), 2.13x (UIAA), 3.17x (Vermin bouldering) difficulty increase. Community ascent-count slopes 0.7-0.79 across countries/styles, matching the Bayesian analysis. Median explicit fails per climber: 126 (range 18-642); fails were 29.9% of ascents (IQR 4.7%-72.0%). MCMC runtime: 1 hr 8 min on an iMac 3.6 GHz 8-core i9. Bias: selective failure logging overestimates slope (known complete loggers show slopes slightly below 2). Reader flags: all inference in-sample, no held-out prediction; route grades treated as truth; self-reporting biases (embarrassment logging) partially modeled only by argument; external NFL validity is nil except for the method.

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Ismail (2023, arXiv:2312.12700) introducing the Estimated Performance Rating (PRe), defined via a constrained binomial-probability maximization, which is proved equivalent to Tournament Performance Rating (TPR) whenever TPR is defined but remains defined for perfect/zero scores where TPR is undefined. Ledger verdict ADAPT: the PRe theorem is worth porting as the mathematical basis for "peak-performance" ratings of NFL teams/units over streak windows.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Elo win probability: W(A,B) = 1/(1 + 10^((B-A)/400)). Average opponent rating: R_a = (1/k) sum_j b_j. TPR: m = n/(1 + 10^((R_a - TPR)/400)). Binomial score probability: S(w,m,n) = C(n,m) w^m (1-w)^(n-m). PRe: max_{w in [0,1]} S(w,m,n) subject to S(w,m,n) <= t, default t = 0.75. Back-translation: w* = 1/(1 + 10^((R_a - A*)/400)); A* = R_a - 400 log10((1-w*)/w*). Main theorem: for 0 < m < n, a rating R is the TPR iff W(R, R_a) in argmax_w S(w,m,n) (proof: d/dw of the binomial gives w* = m/n, matching TPR's defining equation). Draws handled by doubling m,n; alternative objective S_bar(w,m,n) = sum_{k=m}^{n} C(n,k) w^k (1-w)^(n-k).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Theorem empirically illustrated: TPR and PRe coincide for every m and n <= 30 (Figure 1). Table 1 (R_a=2700): 3/3 -> TPR N/A, FIDE FPR 3500, PRe 3099; 5/5 -> PRe 3191 (FIDE's FPR is streak-length-blind). Table 2 (tennis 2023): Alcaraz Wimbledon 7/7 (R_a=1927) -> PRe 2478 (highest, strongest field); Djokovic French Open 7/7 (R_a=1867) -> 2417; Australian Open (R_a=1865) -> 2416; US Open (R_a=1798) -> 2349. Table 3 (World Cups): Brazil 1970 6/6 (R_a=1900) -> PRe 2424; Brazil 2002 7/7 -> 2369; Italy 1938 4/4 -> 2253; Uruguay 1930 4/4 -> 2150. Chess: Fischer 20-win streak (R_a=2705) -> PRe 3441 vs Steinitz 25-win -> 3356 (quantifies the informal judgment that Fischer's was more impressive); Fischer 11/11 US Ch 1963 -> 3224; Caruana 7-win (R_a=2793) -> 3344. Table 6 (R_a=2700, n=2): TPR and PRe agree at 2891 for 1.5/2; PRe = 2700 at 1/2, 2509 at 0.5/2, 3024 for 2/2, 2376 for 0/2. Reader flags: average-opponent collapse discards opponent variance (Jensen gap); per-game independence assumed; t=0.75 threshold is arbitrary and entirely drives long-streak PRe; no predictive validation as a forecaster; text's w*=0.29 for 0/2 contradicts table's w*=0.13 (table value used).

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0519-first-order-approximations-of-the-pythagorean.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Dayaratna & Miller (2012, arXiv:1205.4750) proving the linear MLB win% model WP = 0.500 + beta(RS - RA) is the first-order Taylor approximation of Bill James's Pythagorean formula, pinning the slope to a closed form, verified on 20 MLB seasons. Ledger verdict ADAPT: the linearized coefficient transfers to NFL point-differential models but must be re-fit per sport.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Pythagorean: WP ≈ RS^gamma / (RS^gamma + RA^gamma). Linear predictor: WP = 0.500 + beta(RS - RA). Taylor result: beta = gamma / (4*R_avg). Partial derivatives at (R_avg, R_avg): df/dx = gamma/(4*R_avg), df/dy = -gamma/(4*R_avg). Appendix identity: WP = (1 + (RA/RS)^gamma)^-1 ≈ 1/2 + (gamma/4)(ln RS - ln RA) ≈ 1/2 + (gamma/(4*R_avg))(RS - RA). Empirical: per-season OLS of WP on (RS - RA) with intercept at 0.500; gamma_hat recovered via gamma_hat = beta_hat * 4 * R_avg; 95% CIs with Bonferroni correction (alpha = 0.05/20 = 0.0025). Jones & Tappin (2005) reference: beta range 0.00053-0.00078 (mean 0.00065) over 1969-2003; benchmark gamma ≈ 1.82 (Miller 2007).
## Data sources named
MLB seasonal team data 1991-2011 (20 seasons, 600 team-seasons), scraped from BaseballAlmanac.com (script by Eric Fritz; MLB.com standings as corroborating source). No code link, no downloadable dataset stated.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Per-season beta_hat ranges 0.084-0.126 (1991: beta_hat=0.119, R^2=0.922; 2010: 0.094, R^2=0.950; 2011: 0.104, R^2=0.867); R^2 across all 20 seasons from 0.807 to 0.950. Recovered gamma_hat ranges 1.634 (2010) to 2.108 (2004); every season's 95% CI except 2010 contains 1.82, and with Bonferroni correction 2010's CI [1.399, 1.870] also contains it. All estimates significant at 0.0025 level. Jones & Tappin cross-check: beta_bar=0.00065 with gamma=1.81 implies 696 runs/team/year (matches observed scale ~4.3 runs/game). Reader flags: retrospective in-sample OLS only — no out-of-sample prediction; n=30 teams/season gives wide CIs; MLB-only constants (gamma ≈ 1.82, R_avg scale) do not transfer to NFL (accepted NFL gamma ≈ 2.37).

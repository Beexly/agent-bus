# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0580-group-activity-recognition-in-basketball-tracking.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Hauri & Vucetic (2022, arXiv:2209.00451) introducing NETS — per-object LSTM trajectory embeddings, Transformer self-attention, permutation-invariant team pooling, and a three-stage recipe (self-supervised trajectory pretraining -> weak-label fine-tuning -> manual-label fine-tuning) to recognize pick-and-rolls and handoffs from NBA tracking data. Ledger verdict ADAPT: the architecture pattern ports directly to NFL tracking data for play-concept/formation recognition; the basketball label taxonomy does not transfer.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Transformer layer: z_o^{l+1} = LN(FF(LN(Att(Q,K,V)))) + z_o^l; head_i = softmax((QW_i^Q)(KW_i^K)^T/sqrt(d_k)) V W_i^V. Classification head: y = softmax(FF(z_B^N concat sum_{i=1..5} z_{A_i}^N concat sum_{i=1..5} z_{D_i}^N)). Class-weighted NLL: L = -(1/|D|) sum_D sum_k alpha_k y_k ln(y_hat_k), alpha = (0.77, 2.34, 0.77). Trajectory MSE: L_MSE = (1/2H)||nu - nu_hat||^2_2. Hyperparameters: d_h=256, N=8 layers, h=64 heads, Adam lr 5e-5, batch 512, early stopping (50 epochs), ~400 epochs (~30h on one GPU) pretraining. Input windows L=10 frames (1.2s) at 0.12s after 3x downsampling of 25 Hz data. Weak-label rules: possession if a player is closest to ball >=5 consecutive frames with ball within 5 ft, height <10 ft, speed <25 ft/s; pick-and-roll if triangle distances delta_a, delta_d1 < 6 ft and delta_d2 < 3 ft; handoff if possession changes between two offensive players within 6.5 ft.
## Data sources named
Public NBA SportVU movement data, 632 games of 2015-2016 (github.com/sealneaward/nba-movement-data): 25 Hz, 11 objects (ball + 10 players); 113,760 possessions after filtering (1.1M seconds); 869,905 non-overlapping 1.2s sequences. Weak labels: 45,802 pick-and-rolls, 15,251 handoffs, 808,852 other (specificity vs manual: 82% p&r, 90.5% handoff). Manual labels: 1,800 sequences (600/class), 50/50 train/test.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Trajectory prediction ADE/FDE (feet), best at all horizons: H=10 NETS 1.08/2.34 vs LSTM 1.61/2.98, SocialGAN 1.25/2.75; H=20 NETS 2.78/5.87; H=40 NETS 5.70/10.88. Weak-label 3-class F1 (p&r, handoff, other): NETS (0.856, 0.768, 0.988) vs GBoost (0.398, 0.443, 0.915), LSTM (0.360, 0.490, 0.905). Ablation: removing pretraining -> (0.802, 0.675, 0.982); removing LSTM embed -> (0.803, 0.731, 0.983); removing pooling -> (0.829, 0.718, 0.985). Manual-label test F1: weak-label rules alone (0.869, 0.874, 0.893); NETS trained on weak labels (0.915, 0.863, 0.908); NETS on 900 manual labels only (0.784, 0.813, 0.844) — worse than rules; pretrain->weak->manual sequential fine-tune (0.951, 0.938, 0.902) — best, beating the rules. Without team pooling, t-SNE splits into C(5,2)=10 sub-clusters keyed on player indices. Reader flags: random 80/10/10 split (not time-ordered) inflates scores; the recipe needs a weak-label rule engine first (manual labels alone fail); NFL tracking is 10 Hz x 22 players with play segmentation, not continuous possessions.

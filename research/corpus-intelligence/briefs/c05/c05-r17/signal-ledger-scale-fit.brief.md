# brain/signal-ledger-scale-fit.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A shipped (2026-09-30) diagnostic-and-fix report for the `signals` ledger table (118,462 rows, seasons 2020–2026): it documents the defect (uniform weight=1 across keys on incomparable raw scales), the fix (per-key normalized −1..1 scale + evidence-scaled fitted weights from within-player correlations), and an injury-encoder bug it surfaced.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- The defect: raw SDs across keys span 0.093 (`pgs.target_share`) to 9.626 (`pgs.passing_epa`) — a 103x unit spread a uniform weight treated as interchangeable; a ±35 passing-EPA reading carried ~100x the influence of a ±0.067 target-share reading.
- Fix: each key's raw reading normalized onto −1..1 by measured anchor/spread (`value` normalized, `valueRaw` keeps source verbatim); per-key weight fitted from within-player correlation against settled outcome, evidence-scaled by distinct fixtures.
- Between-vs-within player correlation inflation (2024 `pgs.target_share`: between 0.314 vs within 0.0046 — 68x gap): `pgs.fantasy_ppr` 0.373 vs 0.094 (4.0x); `pgs.target_share` 0.293 vs 0.091 (3.2x); `pgs.receiving_epa` 0.096 vs 0.025 (3.8x); `pgs.passing_epa` 0.084 vs 0.002 (36.1x); `pgs.rushing_epa` 0.041 vs 0.004 (11.3x); `injury.availability` 0.071 vs 0.063 (1.1x).
- Fitted table (125 fixtures for the five PGS keys): `pgs.fantasy_ppr` w=0.105064 (r=0.0940, earned); `pgs.target_share` w=0.101859 (r=0.0911, earned); `pgs.receiving_epa` w=0.028479 (r=0.0255, earned); `pgs.rushing_epa` w=0.004009 (r=0.0036, earned); `pgs.passing_epa` w=0.002593 (r=0.0023, earned). `pgs.target_share` now outranks `pgs.passing_epa` 39:1 (was ~1:100 under uniform weights).
- Outcome: next-week `player_game_stats.fantasyPointsPpr` above population median (base rate 0.4966, median 6.10 — balanced target). Team win/loss not joinable: `player_game_stats.team` joins 0 of 32 strings to `games` home/away team names, and `games` carries no season/week column.
- Evidence counted in distinct fixtures (season × week), never rows: 24,497 `pgs.fantasy_ppr` rows = 125 independent games.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Eight of thirteen keys earn weight 0, each for a measured reason: 4 `ngs.*` keys unjoinable (writer keys by `gsisId`, outcomes by `playerId`; 0 of 380 distinct gsis match); 3 `snap.*` keys never persisted (31,100 `snap_counts` rows all NULL playerId); `injury.availability` within-r 0.063 (strongest of any key) refused on 23 fixtures below the 100-fixture floor. Weight 0 = present, honest, not allowed to move a score — never back-filled with a plausible constant.
- Injury encoder bug: `??` falls through only on null/undefined, not empty string; on prod every one of the 6,812 injuries rows stores `reportStatus` as `''` (0 rows NULL, 3,744 `=''`), so the encoder saw `""` and dropped the row. Consequence: 2,955 "Full Participation in Practice" rows (the healthiest reading) discarded; only 3,068 of 6,812 injuries ever became a signal; every persisted `injury.availability` was −1 or 0 — the `+1` case had never once been written. Fix encodes 6,065 of 6,812 rows and restores `+1`.
- No published projection, gate, floor, or MODEL_VERSION touched; `signals` still not read by any production consumer (only `/api/ops/signal-ledger-state` counts rows).
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- TRUST-SIGNAL: the between-vs-within-player correlation gap (4x–68x inflation) is the honesty mechanism — weighting by between-player correlation would advertise predictive power the data does not contain; weight answers only "on a week this player looks unusually good, do they do unusually well next week?"
- OTHER: fixture-not-row evidence counting is the same law as `tune-signal-weights-grouped.ts`; the gsisId↔playerId crosswalk gap is an explicit measured join failure, not a guess.

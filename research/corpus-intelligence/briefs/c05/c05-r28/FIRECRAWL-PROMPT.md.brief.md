# props/research/2026-09-18/FIRECRAWL-PROMPT.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A master reconnaissance prompt for Firecrawl's autonomous agent to reverse-engineer the data pipelines behind nine NFL analytics creators (FTN, PFF, SumerSports, StatRankings, Fantasy Points, RotoBaller/ThunderDan, Ben Baldwin, DraftKings, nflverse) plus The Odds API, extracting endpoints, schemas, and formulas from public surfaces only. It includes non-negotiable rules (public surfaces only, provenance, CONFIRMED/INFERRED/UNVERIFIED verdicts, no fabricated URLs/formulas) and an output contract per target plus a master prop-input coverage matrix.

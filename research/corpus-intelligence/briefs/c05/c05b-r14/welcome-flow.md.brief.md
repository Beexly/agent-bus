## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 5-email founder-voice nurture sequence for new free-account signups during the silent-collection phase (before `PUBLIC_PICKS_ENABLED=true`), spaced over 14 days in first-person Garrett voice, ending with the live-board alert.

A/B tests: Email 1 subject (curiosity vs explanatory), "From: Garrett Baxley" vs "From: Garrett @ Galaxy Sports Edge", Email 5 with vs without explicit pricing in body, Email 3 on Day 5 vs Day 7, optional 6th email Day 21 with calibration-report preview. Sequence structure: Email 1 welcome (immediate), Email 2 methodology deep-dive (Day 2), Email 3 "why my Performance page is empty" (Day 5), Email 4 Eclipse Gate + Edge Index explainer (Day 9), Email 5 board-open alert (triggered on `PUBLIC_PICKS_ENABLED` flip). Pricing stated in Email 5: Pro $19/mo, Elite $49/mo, 7-day refund window on paid plans. Free plan gets one signal/day (highest Edge Index, confidence/factor trail gated).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Public win-rate stays gated until a statistically defensible count of settled signals exists ("could be 100 settled signals, could be 500"); Calibration Report fire expected 30–60 days after board-open.
- The four-check signal pipeline described to users: (01) read the board (spread, total, moneyline, bookmaker count, freshness timestamp), (02) measure pressure (movement, consensus, volatility, matchup context), (03) gate the signal (only explainable edges ship; weak inputs/stale prices/thin markets stay off), (04) learn slowly (settled outcomes inform calibration; every weight change reviewed).
- Every published card carries two scores: **Edge Index** (0–100 composite of signal strength, volatility, EV context — explicitly NOT a win probability) and **Eclipse Gate** (verified conviction state: all gates cleared by a significant margin, high factor agreement, solid market depth — still loses sometimes). "A 64% calibrated confidence still loses 36 out of 100 times."
- Coverage: all 7 sports (NFL, NCAAF, NBA, NCAAB, MLB, NHL, MLS), 30-minute refresh loop.
- Exit conditions: upgrade → skip remaining nurture + send Pro/Elite welcome; 30-day inactive no-opens → drop to re-engagement queue at win-rate-publish time; early `PUBLIC_PICKS_ENABLED` flip → Email 5 immediately, methodology emails backfilled 1/week; hard unsubscribe exits all (CAN-SPAM).

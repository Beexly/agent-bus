## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A self-contained research-agent prompt written 2026-09-18 for an outside ML researcher to redesign the prediction core of the betting/fantasy engine: it documents the engine's true measured state and demands evidence-graded answers across 15 ML research areas with falsifiable kill criteria, an evidence standard (cited primary sources or UNVERIFIED), and a ranked build list.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Engine's measured state (the numbers that matter):
- Prediction core is a hand-weighted additive score (consensus, book depth, edge, line movement, rest, form, venue, summed with fixed caps) — **measured anti-predictive at the top of its range: picks scored 80+ claim ~87% and realize ~52%**.
- Beneath it sit market-blind independent estimators: Elo, Poisson, Dixon-Coles, Skellam, Bradley-Terry standings model, opponent-adjusted EPA — on football they frequently collapse to a single source.
- The only component measured well calibrated is the **de-vigged market consensus probability**.
- ~2,600 settled picks total; **~70 ever settled for the NFL**, spread across sports/markets/seasons.
- Closing-line value: ~23% beat-close vs 52.4% target.
- Data free: nflverse PBP 1999–present (EPA, WP, CPOE, air yards); NGS weekly aggregates; injuries, snap counts, depth charts, rosters, officials, contracts; multi-book odds with append-only line-snapshot archive; weather; MLB Statcast; public exchange prices. Paid charting/grade data not licensed.
- Stated ML regime: (1) near-coin-flip single games, edges 1–3 pts; (2) closing line is a strong public-info aggregator — must beat it, not just predict; (3) small uneven samples, thinnest where most wanted; (4) non-stationarity (rules, rosters, coaching, market efficiency drift); (5) leakage hazards (play rows dependent within drive/game; retrospective features contaminated; in-play prices encode partial outcomes); (6) heterogeneous/irregular/missing inputs; (7) calibration + honest abstention > raw accuracy.
- Research areas demanded: tabular learners (boosting vs regularized linear at small n; monotonic constraints; quantile objectives; early stopping under dependent folds); hierarchical partial pooling / multi-task (70-row stratum borrows from 2,000-row one; empirical Bayes shrinkage); representation learning on PBP (self-supervised: next-play prediction, masked play modeling, contrastive drive embeddings); sequence/state-space team strength (Kalman, particle filters, dynamic Elo, GPs over time, temporal fusion transformers; process noise fit not assumed); learning to rank/selection (LambdaMART, top-k under budget, learning to abstain, selective prediction coverage-risk curve); inherently interpretable models (EBM/GA2M, neural additive models, monotone constrained ensembles; SHAP critique); uncertainty/calibration (conformal, Venn-Abers, conformalized quantile regression; exchangeability violations under drift; when the correct answer is refusal); market-relative learning (close as baseline, residual-only learning, beat-the-close as training target, steam/order-flow features); online learning/non-stationarity (FTRL, exponentially weighted forecaster aggregation, OGD on proper scoring rules, contextual bandits, change-point detection, drift-aware retraining); causal inference (double ML, uplift, synthetic controls, causal forests — injury impact, coaching change, rest); ensembling/distillation (stacking, BMA, mixture-of-experts router over sport/market — fit without overfitting router at small n); automated discovery (automated feature engineering, symbolic regression, NAS — noted: earlier internal symbolic-regression effort produced NO surviving finding); multimodal fusion (structured + text news/injury language + weather grids + market ticks; has text ever added info beyond price in a liquid market?); frontier-model transfers (retrieval augmentation, test-time compute/self-consistency, MoE, curriculum, distillation, reward modeling on realized outcomes, critic/verifier veto layer, scaling-law analysis, continual learning); the continuous learning loop (feature stores, point-in-time correctness, purged/embargoed CV backtests, champion/challenger promotion, shadow deployment, experiment tracking + pre-registration, multiple-hypothesis control, data versioning, drift/calibration-decay monitoring, active learning).
- Framing correction: "Large models do not improve because of the transformer. They improve because a loop runs continuously: data lands, the model trains, an eval scores it, the winner deploys, the deployment produces more data. Every component of that loop already exists in this repository and none of it is wired to run."
- Evidence standard: cite primary sources (title, authors, venue, year, link/DOI actually seen); unverifiable citations marked UNVERIFIED; separate demonstrated-in-sports-out-of-sample vs other-domains-only vs plausible-but-untested; effect sizes with sample sizes and time-ordered splits; negative results reported; ask whether claimed edges survive the vig.
- Admission rule: any recommended method enters through the same door — pre-registered hypothesis with kill line written alongside the prediction, shared test set, fixture-grouped time-ordered folds, result recorded live-or-die.

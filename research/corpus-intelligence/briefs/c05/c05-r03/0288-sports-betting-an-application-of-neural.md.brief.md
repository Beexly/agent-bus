# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0288-sports-betting-an-application-of-neural.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Vélez Jiménez, Lecuanda Ontiveros & Possani (ITAM/CETYS, 2023) compare Sharpe-ratio (quadratic utility) vs Kelly (log utility) portfolio staking on EPL 1x2 betting over the second half of the 2020/21 season, using a funnel deep neural net for probabilities. Verdict in file: ADAPT — the usable assets are the matrix-form multivariate simultaneous Kelly formulation (concave, SQP-solvable) and the fractional-Kelly calibration method (17% via Dirichlet simulations); the EPL net (54% accuracy) is not worth porting and the 135.8% profit is non-significant.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Return: R(ℓ̄) = 1 + ℓ̄′ϱ̄; ϱ̄ = D_o·m − 1. Sharpe: S(ℓ̄) = (ℓ̄′μ̄ − R_f)/√(ℓ̄′Σℓ̄), convexified via ȳ = κℓ̄ (Eq. 7). Kelly: max log-growth = D_KL(p‖p̃) (Eq. 10); bivariate ℓ* = (op−1)/(o−1). Multivariate simultaneous Kelly (claimed novel, Eq. 13): max p̄′log(1 + W′ℓ̄ − Σℓ_i·1) s.t. Σℓ_i ≤ 1, ℓ̄ ≥ 0 — concave, SQP-solvable. Fraction calibration: 200 Dirichlet(1) simulations on validation → 17% fractional. Complete (all 30 bets) vs restricted (one max-EV bet per match). Gains reinvested weekly; wagers < 0.0001 dropped. Assumptions: no short selling/borrowing, infinitely divisible money, odds/probabilities fixed, match independence, true probabilities known.
## Data sources named
2,660 EPL games 2014/15–2020/21; train 2014/15–2019/20 (~2,200 obs), validation first half 20/21, test second half 20/21 (20 matchweeks × 10 matches; 30 bets, 59,049 outcome combos per matchweek). Features: SoFIFA team ratings (ova/att/mid/def, 1-matchday delay), Understat xG-family (npxGD moving avg/variance, Dixon–Coles exponential decay ξ=0.1), table position, points, big-six/promoted dummies, transfer budget, Pinnacle final odds as market features. Bets at max odds across 6 books (Bet365, Bwin, Interwetten, Pinnacle, Victor Chandler, William Hill). No code released.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Neural net: 54% accuracy vs crowd (Pinnacle-implied) 51.5%, nulls 38/20/42%; cross-entropy 1.0318 vs crowd 0.9966 (model WORSE than market on information loss — accuracy edge without calibration edge). Portfolios: best = restricted + 17% fractional Sharpe → 135% final wealth (+35.8% profit); restricted fractional Kelly 111%; complete fractional Kelly 102%; complete fractional Sharpe 97%. Full-stake: 24%, 16%, 3%, 0% — Sharpe complete went to ruin two weeks before end (final wealth 0%, log-growth −∞). Betting/wealth p-values 0.16–0.50 — NONE significant; profit indistinguishable from luck. Model beats 78/100 Dirichlet random sims. Arbitrage found 3 times (e.g., Everton–Aston Villa 2021-06-05, tt = −0.0085); Sharpe converges to arbitrage vector with infinite Sharpe. Kelly max bet avg 29.7% of portfolio stake (vs Sharpe 25.9%); SQP 20.9 s/fixture (Kelly), 5.3 s (Sharpe); matchweek 23 Kelly SQP failed (gradient overflow, last-iteration patch). Per file: Pinnacle final odds used as INPUT features — the accuracy-vs-crowd comparison is contaminated; multivariate Kelly scales as N = Πm_k outcomes (intractable for full NFL prop portfolios without factorization).

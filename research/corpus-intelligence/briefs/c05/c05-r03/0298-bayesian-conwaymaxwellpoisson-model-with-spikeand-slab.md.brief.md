# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0298-bayesian-conwaymaxwellpoisson-model-with-spikeand-slab.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Zhang, Rastelli & Friel (UCD/Insight, 2026) generalize the Maher Poisson goal model with a Conway-Maxwell-Poisson likelihood whose team-specific dispersion parameters carry spike-and-slab priors (spike at ν=1 = Poisson regime; slab LogNormal around 1 = non-equidispersed regime), inferred via Metropolis-within-Gibbs with the Exchange algorithm. Verdict in file: ADOPT — this is the count-data model GSE should be running for NFL scoring distributions, most directly for totals (over/under) modeling where dispersion is the entire game.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
CMP pmf (Eq. 1): f(y|μ,ν) = (μ^y/y!)^ν / Z_f(μ,ν); ν=1 recovers Poisson; E(Y) ≈ μ + 1/(2ν) − 1/2, Var(Y) ≈ μ/ν. Likelihood (Eq. 3): y^H|μ^H,ν_i ~ COM-Poisson(μ^H,ν_i) — dispersion follows the scoring team. Maher centring (Eq. 4): log(μ^H_{i,j}) = α_i + β_j + γ; log(μ^A_{j,i}) = α_j + β_i; Σ-constraints (Eq. 6). SAS prior (Eq. 5): Z_i|p_i ~ Bern(p_i), p_i ~ Beta(1,1), η_i ~ N(0,1), ν_i := exp(Z_i η_i). Inference: Metropolis-within-Gibbs (binary-flip MH + Exchange for Z_i; correlated bivariate (α_i,η_i) proposal ρ=0.85; 250,000 iterations, 50,000 burn-in). Fit: WAIC (Eq. 17); prediction: Ignorance score IGN = −log2(f̃(y*)) (Eq. 18) on Outcome / O-U 2.5 / Goal Difference; RPS checked. Code: github.com/nzhang98/compoisson_goal (verify live).
## Data sources named
Simulations: synthetic 20-team leagues, 10 equidispersed (ν_true=1) + 10 non-equidispersed (0.2≤ν_true≤4), 5 replicates each. Real: EPL seasons 2020/21–2024/25 from football-data.co.uk (2023/24: 380 matches, 1,246 goals, 3.28/match; Man City champions, 91 pts). Out-of-sample: estimate through game-day t from mid-season (190 games), predict next 10, roll forward — 19 training sessions/season.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Simulation TP at 0.5 threshold: 1.00 at ν=0.2, 0.94 at 0.3, collapses to 0.58 at 0.5 and 0.14 at 0.8; FP ≈ 0.04–0.16 stable. WAIC: at ν=0.3, Poisson 2836.5 vs CMP-SAS 2640.5; near equidispersion (ν=0.9) Poisson best but CMP-SAS close (2130.5 vs 2131.3) — SAS robustness. EPL 2023/24 (threshold 0.5): 3 over-dispersed (Fulham P(Z)=0.94, ν̂=0.323; Brighton 0.58, ν̂=0.729; Wolves 0.51, ν̂=0.766) + 1 under-dispersed (Nott'm Forest 0.53, ν̂=1.395). Fulham's attack shifts from −0.090 (Poisson) to −1.831 (CMP-SAS) — extreme overdispersion (boom/bust) masked by Poisson. In-sample WAIC 2023/24: CMP-SAS 2370.0 < CMP-Full 2386.1 < Poisson 2400.3. Out-of-sample IGN 2023/24: Outcome 1.326 vs 1.327; O/U 2.5 0.929 vs 0.974 (largest gain); GD 2.937 vs 2.940; CMP-Full worse than Poisson on Outcome/GD. Man Utd–Fulham 1–2: CMP-SAS exact-score P 4.68% vs Poisson 4.22%; away-win prob 0.270 vs 0.196 (+8pp). Five seasons: CMP-SAS WAIC best every season (e.g., 2024/25: 2293.1 vs 2312.8); CMP-SAS IGN ≤ Poisson every season × market; ~3 dispersed teams/season (mostly overdispersed); Liverpool dispersed 3/5 seasons (direction changes); Nott'm Forest underdispersed 2 straight seasons. Limitations in file: detection power collapses near equidispersion; NFL's 17-game season is thinner than 38-game EPL; no recency weighting; conditional independence under-predicts draws; 250k-iteration MCMC too heavy for weekly production (needs VI/Laplace).

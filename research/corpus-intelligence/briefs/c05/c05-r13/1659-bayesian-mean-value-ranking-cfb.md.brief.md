# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1659-bayesian-mean-value-ranking-cfb.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: ADAPT. Full 33-page read of Ashburn & Colvert (2006), physics/0607064: a Bayesian mean-value (posterior-mean, not MAP) college football rating model whose Normal prior is determined self-consistently from the data's own schedule structure (MSV/MTV) rather than hand-tuned — the portable prior-calibration module for GSE's Bayesian rating stack.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Win probability: P(i beats j) = Φ(r_i − r_j, σ_v), σ_v = 1 (½+½ per-team performance variance). Ratings are posterior means via moment integrals: m_i(n) = ∫ r^n Φ_p(r,σ_p) Π_j Φ_ij(r) dr; r̃_i = m_i(1)/m_i(0); σ_i² = m_i(2)/m_i(0) − (m_i(1)/m_i(0))². Prior: Normal(r_ik̄, σ_p) centered on mean scheduled opponent; σ_p² = σ² − σ'²/N_i (eq. 36), from mean schedule variance MSV (σ'²) and mean team variance MTV (σ²), iterated to convergence. Outcome correlation diagnostic: ρ_i = [½W_i(W_i−1) + ½L_i(L_i−1) − W_iL_i] / [½(W_i+L_i)(W_i+L_i−1)] (eq. 19). Home field h via Newton iteration on the win-count equation (eq. 42); mean h = 0.244, SD 0.016 (2001–2005). Published metric: r̃̃_i = mean over j≠i of P(r̃_i − r̃_j, σ_i²+σ_j²+σ_v²), a scale-free "winning percentage" in [0,1].
## Data sources named
All Division I-A, I-AA, II, III, NAIA college football games, seasons 2001–2005 (~700 teams, ~3,600 games/season in 2005; data through 11/19/05). Per game: teams, winner/loser, home/away/neutral site only — no margins (BCS restriction). Benchmark: BCS computer top-10 rankings 2001–2005. No code or data published (atomicfootball.com defunct); method fully specified mathematically.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Outcome correlation, model-implied vs actual per season (2001–2005): 0.161 vs 0.164, 0.158 vs 0.158, 0.164 vs 0.164, 0.153 vs 0.151, 0.151 vs 0.157 — differences −0.003, 0.000, 0.000, +0.002, −0.006 ("quite accurate and relatively unbiased"); actual ρ precision σ_ρ ≈ 0.010–0.011. √MSV 0.93–1.02, √MTV 0.76–0.85. Model-implied upset frequency U: 21.2%, 21.3%, 20.7%, 21.6%, 22.2% — inside sanity bounds (16–19% ranking violations / 28% from Fair & Oster 2002). Triplet splits (2007 addendum): estimated 9.24/9.26/8.78/9.51/9.84% vs actual 9.09/8.93/8.94/9.54/9.44%, within ~0.39% one-sigma precision. Sum-of-squared ranking differences vs BCS computer average: 38.4 (model, no HFA) vs median 66.1 of six BCS computers — best by a comfortable margin; with HFA 62.9 vs 62.6 (second by a hair).

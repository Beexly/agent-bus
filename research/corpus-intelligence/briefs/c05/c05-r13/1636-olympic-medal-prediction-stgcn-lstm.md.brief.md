## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: REJECT. A deep-read of arXiv:2501.17711 (STGCN-LSTM for 2028 Olympic medal prediction) found it to be a buzzword-stacked, non-reproducible modeling collage with factual hallucinations, wrong math, and internally inconsistent numbers — nothing in it can be adapted with engineering integrity.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Claimed stack: STGCN (GATConv) → bidirectional LSTM with attention/highway fusion → DeepEnsemble (HistGradientBoosting, XGBRegressor, TemporalTransformer); Zero-Inflated Compound Poisson (EM + elastic net) for zero-inflated counts; BOCPD change points; DDD triple-difference for coach effects; DML/IPW/matching for hosting effects; Markowitz mean-variance for resource allocation. Moderation model given: Treatment Effect = 8.2 + 0.15×GDP + 1.2×Coach_Level + 0.8×Hist_Medals (units never defined). Two equations mathematically wrong as stated: λ=0.05 claimed as "20-year half-life" (true ≈13.9 years; e^(−0.05×20)=0.37≠0.5); η=0.33 claimed "half-life ∼3 Olympic cycles" (true ≈2.1 cycles). Equations (13) and (14) are the identical formula duplicated under two identical subsection titles.
## Data sources named
Claimed: "1,413 observations from 1896 to 2024" across 189 sovereign nations + 47 historical regimes; COMAP historical medal data; World Population Review GDP. No dataset released, no schema, no repository, no code. The coach-mobility variable driving the central "causal" claims has no described data source.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Predicted USA 43 gold [39,47], China 38 [35,41] for 2028 LA; "~30 countries will win medals for the first time" with probabilities to 3 decimals (e.g., ANG 0.762, BOT 0.726); hosting total effect 24.5±2.7 medals, but §6.2 claims a "2.3-medal average host country effect" — internally inconsistent; DML/IPW/matching host estimates 14.8/15.1/15.5 — suspiciously tight agreement. Tables 2 and 3 are both mislabeled "2028 Paris Olympics" (Paris was 2024; 2028 is LA), and Table 2 shows fractional gold declines (−1.60) for countries winning essentially zero medals. Flagship causal example is a hallucination: Lang Ping (a volleyball coach) is claimed to have coached Chinese table tennis 2005–2016. Backtracking SMAPE: Cold War 21.4%, globalization 17.8%, COVID 23.1%.

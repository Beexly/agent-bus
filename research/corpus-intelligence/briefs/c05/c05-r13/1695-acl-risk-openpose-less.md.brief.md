# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1695-acl-risk-openpose-less.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: ADAPT. Full read of Gong, Zhao & Yang (2023), arXiv:2305.14612: the first automated 2D-video implementation of the clinical Landing Error Scoring System (LESS) for ACL injury risk — OpenPose keypoints → 5 biomechanical features → 9/5/1 threshold scoring → AHP-weighted composite, with public code and Jetson Nano edge deployment.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Pipeline: 2D video → OpenPose keypoints per frame → 5 features: (1) knee flexion angle (sagittal), (2) trunk/hip flexion angle (sagittal), (3) lateral trunk angle (frontal), (4) ankle–knee horizontal distance, (5) ankle–shoulder horizontal distance; joint angles from keypoint vectors via cosine rule on projections. Each feature scored 9 (excellent) / 5 (good) / 1 (poor) via threshold intervals; composite via Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) pairwise-comparison weight matrix. Takeoff check: knee varus/valgus from |knee–ankle distance|: < 30° = stable; 30–50° = varus error; > 50° = valgus error. OpenPose confidence threshold > 0.4 selected (PCKh@0.5 = 91.3% at 18.5% keypoint loss; 88.9% at 0.2/7.1% loss; 90.9% at 0.6/36.7% loss; 93.5% at 0.8/76.2% loss). Assumptions: LESS valid for ACL risk (Padua 2009); 2D projections suffice; OpenPose accurate enough at the chosen threshold.
## Data sources named
Authors' own lab collection: 30 subjects — 10 basketball players (#1–10), 10 active (#11–20), 10 less active (#21–30); platform landing / drop vertical jump test, sagittal + frontal video. Subject videos not released. Code: github.com/ZiyuGong-proj/Assessment-of-ACL-Injury-Risk-Based-on-Openpose.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Group separation: basketball > active (p = 0.0193), active > less active (p = 0.0486), basketball vs less active p = 3.59×10⁻⁸. Example kinematics: max knee flexion ≈ 65° (excellent); max hip flexion ≈ 70° (excellent); frontal thigh–trunk cosine ≈ −1 (parallel); peak frontal flexion cosine ≈ −0.75 (30–60°, good). No prospective injury outcomes — validation is group discrimination only, partly circular. Limitations: n=30, single lab setting, controlled landing test (far from broadcast video: occlusion, angles, multiple athletes); AHP weights are expert-subjective, not data-driven; LESS is a screening tool, not a validated individual-level injury predictor.

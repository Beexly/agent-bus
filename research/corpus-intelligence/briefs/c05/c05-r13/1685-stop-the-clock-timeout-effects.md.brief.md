# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1685-stop-the-clock-timeout-effects.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: ADAPT. Full read of Assis, Assunção & Vaz-de-Melo (2020), arXiv:2009.06750: a Pearl-DAG + back-door-criterion + same-game matching causal study finding NBA timeouts have no causal effect on short-term performance — the apparent effect is regression to the mean; the DAG/matching design is the portable template for debiasing GSE's in-game momentum features.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Treatment A_t (timeout at game instant t); outcome Y_t^λ = (P_{t+λ} − P_t)/λ − (P_{t−1} − P_{t−λ−1})/λ = ΔP_t^{t+λ} − ΔP_{t−λ}^t (post-trend minus pre-trend of scoring-margin rate; λ ∈ {2,4,6} possessions). Effect TE = E[Y_t^λ | A_t=1] − E[Y_t^λ | A_t=0], estimated as TE_h (home) and TE_a (away). Confounders: quarter Q_t, scoring margin P_t, seconds since period start S_t, pre-trend ΔP_{t−λ}^t; unobserved inter-game confounders U absorbed by same-game matching. Three matching estimators: exact on pre-trend within same game; Mahalanobis on X_t; propensity score via GBM with Euclidean distance; optimal matching via rcbalance; non-overlapping windows. Inference: 10,000-permutation Monte Carlo on difference-in-means, 99% CIs. Assumptions: conditional ignorability given (U, X_t, ΔP_{t−λ}^t); SUTVA via non-overlapping windows; DAG completeness (asserted, not tested).
## Data sources named
Play-by-play tables crawled from Basketball-Reference; NBA 2014-15 through 2017-18, main analysis 2016-17 (30 teams, 1,309 games incl. playoffs; 281,373 game instants; 17,765 timeouts — 7,754 home-called, 8,011 away-called, 2,000 mandatory/official). Code/data: github.com/pkdd-paper/paper667 (R).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Naïve before/after mean STMC: +0.629 (λ=2, n=14,031), +0.421 (λ=4, n=12,225), +0.302 (λ=6, n=10,296) — Wilcoxon/bootstrap p "numerically equal to zero" (the conventional wrong evidence). Causal estimates (99% CIs): all |TE| ≤ 0.059 points/possession; largest magnitude = propensity matching λ=4 away: TE_a = −0.059, CI (−0.098, −0.020); home λ=4: −0.032 (−0.072, 0.008). Of 18 tests, only 6 significant at α=0.001 (no multiplicity correction), every significant effect negligible vs the 1-point practical relevance threshold. Last-5-minutes stratification: "very similar" — no effect in clutch time either. Complements ledger 1684 (Gibbs et al., ATT −0.35 on a different outcome): convergent evidence timeouts don't help.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- COACHING: direct coaching-behavior finding — timeouts (a coach decision) have no causal short-term performance effect; also exposes that timeout *timing* within a game is strategic and remains an unobserved confounder.
- OTHER: reusable DAG + back-door + same-game exact-matching recipe for GSE's NFL causal questions — most directly "icing-the-kicker" timeouts on FG make probability (nflverse testable); improvement experiment: difference-in-differences on the trend itself, or a learned influence horizon via distributed-lag instead of asserted λ ∈ {2,4,6}.
- QB-BEHAVIOR: INFERENCE — momentum/noise confounding structure applies to any in-game QB streak feature; the pre-trend adjustment is the transferable idea.

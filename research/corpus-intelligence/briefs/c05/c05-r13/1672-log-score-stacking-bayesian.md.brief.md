# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1672-log-score-stacking-bayesian.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: ADAPT. Full read of Yao, Vehtari, Simpson & Gelman (2018), arXiv:1704.02030: the canonical theory for stacking Bayesian predictive distributions via log-score maximization with PSIS-LOO densities — the theoretical foundation of Stan's loo::stacking_weights(); directly replaces BMA for GSE's heterogeneous (ℳ-open) Bayesian ensemble.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Log-score stacking weights: ŵ = argmax_w (1/n)Σᵢ log Σₖ wₖ p(yᵢ|y_{−i}, Mₖ), s.t. wₖ ≥ 0, Σₖ wₖ = 1 (general form (2.2): argmax over any proper scoring rule S). Combined predictive: p̂(ỹ|y) = Σₖ ŵₖ p(ỹ|y, Mₖ) (2.3). PSIS-LOO: importance ratios r_{i,k}^s = 1/p(yᵢ|θ^s,Mₖ), generalized-Pareto fit on largest 20%, smooth/truncate, exact-LOO substitution for Pareto k̂ > 0.7. Pseudo-BMA: wₖ = exp(elpd̂_loo^k)/Σ exp(elpd̂_loo^{k'}); Pseudo-BMA+ (2.6): Bayesian-bootstrap (Dirichlet(1,…,1)) averaging over elpd vectors, wₖ = (1/B)Σ_b w_{k,b} — regularizes weights away from 0/1. Diagnostic gate: distrust weighting when n < 5 × effective parameters.
## Data sources named
Simulation studies + two real datasets: US Senate voting 1988–92 (n=94); Bangladesh well-switching (n=3020, 8 logistic/B-spline models). Code exists: R/Stan loo::stacking_weights() / model_weights(log_lik_list, method="stacking").
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Gaussian-mixture simulation (ℳ-open, truth N(3.4,1), 8 candidates N(1..8,1)): log-score stacking beats BMA on expected log predictive density except at extremely small n; stacking of distributions attains the same optimal MSE as stacking of means. Key robustness: duplicating a candidate model leaves stacking weights unchanged in theory while BMA/Pseudo-BMA concentrate on the duplicate and get worse. Linear subset regression (J=15, SNR 4:1): stacking beats all six competitors (stacking of means, Pseudo-BMA, Pseudo-BMA+, best-LOO, best-marginal-likelihood, BMA) across n=5→200. vs mixture models: stacking and Pseudo-BMA+ beat a full mixture model on log predictive density at every (ρ,n) tested and are ~30× faster. Well-switching: both methods put bulk weight on Model 5 (5th-degree splines) and ~0 on the most complex Model 8; stacking's ~0.09 weight on simple logistic Model 1 visibly smooths boundary fluctuation. Example printed weights: stacking [0.25, 0.06, 0.09, 0.25, 0.35, 0.00] vs Pseudo-BMA+ [0.28, 0.05, 0.08, 0.30, 0.28, 0.00]. Caveat: LOO degrades when n < 5× effective parameters; log-score stacking is outlier-sensitive (CRPS alternative supported).

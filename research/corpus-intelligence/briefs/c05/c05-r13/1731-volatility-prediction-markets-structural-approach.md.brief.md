# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1731-volatility-prediction-markets-structural-approach.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ledger verdict: ADAPT. Full read of Xi, Moallemi, Pai & Wang (2026), arXiv:2607.08199: forecasts the conditional volatility (next price-move scale) of binary prediction-market contracts from structural state variables — deadline-resolution (Wright–Fisher) + adverse-selection/order-flow (Glosten–Milgrom) — in a closed form that beats plain GARCH(1,1) by 34% on ~880,000 out-of-sample hourly forecasts.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Structural variance (closed form): h_t² = p_t(1−p_t)/τ_t + K·ν(V_t)·s_t²/4, with ν(V) = √V (headline), K ≥ 0 fit by MLE. First term = Wright–Fisher deadline-resolution (Bernoulli boundary shape: variance max at p=0.5, forced to zero at resolution); second = Glosten–Milgrom order-flow: spread s_t scaled by activity proxy √V. Benchmarks: plain GARCH(1,1); deadline-resolution-only (DR); DR-AS(√V); probit-Brownian variance shape (Archak & Ipeirotis 2010); GARCH+DR-AS hybrid. Evaluation: 95% prediction intervals for next-hour price, volume-weighted Winkler interval score (VW-IS; penalizes width plus a multiple of miss distance; lower better), paired bootstrap over contract-level clusters. 56-month expanding walk-forward (fit all prior active-update observations, test next month).
## Data sources named
Hourly Kalshi panel, August 2021 – April 2026: binary contracts (Sports, Politics, Economics, Elections, Crypto, Entertainment); per contract-hour: mid implied probability p_t, bid-ask spread s_t, traded volume V_t, time to resolution τ_t; target = size of next-hour probability move conditional on an active update. ~880,000 OOS forecasts; 1,624 contract-hours unlabeled. Kalshi data via public API; no public code or data artifact.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Plain GARCH(1,1): VW-IS 0.7675. Deadline resolution alone: 0.5829. DR-AS(√V): 0.5085 — 34% better than GARCH, 13% better than deadline alone. GARCH+DR-AS: 0.4620 — best overall, 9% better than DR-AS alone. Category portability: global fit best or near-best in most major categories; category-specific refitting does NOT systematically improve OOS. Sports contracts are more event-concentrated/jump-like than Economics (Economics: deadline alone nearly matches the winner; only 4.9% of large-move hours vs 8.0% observation share). Zero-update robustness checks (Appendix D) preserve the structural ranking. Limitations: headline conditions on an active update occurring; sportsbook odds lack a true bid-ask spread (books shade lines, limit bettors) — spread must be re-mapped to cross-book dispersion; no causal identification (manipulation vs information not separated); √V is a modeling choice.
## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER: fills a real gap in GSE's map — no forward-looking volatility model for market-implied probabilities (GSE's uncertainty is on its own outputs via CQR, not on the market's likely movement); extension of, not duplicate of, the market microstructure lane (CLV, de-vigged consensus, beat-the-close, steam).
## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — rebuild h_t on GSE odds data: p_t = de-vigged consensus probability (The Odds API), τ_t = hours to kickoff, s_t = cross-book spread as the adverse-selection proxy, V_t = books-moving/line-move count; fit K by MLE on 2024–2025 NFL for (a) CLV confidence (will the edge persist to kickoff?) and (b) volatility-scaled sizing/abstention. Gate: on 2025 holdout, DR-AS beats GARCH(1,1) by ≥15% VW-IS with paired-bootstrap p < 0.05 (est. ~1 week).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NFL × Bleacher Report/House of Highlights deal: announced Jan 16, 2025 (pre-Super Bowl LIX), multi-year (SBJ: three-year); covers game footage for B/R original content, select-game access-driven content, tentpole event capture (Super Bowl, Draft, Combine); TNT Sports is PAYING the NFL — not a program anyone can apply to.
- NFL × YouTube "Access Pass" (launched Sept 2023): pre-approved NFL archive footage, creators CAN monetize; year one: 24 creators, 200M+ views (153M+ impressions per Fast Company/Whalar); Jan 2025 "Access Pass for Legends" (Marshall, Newton, O'Sullivan, Benkert); NO public application — invite-only via YouTube.
- "NFL Creator of the Week": 12 creators in 2025; 2026–27 season renewed with mega-creators only (Deestroying, Zach King, Dhar Mann 27.6M subs, Jack Mancuso 2.3M, Morgan Riddle); last year's program drove 86M+ views. Invite-only.
- "NFL Creator Network" as a named public program: DOES NOT EXIST (no nfl.com page, no application).
- Chiefs "Kingdom Creators": launched July 2026 (announced July 15), inaugural roster capped at 30 creators, three tiers (Starters / Main Roster / Practice Squad = emerging-creator incubator with no follower minimum published); application on chiefs.formstack.com; inaugurial Creator Camp culminated at the Chiefs' first 2026 preseason game vs. Rams (Aug 2026) — whether the form still accepts applications is UNVERIFIED.
- Rams and Lions run creator programs (Digiday, Nov 2025, one trade-press source); no program names, follower minimums, or application URLs found.
- X Amplify: monetization/pre-roll program, NOT a footage path, exclusive (verified brand + Partner Manager + vetting).
- Zero-cost clean lanes: YouTube embeds expressly permitted by YouTube ToS (NFL has 11 official channels per 2026 YouTube culture report); quote-posting league/team posts on X = zero DMCA surface; NFL × Adobe Express fan templates (Jan 2025) are an official co-creation lane; Greenfly = org-gated B2B, no public creator signup; no UFL creator program found.

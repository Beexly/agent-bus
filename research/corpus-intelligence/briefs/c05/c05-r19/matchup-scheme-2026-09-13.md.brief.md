# dfs/research/2026-09-13/deep/matchup-scheme-2026-09-13.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep scheme/matchup research for the FanDuel Week 1 Sun-Mon slate (Sept 13, 2026): three focus games (Packers@Vikings, Commanders@Eagles, Saints@Lions) analyzed by scheme, coaching, pace, and individual matchups, plus a Week-1 "teams revert to identity" thesis test and stack verdicts.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No formulas. Metrics used: EPA/play and EPA/dropback, success rate, neutral pass rate, neutral pace (seconds/play), play-action and under-center snap rates, blitz rate, pressure rate, PFF grades, YPRR, target share, YPT, sack rate, RZ TD rate, QBR vs blitz.
## Data sources named
FTW (For The Win), SB Nation/JP Acosta, SI, PFF, NBC Sports, FantasyPros implied totals, betting line moves (opened vs steamed lines), camp beat reports (NBC Sports Aug 20, Birkett), Brian Callahan quotes, Saints Wire, USA Today.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Week 1 2025: 12 of 16 games went UNDER; 2024 Week 1: only 2 QBs over 300 yds (vs 6 prior year). Thesis verdict: run identity holds in Week 1, passing efficiency is systematically depressed — run-team stacks > pass-team stacks in Week 1.
- Lions stack ranked strongest: Goff 110.8 dome rating / 110.3 home, DET implied 28.25 on a 49.5 total, Gibbs bellcow (Montgomery traded to HOU), NO PUP safeties (Branch, Joseph) out >= Oct 11, NO LBs "struggle in space" after Demario Davis left in FA.
- Goff's biggest risk: PFF 92.1 kept clean vs 43.8 under pressure; Saints 5th in sack rate (Young career-high 10 sacks) vs DET's rebuilt right side (Sewell flipped to LT, rookie RT Blake Miller, new C Scruggs, LG unsettled).
- Eagles: WAS 2025 defense was 32nd yards (384.3/gm), 30th vs run (141.8/gm), 31st RZ TD allowed (67.7%), 10 takeaways (31st). Trey Amos OUT. Goedert 60/591/11 TDs (franchise TE record) in 2025. Hurts 63 career rush TDs, needs 7 to pass Van Buren's franchise record (69). Total steamed DOWN 46.5 -> 44.5 (79% bets/90% cash on under).
- Vikings: O'Connell offenses 46.9% under-center snaps (3rd) / 29.7% play-action (4th) vs Kyler Murray career 14.7% under-center (3rd-lowest) / 21.5% PA (27th); but 68% comp, 8.5 Y/A on 203 career under-center/PA dropbacks. Gannon (Murray's former HC 2023-25) gave GB players "extra notes" on Murray. GB edge rush: sack rate 7.4% -> 4.2% without Parsons; Nixon PFF 76th/101 CBs (105.2 rating allowed) vs Jefferson.
- Juwan Johnson: 77/889/102 (3rd among TEs), 11.5 FPPG across Shough's 11 games, +32.8 receiving EPA (led Saints pass-catchers), +245 anytime TD.
- Saints were #1 fastest neutral pace (29.11s, 23% no-huddle), trailed for league-high 76.8% of offensive plays in 2025.

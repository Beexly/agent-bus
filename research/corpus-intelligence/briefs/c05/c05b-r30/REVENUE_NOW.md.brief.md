# strategy/REVENUE_NOW.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A strategy memo answering "why would anyone pay before our picks are proven?" — reframing picks as the free trust magnet and tools/intelligence/transparency/education/experience as the paid product. It defines a Founding-phase entitlement remap (picks free; tools, analytics, alerts, academy paid) and a near-term execution sequence.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Entitlement remap (Founding phase, prices unchanged at $14.99 / $24.99):
  - Free: all picks (honest labels, open track record), full proof room (public calibration/CLV), limited fantasy tools (try optimizer/draft kit), track a few bets, intro Academy lessons.
  - Pro $14.99: picks + factor breakdown/why, your personal CLV/calibration analytics, full fantasy suite unlimited, unlimited tracker + analytics, line-movement alerts, full Academy.
  - Elite $24.99: same as Pro + real-time email & push alerts, ask-the-model (evidence-grounded).
- Trust ladder: picks earn their paywall later via PROVEN → ESTABLISHED → AUTHORITY ladder.
- Revenue prioritization: (1) fantasy tools — fastest, biggest, season-timed ("tens of millions" pay for good tools; serious player willing to pay $10–20/mo for a genuinely great draft kit + in-season optimizer; note "It's June — NFL draft season is Aug/Sept"); (2) affiliate revenue (compliant `/go/[slug]` rail, disclosures, responsible-gaming, state-gating done; needs owner EIN→partner signups per AFFILIATE_GO_LIVE.md); (3) transparency tracker + Academy (unlimited tracking + analytics + alerts as Pro feature); (4) audience via honest brand + experience (compounding).
- Execution sequence: de-paywall picks + strip "winners/profitable/edge-as-record" language → remap entitlements → position fantasy as paid-tier hero ahead of NFL season → stand up affiliate on partner signups → keep building honest record (Proven-Edge research program).

# docs/commercial/COMMERCIALIZATION_DOCTRINE.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
GSE's operating doctrine for commercialization: "commercializes trust before it commercializes picks." It lays out a commercial machine chain (Data → Source Rights → ... → Revenue → Audit → Trust), six approved monetization lanes with boundaries, and owner-approval gates — while explicitly not activating any billing, affiliates, or publishing.

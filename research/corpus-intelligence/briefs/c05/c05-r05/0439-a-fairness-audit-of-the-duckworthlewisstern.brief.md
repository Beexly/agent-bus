# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0439-a-fairness-audit-of-the-duckworthlewisstern.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of arXiv:2609.04754v1 (Soumyadeep Roy 2026), the first large-scale empirical fairness audit of cricket's Duckworth-Lewis-Stern target-revision method across 8,150 international matches, proposing DLS-Cal — an interpretable MLP calibration layer that predicts DLS residuals. Verdict: ADAPT — the cricket findings don't transfer, but the audit + state-conditioned residual calibration-layer methodology (and the Win-Flip Rate metric) ports directly to GSE win-probability/total calibration.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
DLS-Cal (eq. 1): r̂_{DLS-Cal} = r̂_{DLS} + Δ(x), x ∈ R^18 (DLS prediction + empirical training-set per-bucket bias prior + 16 enriched features). DLS-Cal = 4-layer MLP (widths [18,128,128,64,1], GELU, dropout 0.25, 27,265 params, final layer zero-initialized), trained on Huber loss (δ=10) with AdamW (η=1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, batch 512, cosine annealing, ≤50 epochs, patience 8) to predict residual r − r̂_DLS; gender-aware variant trains separate male/female calibrators routed at inference. Win-Flip Rate (eq. 2): WFR_k(M) = (1/N) Σ_i 1[|r̂_{M,i} − r_i| > k], reported at k ∈ {3,5,10,15}. Audit machinery: temporal splits (train ≤2022, val 2023, test 2024–Mar 2026); match-level block bootstrap (B=2,000 whole-match resamples) for all audit intervals; bucket-matched gender-gap estimate over 59 cells with ≥30 examples per gender; ablation ladder (lookup vs linear vs MLP-without-prior vs full MLP).
## Data sources named
Cricsheet ball-by-ball data (public, cricsheet.org): 8,150 matches (3,095 ODIs, 5,055 T20Is; 5,708 men's, 2,442 women's), 2,781,465 ball records, 27 June 2002 to 5 March 2026; synthetic interruption sampling (30 random interruption points per first innings) yields 233,550 examples (140,160/24,300/69,090); dedicated held-out set of 417 real rain-affected matches. Code, checkpoints, logs: github.com/sami42200/dls-cal/tree/main/repo, fixed seed 42.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Test (N=69,090), lower-is-better: DLS original RMSE 37.41 / MAE 30.54 / bias +17.40; DLS canonical RMSE 28.92 / MAE 22.61 / bias +7.57 (22.6% RMSE cut); stacking ensemble RMSE 21.49 / MAE 16.19 / bias −0.20 (best); DLS-Cal gender-blind RMSE 23.72 / MAE 17.72 / bias −1.62. DLS-Cal has lowest ODI WFR5 of any method: 83.0% vs XGBoost 84.0%, stacking 85.0%.
- Bias audit (training split): DLS mean error +4.79 runs (median +7.98); per-bucket mean bias spans −111 to +26 runs (ODI), a 137-run total span; ODI ANOVA F=527.16, Kruskal–Wallis H=9,797.69.
- Gender gap (ODI): men's mean bias +1.51 (N=60,660), women's +7.63 (N=10,350) → gap +6.13 runs; match-clustered 95% CI [+3.70,+10.07], bootstrap p(gap≤0)<0.001; bucket-matched gap +8.34 unweighted / +9.64 weighted; women's bias exceeds men's in 55 of 59 cells; T20I gap +0.51 runs, not significant (95% CI [−1.10,+2.12], p≈0.27).
- DLS-Cal bias reduction (test): mean |bias| ODI 33.74→23.29 runs (31.0% reduction); T20I 20.58→16.71 (18.8%). Gender-aware DLS-Cal: women's residual bias +6.19→+0.65 runs (5.54-run cut).
- Real rain-affected matches (417): DLS under-predicts ODIs (bias −11.1, RMSE 45.8), over-predicts T20Is (+8.7, RMSE 25.1).
- Note: female DLS-Cal trained on 32,760 examples vs 107,400 male; learning curve plateaus, female calibrator data-limited. Cricket-specific findings have zero NFL transfer; transferable parts are the audit methodology and calibration-layer design.

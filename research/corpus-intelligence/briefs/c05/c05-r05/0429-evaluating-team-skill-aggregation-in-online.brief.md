# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0429-evaluating-team-skill-aggregation-in-online.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of arXiv:2106.11397v1 (Dehpanah, Ghori, Gemmell, Mobasher 2021), which tests whether a team's skill rating should be computed from member ratings by SUM (equal contribution), MAX (best member "carries"), or MIN (weakest link) inside Elo, Glicko, and TrueSkill on three esports datasets. Verdict: ADAPT — the MAX-vs-SUM aggregation question is worth porting to GSE's unit/team rating construction, but the domain is esports with tiny samples, so port the method, not the results.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Aggregations: SUM μ_t = Σμ_i; MAX μ_t = max(μ_i); MIN μ_t = min(μ_i). Elo team win probability generalized to N-team fields: Pr(t_i wins, F) = [Σ_{j≠i} (1 + e^{(μ_tj − μ_ti)/D})⁻¹] / C(N,2); Elo team update μ′_ti = μ_ti + K[R′_ti − Pr] (K=10, D=400); player update μ′_j = μ_j + w_pj(μ′_ti − μ_ti), w_pj = μ_j/Σμ_members. Glicko: base-10 win probability with g(σ) uncertainty weighting; μ′_ti = μ_ti + [0.0057565/(1/σ²_ti + 1/d²_ti)]·[Σ g(σ_tj)(R′_ti − Pr)]; d²_ti = [(0.0057565)² Σ g(σ_tj)² Pr(1−Pr)]⁻¹. TrueSkill: non-draw win update μ′_ti = μ_ti + (σ²_ti/c)·[N(t/c)/Φ(t/c)], c = √(2β² + σ²_ti + σ²_tj), β=4.16, τ=0.833. Rank normalization R′_ti = (N − R^obs_ti)/C(N,2). Evaluation: purely online/walk-forward (matches sorted by timestamp), NDCG (PUBG) and accuracy (LoL, CS:GO); three setups: all players / best 1000 (top by final rating, first 10 games — survivorship/lookahead flag in sample construction) / most frequent (>100 games, first 100 games).
## Data sources named
PUBG (Kaggle): "over 25,000 matches and 825,000 unique players", duo matches. League of Legends (Harvard Dataverse, Sapienza et al. 2018): "over 52,000 head-to-head matches and 324,000 unique players", teams of five. CS:GO (Kaggle): "over 26,000 matches and 4,900 unique players", teams of five. No code link stated.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- PUBG (%NDCG) all players: Elo SUM 60.2 / MAX 61.5 / MIN 61.3; TrueSkill SUM 61.7 / MAX 62.2 / MIN 61.8; PreviousRank 60.8. Frequent players: TrueSkill 60.3 / 62.8 / 60.4.
- LoL (%Accuracy) all: Elo 49.2 / 50.2 / 49.5; Glicko 49.3 / 50.1 / 49.8; TrueSkill 49.8 / 50.4 / 49.3; PreviousRank 47.6. Best: TrueSkill MAX 76.1 vs MIN 26.1 (extreme gap driven by best-player sample selection on final ratings).
- CS:GO (%Accuracy) all: Elo 64.7 / 64.3 / 60.8; TrueSkill 64.3 / 62.7 / 59.7; PreviousRank 46.6 — MAX roughly tied with SUM here.
- Paper's headline: MAX wins in the majority of cases (all PUBG and LoL setups; CS:GO less consistent — authors attribute to higher density/smaller rating range); "carry" effect interpretation: team performance primarily determined by the most skilled member. SUM "even shows more inaccurate predictions compared to PreviousRank" for PUBG all-players under Elo/Glicko.
- Caveats: esports teams of 2–5; NFL has 11 simultaneous roles with more substitution and specialization — the "carry" mechanism does not transfer cleanly; no confidence intervals; margins like 62.2 vs 61.7 within noise; fixed hyperparameters; predicts match ranks, not probabilities for spread/total/prop markets.

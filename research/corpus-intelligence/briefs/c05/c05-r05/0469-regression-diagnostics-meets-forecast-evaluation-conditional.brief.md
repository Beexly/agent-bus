# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0469-regression-diagnostics-meets-forecast-evaluation-conditional.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-read ledger of arXiv:2108.03210v3 (Gneiting & Resin 2022), a measure-theoretic framework of calibration hierarchies for real-valued outcomes (auto-calibration → threshold/quantile/CEP/probabilistic/marginal) with practical CORP isotonic-recalibration diagnostics and a universal coefficient of determination R* nesting classical R² and R1. Verdict: ADAPT — the CORP diagnostics and R* are directly usable for GSE model diagnostics, ported alongside (not replacing) the existing conformal calibration stack.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Conditional T-calibration: T(L(Y|T(F))) = T(F) for identifiable functionals (means, quantiles, expectiles, moments, Huber); Thm 2.11: auto-calibration implies conditional T-calibration for identification-function functionals. T-PAV algorithm (Alg. 1): pool-adjacent-violators isotonic regression producing increasing recalibrated values x̂_i = T(δ_{k:l}), conditionally T-calibrated and optimal under any consistent scoring function of mixture form. Score decomposition: S̄ = MCB_S − DSC_S + UNC_S (MCB=miscalibration, DSC=discrimination/resolution, UNC=uncertainty; Thm 3.3 guarantees nonnegativity under PAV); extended: S̄ = MCB_u + MCB_c − DSC + UNC. Empirical skill score: S_skill = 1 − S̄̂/Ŝ_mg = (D̂SC − MĈB)/ÛNC (eq. 30). Universal R²: R* = (DSC − MCB)/UNC (eq. 31), in [0,1] only under in-sample loss-minimization-with-intercept conditions (Assumption 3.5). Canonical losses: mean → (x−y)²; α-expectile → 2|1{x≥y}−α|(x−y)²; α-quantile → 2(1{x≥y}−α)(x−y). CORP empirical T-reliability diagrams; consistency/resampling bands (Alg. 2–3, App. B). Code: github.com/resinj/replication_GR21; R package `reliabilitydiag` (CRAN).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Butterfly (MSE): null — MSE 0.263, MCB 0.031, DSC 0.461, UNC 0.693; ridge — MSE 0.224, MCB 0.033, DSC 0.502, UNC 0.693 ("both reliable; ridge has considerably higher discrimination").
- Synthetic mean forecasts (MSE): perfect — MSE 1.027, MCBc 0.091, DSC 0.981, UNC 1.916; unfocused — MSE 1.684, MCBc 0.420, DSC 0.653; lopsided — MSE 1.315, MCBc 0.210, DSC 0.811.
- Table 4 (MSE, UNC=2): perfect — MSE 1, MCB 0, DSC 1; unfocused — MSE 1+η0²/4; lopsided — MSE 1+2δ0²/π.
- Kvålseth (1985) toy: linear mean regression R²=0.779, R1=0.611; linear median regression R²=0.725, R1=0.692; DSC 10.593 (SE) vs 2.333 (absolute error).
- Bank of England CPI 1-quarter-ahead: threshold at 2% — BS 0.113, MCB 0.024, DSC 0.135, UNC 0.224; 75%-quantile — QS 0.331, MCBu 0.009, MCBc 0.121, DSC 0.497, UNC 0.698. R*/skill decays with lead time and goes negative at k≥4 quarters ("informative at ≤3 quarters only"); 4Q-ahead threshold: BS 0.307, MCB 0.091, DSC 0.008.
- Limitations: CORP flattens non-monotone miscalibration into horizontal segments; consistency bands assume forecast–residual independence (violated by serially correlated NFL errors — use block resampling by week); R*∈[0,1] fails out-of-sample (use the possibly negative skill score instead); variance functionals excluded (identifiability counterexample); NFL team-strength persistence violates exchangeability — seasonal retraining needed.

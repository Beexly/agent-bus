## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A full-text ledger read of arXiv:1907.11180v2 (Kurach et al., Google Research, 2020) — the Google Research Football RL environment paper (physics-based 3D soccer simulator + Football Benchmarks + Football Academy curriculum + IMPALA/PPO/Ape-X DQN baselines), assigned as a reserve replacement for REJECTED 2410.10736. Verdict: ADAPT — no NFL-transferable model, but two concrete portable ideas (non-transitive evaluation, spatial mini-map encodings). Note: this is SOCCER, not American football.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No formal equations (environment paper). Key quantitative definitions: Checkpoint reward = +0.1 per region × 10 opponent-half checkpoint regions (Euclidean distance to goal), capped at +1 (= one goal), once per episode, uncollected checkpoints credited on scoring; difficulty θ ∈ [0,1] (easy 0.05, medium 0.6, hard 0.95) via bot reaction-time scaling; game length 3000 frames default (10 fps × 5 min), Academy scenarios 400 frames (end on score, ball loss, or stoppage); 19 actions (8 movement dirs, short/high/long pass, shot, slide tackle, dribble, sprint, stop-dribble, stop-moving, stop-sprint, do-nothing; moving/sprinting sticky until explicit stop; switch-active-player action removed after policies exploited it); 3 observation representations — Pixels (1280×720 RGB), Super Mini Map (4 binary 72×96 matrices: home/away/ball/active player, stackable), Floats (115-dim compact vector); stochastic mode default, deterministic available; OpenAI Gym API. Hyperparameters fully tabulated (Tables 7-9): IMPALA lr 0.00013730 (scoring) / 0.00019896 (checkpoint), γ=0.993, 500 actors, unroll 32; PPO lr 0.00011879/0.000343, 16 actors, unroll 512, GAE λ=0.95; DQN lr 0.00001475/0.0000115, 150 actors, γ=0.999. Hyperparameter search: 100 configs × 3 seeds on easy, best selected at 500M (IMPALA/DQN) or 50M (PPO).

## Data sources named
No static dataset — a simulator. Football Engine: heavily customized GameplayFootball (Schuiling 2017) in optimized C++; full 11v11 soccer rules (goals, fouls, corners, penalties, offsides, yellow/red cards, handballs); per-player stats (speed, accuracy) and fatigue; both teams share the same stat set for fairness; rule-based opponent bot; open-source github.com/google-research/football. Full text fetched from https://arxiv.org/html/1907.11180v2 (2026-09-21).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- **Throughput:** ~140M env steps/day on a single hexa-core Intel Xeon W-2135 [OTHER].
- **Benchmarks, Scoring reward @500M steps (Table 5, mean ± std over 5 seeds, average goal difference):** DQN easy 8.16±1.05, medium 2.01±0.27, hard 0.27±0.56; IMPALA easy 5.14±2.88, medium −0.36±0.11, hard −0.47±0.48; PPO @50M easy 0.09±0.13, medium −0.84±0.10, hard −1.39±0.22 [OTHER].
- **Benchmarks, Checkpoint reward @500M (Table 6):** IMPALA easy 12.83±1.30, medium 5.54±0.90, hard 3.15±0.37; DQN easy 7.06±0.85, medium 2.18±0.25, hard 1.20±0.40; PPO @50M easy 8.71±0.72, medium 1.11±0.45, hard −0.75±0.13. Checkpoint massively helps policy-gradient methods; DQN similar under both rewards [OTHER].
- **Representation experiment (Table 4, 500M steps, IMPALA + Checkpoint, easy 11v11):** stacked SMM 12.89±0.51, SMM 9.75±4.15, pixels-gray (72×96) 7.18±0.85, floats 5.73±0.28 — hand-designed spatial encoding beats raw pixels and compact floats [OTHER].
- **Self-play non-transitivity (Table 2):** agent A trained vs built-in AI (medium); agent B trained vs frozen A. A vs built-in AI 4.25±1.72; B vs A 11.93±2.19; B vs built-in AI −0.27±0.33 — B crushes A yet loses to the bot that A beats [OTHER].
- **Multi-agent (Table 3, 50M steps, 3v1-with-Keeper):** controlling 1 player 0.68±0.03, 2 players 0.81±0.17, 3 players 0.86±0.08 (slower start, higher ceiling) [OTHER].
- **Academy:** 11 scenarios (Empty Goal Close, Empty Goal, Run to Score, Run to Score with Keeper, Pass and Shoot with Keeper, Run/Pass and Shoot with Keeper, 3v1 with Keeper, Corner, Easy/Hard Counter-Attack, 11v11 with Lazy Opponents); empty-goal scenarios solved by PPO/IMPALA in ~1M steps ("unit tests"); mid scenarios need 5-50M steps; Corner is hardest [OTHER].
- **Validation design:** benchmarks 3 difficulties × 2 rewards × 3 algorithms × 5 seeds, mean average goal difference with 95% bootstrapped CIs (Appendix Tables 5-6); Academy 11 scenarios × 5 seeds at 50M steps; representation experiment at 100M and 500M; self-play non-transitivity and multi-agent experiments [OTHER].
- **Leakage/caveats:** baselines are 2019-2020 (IMPALA/PPO/Ape-X DQN) — headroom claims stale, modern methods (e.g., MuZero-style) have since beaten the hard benchmark; hyperparameters tuned on easy then transferred to medium/hard (favors transfer-friendly algorithms); PPO ran only 50M steps vs 500M for IMPALA/DQN (single-machine constraint — uneven comparison); Academy v1.x vs v2.x version mixing in appendix figures [OTHER].
- **Limitations (per paper read):** soccer, not American football — rules, state space, action semantics do not transfer to the NFL; sparse-reward conclusions are simulator-specific; it is a game-AI benchmark, not a prediction-modeling testbed (nothing models betting markets, odds, or probabilistic forecasting); results reflect 2020-era algorithms [OTHER].
- **GSE overlap check:** nothing in the research map covers RL environments or game-AI benchmarks; GSE's engine is a supervised probabilistic prediction system (model v5.2.7), not an RL agent — no duplication [OTHER].
- **Portable idea 1 (evaluation):** the non-transitivity result is a direct caution for GSE's engine-benchmark lane — pairwise model bake-offs can misrank; fix = fixed benchmark suite (frozen historical slates + fixed market/consensus baseline) plus Elo-style rating [OTHER].
- **Portable idea 2 (representation):** stacked spatial encodings 12.89±0.51 ≫ compact floats 5.73±0.28 is evidence for the tracking lane that mini-map-style encodings of player positions beat raw coordinate vectors; proposed experiment: 32×32 spatial grids per frame for offense/defense/ball, stacked over ±5 frames, vs floats on a play-outcome task [OTHER].
- **Portable idea 3 (conditional):** Checkpoint shaping (+0.1/region, capped at +1, once per episode) as a template for dense intermediate rewards if GSE ever trains sequential decision policies (e.g., in-play bet-timing agents) [OTHER].
- **Reproducible test:** take the last 3 candidate engine versions; rank (a) by pairwise head-to-head P&L on 2024 season slates, (b) by P&L against the fixed consensus/market baseline suite; check for rank disagreement (non-transitivity). Gate: adopt the fixed-suite protocol if ≥1 rank disagreement or any pairwise winner loses to the market baseline [OTHER].

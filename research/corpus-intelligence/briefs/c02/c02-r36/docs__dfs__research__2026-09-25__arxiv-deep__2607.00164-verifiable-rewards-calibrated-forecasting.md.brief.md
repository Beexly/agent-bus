# docs/dfs/research/2026-09-25/arxiv-deep/2607.00164-verifiable-rewards-calibrated-forecasting.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A full-paper research ledger on arXiv 2607.00164v1 (Cascade Research, 30 Jun 2026): RL with verifiable rewards trains a 7B LLM to output calibrated NFL in-game win probabilities, comparing a denoised conditional-rate reward against realized-outcome rewards and the live betting market. VERDICT recorded in the file: ADAPT — the empirical-rate reward and market-ceiling test are portable to GSE calibration auditing/CLV; the LLM/GRPO pipeline is not.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Ideal target: η(x) = Pr(win | x). Naive reward: r = 1 − (p − y)², y ∈ {0,1}. Proposed rate reward (eq. 1): r = 1 − (p − p̂(x))². Brier: mean (p − y)², "strictly proper: among all functions of the state, its expectation is minimized uniquely by η(x)."
- Empirical-rate table: training plays binned by score margin (14 bins, edges ±1/±4/±7/±10/±14/±21) × time remaining (7 bins, edges 2/5/10/15/30/45 min) × pregame spread (9 bins, edges ±0.5/±3/±7/±10); sparse buckets shrunk toward coarser parents by hierarchical empirical-Bayes rule with pseudocount M = 25: bucket = (w + M·p̂_parent)/(n + M), applied global → downward.
- Optimizer: GRPO (TRL, vLLM rollouts, importance-sampling correction disabled), LoRA rank 16 α=32 dropout 0.05, bfloat16, 8 completions/state at temp 0.9, 250 steps with 20-step warmup, checkpoint selected by Brier on the full 2023 split. Two gradient-decoupling variants: direct (no CoT, ≤48 tokens, LR 2×10⁻⁵, KL 0.01) and masked-CoT (≤640 tokens reasoning, gradient only on final "Probability: NN%" span, KL β=0, LR 3×10⁻⁵). Single NVIDIA L40S (48 GB).
- Evaluation: Brier, ECE/MCE over ten equal-width bins, Murphy decomposition (reliability/resolution/uncertainty), 95% CIs from 10⁴-resample nonparametric bootstraps over plays, paired bootstrap ΔBrier; evaluation reference = live in-game betting market win probability converted via Štrumbelj [32], withheld from model and reward.
- Splits: 40,246 training states (2015–2022), 5,241 selection (2023), 5,185 test (2024); no game in more than one split.

## Data sources named
- Public nflfastR play-by-play, NFL regular seasons 2015–2024; live in-game betting-market odds (evaluation only); nflverse WP model, gradient-boosted baseline, DeepSeek-V4 zero-shot (frontier baseline); code/data released at https://github.com/jasper-research/nfl-rlvr-release and Zenodo https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21082572 (URLs quoted from the paper, not verified by the reader).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Reward-target ablation (in-training held-out n=128): realized-outcome reward Brier 0.166 / ECE 0.10; ½/½ blend 0.181 / 0.121 ("worse on both, because it reintroduces the outcome's noise"); empirical-rate reward 0.154 / 0.050.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Held-out 2024 (n=5185, Brier [95% CI]): Direct RLVR 0.1443 [0.1394, 0.1491] / ECE 0.0292 / MCE 0.0596 / Acc 0.784 / Res 0.1058; Masked-CoT 0.1522 / 0.0293 / 0.0684 / 0.777 / 0.0986; DeepSeek-V4 zero-shot 0.1438 / 0.0430 / 0.0716 / 0.790 / 0.1078; empirical-rate teacher p̂ 0.1432 / 0.0437 / 0.0993 / 0.783 / 0.1083; betting market 0.1355 / 0.0273 / 0.0824 / 0.799 / 0.1148. Zero-shot direct Qwen2.5-7B: 0.2057 / 0.0569. Full-completion CoT training drove held-out Brier 0.25→0.34 and ECE 0.19→0.30 (gradient corruption of reasoning).
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Paired ΔBrier: Direct−Masked-CoT −0.0079* [−0.0101,−0.0057]; Direct−Market +0.0088* [0.0068,0.0107]; Direct−DeepSeek-V4 +0.0005 [−0.0019,0.0028] (not significant); DeepSeek-V4−Market +0.0083* [0.0064,0.0102]. Headline claims: ECE 0.029 matches market 0.027; MCE 0.060 below market's 0.082; direct model better calibrated than the frontier model (0.029 vs 0.043).
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Resolution decomposition: the 0.008 Brier gap to the market is "resolution, not reliability: the model is as well calibrated as the market but less sharp, 0.106 against 0.115."
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Static-feature forecasters on 2024: nflverse WP 0.1562 / ECE 0.0188; GBM all features 0.1584 / 0.0260; coarse p̂ 0.1432 / 0.0437 — "two models with more features than the coarse buckets are worse on Brier"; coarse vs fine (+field position, +down) on 2023: 0.1532/0.0117 vs 0.1534/0.0099 vs market 0.1495/0.0193.
- [OTHER] Reasoning-consistency (blinded judge, n=250): Base CoT 22.4% [17.6,27.6] inconsistent → Masked-CoT 4.4% [2.0,7.2]; controls: naive CoT-RLVR (p̂ reward) 18.8%, (outcome reward) 14.0%, masked prompt no training 6.8%. The ledger notes the isolated training effect is ~6.8%→4.4% with overlapping CIs ("At 250 plays the format and training intervals overlap").
- [OTHER] Masked-model instability notes: with β=0.01 the loss diverges ("the k3 KL estimator overflow"); LR inverted-U peaking at 3×10⁻⁵, "over-sharpens" (MCE past 0.5) at 4×10⁻⁵.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Reader's adversarial flags (in-file §9): (1) the method distills a lookup table — model can't beat p̂, and the trained model beats its own teacher on ECE (0.029 vs 0.044), unexplained beyond "smoothing"; never compared to an isotonic/spline smoothing baseline. (2) Checkpoint selection uses fixed n=128 held-out sample — noisy selection. (3) "Cascade Research" is not an established institution; v1 preprint; numbers pending independent replication. (4) GSE's regime (pregame picks, ~16 games/week, props) is the sparse long-horizon regime the paper's own conclusion warns weakens the method; props (dense, nightly-resolving) are the better fit. (5) Human structure is injected via bin edges; "no human labels" is true only narrowly.
- [OTHER] Paper's own scope limit: "sparse or long-horizon events would weaken both the reward and the comparison against a market."

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The bin-and-shrink recipe (14×7×9 bins, M=25 empirical-Bayes backoff) is a directly liftable calibration-ladder construction: score GSE's own picks against state-conditioned empirical hit rates with hierarchical backoff, flag buckets where |model_p − p̂| > 0.05 at n ≥ 30 — the paper's §11 spec gives an implementation-complete rate-audit module with test and acceptance gate (variance ratio ≤ 0.5, ≥1 flagged bucket while outcome-ECE < 0.03, three-source |ΔBrier| < 0.01 convergence check).
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The market-as-ceiling three-estimator convergence test (engine, p̂, market-implied probabilities; CEILING_REACHED when pairwise |ΔBrier| < 0.005 over a rolling window) is a principled stop rule for CLV-model tuning — stop adding capacity where the engine already matches the market's calibration.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The gradient-corruption finding (full CoT Brier 0.25→0.34) and the masked-answer-span result are directly relevant if GSE ever fine-tunes an LLM for pick explanations: gradient only on the probability/edge tokens, never on reasoning.

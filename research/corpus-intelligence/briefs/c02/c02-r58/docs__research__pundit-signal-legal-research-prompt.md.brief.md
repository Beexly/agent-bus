# docs/research/pundit-signal-legal-research-prompt.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026-06-13 owner-requested deep-research prompt (engineered to be pasted into ChatGPT Deep Research / o-series) mapping every lawful pathway for using paid sports-radio pundit commentary (e.g., SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio, "Elite Sports with Jeff Mans") as a graded input signal in a commercial prediction product — with citations required for every legal claim.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- No formulas. The prompt demands a ranked verdict: paths scored by legal viability × cost × effort; output format = executive verdict, path-by-path analysis, "no" list with controlling authority, citations appendix, 1-page counsel brief.
- Rules for the research model: "Cite or don't claim"; distinguish copyright vs. contract vs. CFAA independently; prioritize 2023-2026 AI-training fair-use developments; mark unsettled law.

## Data sources named
- SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio (specifically "Elite Sports with Jeff Mans"); SiriusXM Customer Agreement §9 ("AI Matters") — personal non-commercial use only; prohibits using content to create/train/improve any AI service; prohibits scraping, recording, automated extraction.
- The same pundits' PUBLIC podcasts (e.g., "One MANS Opinion" on Podbean/Apple) under different terms.
- Cases named: Feist v. Rural Telephone (1991); Fox News Network v. TVEyes (2d Cir. 2018); Thomson Reuters v. Ross Intelligence (D. Del. 2025 — fair use rejected for AI training on Westlaw headnotes); NYT v. OpenAI (pending); Authors Guild v. OpenAI; Authors Guild v. Google (2d Cir. 2015); A.V. ex rel. Vanderhye v. iParadigms (Turnitin); Van Buren v. United States (2021); hiQ v. LinkedIn; INS v. AP; NBA v. Motorola (2d Cir. 1997).
- Statutes named: 17 U.S.C. §102(b) (idea/expression); 17 U.S.C. §107 (four-factor fair use); CFAA post-Van Buren.
- Vendor categories named: media-monitoring firms, sports-data licensors, transcript licensors (concrete vendors to be found by the research model).

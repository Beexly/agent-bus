# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0295-spacecreating-versus-dead-possession-an-offball.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A two-layer off-ball possession-quality framework (arXiv:2608.09887v1) for soccer: an event-side "junk possession" index that flags low-threat sequences in tied-or-losing states, plus a broadcast-video Space-Creation Index (SCI) that adjudicates flagged windows as spatially dead or space-creating via pitch-control change — a direct template for an NFL "sterile offense" metric.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Event layer: possession segmented into maximal consecutive-event sequences s. Eq. 1: value(s) = max(0, xT_max(s) − xT_start(s)) [peak threat gain, credited without shot] + 0.7·xG(s) [max shot xG in s] + 0.10·𝟙[box touch]. Normalized by corpus-wide 90th percentile, clipped to q(s) ∈ [0,1]; q < 0.15 = junk sequence. Headline metric: junk-open = 100·Σ_{s: state≤0, q_s<0.15} n_s / Σ_{s: state≤0} n_s (junk event share among tied-or-losing possessions). Team metrics: raw%, effective% (threat-weighted), efficiency eff = Σ n_s q_s / Σ n_s ∈ [0,1], field tilt (own final-third event share, x≥80), sterile index = (Raw−50)×(1−eff).
- Spatial layer: SCI = Δ_own + Δ_opp, where Δ_own = C̄^{last}_{P,A} − C̄^{first}_{P,A} (possessing team P's attacking-third control-share change) and Δ_opp = C̄^{first}_{O,Z} − C̄^{last}_{O,Z} (opponent's presence in its own attacking third receding), via Spearman et al. pitch-control model. Prespecified bins: space-creating (SCI ≥ +12), weak progression (+4 ≤ SCI < +12), non-space-creating (SCI < +4).
- Redemption rule: junk sequence redeemed if same team regains ball within 25 s and reaches threat (peak-xT gain ≥ 0.04 or box touch). Assumptions: "possession" = share of on-ball events; lead-protecting circulation is sound tactics, excluded via score-state gating; peak threat reached is worth crediting even without a shot; corpus-wide constants (90th pct, q<0.15, box weight 0.10, SCI +12/+4) apply to all splits.

## Data sources named
- Event layer: 2026 FIFA World Cup, 103 of 104 matches (one absent from feed) → 206 team-match rows; WhoScored public event feeds; knockout penalty ties scored by advancement; xG from own gradient-boosted model (feed's shot-xG is a constant placeholder); xT grid Karun-Singh-style; live scoreline reconstructed from goal events. Event-side code: https://github.com/nowayfootball/junk-possession (CPU-only, no GPU spend).
- Spatial layer: 9 purposively selected World Cup matches, 35 event-flagged junk windows → 31 survived CPU-only broadcast GSR pipeline (PnLCalib calibration, BoT-SORT tracking, Lab-chroma team assignment, training-free off-screen ghosting). Windows 10–60 s of continuous wide-angle play; extra time excluded.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- [SCHEME] Table 2 correlations (206 team-matches): junk-open vs xG −0.38, goals −0.38, points −0.37, xG diff −0.51; efficiency vs xG +0.59; field tilt vs xG +0.58 but points only +0.18 (quality vs territory axes).
- [SCHEME] 71 team-matches >55% possession split by corpus-wide median efficiency: 8 "junk dominators" averaged 1.07 xG, 0.62 goals, 1.00 points vs 63 efficient dominators with 2.05 xG, 2.24 goals, 2.24 points (descriptive, n=8).
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] VAEP non-reducibility regression: points ~ VAEP + tilt + junk-open → VAEP p=0.34, junk-open p<1e-4; xG diff ~ VAEP + tilt + efficiency → VAEP p=0.46, efficiency p<1e-4 (match-clustered re-estimation unchanged). junk-open β=−0.020, p=0.017 iid / p=0.014 under match-clustered CR1. VAEP correlates r=−0.49 with junk-open, +0.58 with efficiency. Shot-prediction logistic: 5-fold AUC 0.907 ± 0.004 (folds not match-grouped — upper bound).
- [COACHING] Out-of-sample: first-half efficiency → second-half xG r=+0.32; first-half junk-open → lower second-half xG r=−0.24; leave-one-match-out team mean efficiency → this-match points r=+0.31; mean junk-open → lower points r=−0.30 and xG diff −0.32.
- [OTHER] Redemption: only 9% of tied-or-losing junk was redeemed; dead-only variant points r: −0.37→−0.41 in-sample, identical out-of-sample (honest null).
- [SCHEME] Spatial: 31 windows — 74% non-space-creating, 19% weak progression, 6% space-creating; stable from 6-match (71/24/5) to 9-match sample. Cases: Germany–Paraguay R32 — Germany 73% possession, SCI −24.4/−14.2, eliminated; Paraguay window SCI +18.0 space-creating but unconverted; Norway–Brazil junk-but-won. Event contrast: South Africa 0–1 Canada (57% poss, eff 0.27, junk-open 57%) = textbook junk; South Korea 1–0 Mexico (57% poss, eff 0.41) = less sterile.
- [OTHER] Limitations noted in the file: xG-term mechanical coupling inflates xG associations (goals/points are the clean endpoints); corpus constants are round, not optimized; spatial sample is purposive, not random; off-screen ghosting imputation not sensitivity-filtered; NFL drives are discrete, so "sequence" must be re-derived for drive/series granularity; 2026 World Cup data — verify competition naming/timing before citing.

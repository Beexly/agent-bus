# docs/math/GSE_EXPECTED_POINTS.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Methodology doc for GSE's four first-principles glass-box metrics — Expected Points (`gse-ep-v1`), Win Probability (`gse-wp-v1`), Success Rate (`gse-success-v1`), and Drive segmentation (`gse-drives-v1`) — describing the actual code in `packages/prediction-engine/src/expected-metrics/`, with nflverse used only as a calibration referee, never as served data. All four enter dark: additive, descriptive, historical, NOT wired into scoring, constants, or confidence/edge math.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **EP**: seven one-vs-rest binary logistics over next-score outcomes, renormalized. Features: `down, ydstogo, yardline100, yardline100Squared, goalToGo, halfSecondsRemaining`. Outcomes (possession frame): TD +7, FG +3, SAFETY +2, NONE 0, OPP_SAFETY −2, OPP_FG −3, OPP_TD −7. `EP(state) = Σ_k P(next-score=k) · value(k)` ∈ [−7, 7]. `EPA = EP(after) − EP(before)`; on possession change EP(after) is NEGATED to the before-team's frame. Required heads {TD, FG, NONE, OPP_FG, OPP_TD} must fit or no surface served; rare safety heads may fail (null → raw prob 0). TD=7 is a v1 simplification inclusive of expected PAT (v2 may use ~6.95). Floor: `MIN_EP_PLAYS_TO_FIT = 1000` usable plays, else null.
- **WP**: single binary logistic of P(possession team ultimately wins). Features: `scoreDifferential, gameSecondsRemaining, scoreDiffPerSqrtTime, yardline100, down, ydstogo, timeoutDifferential, spreadLine`. `WP(state) = σ(β·features)` ∈ (0,1), no clamp. `WPA = WP(after) − WP(before)`; on possession change WP(after) → complement `1 − WP(after)`. Missing spread imputed as 0 (pick'em) — an accepted v1 imputation that conflates missing with genuine pick'em. Floor: `MIN_WP_PLAYS_TO_FIT = 1000`.
- **Success rate**: deterministic rule, no fit. Success = touchdown (forced) OR (not a turnover AND yards_gained ≥ fraction(down)·ydstogo), with `SUCCESS_YARDAGE_FRACTION = {1: 0.4, 2: 0.6, 3: 1.0, 4: 1.0}`. Turnover forces failure and dominates touchdown (pick-six = offensive failure). Unratable downs return null and are dropped. Splits by team, player, down, early/late × short/medium/long situation bucket. No "over expected" surface in v1.
- **Drives**: pure drive segmentation, no fit. Partition-completeness invariant: every play in exactly one drive (`Σ playCount === plays.length`, playIds multiset preserved). Two modes: explicit `driveId` grouping or detect-by-possession-change with synthesized ids; empty-`posteam` play attaches to open drive; empty-`posteam` first play starts a drive (never orphaned). Terminal outcome classification: explicit `terminalOutcome` authoritative; else points ≥ 6 → TD, === 3 → FG, else OTHER; safety never inferred from point total. `pointsScored` is actual points (TD 6, FG 3, safety 2, PAT/XP 1, two-point 2) — must NOT be mapped from nflfastR's binary `sp` indicator (would misclassify TD+PAT as `1+1=2` → OTHER). Aggregates: points Σ, epaTotal Σ (epa ?? 0), successRate over ratable plays only (0 when none ratable, never NaN).
- **Provenance**: every fitted model carries `modelVersion, method, ordered featureKeys, featureSchemaHash` (djb2 over ordered keys), `sampleSize`. Methods: `multinomial-ovr-logistic` (EP), `logistic-regression` (WP), `deterministic-rule` (success), `drive-segmentation` (drives).
- **Validation gates vs nflverse referee**: `ep: {minSample: 200, graduatedPearson: 0.9, provisionalPearson: 0.75}`, `wp: {minSample: 200, graduatedPearson: 0.9, provisionalPearson: 0.8}` (minSample = paired plays). Success/drives are definitional — no correlation gate, only determinism + partition invariant via unit tests.
- **Grain discipline**: per-play, same season, same season type (REG), joined on `game_id`+`play_id`; never mix seasons; EPA validation excludes terminal scoring plays on both sides of the join.
- **The two sign conventions** (flagged top correctness hazard): EP negates on possession flip (signed point expectation); WP complements (probability in [0,1]) — mixing them is the classic bug. Both document the after-play possession-frame caller contract.

## Data sources named
- nflverse play-by-play (CC-BY-4.0) — `ep`/`epa`/`wp` enter ONLY as the y-axis of calibration correlations (referee), never served. FTN charting and participation columns (CC-BY-SA-4.0) are NOT used.
- Exact pbp columns per module: EP reads `game_id, play_id, down, ydstogo, yardline_100, half_seconds_remaining, goal_to_go, sp, touchdown, field_goal_result, safety, td_team, posteam, ep, epa`; WP reads `game_id, play_id, score_differential, game_seconds_remaining, yardline_100, down, ydstogo, posteam_timeouts_remaining, defteam_timeouts_remaining, spread_line, result, wp`; success rate reads `game_id, play_id, posteam, rusher_player_id, receiver_player_id, down, ydstogo, yards_gained, touchdown, interception, fumble_lost`; drives reads `game_id, play_id, fixed_drive, fixed_drive_result, posteam, yardline_100, sp`.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Own model versions (`gse-ep-v1`, `gse-wp-v1`, `gse-success-v1`, `gse-drives-v1`) are unrelated to `constants.ts` `MODEL_VERSION`; none of the four modules is imported by `scoring.ts` or `constants.ts` — shipping them changes no served pick.
- Validation bars are set deliberately high (0.9 graduated Pearson) because EP and WP are almost fully functions of public situation (down/distance/field position/score/time), so a faithful public reconstruction should correlate strongly with the nflverse referee.
- EP ∈ [−7, 7] provably (convex combination of values in [−7, 7]); WP ∈ (0,1) with no clamp.
- Fit-on-load provenance and null-not-guess: `fit*` returns null below its sample floor or on degenerate data — a metric is never served from an unfit model.
- These are descriptive/historical metrics, explicitly not performance claims (no win rate, ROI, profit, or closing-line-value assertions), and not wired into scoring or confidence — that requires a founder-gated `MODEL_VERSION` step under the model-freeze guardrail.
- Source-of-truth rule: if this doc and the code disagree, the code wins and the doc is the bug.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- [QB-BEHAVIOR] EPA and WPA splits by player, down, and situation bucket (early/late × short/medium/long) are the natural substrate for quarterback efficiency profiling — e.g., success-rate fractions {1: 0.4, 2: 0.6, 3: 1.0, 4: 1.0} applied to QB drops/scrambles measure on-schedule play under definitional rules.
- [SCHEME] Drive-level rollups (points Σ, EPA total, success rate, start/end field position) provide a possession-structural lens on offensive scheme efficiency; possession-frame sign conventions formalize whose points a play creates.
- [OL] Play-level EPA/WPA split by down and short/medium/long situations gives a clean on-field signal for OL performance (non-terminal down-to-down transitions only) once wired to personnel groupings.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] nflverse-as-referee-only with owned definitions, provenance-carrying fits, and high correlation bars (0.9 Pearson) — the internal-validation pattern behind any trust claim; enter-dark (no scoring impact) until graduation.
- [OTHER] The `sp`-indicator misclassification hazard (TD+PAT = 1+1 = 2 → OTHER) is a concrete data-quality rule for any consumer of scoring columns.

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
Yes — the EP/WP/success/drives modules are specified glass-box primitives ready for wiring once promoted: EP and WPA are the canonical play-value currencies, and the 0.9-Pearson referee gates define exactly what "proven" means for each.

# docs/ops/edge/2026-08-19-deepseek-adversary-round2.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
External red-team review (DeepSeek, round 2 with internal audit) of GSE's calibration, CLV, and e-process certification design. Each item carries an audit verdict: adopt only what is marked ADOPT; four round-2 items are marked DO NOT ADOPT as errors.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Corrected composite null per bet: H0,i: p*_i <= b_i, b_i = 1/D_i (decimal odds); e-factor L_i = (p_i/b_i)^{Y_i} · ((1-p_i)/(1-b_i))^{1-Y_i}. Ville's inequality gives P(sup E_n >= 1/α) <= α.
- Mixture e-process over unknown edge size (Beta or discrete grid), not a fixed θ=0.60.
- Price-space CLV: p_novig = (1/D_over)/((1/D_over)+(1/D_under)); Δ_i = p_novig,close − p_novig,lock; record both sides' decimal odds + ms timestamps at lock and close. (Audit: use Shin de-vig, not proportional — Shin already in codebase shin-devig.test.ts.)
- Same-slate dependence correction: N_eff = N/(1+(m−1)ρ); at m=4, ρ=0.1, 301 → ~231.
- Power analysis (one-sided, α=0.05, power 0.80): δ=0.02 → 3,844 picks; δ=0.03 → 1,708; δ=0.05 → 615.
- Skeptical prior of real post-vig edge in public MLB totals ≈ 0.05; posterior after contaminated 58.5% still <0.15; require ~0.5 posterior on clean prospective data before operating on an edge claim.
- KL rates: D(0.60‖0.524)=0.0114 → log(20)/0.0114 ≈ 263 picks; D(0.55‖0.524)=0.00135 → ≈ 2,220 picks.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The null H0: p ≤ 0.524 assumes every bet is exactly −110; true break-even is bet-specific (−105 → 0.5122, −120 → 0.5455). Fixed 0.524 inflates the certification rate — a losing 0.530 at −115 tests as skill.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Leak 3: re-grading history after a bug fix destroys the e-process guarantee; certification probability can be driven arbitrarily high by permitting re-grading until the path looks good. CONSEQUENCE: C-15/C-20 re-grading the contaminated census in place is now FORBIDDEN — freeze historical ledger, fixes apply prospectively, corrected history becomes a separate clearly-labelled audit stream.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] A 2% edge is not detectable in one MLB season at any realistic volume (3,844 picks needed at δ=0.02, one-sided α=0.05, 80% power).
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The grouping-loss formula supplied in round 1 (GL = (1/N)Σ N_p·Var(y|p)) is wrong — it measures within-bin outcome variance (~0.25 Bernoulli noise), not grouping loss as defined by Perez-Lebel et al. (variance of true conditional probability within a score bin, E[Var(P(Y=1|X)|S(X))]). C-21 must use the published estimator, not the supplied formula; the <0.01/0.01–0.05/>0.05 threshold table is unsourced/invented.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] The round-2 price-space null (H0: E[Δ_i] ≤ 0 on continuous CLV) was only supplied with a BINARY win-rate e-factor — incomplete; a continuous bounded mean needs a betting martingale (Waudby-Smith & Ramdas style), not the binomial likelihood ratio.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] GSE's own aggregator chain w_i = |p_i − 0.5| + 0.05 rewards extremity not reliability; the 0.03 dead zone discards small edges; ×0.484 attenuation plus 45% market re-anchoring forces p_final ≈ m by construction — identified as the primary engineering cause of near-zero resolution. Replace with logit-space regularized regression; fit α, don't guess it.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Bickel & Kim (2014) actually concluded little evidence of MLB over-under inefficiency — a genuine prior update DOWNWARD on C-22 and the 58.5% totals signal; hunting in a field a competent study already swept. Exception: their study didn't test weather-park interaction, umpire zone, or bullpen fatigue at distribution tails, and didn't price alternates.
- [COACHING] Publication vs betting separation: publication of honest probabilities with no edge claim should be stratified across the full disagreement range so later CLV/resolution measurement stays unbiased; betting/edge-claiming uses a threshold on model-market disagreement chosen to maximise e-process log-growth.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] C-22: negative-binomial totals model with shared game-level random effect and hierarchical shrinkage — independently recommended by both internal dossier and the adversary (convergent).

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026 best-in-class media/content production quality brief for GSN (Galaxy Sports Network / Galaxy Sports Edge), grounded in the repo's actual draft-only content engines, brand tokens, and compliance doctrine plus live 2026 web research, aimed at making media the "proof surface" (show the receipts: calibration curves, loss autopsies, CLV) rather than a promo surface. It specifies two text engines, a three-layer compliance scanner, a Higgsfield + Canva MCP pipeline, per-platform distribution specs, and a prioritized roadmap — with the hard constraint that nothing auto-publishes and nothing fabricates a claim.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- The engine is draft-only by design: "no publish endpoint, no scheduler … `publishedAt` … never set by the engine — operator-only" (`schema.prisma` L1361-1381); export is copy-markdown / save-markdown only — "External publishing is intentionally absent"; audit requirement: "No auto-post endpoint exists in `apps/web/` or `workers/`"; automation tiering caps at Draft-assisted / Template-assisted; "Fully automated … CRITICAL — FORBIDDEN". [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Scanner specifics (verified in templates): `X_THREAD` blocks `🚨🔥💰💎🚀💯🏆` emoji ladders, all-caps hype (`BREAKING|HUGE|MASSIVE|INSANE`), engagement-bait CTAs (`who do you have|comment your locks|RT if`); warns on the word "card"; `TIKTOK_REELS_SCRIPT` blocks clickbait hooks (`you won't believe|the secret nobody is talking about`) and tout-coded `LOCK|HAMMER|FADE|tail`. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Hard liabilities named: no real-athlete AI imagery, no league broadcast footage, no unlicensed music; no win-rate counters or live-confidence animations; AI narration must carry audible+visible "AI-narrated content" disclosure; sponsor/affiliate disclosure is a gating field; 2026 rules require disclosing both paid placement and AI involvement. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- The seven-stage Media Studio Workflow: Brief → Asset selection (claim pre-check) → Creation (MCP draft) → Claim-governance review → Brand-safety review → Rights/attribution → Operator publish; AI permitted stages 1–4 only, stages 5–7 operator-only. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- The single highest-leverage safe improvement: per-entity OG images starting with a track-record/calibration card at `apps/web/app/calibration/opengraph-image.tsx` rendering Brier score, win-rate-by-confidence, CLV, sample size, model version, "Graded in public" + RG line — pure render of already-public values; if sample size is below the gate, render neutral "calibration in progress" rather than a number. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Content calendar tied to the `DailyBrief` clock (one per UTC date, `@@unique([briefDate])`): daily AM slate → atomized X thread + Reels draft; game-day LINE_MOVEMENT_WATCH; post-settlement WEEKLY_RECAP + LossAutopsy on notable misses; weekly ModelJournalEntry pillar → newsletter + accountability thread; quarterly MODEL_ACCOUNTABILITY_NOTE; never atomize a STALE section. [OTHER]

# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1610-information-leakage-at-population-scale-polymarket-insider.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026 paper (Nechepurenko, arXiv:2605.00459, 22,272 words) stress-testing the deadline-resolved Information Leakage Score (ILS^dl) — the share of a prediction market's terminal price move accomplished before the public observation of the underlying event — across a population of 12,708 Polymarket markets (2020–2026), finding its effective interpretable domain is structurally narrow. Ledger verdict: ADAPT — the ILS framework, hazard-decay baseline correction, and category-conditional informed-flow argument are portable to an NFL sharp-flow monitor only with the paper's own caveats (weak complementary signal, anchor-robustness-screened, never standalone).
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Base score: ILS(M) = Δ_pre / Δ_total, Δ_pre = p(T_news) − p(T_open), Δ_total = p_T_resolve − p(T_open).
- Deadline extension: ILS^dl(M) = (p(T_event^−) − p(T_open)) / (p_T_resolve − p(T_open)).
- Hazard-decay-adjusted: ILS^{dl,adj}(M) = (p(T_event^−) − E[p(T_event^−) | T_open, D, λ̂]) / (p_T_resolve − p(T_open)), subtracting the survival-probability-implied baseline for deadline contracts.
- Scope conditions: τ > 0 lead time; edge-effect |p(T_open)−0.5| ≤ 0.4; non-trivial move |Δ_total| ≥ ε = 0.05.
- Anchor-sensitivity check: recompute ILS under 30-min/2h/6h/24h anchor offsets; pass requires no sign change and shift ≤ 0.3.
- Hazard: pooled post-2024 exponential λ̂ = 0.042 (half-life 16.4 days); corporate-disclosure-only reference λ̂ = 0.156, half-life 4.5 days; Weibull used as default production prior; shape parameter k̂ explicitly not interpreted.
- Four-filter pipeline: resolution-typology classification → exclude deadline-resolved NOs → LLM-cascade T_event recovery with confidence ≥ 0.7 (Tier 1 Gemini 2.5 Flash + Search grounding; Tier 2 GPT-4o-mini; Tier 3 Claude Sonnet 4.6; Iran-Apr30 reference hard-assert) → full CLOB price coverage from T_open. Detection thresholds at top-decile/top-5%/top-1% with B=500 market-level bootstrap CIs. Population pipeline cost ≈ $1.20 in LLM spend. Murphy-decomposition reading: ILS quantifies the share of the Brier-score resolution component accumulated before the public-observation anchor.
- Sequenced (NOT built here): gradient-boosted-tree detector combining ILS^dl with wallet-level features — PIN, VPIN, Kyle's lambda, wallet novelty score, two-sidedness, variance ratio, trade-size kurtosis, Hawkes self-excitation (follow-up paper 3b).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Coverage: 88 of 12,708 candidate markets (0.7%) yield computable ILS^dl; 12 of 88 (13.6%) satisfy anchor-sensitivity — effective interpretable domain ≈ 12/12,708 markets. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- FFIC: only 1 of 32 documented insider markets in scope (Bitcoin ETF approval, ILS^dl = 0.012, at the regulatory_announcement pre-2024 cell median, not the right tail). Dominant exclusion: 14 of 32 (43.8%) unclassifiable by resolution-typology; 8 (25.0%) deadline-resolved NO; 2 (6.25%) edge-effect (p_0 ≥ 0.9). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- T_event validation: 57.8% exact-date agreement overall — below the ≥90% ex-ante criterion; by sub-bucket milgeo_corporate 78.9%, regulatory_formal 35.7%, regulatory_announcement 50.0%; of 24 disagreements: 9 minor (≤7 days), 10 major (>7 days), 5 no usable timestamp. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Distribution: raw ILS^dl medians negative across all six cells (range −0.88 to −0.16). Hazard-decay adjustment: regulatory_formal post-2024 −0.21 → −0.02; milgeo_corporate post-2024 −0.55 → −0.34; regulatory_announcement post-2024 −0.80 → −0.84 with bootstrap CI entirely below zero [−1.45, −0.24]. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Anchor robustness: Spearman rank correlation 0.30 between article-derived anchor and 24h variant; 86% anchor-fragility rate; fragility concentrates where independent recovery disagrees on date. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Hazard functional form: pooled post-2024 (n=57) Weibull AIC 454.66 vs exponential 476.59 (Δ=21.9, above 4-unit threshold); parametric-bootstrap KS rejects exponential (p<0.01), accepts Weibull (p>0.50); post-2024 regulatory_formal alone (n=22) prefers exponential (AIC 179.45 vs 180.50) and rejects Weibull (KS p=0.043) — pooled preference reflects category mixture, not within-cell duration dependence. [OTHER]
- Detection thresholds: post-2024 90th percentiles 0.62 (regulatory_formal), −0.01 (milgeo_corporate — right tail doesn't cross zero), 0.11 (regulatory_announcement); 9 of 10 right-tail markets classify as qualitatively leakage-shaped after hazard adjustment; left tail median −1.85 raw. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Selection penalty: insider-relevant subpopulation is 15.3% of resolved / 11.3% of ≥$50K-vol markets; pooled detectors bucket signal at 1:7.9 against a structurally-irrelevant control (sports, crypto price, weather) — detection should condition on insider-relevant categories (up-weights prior ≈7.9×). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Cross-method positioning (not evidence): Mitts–Ofir 2026 flagged 210,718 wallet–market pairs ($143M flagged profit, 60σ deviation); Gómez-Cram et al. 2026 found 1,950 informed accounts of 1.72M — flagged sets not public. [OTHER]
- NFL external-validity caveat stated: paper covers political/regulatory/geopolitical contracts; NFL moneyline/spread markets resolve on observable field outcomes, so the analog is scheduled-information markets (coach firings, trade deadlines) and pre-kickoff line discovery. [OTHER]

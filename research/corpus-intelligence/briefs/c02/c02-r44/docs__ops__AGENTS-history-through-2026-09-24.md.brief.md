## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **Two-path calibration (2,641 settled picks, measured 2026-09-13):** signal path 60–69 → 57.0% (n=423), 70–79 → 66.0% (n=247), 80–89 → 67.8% (n=59) — roughly honest, slightly conservative. Book path 80–89 → 41.5% (n=130), 90–99 → 31.2% (n=32) — INVERTED at top. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- **Confidence is anti-predictive at the top (n=2,385 settled published non-bootstrap picks):** conf 80+ claims 0.8663, realizes 0.5191, gap −0.3472, z=−10.7; Brier of the conf band as a probability = 0.3617 vs 0.25 for a constant 0.5 forecast. Realized win rate peaks at conf 75–79 (0.6146), falls to 0.4643 by conf 90–94. `rankingP` (n=1,390): monotone, top band 0.8934 claimed vs 0.8286 realized. `marketFairProb` (n=622, books≥2): monotone, every gap within 0.07. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- **Isotonic regression (PAVA) is monotone by construction — it can flatten a curve, never invert one; no calibrator can fix an inverted score, and more sample does not help.** The truth surface's `confidenceTail` read `verdict=inverted` since at least 2026-09-05 (n=167). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- **Adverse-edge suppression (PR #811):** publish rule = never mint a row whose own `expectedClv` is negative. Seven rows were published with `independentEdge.decision="PASS"` AND negative expectedClv (worst −0.1742); re-measured 20:20 UTC: 71 published PENDING rows with edge estimates, 9 with expectedClv<0, and all 9 carried decision=PASS (predicates agreed exactly). Display-side rule in `/api/picks` and `lib/board/state.ts`; writes NOTHING (rows still settle and count in the record). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- **Factor-weight forensics:** in the stored breakdown of a conf-91 pick, `consensusScore` 30 + `marketDepthScore` 20 are constants for any 11-book MLB run line — 50 of the points fixed before the engine looks at the game; `Independent Edge (skellam_cover)` stored with `"weight": −31` and `"impact": "positive"` — a negative weight labelled positive. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- **PROVEN gate (floors n=100 / Brier 0.22 / ECE 0.05):** Brier floor is cleared by a constant base-rate forecast with no skill (uncertainty alone 0.2139 vs 0.22 floor). Perfectly calibrated forecaster reads ECE ≈0.09 at n=100 and ≈0.04 at n=487 — so the gate reads debiased ECE (raw − noise). PROVEN went live 2026-09-09: pool n=380, Brier 0.2099, debiased ECE 0.0374; deployed v5.2.7 n=258, debiased 0.0582, bound 0.0438. Streak required: 3 consecutive GREEN six-hourly runs.

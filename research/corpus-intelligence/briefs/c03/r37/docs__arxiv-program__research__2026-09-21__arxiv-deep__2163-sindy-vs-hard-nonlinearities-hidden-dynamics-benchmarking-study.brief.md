# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2163-sindy-vs-hard-nonlinearities-hidden-dynamics-benchmarking-study.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-dive ledger on Ugolini, Breschi, Manzoni & Tanelli (2024, arXiv:2403.00578v1) — a brutally honest benchmarking study of SINDy (Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics) on three real-world nonlinear-identification benchmarks with unobserved states and hard (non-smooth) nonlinearities, plus hands-on practitioner strategies to recover good sparse models anyway. Verdict: ADAPT — a field manual for discovering sparse governing equations of NFL game-state dynamics.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- SINDy (with control): ẋ(t) ≈ Θ^⊤φ(x,u); sparse problem min_Θ ‖Ẋ−Φ(X,U)Θ‖²₂ + λ²Σᵢ‖Θᵢ‖₀; STLS iteration converges in ≤ n_φ steps to a LOCAL minimizer — no guarantee of true structure even with correct bases in the library.
- Metrics: BFR = max(1 − Σ(y_k−ŷ)²/Σ(y_k−ȳ)², 0) [%]; RMSE — computed on OPEN-LOOP simulation (harsher than one-step prediction).
- Strategy 1 (pick-and-place): derivative-coordinate augmentation — add z(t)=ẏ(t) as constructed state, 2nd-order polynomial library (handles saturations via higher model order).
- Strategy 2 (Bouc–Wen): iterative hidden-state reconstruction — guess linear params (m_L, c_L, k_L), reconstruct z(t)=u−c_L ẏ−k_L y−m_L ÿ, fit ż with library including |ẏ|, |z| (true model: m_L ÿ = u − c_L ẏ − k_L y − z; ż = αẏ − β(γ|ẏ|z + δẏ|z|), admits no finite Taylor expansion), iterate minimizing validation RMSE.
- Strategy 3 (cascaded tanks): structured hidden dynamics + initial-condition estimation — assume ẋ₁=−k₁√x₁+k₂u; simulate x̂₁ per guess of (k₁,k₂); grid-search x₁(0) minimizing (ẏ(0)−φ^tanks(y(0),x₁,u(0))^⊤θ)² (Eq. 17).
- Hyperparameters via Bayesian optimization (Hyperopt); derivative estimates via jointly-optimized tunable regularization; PySINDy.
## Data sources named
Three public benchmarks: pick-and-place machine (Juloski et al. 2004; 3840 train/960 val/1200 test @ 400 Hz); Bouc–Wen hysteretic oscillator (Noël & Schoukens 2020); cascaded tanks (Schoukens et al. 2020; 768 train/256 val); code at github.com/aurelio-raffa/benchmarking_sindy; PySINDy (Kaptanoglu et al. 2022).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Pick-and-place BFR %: SINDy (2nd-order, derivative-coordinate) 76 (94 with better threshold); PWA 75; jump models 83. Naïve 1st-order SINDy (ẏ=−111.16y+133.73u−3.48yu) produced slower, non-oscillatory, plateau-free output — missed all key dynamics; 2nd-order captured oscillations + saturations and beat both competitors.
- Bouc–Wen RMSE ×10⁻⁴ [m] (low-freq/multi-sine/sine-sweep): naïve 10.99/2.75/3.32; hidden-SINDy HPO 11.26/1.83/1.72; hidden-SINDy Ideal (true params) 8.87/2.52/3.03 — structurally CORRECT model scored WORSE on test metrics than structurally wrong HPO model (recovered ż=αẏ−βz|ẏ|+γ|z|ẏ−δ|z||ẏ|, α=68815.219, β=570.725, γ=700.135, δ=0.815; ÿ=−0.213ẏ−27685.724y−0.379z+0.379u). Only the Ideal variant reproduced the hysteresis cycle shape.
- Cascaded tanks BFR % / RMSE: hidden-SINDy Poly 93.24/0.55; hidden-SINDy Sqrt 92.82/0.56; NN-ARX 95.10/0.47 (slightly better, less interpretable, more hyperparameter-sensitive); ARX 86.55/0.77; naïve SINDy Poly 73.16/1.09; SINDy Sqrt 53.09/1.44; 2nd-order I/O-only SINDy 0/2.12. Linear ARX beat naïve nonlinear SINDy — stated cautionary result.
- Core finding: test-metric-optimal ≠ structurally correct; stronger priors (structured hidden states) were required for accuracy in all three benchmarks; validation RMSE preferred the polynomial model (0.356) over the physics-informed sqrt (0.383) — qualitatively wrong.
- Adversarial notes: derivative-fitting + noise is the Achilles' heel; the hidden-state strategies are "excessively reliant on partial knowledge of the dynamics" (authors' own words); small datasets (15 s at 400 Hz; 1024 samples) raise sample-size/overfitting concerns; weak/integral formulations give guarantees only for scalar systems with polynomial/harmonic libraries.
- GSE mapping: state = [score_diff, time_remaining, yardline, down, distance, EPA_rolling, timeouts] per play from nflverse pbp (~60–160 steps/game); library = 2nd-order polynomials + |score_diff| (garbage-time nonlinearities) + turnover/red-zone indicators (hard nonlinearities); latent "momentum" h via Bouc–Wen iterative recipe; evaluate on open-loop simulation of 2025 games from kickoff.
- GSE acceptance gate (pre-registered): ADOPT if hidden-state SINDy BFR ≥ 70% on held-out 2025 Weeks 1–8 AND beats both baselines by ≥ 10 BFR points AND recovered equation ≤ 8 nonzero terms; REJECT if BFR < 50% or metric-optimal model structurally nonsensical on human review.
## Intelligence connections (tag each finding: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- SCHEME: Sparse governing equations of game-state evolution (score differential, time, field position, momentum proxies) — a new capability; engine v5.2.7 has no governing-equation layer; Wave-5a covered DSR/PySR regression but not SINDy dynamics-from-time-series or hidden-state equation discovery.
- QB-BEHAVIOR: Bouc–Wen hidden-state recipe (hysteresis with unmeasured internal state z) maps to latent momentum/fatigue in NFL games — partially observed states exactly as in sports.
- COACHING: Structured-hidden-state strategy suggests positing latent play-calling intent with assumed restoring structure rather than naïve observable-only fitting.
- TRUST-SIGNAL: Metric-optimal ≠ structurally correct — metric-driven selection can actively reject domain knowledge; never let BFR alone choose the game-dynamics model; NN-ARX's small BFR edge (95.10 vs 93.24) buys non-interpretability, and recovered equations are auditable (public-record value).
- OTHER: Derivative-coordinate augmentation and abs-value library terms (|score_diff| for garbage-time nonlinearities, turnover/red-zone indicators) give a concrete library design.
- OTHER: Linear ARX beating naïve nonlinear SINDy — complexity must earn its keep vs. simple baselines.
- OTHER: Improvement experiment — Weak-form SINDy (integral formulation, Messenger & Bortz 2021; Russo & Laiu 2022) for noisy play-level sports data; test term-count stability and coefficient variance across bootstrap resamples of games.

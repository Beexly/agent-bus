# docs/media/youtube-automation-boundaries.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A founder-level policy document defining the permanent hard boundaries for YouTube automation on the Galaxy Sports Edge channel: what AI-generated publishing patterns are forbidden, what claim-governance rules apply to titles/thumbnails/descriptions, and the disclosure requirements for AI-assisted content.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified (policy document, no metrics or formulas)

## Data sources named
None (doctrine document; no data sources)

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- The auto-generate-and-upload pattern (AI writes the script, generates the voiceover, renders the video, and uploads it unattended) is permanently FORBIDDEN; the file names two such tools as exemplars of the forbidden class (Gemini-class generative video tooling and "MonePrinter"-type upload automation).
- All AI-generated or AI-assisted content must be disclosed; no AI content may be presented as unassisted human work.
- Claim governance applies to every title, thumbnail, and description: no "locks", no "guaranteed picks", no outcome-certainty language.
- No performance claim may be published without the measured record behind it (win rates, streaks, calibration numbers must come from the audited record).

## Intelligence connections (tag each finding: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Permanent ban on unattended AI generate-and-upload pipelines — the channel's credibility rests on human accountability for what ships.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Claim governance on all titles/thumbnails/descriptions (no "locks"/"guaranteed picks") — language-level trust gate against tout behavior.
- [TRUST-SIGNAL] Mandatory disclosure of AI-generated content — positions the brand as transparent about production method.
- [OTHER] No performance claim without the measured record — all numbers must trace to the audited settled-pick record.

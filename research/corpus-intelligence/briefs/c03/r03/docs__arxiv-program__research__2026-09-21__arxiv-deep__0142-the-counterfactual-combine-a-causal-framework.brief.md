## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read of Susmann & D'Alessandro (2026), *The Counterfactual Combine: A Causal Framework for Player Evaluation* (arXiv:2602.23233). Verdict in file: ADOPT — a doubly-robust TMLE causal framework for player evaluation demonstrated directly on NFL kickers (nflreadr data), portable to GSE with valid uncertainty quantification that point-estimate metrics lack.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Structural causal model Z=(X,A,Y): X attempt covariates, A∈{1..m} player index, Y∈{0,1} success. Three counterfactual estimands via stochastic interventions A*~P_{A*|X}: (1) Direct standardization ψ_a^direct=E[Y(a)|X∈X′] (league-table rankable); (2) Indirect standardization ψ_a^indirect=E[Y(A*)|A=a] with P_{A*|X}=P_{A|X} (NOT rankable — file cites Shahian et al. 2020 misuse warning); (3) Performance above random replacement Δ̂^b_rand_a=Ê[Y|A=a]−ψ̂^rand_a. Estimation: TMLE — fluctuate initial estimate along low-dimensional parametric submodel so (1/n)Σ D_P̂*(Z_i)≈0, then plug in; guaranteed in [0,1]. Crossfitting required (10-fold NFL, 5-fold MLB). Nuisance models: Super Learner ensemble (mean, glm, lightgbm) via mlr3superlearner R for both μ_P(a,X) and π_P(a|X). Theorem 1: doubly robust — consistent if either propensity or outcome model converges; √n-asymptotically normal and efficient if both converge at o_P(n^{−1/4}); Wald CIs from empirical EIF SD. Assumptions: A1 no unmeasured confounders; A2 positivity (π_P(a|x)>0); A3 intervention independence. EIF structure: direct standardization weights residuals by INVERSE propensity (small π inflates variance); indirect weights by propensity itself.
## Data sources named
NFL kickers: all NFL games 2013–2023 via nflreadr (Ho and Carl 2025). Raw: 11,523 FG attempts by 110 kickers (XP excluded); analysis set: 46 kickers with ≥100 attempts → 9,786 attempts; empirical FG success 85.3%. Covariates: kick distance, outdoor indicator, grass vs turf, temperature (°F), wind speed (mph; 0 for enclosed), final-30-seconds indicator. MLB batters: all 2025 regular-season via baseballr (Petti and Gilani 2024); 163,664 ABs by 673 players; analysis: 84 players with >150 games or >600 PA → 40,107 ABs; BA .260. Code: stated as "[anonymized]" (double-blind) — effectively none retrievable.
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NFL direct-standardization top 3: Evan McPherson, Tyler Bass, Justin Tucker; lowest point estimate: Phil Dawson (period covers only latter part of career, excluding 1999–2012 Browns tenure; statistically indistinguishable from many others under CIs).
- Tyler Bass's standardized rate EXCEEDS his observed rate (harder-than-league-average attempt mix); others standardized lower (easier mixes or good situation-matching).
- Justin Tucker: statistically significant positive values for BOTH indirect and random-replacement contrasts. No kicker significantly below zero on either contrast — consistent with selection (poor kickers don't stay in league).
- MLB: top 5 standardized BA — Aaron Judge, Freddie Freeman, Bo Bichette, Vladimir Guerrero Jr., Xavier Edwards. All 84 players' 95% CIs contain their observed 2025 BA. Ohtani, Soto, Schwarber outside top 20 (Schwarber: 56 HR but 197 Ks, .240 observed BA — outcome collapses all hits equally). Adames, Ward, Volpe, Nootbaar, McMahon significantly negative indirect/replacement contrasts (defensive roles or injury-hampered seasons). Harper's propensity distribution skewed (fewest right-handed opponents) — strategic lefty deployment.
- MLB propensity diagnostics: 3,368,988 estimated propensity scores, mean 0.0119, 75% between 0.0110 and 0.0127 — "some evidence for practical positivity violations," widening CIs via inverse weights. NFL: no pronounced positivity violations.
- No comparison vs existing adjusted metrics; no forward-prediction check that standardized metrics predict next-season performance — flagged in file as the test GSE should run.

# docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1497-sports-intelligence-llm-vlm-qa-benchmark.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2024 benchmark paper (Yang et al., arXiv:2406.14877v1) evaluating LLMs/VLMs on three consolidated sports-understanding subtasks (basic facts, advanced multi-hop scenario reasoning, video action recognition); ledger verdict is ADAPT as GSE's NLP-lane evaluation discipline — error taxonomy, CoT prompting standard, and the quantified human-gap justification for human review.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Benchmark = SportQA Level-1 (21,385 fact questions) + Level-2 (45,685 rules/tactics questions, 35 sports) + BIG-bench Sports Understanding (986 plausibility MCQs); SportQA Level-3 (3,522 manually designed scenario questions, easy/hard × single-hop/multi-hop); Sports-QA (6,000 videos, 94,000 questions, 8 sports; Descriptive/Temporal/Causal/Counterfactual types).
- Models: Llama3-70B, Gemini 1.5 Pro/Flash, Claude 3 Opus, GPT-4, GPT-4o (0-shot, 0-shot CoT, 5-shot, 5-shot CoT); VLMs: MiniGPT-4-7B, Chat-UniVi-7B, PLLaVA-7B, Video-LLaVA-7B (0-shot CoT); GPT-4 as judge for VLM answers (temperature 0, 1–5 scale).
- Four-class text error taxonomy (from 40 sampled wrong answers per task, human-categorized): (A) domain-knowledge errors, (B) inaccurate recall, (C) context/nuance confusion, (D) reasoning errors. Video errors dominated by "Lack of Domain Knowledge."
## Data sources named
Public datasets only, no new data: SportQA, BIG-bench Sports Understanding subtask, Sports-QA. Human performance upper bounds from SportQA. No new code/data released. Note: potential train-test contamination acknowledged for LiveQA-style broadcast questions (reason LiveQA was excluded).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Basic sports understanding (Table 1): Level-1 best GPT-4o(0S,CoT) 85.35; Level-2 best Claude 3 Opus(5S,SP) 77.38; BIG-bench best Gemini 1.5 Pro(0S) 95.40. No ceiling improvement vs SportQA's benchmark six months prior. (OTHER)
- Advanced Scenario Analysis (Table 2): best single-hop GPT-4o(0S,CoT) 79.37 (easy) / 73.17 (hard); best multi-hop GPT-4(5S,CoT) 44.49 (easy) / GPT-4o(5S,CoT) 29.11 (hard) vs human 96.63/96.02/94.90/91.84. Six-month progress: +4.5%/+7.0%/+10%/+7% on easy/hard single/multi-hop. ALL four best configurations used CoT. GPT-4o better at single-hop, GPT-4 better at multi-hop. (OTHER: hard multi-hop gap ~29% vs ~92% human is the quantitative case for human-in-the-loop on chained sports reasoning)
- Video-based (Table 3): task-specific Auto-Focus Transformer baseline 59.2/59.1 overall vs VLMs 19.28–31.52 overall; GPT-4 judge scores 1.49–1.92/5 — VLMs far behind the specialized model, especially Temporal (8.82–16.23) and Counterfactual questions. (OTHER: do not use general VLMs for play/action recognition; specialized detectors outperform 2–3×)
- Error distribution: advanced scenario errors dominated by "Context and Nuances Confused" (class C); video errors dominated by "Lack of Domain Knowledge." (TRUST-SIGNAL: when GSE's CoT test shows errors concentrating in class C rather than class A, that matches the healthy signature)
- Limitations stated: could not evaluate larger VLMs (e.g. PLLaVA-34B) due to compute; GPT-4-as-judge is a proxy; error taxonomy from only 40 samples per task; evaluation is API-snapshot dependent (models have since changed).
## Intelligence connections (tag each finding: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- CoT prompting standard (all four best advanced configurations used CoT): OTHER — require zero/few-shot CoT on every GSE LLM call doing multi-step sports reasoning (rule application, scenario analysis, matchup write-ups).
- Four-class error taxonomy as QA checklist: TRUST-SIGNAL — every LLM-generated sports content piece checked against (A) domain knowledge, (B) recall accuracy, (C) context/nuance confusion, (D) reasoning errors; video/clip work additionally checked for number recognition, action recognition, domain terminology.
- Hard multi-hop gap (~29% vs ~92% human): OTHER — any pipeline step requiring chained sports reasoning ("given these injuries and weather, how does the game script change") must be human-verified or decomposed into single-hop checks.
- Specialized detectors over general VLMs (2–3× gap): OTHER — GSE clip analysis uses specialized detectors, VLMs only for captioning.

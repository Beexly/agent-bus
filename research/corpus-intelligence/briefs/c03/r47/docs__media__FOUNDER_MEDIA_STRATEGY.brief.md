## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Founder media strategy doc (updated 2026-07-04) for Garrett/Beex posting alongside GSE: founder-led content should build credibility by making the work legible — shipped artifacts, blockers, lessons — while keeping pressure as disciplined context, not desperation, and never weakening the official evidence posture.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Thesis: "people trust people before they trust a new sports intelligence brand"; "The hardest part of a sports prediction company is not the model. It is proof."
- Allowed unemployment framing: "I am unemployed, building GSE, and this is what actually moved today." / "The pressure is real, so the proof has to be real." / "I cannot afford a fake picks brand, so I am building the audit trail first." Avoid: pity framing, chaotic venting, implying the audience owes support, replacing proof with intensity, private financial/family details.
- Personal vs official split: founder can show human pressure (disciplined), shipped artifacts, direct partner asks; founder never makes unsupported model claims — only approved evidence language on official surfaces; sponsor copy always disclosed and reviewed.
- Boundaries: no secrets, no private dashboards unless safe, no paid-tool automation, no fake audience/sponsor claims, no performance claims without existing evidence (TRUST-SIGNAL: claim governance applied to founder voice too).

# engine/research/2026-09-24/model-owned-projection-pipeline.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Architecture document (generated 2026-09-25 from Firecrawl research) defining a 10-step model-owned projection pipeline to replace illustrative DFS constants with real, versioned, calibrated projections. It lays out the decision spine, observation ledger, point-in-time feature materialization, distribution modeling, calibration wrapper, contest decision layer, and evaluation/promotion loop, plus a durable schema and output contract.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No formulas. 10 pipeline steps: (1) decision spine/identity; (2) immutable observation ledger (append-only SourceSnapshot/Observation); (3) point-in-time feature materialization at decision timestamp t0 (FeatureSnapshot keyed by entity, slate, t0, feature-set version, rights state); (4) role/availability state model (availability probability, expected snaps/PA, role distribution, scenarios); (5) sport-specific hierarchical distribution model (joint player/team/game distributions); (6) calibration/uncertainty wrapper (calibrated probs, quantiles, intervals, coverage, sharpness, refusal state); (7) ownership/contest decision layer (expected fantasy points, percentile outcomes, leverage, duplication-adjusted utility, lineups, risk); (8) evaluation/promotion (sealed historical replay, live shadow, EvaluationReceipt, champion/challenger); (9) serving/evidence publication (projection + uncertainty + freshness + factors + lineage + counter-evidence + limitations + gate state); (10) monitoring/feedback/retraining. Output contract required_fields listed (entity_id, sport, league, event_id, decision_time, projection_run_id, model_version_id, feature_snapshot_id, availability_probability, role_state, mean, median, quantiles, floor, ceiling, probability_over_threshold, calibration_version, interval_coverage_target, interval_width, missing_features, stale_features, rights_state, evidence_ids, gate_state).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Design principle: the model owns the projection; sources own observations; the contest engine owns constraints; the evidence layer owns explanation; the registry owns promotion — no source, heuristic rank, LLM narrative, or optimizer may silently become the projection.
- Anti-patterns named: no training on latest tables without as-of/created-at filtering; never call manual illustrative constants model-owned projections; never blend probability, edge, rank, confidence, ownership, narrative into one unexplained number; never activate a source because it's publicly visible (rights/redistribution are separate gates); never treat tests/fixtures/backtest harness as historical proof without sealed point-in-time replay + evaluation receipts; consent/minimization controls for health/wearable/cognitive data.
- Schema includes OwnershipProjection kept SEPARATE from performance probability (selection probability vs. performance probability).

Yes — adopt the pipeline as the promotion-gate standard: no projection ships to users without a FeatureSnapshot t0, a calibration_version, an EvaluationReceipt from sealed replay, and the required_fields output contract.

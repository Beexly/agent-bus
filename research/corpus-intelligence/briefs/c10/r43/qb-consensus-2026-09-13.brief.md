# dfs/research/2026-09-13/qb-consensus-2026-09-13.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Consensus QB ranking for the FanDuel NFL Classic Week 1 Sun–Mon 14-game slate (Sept 13–14, 2026), compiled ~11:25am CT Sept 13 from 10+ free sources (FantasyPros, numberFire, DK Network, FanDuel Research, thehuddle, FantasyAlarm, FantasyLabs, RotoWire, etc.); no paywalled numbers used.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Consensus proj = mean of FantasyPros (std scoring), numberFire (FD scoring), DK Network (DK scoring) where available. No formula beyond the mean.
- Ownership = qualitative model-sentiment reads (RotoGrinders numeric ownership is Premium-only, not used).
- FD scoring: 4 pts/pass TD, 1 pt per 25 pass yds, +3 bonus at 300 pass yds, 6 pts/rush-rec TD, 1 pt per 10 rush/rec yds, 0.5 PPR, $60,000 cap.

Deep-research ledger of arXiv:2601.14727 (Fang et al., 17 Sep 2026), a SURVEY of recent Bradley–Terry model advances with no novel method. Verdict recorded in file: ADAPT — three implementable upgrades for GSE: Newman's async fixed-point MLE iteration, EM-MAP gamma-prior regularization for small-sample ratings, and the PlusDC dynamic-covariate model (home-field advantage as special case).

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
(BT-S): P(Yij=i≻j) = γi/(γi+γj). (BT-U): P = exp(ui)/(exp(ui)+exp(uj)) = σ(ui−uj), σ(x)=(1+exp(−x))^(−1). (BT-MLE): û = argmax_{u,1ᵀu=0} Σ log σ(Yij(ui−uj)). (Newman FPI): γi = [Σj wij γj/(γi+γj)] / [Σj wji/(γi+γj)]. (EM-MAP): γi = (a−1+Σj wij)/(b+Σj nij/(γi+γj)) (contains Zermelo as a=1,b=0). (PlusDC): P = σ(ui−uj + (x{i,j},i−x{i,j},j)ᵀv). (PL): P(Ye=iπ(1)≻···≻iπ(k)) = Π_{j=1}^{k−1} exp(uiπ(j))/(Σ_{ℓ=j}^{k} exp(uiπ(ℓ))). (Zermelo): γi = Σj wij / Σj nij/(γi+γj), Σγi=1. (GD): u(t+1)=Π_{1⊥}(u(t)+ηt∇l(u(t))). Rao–Kupper/Davidson tie models and ordinal models cited. Elo update = online SGD step on BT log-likelihood (Király & Qian 2017). SST: min{pij,pjk}≥1/2 ⟹ pik≥max{pij,pjk}. MLE existence needs Ford (1957) strong connectivity; undefeated team sends ûi→∞. Optimal sparsity pn≳(log n)/n (Chen et al. 2022b). Newman FPI converges in fewest full-data passes of Zermelo/Newman/GD horse race, but async updates are mandatory (sync may diverge near-bipartite). PL mixture non-identifiable if H≥(n+1)/2; generically identifiable if 1≤H≤⌊(n−2)/2⌋!, n≥6 (Zhao et al. 2016). Table 2 reference: NFL |e|=2, |E|=5.3K, n=32. S.3 benchmark numbers: ATP n=1103, λ2(Λ)=0.053, dmax/λ2(L)=157.35; Simulation n=1000, 16,481 edges; GD η=2/dmax and η=4/λmax(L).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Newman's (2023) fixed-point iteration converges in fewest full-data passes across all four S.3 settings; Zermelo MM substantially faster than GD on real datasets; spectral step-size improves GD but gap remains on ATP/Algebra.
- Async Newman update discipline is critical: synchronous version may diverge on near-bipartite graphs (Algebra is bipartite, λn(L)=2).
- EM-MAP gamma-prior regularization fixes the early-season undefeated-team MLE divergence (Ford condition); Zermelo is special case a=1,b=0.
- PlusDC generalizes BT with home-field advantage as special case (Agresti 1990); utility = ui + xᵀv.
- SST assumed by all valid-f models; violated by matchup-cycle sports (paper flags this); BT silently eats misspecification.
- Uniform consistency optimal rate pn≳(log n)/n for MLE and regularized MLE (ER graphs).
- 2-component BT mixture generically identifiable on complete graph if n≥5 (Zhang et al. 2022) — relevant to home/road split ratings.
- File's GSE spec proposes `bt-team-ratings` TS module: time-decayed weights (default half-life 365d), Newman async FPI, PlusDC home scalar h, EM-MAP fallback, outputs `{utility, strength, gamesWeighted}` per team + fair moneyline prices; acceptance gate: walk-forward (2022–2024) moneyline Brier ≥2% lower than engine baseline, mean |fair prob − Pinnacle closing| ≤0.03, median weekly |Δu| ≤0.08. Honest uncertainty note in file: described engine modules `calibration-ladder`, `pick-clv`, `source-reliability`, `oos-split` were not found in local checkout (may be on other branches) — integration points unverified.
- Improvement experiments listed: ordinal margin-of-victory BT (spread-model feeder), H=2 mixture for game script, learned decay half-life grid (60–730d), Fisher-information SEs for the calibration ladder, RankCentrality cold-start for CFB week 1.

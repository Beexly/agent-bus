## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- The consumer frame: "We're not AI. We're math you can read."; the business frame: "Galaxy Sports Edge is a Sports Intelligence platform. Sports betting is the wedge product."
- Free-forever surfaces (explicit anti-paywall list): public Edge Index per tracked game, methodology page, Pass List, Public Ledger, Live Calibration chart, model changelog, Loss Room.
- Anti-products: aggressive affiliate placement, "VIP card" tout tiers, multi-asset prediction markets, white-labeling the consumer brand, banned-vocabulary ads.
- Trust toolkit monetization = Codex's insight that "the restraint posture is itself a product": claim scanner API, promo guard API, white-label Loss Room widget, evidence registry data product.
- Sportsbook affiliate enrollment (DK, FD, MGM, Caesars, BetRivers, Underdog, PrizePicks) deferred to owner decision (DEC-OPEN-A).
- NOTE against standing doctrine: the file says consumer subscription is "Live" and Free tier includes "One pick a day plus all free public surfaces" — but the 2026-09-28 HARD doctrines say the public site shows only projections/rankings and NGS/methodology are internal. Surfaces like "methodology page," "Public Ledger (every settled pick, with the signal snapshot)," "factor breakdown," and "quick reasoning" named here as free/public are exactly what the newer doctrines pull internal. Flagging the tension; not resolving.

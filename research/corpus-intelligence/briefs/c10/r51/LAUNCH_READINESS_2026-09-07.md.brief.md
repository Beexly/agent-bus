# docs/ops/LAUNCH_READINESS_2026-09-07.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026-09-07 launch-readiness audit (provenance-marked claims) of the Sports pick pipeline: measured pick-grading correctness against ESPN ground truth, plus a ranked list of 13 launch blockers. Verdict was NO GO: picks are largely right, but the data layer beside them (corrupt game rows, pre-kickoff grading, unauditable history) is not trustworthy.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Ground-truth audit method: picks re-derived against ESPN event id (not team-and-time). Reported numbers: **68 wrong moneyline results out of 590 checkable**; **187 of 670 checkable picks sit on a game row carrying another fixture's final** (MLB 34.4%, NFL 0 of 66); **1,529 of 2,199 settled published picks = 69.5% carry no ESPN event id** and cannot be checked at all. Published ECE value **0.0524 is flagged untrustworthy**: C-114 records 63 moneyline rows graded before kickoff inside the sample; those must be re-graded or excluded. Corruption control: in the CORRECT cohort, corruption was 39.6% under 5h vs 50.0% at 5h+ (flat/inverted) — corrupt baseline cannot explain the timing signal (unexplained). Remediation populations: 87 settled-before-kickoff + 148 soccer two-way moneylines + 352 rows = **585 unique rows** (not 586/587; 87+148+352=587 minus exactly 2 overlap rows). C-92 fix: update scoped to `result: "PENDING"`, slate writes MONEYLINE only, so of 585 remediation rows only **4** were reachable; 70 published PENDING moneylines could be silently re-published after operator withdrawal. C-143: displayed `pick.line` vs graded `selectGradingLine` (prefers `clvLockLine`); 432 of 588 differing measured, 46 outcome-flipping. Causal chain: wrong score written → `SCORE_MISMATCH_CROSS_PATH` refuses overwrite of existing final (correct guard) → wrong score permanent (six verified-wrong rows frozen 16h to 1d17h while settle cycles ran several times hourly). Missing capability: authenticated correction lane; the writer may be mis-attributed (dead `!conflicts` gate at settle-sport.ts:552 makes paid lane unreachable — narrowing is [A], unverified).
## Data sources named
ESPN event ids (ground truth), production read-only SQL, ESPN public scoreboard, the paid odds lane, CodeRabbit review (#719), ledger/triage agents [A].
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Two failure modes separated by grading time: graded AFTER kickoff → pick correct, row corrupted later (C-115); graded BEFORE kickoff → pick graded off whatever the row held, right/wrong by coincidence (C-114, measured 3-of-4 correct — coin flip against a wrong score). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- One confirmed mis-graded published pick: `cmtp1qor6052dx2r51abbm3ik`, MONEYLINE "Seattle Mariners ML" v5.2.7 on `espn:mlb:401816839`, settled 18.09h BEFORE first pitch, stored LOSS; ESPN true final Mariners 2-0 → true result WIN. [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- Corrupt game rows verified by ESPN event id: 401816824 (true Mariners 2-6 Athletics, row shows 6-7), 401816839 (true Mariners 2-0 Athletics, row shows 6-7), 401816841 (true Dodgers 7-5 Nationals, row shows 5-3). [TRUST-SIGNAL]
- 87 picks settled before kickoff: 3 of 4 checked against ESPN are CORRECT — correct instrument is re-grade, not unpublish (agent recommendation superseded). [TRUST-SIGNAL]

- Seven consecutive review rounds each found a defect in the previous round's work; three queued "ready" remediations would each have caused the damage they meant to repair (C-147 unpublishing rows still shown published, C-145 unpublishing legitimate postponement VOIDs, C-114 unpublishing correct results).

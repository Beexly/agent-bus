# data-sources/research/2026-09-17/dossiers/dossier-v2-methods.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Methods literature dossier v2 (2026-09-17) giving implementation-grade summaries of seven statistical methods for the Elo-based prediction engine (EP, CPOE, DVOA/DAVE, fourth-down WP, turnover regression, pressure stability, EPA predictive validity), each with verification status (VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED/PARTIAL) and an engine implementation takeaway.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **EP (Yurko et al. 2018, VERIFIED):** multinomial logistic regression over 7 scoring events (TD +7, FG +3, safety +2, no score 0, opp safety −2, opp FG −3, opp TD −7); 6 logits vs no-score baseline; EP = probability-weighted sum. Covariates: down, seconds remaining in half, yardline, log(yards to go), goal-to-go, under-two-minutes + 3 interactions. Observation weighting by score differential and drives-until-next-score; model selected by LOSO calibration error (final e ≈ 0.013). Kick overrides: 2-pt attempts flat 47.35% (0.947 EP); XP/FG = GAM on kick distance only; missed-FG correction 8 yards + 5.07 s. Current nflfastR `ep_model` is XGBoost (exact features UNVERIFIED).
- **CPOE:** play-level `cpoe = complete_pass − cp`; aggregation = mean residual. Current nflfastR `cp_model` is XGBoost (features UNVERIFIED). Low-attempt aggregates need shrinkage toward zero.
- **DVOA/DAVE (Schatz/FTN 2026, VERIFIED):** per-play value vs situational baseline, first-down-progress weighting, opponent adjustments; all fumbles counted equally. DAVE after 2026 Week 1: 83% preseason forecast (offense), 98% (defense/special teams). Early-season opponent adjustments: 50% prior-year opp pass-offense, 30% run offense, 20% opp defense. Preseason inputs: 3 prior DVOA seasons, starting-QB projection, regression to mean, personnel, coaching experience, draft history, OL age/tenure, injuries.
- **Fourth-down (nfl4th):** recommend argmax of WP(go) vs WP(FG) vs WP(punt). Go model excludes turnover returns; FG model ignores kicker identity, outdoor weather, blocked-return outcomes. Literature corrections: selection bias (Heckman, Daly-Grafstein 2023), yardline-rounding inflates aggressiveness (Lopez 2020), WP policies understate uncertainty (Brill–Yurko–Wyner 2023). Conservative coaching costs ~0.4 wins/year (Yam & Lopez 2019); coaches optimize low quantiles of the outcome distribution.
- **Turnover regression:** Bock 2017 GBM next-play model (features: drive, quarter, down, time, field position, yards to go, drive yards, play type, score differential, run/pass location, prior-play outcome); event rates under 3% INT/pass, under 1% defense-recovered fumble/run. Stuart 1990–2012: fumble-recovery year-to-year correlation 0.00 (own) / −0.02 (opponent); top-20 recovery teams 75.4% → 50.4% next year. Burke 2007: recovery ratio not significant for points/wins.
- **Pressure stability:** PFF — pressure-to-sack conversion R² < 0.005 with pressure level; Alexander 26% (2016) → 14.6% (2017–19). STRAIN: half-to-half r = 0.8545; early STRAIN → future pressure r = 0.3217 vs 0.0965 for prior pressure rate; concurrent r = 0.6255.
- **EPA predictive validity:** expected-wins repro — off passing efficiency corr with wins 0.61/0.59/0.53 vs rushing 0.18/0.19/0.13; def passing −0.47/−0.45/−0.49 vs def rushing −0.04. PFF hot-start: off EPA r = 0.57, def EPA allowed r = 0.313; scripted off 0.26 / def 0.16 vs non-scripted 0.51 / 0.29; scripted splits had 10× less model importance. Zhou 2026: success rate stability r ≈ 0.60, passing EPA → future point differential r ≈ 0.42. Stability ≠ predictive relevance.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Three most implementation-relevant findings (dossier's own handoff): (1) validate EPA/WP features with drive- or game-grouped splits, never random play splits (label leakage); (2) split sacks into pressure generation × QB-conditioned conversion; (3) decompose turnovers into occurrence vs ~50%-prior recovery.
- Passing EPA dominates team strength: 0.53–0.61 vs 0.13–0.19 rushing correlation gap — "the single most actionable number in this dossier."
- DAVE's offense/defense asymmetry (83%/98%) independently corroborates PFF's EPA stability asymmetry (0.57/0.313).
- Thin topics flagged: exact nflfastR CPOE/EP XGBoost specs, full DVOA formula, peer-reviewed EPA forward-validation, precise luck/talent split for turnover differential.

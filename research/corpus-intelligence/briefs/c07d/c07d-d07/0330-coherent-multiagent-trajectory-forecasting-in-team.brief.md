# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0330-coherent-multiagent-trajectory-forecasting-in-team.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A deep-read ledger note on Teoh's CausalTraj (arXiv:2511.18248v2), a temporally-causal likelihood-based multi-agent trajectory forecaster that factorizes p(X_{P+1:T} | X_{1:P}) per timestep and emits a mixture-of-Gaussians over joint displacements — evaluated on per-agent AND joint metrics (minADE/minFDE vs minJADE/minJFDE) across NBA SportVU, Basketball-U, and Football-U (NFL Big Data Bowl) benchmarks. Verdict recorded: **ADAPT** — port the causal MoG forecasting stack and the joint-metric evaluation protocol to NFL tracking for route/defender trajectory prediction, with richer player–ball covariance than their block-diagonal assumption.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Causal factorization: `p(XP+1:T | X1:P) = ∏_{t=P}^{T−1} p(Xt+1 | X1:t)`; predicts per-timestep displacements `ΔXt+1 = Xt+1 − Xt`; training parallelized in teacher-forcing fashion; inference autoregressive `X̂t+1 = X̂t + ΔX̂t+1`.
- Architecture: (1) agent history encoder — Causal PointNet (MLP → Lb-MaxPool → Concat → MLP → Lb-MaxPool → MLP, causal via zero-padding/sliding-window) OR Mamba2 (2-layer MLP projector; 3 Mamba2 layers; dmodel 64, dstate 128, dconv 4, expansion 4, head dim 16, chunk size 32); (2) agent embedding — 3 learned vectors (2 teams + ball) concatenated + MLP; (3) inter-agent relation encoder — per-timestep 4 transformer blocks (dmodel 128, 8 heads, dff 512, no cross-temporal attention) + 4 Spatial Relation Transformer Encoder blocks (dmodel 128, 8 heads, dff 256) injecting mesh tensor `Mt[q,k] = [xq,t − xk,t ; zq,t ; zk,t]`; (4) scene aggregation — agentwise 1-layer MLP (dim 64) on concatenated features + position/velocity; all agents concatenated per timestep; 3-layer MLP (dim 768) → head (dim 448) emitting MoG params.
- Output: mixture of M=8 Gaussians per timestep: `p(ΔXt+1 | X1:t) = Σ_{m=1}^{M} πt+1,m N(ΔXt+1 ; μt+1,m , Σt+1,m)`; per-agent 2×2 covariance via Cholesky `Lt,m,n = [[exp(ℓt,m,n^(11)), 0], [ℓt,m,n^(21), exp(ℓt,m,n^(22))]]`, `Σ̂t,m,n = Lt,m,n L⊤_{t,m,n}`; block-diagonal across agents within each component (joint structure only via shared mixture weights π).
- Loss (verbatim): `LNLL = − Et log ( Σ_{m=1}^{M} π̂t,m N(Yt ; μ̂t,m , Σ̂t,m) )`; `Lent = − (1/log M) Σ_{m=1}^{M} π̂t,m log(π̂t,m + ε)`; `L = LNLL − λent Lent`, λent = 0.05; log-sum-exp normalization in log space; double precision for log/matrix ops; velocity (current-step displacement) as auxiliary input; AdamW + OneCycle, max LR 0.02, weight decay 0.01; 3.0M params (Causal PointNet), 3.2M (Mamba2) for NBA; 20 scenario samples at inference.
- Metric definitions (verbatim): `minADEk = (1/N) Σ_i min_j (1/F) Σ_{t=P+1}^{T} ‖x̂j_{i,t} − xi,t‖`; `minFDEk = (1/N) Σ_i min_j ‖x̂j_{i,T} − xi,T‖`; `minJADEk = min_j (1/NF) Σ_i Σ_t ‖x̂j_{i,t} − xi,t‖`; `minJFDEk = min_j (1/N) Σ_i ‖x̂j_{i,T} − xi,T‖`.

## Data sources named
- NBA SportVU: NBA player-movement logs (github.com/linouk23/NBA-Player-Movements); 30 frames at 5 Hz, 10 players + ball (N=11); first 10 frames context, predict next 20; foot→metre scaling factor 28/94.
- Basketball-U (Xu & Fu 2025): derived from the NBA dataset (Zhan et al. 2019); 50-frame sequences; predict final 20.
- Football-U (Xu & Fu 2025): built from NFL Big Data Bowl (github.com/nfl-football-ops/Big-Data-Bowl); 50-frame samples at 10 Hz, 22 players + ball; predict final 20; results in yards.
- Baselines re-evaluated: GroupNet (CVPR'22), LED (CVPR'23), MoFlow (CVPR'25; default + joint-objective variant reproduced from official open-source implementation), Sports-Traj (ICLR'25).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- NBA SportVU (metres, minADE20/minFDE20 per-agent; minJADE20/minJFDE20 joint):
  - 1.0 s per-agent: CausalTraj Mamba2 **0.14/0.20** (best), C-PointNet 0.15/0.21, MoFlow 0.18/0.25.
  - 4.0 s per-agent: MoFlow 0.71/0.87 (best); CausalTraj Mamba2 0.77/1.02; LED 0.81/1.10; GroupNet 0.95/1.22; MoFlow joint-obj 0.89/1.32.
  - 4.0 s joint: CausalTraj Mamba2 **1.38/2.57** (best), C-PointNet 1.34/2.47; LED 1.63/2.99; MoFlow 1.69/3.31; GroupNet 2.12/3.72. Joint metrics beat all baselines at all horizons (e.g., 2.0 s: 0.62/1.21 vs MoFlow 0.80/1.61).
- Basketball-U (metres, 20-frame): per-agent — CausalTraj Mamba2 0.56/0.71 vs MoFlow 0.50/0.61; joint — CausalTraj Mamba2 **0.97/1.77** vs MoFlow 1.18/2.30, MoFlow joint-obj 1.21/2.34, Sports-Traj 1.52/2.62.
- Football-U (yards, 20-frame): per-agent — MoFlow 0.16/0.27 (best), CausalTraj Mamba2 0.16/0.31; joint — CausalTraj Mamba2 **1.12/2.68** vs MoFlow 1.16/2.82, MoFlow joint-obj 1.19/2.87, Sports-Traj 3.66/3.46.
- Ablation (Basketball-U 20-frame joint): minJADE20 0.97 (full) → 0.99 (no SRTE) → 1.03 (single Gaussian) → 1.05 (component-mean sampling); minJFDE20 1.77 → 1.81 → 1.86 → 2.13. The MoFlow joint-objective variant improved averageJADE but barely moved min-based joint metrics — loss changes alone don't learn joint structure.
- Qualitative: coordinated directional changes, straight fast ball passes, strategic positional allocation vs. baseline homogeneity; failure modes: ball carrying with unrealistically large ball–player gap, ball–boundary collisions — traced by the reader directly to the block-diagonal covariance (player–ball coupling is the weak point; exactly what NFL ball-carrier/receiver–defender use cases need).
- Limitations: block-diagonal covariance (zero cross-agent covariance within a component); min-of-20 best-of-k metrics flatter all models (no calibration/coverage metrics); short horizons (4.0 s at 5 Hz NBA, 2.0 s at 10 Hz Football-U — NFL route development/QB decision-making spans 2.5–4+ s at 10 Hz, untested); no temporal-split validation reported; no CIs; MoFlow joint-obj numbers reproduced, not author-reported; single independent researcher, no independent replication yet (mitigated by public code).
- Code: https://github.com/wezteoh/causaltraj; project page https://causaltraj.github.io.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **OTHER (tracking lane / NGS replacement — most direct)**: CausalTraj is the joint-forecasting + joint-metric (minJADE/minJFDE) evaluation protocol the NGS-replacement spec lacks for its predictive components. Serves the tracking lane and machine-discovery program: (i) **route prediction** — forecast WR/TE post-snap trajectories for separation-at-catch modeling feeding CPOE/separation features into the props/pick engine; (ii) **ghosting** — compare actual defender trajectories against the joint model's expected coverage distribution to grade coverage busts (feeds matchup grades, complementing CoverageIQ cards from the 2026-09-20 tables); (iii) scenario simulation for 2-pt/4th-down win-probability extensions.
- **QB-BEHAVIOR**: The causal per-timestep MoG likelihood factorization gives a natural uncertainty representation over QB-scramble/escape trajectories — joint future distributions over the QB plus nearest rushers would feed an expected-sack/expected-scramble feature. Requires the ball-coupling fix (the paper's own failure mode is ball–carrier decoupling); mark as ADAPT-with-modification, UNCERTAIN until horizons extend past 2.0 s at 10 Hz.
- **SCHEME**: Joint coherent futures are the right primitive for modeling offensive scheme as multi-agent coordination — the reader notes qualitative "strategic positional allocation" in generations. A CausalTraj port conditioned on play-type/formation embeddings could quantify how often a defense's realized joint trajectory falls outside the model's expected coverage distribution — a coverage-bust/disguise detector, which serves the scheme-recognition side of the coaching-tendency program. UNCERTAIN: asserted by extension, not demonstrated in the paper.

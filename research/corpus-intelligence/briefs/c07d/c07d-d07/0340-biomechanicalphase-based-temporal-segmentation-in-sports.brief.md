# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0340-biomechanicalphase-based-temporal-segmentation-in-sports.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A deep-read ledger note on Badatya et al. (arXiv:2509.24606v1), a two-stage unsupervised temporal segmentation method for sports video (demo on javelin throw): an Adaptive ST-GCN denoising autoencoder learns a latent pose representation, then structured unbalanced optimal transport over latent phase prototypes assigns per-frame phases (steps / drive / throw / recovery), closed with a pseudo-label cross-entropy loop. Verdict recorded: **ADAPT** — the ASTGCN-denoise + structured-OT pipeline is a transferable recipe for unsupervised play-phase segmentation in NFL film, but must be rebuilt without the paper's phase-count oracle (K) and validated on NFL play segments, not adopted as-is.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Stage 1 — ASTGCN denoising encoder: 3 Adaptive ST-GCN blocks with Chebyshev filters of order 7 and 64-d features → MLP projecting to 40-d latent space. Gaussian noise (σ=0.1) injected as denoising objective. Adam: lr 1e-4 (encoder), lr 1e-3 (MLP), weight decay 1e-4. Temporal window 30. Training samples one frame from each of five temporal bins per video.
- Stage 2 — structured unbalanced OT: visual cost = cosine distance between latent embeddings; objective = fused Gromov-Wasserstein temporal-structure term + Kantorovich appearance-cost term + KL relaxation toward a prior class distribution. Final frame label = rowwise argmax of transport plan. Pseudo-label cross-entropy loss (labels from the transport plan) closes the unsupervised loop. K (number of phases) fixed to the known ground-truth phase count.
- Denoising objective (verbatim): `L_total = L_MSE + λ_vel L_vel` (L_MSE pose-reconstruction MSE; L_vel velocity-consistency loss; exact λ_vel not stated in the extracted text).
- Assumptions: (1) K known in advance; (2) phases temporally ordered and contiguous (GW temporal structure); (3) KL prior over class proportions approximately correct; (4) MMPose skeleton quality sufficient — pose errors treated as absorbable noise.
- Metrics: mAP, F1, mIoU, MoF against manual phase annotations. Baselines: TOT, TOT+TCL, CTE, ASOT. Evaluation mapping via global Hungarian alignment across the entire dataset.

## Data sources named
- New javelin dataset: 211 videos (111 men / 100 women) from major competitions; poses via MMPose as 16-joint MPII skeletons; four manually annotated phases (evaluation only): steps, drive, throw, recovery.
- Dataset released: https://github.com/Bikudebug/Javelin_Throw_Dataset. Frame counts / per-phase durations not stated. Train/test split sizes and proportions not stated; subject-disjointness not stated. Model code: not stated (None).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Test results: Ours **71.02 mAP, 74.61 F1, 48.01 mIoU, 64.04 MoF**. Baselines (same order): TOT 33.32/33.70/20.21/34.90; TOT+TCL 39.30/50.03/41.32/50.87; CTE 54.82/62.74/50.58/63.42; ASOT 60.20/57.55/41.80/61.19.
- mIoU (48.01) markedly lower than F1 (74.61) → boundary-localization weakness even where frame-level classification is strong; problematic for timing-sensitive NFL uses (snap timing, release time). No CIs or significance tests.
- Limitations flagged: **K oracle** — true number of phases required as input (never tested with unknown K; hard blocker for NFL film where phase inventory is unknown); global Hungarian alignment across the entire dataset at evaluation is an offline transductive step overstating per-video operational accuracy; split sizes/subject-disjointness unstated (same athletes may appear in train+test on a 211-video dataset); pose-pipeline dependency — MMPose errors on occluded/fast frames propagate, "denoising" claim not ablated vs. pose quality; single sport/single movement, no quantitative cross-sport transfer (generality asserted, not shown); javelin is single-athlete/fixed-camera/closed-skill vs NFL's 22-player/multi-camera/open-skill — temporal-structure prior may not transfer.
- GSE-relevant architecture values: ASTGCN (3 blocks, Chebyshev order 7, 64-d → 40-d latent), σ=0.1 denoising noise, window 30, five temporal bins/video sampling, Adam 1e-4/1e-3, wd 1e-4.

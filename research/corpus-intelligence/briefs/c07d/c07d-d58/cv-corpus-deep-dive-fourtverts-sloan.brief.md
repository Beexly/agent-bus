## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Full read of the 9-page (~349 lines) MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference 2018 paper by Omar Ajmeri & Ali Shah (Paper ID #5571, "Other Sports" track): using computer vision + ML to classify NFL game film and build a player tracking system from All-22 screenshots at 5 fps. The file extracts three narrow reusable kernels (rotation deskew preconditioner, per-frame yard-scale cross-check, reference-point drift correction) plus test assertions, an implementation spec against `packages/prediction-engine/src/tracking/`, and explicit confidence ratings.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas verbatim from the file:
- **Rotation deskew (reconstructed):** let v_axis = unit direction vector of the detected field-axis line in image coordinates; canonical field long axis e_x = (1, 0); camera roll angle **φ = arccos(v_axis · e_x)**, sign from cross product (v_axis × e_x)_z; deskew rotates the image by −φ about its center. Pair-average with (φ from LOS − 90°); file flags the exact operands of "arccos(x)" as [RECONSTRUCTED] (paper only names the function and the two lines). Confidence: MEDIUM.
- **Per-frame yard calibration:** yard lines painted every 5 yards; detect adjacent full-field lines, measure pixel separation d_5yd; **px_per_yard = d_5yd / 5**, recomputed per frame. In the worked example ~82 distance-units per yard. GSE cross-check: extract local scale of fitted homography H at image center (‖∂(xM,yM)/∂(xPx,yPx)‖ → meters per pixel, invert to px per meter, ×0.9144 → px per yard) and assert agreement with measured px_per_yard within tolerance (start: 10% [DERIVED]). Disagreement ⇒ reject H.
- **Euclidean speed:** `d(p, q) = √((q1 − p1)² + (q2 − p2)²)`. Worked example (DeSean Jackson top speed): 215.5 distance-units over 0.2 s (5 fps) × (1 yard / ~82 d) = **12.8 yd/s**; ×3 ft/yd = 38.4 ft/s; /5280 ft/mi × 3600 s/hr = **26.2 mph**. Paper notes this reads HIGH vs. Next Gen Stats — recommends relative comparison across players, not absolute values. Acceleration application: first 0.6 s off the line — Jackson **22.4 mph** vs. Harris **19.3 mph** (finite-difference velocity).
- **Formation classifier results:** CART best — **86.5%** QB position classification (Center/Shotgun/Pistol; Pistol precision **.50**/recall **.12** on tiny samples); **72.3%** formation accuracy over **29 classes**; 500+ auto-tagged formation images.
- Pipeline steps: screenshots at **5 fps**; Hough Lines → heuristic filtering (paper does not publish heuristic details — [RECONSTRUCTED] as "longest lines spanning the frame"); LOS = detected full-field line nearest the offensive line's pixel mass; NFL logo removal via fixed-position mask; jersey-color player detection (burgundy for Washington).
- Test assertions from the implementation spec: (1) deskew accuracy — synthetic field rotated exactly 12° (plus noise lines), `estimateRollAngle` recovers φ within **0.5°**, after deskew all true yard lines within **±2°** of vertical; (2) yard-scale calibration — synthetic with known 96 px per 5-yard spacing → `calibrateYardScale` returns 19.2 px/yard within **5%**; (3) scale cross-check catches bad H — fit H on deliberately mislabeled yard lines (shifted one 5-yard interval); small reprojection on fitting points but `validateHomographyScale` rejects (scale mismatch > tolerance); (4) shadow robustness — synthetic frame with shadow gradient halving V in part of field; pipeline exposes `lightingVariance` metric and *flags* the frame rather than returning skewed lines silently.
- Play-by-play merge: LOS standardized to 1–99 scale (own 20 = 80).
- No p-values, R², confidence intervals.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Relevance to GSE gap (c) rated **MEDIUM** — this is All-22 film (fixed high sideline camera), not broadcast; geometry assumptions do not transfer wholesale.
- Three extractable kernels in priority order: (1) rotation deskew via arccos as a cheap preconditioner before Chung's angle filtering — tightens filter tolerances from ±12°+ to ±6–8°, cutting false-line acceptance roughly proportionally; (2) per-frame px/yard from 5-yard line spacing as an **independent, label-free cross-check** on any fitted homography — the only label-free H validation in the corpus besides Mendez's warp overlay, and the two compose (photometric + geometric); (3) frame-1 reference-point drift correction (subtract a stable field landmark's displacement before measuring player motion = 2D translation-only camera-motion compensation) — ancestor pattern of the desired temporal landmark smoothing.
- Documented limitations (§5.2): jersey-color tracking breaks under shadows/sunlight RGB variance — ANY color-threshold stage (including HSV white filter) inherits this; white paint in shadow shifts hue/saturation; mitigation = adaptive thresholds or per-frame white-point normalization, never one hard-coded HSV range. Small training sample (500+ images); accuracy expected to scale with data.
- What does NOT transfer: LOS-from-O-line-proximity assumes All-22's clean horizontal O-line band — on broadcast sideline views the O-line is a foreshortened cluster; use play-context LOS instead (as in the Chung spec). Jersey-color player finder is irrelevant to gap (c) and carries the documented RGB-variance fragility.
- Improvement path items: (1) log φ per frame — sudden φ jumps are a free shot-change detector (reset temporal smoothing there); (2) adaptive white filtering via per-frame white-point estimation; (3) replace single frame-1 reference point with 2–3 persistent landmarks (yard-line intersections) tracked across the shot, reprojection drift as running H quality metric; (4) speed sanity harness — reuse the d(p,q)/fps → yd/s → mph chain as unit-test oracle for tracklet velocities; synthetic constant-velocity tracklet must recover speed within **5%**.
- Future work listed in paper: defensive formations, kickoff/punt, RFID.
- Confidence ratings: HIGH on pipeline structure, 5 fps, calibration, drift correction, speed math, 26.2 mph example, classifier tables, limitations; MEDIUM on arccos reconstruction and heuristic details; HIGH on All-22-vs-broadcast transfer caveat.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **Rotation deskew + tighter angle filters (OTHER/tracking lane):** the mechanism is that a one-pass arccos deskew makes the yard-line/hash-line orientation filters tight (±6–8° instead of ±12°+), cutting false-line acceptance proportionally with zero new detectors — this hardens the CV tracking lane that will eventually produce QB kinematics, but it is an indirect dependency, not a direct QB-behavioral input.
- **Per-frame px/yard as label-free H validation (OTHER/tracking lane):** mechanism — the fitted homography's local scale must agree with independently measured line-spacing within 10% [DERIVED], or the H is rejected; this catches bad fits that pass reprojection on mislabeled points, which protects downstream tracklet quality that the QB-behavioral profiles consume. This is the only label-free geometric H check in the corpus (composes with Mendez's photometric warp overlay).
- **Jersey-color fragility under shadows/sunlight (OTHER):** CONTRADICTION-adjacent caution — multiple corpus sources (this paper §5.2, the-playmakers LAB color approach, "sensitive to lighting, logos, end zones") independently converge on color-based identification breaking under lighting variance; any engine lane relying on color-thresholded vision must carry adaptive white-point normalization, not fixed HSV ranges. Serves the tracking lane only, not behavioral profiling directly.
- **φ-jump shot-change detection (OTHER):** logging the per-frame roll angle gives a free shot-change detector (sudden φ jumps → reset temporal smoothing) — a process kernel for the CV lane.
- **Speed sanity harness (OTHER/tracking lane):** the d(p,q)/fps → yd/s → mph chain with the 5% recovery tolerance is a unit-test oracle for tracklet velocities (gap b lane) — direct support for the velocity features that feed QB movement profiles.
- The 86.5%/72.3% formation classifiers are filed as context, not gap (c): "GSE's formation lane is separate."

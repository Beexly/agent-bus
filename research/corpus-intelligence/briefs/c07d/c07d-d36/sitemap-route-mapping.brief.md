# design/redesign-2026-09/sitemap-route-mapping.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Input for the founder's redesign brief (`8970241d-claudedesignprompt.md`), deliverable §9: a sitemap and route-to-destination mapping for all 236 `page.tsx` files under `apps/web/app`, each with purpose, access level, and whether its destination is an explicit brief citation or a stated judgment call — plus a mermaid sitemap diagram, a mobile navigation proposal, and 10 open decisions.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **236 routes total**, counted by `find apps/web/app -name page.tsx` (matching the brief's count). Method: URL path = file path minus `apps/web/app` and `/page.tsx`; no route groups in parentheses; purpose from each file's `metadata.title`, first `<h1>`, `<Shell title="...">` prop, or code comment (checked with grep, not full reads); purpose stated plainly where none gave a clear answer.
- **Destination breakdown:** Today 8; Redirect/merge 19; How it works 21; Fantasy 20; Stats 68; Utility rail 4; Footer-only 20; Internal 75 (`/admin/*` 40 + `/cockpit/*` 35); Home 1. Total 236.
- **Record merge composition:** 17 routes merge into Record (a new page, not a route today) + `/contests` → Fantasy (already redirects to `/fantasy/contests` in live code) + `/sealed` → How it works (noindex duplicate of `/engine`). The brief's §4.2 names nine explicitly (`/performance`, `/calibration`, `/clv`, `/proof`, `/ledger`, `/verify`, `/accountability`, `/performance/losses`, `/kill-ledger`); this mapping adds three child routes (`/calibration/market`, `/performance/losses/[id]`, `/verify/slate/opening`) and five more the app's own code treats as cluster members (`/changelog` from `/accountability`'s source comment; `/track`, `/track/platform` from `/calibration`'s routing comment; `/glass-ledger`, `/vault` — both noindex, duplicating ledger-style content). 9 + 3 + 5 = 17.
- **Access taxonomy (what the code actually does):** public; public with server-side entitlement gating (rule 3: no frontend-only paywalls); admin (`session.user.role === "ADMIN"`); internal cockpit (gated by `apps/web/app/cockpit/layout.tsx`, same admin check); legal/compliance.
- **Mobile nav proposal:** bottom tab bar, five tabs always visible in this order — Today (maps to `/picks`; fuller board at `/board`), Record, How it works, Fantasy, Stats; outline icons, single consistent set (brief §5); no numeral badges (brief bans hype); tapping the active tab scrolls to top. Utility rail in a sheet from a top-bar icon: Pricing, Sign in (becomes Account post sign-in), Search — sheet dismissible by swipe/close control/Escape, never traps focus (brief §7).
- **Formulas:** none. "No performance numbers appear anywhere in this document" (stated in the file itself).

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **QB-BEHAVIOR:** The sitemap inventories live QB intelligence surfaces the engine feeds: `/intelligence/qb-forward` (redirect into the engines hub) and the Player Lab's `/players/qbr` view. Mechanism: these are the public endpoints of whatever QB-forward signals the engine produces; they are the surfaces where QB-behavioral intake (CPOE-grade metrics) would surface as rankings — consistent with the 9/28 doctrine that the public shows only projections/rankings.
- **OL:** `/stats/trenches` (Trenches: Line Play & Pressure Context), `/players/trenches` (Player Lab trenches view), and `/intelligence/rushing-contact` are live line-play intelligence surfaces. Mechanism: these routes are where OL/pressure/rushing-contact signals the engine computes would be exposed as public rankings — and the 2026-10-01 CV watch loop (trenches monitoring) has a named public surface family to land on.
- **TRUST-SIGNAL:** `/stats/expert-board` (Tracked Analyst Signals) plus the admin surfaces `/admin/statking/experts` (Expert Registry), `/admin/statking/expert-signals`, `/admin/statking/source-trust` (Source Trust), `/admin/statking/signal-calibration` (Signal Calibration), and `/admin/statking/rights` (Rights Ledger) are the trust-signal program's intake and governance stack. Mechanism: expert signals and source-trust scores feed the engine's trust-target intake; the Record-merge cluster (performance, calibration, clv, proof, ledger, verify, accountability, losses, kill-ledger) is where the honest record publishes — the public side of the calibration evidence's "collecting" state.
- **SCHEME:** `/fantasy/scheme` (Scheme Intelligence) and `/stats/teams` (Team Environments: Pace, Offense & Defense) plus `/stats/depth` (Depth Charts: Role & Opportunity by Team) are live scheme/environment surfaces. Mechanism: scheme intake (pace, offensive/defensive environment) feeds projections surfaced here — open decision #7 flags the `/intelligence/*` vs Fantasy split (waiver-trends, qb-forward) as not clean, so scheme-related signals may straddle both clusters.
- **OTHER — media/noise intake:** `/stats/media` (+6 sub-routes: podcasts, reddit, rss, signals, trending, youtube), `/the-beat` (Reliability-Tiered Newsroom), `/gsn`, `/airwave` (The Airwave Ledger: Pundits, On the Record), `/stats/media/signals` (Cross-Source Player Buzz) are the media-noise intake surfaces feeding the GSN narrative layer — the "media noise → graded record" pipeline.
- **OTHER — Record as calibration home:** The new Record destination (17 routes collapsing) is the single public accountability surface the calibration evidence doc's consequences presuppose — PERFORMANCE_STATS/LIVE_BOARD/PUBLISH_LEDGER closed, honest "collecting" state published, decision autopsies public.
- **UNCERTAIN:** `/fantasy/props` is "Pick'em Edge (props tool)" in the mapping, but the pick'em lane was darked by the founder ("get rid of the pick them for right now" — five intakes env-gated/default-off, code kept). The sitemap file predates or ignores that decision; whether `/fantasy/props` stays live, env-gated, or dark is UNCERTAIN and needs reconciliation with the current pick'em-dark status.

# design/GALAXY_2026_PUBLIC_WORLD.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Single source of truth for the Galaxy Sports Edge (GSE) + Galaxy Sports Network (GSN) public experience: the market-noise-resolving-into-signal visual metaphor, semantic palette and motion doctrine, the public IA (world modules/routes), the homepage's 10-chapter order, trust-safe copy law, the test-pinned homepage contract, and the owner's 2026-06-11 doctrine (LAW) overriding everything above — including "don't show our hand" vendor anonymity.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- **Color usage ratio:** ~80% void/nebula dark, ~10% cyan, ~6% magenta, ~3% white, ~1% amber/red.
- **Semantic palette (verbatim tokens):** void black `#050608` (unknown space, market depth); cosmic dark `#0D1117` (page canvas); deep nebula purple `--ultraviolet` `#7A5CFF` (model layer, atmospheric depth); electric cyan `--orbital-cyan` `#00E5FF` (verified signal, clarity, model alignment); hot magenta `--plasma` `#FF2DD6` (volatility, market heat, public distortion, media noise); ice white `--ion-white` `#F6F7FA` (canonical truth, headlines, final readouts); amber `--caution` `#FFB454` (caution, incomplete data, review needed); red `--alert` `#FF6470` (hard gate, trust failure, stop).
- **Owner-dictated surface tokens (violet-shifted, no grey):** carbon `#100D1D`, eclipse `#171228`, titanium `#211A33`, mineral `#3B3158`, mineral-hi `#4D4175` — "keep luminance when adjusting."
- **Motion budgets:** hover motion ≤ 200ms; ambient motion ≥ 8s loops, low amplitude; nothing flickers; `CinematicEntrance` is the only full-screen takeover — never add a second; essential content never animation-gated; global `prefers-reduced-motion` kill-switch in `globals.css` stays and every new `gw-*` animation must be listed in its reduced-motion overrides.
- **No performance/accuracy numbers appear** in this document (it is design doctrine, not analytics).
- **Formulas:** none; "not specified" for the engine sense.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
1. **Thesis:** "The market is full of noise. Galaxy turns it into signal." Supporting doctrine lines: "The edge is not the pick. The edge is knowing what not to trust."; "No-Bet is not absence. It is intelligence."; "Every edge earns a receipt."; "Sometimes the sharpest pick is no pick."; "The board is only as smart as the data behind it." (test-pinned — must remain in `app/page.tsx`); canonical closer "We detect. You decide." (`CLOSING_LINE` in `lib/brand.ts`).
2. **Two faces:** GSE = the decision engine (Board, odds, market gravity, no-bet gating, evidence trails, decision autopsies, calibration, receipts); GSN = the media intelligence layer (Airwave ledger, paraphrased claims, entity tags, review gates, studio briefs, the transmission). GSE produces evidence; GSN narrates and audits it.
3. **Visual metaphor grammar (fragment → signal):** odds fragments → drifting mono-type shards/dashed strokes; public pressure → magenta distortion, gravity-bent lines; injury & context → amber flags on nodes; media noise → scattered low-opacity text fragments; model disagreement → split/forked strokes; price movement → gravity lines bending toward a node. Five canonical outputs: signal beam (cyan vertical/convergent beam), market map / Galaxy Twin (node constellation with gravity lines), edge window (cyan ring/window around a node), no-bet gate (closing aperture / barred ring), decision receipt (white-on-dark ledger row with timestamp). "If a visual cannot be traced to this grammar, it is decoration — remove it."
4. **Fixed color semantics (never anything else):** cyan = verified signal (never marks danger); magenta = volatility/heat/distortion (never marks confirmation); amber/red are rare (caution / hard gate). Usage ratio 80/10/6/3/1. Energi pass: purple-forward nebula backgrounds (`gw-nebula`, `gw-nebula-deep`); identity chips solid saturated fills with dark ink (`gw-chip-cyan`, `gw-chip-plasma`); headline accent words only may use chrome gradients (`gw-chrome-ice`, `gw-chrome-plasma`, `gw-chrome-violet`) and text glow (`gw-text-glow-*`) — one chrome treatment per headline max. Hard rules: no raw Tailwind palette classes on public pages (tests enforce on `page.tsx`, methodology, risk-disclosure); long prose `--ion`/`--ion-1` only; no neon soup (max two accent hues per viewport-height region); no gray-on-gray SaaS wash.
5. **World modules (public IA):** Galaxy Twin / Market Observatory `/observatory` (preview on home); Board `/board` (published + gated rows); Signal Lab / Trend Lab `/trends`; No-Bet Gate (board gating + home chapter); Decision Autopsy `/performance/losses`; Parlay MRI `/parlay-mri`; GSN / Airwave Studio `/gsn`, `/airwave`; Academy `/academy`; Receipts Ledger `/performance`, `/vault`, `/ledger`; Cost of Noise calculator (home chapter).
6. **Homepage chapter order (doctrine):** 1 Hero (GSE/GSN identity, thesis, trust-safe subcopy, live board-state telemetry — real data, honest empty states — aurora + starfield); 2 Galaxy Twin (illustrative market-map schematic, labeled "illustrative system schematic"); 3 Signal vs noise (fragment field resolving into a beam + the real telemetry panels — the ten-second product test, test-pinned); 4 Market Mirage (a popular-looking pick degrading as risk layers reveal); 5 No-Bet Gate (heroic restraint + the real scored/published/gated lanes); 6 Decision Autopsy (evidence-trail preview → `/performance/losses`); 7 Parlay MRI (stacked-risk preview); 8 GSN/Airwave (noise → paraphrase → tags → review gate → studio brief; never claim live SiriusXM/scraper capture); 9 Cost of Noise calculator (educational, no profit math); 10 Receipts (source health ledger, trend targets, methodology, responsible close).
7. **GSE speaks in receipts** (every public row shows its inputs, freshness, gate status; the empty board = discipline, not failure; calibration and losses are public; the conversion promise is decision quality, never outcomes). **GSN speaks in records** (pundit claims paraphrased, timestamped, entity-tagged, human-review-gated, graded; narrative layer of the same evidence engine; until live capture is licensed and wired, all Airwave personas/claims labeled illustrative).
8. **Trust-safe copy law:** registry `apps/web/lib/trust-claims.ts` is law. Banned (test-enforced): "guaranteed", bare "lock", "sure thing", "risk-free", "easy money", "can't lose", "verified track record", "thousands of bettors", "trusted by serious bettors". Also avoid: "beat the books", "AI predicts every winner", bare "track record" (brand-voice test), fake testimonials, fake performance, fake live odds, invented matchups/teams on the homepage. Every illustrative visual carries an explicit "illustrative" label. Responsible-play helpline (`HELPLINE` in `lib/brand.ts`) stays in the footer; the responsible close (`homepage-responsible-close`) stays on the homepage.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **TRUST-SIGNAL:** The trust-claims registry, the no-bet gate as a "heroic restraint" first-class output, the decision-autopsy module, and the receipts ledger together form the trust-signal program's public apparatus. Mechanism: honesty is instrumented — empty boards read as discipline, calibration and losses are public, every row carries inputs/freshness/gate status — which is the public complement of the internal calibration floors (Brier ≤ 0.22 / ECE ≤ 0.05) and the "collecting" state from the calibration evidence doc.
- **OTHER — public/private doctrine:** The owner rule that real names and license attribution live ONLY on `/integrations` and `/nflverse` (never front-of-tool) is the public-side execution of the 9/28 doctrine (public website shows only projections and rankings; NGS and all proprietary data/metrics/methodology stay internal). Mechanism: the sitemap's deep-attribution pages are the single compliance placement for nflverse CC attribution, which pairs with the NGS ground-truth map's requirement that attribution propagate to derived outputs.
- **OTHER — engine telemetry surfaces:** The test-pinned loaders (`loadBoardState`, `loadBoardPasses`, `loadNflverseUsagePulse`, `loadPublicCalibrationReport`, `state.scoringNow`, `state.publishedToday`, `state.gatedTodayRows`) name the real-data telemetry the engine must feed to the public world; "Trend engine is ready; observations are waiting on live intake writes" is an explicit honest-empty-state contract.
- **UNCERTAIN:** The doc says "nflverse CC BY-SA attribution stays there, always" for the `/nflverse` page, while the NGS ground-truth map states the expected-metrics feature uses CC-BY-4.0 assets and excludes CC-BY-SA ones (FTN charting, participation). Not a direct contradiction — they cover different scopes (site-wide nflverse attribution vs the expected-metrics feature's subset) — but the licensing nuance on the `/nflverse` page deserves a precise legal pass before attribution strings are finalized.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Dynamic Rank Centrality (2109.13743, ADAPT): dynamic BTL with Lipschitz-smooth strengths; pipeline = time-neighborhood union graph → locally averaged win fractions → Rank Centrality transition matrix → leading left eigenvector. Stated theory: optimal window `delta* ~= T^{2/3}` giving `O(T^{-1/3})` l2 rate; static case recovers the Negahban/Chen `O(1/sqrt(Lnp))` rate. (Formulas as stated; "Lnp" verbatim from the log.)
- PLRank (1909.06722, ADAPT): Plackett–Luce loss (ListMLE) in a gradient-boosting framework with regression-tree weak learners; exact functional gradient (Eqn 9 in the paper) and leaf Newton step; multi-permutation ground-truth handling with compression. Note in log: PL loss was first proposed for horse gambling (Plackett 1975).
- 2312.13619 (ADAPT): Bradley–Terry foundations survey — odds transitivity, Luce choice axiom, reciprocity/sufficient statistics, MaxEnt/ML under retrodictive criterion, geometric minimization, Gumbel/Weibull/Fréchet discriminal processes, Rasch/Mallows/von Mises-Fisher/Cox-hazards/network formulations, Poisson scoring, sudden death, quasi-symmetry links (PageRank, fair bets, Wei-Kendall, RPI, Barker's algorithm), exponential-family synthesis, ties and multi-competitor extensions. When-BT-fails diagnostics; fast spectral approximations.
- 2401.11016 (REJECT): Plackett–Luce with independent consideration sets; consideration probabilities non-identifiable (Thm 4.1); relative bounds (Thm 4.4: `pj/(1-pj)` vs `pi/(1-pi)` from top-l ranking flips); absolute Chernoff-based bounds (Thms 5.2/6.3) given expected consideration-set-size lower bound `alpha>1` and known utilities; tightening via DAG propagation (Alg 1/2).
- 1211.5037 (REJECT): BNP Plackett–Luce via gamma process prior `G~Gamma(alpha,tau,H)`; PL as partial size-biased permutation of atoms (Eq 3); Thurstonian exponential race + inter-arrival latents Z; posterior via Palm formula (Thms 1–2); conjugate Gibbs sampler; Dirichlet process mixture of BNP-PL with Pitt–Walker atom-sharing.
- 2604.03840 (ADAPT): Elo-algorithm insights — decouples ranking model from prediction model; closed-form noise corrections; applied to six years of FIFA rankings.
- 1802.00527 (ADAPT): margin-of-victory Elo producing full point-spread distributions.
- Elo/skill-rating tuning for luck-driven hidden-information games (2512.18858, REJECT): score-based Elo variant; reported parameters K=-0.625, alpha~=-0.0032, beta=-0.012690, F1 0.7927, claimed stabilisation ~5,000 games. CONTRADICTION (internal to the paper as reported): derivation requires beta>0 yet reported optimal beta is NEGATIVE (-0.012690); discussion claims top strategies exceed 1350 but the custom-Elo table maxes at 1300.788; discussion cites Defeat Heuristic custom CV above 180% but the table gives 14.603% (256.939% appears only in traditional Elo); claims Glicko/TrueSkill comparison but shows only traditional Elo. Reader judged no reported number trustworthy.
- Gambling-on-Momentum (2211.06052, ADAPT): three regressions with match-clustered SEs — (1) outcome logit, (2) bookmaker odds, (3) bettor stakes — all on second-by-second bookmaker odds + staked volumes.

Bettor stakes: β2 = 0.127*** (0.028) — +12.7pp relative stakes on the equaliser-scorer, +35.7% at covariate means (+46.5% with pre-stakes control; +19.4pp in second half). Always-back-the-momentum ROI: −20.1% (moderate favs, 70 bets), −7.4% (moderate longshots, 50 bets), −23.3% (strong longshots, 72 bets); +0.6% for strong favs (20 bets) vs 7.9% overround. Verdict: cleanest behavioral-bias evidence in the wave; fade-the-narrative feature for GSE in-play models.

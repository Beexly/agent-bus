## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Ranked top-20 build queue derived from the 48 ADOPT verdicts in the arXiv-program corpus index, dated 2026-09-22; each entry is a proposed GSE engine capability (INVENT, CALIBRATE, DECIDE, MODEL, INFRA/INGEST buckets) with an owner and a numeric acceptance gate from the source paper's ledger.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
No full equations in the file; gates are numeric thresholds. Named methods: torch-symbolic + PySR symbolic distillation; symbolic regression; grammar-based formulaic-alpha miner ("factor zoo"); corrective feature discovery on residuals; MinervaScore statistical seal; CRPS as primary scoring rule for spreads/totals ("closed form via Φ/φ", alongside log score) vs Brier-only; calibrator stress harness (Platt, isotonic, temperature, ensembles) under temporal shift; conformal selection publish/no-publish layer; conformalized selective regression (CSR, 90%-coverage intervals with learned reject); in-play calibration evaluation via functional test; weather-aware conformal coverage; Conservative Q-Learning (CQL, 2006.04779v2) slate selection/sizing; C51 distributional-RL optimal stopping for bet timing (2105.08877v2); difference-in-differences + synthetic control; estimand-first causal SOP (balance checks, bootstrap SEs); hybrid season simulation (per-predictor windows + incentive modifiers); ChronosX TSFM with exogenous adapters; flexBART Bayesian trees; NL-to-SQL over Neon Postgres (read-only role, statement timeout, row limits); ATB adaptive token-bucket retry/backoff.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
1. **Neural-head symbolic distillation (2602.21307v2, SymTorch)** — Owner: Motif-lab. INVENT/MODEL. Gate: distilled equation uses ≤10 terms AND matches the neural head within 0.01 MAE on held-out 2025 Weeks 1–4 AND beats the linear baseline by ≥20% MAE.
2. **PySR equation discovery on nflverse (2305.01582v3)** — Owner: Motif-lab. INVENT/MODEL. Gate: discovered equation beats the better of passer rating / QBR by ≥0.05 Pearson r on held-out 2024–2025 AND has ≤15 tree nodes.
3. **"SportsAlpha" signal miner (1601.00991v1)** — Owner: Motif-lab. INVENT/INGEST. Gate: mined factor zoo achieves mean out-of-sample |IC| ≥ 2× the hand-built baseline IC on the 2023–2025 test block AND mean pairwise |corr| ≤ 0.25.
4. **Engine residual mining (2608.05207)** — Owner: Motif-lab. INVENT/MODEL. Gate: ≥0.003 held-out NFL log-loss improvement on 2025 games vs both the RAW engine and the covariate-only corrector; no shipped corrector worse than "do nothing" on validation.
5. **MinervaScore signal validation (2608.23808v2)** — Owner: Motif-lab. INVENT/MONITOR. Gate: on permuted-label nulls, MinervaScore achieves AUROC ≥ 0.95 separating null from historically-profitable signals AND the seal's pass rate on null signals is ≤ 5%.
6. **CRPS + log-score doctrine (2504.01781)** — Owner: Mimo. CALIBRATE. Gate: model rankings by CRPS are stable across 2023/2024/2025 (Spearman ≥ 0.8 year-to-year) and CRPS-selected models also win on realized betting utility. Rationale given: Brier-only evaluation can't score full margin distributions.
7. **Uncertainty-under-shift harness (1906.02530)** — Owner: Mimo. CALIBRATE/MONITOR. Gate: harness reproduces the paper's signature pattern on GSE data (post-hoc calibrators lose their i.i.d. edge under temporal shift; ensembles most robust). No numeric threshold stated.
8. **Conformal selective prediction / no-bet gate (2603.24704)** — Owner: Mimo. DECIDE/CALIBRATE/MONITOR. Gate: walk-forward realized average loss stays within ±0.03 units of nominal across all four test seasons AND posts ≥ 70% as many picks as the baseline gate at matched realized loss. Notes it extends the `cqr.ts` repair into a full publish/no-publish system.
9. **Conformalized selective regression (2402.16300)** — Owner: Mimo. DECIDE/CALIBRATE. Gate: on held-out weeks 15–18, CSR's 90%-coverage MAE is ≥ 5% lower than the conditional-variance reject baseline at the same coverage, with empirical coverage holding.
10. **In-play calibration evaluation (2010.00781v1)** — Owner: Mimo. CALIBRATE/MONITOR. Gate: on 2024–2025 pooled games the pipeline runs end-to-end and the functional test significantly separates GSE live forecasts from coin-flip/HomeWP baselines.
11. **Weather-aware conformal coverage (2606.19642)** — Owner: Mimo. CALIBRATE/SITUATIONAL. Gate: within 30 days of simulated burn-in, empirical 90% coverage lands within ±0.02 of nominal for temperature and wind across ≥ 25/30 stadiums, with CRPS no worse than raw.
12. **Offline RL for the bet slate (2006.04779v2, CQL)** — Owner: Motif-lab. DECIDE/MODEL. Gate: on 2024 holdout the CQL policy beats the fractional-Kelly baseline by ≥2pp ROI with max drawdown no worse than baseline (within 0.5u), with the empirical lower-bound diagnostic holding.
13. **Bet timing as optimal stopping (2105.08877v2, C51)** — Owner: Motif-lab (Hermes for serving). DECIDE. Gate: on the strictly-future test window the stopping policy beats the best timing benchmark by ≥1.5pp of CLV per bet with realized ROI no worse than the "bet at open" baseline.
14. **DID + synthetic-control causal toolkit (2411.15075v1)** — Owner: Motif-lab. MODEL/SITUATIONAL. Gate: the kickoff replication passes its placebo gates; method adopted as the standard toolkit.
15. **Causal SOP / estimand-first protocol (2505.11841v2)** — Owner: Motif-lab. MODEL. Gate: the QB-injury replication passes its balance gate (all SMDs < 10%) with ATT stable across two independent season-blocks.
16. **Hybrid season simulation (2304.09918v2)** — Owner: Motif-lab (Hermes for surfacing). MODEL. Gate: pre-registered — swept window improves pooled held-out log-loss by ≥ 0.003 over the fixed window on 2019–2024, with gains in ≥ 4 of 6 seasons.
17. **Chronos/Moirai TSFM backbone (2503.12107v1, ChronosX)** — Owner: Motif-lab. MODEL. Gate: adapters beat the covariate-free fine-tuned backbone by ≥0.01 WQL on 2022–2024 AND the shuffled-covariate ablation shows no gain (proving real signal, not capacity).
18. **flexBART tabular learner (2211.04459v3)** — Owner: Motif-lab. MODEL/CALIBRATE. Gate: out-of-sample RMSE on 2024 improves ≥3% over one-hot XGBoost AND 80%/90% posterior intervals achieve nominal coverage. Paper-reported 5–30% benchmark gains on categorical-heavy data.
19. **SportSQL NL-query layer (2508.17157v1)** — Owner: Hermes. INGEST/INFRA. Gate: ≥75% overall correctness on the NFL benchmark with zero write queries escaping the read-only guard (deterministic SELECT-only allowlist check).
20. **ATB adaptive retry (2510.04516v3)** — Owner: Hermes. INFRA/INGEST. Gate: 429s drop ≥50% vs the fixed-sleep baseline with completion-time increase ≤30% over the 1-week test.

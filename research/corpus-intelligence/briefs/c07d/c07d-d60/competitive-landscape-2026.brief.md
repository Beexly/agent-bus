# strategy/competitive-landscape-2026.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A 2026 competitive teardown of four competitor segments (pick/tout services, sharp +EV tools, AI prediction sites, bet trackers) arguing that Closing Line Value (CLV) is the only sharp-accepted benchmark of skill, that almost no competitor publishes it, and that GSE's latent `packages/prediction-engine` capabilities (CLV, de-vig, calibration, edge engine) are its biggest moat. Includes a 2026-06-16 status update on surfaced capabilities.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- TL;DR thesis: CLV is the only sharp-accepted benchmark of skill; almost nobody in the competitive set publishes it.
- 2026-06-16 status: recommendations were latent capability, not missing code. Surfaced this session: #1 public CLV report (/clv — gated like the win rate, cross-linked from anti-tout page, methodology hub, accountability hub, homepage callout "the one number touts never show"); #2 line shop (/observatory — best available price/line per side across captured books; buildBestLines unit-tested; transparency, not arbitrage); #4 user CLV tracking (/track — glass-box ledger: CLV, ROI, own Brier calibration; woven into proof suite + footer); #3 education moat partial (methodology now explains consensus → line shop → CLV; glossary + /clv + /vs/tout carry the plain-language layer; ongoing). Remaining: depth (#3) and elevating CLV to a primary homepage/nav headline.
- Pick/tout segment: long-run win rates above ~55–57% at -110 are vanishingly rare; anything advertised at 65%+ over a real sample is almost always manipulated or small-sample. Documented tactics: cherry-picked/fabricated records, the split-pick scam (Team A to half the list, Team B to the other half), an always-on "premium pick" even on slates that don't earn one. The real tell is CLV, which they never show.
- Sharp/+EV segment: aggregate odds from 100–150+ books in real time, de-vig to a "fair" line; surface +EV bets, arbitrage, middles, line-shopping; Outlier adds AI player-prop models and the juice-free "Unabated Line"; terminology devig, Kelly, EV, arbitrage. Pricing spans ~$20/mo entry to $200–400/mo for the real +EV/arb feeds. Built for high-volume pros ($1,000+/week), everyone else churns. Industry voices argue "+EV is dead in 2025." Books treat consistent +EV bettors as a liability: reduced limits or account bans; arbitrage isn't risk-free (a book can void one leg); tools are overwhelming (wall of devig/Kelly/arb casual bettors can't action).
- AI prediction segment: marketed on accuracy claims of 60–85%. The category's own reviewers say most publish picks with no methodology, no accuracy tracking, no calibration, so "90% accuracy" claims are indistinguishable from marketing fiction (often measured on the trivial subset of obvious favorites). No CLV, no reliability curve, no audit.
- GSE's calibration baseline: market closing line at Brier 0.2111, ECE 0.0180 over 5,281 games; the file states GSE refuses to publish its own model's number until settled picks exist.
- Bet tracker segment: auto-sync user's real bets from 30+ books (Pikkit BookSync, Action BetSync); personal P/L and community leaderboards verified (no manual entry = no faked records). GSE's gap: they don't let users track bets at all — flagged as a real engagement gap.
- Capability-vs-competitor table: de-vig/fair line (`removeVig`, `americanToImpliedProbability`) vs "core of every sharp tool"; CLV functions vs "the benchmark nobody publishes"; calibration (real market baseline computed; pick-model gated) vs "AI sites fake it; touts ignore it"; edge engine (`assessEdge`, founder-gated) as "a more disciplined +EV than the feeds"; factor trail on every published signal; loss-complete ledger (the Vault); multi-book odds via The Odds API ingestion.
- Priority order: 1) CLV report surface (gated); 2) best-line/book context per pick; 3) honest-education content moat; 4) user CLV-tracking.
- Doctrines stated: no fabricated stats, no fake win-rates, server-side gating, gate-until-defensible; anti-tout doctrine on /vs/tout-services; named pricing ladder; "no autonomous money" implied by the honest-affiliate-only stance.

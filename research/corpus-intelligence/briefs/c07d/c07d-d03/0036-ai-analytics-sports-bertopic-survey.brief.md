# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0036-ai-analytics-sports-bertopic-survey.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A bibliometric systematic literature review (PRISMA, Web of Science Core Collection SSCI, 2002–2024) that runs BERTopic topic modelling over the abstracts of 204 AI/analytics-in-sports journal articles to extract four latent research themes. Verdict in the file: REJECT — no predictive model, no measured effect, no transferable technique.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas: not specified (no equations stated). Method as stated:
- PRISMA (Moher et al., 2009) corpus build: keyword string "Artificial Intelligence" OR "Analytics" OR "Machine Learning" OR "Deep Learning" OR "AI" OR "ML" AND "Sports" in WoS Topic field, January 2002–December 2024.
- Funnel: 598 articles → 539 journal articles → refined by research area (ten highest-volume disciplines) → 459 → journal-quality filter (5-year JCR-2025 Impact Factor ≥ 2.0) → 339 → abstract screening by three academicians, binary coding (1 = focused on AI/analytics in sports, 0 = no clear focus), disagreements resolved by full-paper read + discussion, 135 excluded → **204 included articles** in 101 journals.
- BERTopic (Grootendorst, 2022): generate contextualized embeddings with a pre-trained BERT model → cluster embeddings → CountVectorizer preprocessing applied AFTER embedding generation and clustering (per Grootendorst's recommendation, "because the transformer model requires complete textual input to produce accurate contextual embeddings"); class-based TF-IDF for coherent topic representations.
- Topic interpretation per DiMaggio et al. (2013): "identify the lens through which one can see the data most clearly"; labels via topic-word associations (Crain et al., 2012; DiMaggio et al., 2013; Hannigan et al., 2019), prioritizing higher-weight words, cross-checked against representative studies per topic (Table 1).
- Word cloud of 204 titles via Python wordcloud 1.8.2.2; two-dimensional inter-topic distance map (Figure 5): circle size = relative significance, spatial position = semantic proximity/dissimilarity. No topic-coherence metric (c_v) reported.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Top journals by volume: Frontiers in Psychology 19; International Journal of Sports Science & Coaching 15; International Journal of Forecasting 6; Sports Management Review, Big Data, PLOS One, Applied Sciences-Basel, Sustainability 5 each.
- Dominant title terms (word cloud): "sport," "model," "data," "player," "machine learning," "performance."
- The single substantive result — four extracted themes, ordered by prominence in the inter-topic distance map (Figure 5): **(1) performance modelling, (2) physical and mental health, (3) social media sentiment analysis, (4) tactical tracking**.
- Map structure: the four topics are "largely distinct" (well-spaced circles); the two most prominent (performance modelling, physical/mental health) are positioned far apart (thematic divergence); closer proximities observed between physical/mental health ↔ tactical tracking and between performance modelling ↔ social media sentiment analysis (conceptual overlap).
- Topic 1 example terms: "performance," "team", "football," "results," "match," "player" → labelled "performance modelling."
- No quantitative results of any kind: no accuracy, no effect size, no coherence score.
- Future-research directions proposed per topic: (1) tactical tracking — multimodal tracking-data frameworks, real-time AI tactical decision-support systems, extension beyond soccer (cricket, field hockey, volleyball); (2) sentiment — multilingual/cross-cultural sentiment, multimodal data (images, video, emojis, gifs, live streams), sentiment → behavioral/market outcomes; (3) health — preventive injury detection from biomechanical/physiological precursors, mental-health risk modelling from multimodal data (social media, sleep, conversational cues), integrated physical–emotional frameworks; (4) performance modelling — holistic technical/tactical/physical/psychological models, situational factors (game context, opponent quality, environment), backward integration into talent identification/development/recruitment.
- Biases (paper's own/self-documented): WoS-only, journal-articles-only (conferences, preprints, industry work excluded by design), SSCI-only, JCR-5yr-IF ≥ 2.0 quality filter (excludes lower-impact venues), abstracts-only modelling (~150-word summaries); corpus ends December 2024.

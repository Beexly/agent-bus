# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0380-learning-group-interactions-and-semantic-intentions.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Qi, Yang & Ma (2024, arXiv:2412.15673) propose an end-to-end diffusion framework for multi-agent sports trajectory prediction that conditions generation on team-level tactic labels via classifier-free guidance and models group interaction as a cooperative game using a learned Banzhaf Interaction, plus a new NBA SportVU benchmark with 25 human annotators' tactic labels (16 classes). Verdict recorded in the file: ADAPT — the closest published analog to what NFL tracking-based live win-probability and prop pricing needs; the port requires swapping human-annotated basketball tactics for NGS scheme labels and scaling from 11 to 22 agents.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Interaction Encoder: Transformer encodes observed trajectories X∈R^{N×T_obs×2} into embeddings A∈R^{N×D_A}; tactic labels L mapped through learnable tactic-vocabulary embedding matrix W∈R^{V×D_C} (Φ_tactic, Eq. 6), expanded per-agent by team correspondence (Eq. 7); per-agent condition g_i = [a_i ; c_{i,m(i)}] (Eq. 8).
- Denoising (diffusion) with classifier-free guidance (Eq. 9, verbatim): **f_ε(y,G) = f_ε(y,A) + s_g·(f_ε(y,G) − f_ε(y,A))**; tactic info randomly dropped out during training, replaced with raw trajectory embedding A; **s_g = 0.1**; 100 diffusion steps.
- DDPM reverse step with leapfrog-style initializer [14] to reduce denoising steps (Eq. 10, verbatim): **ŷ^τ = (1/√α_τ)(ŷ^{τ+1} − (1−α_τ)/√(1−ᾱ_τ) · ε̂^τ_θ) + √(1−α_τ)·z**, ᾱ_τ = ∏ α_i.
- Losses (Eqs. 11–12, verbatim): **L_noise = ‖z − f_ε(y^{τ+1}, τ+1, f_θ(X, L^{-T_obs+1:0}))‖_2**; **L_dist = min_s ‖Y − Ŷ_s‖_2** (variety min-over-S distance with S=20); **L_unc = Σ_s ‖Y − Ŷ_s‖_2/(σ_θ²S) + log σ_θ²** (initializer uncertainty loss).
- Multi-Grained Feature Enhancement: global attention over team tokens (Eq. 13: **G_j' = Softmax(QKᵀ/√D_k)V**), then local attention using ball tokens as keys/values; fusion Ĝ_j via global-as-query over local.
- Semantic Intention (auxiliary): agents + top-k potential tactics as players in a cooperative game; exact Banzhaf Interaction is NP-hard so a **Banzhaf Interaction Learner** predicts the N×k agent–tactic similarity matrix: (Eq. 14, verbatim) **I_pred = f(A) = γ_o(SA(γ_ctx(A))) ∈ R^{N×k}** (MLP–self-attention–MLP); supervised by Banzhaf Interaction Calculation (Eqs. 22–24, verbatim): **S_a2t = softmax(S_{a,t} × mask_a[a])**; **S_t2a = softmax(S_{a,t} × mask_t[t])**; **I^j_B = (S_a2t + S_t2a)/2**, with L2 loss (Eq. 15): **L_bi = Σ_j ‖I^j_pred − I^j_B‖_2**.
- Fusion and tactic head (Eqs. 16–18, verbatim): **w_j = Fusion(Ĝ_j, I^j_pred)** (dot product); **p_j = softmax(MLP(GAT(w_j))) ∈ R^{V×1}**; **l̂_j = argmax_k(p_j)**.
- Focal loss (Eqs. 19–21, verbatim): **L^j_tactic = α^j(1 − p^{tactic}_j)^{γ^j} log(p^{tactic}_j)**, with **γ=4.0**, class-frequency α.
- Total loss (Eq. 25, verbatim): **L = L_noise + L_distance + η·L_unc + α·(L_tactic + β·L_bi)**, with η, α, β hyperparameters; game factor set to 0.001.
- Implementation: PyTorch, single RTX 3090, Adam. Denoising module: 100 epochs, lr 1e-3 halved every 16 epochs; tactic model: 100 epochs same schedule; two-fold framework: 30 epochs, lr 2e-3 decayed ×0.9 every 32 epochs; tactic prediction rate 1000. (Note: exact meaning of "tactic prediction rate is set at 1000" is underspecified in the main text.)

## Data sources named
- **Extended NBA SportVU (new benchmark, public via code repo):** raw SportVU logs from the 2015–16 NBA season (github.com/linouk23/NBA-Player-Movements) resampled to 5 Hz; **90,618 trajectory sequences** extracted (30 frames at 5 Hz); visualized as videos and annotated by **25 trained human annotators** with **16 team tactic labels** (7 offensive: e.g., Pick-and-Roll, Ball Movement, Fast Break, Single; 9 defensive: 5 zone-defense types, Man-to-Man, defensive rebound, defensive transition, scramble). Annotation: one tactic per team for the past 10 frames and the following 20 frames; 8,238 entries (JSON: scene ID, team tactics, time period; .npy trajectories), **11 agents per scene** (5 offense + 5 defense + ball). Train 70% / test 30%.
- **TeamTrack-basketball and TeamTrack-soccer [68]:** used with unsupervised K-means pseudo-labels (16 clusters) as tactic guidance; observed 3.2 s (96 frames), predict 4.8 s (144 frames).
- Task setup on NBA: observe 2 s (10 frames), predict 4 s (20 frames) for all 11 agents (2D court coordinates).
- Code and data: https://github.com/aurora-xin/Group2Int-trajectory.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- **NBA SportVU trajectory (minADE/minFDE):** 1 s: 0.19/0.29; 2 s: 0.39/0.59; 3 s: 0.61/0.86; 4 s: 0.84/1.17. Paper's claim: beats SOTA LED by up to 9.5% and 6% in minADE at 1 s and 4 s respectively (file's check: LED 4 s = 0.89/1.24 vs ours 0.84/1.17 → ~5.6% ADE improvement; 1 s: LED 0.21/0.31 vs 0.19/0.29 → 9.5% ADE. Consistent).
- **TeamTrack-soccer (ADE/FDE at 1.2/2.4/3.6/4.8 s):** ours 0.23/0.45, 0.58/1.10, 1.00/1.89, 1.48/2.72; beats LED by 15.4% ADE and 9.6% FDE at 4.8 s. **TeamTrack-basketball:** 0.42/0.72, 0.88/1.44, 1.31/2.05, 1.72/2.78; beats LED by 14.4% ADE and 30.3% FDE at 4.8 s.
- **Tactic prediction (Top-1/2/3/5 %, Table III):** Baseline 34.56/59.77/73.78/88.53; Baseline* 53.31/71.64/81.99/91.78; Pooling* 55.22/75.82/86.05/94.67; Llama3-8B 22.39 (Top-1 only); Llama3-8B* 38.17 (Top-1 only); **Ours 60.23/81.15/90.53/96.79**. Paper claims +5 pp Top-1 over Pooling* and +22 pp over finetuned Llama3-8B.
- **Ablation denoising (Table IV, minADE/minFDE):** MLP-based at 4 s: 5.28/8.71; query-based: 1.24/1.63; diffusion: 0.84/1.17.
- **Ablation Banzhaf (Table V):** without BI: Top-1 58.44; with BI team-level 60.09; scene-level 60.23 (≈+2 pp Top-1/Top-2, consistent across Top-5).
- **Qualitative (§VI-F):** on man-to-man transitions the model's predicted defensive trajectories mirror offensive movement where LED places defenders too far apart; the ball has the highest Banzhaf interaction (~0.285) for both teams.
- UNCERTAIN / limitations recorded: split protocol unclear — 70/30 split on extracted sequences with no statement it is game-disjoint (sequence-level random splits leak near-identical context into test); inter-annotator agreement statistics not reported; tactic-window stationarity assumed, not tested; human tactics not available at live inference — the primary task conditions on *observed* tactic labels, and the paper predicts only future tactics, leaving a tactic-recognition gap for deployment; best-of-20 evaluation flatters multi-modal generators vs deterministic baselines; K-means pseudo-labels validated only via t-SNE visuals; the "Banzhaf ground truth" (Eqs. 22–24) is a masked-attention heuristic, not a true characteristic-function game — a naming stretch; continuous-flow basketball/soccer vs discrete-play NFL; 11 agents → 22 players + ball.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **SCHEME (primary, coaching-tendencies / live-prediction program):** Tactic-conditioned trajectory generation ports directly to NFL in-play modeling: condition on NGS Route Classification 2.0 + Run Scheme Classification + FTN charting coverage/blitz tags (structured labels GSE already has — no human annotation needed, unlike the paper), observe post-snap frames 0–20 (2 s), predict remaining player trajectories, and use predicted trajectories to update expected play outcome / live prop pricing. The paper's auxiliary tactic-classification head (Top-1 60.23%) becomes a scheme-recognition module for NFL.
- **OTHER (calibration/sizing lane, novel):** The file's improvement experiment goes beyond the paper: **counterfactual tactic conditioning for live pricing** — generate trajectories under counterfactual schemes via the CFG mechanism (Eq. 9 already supports swapping G) and measure divergence of expected play outcome between factual and counterfactual rollouts. Plays where outcome is highly tactic-sensitive get wider live-prop uncertainty bands — directly monetizable for in-play products.
- **TRUST-SIGNAL (player-importance features):** Banzhaf values identify which agents drive a tactic (e.g., which receiver's movement most influences the coverage call); the ball had the highest interaction (~0.285). For NFL: Banzhaf values → which players drive a scheme — usable for matchup-analysis content and trust-target intake (which players' routes determine coverage).
- **OTHER (tracking lane):** Extends the already-absorbed diffusion trajectory family (2503.18589) with tactic-conditioning and Banzhaf intentions; complements STRAIN tracking-metric work. File's acceptance gate: adopt only if the tactic-conditioned model beats the unconditioned diffusion baseline by ≥8% minADE on a weeks 17–18 test window AND scheme classification Top-1 ≥ 55%; reject if conditioning gains vanish when tactics are *recognized* rather than given (<3% gain) — the production-realistic variant the paper never reports.
- **OTHER (latency caveat):** Diffusion with 100 steps is too slow for real-time — distill or use the leapfrog initializer aggressively, or fall back to the query-based variant for latency-sensitive paths.

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1944-transformers-are-sample-efficient-world-models.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Transformers are Sample-Efficient World Models (IRIS, arXiv:2209.00588, ICLR 2023) — casts dynamics learning as language modeling: a VQ-VAE discrete tokenizer plus a GPT-like autoregressive transformer over interleaved frame/action tokens, with an actor-critic trained purely on imagined rollouts, achieving mean above human-level on the Atari 100k benchmark without lookahead search. Verdict: ADAPT — the token-LM framing is arguably MORE natural for NFL than for Atari because plays are already discrete tokens (no VQ-VAE needed).

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Tokenizer: VQ-VAE-style discrete autoencoder E: R^{h×w×3} → {1..N}^K (K tokens from vocabulary size N; CNN encoder/decoder); encoder z_t^k = argmin_i ‖ y_t^k − e_i ‖_2, y_t = CNN(x_t) ∈ R^{K×d}, codebook E ∈ R^{N×d}.
- Autoencoder loss: L(E,D,E) = ‖x−D(z)‖_1 + ‖sg(E(x))−E(z)‖²_2 + ‖sg(E(z))−E(x)‖²_2 + L_perceptual(x, D(z)); straight-through estimator; L1 reconstruction + VQ commitment + perceptual loss equally weighted.
- World model G: GPT-like transformer over (z_0^1..z_0^K, a_0, r_0, d_0, z_1^1..z_1^K, a_1, …); trained on segments of L timesteps with cross-entropy for next-token (transition) and termination predictors, MSE or cross-entropy for reward predictor.
- Hyperparameters (Table 3): L=20 timesteps, embedding dim D=256, M=10 layers, 4 attention heads, weight decay 0.01, embedding/attention dropout 0.1.
- Imagination loop: encode initial frame to tokens, policy π acts on tokens, G autoregressively unfolds next-frame tokens + predicts reward r̂_t and termination d̂_t; policy trained exclusively on imagined rollouts; real env used only to improve the world model.
- RL objective: maximize E_π[ Σ_{t≥0} γ^t r_t ] with π trained on imagined rollouts.
- Key demonstration: after only 120 games of training on Pong, the world model reenacts a test trajectory "pixel perfect", including the scoreboard-update mechanic.
- Evaluation: Atari 100k benchmark (26 games, 100k steps ≈ 2 hours each), metrics mean/median/IQM human-normalized scores, #superhuman games, 5 seeds.

## Data sources named
Atari 100k benchmark (Kaiser et al. 2020): 26 Atari 2600 games, image observations, discrete actions, scalar rewards, termination flags; no external dataset — agent collects own experience. Baselines in results table: random, human, SimPLe, DER, OTRainbow, DrQ, SPR, CURL variants. Code: https://github.com/eloialonso/iris. GSE-side spec: nflverse play sequences 2006–2025, play tokens (play_type, formation_family, direction, rusher/receiver bucket, yardage bucket, EPA bucket, score_event, turnover flag), vocabulary ~2–5k, conditioned on pregame matchup embedding, ≤~170 play-tokens per game, 100k simulated matchups aggregated to score distributions.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- IRIS achieves mean human-normalized score 1.046, median 0.289, superhuman on 10 of 26 games — "setting a new state of the art for methods without lookahead search", surpassing SimPLe, DER, OTRainbow, DrQ, SPR, CURL (next-best mean 0.616, next-best superhuman count 6). First method with mean above human-level in the 100k regime without search.
- After only 120 training games on Pong, the world model reenacts a test trajectory "pixel perfect", including the scoreboard-update game mechanic — the sample-efficiency claim's sharpest datapoint.
- Limitations: (i) VQ tokenizer is brittle — codebook collapse kills the world model; (ii) pixels→tokens is wasteful for NFL where the true state is structured (down/distance/score) — but the token-LM framing transfers; (iii) Atari 100k games are short-horizon and reactive; long-horizon sparse-reward games (Frostbite) remain weak — NFL full-game credit assignment is similarly hard; (iv) the policy is trained for CONTROL, not unbiased simulation — for pricing GSE wants the generator, not the agent; (v) transformer imagination is O(L²) per rollout vs RSSM's O(L).
- Complements 1942/1943 (Dreamer line): alternative world-model backbone (discrete tokens + GPT) vs RSSM; the repo has no token-based sequence model of games. NFL advantage: no VQ-VAE needed — the tokenizer problem disappears.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER (simulation engine): "GSE-IRIS" is a concrete game-simulator blueprint — tokenize each play as a discrete multi-token tuple (play_type, formation_family, direction, rusher/receiver bucket, yardage bucket, EPA bucket, score_event, turnover flag), vocab ~2–5k, train a GPT-style causal LM on nflverse play sequences 2006–2025 with next-token CE + per-play score/termination heads, conditioned on a pregame matchup embedding as context tokens; autoregressively sample full games (~170 play-tokens) 100k times per matchup and aggregate to score distributions. Serves the simulation/prediction engine.
- SCHEME: the token vocabulary IS a scheme representation — (play_type, formation_family, direction, EPA bucket) tuples capture play-call tendencies directly; the per-play next-token distribution is a learnable scheme model that predicts play calls conditioned on down/distance/score. Drives get explicit structure in the improvement experiment: a drive-level summary token (result, points, plays, starting field position) every drive with an auxiliary drive-outcome head — drives as the natural "paragraphs" of a football game (~10–13 per team), expected to improve 4th-quarter score-distribution tails (comeback regimes). Serves coaching-tendency and scheme modeling.
- OTHER (tail calibration): the improvement experiment's hypothesis — hierarchical drive tokens improve tail calibration, specifically predicted vs empirical P(margin > 14) on 2024 games — targets exactly the blowout/comeback tail that GSE's margin models must price for spreads and totals.
- OTHER (architecture decision): the acceptance gate is a clean A/B — token-autoregressive vs the RSSM variant from 1942 §12 vs bootstrap historical simulator on 2024 (one-step-ahead log-likelihood, final-score TVD, kickoff WP ECE); ADOPT the token LM if one-step log-likelihood ≥5% better AND final-score TVD ≤5% per week AND WP ECE ≤0.03; otherwise drop the loser. Honest caveat recorded: transformers are hungrier for data and NFL has only ~500k plays — RSSM may win on data efficiency.
- OTHER (usage note): for PRICING, train the generator and discard the control policy — IRIS's agent optimizes control, but GSE needs unbiased simulation; the imagination loop's value is the world model, not the actor-critic.
- TRUST-SIGNAL (caution): the Frostbite datapoint — long-horizon sparse-reward games remain weak for this architecture — maps to NFL full-game credit assignment (late-game leverage sequences); expect weaker reliability on long-horizon regime predictions until validated.

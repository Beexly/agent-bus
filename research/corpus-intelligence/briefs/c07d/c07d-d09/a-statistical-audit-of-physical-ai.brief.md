# arxiv-deep/0441-a-statistical-audit-of-physical-ai.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Navasardyan & Davtyan (2026, arXiv:2608.25940v2) audit a 51-models × 12-benchmarks matrix of physical-AI evaluations for redundancy (pairwise Spearman ρ, leave-one-model-out ridge R² predictability per benchmark, pooled-average inflation demo) and sufficiency (greedy forward selection under utility U(b|S) = g(b)·(1−R²(b∼S)) picks a 4-benchmark core retaining 78.5% of total utility; Bradley–Terry benchmarks-as-judges Elo ranking). The domain doesn't transfer to NFL, but the audit machinery ports directly to pruning GSE's metric catalog and model-variant zoo — the paper's central empirical fact is that roughly half the variance of any single benchmark is reconstructible from the others.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Preprocessing: z-score every benchmark column before multivariate steps; Spearman rank correlations as default pairwise measure (immune to difficulty differences); difficulty re-enters via saturation penalties.
- RQ1 redundancy: (a) pairwise Spearman ρ with average-linkage hierarchical clustering on distance 1−ρ; (b) per-benchmark predictability: ridge regression of each benchmark on the other 11, scored by leave-one-model-out cross-validated R²; uniqueness = 1 − LOO R²; (c) inflation demo: replace each substitute pair with the mean of its two columns, recompute equal-weight averages and rankings.
- Greedy forward-selection utility (eq. 1, verbatim): U(b|S) = g(b)·(1 − R²(b∼S)), where g(b) = Gini coefficient of the benchmark's score distribution (discrimination), R²(b∼S) = OLS fit of candidate on selected set (marginal information); product form so failure of either criterion zeroes utility. Starts S=∅ (first pick = most discriminating), cumulative utility reported as % of all-12 total.
- Bradley–Terry ranking (eq. 2, verbatim): P(i≻j) = σ(ri − rj) by MLE with L2 penalty 1e-3, strengths centered; Elo_i = 1500 + 400·ri/ln10. Each benchmark = one judge; every model pair × every shared benchmark = one binary vote for the higher scorer; 4,231 observations.
- Appendix B: single principal component of shared variance; residualize each benchmark on the general-capability axis (from 9 general benchmarks).

## Data sources named
- Registry of 51 physical-AI benchmarks and 152 models from model cards, papers, official blogs; selection filters: density (≥5 models), recency, diversity. Final: 51 models × 12 benchmarks. Models released 2024–2026 from 15 providers (open and closed, 1B–241B params); retained if ≥8/12 scores reported. Matrix completed by authors' own runs (159 additional cells, greedy decoding, benchmark-native parsing); published cells not systematically re-verified.
- The 12 benchmarks (all 0–100 scale; 10 multiple-choice, 2 coordinate-pointing), items and n: VSI-Bench (5,130 items, n=51), EmbSpatial (3,640, n=51), RefSpatial-Bench (200, n=51), Where2Place (100, n=50), ERQA (400, n=49), CV-Bench (2,638, n=49), SAT (150, n=49), RoboSpatial (350, n=47), RealWorldQA (765, n=42), OmniSpatial (1,533, n=42), MindCube (21,154, n=42), BLINK (3,807, n=41).
- Plus 9 general benchmarks on the same models (Appendix B).
- Project home: https://metric-ai-lab.github.io/metabench/ (code/evaluation matrix per paper).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Pairwise redundancy: average Spearman ρ = 0.487 across all 66 pairs, all positive. Substitute pairs above 0.8: EmbSpatial↔CV-Bench ρ = 0.876, 95% CI [0.78, 0.93], n=49; Where2Place↔RefSpatial-Bench ρ = 0.860, [0.73, 0.93], n=50. Also ERQA+RealWorldQA ρ=0.758; RealWorldQA+OmniSpatial ρ=0.748. BLINK most isolated (own dendrogram branch).
- LOO predictability (Table 2, R² and uniqueness=1−R²): Where2Place 0.727 (0.273), RefSpatial-Bench 0.721 (0.279), ERQA 0.706 (0.294) — most redundant; RealWorldQA 0.319 (0.681), RoboSpatial 0.351 (0.649), BLINK 0.378 (0.622) — least predictable. Median benchmark has roughly half its variance reconstructible from the other 11. MindCube is the most frequent top predictor of other benchmarks (7 of 11).
- Ranking impact of collapsing the two substitute pairs: 22 of 51 models move by ≥3 places under equal weighting; MiMo-Embodied-7B drops 9 places, Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 drops 8; GPT-4o gains 9, Claude-Sonnet-4 gains 8.
- Minimal suite: forward selection picks RefSpatial-Bench → MindCube → VSI-Bench → BLINK; these 4 reach 78.5% of the all-12 utility. Where2Place (second-highest Gini) is passed over three times, enters 5th at 85.0% cumulative; final four benchmarks add only 4.4% between them.
- Stability check (Appendix C, Table 8): forcing VSI-Bench or BLINK into the opening slot returns the identical four benchmarks in different order (4/4 kept, 77.8–78.3% utility vs 78.5% unconstrained); forcing Where2Place re-admits RefSpatial-Bench at step 4 (U=0.343×0.248=0.085, displacing BLINK to fifth; 74.5%); forcing rejected benchmarks (RoboSpatial, SAT) displaces one core member to fifth (69.2–71.6%). MindCube and VSI-Bench enter within the first four in every run; RefSpatial-Bench is never kept out.
- Bradley–Terry top-10 Elo (Table 3): HY-Embodied-0.5 MoE-407B-A32B 2251; Qwen3.5-397B-A17B 2032; Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct 1828; Seed 2.0 1790; Kimi K2.5 1750; HY-Embodied-0.5 MoT-4B-A2B 1744; Gemini 3.0 Pro 1716; Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct 1662; Gemini-2.5-Pro-preview-05-06 1643; RoboBrain-32B-2.0 1630. Domain post-training gains (3 of top 10 are embodiment/spatial post-trained); size monotone only within a recipe.
- Appendix B: one principal component explains 55.2% of suite variance (all 12 benchmarks load positively, 0.48 RealWorldQA to 0.88 CV-Bench); tracks general vision-language capability at Spearman ρ=0.952 (general PC1 of the 9 anchors, n=32, 95% CI [0.82, 0.97]; 0.950 vs MMStar, 0.942 vs Video-MME). Residualizing halves mean pairwise correlation 0.487→0.250 and drops pairs above 0.5 from 34 of 66 to 6 — roughly half the shared variance is general capability, not physical-AI-specific. Three specific bonds survive at near-full strength: EmbSpatial↔CV-Bench 0.876→0.784 residual; Where2Place↔RefSpatial 0.860→0.801; ERQA↔MindCube 0.656→0.613. Suppression: VSI-Bench and BLINK uncorrelated in raw scores (ρ=0.002) but −0.363 in residuals (−0.425 in dense block) — among models of equal general capability, video-spatial strength trades off against multi-image perception.
- Caveats: aggregates only (no item-level data); low R² can mean "distinct" OR "noisy" (needs repeated evaluation — not available); greedy selection has no optimality guarantee; core depends on Gini-based g(b), untested against other discrimination definitions; purely observational (no causal claim); none of the 12 benchmarks measures downstream physical-task success.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **OTHER — metric-catalog pruning (new capability):** the corpus map inventories a 26-metric catalog (v1) + 9 lanes, 15 metric families in gse-lab (29 CSVs) — inventoried, never pruned. The Spearman + LOO-ridge machinery is the pruning rule: build a 32-teams × 15–26-metrics matrix over 2015–2025, find substitute pairs (expected: EPA/play vs success rate vs DVOA variants cluster), collapse them, and show power-rating rank displacement — the analogue of the 22-of-51-models demonstration makes the redundancy decision-relevant. Serves the calibration/sizing and public/private-surface programs (public edge sheet shows only non-redundant metrics).
- **OTHER — model-variant auditing:** run the same machinery on GSE engine variants (rows = variants, columns = evaluation metrics) to test which variants add unique signal vs. which are double-counted substitutes; Bradley–Terry metrics-as-judges replaces averaged model leaderboards for variant comparison. Serves the engine calibration program.
- **OTHER — discrimination measure choice:** the authors note the core is untested against a different g; the GSE improvement swaps Gini spread for univariate out-of-sample predictive R² for next-season EPA/play (U = predictive-R² × (1−R² on selected)) — a minimal forecasting-power suite ("which metrics earn their place on the edge sheet") rather than a spread suite. Serves calibration/sizing.
- **TRUST-SIGNAL — trust-signal intake auditing:** the same audit applies to trust-target intake: pairwise-correlate the incoming analyst/model signals (the 86+ X analyst accounts benchmark) and drop near-duplicates so the consensus isn't one loud cluster counted twice. CONTRADICTION-risk: none identified with corpus, but a caution — the paper's "median benchmark half reconstructible" finding is for AI benchmarks; GSE metrics may or may not be that redundant (gate requires ≥2 substitute pairs at ρ>0.8 before acting).
- UNCERTAIN: whether GSE's metrics are actually redundant — the file's gate says REJECT pruning if all LOO-R² < 0.35 or the ≤6-metric core fails a second-split replication; also the 0.487-average-correlation figure does not transfer from AI benchmarks to football metrics.

# media/PLATFORM_PLAYBOOK.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
The manual-review platform strategy for GSE's media surfaces (updated 2026-07-04): a 9-platform table (YouTube Long-Form/Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels/Carousels, X/Threads, LinkedIn, Newsletter, Podcast) with goals, pillar fit, ideal lengths, hooks, title/caption rules, visual rules, CTAs, compliance notes, repurposing flow, and early/long-term monetization per platform, plus cadence targets. It does not approve auto-posting, auto-uploading, scraping, platform API integration, or paid tooling.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
not specified — no formulas or equations.

Ideal lengths / counts stated per platform:
- YouTube Long-Form: 8–14 minute explainers; 20–45 minute board meeting (later format).
- YouTube Shorts: 20–60 seconds.
- TikTok: 15–60 seconds.
- Instagram Reels: 20–45 seconds.
- Instagram Carousels: 5–8 slides.
- X / Threads: 5–9 posts (per thread).
- LinkedIn: 150–600 words.
- Newsletter: 700–1200 words.
- Podcast: 20–45 minutes (when ready).
- Hook rule for YouTube Long-Form: state the misconception and evidence question in the first 20 seconds.

Cadence targets:
- YouTube Long-Form: 2 videos/week when production capacity supports it.
- YouTube Shorts: 1–2/day when review capacity supports it.
- TikTok: 1–3/day if possible, used for discovery and hook testing.
- Instagram: Reels, carousels, stories, polls, partner credibility.
- X/Threads: real-time commentary, founder voice, sports data thoughts, build logs.
- LinkedIn: B2B, partner, API, founder credibility, sports data business, AI governance.
- Newsletter: 1–2/week, the owned-audience conversion spine.
- Podcast: future board meeting authority platform.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Per-platform table rows (primary goal / secondary goal / best pillars / ideal length / hook / title-caption rules / visual rules / CTA / compliance / repurposing / early monetization / long-term monetization):
  - YouTube Long-Form — primary Authority; secondary search, watch time, sponsor inventory, embedded website content, podcast archive; pillars Market Mirage, Loss Autopsy, GSE Lab, Data Business, Board Meeting; 8–14 min explainers (20–45 min board meeting later); hook: misconception + evidence question in first 20 seconds; titles: clear promise, no unsupported performance claim, source notes in description; visuals: original graphics, app screenshots, diagrams, **no broadcast footage without rights**; CTA: Newsletter; compliance: paid promotion box when sponsored, no copyrighted footage assumption; repurposing: clip into shorts, newsletter, thread, carousel; early monetization: founder support, founding sponsor packages; long-term: sponsorships, podcast archive, course/API demand.
  - YouTube Shorts — primary Reach; secondary funnel to long-form; pillars No-Bet Clinic, Market Mirage, Player Signal Lab; 20–60 seconds; hook: one idea, no intro, strong first frame; titles: plain language, no outcome certainty; visuals: captions always; CTA: watch long-form; compliance: disclosure if sponsored; repurposing: 5–8 clips from each long video; early: discovery; long-term: sponsorship extensions and funnel.
  - TikTok — primary Reach; secondary viral testing, founder personality; pillars Founder Build Log, No-Bet Clinic, Decision Psychology; 15–60 seconds; hook: start with tension or lesson; titles: raw, direct, not spammy; visuals: captioned phone-native clips; CTA: watch long; compliance: discovery, not immediate revenue; repurposing: test hooks before long-form; early: audience discovery; long-term: creator collaborations.
  - Instagram Reels — primary Reach; secondary relationship and shareable proof; pillars No-Bet Clinic, Player Signal Lab, Partner Tools; 20–45 seconds; hook: first frame carries lesson; titles: caption concise, disclosure visible; visuals: original visuals, no casino-style styling; CTA: Newsletter; compliance: sponsor disclosure near mention; repurposing: clip from long-form; early: trust and partner credibility; long-term: sponsor integrations.
  - Instagram Carousels — primary Authority; secondary saves and frameworks; pillars Player Signal Lab, Decision Psychology, Market Mirage; 5–8 slides; hook: first slide states counterintuitive lesson; titles: each slide one claim; visuals: original diagrams, app screenshots; CTA: Newsletter; compliance: disclosure slide if sponsored; repurposing: convert video lesson to checklist; early: save/share audience; long-term: sponsored frameworks.
  - X / Threads — primary Reach; secondary real-time commentary and founder voice; pillars Market Mirage, Data Business, Build Log; 5–9 posts; hook: thesis in first post; titles: no unsupported injury or market claims; visuals: sourced charts only; CTA: site; compliance: state evidence level; repurposing: newsletter section to thread; early: audience and feedback; long-term: partner relationships.
  - LinkedIn — primary Partnership; secondary B2B credibility, API demand, founder trust; pillars Sports Data Business, GSE Lab, Founder Build Log; 150–600 words; hook: business or proof problem first; titles: concrete shipped artifacts, no fake traction; visuals: screenshots and diagrams only; CTA: partner; compliance: disclose partner ties; repurposing: build log to B2B post; early: partner outreach; long-term: B2B sponsorships and API demand.
  - Newsletter — primary Conversion; secondary retention and trust; pillars Board Meeting, Market Mirage, Loss Autopsy, Player Signal Lab; 700–1200 words; hook: specific evidence lesson; titles: subject line must be honest; visuals: embedded charts only if cleared; CTA: site; compliance: disclosure near sponsor mention; repurposing: long-form to owned summary; early: waitlist and founding subscribers; long-term: conversion spine.
  - Podcast — primary Authority; secondary sponsorship and guest pipeline; pillars Board Meeting, Loss Autopsy, Data Business; 20–45 minutes when ready; hook: what shipped, broke, or changed; titles: segment titles, not hype; visuals: manual upload only; CTA: partner; compliance: sponsor copy separated; repurposing: video to audio and newsletter; early: guest pipeline; long-term: sponsorship and media umbrella.
- Cross-cutting compliance patterns: paid-promotion disclosure at/near the mention on every monetizable surface; "no outcome certainty" language rule on Shorts; "state evidence level" on X/Threads; "no casino-style styling" on Reels; charts embedded "only if cleared" in newsletter; "sourced charts only" on X/Threads.
- Content pipeline logic: TikTok used for discovery/hook testing before long-form; Shorts supply 5–8 clips per long video; carousels convert video lessons into checklists; newsletter is the owned-audience conversion spine; podcast is future, not current.

# dfs/research/2026-09-13/verify/tier2-qbwr-salaries-2026-09-13.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A salary-verification dossier testing 13 claimed FanDuel salaries (Tier 2 QBs + WRs) for the Week 1 2026 NFL Classic Sun–Mon slate against a found FanDuel salary export and independent editorial sources, under a strict CONFIRMED (≥2 independent non-banned sources, same target slate) / CORRECTED / UNVERIFIABLE verdict standard. Bottom line: NO qualifying Sun–Mon-slate evidence was found for any of the 13 players — all 13 are UNVERIFIABLE for the target slate; 12 of 12 Sunday players' numbers match FanDuel's own Sunday-main-slate export exactly, and Dak Prescott ($8,300) has no qualifying evidence on any slate.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Verdict standard: CONFIRMED = ≥2 independent, non-banned sources agree on the same target-slate FanDuel salary; CORRECTED = credible target-slate evidence establishes a different salary; UNVERIFIABLE = fewer than two qualifying target-slate sources.
- Non-goals: Sunday-main-slate evidence NEVER promoted to Sun–Mon confirmations; DraftKings figures never counted.
- Banned sources (documented, not counted): FantasyPros, numberFire, DK Network, thehuddle, FantasyAlarm, FantasyLabs, FanDuel Research, RotoBaller, RotoWire, ESPN, CBS, NFL.com, PlayerProfiler, Reddit, FOX Sports, Sporting News, SI, Athlon, USA Today, NBC Sports.
- Slate: FanDuel NFL Classic Sun–Mon (Sept 13–14, 2026), $60,000 cap, 0.5 PPR.
- No formulas.

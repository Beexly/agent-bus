# dfs/research/2026-09-13/verify/defense-matchup-2026-09-13.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
A claim-verification dossier checking seven 2025 NFL team-defense claims (for Week 1 2026 DFS matchup analysis) against primary data — nflverse 2025 play-by-play, Pro-Football-Reference team pages, and StatMuse tables — with a CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / UNVERIFIABLE verdict per claim. Six of seven claims were confirmed exactly; one (Arizona EPA/dropback rank) was corrected from 26th to 27th.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Sack rate = defensive sacks ÷ defensive dropbacks (dropbacks = pass attempts including sacks; verified all 1,287 sack plays carry pass_attempt=1 in the dataset).
- EPA/dropback = sum of play EPA over defensive pass plays ÷ dropbacks (nflverse EPA model, 2025 regular season, `season_type == 'REG'`, attempts + sacks grouped by defending team).
- TE stats = completed passes to players rostered at position TE, grouped by `defteam`; fantasy points computed on half-PPR (FanDuel-style) and full-PPR bases.
- Red-zone TD rate = red-zone TDs ÷ red-zone trips (42/62 = 67.74% → 67.7%).
- Banned-source compliance list: no PFF, Sharp Football, FTN, FantasyPoints, Next Gen Stats, Warren Sharp, ESPN, NFL.com, CBS, NBC Sports, USA Today, SI, thehuddle, RotoBaller, RotoWire, FantasyAlarm, FantasyLabs, numberFire, FantasyPros, or PlayerProfiler data used. Analysis scripts retained at `~/workspace/dfs-research/verify/nflverse/`.

## Data sources named
nflverse `play_by_play_2025.csv` (tag `pbp`, https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/download/pbp/play_by_play_2025.csv.gz) + `roster_2025.csv` (tag `rosters`); Pro-Football-Reference `teams/was/2025.htm`, `teams/nor/2025.htm`, `teams/crd/2025.htm`; StatMuse (sacks table, rushing-yards-allowed table, passing-TDs-allowed table).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
1. **Saints 5th-ranked sack rate — CONFIRMED.** New Orleans: 45 sacks on 536 opponent dropbacks = 8.40%, ranked 5th of 32. Teams ahead: DEN 10.26% (68/663), MIN 9.86% (49/497), CLE 9.78% (53/542), ATL 9.64% (57/591). Next behind: LAC 8.15% (45/552). Cross-check with PFR/StatMuse opponent attempt totals (DEN 593, MIN 447, CLE 487, ATL 529, NO 491, LAC 505) reproduces identical ordering: NO 5th at 45/(491+45) = 8.40%. By raw sack total the Saints were only tied 10th–11th (45, tied with LAC); the rate rank is higher because they faced the 4th-fewest opponent pass attempts.
2. **Washington worst overall defense — CONFIRMED (by yards).** 6,533 total yards allowed — 32nd of 32 (last), the conventional "total defense" ranking. Qualification: by points allowed (451), Washington was 27th, not last.
3. **Washington 30th versus the run — CONFIRMED.** 2,411 rushing yards allowed on 504 attempts — 30th (3rd-most; only two teams allowed more).
4. **Washington fourth-worst versus tight ends — CONFIRMED (half-PPR basis).** Half-PPR fantasy points allowed to TEs: 219.0 — 4th-most (CIN 298.4, ARI 236.1, PIT 231.1, then WAS 219.0). Underlying: 86 receptions, 1,040 yards, 12 TDs. Context: full-PPR points allowed 262.0 (6th-most); TE receiving yards allowed 1,040 (7th-most); TE TDs allowed 12 (tied 2nd-most with TB, behind CIN's 16). The "fourth-worst" figure matches the half-PPR (FanDuel-style) defense-vs-position scoring exactly.
5. **Washington 67.7% red-zone TD rate — CONFIRMED.** Opponents: 42 TDs on 62 red-zone trips = 67.7%, PFR red-zone defense rank 31st.
6. **Arizona 26th in EPA/dropback allowed — CORRECTED to 27th.** nflverse EPA model, 2025 regular season: Arizona allowed +0.149 EPA per dropback — 27th of 32 (6th-worst), not 26th. Worst five: NYJ (+0.253), DAL (+0.217), WAS (+0.200), TEN (+0.167), CIN (+0.164), then ARI (+0.149). Best: HOU (−0.186). Caveat: EPA values vary slightly by model/provider; nflverse open model is the standard free reference. Closest conventional alternative from PFR: Arizona allowed 6.5 net yards/attempt (3,923 net pass yards on 572 att + 30 sacks), ranked 23rd — UNCERTAIN as to which rank a given provider would publish.
7. **Washington third-most passing TDs allowed — CONFIRMED (tied).** 33 passing TDs allowed — tied for 3rd-most with Cincinnati (33), behind the Jets (36) and Cowboys (35). Next: Bears 32. StatMuse's full-season table and nflverse pbp count agree exactly (WAS: 350 comp / 540 att / 33 TD). CONTRADICTION: PFR's team-page "Lg Rank Defense" cell for this column reads 25th, inconsistent with the sorted league table; the direct sorted count (tied 3rd-most) from two independent sources is treated as authoritative.

# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/1445-favorite-longshot-bias-prediction-markets-polymarket.md

## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep-research ledger (full 51-page read, Sections 1–8 + appendices A–E) of arXiv:2609.12878v1 (Cardozo & Rivero-Wildemauwe, 2026, econ.GN): "The Favorite–Longshot Bias in Prediction Markets: Evidence from Polymarket" — tests whether the favorite–longshot bias (low-priced bets earning lower average returns than high-priced bets) holds on Polymarket across aggregation schemes, categories, and wallet-level persistence. Verdict ADAPT.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Return per purchase (eq. 1, hold-to-resolution): `r_f = (y_f − p_f)/p_f` where y_f ∈ {0,1} is terminal payoff, p_f price paid; equivalently `r_f = (y_f q_f − p_f q_f)/(p_f q_f)`.
- Three aggregation schemes: (i) equal child-market weights — return per child market (dollar-weighted within market), averaged equally (eqs. 2–3: `R_mT = Σ_{f∈F_mT} q_f(y_f−p_f)/Σ p_f q_f`; `RTO = (1/|M_T|) Σ R_mT`); (ii) pooled purchases — single dollar-weighted return across all purchases (eq. 4: `RTD = Σ_m Σ_f q_f(y_f−p_f)/Σ_m Σ_f p_f q_f`); (iii) equal parent-event weights — purchases pooled within each parent event, then events averaged equally (eqs. 6–7: R_gT, RTP).
- Probability error (eq. 5): `E(A) = Σ q_f y_f/Σ q_f − Σ q_f p_f/Σ q_f` (token-weighted payoff-minus-price, in percentage points).
- Tails: longshots p < 0.10, favorites p ≥ 0.90; sensitivity at 5/95, 15/85, 20/80 (Appendix A.1).
- Wallet classification: per month, regress six-month tail purchase share on activity/category covariates, rank by residual, top decile = longshot/favorite groups; next-month purchases evaluated. Confidence intervals clustered by parent event throughout.
- Assumptions: (1) hold-to-resolution convention (actual sale timing ignored); (2) wallets ≠ people (one person may use several wallets and vice versa); (3) wash-trade filter via counterparty-HHI ≥ 0.5 exclusion; (4) returns exclude trading fees and liquidity rebates; (5) resolution coverage declines near sample end (markets must have resolved by cutoff).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
Tail returns (quoted exactly):
- Equal child-market weights: longshots **−6.30%** [−8.38, −4.22]%; favorites **+0.277%** [0.242, 0.312]%.
- Pooled purchases: longshots **−19.35%** [−46.99, +8.30]% (CI includes zero); favorites **+0.83%** [0.60, 1.07]%.
- Equal parent-event weights: longshots **+4.09%** [1.29, 6.90]%; favorites **+0.392%** [0.345, 0.440]%.
- Decomposition (Table 7): equal child→equal parent+child weight: **+10.329 pp** (events with more child markets have lower longshot returns); pooling within events: +0.063 pp; weighting events by longshot dollars: **−23.440 pp** (spending concentrates in low-return events).
- By category (equal child-market weights): Crypto longshots −14.84%; Politics −16.34% (all CIs exclude zero; two-sided). **Sports: longshots +2.43% [−0.93, 5.79]%, favorites −0.230% — no two-sided FLB.** Sports pooled: longshots +18.11% [−74.68, 110.91]%, favorites +0.137%. Weather: −25.15% equal-weight vs +24.74% pooled (sign flips).
- Wallet level: top longshot-demand decile supplies 26.6% of next-month longshot dollars; their return −21.96% vs −20.33% for others; share of gross longshot losses 22.6% < share of spending. Favorite decile: 15.1% of dollars, return +0.25% vs +0.88% for others, only 4.8% of aggregate net gain.
- Experience: all five experience groups lose on longshots (−38.34 to −21.55%); most-experienced earn within-comparison +0.83 pp [0.22, 1.44] pp above group averages (relative, not positive absolute).
- Robustness: losses persist after substantial previous trading (most-traded group −28.72%), via posted offers (−17.56% pooled) and accepted offers (−22.17%), with no reported fee (−20.78%), and with shared collateral (−25.76%).
- Short-horizon check: posted-offer longshot purchases gain +3.92/+16.91/+7.56% at 5 min/1 h/1 day, then fall to −17.56% at resolution — losses arise at resolution, not from stale posted prices.
- Limitations: returns are hypothetical (hold-to-resolution); actual realized PnL differs when traders sell early — do NOT misread −19.35% as a tradable short-longshot edge (interim prices were *above* purchase cost shortly after trades, +3.92% at 5 min). Sports longshot pooled CI enormous ([−74.68, 110.91]%) — "Sports is positive" rests more on the equal-child-market estimate. Coverage declines near sample end; cross-sectional comparisons descriptive, not causal. No NFL/betting-exchange (Betfair) data — sportsbook-style odds (esp. NFL spreads) may differ; the "Sports" category is dominated by match-winner markets, not point spreads.
- Reproducible test (ledger §12): The Odds API historical NFL moneylines 2021–2025 + nflverse outcomes; de-vig to probabilities; p < 0.10 longshots, p ≥ 0.90 favorites; hold-to-resolution return under three aggregations (equal game, dollar-equal-stake pooled, equal week); week-clustered CIs. Success for "FLB present in NFL moneylines": longshot return < 0 AND favorite return > 0 under BOTH primary aggregations.
- Acceptance gate (ledger §13): ADOPT the FLB audit as a standing GSE check if replication finds a statistically significant two-sided pattern (week-clustered CI excluding zero) under at least the equal-market aggregation — then build a tail-specific calibration correction for GSE's moneyline probabilities. REJECT a longshot-correction feature if NFL moneylines show the paper's Sports-category result (positive longshot returns or non-positive favorite returns).

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- OTHER (calibration program — tail-specific): the two-sided FLB with aggregation-unit sensitivity is a hard audit for GSE's own de-vigged probability surfaces — run market-level vs event-level vs dollar-weighted FLB checks on GSE's NFL moneyline probabilities. If GSE's probabilities show the same "probability error small, return large" amplification at low prices, hard-cap longshot stake sizing or apply probability-error-based calibration at the tails. Serves the calibration/sizing program.
- OTHER (market-efficiency / model-correction lane): the empirical fact that **Sports shows no two-sided FLB** (+2.43% longshots, −0.230% favorites) is a caution against importing generic FLB-based corrections (e.g., Snowberg-Wolfers-style probability adjustments) into GSE's NFL moneyline modeling — do not apply mechanical longshot corrections to NFL prices until the §12 replication proves FLB exists in NFL moneylines specifically. This is a guardrail, not just a finding.
- OTHER (trust-target/props intake): the wallet-level result — recurrent tail demand ≠ disproportionate losses (top longshot decile's 22.6% loss share < its 26.6% dollar share; top favorite decile captures only 4.8% of aggregate net gain) — informs GSE's props/ensemble customer-behavior modeling: do not treat recurrent longshot-taking as a marker of disproportionate loss-bearing. Relevant to the trust-target intake program.
- OTHER (timing/price-path insight): the short-horizon result (+3.92%/+16.91%/+7.56% interim gains at 5 min/1 h/1 day, falling to −17.56% at resolution) shows the FLB loss is an *outcome* phenomenon, not an entry-pricing one — so GSE's edge comes from modeling outcome tails, not from finding "better" longshot prices. The improvement experiment proposes a full price-path decomposition on NFL in-play/pre-game steam (adverse interim movement vs resolution outcome) as the test.
- CONTRADICTION: the pooled-purchases result (longshots −19.35%, CI including zero) vs equal-child-market result (−6.30%, CI excluding zero) is not a contradiction in the paper's own framing but a methodological warning for GSE: the *sign of the measured FLB depends on the aggregation unit* — any GSE claim of "FLB in NFL moneylines" must be stated conditional on aggregation, and single-aggregation claims are incomplete.
- UNCERTAIN: whether the Sports-category result (no two-sided FLB, Polymarket match-winner markets) transfers to NFL point spreads or sportsbook moneylines — the paper has no NFL/betting-exchange data, and the ledger flags this explicitly. Treat "no FLB correction on NFL prices" as provisional until the §12 NFL-moneyline replication is run.

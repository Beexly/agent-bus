# research/2026-09-19-dk-week2/deep/referee-crews-mnf-totals-2026-09-19.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
All 16 Week 2 2026 NFL referee crew assignments (Football Zebras) joined to 2019–2025 crew penalty/ATS/totals data (RotoWire Referee Impact Calculator, 1,886 games, min 40 games per official) plus a multi-source history of Monday Night Football totals (2019–2025), for DFS game-environment analysis (pace/plays/penalty-driven drive extension). Compiled 2026-09-19; not betting advice.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Penalties/game = combined accepted + offsetting flags per game, 2019–2025 (RotoWire). League average ≈ 12.3.
- RotoWire significance-tested all 17 officials simultaneously: **only Alan Eck's low penalty rate survives correction ("strong signal")**; everything else is "suggestive" or "not significant." Totals splits explicitly flagged as the dataset's least trustworthy referee metric — none clear significance.
- Reading rule used in file: fewer flags (Eck 10.9, Allen 11.1, Novak 11.1) → fewer free first downs via DPI/holding, drives must be earned, marginal drag on total plays; more flags (Blakeman 13.2, Rogers 13.1, Hochuli 13.0, Hill 13.0) → more drive extension, especially Hill (No. 1 in DPI/roughing auto-first-downs since 2021) and Blakeman (top-5 flag volume + yardage). Pre-snap heavy (Hussey 74% W1; Hill No. 1 in false starts) → drive-killers that offset the extension effect.

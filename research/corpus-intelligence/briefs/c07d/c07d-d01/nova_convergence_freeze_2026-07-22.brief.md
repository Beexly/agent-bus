## What it is (1-2 sentences)
The binding convergence freeze (2026-07-22, owner directive) between the NOVA opportunity-engine branch (#146) and the AI control-plane stack (#162→#163→#164): it assigns canonical ownership of each domain, freezes read-model consumption contracts, and sequences #146's landing through six ordered split units with hard preconditions and acceptance gates.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- Canonical ownership table — 6 domains, one owner each (no exceptions): Credit-program lifecycle → **NOVA**; AI invocation policy/routing/attempts/budgets/financial attribution → **AI control plane**; Sports settlement observations/anomalies/grading/decisions → **Settlement domain (#161)**; Actor identity & audit receipt → **Shared infrastructure (#159 TrustedActor)**; Transactional outbox & idempotent delivery → **Shared infrastructure (#161's PickSettlementEvent pattern, generalized once)**; Source monitoring, opportunity lifecycle, Founder OS, owner decision queue, revenue prioritization → **NOVA**.
- Credit truth flow (NOVA → control plane, never reverse): control plane consumes only an immutable snapshot read model — blueprint name `CreditGrantSnapshot` — of shape `{program, providerScope, state, remainingUsd, expiresAt, observedAt, sourceReceipt}`, written exclusively by NOVA-side reconcilers/attestation. Rules: "No receipt, no snapshot. Stale snapshot, no credit admission." `CONFIRMED_CREDITS_ONLY` admits a provider only on a **fresh, covering, sufficient** snapshot; everything else fails closed.
- `MoneyState` (types.ts:73) — 13-value union: `not_applicable | hypothetical | discovered | eligibility_unverified | eligible | applied | approved | activated | earned | invoiced | paid | expired | rejected`. Transition machine (`lifecycle.ts`): `MONEY_STATE_ORDER` (forward-only through `paid`; `expired`/`rejected` terminal), enforced by `canTransitionMoneyState()` / `assertMoneyStateTransition()`. Credits carried as `OpportunityEconomics.availableCredits?: EconomicRange | null` alongside `moneyState: MoneyState` (types.ts:186–191).
- Binding for PR-D: `CreditGrantSnapshot.state` vocabulary (`APPROVED|ACTIVATED|PARTIALLY_CONSUMED|EXHAUSTED|EXPIRED|REVOKED`) is NOT yet expressible in `MoneyState` (no consumed/exhausted/revoked) → per the NOVA-name-wins rule, S1 adds a `CreditGrantState` refinement (a per-grant sub-state of an `activated` MoneyState opportunity); PR-D consumes that refinement through the §3.1 snapshot interface and does not mint its own vocabulary.
- S3 acceptance gate: a fetch producing no reproducible immutable receipt is `HELD`/`FAILED`, never promoted; historical FAILED_CLOSED receipts stay labeled failed.
- Collision policy: control-plane names win for invocation/telemetry (counterpart marked SUPERSEDED, useful fields/tests folded in — never both); NOVA names win for credit concepts (control plane consumes, never redefines). Verified prophylactic: `git grep` finds zero collisions.
- `FounderWorkAuthority = "AGENT_INTERNAL"|"OWNER_ONLY"|"AGENT_THEN_OWNER"`. No numeric formulas in the file.

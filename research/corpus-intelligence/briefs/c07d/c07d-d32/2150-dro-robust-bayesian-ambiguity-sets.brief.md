# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2150-dro-robust-bayesian-ambiguity-sets.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
arXiv:2505.03585 (Dellaporta, O'Hara & Damoulas, 2025): DRO-RoBAS — distributionally robust optimization with ambiguity sets centered at a *misspecification-robust* posterior predictive (Bayesian Nonparametric Learning with MMD loss) instead of the standard posterior or empirical distribution, with a tractable RKHS dual and high-probability guarantees on the ambiguity radius ε. The ledger verdict is ADAPT, framed as the missing *outer* decision layer for the engine: it gives stake/pick decisions worst-case guarantees that survive model misspecification, the exact failure mode of naive DRO (optimizer's curse or degenerate conservatism).

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas copied verbatim from the file:
- DRO game: `min_{x∈X} sup_{P∈A} E_{ξ∼P}[f_x(ξ)]` (1).
- BDRO: `min_x E_{Π(θ|ξ_{1:n})}[sup_{P: d_KL(P‖P_θ)≤ε} E_P[f_x]]` (2).
- DRO-BAS_PP: KL ball around posterior predictive (3); DRO-BAS_PE: expected-KL ball (4).
- DRO-RoBAS: `min_x sup_{P: MMD(P, P̂_n^{pred,NPL}) ≤ ε} E_{ξ∼P}[f_x(ξ)]`, where the nominal P̂ is the posterior predictive of the NPL-MMD robust Bayesian posterior; tractable dual in the reproducing kernel Hilbert space; high-probability bounds on ε so the ambiguity set contains the DGP and the DRO objective upper-bounds the true objective.
- Newsvendor cost: `f(x,ξ) = h·max(x−ξ,0) + b·max(ξ−x,0)`, with b=8, h=3.
- MMD discrepancy with a Gaussian kernel (kernel choice affects everything; not automated).
- Baselines: BDRO (Shapiro et al. 2023, expected worst-case over standard posterior + KL ball), DRO-BAS_PP / DRO-BAS_PE (Dellaporta et al. 2024, KL balls around posterior predictive / expected KL), empirical MMD (Staib & Jegelka 2019, MMD ball around empirical measure).
- Flexibility claim: works for any model family, including likelihood-free models (DRO-BAS_PE is limited to exponential families; BDRO/DRO-BAS_PP need closed-form or MCMC posteriors).

## Data sources named
- Simulated only — no real-data validation. Newsvendor: demand ξ, n=20 observations, J=100 repetitions, D=1 and 5. Portfolio: 5-D Gaussian returns, n=100, weights x≥0, Σx_i=1, maximize return.
- Misspecification cases: (1) bimodal Gaussian DGP vs unimodal Gaussian model; (2) Huber contamination η∈{0, 0.1, 0.2} of the training set (Gaussian→Gaussian, Exponential→Gaussian, 5-D Gaussian on 3/5 dims).
- Contamination model assumes test = clean distribution P̃ (the paper's assumption, flagged as mismatched to sports where the next week's games can themselves contain the outliers).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- **Bimodal DGP, misspecified unimodal model (newsvendor):** standard posteriors concentrate *between* the modes → DRO-BAS/BDRO need huge ε → conservative, high out-of-sample cost. DRO-RoBAS's NPL-MMD posterior is bimodal despite the unimodal model; consistently lower out-of-sample variance than empirical MMD (univariate); competitive in multivariate (empirical MMD slightly better on mean, unaffected by misspecification by construction).
- **Huber contamination (newsvendor):** at η=0, DRO-BAS beats RoBAS — the well-specified case favors standard Bayes. At η>0, RoBAS shows the best out-of-sample mean-variance tradeoff; even empirical MMD degrades (training≠test discrepancy hits empirical methods too).
- **Portfolio, η=0.1:** for ε<0.2, empirical DRO degrades fast while RoBAS is unaffected; at ε≥0.2 they converge (MMD's intrinsic robustness kicks in). At η=0.2 the gap magnifies — the empirical nominal is corrupted by outliers; RoBAS's robust nominal is not.
- **Cost:** RoBAS is computationally heavier (RKHS optimization + NPL sampling); scalability via Fourier features/low-rank approximations left to future work.
- Validation design: J=100 repetitions; n=20 (newsvendor) / n=100 (portfolio); out-of-sample mean and variance of cost/return on fresh DGP draws; ε swept (markers per ε); well-specified (η=0) vs contaminated (η=0.1, 0.2) vs structurally misspecified (bimodal DGP) comparisons.
- Limitations stated: simulations only; robustness has a price (a misspecification detector should gate which method to use); ε still must be chosen; guarantees are high-probability not exact; kernel choice affects everything and isn't automated; weekly re-solving may be heavy without the suggested approximations; NPL requires prior beliefs about the DGP — another modeling choice that can itself be wrong.
- Referenced external results: NPL (Lyddon et al. 2018; Fong et al. 2019); BDRO (Shapiro et al. 2023); DRO-BAS_PP/DRO-BAS_PE (Dellaporta et al. 2024); empirical MMD (Staib & Jegelka 2019). No public code link extracted; implementation details in Appendix B.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- **OTHER (decision layer / calibration/sizing):** This is the engine-actionable core — the robust-Kelly decision rule: stake = Kelly fraction computed against the *worst-case* distribution in the MMD ball (adversarial expected log-growth), not the nominal; bet/no-bet gate = post only if worst-case edge > 0. Serves the **calibration/sizing** program directly. The implementation spec: replace the engine's point-estimate outcome distribution with an NPL-MMD robust posterior predictive per game (bootstrap-resample historical game outcomes with MMD-based reweighting, downweighting contaminated/outlier games — tanking teams, backup-QB games, weather anomalies, COVID-season weirdness); MMD ball (Gaussian kernel on the outcome space: {home win by >3, ...} or point-differential bins) around the robust predictive, ε calibrated per the paper's high-probability rule on a validation window.
- **OTHER (regime detection / gating):** The paper's own finding that standard DRO-BAS beats RoBAS at η=0 with lower compute motivates a **misspecification gate**: run a quick MMD two-sample check between recent outcomes and the model's predictive; if misspecification is low, fall back to standard (cheaper) sizing. Serves the **tracking lane** (regime/coaching-change monitoring) — an adaptive per-game ε from the misspecification detector (high MMD distance → larger ball) is proposed as the improvement experiment beyond the paper, as is learning the kernel (deep kernel on game features) so the MMD ball respects football-relevant similarity (divisional games near each other in kernel space).
- **OTHER (contradiction to watch / CONTRADICTION-adjacent):** The ledger connects to ledger 2143's composite risk framework, which has an *outer* risk over uncertain distribution parameters but uses a standard (non-robust) posterior — exactly the fragility this paper fixes. Also flags that naive empirical DRO for the engine would fail per the paper's Fig. 1 (right): stretched sets, absurd conservatism → posting nothing — relevant to the engine's bet-volume floors (the acceptance gate rejects if robust Kelly posts <50% of baseline bet volume).
- **OTHER (diagnostics):** The "optimizer's curse" diagnostic = gap between predicted edge and realized edge (should shrink under RoBAS); acceptance requires final bankroll ≥ 1.1× best baseline AND max drawdown ≤ 0.85× best baseline's AND the predicted-vs-realized edge gap ≤ 0.7× the standard-Kelly baseline's. Stress split: evaluate separately on "contaminated" weeks (backup QBs, extreme weather, divisional chaos) vs clean weeks.

## Engine-actionable? (yes/no + one-line what)
**Yes** — ADOPT as the outer decision layer: robust-Kelly sizing against worst-case edge in an MMD ball around an NPL-MMD robust posterior predictive, with a misspecification detector gating standard vs robust sizing; ~2–3 weeks (NPL-MMD resampling, kernel MMD machinery, robust-Kelly solver), weekly batch before slate finalization, log ε and MMD distance as audit fields.

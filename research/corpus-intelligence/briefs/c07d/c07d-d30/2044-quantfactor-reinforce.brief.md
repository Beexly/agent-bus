# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/2044-quantfactor-reinforce.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Deep read (ledger completed 2026-09-22, verdict ADAPT) of Zhao et al. (arXiv:2409.05144v3): QuantFactor REINFORCE (QFR), an RL miner for formulaic alpha factors over RPN token sequences that replaces AlphaGen's PPO with plain REINFORCE plus a theoretically variance-bounded greedy baseline and Information-Ratio (IR) reward shaping that pushes the policy toward "steady" factors (consistently predictive across volatility regimes). The ledger maps the steadiness idea to GSE's need for signals that survive regime change — with the Dirac-MDP framing needing re-validation on weekly sports panels.

## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
- MDP formulation (from AlphaGen): states = partial RPN token sequences; actions = next token from operator/feature vocabulary; transition deterministic (Dirac — next state is uniquely current sequence + chosen token); reward sparse: r(s_t, a_t) = 0 for t≠T, r(a_{1:T}) = IC-bar of the completed formula's factor values vs. 5-day returns at step T. Objective: J(θ) = E_{a_{1:T}~π_θ}[r(a_{1:T})] (eq. 4–5). Syntactically valid but non-evaluable expressions (e.g., Log of negative) get penalized/zeroed.
- QFR innovations:
  1. Drop the critic (no actor-critic): deterministic transitions + trajectory-level feedback make PPO's critic add bias without benefit; plain REINFORCE is argued more appropriate.
  2. Variance-bounded baseline: novel subtractive baseline generated by a greedy policy (argmax token at each step); paper proves an upper bound on post-baseline variance lower than vanilla REINFORCE (Sec. IV-C propositions; proofs in appendix).
  3. IR reward shaping: shaped term rewarding the Information Ratio of the mined factor, gated by an "IR test" that activates after time delay α = 9×10⁴ steps, ramps with slope η = 2.65×10⁻⁶, capped at δ = 0.3, weighted by λ = 0.02 (eq. 11: r_shaped = r + λ · clip-IR-test term with indicator I(·)). Pushes toward factors consistently predictive across volatility regimes rather than short-term IC spikes.
- Evaluation metric: RankIC(z'_t, y_t) = IC(r(z'_t), r(y_t)), r(·) = ranking operator.
- Operator tokens: cross-sectional (Abs, Log, +/−/×/÷, Larger, Smaller) and time-series (Ref, Mean/Median/Sum/Std/Var/Max/Min/Mad/Delta/WMA/EMA/Cov/Corr with lookback l).
- Training: policy = LSTM feature extractor (2-layer, hidden 128, dropout 0.1); 5 random seeds; compared on equal footing with PPO/A3C/TRPO sharing the same extractor (PPO clip ε=0.2, TRPO δ=1.1×10⁻⁵). Hardware: single machine, i9-13900K + 2× RTX 4090.
- Assumptions: deterministic (Dirac) transitions — true by construction for RPN generation; greedy-rollout baseline near-optimal enough to reduce variance (not proven to be the optimal baseline); IR stability in training transfers to live stability; 5-day return is the right horizon; IC-bar is the right factor-quality proxy.

## Data sources named
- Six equity-index constituent panels: CSI300, CSI500, CSI1000, SPX, DJI, NDX; raw data from Chinese A-shares + US markets via Qlib. Six features only: open, close, high, low, volume, vwap. Target: 5-day forward asset return. Splits: train 2016-01-01–2020-01-01, validation 2020-01-01–2021-01-01, test 2021-01-01–2024-01-01 (time-ordered). Prices forward-dividend-adjusted to 2023-01-15 factors.
- Follows open-source implementations of AlphaGen, gplearn, Stable Baselines 3, Qlib. No new code URL stated in the paper ("None stated" for a QFR repo).
- Cross-referenced corpus items: 2042 (grammar), 2043 (AlphaForge — QFR as an alternative *miner* to AlphaForge's generator-predictor; QFR-mined factors could feed the AlphaForge-style zoo + dynamic combination).

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Table V (IC / RankIC, 5-seed std in parens). CSI300: MLP 0.0123/0.0178, XGB 0.0192/0.0241, LGBM 0.0158/0.0235, GP 0.0445/0.0673, AlphaGen 0.0500/0.0540, **QFR 0.0588(0.0022)/0.0602(0.0014)**. CSI500: MLP 0.0158/0.0211, XGB 0.0173/0.0217, LGBM 0.0112/0.0212, GP 0.0557/0.0665, AlphaGen 0.0544/0.0722, **QFR 0.0708(0.0063)/0.0674(0.0033)**.
- Vs. AlphaGen's PPO on six indices: QFR improves RankIC by **3.83%** (headline claim), winning on all six, largest margin late in training.
- Reward-shaping hyperparameters: η = 2.65×10⁻⁶ best trade-off; α below 7×10⁴ harms quality (IR test too early); robust to δ (settled 0.3). λ = 0.02, α = 9×10⁴.
- Backtest: QFR's mined factors give the highest terminal cumulative return among all methods on CSI300 2021–2024 (Fig. 7), and the most PnL in all three CIMV volatility regimes, with the largest edge in the high-volatility period (Fig. 8). Three CIMV regimes: high vol 2021-01-01–2021-03-24 (CIMV>20%); rapid decline 2022-07-01–2022-09-15; low vol 2023-01-01–2023-03-31.
- Ablation: removing either the greedy baseline or the IR shaping degrades learning curves on all six indices (Fig. 9).
- Limitations flagged: reward-shaping hyperparameters (η, α, δ, λ) tuned on the same indices used for evaluation — "appropriate slope" claim risks tuning-to-test; IC-only optimization with shaping toward IR is internally inconsistent (they optimize IC but compare on RankIC because shaped rewards aren't comparable — metric sleight-of-hand flattering learning curves); 5-day forward return with vwap features: no strict point-in-time audit, possible lookahead; no empirical measurement of actual gradient-variance reduction reported; backtest ignores transaction costs (index-enhancement top-50 with rebalancing is turnover-heavy); external validity: equity cross-sections are dense (hundreds of names × daily) vs weekly NFL panel (~32 teams × 18 weeks) — MDP sample efficiency at that scale unproven.

## Intelligence connections (tag each: QB-BEHAVIOR, COACHING, OL, TRUST-SIGNAL, SCHEME, OTHER)
- [OTHER — automated signal mining / "invented stats" mandate] New capability; no RL-based signal miner exists in the research map. This is the machine behind the self-learning engine's "statistical engineering to invent stats humans never thought of" mandate: MDP over sports formula tokens (state = partial RPN sequence, actions = operator/feature tokens over team-game stats + line-movement features, deterministic transitions, terminal reward = IC of formula vs. ATS cover residual on training seasons), policy = small LSTM/Transformer (hidden 64 suffices since sports vocab is smaller), REINFORCE + greedy-rollout baseline. Serves the trust-signal intake program — mined formulas feed the zoo.
- [OTHER — steadiness/regime-robustness shaping] The core transferable idea: replace IR with a *regime-robustness* term for sports — IC computed separately on early-season / mid-season / late-season splits, shaped reward = min (or harmonic mean) across splits, so the miner favors signals that work in all phases, not just one; keep the α-delay gating so shaping kicks in after the policy first finds valid formulas. The improvement experiment goes further: forward-walk IR — evaluate candidates on expanding-window out-of-sample folds during training and use the worst-fold IC as the shaped reward, stricter than the paper and directly targeting Garrett's failure mode (signals that die out of sample). Serves the calibration/sizing program (regime-stable signals size better).
- [OTHER — factor-set synergy] Second improvement experiment: co-mine a small set jointly with a diversity bonus (like AlphaForge's zoo penalty) instead of mining factors one-by-one, since QFR as written mines single factors and never addresses factor-set synergy. Serves the signal-zoo construction program.
- [SCHEME / COACHING — seasonal-regime interpretation] The early/mid/late-season split shaping implicitly encodes coaching-driven regime structure (scheme installation early, adjustments mid, attrition/playoff-picture late) — mined factors that survive all three splits are robust to coaching adaptation cycles. UNCERTAIN: this is an INFERENCE about what the splits capture, not a claim in the paper.
- [OTHER — evaluation hygiene warning] The paper's metric sleight-of-hand (optimize IC, compare on RankIC because shaped rewards aren't comparable) plus tuning shaping hyperparameters on the evaluation indices is a cautionary template for the GSE acceptance gate: the ledger's gate therefore requires beating GP by ≥20% relative RankIC on the 2022–2025 test block (nflverse team-game panel; train 2009–2018, validate 2019–2021, test 2022–2025) with ≤ the same GPU budget, AND each top-10 mined factor passing a White's-reality-check gate (p < 0.05) on the test block; REJECT if no better than GP or if factors show early-season-only IC (shaping failed to generalize).

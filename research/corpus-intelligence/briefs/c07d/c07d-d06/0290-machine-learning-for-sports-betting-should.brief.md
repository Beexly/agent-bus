# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0290-machine-learning-for-sports-betting-should.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Walsh & Joshi (2024, arXiv:2303.06021v4, Machine Learning with Applications) run two identical NBA betting pipelines that differ ONLY in the model-selection criterion — classification accuracy vs classwise expected calibration error — and show calibration-driven selection beat accuracy-driven selection by +34.69% vs −35.17% average ROI (and saved eighth-Kelly from ruin: +36.93% vs −75.9%), directly mandating calibration-based model selection for any GSE probability engine.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas (verbatim):
- Eq. 1 (Kelly): k = (pb − q)/b, where p = model probability, q = 1 − p, b = odds − 1.
- Eq. 2 (perfect calibration): P(Ŷ = Y | P̂ = p) = p ∀ p ∈ [0,1].
- Eq. 3 (classwise-ECE): (1/k)Σ_i Σ_j (|B_{j,i}|/n) |y_i(B_{j,i}) − p̄_i(B_{j,i})|, with k classes, m bins of equal width over [0,1], bin-size-weighted.
- Eq. 4 (LR): P(Y_i = C|X_i) = exp(X_iβ)/(1 + exp(X_iβ)).
- Eq. 5 (negative log-likelihood): −Σ[y_i log f(X_iβ) + (1−y_i) log(1−f(X_iβ))].
- Eq. 6 (SVM): argmin_w {(1/n)Σ max(0, 1 − y_i f(x_i)) + C w^T w}.
- Kelly simulation (Table 1): K = (P × Bankroll − (1−P))/Bankroll (Kelly fraction on stake scale); stake = (1/8)×K×Bankroll (eighth-Kelly) or $100 fixed.
Pipeline: candidate algorithms = logistic regression, random forest, SVM, MLP. Common filter: drop one of each Spearman-|ρ|>0.7 correlated pair (keep the feature most correlated with target). Branch A (accuracy): sequential forward selection (SFS, LR learner) maximising accuracy → subset C; BO-TPE hyperparameter optimisation minimising negative accuracy; pick the model with best test accuracy. Branch B (calibration): SFS minimising classwise-ECE (20 bins) with an 80%-nonempty-bins guard (if violated, ECE set to 1) → subset B; BO-TPE minimising classwise-ECE; pick the model with lowest test classwise-ECE. Feature engineering novelty: features are season-to-date averages of differences in box-score stats vs opponents (relative "out-performance"), claimed more robust to league drift than absolute averages (citing Dutta et al. 2017); covariate shift handled by two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests at 1% on validation vs training distributions — failing features dropped. Betting: strategy = bet every value bet (model P > bookmaker implied 1/O); rules = fixed $100 stakes or eighth-Kelly sizing; starting bankroll $10,000; games processed chronologically. Assumptions: bookmaker implied probability treated as proxy for true probability (citing Wheatcroft 2020) in the scatter analysis; covariate shift tested at 1% significance; ≥80% of ECE bins must be non-empty else ECE := 1 (prevents "predict the base rate" degenerate calibration). Target: binary home-team victory per NBA game (no draws). Validation: chronological splits (cross-validation explicitly rejected as invalid for sports) — initial training 2014/15–2015/16 (feature selection, HPO; 2016/17 as validation); extended training 2014/15–2016/17 → model selection on test 2017/18; final fit 2014/15–2017/18; betting simulation on single 2018/19 season with Westgate closing moneylines. Baselines: accuracy-branch vs calibration-branch under identical strategy/rules; metrics: accuracy, classwise-ECE (20 bins), % games bet on, % bets won, final bankroll, ROI.

## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Test-set (2017/18) scores — calibration branch (classwise-ECE): SVM 3.23%, MLP 3.59%, LR 3.61%, RF 4.39% (SVM selected). Accuracy branch (accuracy): SVM 66.55%, LR 65.69%, MLP 65.69%, RF 65.34% (SVM selected).
- Feature subsets: calibration branch (subset B) = 3P, BLK, FT%, ORB, AST%, Previous Season Winning Percentage; accuracy branch (subset C) = STL%, eFG%, DRB, AST, DRtg, ORtg, 3P, Previous Season Winning Percentage (features are home-minus-away differences of season-to-date average out-performance vs opponents).
- Betting simulation on 2018/19 ($10,000 start) — fixed $100 betting: calibration SVM $13,244.51 (ROI 32.45%), accuracy SVM $10,556.29 (ROI 5.56%). Eighth-Kelly: calibration SVM $13,692.86 (ROI 36.93%), accuracy SVM $2,409.66 (ROI −75.9%).
- Summary (Table 6): calibration-driven SVM — 87.55% of games bet, 38.8% bets won, accuracy 64.27%, classwise-ECE 4.46%, max ROI 36.93%, average ROI 34.69%. Accuracy-driven SVM — 89.89% of games bet, 38.46% won, accuracy 64.62%, classwise-ECE 5.03%, max ROI 5.56%, average ROI −35.17%.
- Abstract headline: average ROIs +34.69% versus −35.17%; best case +36.93% versus +5.56%.
- Supporting observation: accuracy-driven model identified more false-positive value bets; its value-bet predictions formed two clusters at probability extremes (variance 0.036 vs 0.034 for the calibrated model) — overconfidence on value bets that self-destructs under Kelly.
- Limitations (file's own): single betting season (2018/19) — no cross-season replication, no significance test on ROI (significance argument qualitative); both final models were SVMs, so the calibration/accuracy contrast is partly confounded by HPO/feature-subset differences (subsets B and C differ in 6/8 features) — the "selection paradigm" bundles feature selection + HPO + model choice; the 80%-nonempty-bins ECE guard is acknowledged as "somewhat arbitrary"; NBA moneyline only, NFL spreads/totals transfer untested; no transaction-cost/slippage/limits modelling; closing odds assumed obtainable (Westgate published odds ≠ always bettable at scale). The fixed-stakes contrast (32.45% vs 5.56%) is the cleaner number than the Kelly-amplified gap.
## Intelligence connections
- (TRUST-SIGNAL → calibration/sizing program) Direct ADOPT mandate: run GSE model selection on classwise-ECE (20 bins, with the 80%-nonempty-bins guard that sets ECE=1 when violated) rather than accuracy for any moneyline/spread probability model, and replace full Kelly with fractional Kelly ≤ 1/8 in any sizing module. The mechanism — accuracy-selected models are overconfident on value bets (probability clustering at extremes, variance 0.036 vs 0.034) and self-destruct under Kelly (−75.9% ROI) while the calibrated model survives (+36.93%) — maps exactly onto GSE's bet-sizing lane gap #1 (Kelly under estimation error, mentioned 12× in corpus, zero papers read). The paper's bundled two-branch design (SFS-on-ECE, BO-TPE-on-ECE, Spearman |ρ|>0.7 filter, KS covariate-shift test at 1%) is a directly portable bake-off protocol.
- (QB-BEHAVIOR / OTHER) UNCERTAIN: the reported ROI gap is driven heavily by the Kelly interaction with miscalibration, and the selection-paradigm comparison is confounded (subsets B/C differ in 6/8 features; both winners were SVMs). Treat the fixed-stakes 32.45% vs 5.56% as the headline evidence, not the Kelly-amplified numbers. Single-season NBA result — needs the NFL replication (proposed gate: ECE-selected model achieves ≥3 pp lower classwise-ECE than accuracy-selected AND beats its ROI under both fixed and 1/8-Kelly on 2024 NFL moneylines) before promotion.
- (OTHER) Improvement experiment the paper leaves on the table: hybrid two-stage selection — first constrain to feature subsets within 1 pp of minimum 20-bin ECE, then pick the most accurate among them (Pareto frontier of accuracy vs ECE) — tests whether accuracy still carries signal once calibration is satisfied; plus repeating on NFL spreads (three classes: cover/push/fail) to test whether the calibration advantage survives away from binary moneylines.

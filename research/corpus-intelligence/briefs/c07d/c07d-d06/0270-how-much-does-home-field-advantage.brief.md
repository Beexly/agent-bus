# arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0270-how-much-does-home-field-advantage.md
## What it is (1-2 sentences)
Price, Cai, Shen & Hu (2022, arXiv:2205.07193) estimate the *causal* (not merely correlational) effect of home-field advantage in the 2020–21 English Premier League via a hierarchical causal model with paired home/away differencing: summing each home/away fixture pair cancels the neutral-field nuisance term, identifying team-level causal home effects β_i and a league effect Δ from 380 games (20 teams, full double round-robin) across 11 offensive/defensive/referee statistics.
## Key metrics/methods (formulas where given, else "not specified")
Formulas (verbatim, with equation numbers as in the paper):
- (1) Team causal effect: β_i = E_j{Y*(δ_i=1, δ_j=0) − Y*(δ_i=0, δ_j=0)}, i = 1..n.
- (2) League causal effect: Δ = E_i{β_i}; finite-sample estimand Δ ≡ Σ_i β_i/n.
- (3) Match model: Y_{i,j} = α_{i,j} + β_i + ε_{i,j}; α_{i,j} = E{Y*(δ_i=0, δ_j=0)}, ε_{i,j} ~ N(0, σ₀²).
- (4) Hierarchical: β_i ~ N(Δ, σ²).
- (5) Return fixture: Y_{j,i} = −α_{i,j} + β_j + ε_{j,i}, with antisymmetry α_{i,j} = −α_{j,i}.
- (6) Paired cancellation: Y_{i,j} + Y_{j,i} = β_i + β_j + ε_{i,j} + ε_{j,i}.
- (7) Linear system: H_{N/2×n} β_{n×1} + ε_{N/2×1} = Y_{N/2×1}; estimator β̂ = (HᵀH)⁻¹ H Y (paper prints (HᵀH)⁻¹H Y, missing the trailing ᵀ on the second H — typesetting slip for (HᵀH)⁻¹HᵀY).
- (8) Δ̂ = Σ_i β̂_i/n.
- (9) Team CI: β̂ ± z_{α/2}√(diag{σ̂²_β(HᵀH)⁻¹}), σ̂²_β = 2‖Hβ̂−Y‖²₂/N.
- (10) Unbiased league-variance estimator via law of total variance: σ̂² = (1/(n−1))Σ_i(β̂_i−Δ̂)² − (1/n)Σ_i diag{σ̂²_β(HᵀH)⁻¹}_i.
- (11) League CI: Δ̂ ± z_{α/2}√(σ̂²/n).
Assumptions: (A1) SUTVA — Y_{i,j} equals the potential outcome under realized venue assignment; (A2) Ignorability — potential outcomes independent of venue assignment, holds automatically because the schedule is pre-determined; (A3) Non-monotonicity — no transitivity of team dominance (rules out correlated α across matches involving the same team); i.i.d. Gaussian noise; β_i Gaussian for exact (not merely asymptotic) inference; n ≥ 3 so H is full rank. Prop. 3.1: exact normality of β̂ − β with estimated covariance σ̂²_β(HᵀH)⁻¹.
Simulation design: n ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80}; σ₀² ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}; Δ = 1 fixed; β_i ~ N(1, 0.3²); 1000 replicates; two α_{i,j} scenarios (i.i.d. N(0,2²) antisymmetric; or α_{i,j} = Ab_i − Ab_j with Ab_i ~ N(0,2²)). Metrics: bias, coverage probability (nominal 95%), sample variance of estimates (SV), mean of variance estimates (MV).
## Data sources named
Hudl & Wyscout scouting data (proprietary; not publicly downloadable) for the 2020–21 EPL season (380 games, 20 teams). Eleven team-level per-game statistics: Attacks w/ Shot, Defence Interceptions, Reaching Opponent Box, Reaching Opponent Half, Shots Blocked, Shots from Box, Shots from Danger Zone, Successful Key Passes, Touches in Box, Expected Goals (XG), Yellow Cards (referee-bias proxy). Simulations are synthetic and fully described (§4.1) — no proprietary data needed. Descriptives: 144 home wins (37.89%) vs 153 away wins (40.26%); 514 home goals (50.19%) vs 510 away goals (49.81%) of 1024; per-stat home-mean vs away-mean in Table 1 (XG: home 1.577 vs away 1.395; Yellow Cards: home 1.447 vs away 1.474).
## Findings (numbers and facts, not vibes)
- Simulation (Table 2, Δ̂): biases tiny across all scenarios — e.g., scenario 1, n=20, σ₀²=1: bias −0.0059; scenario 2, n=80, σ₀²=2: bias −0.0010. Coverage approaches 95% nominal as n grows: n=10 CPs 0.882–0.913; n=20: 0.914–0.950; n=80: 0.947–0.955 (SE of CP estimate = √(0.05·0.95/1000) = 0.0069, per paper). SV and MV match closely in all rows — e.g., scenario 1, n=40, σ₀²=1: SV 0.0030, MV 0.0030. Team-level β̂ boxplots "essentially unbiased" in all scenarios (Figures 4–7); CPs "fairly close to 0.95, especially when n ≥ 20" (Figure 8). Estimator performs well even when α_{i,j} are not i.i.d. (scenario 2), because α is never estimated.
- EPL 2020–21 league-level Δ̂ (Table 3, Δ̂ / σ̂ / p-value): Attacks w/ Shot 1.568 / 0.556 / 0.005; Defence Interceptions −1.997 / 1.200 / 0.096; Reaching Opponent Box 1.732 / 0.608 / 0.004; Reaching Opponent Half 3.592 / 0.949 / 0.000; Shots Blocked 0.350 / 0.329 / 0.287; Shots from Box 1.066 / 0.379 / 0.005; Shots from Danger Zone 0.786 / 0.279 / 0.005; Successful Key Passes 0.489 / 0.237 / 0.039; Touches in Box 2.253 / 1.043 / 0.031; XG 0.232 / 0.092 / 0.011; Yellow Cards −0.026 / 0.126 / 0.834.
- 7 of 11 statistics significant at α=0.05 — all offense-side; defensive stats (interceptions, blocked shots) and referee proxy (yellow cards) NOT significant. Home advantage lives in offensive chance creation, not goals (goals showed no significant HFA), not defense, not officiating.
- Raw win record that season showed no home edge: 144 home wins (37.89%) vs 153 away wins (40.26%) — yet causal offensive effects remain positive, demonstrating the naive home-vs-away mean difference is confounded by team strength.
- Teams with the most significant team-level statistics: Fulham, Brighton, Newcastle United, Wolverhampton Wanderers — all bottom-half finishers (Fulham relegated), i.e., weaker teams retain larger home advantage; top teams (Man City, Man Utd, Liverpool) dominate regardless of venue.
- Caveat: 2020–21 was a COVID season with empty/reduced crowds — the headline findings (no officiating bias, no goal-level HFA) may be a crowd-absence artifact; the authors themselves propose comparing to normal seasons as follow-up.
## Intelligence connections
- (COACHING / SCHEME) The paired home/away differencing trick (eq. 6) that cancels the neutral-field nuisance term is a portable causal-identification device: it transfers to NFL division games, where each division pair plays home+away in the same season, yielding 6 paired differences per team-season to estimate team-specific causal home effects on offensive EPA/play, defensive EPA allowed, and penalty margin (referee-bias proxy). This serves the coaching-tendencies program (venue-dependent play-calling/officiating effects) and the matchup engine — replacing a flat +2.5-point home edge with team- and unit-specific causal HFA coefficients. The finding that HFA lives in offensive chance creation and is largest for weak teams suggests modeling home advantage as an offense-side team effect that compresses for elite teams, rather than a uniform spread adjustment.
- (TRUST-SIGNAL) The unbiased variance estimator (eq. 10) via the law of total variance, which avoids the biased quadratic-form estimator, is directly reusable anywhere GSE does hierarchical shrinkage of team effects — it gives an honest estimate of league-level heterogeneity in any per-team parameter.
- (OTHER) UNCERTAIN: A3 non-monotonicity (no style-matchup correlation in α_{i,j}) is motivated by anecdote rather than evidence; if neutral-field pair outcomes correlate within teams, the paired sum does not fully remove confounding. Also Gaussianity and i.i.d. noise assumptions are strong for NFL outcomes, which are heteroskedastic; exact CIs will need a robust or bootstrap variant.

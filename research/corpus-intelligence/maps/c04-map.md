## Top 20 most engine-actionable findings

1. **Fractional-Kelly with a survival-first tuning gate** — plain Kelly ruined 100% of trajectories (median final wealth 9.1e-6 NBA, 2.3e-9 soccer) in negative-AKL scenarios; grid-tuned fractional Kelly stayed profitable (NBA median 2.4 / ruin 0%; soccer KellyDrawdown median 10.25 / ruin 0%); gate = maximize median wealth subject to ≤5% of trajectories drawing below 90%. Fills research-map GAP #1 (Kelly under estimation error — mentioned 12× in repo, zero papers read). (`docs/arxiv-program/research/2026-09-21/arxiv-deep/0171-optimal-sports-betting-strategies-in-practice.md`, r69 — ADOPT)

(`docs/CLAUDE_SESSION_HANDOFF_2026-06-24.md`, r68)

(`docs/calibration-proposals/2026-09-05-market-anchored-display-probability-v5.2.8.md`, r80)
4. **Complementary football is real and measurable.** Previous-drive non-scoring turnover selected in ≥80% of 3×10-fold CV replicates (turnover×starting-position 100%); a takeaway adds +0.6–1.0 points/drive with a +21-yard field-position bump (median post-turnover start: own 41). 5. **Odds→probability upgrade: OO-EPC + FL-GLM.** OO-EPC is a parameter-free analytic converter beating naive multiplicative normalization on a majority of 5 books (Pinnacle 1.00428 log-loss); one-parameter FL-GLM beat multinomial/ordered logistic for all 5 books with fitted β = 1.06–1.15 confirming favorite-longshot bias. ## Cross-file patterns

### Metrics that recur
- **Brier/log-loss as the universal currency** across ~40 ledgers; **ECE variants** (binned, rankECE, dual-ECE) as the calibration lingua franca; **CLV** (vs close, vs open — the latter named as the untested avenue) as the market-efficiency check.
- **Kelly-family sizing** appears in 6 independent sources (0171 fractional+survival gate, 1625 ridge fractional-Kelly, 1369 unpopularity premium, 0839 conformal-Kelly, 0247 bootstrap-CI-fed Kelly, 1753 streak-aware ruin gate) — they compose into one sizing stack: fractional Kelly × unpopularity tilt × conformal-width scaling, floored by a ruin gate, fed the lower confidence bound.
- **Abstention/no-bet consensus** across 5 sources (1003 metric-targeted +10–30pt lifts, 1013 predictor-rejector, 1147 reject-option, 1157 selective-prediction-from-agreement, 0698 ranking-with-abstention) — the corpus independently re-derived learning-to-abstain; the 1013 two-stage head is the concrete build.
- **Ensemble doctrine** converging: BLP 2-parameter post-processing (1554), two-level AFTER (0786), WALLA per-game advantage weights (0796), SGP stacking (1177), market leg via logit(P)=β₀+β₁·spread (0518). Compose: market-leg + model-leg → SGP/WALLA weights → BLP calibration.
- **Resolution-starvation** as the binding constraint (RES ~0.002–0.005, needs 6–10×; no calibration maps while RES<0.02; r47, r48, r85) — repeated independently in engine docs and arXiv ledgers.

### Contradictions between sources
- **Shin vs proportional de-vig:** the calibration proposal (r80) shows proportional de-vig beats Shin head-to-head on 621 settled book-priced picks (paired Brier diff +0.002151, t=1.798; Shin's entire moneyline advantage came from 11 pathological books) and commits `MARKET_FAIR_METHOD_TAG = "proportional_devig_v1"` — while `docs/data/CARDS_SCANNERS.md` (r81) prescribes Shin devig via `noVigFromAmericanPrices` and bans proportional for props. Tension is scope-dependent (moneyline vs props) but unresolved as a single engine-wide fair-probability rule.
- **Confidence inversion population is dead:** the ranking-basis census (r84) shows the 34.8% "confidence branch" was entirely legacy Jun–Jul data; the current Aug–Sep cohort is roughly monotone. The AGENTS.md inversion work is aimed at a population that no longer exists — pooled-era fits average two different populations.
- **HFA constant:** 1.73 pts declining (1077, Bayesian, strength-adjusted) vs the standing 3-pt constant used elsewhere in the repo.
- **Negative results documented as lanes to keep closed:** plain Kelly (100% ruin in AKL-negative), tracking-only DPI classification (F1 0.164 — data modality can't solve it), line-path-geometry features for CLV (MLB totals), CRPS-Sum.

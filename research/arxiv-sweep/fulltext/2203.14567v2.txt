There are additional complications in real-world implementations of Elo. For legibility and practicality, fractional and negative rating points are avoided by scaling and shifting points up and rounding to the nearest integer, and by imposing an artificial floor on possible ratings (by gifting a player points if they would otherwise dip below the floor).

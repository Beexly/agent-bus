Evaluation Metrics. We use four standard metrics to evalu-
ate ball tracking performance:
 • Precision (Prec.): The ratio of correctly predicted ball
   positions (within a distance threshold) to the total number
   of predictions.
 • Recall (Rec.): The ratio of correctly predicted ball posi-
   tions to the total number of ground-truth visible balls.
 • Mean Distance Error (MDE): The average Euclidean dis-
   tance in pixels between predicted and ground-truth posi-
   tions for visible balls, assuming a 1920x1080 resolution.

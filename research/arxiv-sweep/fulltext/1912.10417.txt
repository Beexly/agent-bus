Despite this recognized central role, the main part of the studies on this topic focus on performance level measurement, neglecting other important characteristics, such as variability. In this paper, shooting performance variability is modeled with a Markov Switching dynamic, assuming the existence of two alternating performance regimes. Then, the relationships between each player&#39;s variability and the lineup composition is modeled as an ARIMA process with covariates and described with network analysis tools, in order to extrapolate positive and negative interactions between teammates Comments: 19 pages, 6 figures Subjects: Applications (stat.AP) Cite as: arXiv:1912.10417 [stat.AP] &nbsp; (or arXiv:1912.10417v1 [stat.AP] for this version) &nbsp; https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1912.10417 Focus to learn more arXiv-issued DOI via DataCite Submission history From: Rodolfo Metulini [ view email ] [v1] Sun, 22 Dec 2019 10:51:29 UTC (671 KB) Full-text links: Access Paper: View a PDF of the paper titled Modelling basketball players&#39; performance and interactions between teammates with a regime switching approach, by Paola Zuccolotto and Marco Sandri and Marica Manisera and Rodolfo Metulini View PDF view license Current browse context: stat.AP &lt;&nbsp;prev &nbsp; | &nbsp; next&nbsp;&gt; new | recent | 2019-12 Change to browse by: stat References &amp; Citations NASA ADS Google Scholar Semantic Scholar export BibTeX citation Loading...

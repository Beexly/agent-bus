Yohai Affiliation: University of Buenos Aires and CONICET Abstract In this paper we propose a family of robust estimates for isotonic regression: isotonic M-estimators. We show that their asymptotic distribution is, up to an scalar factor, the same as that of Brunk ’s classical isotonic estimator. We also derive the influence function and the breakdown point of these estimates. Finally we perform a Monte Carlo study that shows that the proposed family includes estimators that are simultaneously highly efficient under gaussian errors and highly robust when the error distribution has heavy tails. Keywords : Isotonic Regression, M-estimators, Robust Estimates. 1 Introduction Let x 1 , … , x n x_{1},\ldots,x_{n} be independent random variables collected along observation points t 1 ≤ … ≤ t n t_{1}\leq\ldots\leq t_{n} according to the model x j = μ ⁡ ( t j ) + u j , x_{j}=\mu(t_{j})+u_{j}, (1) where the u j u_{j} ’s are i.i.d. symmetric random variables with distribution G G . In isotonic regression the trend term μ ⁡ ( t ) \mu(t) is monotone non-decreasing, i.e., μ ⁡ ( t 1 ) ≤ … ≤ μ ⁡ ( t n ) \mu(t_{1})\leq\ldots\leq\mu(t_{n}) , but it is otherwise arbitrary. In this set-up, the classical estimator of μ ⁡ ( t ) \mu(t) is the function g g which minimizes the L 2 L_{2} distance between the vector of observed and fitted responses, i.e, it minimizes, ∑ j = 1 n [ x j − g ⁡ ( t j ) ] 2 \sum_{j=1}^{n}[x_{j}-g(t_{j})]^{2} (2) in the class 𝒢 \mathcal{G} of non-decreasing piecewise continuous functions. It is trivial but noteworthy that Equation ( 2 ) posits a finite dimensional convex constrained optimization problem. Its solution was first proposed by Brunk (1958) and has received extensive attention in the Statistical literature (see e.g., Robertson, Wright and Dyskra (1988) for a comprehensive account). It is also worth noting that any piecewise continuous non-decreasing function which agrees with the optimizer of ( 2 ) at the t j t_{j} ’s will be a solution. For that reason, in order to achieve uniqueness, it is traditional to restrict further the class 𝒢 0 \mathcal{G}_{0} to the subset of piecewise constant non-decreasing functions. Another valid choice consists in the interpolation at the knots with non-decreasing cubic splines or any other piecewise continuous monotone function, e.g., Meyer (1996). We will call this estimator the L ​ 2 {}_{\emph{2}} isotonic estimator . The sensitivity of this estimator to extreme observations (outliers) was noted by Wang and Huang (2002), who propose minimizing instead using the L 1 L_{1} norm, i.e. , minimizing ∑ j = 1 n | x j − g ⁡ ( t j ) | . \sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|x_{j}-g(t_{j})\right|. This estimator will be call here L 1 Isotonic estimator . Wang and Huang (2002) developed the asymptotic distribution of the trend estimator at a given observation point t 0 t_{0} and obtained the asymptotic relative efficiency of this estimator compared with the classical L 2 estimator. Interestingly, this efficiency turned out to be 2 / π = 0.637 2/\pi=0.637 , the same as in the i.i.d. location problem. In this paper we will propose instead a robust isotonic M-estimator aimed at balancing robustness with efficiency. Specifically we shall seek the minimizer of ∑ j = 1 n ρ ⁡ ( x j − g ⁡ ( t j ) σ ^ n ) \sum_{j=1}^{n}\rho\left(\frac{x_{j}-g(t_{j})}{\widehat{\sigma}_{n}}\right) (3) where σ ^ n \widehat{\sigma}_{n} is a an estimator of the error scale previously obtained and ρ \rho satisfies the following properties A1 (i) ρ ⁡ ( x ) \rho(x) is non-decreasing in | x | |x| , (ii) ρ ⁡ ( 0 ) = 0 \rho(0)=0 , (iii) ρ \rho is even, (iv) ρ ⁡ ( x ) \rho(x) is strictly increasing for x &gt; 0 x&gt;0 and (v) ρ \rho has two continuous derivatives and ψ = ρ ′ \psi=\rho^{\prime} is bounded and monotone non-decreasing. Clearly, the L 2 L_{2} choice corresponds to taking ρ ⁡ ( x ) = x 2 \rho(x)=x^{2} while the L 1 L_{1} option is akin to opting for ρ ⁡ ( x ) = | x | \rho(x)=|x| . These two estimators do no require the scale estimator σ ^ n . \widehat{\sigma}_{n}. Note that the class of M-estimators satisfying A1 does not include estimators with a redescending choice for ψ \psi . We believe that the strict differentiability conditions on ρ \rho required in A1 are not strictly necessary, but they make the proofs for the asymptotic theory simpler. Moreover, some functions ρ \rho which are not twice differentiable everywhere such as | x | |x| or the Huber s’ functions defined below in ( 7 ) can be approximated by functions satisfying A1. The asymptotic distribution of the L 2 isotonic estimators at a given point was found by Brunk (1970) and Wright (1981) and the one of the L 1 estimator by Wang (2002). They prove that the distribution of these estimators conveniently normalized converge to the distribution of the slope at zero of the greatest convex minorant of the two-sided Brownian Motion with parabolic drift. In this paper, we prove a similar result for isotonic M-estimators. The focus of this paper is on estimation of the trend term at a single observation point t 0 t_{0} . We do not address the issue of distribution of the whole stochastic process { μ ^ n ​ ( t ) , t ∈ 𝒯 } \{\hat{\mu}_{n}(t),t\in\mathcal{T}\} . Recent research along those lines are given by Kulikova and Lopuhaä (2006) and a related result with smoothing was also obtained simultaneously in Pal and Woodroofe (2006).
